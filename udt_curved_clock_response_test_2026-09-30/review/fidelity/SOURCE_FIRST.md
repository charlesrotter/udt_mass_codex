## Necessary condition and genuine curved-sector obstruction

Define M(s)=integral D(s,a)dnu(a) and
B(s)=integral D(s,a)D_s(s,a)dnu(a). Compact regularity and finite measure give

    delta C=integral w(s)[M(s)psi'(s)+B(s)psi(s)]ds.        (F2)

Stationarity against all reciprocal strains therefore requires, already on this
restricted tube family,

    (w M)'=w B                                           (F3)

as a distribution on the emission interval (or its open extension when the
common tube extends there). For smooth product data, B=(1/2)partial_s integral
D^2 dnu. Integration by parts retains the derivative of w and uses its compact
support; it is incorrect to infer M'=B after dropping w'. Any nonvanishing
endpoint weights would instead supply boundary terms. CES1 uses smooth compact
weights, so those terms vanish here for the stated reason.

F3 is necessary, not sufficient for stationarity against all strains. It does
not force D=0 query by query and does not justify varying a receiver independently
when many rays share the same spacetime tube. For example, signed constant D
with zero mean passes these restricted tube tests; this algebraic cancellation
is not a constructed stationary physical population or curved solution.

If D is uniformly positive on the fixed compact population and D_s is bounded,
choose psi positive on the whole sampled emission support and psi'=K psi there,
with K>sup|D_s|. A compact cutoff equals one on a neighborhood of that support

    delta C=integral D psi(K+D_s)dmu>0.                   (F4)

K and the amplitude are variation controls, not a selected physical scale or

contrasts fails the proposed exact stationarity, irrespective of whether the

## What local SR does and does not add

A second, explicitly stronger quantifier would require the same stationarity
law for every sufficiently small CES1 laboratory in one fixed smooth metric.

    D_ell(s,a) -> eta>0 uniformly as ell->0.

The label normalization uses the rescaled emission/separation densities; there
is no outcome-dependent reweighting. Positive eta_min supplies a margin, so F4
applies for sufficiently small ell>0. This is a local limit inside an arbitrary
fixed smooth curved metric, not exact flatness as a physical gate and not a
globally flat solution demand. Continuity with an explicit positive margin
suffices; a claimed numerical curvature threshold or error coefficient would
need a separate bound and is not supplied here.

The all-laboratory statement therefore has a conditional obstruction without
requiring globally Minkowski UDT. But the extra quantifier is material: a law
defined only for one fixed physical or cosmic ensemble cannot be replaced by
all separately prepared small experiments. Their functionals are different.
Curvature/gravitational shifts might change signs in a large fixed ensemble;
the present proof neither constructs a stationary such ensemble nor excludes it.
