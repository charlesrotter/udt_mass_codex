# G142 lay report

The missing “shared ruler tape” was partly already there and partly not.

UDT's founding supplies an abstract instrument design: one clock channel, one ruler channel, the
posited reciprocal gearing, and the observed `c_E` conversion between their units. Reciprocity
derives how a supplied depth acts on that design; it does not derive the design as a physical
observer carrier. It is a blueprint, not yet an installed instrument.

Each actual observer still needs an adapter connecting that blueprint to their local geometry. A
comparison also needs a coupling between A's adapter and B's adapter. That coupling is the carry
`M_BA`.

The complete comparison is not merely “B's reading minus A's reading.” It is:

```text
B's calibration × A-to-B carry × inverse of A's calibration.
```

Changing the private coordinate markings on either adapter changes the individual pieces but not
the complete comparison. That is the important simplification: endpoint dilation and transport are
parts of one object, not separate effects bolted together afterward.

In one fixed, matched set of adapter markings, a carry with no reciprocal imbalance gives the same
scalar `Phi_B-Phi_A` law as G141 while retaining shift or common-scale information. That neutrality
label changes if A and B independently remark their adapters; only the complete comparison is
invariant. Identity carry is the special case that recovers G141's whole transition matrix.

Co-presence says A and B belong to the same complete universe; it does not manufacture their
adapters or decide which comparison protocol they use. That may be ordinary experimental/query
input rather than missing physics. What remains open is whether UDT intends a unique comparison
from bare endpoints. If it does, the physical carry law—and any physical reason to restrict it to
the triangular class used in this audit—is still missing.
