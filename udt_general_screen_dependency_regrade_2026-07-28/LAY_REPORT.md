# Lay report

The earlier work was mostly done with the angular part of the metric simplified so that its two
directions changed together. That simplification gave one main control knob, called `lambda`.

We now know the complete angular screen has three genuine geometric controls: one changes its
overall area and two change its shape. The old calculations did not become false. They describe
the special case where the two shape controls were held fixed.

An analogy is testing a piano while its left and right pedals are mechanically linked. Every note
you recorded is still a real note. But a conclusion such as “the piano has only one pedal” must be
corrected once the linkage is removed.

That is what happened here:

- the old exact formulas survive in their restricted setting;
- the old failure to find a unique choice generally survives, because more freedom does not create
  uniqueness by itself;
- a few statements saying the extra shapes were unknown are now obsolete because we constructed
  them; and
- before using the angular geometry to argue that the Hopf-like branch is uniquely preferred, we
  must repeat that particular comparison with all three geometric controls free.

We do **not** need to redo the reciprocal clock law, the Lorentz/frame algebra, the SNe fit, the
conditional finite-box Hopfion stability calculation, or the action adjudication because of this
screen correction. The most important scientific rework is narrow: revisit the Hopf/toric
selection question in the full angular geometry.
