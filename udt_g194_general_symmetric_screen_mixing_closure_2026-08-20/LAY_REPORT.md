# G194 lay report

G193 had turned on most, but not all, of the symmetric two-direction screen machinery.  One entry
was still frozen to zero.  G194 freed it.

The result is clean: the exact structure survives for **every smooth symmetric two-direction
mixing pattern** in this metric family.  The instruments can change strength, exchange influence,
cross zero, change rank, and rotate their preferred axes with distance/time.  They do not have to
march in lockstep.

Even so, the light-screen calculation still reorganizes into one ordered geometric expression.
That expression is automatically nonzero away from the starting observer, so this whole family
does not form an extra focusing point or caustic after launch.

What changed is important: the result no longer depends on the missing symmetric component being
turned off.  The full symmetric `2 x 2` sector is now covered.  The simplicity came from the
metric's symmetric architecture, not from a convenient diagonal or rank-one shortcut.

What did not change: this is still one supplied central observer-pair germ in one declared
coframe family.  It does not choose the universe's history, observer population, light-transfer
law, or `X_max`.  The next genuinely new question is what happens when the omitted antisymmetric
screen-rotation channel is activated.

A fresh external reviewer also reran the sealed write-free evidence package and accepted the
repairs without widening that conclusion.
