# G287 lay report

Two different minus signs had been discussed as if they were the same.

The first belongs to the universe's supplied metric profile. In Charles's intended picture,
negative `phi` is the small-scale side, near-zero is the ordinary GR-overlap side, and positive
`phi` is the cosmological side.

The second minus sign merely says that we reversed the comparison arrow. If Alice compares to Bob,
the arrow has one sign; if Bob compares to Alice, it has the opposite sign. Reversing that arrow
does not turn a cosmological region into a microscopic one and does not change the metric.

The good news is that the repository's mathematics already knew this. G263 stated it explicitly.
Our recent conversational explanation blurred the two signs; the kernel itself did not.

The negative-profile sector therefore remains a genuinely different geometric sector that could
matter when the micro/mass side is eventually joined to the complete history. This audit does not
prove that it creates mass or supplies the missing evolution law. It simply restores the correct
map so that future work does not erase that possibility by mistaking it for observer reversal.
