The useful new finding is that clocks can become quiet while beam geometry still
changes strongly. For an exactly specified family of paths across our evolving
example spacetime, the clock ratio approaches its background value, but the area
contrast grows and the two beam widths become increasingly unequal. Looking only
at clocks would miss that structure. “Quiet” here means a ratio approaching one,
not no clock evolution, no curvature or disappearance of geometry.

We also have a proof that neighboring rays in these specified infinitesimal beams
do not refocus to a conjugate endpoint. That proof covers transverse paths starting
on the metric's reflection planes, including mixed transverse directions. For
more general tilted paths we built and checked the complete beam evaluator; the
finite examples passed, but their all-time focusing behavior remains open.

This advances the roadmap by joining timing, area and shape along the same paths.
It is conditional geometric progress, not a new construction of spacetime or a
demonstration that these beams are physical light. The supplied observer family
is explicit. The existing reciprocal relations remain intact under the checked
observer changes; no universally preferred observer frame is introduced.

a test. It does not yet qualify an optical instrument or supply independently
calibrated source/distance data for the ZDR1 observational target. A future test

it need not wait for a unique universe or complete matter theory. These data and
