# G340 preregistration — finite-separated normal-observer relations

Date: 2026-09-03
Outcome status: unseen at preregistration

## Frozen spacetime and queries

Use only

\[
 g=-dT^2+C_X^2T^{-2/3}dX^2+C_\perp^2T^{4/3}(dy^2+dz^2),\qquad T>0,
\]

on a supplied compact translation quotient, with supplied normal observers at fixed spatial
labels. Work first on the universal cover and attach an explicit lattice lift to every spatial or
null route. `T` is geometric proper-clock length; an observer's proper time is `tau=T/c_E`.

## Pinned and free items

- metric, time orientation, normal congruence: `pinned-by-THEORY`, conditional on the cited chain;
- observer labels, emission/reflection/reception events, lattice lifts: `free-and-explored` query
  data;
- ideal two-leg radar reflection and arithmetic midpoint: `pinned-by-HABIT`, supplied operational
  control only;
- endpoint frequency ratio and G298 transported-source projective readout: `pinned-by-THEORY` on a
  supplied regular directed null leg;
- light source, Maxwell field, luminosity, opacity, transfer, matter, scale, and `X_max`: omitted.

## Preregistered alternatives

1. `A__METRIC_NULL_GEOMETRY_CLOSES_A_PATH_LABELLED_FINITE_PAIR_FAMILY__NO_LIGHT_MODEL_REQUIRED`:
   the metric derives the null exchange, radar timing conditional on its protocol, and one-leg
   projective readout; these are related but nonidentical objects.
2. `B__AN_EXTRA_LIGHT_OR_TRANSFER_MODEL_IS_REQUIRED_EVEN_FOR_CAUSAL_TIMING`:
   the metric and supplied routes do not suffice to determine endpoint timing or redshift.
3. `C__PRINCIPAL_PATHS_CLOSE_EXACTLY_BUT_GENERIC_FINITE_PAIRS_REMAIN_ONLY_A_SET_VALUED_QUADRATURE`:
   the axial subfamilies close, while the generic branch cannot be stated without an added model.

## Required derivations

1. Derive the conserved spatial covector and null Hamiltonian directly from the metric.
2. Derive the general path-labelled endpoint quadrature without linearization.
3. Derive exact longitudinal and transverse principal-axis arrival maps for every supplied winding.
4. Derive same-slice distance and prove it is not generally radar range or one-leg projective
   position.
5. Derive two-leg radar relations for independently supplied outgoing and return lift lengths;
   specialize to equal lift lengths only as an explicit symmetric control.
6. Derive the endpoint frequency ratio, ordered depth, and planar projective coordinate on each
   principal null leg.
7. State earliest-arrival and cut-locus multiplicity without deleting later winding branches.
8. Audit scale, causality, route, and physical-protocol ownership.

## Certification and falsification

- production: at least 500 analytic/exact or high-precision nonlinear checks over both principal
  sectors, multiple times, separations, boosts/depths, and windings;
- independent route: reconstruct null propagation and frequency from the four-metric without
  importing production code or reading production results;
- hostile controls must catch at least: wrong Kasner exponent, missing winding, radar=slice
  conflation, radar=projective conflation, reversed frequency ratio, negative-distance/sign
  conflation, `c_E` used as a history/scale selector, and a null route promoted to a light field;
- raw residual tolerance: `2e-11` for double-precision numerical identities; exact integer/rational
  identities where available;
- a failed general inverse endpoint solve cannot be hidden by reporting only axial formulas;
- no result may exceed the maximum conclusion in `MAP.md`.

## Omitted scope

Generic accelerated observers, nonprincipal screen mixing, caustics, rank-changing germs,
electromagnetic fields, luminosity/flux, sources, matter/mass, stability, observations, occupancy,
physical protocol selection, absolute scale, physical `X_max`, and canon.
