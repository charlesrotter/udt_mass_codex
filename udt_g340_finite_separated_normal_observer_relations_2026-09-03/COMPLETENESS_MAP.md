# G340 completeness map

## Included

- all positive emission/reflection/reception times on the regular principal-axis route strata;
- every supplied integer winding on a split compact-axis control;
- general nonprincipal supplied-momentum null quadrature;
- same-slice distance on a general compact translation lattice;
- unequal and equal outgoing/return lift lengths;
- endpoint frequency/depth/projective readout for both principal sectors;
- exact scale and path-label typing.

## Excluded

- inversion/classification of every generic endpoint boundary-value problem;
- generic caustics, cut loci beyond the explicit lattice multiplicity, and rank change;
- accelerated or nonnormal observers;
- nonprincipal transported screen state and full G298 projection choice;
- physical route, radar convention, or observer-population selection;
- light fields, transfer, luminosity, matter, action, source, fit, scale, and `X_max`.

Any positive result is one-spacetime, supplied-query, and branch-labelled.
