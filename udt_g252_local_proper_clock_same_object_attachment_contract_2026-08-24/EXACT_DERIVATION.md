# G252 exact derivation — local proper-clock same-object attachment

Date: 2026-08-24

## 1. Bounded metric arena

Take the already supplied G249 one-scale family

\[
g_\ell=\ell^2\bar g,\qquad \ell>0,
\]

on one supplied regular branch. Let \(\Gamma\) be one supplied timelike observer segment between
ordered event markers \(p_0\) and \(p_1\). Its dimensionless-history proper time is

\[
\bar\tau[\Gamma;p_0,p_1]
=\int_\Gamma\sqrt{-\bar g(v,v)}\,d\lambda>0.
\]

Positive homothety gives, exactly,

\[
\tau_\ell[\Gamma;p_0,p_1]
=\int_\Gamma\sqrt{-g_\ell(v,v)}\,d\lambda
=\ell\bar\tau[\Gamma;p_0,p_1].
\]

This is the G250 weight-\(+1\) proper-time class. It changes no reciprocal-kernel operator.

## 2. Empirical attachment and unique scale

Now supply an independently calibrated proper-clock record \(\tau_*>0\) attached to exactly the
same observer, branch, start event, and end event. The empirical attachment statement is

\[
\tau_*[\Gamma;p_0,p_1]=\tau_\ell[\Gamma;p_0,p_1].
\]

Since \(\bar\tau>0\), it has the unique positive solution

\[
\boxed{\ell=\frac{\tau_*}{\bar\tau}}.
\]

The equality is not derived by the metric. It is the declared bridge between one independently
calibrated record and the metric evaluator on the same physical object. Replacing \(\tau_*\) by a
metric self-evaluation makes the equation circular and supplies no independent scale.

## 3. Reparameterization and units

For an orientation-preserving parameter change \(\lambda'=a\lambda\), \(a>0\),

\[
\frac{dx}{d\lambda'}=\frac1a\frac{dx}{d\lambda},
\qquad d\lambda'=a\,d\lambda,
\]

so the proper-time integrand and recovered \(\ell\) are unchanged. Rescaling the numerical unit of
both \(\tau_*\) and \(\bar\tau\) by the same positive factor also cancels in their ratio.

After the attachment, the observed calibration constant \(c_E\) supplies the clock-equivalent
length

\[
L_{\rm clock}=c_E\tau_*.
\]

This is a time-to-length conversion. It contributes no new scale equation or fitted coefficient.

## 4. Additional attachments test the supplied history

For two independently frozen attachments on the same supplied dimensionless history,

\[
\ell_1=\frac{\tau_{*,1}}{\bar\tau_1},\qquad
\ell_2=\frac{\tau_{*,2}}{\bar\tau_2}.
\]

The one-scale history is consistent exactly when

\[
\boxed{\ell_1=\ell_2}.
\]

Failure rejects that supplied dimensionless history under this attachment contract. It does not
license one fitted scale per observer or per interval.

and the equal-scale consistency criterion.
