# Primary-source note — megamaser local rate

Primary source:

```text
Pesce et al. (2020), The Megamaser Cosmology Project. XIII.
Combined Hubble constant constraints, arXiv:2001.09213.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.09213
```

The abstract reports `H0=73.9 +/- 3.0 km s^-1 Mpc^-1`, combining six megamaser systems, and states
that the value is independent of the distance ladder and CMB. It also records a fixed 250 km/s
velocity uncertainty and notes that the headline value uses the maser distances and velocities
without peculiar-velocity corrections.

This audit uses only that published combined rate. It does not claim to reproduce the individual
distance table, the branch's five-object exclusion, or its approximate ten-percent `X` result.
Those require a separate local data acquisition, preregistration, and replay if they become
load-bearing.
