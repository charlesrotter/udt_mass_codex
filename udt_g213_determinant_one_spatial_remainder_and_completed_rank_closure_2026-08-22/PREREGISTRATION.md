## Frozen tests

### T1 — exact determinant-one spatial census

For a positive determinant-normalized spatial metric relative to a supplied positive reference,
construct the unique positive relative endomorphism and its unique self-adjoint trace-free
logarithm. Relative to the supplied radial line and two-dimensional screen, test whether it splits
uniquely into:

- one radial-versus-screen grading scalar;
- two radial-screen mixing components;
- two trace-free screen-shape components.

The required total is five, matching the dimension of positive definite `3 x 3` forms at fixed
determinant. A global Schur-complement factorization will independently check the same `1+2+2`
count without relying on the matrix logarithm.

claim is a coordinate-coverage statement in the supplied split.

### T3 — completed-pair information equivalence

typed completed relation `(known germ, m, h_s)` reconstructs `h_sigma` exactly by the coordinate
Jacobian `diag(1,m)`, and conversely whether `h_sigma` uniquely determines `(m,h_s)` under the G176
working clarification.

### T4 — finite rank-ten witness

Replace each full pullback in the exact G129 six-plane rank-ten design by its completed tuple
`(m,h_s)`. Reconstruct the full pullbacks and require exact recovery of all ten ambient metric
components. The production and independent implementations must not import one another.

### T5 — density-blind hostile control

Delete `m` while retaining the completed metrics and known germs. Test the spatial rescaling family

`g -> diag(1,lambda I_3)^T g diag(1,lambda I_3)`

at fixed calibrated pair maps. All completed normalized pullbacks must remain equal while the
ambient metric changes for `lambda != 1`. This is the preregistered counterexample to claiming that
`h_s` alone is metric-faithful.
