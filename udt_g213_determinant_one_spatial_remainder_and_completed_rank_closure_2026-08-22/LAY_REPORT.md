# G213 lay report

The completed kernel did **not** throw away part of the metric, provided we keep the calibration of
its ruler tape.

Think of each observer pair as returning a normalized chord plus a note saying how densely its
ruler marks were packed. The normalized chord alone forgets one kind of overall spatial scale.
The ruler-density note restores it exactly. Six suitably different pair chords, with their ruler
densities and known directions, can reconstruct all ten local components of the UDT metric.

We also finished the local spatial instrument count. After overall spatial volume is separated,
there are five shape/mixing controls: two distort the screen, two mix radial and screen directions,
and one trades radial scale against screen scale. Earlier work explicitly exercised the first four;
the fifth was simply not catalogued as its own coordinate.

What this does **not** do is choose how those controls vary across the universe. It proves the
completed observer network can faithfully *be* the metric state once its values are present. It
does not yet compress those values into a handful of constants or observational anchors.
