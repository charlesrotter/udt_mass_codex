# Lay report

We tested whether the rebuilt observer relation only looked coherent because its first examples
were simple and flat. It did not.

Think of one beam leaving one observer as a small four-part machine. Two parts say how the
observer's clock-and-distance comparison changes. Two other parts say how neighboring viewing
directions spread, squeeze, and shear across the sky. In the old scaffolding those pieces were
sometimes forced to be the same object. G110 separated them while keeping them inside one machine.

G111 put that machine into a genuinely curved, twisted, angular UDT metric. The clock-distance
part and the sky part both became active, and the same underlying metric connected them without
making them identical. Nothing was frozen or bolted on afterward in this local calculation.

That is a real structural pass. It is not yet a universe model: the R17 history and profile were
test controls, not selected by the metric. The next honest question is whether this type correction
leaves the already frozen SNe calculations unchanged—without refitting them.
