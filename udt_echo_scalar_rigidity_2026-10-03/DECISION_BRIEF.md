# ESR1 lay discussion return

The proposed simple echo rule is selective. In the controlled class we studied,
requiring one scalar rule for every prepared observer and direction forces the
geometry to have constant sectional curvature. That conclusion follows already
from the first relevant nonlinear clock terms; it does not require a large
numerical search. Positive, negative and flat curvature all survive, with no
chosen physical scale.

The requirement is stronger than “the same governing law applies to everyone.”
It says that the first signal's single clock ratio must contain everything needed
to determine the echo, regardless of the other directional information available
in the geometry. Our theorem makes that extra requirement's cost explicit: within
this class it removes anisotropic curvature. It does not establish that UDT
requires it or that this is a new physical prediction beyond known geometries.

Recommendation: retain FC as a conditional diagnostic and do not adopt it as a
universal law of total UDT clock comparisons on this evidence. The founding
positional interpretation, ordinary clocks, additional-effect requirement and
tested SR/GR recovery remain unchanged. Applying FC only to a supposed positional
part would first require a justified way to define that part; no subtraction is
supplied here. Generic gravity with ∇R≠0 was not classified or empirically tested.

The immediate return is discussion of this selectivity and its physical motivation.
A possible next mathematical question is whether the rigidity survives removing
the parallel-curvature restriction. That would require new variable-curvature
clock terms, proof and counterexample checks; ESR1 does not answer it or authorize
it automatically. More numerical examples inside the already-classified sector
would not resolve the missing physical implication. FCW1's separate completion
lead also remains an available, unstarted alternative.

No new premise or canon decision is requested by banking this result. If another
research step is chosen, make its quantifier, review/repair budget and return
point explicit before starting. The present authorized work ends with the
reviewed conditional theorem, central integration, required checks and push.
