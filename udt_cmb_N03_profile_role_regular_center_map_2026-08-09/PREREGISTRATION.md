that can support the conditional C1/C2 angular architecture while preserving—rather than
identifying by habit—the observer-pair `c_eff`/depth/`X_max` relation? If not, what is the full
regular-center jet space and which role-joins remain open?

This is metric-led. It is not a search for a profile that yields a desired spectrum.
