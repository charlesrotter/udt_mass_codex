# Lay report

We had been asking one formula to do two different jobs.

The supernova/P1 formula describes how two observers compare their clocks as their separation
changes. A centered local metric describes how spacetime behaves in the immediate neighborhood of
one smooth point. A two-observer comparison may change linearly with separation; copying that same
linear behavior into every direction around a single center creates a mathematical corner.

That does not damage P1 or the observer-pair clock law. It tells us not to paste the pair law
directly into a centered local lapse as though they were identical objects.

The angular orchestra survives this correction. At a smooth point its leading note is round because
every direction collapses to one point, but it need not go silent. Mu remains on: normalized mixing
may remain finite and the full screen, shift, and angular coupling can turn on away from the point.
The exact regularity result does not pick the strength or physical meaning of that mixing.

We also made the inventory honest. It now explicitly includes the reference-observer `c_eff`
readout, the complete-arrow strain readout, and RA1's two different center possibilities. None of
the mapped objects is yet a complete universe-wide profile.

The remaining bridge has a precise name: the physical observer/path **groupoid cocycle**—the rule
that assigns one compositional clock-depth comparison to a complete A-to-B geometry.
Transport is one possible way to realize such a rule, but it needs an extra local first-order premise and is not something the present result derived.
The next investigation can test whether the complete coframe naturally supplies that candidate;
it cannot assume the answer.
