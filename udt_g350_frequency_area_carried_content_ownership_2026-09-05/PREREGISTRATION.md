## Frozen question and regular domain

For a supplied labelled null ray and ordered regular cuts `i,j,k`, let `omega_i>0` be the metric
frequency measured by a supplied finite future-timelike observer and let `J_i>0` be the G349 metric
two-Jacobian from one common supplied celestial source presentation to cut `i`. Define

```text
R_ji = omega_j / omega_i,
A_ji = J_j / J_i.
```

Freeze the candidate domain to continuous functions

```text
T : R_+ x R_+ -> R_+
```

that are local to one supplied ray label, satisfy `T(1,1)=1`, and obey

```text
T(R_kj R_ji, A_kj A_ji)=T(R_kj,A_kj) T(R_ji,A_ji).
```

No value of a carried scalar is supplied by this definition. The two-ratio restriction is a chosen
classification class, not a physical premise or completeness claim for radiation.

## Frozen analytic claims to prove or refute

1. `R_ki=R_kj R_ji` and `A_ki=A_kj A_ji` hold pointwise on every retained regular label; identity
   and reversal are componentwise group inverse.
2. With `x=log R`, `y=log A`, and `f=log T`, sewing becomes the continuous additive Cauchy equation
   on `R^2`. Therefore `f(x,y)=p x+q y` and `T=R^p A^q`, if alternative B holds.
3. Reversal then follows as `T(R^-1,A^-1)=T(R,A)^-1`; it cannot select `p` or `q`.
4. Under `omega_i -> D_i omega_i`, a character with exponent `p` is covariant when the carried
   scalar is assigned endpoint observer weight `p`. Because the scalar's physical type is absent,
   covariance alone cannot choose that weight.
5. The statement that `C_i J_i / omega_i^p` is conserved along a ray forces `q=-1`, but the
   conservation statement is not contained in the metric area theorem. Choosing `p=0`, `1`, `2`,
   or any other value remains a carried-object premise.
6. Every finite positive multiplier is homogeneous: `C_i=0` implies `C_j=0`. A nonzero source
   magnitude or distribution is not generated by the transfer factor.
7. For any positive endpoint-local scalar weight `W_i`, the coboundary

   ```text
   T_ji=(W_j/W_i) R_ji^p A_ji^q
   ```

   obeys identity, reversal, and sewing. Thus enlarging the local input class cannot restore
   uniqueness without a new restriction.
8. If `J_j/J_i` approaches zero, characters with `q>0`, `q=0`, and `q<0` respectively approach
   zero, a finite frequency factor, and infinity (for finite nonzero frequency ratio). Reversal
   exchanges zero and infinity. No strictly positive finite pointwise group character extends
   bidirectionally through all zero-area strata while retaining nonzero area dependence.
9. A supplied source measure can be pushed forward through each complete G349 labelled map, but
   its magnitude, conservation, repeated-sheet aggregation, and cross-label rule remain supplied.
