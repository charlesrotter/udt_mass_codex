## What would fix the weights

Saying “the amount carried by each labelled sheet is conserved” would select inverse-area behavior,
`q=-1`. That is a genuine additional conservation premise, not something already contained in the
area formula.

Choosing `p` requires defining how the carried component responds to measured frequency. A frequency-
neutral scalar, a one-frequency-weight quantity, and a two-frequency-weight quantity are different
physical definitions. The present metric geometry accepts all of them.

Even after both weights were chosen, the transfer multiplier would only move supplied source data.
It cannot create nonzero content from a zero source or determine the source's strength or angular
distribution.

## Why caustics matter

At a caustic the local sheet area can approach zero while the spacetime and full ray evolution
remain regular. With the other cut area positive and frequency ratio finite, an inverse-area
density then diverges. Reversal exchanges the two one-sided limits; it does not divide by zero.
If both compared areas vanish together, this simple limit test does not decide the answer. A complete physical law through such a point
needs a rule for a carried measure or field and for what happens when several labelled sheets meet.
The metric supplies the map and its multiplicity, but not whether physical contents add, cancel,
interfere, or are populated at all.
