# G80 lay report

G79 measured one chosen stretch of the model from observer A toward observer B. G80 turned the
entire measuring setup around—not just the radial arrow—and asked whether B looking back toward A
gets the mathematically reciprocal answer.

It does. The clock-dilation number becomes its exact inverse. The signed depth changes sign. The
two-dimensional image-size map comes back transposed and enlarged by exactly the clock-ratio
factor required by the different endpoint normalization. The reversed calculation also lands back
on the original event with the original screen orientation.

That is a strong consistency check on the observer-pair machinery. It says the two directions are
two views of one geometric relation, not two unrelated formulas. It still tests only one chosen
control geometry. It does not determine the real cosmic profile, `X_max`, universe size, supernova
fit, CMB temperature pattern, or a physical signal travelling backward in time.
