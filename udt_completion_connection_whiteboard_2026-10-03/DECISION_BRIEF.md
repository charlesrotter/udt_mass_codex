# CCW1 lay return

The whiteboard found a concrete connection within the proposed geometry:
the rate at which received ticking fades is tied to the geometry's limiting
curvature. It can be expressed using ordinary source and receiver clocks,
without treating an arbitrary coordinate as distance. As reception time grows
without bound, the source emissions approach a finite timestamp on this
particular branch. The remaining source-time interval multiplied by redshift
approaches a finite positive value fixed by the limiting curvature.

It also proved that suitable signals can be constructed locally for a chosen
nearby source, under the explicit regular-extension condition. That removes
one local existence assumption. It does not guarantee signals from any
prescribed distant source or an available return echo.

A useful counterexample explains the next gap: different freely falling
receivers, prepared with increasingly extreme initial motion, can cancel the
redshift at their reception events. Every receiver still obeys the earlier
individual-clock theorem. A distance experiment therefore needs its initial
clock preparation and actual signal paths stated and checked.

The next recommendation is a bounded prepared-distance test: specify ordinary
free clocks and their initial separation, hold the emission event fixed, then
derive the received redshift and the range where signals exist. The written
NEXT_TEST_PROPOSAL includes review, repair, resources and its stopping point.
It is unexecuted. A positive result would connect this proposed geometry to a
distance experiment; it would not explain why UDT selects that geometry.

That physical-selection question remains open. The whiteboard did not derive
RG from UDT or adopt it. Existing consequences, explicit optional geometric
assumptions and unsupported physical connections stay distinct. Keeping the
conditional result without pursuing RG is an available no-change option.
No scale, X_max, new local clock law or empirical success is claimed. The
reviewed argument is maintained centrally in UDT_DEVELOPMENT R16CCW/R18.
