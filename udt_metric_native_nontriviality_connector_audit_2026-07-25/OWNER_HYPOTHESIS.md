# Owner bootstrap hypothesis frozen for this audit

Status: `WORKING`

The bootstrap may include energy, total proper density, spacetime curvature,
boundary/global geometry, and other native parameters. Matter and energy shape
the macro geometry; the resulting geometry supplies the conditions under which
matter can exist; and the complete universe tunes these sectors until both
directions agree.

The owner's word *optimize* means tuning an instrument or orchestra to mutual
compatibility. It does not require a scalar objective, minimum, or maximum.

The audit may test whether nontriviality, rank change, kernel formation,
holonomy descent, or another already-present metric property could implement
part of this tuning. It may not assume that any such property is matter or
invent a new coupling to make the loop close.
