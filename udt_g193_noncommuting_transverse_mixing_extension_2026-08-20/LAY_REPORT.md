# G193 lay report

G192 had one active sideways voice and one quiet sideways direction.  It was reasonable to worry
that its unusually clean behavior came from that simplification.

G193 turned on a second voice that couples the two directions, and allowed their relative
loudness to change continuously.  This means the preferred sideways axes can rotate as the pair
evolves; there is no single fixed rotation that separates the music into two independent notes.

The clean structure still survived.  The metric combined the two voices into an ordered matrix
flow whose area factor is built from a positive sum of squares.  That positivity prevents the
two-dimensional image from collapsing to zero area anywhere away from the starting point in this
family.  So G192's no-caustic result was not merely caused by having only one active direction.

What this does **not** say: every UDT metric is caustic-free.  We have tested a wider but still
symmetric family on one supplied observer pair.  A genuinely rotational/antisymmetric channel,
other coframe functions, other pair germs, and global topology remain outside the result.
