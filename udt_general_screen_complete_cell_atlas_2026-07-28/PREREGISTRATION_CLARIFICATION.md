Date: 2026-07-28

This clarification is committed before any general-screen response calculation.

`P:S3 -> GL(2,R)` is part of the explicitly stationary constructive control. Therefore “all first
jets” means all three independent spatial coframe derivatives `E1(P)`, `E2(P)`, and `E3(P)` at each
point. The time derivative is not silently omitted:

```text
E0(P)=0
```

is `pinned-by-CHOSE_EXISTENCE_CONTROL` through stationarity and is recorded as a dropped time-live
degree of freedom. No result from this atlas may be extended to nonstationary `P(t,x)`.

Falsification catch `F03` applies to every invertible matrix component and every registered
spatial first-jet direction. Dropping any of those fails. Treating the time derivative as explored
also fails the premise audit.
