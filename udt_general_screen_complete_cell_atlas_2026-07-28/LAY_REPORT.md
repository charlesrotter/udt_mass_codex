# Lay report

We opened the angular part of the metric all the way, within one honest complete test universe.
Instead of forcing its two angular directions to stretch equally, we allowed them to change area,
stretch differently in either direction, rotate their stretch axis, and rotate the measuring frame.

Three useful things happened.

First, the two missing “instruments” really are there. The angular metric has one overall-size control
and two independent shape controls. A fourth apparent control is only how we turn our measuring
rulers. Earlier diagonal formulas hid one shape direction when the screen happened to be round; a
regular coordinate system shows that both shape directions remain present.

Second, the cell’s native angular twist reacts to those shape controls. With a round screen it looks
like pure rotation. With a stretched screen the same underlying twist appears as a precise mixture
of rotation and two shears. That is a real interaction already contained in the geometry—not an
added mechanism—but it is not yet a force or matter law.

Third, making the screen completely general does not let us separate the clock/ruler pair from the
angular pair everywhere. The angular two-plane in this `S3` test cell is intrinsically twisted, like
a contact structure. A plane field with that twist cannot stay parallel everywhere. Two independent
calculations reached the same result.

So the result is constructive, but bounded:

- the “orchestra” has the full local angular vocabulary we suspected;
- the instruments genuinely interact through the complete coframe;
- one tempting simplification—a permanent split into two independent two-dimensional blocks—is
  impossible on this particular complete twisted cell;
- nothing yet tells us which screen shape the universe chooses or how it evolves.

This is forward movement because an ansatz gap has been removed. The old absence of shear was not a
law; it was caused by making both angular legs identical. We now have the honest geometric stage on
which a later selector or time-dependent equation would have to act.
