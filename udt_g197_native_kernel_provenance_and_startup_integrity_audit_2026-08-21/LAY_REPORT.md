# G197 lay report

The kernel's working heart survived the audit.

The clean picture is: give the UDT metric a specific observer-pair setup, let **all** clock, ruler,
angular, screen, and mixing pieces combine, and only then apply the provisional completed-pair
Reciprocity rule. The resulting response is calculated from the metric. Older fitted curves,
`X_max`, observational transfer rules, and the earlier scaffold are not secretly doing the work.

What is still provisional is clearly isolated. One rule—completed-pair Dual Reciprocity—has been
adopted as a working clarification, not derived or canonized. The observer setup and the particular
metric family being examined are supplied. Ordinary connection, curvature, and ray-spreading math
then tells us what that supplied geometry does.

The audit also caught an overstatement in some numerical checks: they independently solved the
equations we had written down, but did not independently rebuild those equations from the metric.
The exact mathematics remains; the evidence label is now honest.

So this is not the end of the theory, but it is a trustworthy platform for expansion. The next
small step is to give the same metric a second, opposite viewing direction. One ray tells us what
the metric does along that ray; two related rays may begin to reveal how the surrounding geometry
is tied together. We will map that before deriving anything new.
