# G197 terminology and provenance crosswalk

This file repairs current interpretation without rewriting fixed historical evidence.

## Reciprocal scalars

- `phi_control = (1/4) log[(-det h)/h00^2]` is the arbitrary-calibration reciprocal ratio used in
  G166--G175. Several historical reports called it `phi_pair`.
- `Phi = -log T = -(1/2) log(-h00)` is the completed physical-pair scalar after the G176
  `WORKING_FOUNDATIONAL_CLARIFICATION` imposes `T_s L_s = 1` on the fully assembled pullback.
- These coincide only when the completed-pair normalization is active. They must not be silently
  interchanged.

## What `metric-native` means here

In G197, `metric-native` means that after a metric, a typed observer-pair germ, orientation, and the
G176 working completed-pair premise are supplied, the stated result follows from the complete
pullback and standard differential geometry. It does **not** mean that:

- the founding two-channel algebra selected the metric history;
- the metric selected the supplied observer/event germ or branch;
- G176 was derived from the bare metric;
- one explored coframe family is the unique physical UDT family; or
- transfer, luminosity, observations, `X_max`, matter, or an action were derived.

## Standard evaluators

Pullback, Levi-Civita connection, geodesic and null-IVP evolution, curvature, Jacobi transport,
Gram determinants, and numerical integration are `STANDARD_GEOMETRIC_EVALUATOR` objects. They
evaluate supplied metric geometry. They are not additional UDT interactions.

## Coframe wording repair

The bounded G167 spherical slice has `B,Q` fixed by the supplied metric and `S=0`. Historical text
occasionally compressed this as `B,Q,S=0`, which can be misread as setting all three blocks to zero.
Current startup documents use the unambiguous wording. Fixed historical evidence is not rewritten.

## Evidence-independence repair

For G190--G192, the direct numerical Jacobi/IVP legs independently integrate or census the displayed
formula system without importing production modules or cached results. They do not independently
derive the connection/tidal operator from metric jets. Their numerical evidence is therefore
`formula-level implementation-distinct regression`, not an independent metric-to-Jacobi
reconstruction. The exact symbolic identities and bounded family theorems remain intact.

G193--G196 already state the distinction correctly: the metric-side Torch contractions are the
independently implemented leg, while the IVP comparison is formula-level.
