Reviewed conditional/empirical return, VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEATS and UNPROMOTED.
The independent review and its precision repairs are recorded in
[REVIEWED_RESULT.md](REVIEWED_RESULT.md) and [CLOSEOUT.md](CLOSEOUT.md).
This campaign preserves the founding
interpretation: positional dilation is spacetime geometry underlying observed
c_E, causal timing and received clock comparisons. Ordinary proper clocks and
the motion/gravitational effects remain part of that one geometry.

We now have concrete curves to work against. Six forms were fitted to 1,371
processed supernova measurements with their full covariance. A compact curve
with two shape coefficients describes them about as well as the conventional
comparison model. Keeping those coefficients unchanged also gives reasonable
agreement with five galaxy-clustering ratios, under a stated geometric and
measurement interpretation. This is useful empirical guidance; it does not
establish that UDT predicts the curve or outperforms standard cosmology.

The mathematics also advanced. We derived an exact construction that turns a
suitable curve into a compatible spacetime in a restricted homogeneous class,
using one metric for clocks and beam spreading. We did not insert Einstein's
field equations. But this construction still takes the curve as input and uses
an expanding observer congruence. It demonstrates compatibility, not the
intended native positional explanation without requiring Hubble expansion.

The extreme-distance check explains why that distinction matters. Two curves
fit the observed range almost equally well but imply very different distant
continuations. One produces a curvature singularity if extended unchanged in
the flat comparison geometry; the other supplies a mathematically compatible
unreachable past limit in an expanding geometry.
Neither tail is observationally established or selected by UDT. The first
failure is outside the data and is not a prediction that redshift reverses.
Your requirement of an unreachable asymptotic dilation limit remains in place.

metric/kernel relation restricts the change of received clock rate along a
light path together with the spreading of that same beam? In the restricted
construction, this becomes one still-supplied function. Finding that relation
does not require selecting a unique universe or explaining why the founding
postulate is true. It does require more than substituting the observed curve
into a free metric function.

My recommendation for the next discussion is to focus on that selector and
its existing premise support. Use the empirical curves as constraints and
reject any proposed derivation that merely hides them as inputs. If an extra
physical relation is needed, expose it as a proposed premise for Charles;
do not treat it as an established consequence. No successor is started here.

Quasar variability supplied measurement-method guidance, not a new distance
fit. Terrestrial/solar precision tests and an absolute distance calibration
