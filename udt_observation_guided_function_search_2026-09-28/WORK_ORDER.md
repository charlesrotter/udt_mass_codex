## Fixed physical direction and scientific scope

  signal timing. Local proper clocks remain ordinary. Negligible local extra
  contrast does not remove this geometry.
- Received ticks determine the clock comparison; slower received ticks mean
  redshift. Positional, motion and gravitational effects belong to one geometry.
  No independently multiplicative positional correction is assumed.
- No physically preferred observer or cosmic center. Explaining cosmological
  redshift without requiring Hubble expansion is the aim; retaining Doppler and
  changing geometry is allowed. A receding-observer or expansion-only control
  does not by itself establish the intended native positional explanation.

  demonstrate. Existing X_max value, profile and realization remain WORKING/OPEN;

- GR remains a comparison/filter, not an imported response equation. Suggested

  No source law, matter action, expansion law or new physical coupling is adopted.
