# G258 lay report

We now know more than just “redshift comes from phi.” At each of twelve frozen SNe checkpoints,
redshift tells us exactly how the reciprocal clock and ruler channels are scaled. The already
derived spherical area rule, together with the temporary light-transfer assumption used to process
the SNe state, tells us the relative sphere size at that checkpoint.

So those twelve points of the UDT metric are no longer free guesses. They are reconstructed up to
one overall ruler scale, with no new UDT shape coefficient and no continuous fitted curve.

The last checkpoint appears slightly smaller than the preceding one, but the uncertainty is more
than three times larger than that change. It is not evidence that distance really turns around.

What remains is the connective tissue: the continuous law between checkpoints, its absolute scale,
native light transfer, and the evolving nonspherical universe. GR anchors the quiet end; these SNe
points conditionally anchor part of the large-distance direction. The next law must join them.
