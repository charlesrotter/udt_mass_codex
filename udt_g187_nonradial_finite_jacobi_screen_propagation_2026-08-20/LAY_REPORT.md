# G187 lay report

The local observation screen found in G186 now propagates into a complete finite two-direction
response for one supplied nonradial line of sight.

The simple picture is this: once the metric and viewing direction are supplied, the metric itself
tells the two perpendicular screen directions how to spread. One direction bends within the ray's
orbital plane; the other tips out of that plane. They generally spread by different amounts. That
difference is a real angular shape distortion produced by the metric—no fitted angular knob was
added and nothing was bolted onto the reciprocal readout afterward.

This is a substantial bridge, but not yet a brightness law or a prediction of which rays Nature
uses. It says how one declared nonradial beam geometry propagates. The next scientific expansion is
to test this same construction beyond the static-spherical arena, after fresh adversarial review.
