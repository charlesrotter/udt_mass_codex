# G211 ponder — is lapse another instrument?

Date: 2026-08-22

The simplest possibility is that we were about to count the same instrument twice.

With a supplied clock direction and three-dimensional spatial block, diagonal rescaling has only two
scalar freedoms: scale the clock and scale the spatial volume. Those may be played in different
bases. One basis says “lapse plus spatial volume.” Another says “common scale plus clock-versus-space
contrast.” If the change of basis has full rank, a lapse-only deformation is a chord made from the
G206 and G210 notes, not a new note.

This is testable without dynamics. Four-volume hears one linear combination. Causal-cone width
hears another. Null affine parameter and the completed pair can then reveal how the two combinations
interlock. The calculation must not promote either scalar function into a physical history.
