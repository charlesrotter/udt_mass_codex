# Preregistration source amendment 01

This amendment is made after the primary universes were frozen and before any
completion-by-readout output was generated.

The original source scope includes the controlling reports, completion
registry, and motif/completion table. Mechanical source inspection showed that
five already-frozen per-completion tables are required to support exact rows
for holonomy, cap data, monodromy, and systole transport. They are added in
`SOURCE_MANIFEST_AMENDMENT_01.tsv` without modifying the original
preregistration or source manifest.

The candidate, completion, gate, relation, conclusion, and stop universes are
unchanged. These sources may support classifications but cannot add candidates
or authorize selection.
