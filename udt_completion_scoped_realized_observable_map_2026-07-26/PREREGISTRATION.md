## Whole question

For each of the twelve registered finite-cell completion classes, which
metric-native geometric readout families are:

1. mathematically defined as a type;
2. evaluable only after a complete metric branch and domain data are supplied;
3. globally descended or obstructed by boundary, cap, gluing, monodromy,
   stratification, or nonintegrability;
4. differentiable, stratified/set-valued, discrete, or unavailable; and
5. linked to other readouts by a genuine identity or compatibility condition?

The audit asks whether these linkages supply any non-identity physical closure
relation. It does not ask which completion resembles the desired universe.

## Frozen universes

- `COMPLETION_UNIVERSE.tsv`: all twelve registered completion classes exactly
  once.
- `READOUT_UNIVERSE.tsv`: seventeen geometric readout families, typed before
  completion-by-completion adjudication.
- `MAP_GATE_UNIVERSE.tsv`: ten independent definition, witness, global,
  variation, and authority gates.
- `RELATION_UNIVERSE.tsv`: ten candidate linkage families, including explicit
  identity and source-gap controls.

Generated audit records cannot alter these universes or become evidence for
their own selection.

## Required distinctions

Keep separate:

- a type schema from an actually evaluable map;
- local tensors from global invariants;
- open-path transport from loop holonomy;
- continuous torus holonomy from discrete lattice monodromy;
- squared character norm, character length, systole value, and argmin set;
- continuous moduli from discrete topology;
- fixed-domain variation from moving-boundary/gluing/modulus variation;
- a geometric identity from a physical bootstrap closure law; and
- a redundant invariant atlas from a selected physical state vector.

No completion class is presumed to contain a complete on-shell metric witness.
Where only parametric topology/gluing data are registered, the return must say
`TYPE_OR_DISCRETE_SCHEMA_ONLY`, not `REALIZED_OBSERVABLE`.

globally conditional, stratified/set-valued, discrete, or unavailable. A
family of conditional component maps is not a complete physical `R[X]`.

## Relation test

A linkage can be classified as:

- `GEOMETRIC_IDENTITY`;
- `COMPLETION_COMPATIBILITY`;
- `DISCRETE_CLASSIFICATION_RULE`;
- `CONDITIONAL_ON_COMPLETE_BRANCH`;
- `SOURCE_GAP`;
- `NOT_DERIVED`; or
- `PHYSICAL_CLOSURE_RELATION` only if it supplies a non-identity constraint
  among physical readouts on one coherent complete domain with a derived
  normalization and off-shell response.

Bianchi, Cartan, gauge covariance, topology definitions, cap determinants,
monodromy definitions, and coordinate identities cannot be renamed physical
closure.

## Maximum conclusion and stops

Maximum positive: a completion-scoped conditional redundant `R_geom` atlas
with exact domains, obstruction types, and stratified derivative rules, plus a
derived non-identity relation if one genuinely survives every gate.

Maximum negative: the frozen completion/readout universe supplies only local
objects, conditional component maps, identities, compatibility rules, and
discrete classifications; no physical closure relation is derived.

Stop before selecting a completion, state vector, topology, boundary
functional, action, carrier, observer protocol, physical representative,
