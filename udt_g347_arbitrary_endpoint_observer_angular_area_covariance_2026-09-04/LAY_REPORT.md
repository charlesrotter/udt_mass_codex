# G347 lay report

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: externally accepted, bounded and conditional

We kept the spacetime and the same lightlike route fixed, then allowed the observer at either end
to move in any direction at any ordinary speed below the speed associated with the metric's light
cone.

The result is coherent. The geometric spreading carried along the route does not change merely
because an endpoint observer moves. What changes is that observer's clock reading and the size of
the tiny patch of sky assigned to the route. Those two changes have exactly matching factors.

Consequently, the forward and reverse area numbers are not the same for every observer. They
change in a precisely controlled way. But the rules discovered in G346 survive unchanged in form:
the forward/reverse ratio is still the square of the two observers' measured frequency ratio, and
their geometric mean is still the inverse of the G345 quantity recalculated for those observers.
The three-segment joining rule also survives once the middle observer's clock factor is included.

This matters because the normal observers used earlier were not secretly a preferred frame. They
were a convenient reference. The construction now works for every finite timelike observer in the
bounded spacetime.
A fresh external reviewer independently rebuilt the key geometry and accepted this conclusion
without requiring a scientific repair.

It does **not** yet say which observers or routes are physically populated. It does not turn the
infinitesimal geometric area into brightness, luminosity, a theory of light, or an observational
distance. It does not set a scale or `X_max`, and it does not select a universe. The metric, kernel,
angular cancellation, and provisional field equation were not changed.
