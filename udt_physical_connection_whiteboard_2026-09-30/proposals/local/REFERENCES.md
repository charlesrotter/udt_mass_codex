# External method references

Browsed 2026-09-30. These supply conventional geometric comparison methods,
not UDT physical premises. No large excerpts or external file copies saved.

- Dirk Puetzfeld, Yuri N. Obukhov, Claus Lämmerzahl, *Gravitational clock compass
  in General Relativity* (2018), [arXiv:1805.10673](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.10673),
  journal [Phys. Rev. D 98, 024032](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.98.024032).
  Read abstract and primary search excerpt: suitably prepared clocks and reference
  frames permit curvature determination. This is not a claim that uncalibrated
  received ratios alone identify the Weyl tensor or that reconstruction supplies
  a metric field equation.
- V. Pravda, A. Pravdová, A. Coley, R. Milson, *All spacetimes with vanishing
  curvature invariants*, [arXiv:gr-qc/0209024](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0209024).
  Read abstract; used only as primary attribution of nonflat vanishing-invariant
  Lorentzian geometries. The proposal does not reproduce its classification.
- Matthias Blau, *Plane Waves and Penrose Limits*,
  [author lecture notes, §2.6](https://blau.itp.unibe.ch/lecturesPP.pdf),
  primary-source indexed excerpt explicitly explains vanishing plane-wave scalar
  curvature invariants and nonzero trace-free tidal matrices. Used as a cross-check
  for the stated vacuum plane-wave degeneracy, not a new UDT solution admission.
