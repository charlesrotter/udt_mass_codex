## L1: useful observable, unsupported physical-response identification

**Exact defect/open join.** The proposal has not explained why spacetime should
be stationary under the contrast cost of a particular population of prepared
clock comparisons. Raw `log Z` includes ordinary Doppler effects; two ensembles
with different rapidity weights can assign different responses to the same
metric. A covariant preparation rule would remove coordinate ambiguity, but
covariance alone does not remove this physical population/weight dependence.
Calling the ensemble “all observers” would not fix the absence of a finite
uniform invariant measure on the full timelike hyperboloid.

The quoted flat-coordinate witness is correct: with the fixed coordinate
receiver velocity, proper rapidity changes under the metric change. Its nonzero
derivative therefore compares different physical queries. Holding physical
rapidity fixed removes that particular derivative. Neither result tests
stationarity under every compactly supported metric perturbation of a fully
declared physical protocol. In particular, the first result is not a gauge
failure and the second is not an SR-recovery certificate.

Integrating enough ray distributions can address FCV1's support obstruction,
but a smooth response at an event can still depend on distant parts of the
queries, their populations and their histories. Smooth volume representability
and finite-metric-jet determination are separate gates. Finiteness of the
`D²` integral and permission to differentiate its metric-dependent measure also
need hypotheses; a normalized measure alone does not bound an unbounded `D`.

**Strongest survivor.** Once query preparation and measure are fixed, the
functional is a concrete operational susceptibility. It can compare how a
physical ensemble responds to metric changes without introducing a fitted
distance-redshift curve. DDR's existing annihilator result can be applied to
a sufficiently regular tensor once it exists, but supplies no reason to
identify this particular susceptibility with the physical response. A global
functional extremum is itself an additional law, not an escape from that issue.

**Smallest honest next test.** Freeze a covariant finite-duration preparation,
finite integrable weights, physically fixed initial rapidities and separations,
and their variation rules. Use a Minkowski ensemble with nonzero Doppler `D`
and an interior compact trace-free metric variation. Determine whether
`delta C` vanishes with all ray/clock/measure terms carried. A nonzero answer
rejects that displayed ensemble law as an exact flat/SR-compatible stationary
law; a zero answer only passes that test. The comoving `D=0` first variation
cannot discriminate. Only after this test compare globally different query
histories with identical local metric jets to test the proposed local-response
identity. This is a calculation on the actual new relationship, not another
general response classification.

## L2: the nonzero added term fails its universal smooth-response ambition

**Exact defect.** For `beta!=0`, the admitted flat family gives a nonzero
`|epsilon|` term. A flat open patch and compact perturbation with nonzero Weyl
curvature on positive volume turn the pointwise cusp into an action cusp;
the smooth Einstein contribution does not cancel its two-sided discrepancy.
`beta=0` remains the familiar Einstein comparison and must be retained
explicitly. It supplies no new tidal-magnitude mechanism.

There is a sharper algebraic concern at a nonzero null-Weyl point. If `I=J=0`
there, and a curvature variation has
`I(epsilon)=a epsilon+O(epsilon²)`,
`J(epsilon)=b epsilon+O(epsilon²)` with `(a,b)!=0`, then
`W(epsilon)=(a²+b²)^(1/4)|epsilon|^(1/2)+o(|epsilon|^(1/2))`.
Thus these directions can be more singular than the flat cusp. This statement
is conditional on the specified realizable curvature variation; no new global
plane-wave/action theorem is claimed here. It deserves inclusion if the
proposal is ever restricted to nonflat sectors, because excluding flat space
alone does not cure it.

**Strongest survivor.** On a supplied regular sector `I²+J²>0`, this is a
concrete dimensionally homogeneous tidal functional. Its smooth local formula
there can be studied as an explicitly restricted comparison. Failure to detect
null-wave tides and failure of the extension through zero invariants prevent
calling it a faithful universal tidal magnitude. They do not reject all Weyl
responses, all higher-order actions or the existing UDT premises.

**Smallest honest next test.** The existing flat cusp is already decisive
against the displayed universal smooth-response proposal for nonzero `beta`.
No solar-system solve is justified by that proposal. A narrower regular-sector
study would first have to identify why that restricted domain has physical
standing; inserting a smoothing scale or another invariant defines a different
candidate. Preserve the failed formula and the `beta=0` control.
