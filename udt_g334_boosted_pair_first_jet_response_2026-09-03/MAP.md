# G334 map — boosted pair first-jet response

Date: 2026-09-03

## Whole question

Starting only from G333's exact metric first response on a G332 initial slice, choose an arbitrary
unit spatial direction and an arbitrary finite local Lorentz boost in its normal--spatial plane.
Determine what the first normal jet of the complete pair metric reads in its clock, cross, and
ruler entries, and what its terminal scalar reads. At the same time, separate information fixed by
the boost value from information supplied by the first-order transport of the pair basis.

This is observing, not targeting. A large clock response, a preferred observer, a topology, a mass
effect, or agreement with an observation is not an acceptance criterion.

## Exact bounded regime

- every strict G332 datum already covered by G333, including both square-root branches;
- every initial unit spatial direction `v`, encoded by `mu=gamma(v,xi)^2` in `[0,1]`;
- every finite rapidity `z` in the local plane spanned by the future unit normal `n` and `v`;
- the first derivative along `n` at the initial slice only;
- the inherited G333 Gaussian/Lie-transported coordinate germ as one declared transport class;
- a general supplied first-order pair-basis transport retained symbolically as a separate class;
- no screen directions, null-limit claim, accelerated-observer evolution, or nonzero-time solve.

The vectors are orthonormal at the evaluation point. They are not silently re-orthonormalized on
neighboring slices. Any such re-orthonormalization is a distinct transport prescription and must be
shown explicitly.

## Metric-led route

Begin with G333's local metric tensor jet and apply a local Lorentz change of pair basis. Then add
the exact tensor identity for arbitrary vector extension. Do not modify the kernel, import a
response law, add a carrier or topology, or infer a physical population of boosts.

   congruence law, while general pair-germ transport contributes additional declared terms.

No candidate is privileged before execution.

classification for finite local boosts of the G333 plane. It cannot establish the derivative along
