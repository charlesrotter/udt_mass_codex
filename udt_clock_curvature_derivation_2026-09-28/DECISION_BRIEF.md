# CRD1 — what the derivation achieved

**We derived explicit clock–curvature equations, but did not close the native
UDT field equation.** Fresh independent review verified the conditional
mathematics with stated limits. Positional dilation remains the founding
interpretation; no new physical premise was adopted.

The main equation links changes in the clock comparisons in both directions to
curvature. It keeps the ruler calibration and observer motion inside the same
geometry. We also derived its full four-dimensional counterpart. These equations
are consistency relations: every supplied geometry in their stated scope obeys
them. They become predictive dynamics only when an actual response law restricts
the geometry; computing both sides from a chosen metric does not select that law.

One attempted simplification has a concrete problem. When only one dilation
variable is retained in the stated restricted metric, independently prescribing
curvature gives an elliptic equation. It cannot simply be used as a standalone
causal evolution equation. It could still be a constraint within a larger system;
this finding does not rule out UDT or causal dynamics in the complete metric.

The exact unresolved step is now explicit. Reciprocity constrains a response
tensor, but this derivation does not establish which complete-metric response
that tensor represents. Substituting Ricci curvature at that point would recover
the existing conditional Einstein class by an additional identification. The
curvature of a chosen pair surface is also insufficient: it can include the
surface's own bending, even in flat spacetime.

This attempt supplies usable equations and eliminates one unjustified shortcut.
It does not solve the longstanding response-identification gap. Repeating these
identities will not close it; further constructive work must address that exact
identification with a new argument from the premises, or a clearly separated
proposed principle for Charles to consider. We have not proved that a new
physical postulate is necessary.
