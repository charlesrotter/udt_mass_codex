# CRD1 — initial lay return for review

We derived an explicit nonlinear relation connecting changes in received-clock
comparisons to curvature. It retains the ruler calibration, observer motion and
the way a pair surface sits in the full spacetime. These are parts of the same
geometry, rather than extra redshift mechanisms.

The derivation also tested a tempting simplification: keeping only one dilation
variable. In a precisely stated restricted metric, prescribing curvature then
gives an elliptic equation, not a standalone causal evolution equation. This
does not rule out UDT. It shows exactly why that scalar shortcut cannot yet do
the job of the complete spacetime response.

We have not derived the native UDT field equation. The present reciprocity
postulate constrains the shape of a response tensor, but the current premises
have not identified which complete-metric response it is. Choosing Ricci at that
step would recover the existing conditional Einstein class by assumption.

The useful result is a concrete differential equation and a tested obstruction
to one attempted closure. It is not a discovery of new geometry or proof that
UDT necessarily needs another physical postulate. Positional dilation remains
the founding interpretation. Exact initial argument: INITIAL_DERIVATION.md;
fresh review and any qualifications must control the final return.
