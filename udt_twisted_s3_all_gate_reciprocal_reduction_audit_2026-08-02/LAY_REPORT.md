# Lay report

We have now found one complete mathematical UDT geometry in which the clock-and-ruler structure is
not painted onto the metric afterward.

Think of a building with many possible directions. Previously, we knew the blueprint allowed a
special clock hallway and that twisting around it could point toward a ruler hallway—but we had not
proved the building itself identified which clock hallway was the real one.

For this explicit complete-cell geometry, three independent curvature fingerprints label the three
spatial directions. Any continuous symmetry trying to move sideways would change at least one
fingerprint, so it cannot be a symmetry. The only continuous symmetry left is translation along the
time hallway. That makes the clock direction intrinsic. Its geometric twist then points everywhere
along one nonvanishing spatial ruler direction. Together they form the reciprocal clock/ruler plane
throughout the whole finite cell.

This is a real structural advance, but it is an existence result—not yet a universe. We chose one
smooth profile to ask whether such a complete geometry is possible. UDT has not selected that
profile, its transverse weighting, its stationary state, or its equations of motion. We have found
a sound piece that the metric can build, not proved that the completed theory must build it. We also
have not tested whether every freely falling trajectory can be extended forever; “complete” here
means the geometry is smooth and nondegenerate over the entire registered cell.
