# G260 lay report

The quiet GR result does not turn off UDT's angular instruments.

Imagine testing only the clock-and-radial-distance face of the metric. In two dimensions, the
Einstein test is automatically zero no matter what distance profile is supplied. That stripped
calculation cannot tell one universe from another. The curvature of the surrounding sphere is what
makes the four-dimensional vacuum test meaningful.

With the complete metric restored, the two angular responses remain present. On the GR-like vacuum
family they have equal and opposite strength. The result is quiet because they balance, not because
they were discarded.

There is also a useful caution. Equal-and-opposite angular response permits a slightly larger
family containing both a `1/r` term and an `r^2` term. Requiring vacuum removes the `r^2` term. So
angular cancellation is an important piece of the score, but it is not by itself the complete UDT
law.
