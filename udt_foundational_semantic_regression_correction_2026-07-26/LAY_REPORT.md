# Lay report

Your concern was justified. The repository had enough correct mathematics to reproduce a wrong
conceptual framing: a newer audit picked up an old exploratory branch and forgot that a later audit
had already decided what `phi` means at the foundation.

The fix is not “trust the AI more carefully.” The repository now has a dashboard of the dangerous
terms. For each one it says what is actually known, what is merely chosen or conditional, what is
still open, what mistake is forbidden, and which evidence controls the wording. Every startup path
points to that dashboard, and the verifier deliberately tries the wrong interpretations to make
sure they fail.

The corrected picture is simple: `phi` is the known reciprocal depth between the founding clock and
ruler channels. We still have to learn how that known rule fills out the whole four-dimensional
metric. The general metric has a six-function mathematical arena, but that does not tell us UDT has
six physical fields. Adding a separate test scalar makes seven only in an intentionally enlarged
experiment. Strong scale neutrality is not active physics unless you explicitly restore it.

The same guard now covers less familiar terms too: carrier, actions, bootstrap, Maxwell-like
geometry, `X_max`, Hopfion stability, source, boundary charge, and mass. This does not settle those
questions. It makes their current uncertainty explicit and much harder to accidentally erase.
