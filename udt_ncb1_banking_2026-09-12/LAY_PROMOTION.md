# What G415 adds to the accepted record

Charles authorized promotion of the reviewed NCB1 result. BANKING_RECORD.md
owns its exact conditional scope; CLOSEOUT.md and the review/publication
receipts own completion of the banking gates.

We have exact clock and narrow-beam calculations inside a supplied evolving
geometry. They show that a disturbance can fade in one measure while its
accumulated effects on clocks and beam area keep growing. The two beam
widths can also approach the same ratio as a background comparison without
the whole geometry's accumulated effects disappearing.

The result separates two experiments that can sound similar. Following a
signal over an increasingly long journey produces one behavior. Repeating
a fixed coordinate-length journey at later times produces a bounded,
usually oscillating clock comparison. Fixed coordinate length here does
not mean an instrument held at fixed physical size.

Promotion makes this complete reviewed conditional result available as a
named dependency, G415, in later work. It preserves the specified geometry,
observers, directions and comparisons. It does not establish that these
rays are physical light or supply an observational redshift law. It also
does not select this geometry as the unique or native UDT history.

The roadmap now retains this gain explicitly. A possible next discussion is
how far the result extends beyond these two axial directions, with a small
question and clear limits. No successor study is authorized by this promotion.
