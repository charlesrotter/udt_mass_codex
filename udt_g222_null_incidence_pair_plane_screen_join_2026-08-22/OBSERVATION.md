# G222 observation

G221 owns the clock tangent at the target boundary but deliberately stops before constructing the
second pair-plane tangent. G188 owns the null generator and its quotient-screen Jacobi transport but
does not join them to the completed reciprocal pair pullback.

For one smooth null family these are not separate mechanisms. They are different derivatives of the
same map `F(y,lambda)`:

```text
clock/incidence tangent J = partial_y F
null/ruler tangent       K = partial_lambda F
```

The missing calculation is therefore a compatibility calculation on one supplied family, not a
search for another scalar law.
