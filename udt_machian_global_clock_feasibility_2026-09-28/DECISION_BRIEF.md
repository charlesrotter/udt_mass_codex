# MGC1 — lay return

**The Machian idea gives us a useful route toward scale, but has not yet given
us UDT's clock-dilation law.** We tested what it could accomplish without adding
a matter coupling or importing GR's field equations as UDT premises.

Imagine two questions: how large is the geometry, and how do clocks compare
across it? A justified relationship between mass and size could answer the first
using an independently known mass and the accepted c_E/G_obs inputs. Density
could play a similar role. We do not have to explain the origin of matter before
testing such a relationship.

The catch is whether the mass supplies genuinely independent information. If we
calculate both the mass and size from the same freely rescalable geometry, their
ratio can stay fixed while the whole construction grows. In that case we have
described a relationship without determining an absolute size. Earlier UDT work
already established the general need for a matching independent scale anchor;
this study applies it to your Machian lead.

We also checked a known GR comparison universe with a definite mass–size relation
and no preferred spatial center. Its stationary matter-rest clocks show no
redshift at all, even if its mass fixes its size. This is an external comparison,
not a proposed UDT universe or a refutation of positional dilation. It shows why
mass and size alone are not yet an explanation of the clock curve. The actual
geometry relating observers and signal paths matters.

What remains is specific: **derive how physical matter or global geometric
conditions constrain the geometry that determines received clock ticks.** Then
we can calculate whether that clock family has the intended small nearby effect
and distant asymptotic climb. A finite radius is not automatically X_max, and
the existing directional clock checks still apply. No new premise has been
shown necessary; this bounded study has not exhausted existing UDT implications.

Your central interpretation stays in place: ordinary local clocks, positional
dilation expressed between frames, slowing received ticks automatically giving
redshift, and c_E as the observed calibration of that geometry. This study
records the Machian idea as a research direction, not an adopted mass law.

The calculations received fresh separate-context review, including a different
