## What the Machian lead contributes

With independently meaningful physical mass M and geometric extent L, the ratio

    Q = G_obs M/(c_E^2 L)

is dimensionless. IF a physical argument separately establishes Q=q_*>0, an
independent M gives L=G_obs M/(q_* c_E^2). For a specified slice/region with
uniform mass density rho and volume C_V L^3, an independent rho gives
L=c_E sqrt(q_*/(C_V G_obs rho)). These are conditional attachments, not selected
values of q_*, M, rho, L or X_max. C_V and the type of mass belong to the model;
there is no universal spherical volume factor or assumed cosmic slice.

This lets accepted c_E/G_obs participate without requiring their derivation or
a microscopic matter theory first. A measured mass could be legitimate input
once its interface and the proposed relation are justified. Merely defining M
from the same relation, or calling a geometric readout physical mass, supplies
no independent constraint. G351's conserved label measure is not physical mass.

G275/G276 already own the general result that a matching independent datum of
nonzero scaling weight fixes one supplied homothety. MGC1 applies that existing
distinction to Machian proposals; it does not rediscover it as a new theorem.

## Scale selection is distinct from clock comparison

For supplied g_lambda=lambda^2 g with constant lambda>0, matched events, paths
and ordinary proper-clock normalization, lengths and durations scale by lambda.
An arrival map changes as f_lambda(s)=lambda f(s/lambda), so its derivative at
matched events, the received/emitted tick-interval ratio Z, is unchanged.
This is a conditional comparison between geometries in fixed units, not a
passive unit change or an adopted UDT scaling symmetry. It does not assert
invariance of G176 depth at fixed unrenormalized embeddings.

For an optional mass functional with M_lambda=lambda^w M,
Q_lambda=lambda^(w-1) Q. Thus weight1 geometric mass and extent can co-scale
without changing Q. Independently fixed mass or density give different weights
and can fix scale through a justified relation, but those variations must also
satisfy any proposed dynamics. A simple nonzero derivative of a scalar constraint
at a root isolates that root only locally in its specified one-parameter family.
It proves neither unique geometry nor a physical selection law.

Two explicit **external GR controls** sharpen the distinction:

- In a spatially flat expanding FLRW model, Friedmann-inferred total density
  and the mass assigned to the Hubble-radius ball give Q=1/2 identically for
  every H. This is no independent Machian confirmation or UDT law. That radius
  is not automatically a causal horizon, total cosmic extent or X_max.
- In the Einstein-static dust+Lambda comparison, the round S^3 radius a obeys
  rho=c_E^2/(4 pi G_obs a^2), Lambda=1/a^2. Total proper dust rest mass, excluding
  vacuum energy, gives M=pi c_E^2 a/(2 G_obs) and Q=pi/2. Even fixing that M and
  hence a leaves Z=1 between stationary dust-rest clocks on regular ray branches.
  The shortest-branch delay approaches the finite value pi a/c_E at the antipode.
  Finite diameter or branch degeneracy is not divergent clock slowing. The
  model has no preferred spatial center but does have a dust rest congruence.

Across that static comparison family Lambda and density co-vary with a; a
separately fixed Lambda would fix a. No on-shell rescaling at fixed inputs is
claimed. Moving observers can have Doppler shifts in the same spacetime.

No stability or empirical viability claim is made.
