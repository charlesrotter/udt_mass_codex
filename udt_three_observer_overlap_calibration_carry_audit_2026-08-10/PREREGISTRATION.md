## Whole question

Given supplied compatible candidate pair relations `A-B`, `B-C`, and `A-C` on one solved complete
Lorentzian metric, determine whether their complete overlap maps and Jacobians already provide an
associative transition for the carried reciprocal calibration state, or instead define a precise
triple-overlap or loop obstruction.

The audit asks what the supplied relation family does. It does not ask which family resembles a
desired universe and does not filter branches by merit.

## Typed objects to keep separate

1. each pair relation or branch-labelled pair surface;
2. its complete pullback metric and terminal reciprocal readout;
3. a local calibration frame/state at each end;
4. a coordinate/frame transition on a genuine overlap;
5. the intermediate `B` identification used by both adjacent relations;
6. the triangle product and loop product;
7. a scalar reciprocal depth only where its character is actually typed.

An ordered observer pair fixes a hom-set, not one relation. A full declared query and regular branch
are supplied premises, not founding deductions.

## Exact tests frozen before outcomes

1. **True-atlas chain:** for genuine transitions, verify `J_BC J_AB = J_AC` with consistent typing.
2. **Triangle obstruction:** define the typed product comparing the composite with the direct map;
   identity means descent on that overlap, nonidentity means obstruction.
3. **Frame covariance:** independently change endpoint/intermediate frames and verify that identity
   versus nonidentity is unchanged; record conjugation of the obstruction.
4. **Common scale:** coherent endpoint common-scale changes must cancel around a triangle.
5. **Coherent reciprocal gauge:** the same reciprocal calibration state used on both incident edges
   at `B` must cancel as a change of presentation.
6. **Mismatched middle reset:** distinct incoming/outgoing `B` states must survive as an explicit
   middle mismatch unless an overlap transition identifies them.
7. **Full orchestra:** off-diagonal/mixing entries must survive the matrix product; no diagonal
   quotient may substitute for the complete overlap test.
8. **Scalar projection:** test whether terminal pair depths add only under the declared reduction;
   do not promote a full-matrix invariant to a general scalar cocycle.
9. **Loop:** classify the ordered transition product around a supplied closed chain.
10. **Branch and degeneracy:** report branch-labelled, undefined, divergent, or partial-overlap
    outcomes rather than filtering them.
11. **Reciprocity:** test its action on the obstruction under reversal/exchange; do not assume it
    forces identity.
12. **Counterfamilies:** construct both a consistent nontrivial frame-change family with zero
    obstruction and an independently rebuilt admissible family with nonzero obstruction.

## Falsification and certification contract

- `AUTOMATIC_ASSOCIATIVE_CARRY` is falsified by one admitted supplied relation family whose typed
  triangle obstruction is nonidentity after consistent frame accounting.
- `GENERIC_OBSTRUCTION` is falsified as a universal statement by one genuine common-atlas family
  whose full transition matrices obey the chain rule, including nonzero mixing.
- A claimed middle reset is not physical evidence if it is only a coherent endpoint frame change
  appearing on both adjacent edges and cancelling exactly.
- A claimed gauge removal fails if the incoming and outgoing `B` calibrations are different objects
  and no supplied transition identifies them.
- Any uniqueness or no-go conclusion is bounded to the frozen cases and declared arrow types.

Certification requires: exact controller, independent implementation, exercised catch-proofs,
frozen source identities, premise audit, startup/premise gates, six frozen package manifests,
current paths/frontier links, and the documented test baseline.
