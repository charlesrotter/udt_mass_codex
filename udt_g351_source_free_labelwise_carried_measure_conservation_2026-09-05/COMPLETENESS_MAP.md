# G351 completeness map

## Covered

- The full abstract positive `(R,A)` domain declared by G350.
- Every real observer weight symbolically; rational witnesses span positive, zero, and negative
  values in two independent implementations.
- Regular screen-rank-two cuts, all cut orderings, identity, reversal, and three-cut sewing.
- Finite endpoint observer-frequency changes.
- Full finite nonnegative additive measures on arbitrary supplied populated label sets, while the
  density theorem is restricted to their nonzero absolutely continuous regular component.
- Zero source, nonzero supplied source, disjoint-label additivity, and many-to-one pushforward.
- Weak measure conservation through caustic rank loss, while explicitly allowing singular density.
- Exact atomic counterexamples showing that an arbitrary finite measure need not possess an
  ordinary area density or exponent.

## Not covered and not silently excluded

- Selection of `p`, initial measure, source distribution, or label population.
- Signed, complex, phase-carrying, interfering, interacting, nonlocal, or field-valued content.
- Source/sink balance, emission, absorption, scattering, or detector response.
- Physical aggregation across distinct path families or unresolved images.
- A finite pointwise inverse-area scalar at a caustic or simultaneous-zero area ratio.
- An ordinary density exponent for singular carried-measure components.
- A native light, photon, energy, brightness, flux, luminosity, or distance theory.
- Metric/history/occupancy/stability, matter/mass, absolute scale, `X_max`, or canon.

G351 fully classifies only the nonzero absolutely continuous regular-density consequence of the
owner-adopted provisional positive-measure conservation premise inside G350's bounded character
class. It preserves the full finite measure outside that density stratum and does not claim
completeness over all possible carried-content theories.
