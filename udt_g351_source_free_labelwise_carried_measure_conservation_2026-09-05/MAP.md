# G351 map — source-free labelwise carried-measure conservation

Date: 2026-09-05

## Question

After G350 proved that the metric frequency and sheet-area ratios admit the nonunique local
multiplicative family

```text
T_(p,q)=R^p A^q,
```

what follows if Charles provisionally adopts the narrow statement that a source-free labelled null
family carries an additive measure without creating or destroying its total amount?

This is a premise-conditioned derivation. The conservation statement is not claimed to follow from
the metric, reciprocal kernel, Universal Reciprocity/DDR, G312, or G350.

## Frame

- **Whole frame:** one supplied smooth time-oriented four-dimensional Lorentzian metric, one
  supplied regular future-null labelled family, arbitrary ordered cuts, and arbitrary finite
  future-timelike endpoint observers.
- **Metric-led or template-led:** metric-led geometry plus one owner-adopted provisional carried-
  content premise. No light or particle template is imported.
- **Regular slice:** local density formulas use screen-rank-two cuts with positive Jacobian. The
  measure statement itself is retained on label space through rank loss.
- **Question type:** characterize consequences and remaining freedom; do not target brightness,
  luminosity, an expected exponent, or observational agreement.

## Alternatives retained

1. **No carried layer:** G350 remains the final boundary and no transfer is physical.
2. **Positive additive label measure (adopted provisionally):** classical measure-like carry;
   regular density follows by division by metric sheet area.
3. **Signed or complex amplitude:** could support cancellation/interference but needs phase and
   composition premises; omitted and open.
4. **Local balance with sources/sinks:** needs a source law; omitted and open.
5. **Nonlocal or interacting carry:** outside G350 and G351's bounded class.

## Anticipated consequence and ceiling

If conservation is represented by cut-independent label measure `mu`, then on every positive-
Jacobian cut its density is `dmu/dArea`. Therefore density changes as `A^-1`. An observer-typed
component of weight `p` changes as `R^p A^-1`.

At most G351 may derive `q=-1` conditional on the adopted premise, establish identity/reversal/
sewing and a caustic-safe weak measure statement, and prove that `p`, source content, population,
emission/detection, physical cross-label combination, history, matter, scale, `X_max`, and canon
remain open.
