# Suggested cold adversarial review of the P4 arc

Date: 2026-08-01

Status: proposed quality checkpoint only; not launched, not a scientific result, and not
authorization for A3, T4, adoption, canonization, or further derivation.

## Why

The P4 arc accumulated rapidly and its ordinary blind verifiers mostly ran inside the same active
development window. Those checks caught real errors, but a fresh model in a clean worktree can
better test whether shared framing, vocabulary, or code lineage allowed a common mistake to pass
through several packages.

## When

Run after all three A3 stages (`alpha` torus, `beta` full `S3`, `gamma` fine detail) are banked and
their normal blind-verifier, amendment, and same-verifier closure is complete. Preferably run before
T4 dynamics or any response-law, carrier, source, θ, mass, or physical-branch adoption.

## Isolation

- fresh clean worktree and review branch from the exact post-A3 `grok` tip;
- no conversational summary of the desired result;
- frozen list of load-bearing commits, claims, premise stamps, scripts, and raw outputs;
- reviewer changes confined to a new review package; existing evidence remains untouched;
- CPU/read-only recomputation unless a separately authorized bounded check genuinely requires more.

## Questions to attack

1. Does the P4 inverse-problem domain actually follow from the metric and registered premises, or
   does it silently choose a response alphabet, pairing, wall posture, census, or variation rule?
2. Do the static, time-live, and angular-live response spaces embed exactly as claimed, with no
   quantifier change from a formal family to a fixed realized metric?
3. Are the mass branches and stability results still valid under their complete premise stacks,
   and are any conditional definitions being narrated as physical mass?
4. Is the real-character versus compact-circle integer distinction a theorem of the admitted
   domain, or an artifact of the chosen topology/transition class?
5. Does A3 exhaust its stated torus, full-sphere, and fine-detail layers, including negative and
   mixed outcomes, without steering toward integers?
6. Are the coordinate/projected reading forks genuinely shape-neutral through A2, and where do
   they first become physically distinguishable?
7. Which P4 claims are independent, which share one load-bearing derivation or implementation, and
   which need a different-method recomputation?
8. Does the nuclear scoping material remain correctly separated: banked structural theorems,
   textbook/SEMF reimplementation, and conditional identifications?

## Required return

- mechanical agreement/disagreement table for every load-bearing P4 claim through A3;
- premise and quantifier audit, including all inherited forks and conditional definitions;
- independent recomputation of each load-bearing algebraic identity using a different method where
  practical;
- shared-code and circular-verification map;
- exact regrades: retained, narrowed, contradicted, or open;
- one smallest justified next step, or an explicit stop if the architecture needs repair first.

Maximum conclusion: a cold regrade of the accumulated P4 evidence. The review cannot adopt a law,
field, carrier, source, boundary, mass interpretation, or physical branch and cannot canonize.
