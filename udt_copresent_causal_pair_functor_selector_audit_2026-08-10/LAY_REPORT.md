# Lay report

The complete observer tape has two light-cone edges. The angular orchestra can tilt both edges
together; that tilt is `beta`. Their centered separation is controlled by `phi`. The inverse of
that centered opening is exactly the conditional `c_eff/c_E` reading already derived from the pair
metric.

So the three mathematical regimes really are one cone law:

- at `phi=0`, the centered reading is `c_E`;
- toward positive infinite depth, the pair reading tends to zero;
- toward negative infinite depth, it tends to infinity.

This is a real unification, but local causality does not yet choose the markings or transition rules
on a supplied tape. Once a tape is supplied, its metric automatically says which directions are
causal. There are still infinitely many smooth ways to relabel that tape, even after fixing the
ordinary `c_E` calibration and both extreme limits. Composition tells those maps how to join; it
does not choose one. This does not yet prove that several physically different tapes exist—the rule
constructing the physical ambient tape remains open.

The next potentially selective layer is global causal faithfulness: whether a proposed pair tape
captures the complete geometry's causal order without losing angular alternatives, developing
closed causal loops, or becoming path-inconsistent. That is a stronger and more useful question
than another local connection or scalar-reset search.
