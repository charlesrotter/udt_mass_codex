# MAP / PONDER — causality in a co-present pair geometry

## Lay picture

Co-presence says the complete history belongs to one geometry. It does not say every event can
communicate with every other event. The Lorentzian cone still records which comparisons can be
causally ordered.

The candidate missing joint is therefore not a new messenger or local stretching mechanism. It is
the possibility that only certain complete observer-pair tape systems preserve causal order,
Reciprocity, and consistent composition across the whole geometry.

## Non-leading map

The audit must allow all outcomes:

- the cone may determine the reciprocal reading and the physical pair family;
- it may determine the reading but leave an infinite causal-coordinate freedom;
- global chronology or asymptotic completion may reduce that freedom;
- causal preservation may be automatic after a family is supplied and therefore nonselecting;
- the desired causal functor may fail to exist on some strata.

## Three mathematical sectors, not preassigned physics

The sign and magnitude of `phi_pair` will be mapped as:

- finite neighborhood of `phi_pair=0`;
- `phi_pair -> +infinity`;
- `phi_pair -> -infinity`.

Calling these ordinary, cosmological, or microscopic is a later physical interpretation. The audit
tests cone opening, orientation, composition, and degeneracy only.

## Stop condition

If causal order plus current premises leaves arbitrary monotone null reparameterizations, conformal
factors, pair immersions, or branch/path choices, stop with their exact class. Do not add a new
condition merely to obtain uniqueness.
