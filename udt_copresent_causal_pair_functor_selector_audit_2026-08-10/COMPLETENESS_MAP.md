# Completeness map

## Whole frame

The whole object is a smooth time-oriented four-dimensional complete coframe plus an ordered query
that supplies a regular calibrated two-dimensional pair immersion. The audit follows the induced
Lorentzian metric through local cones, transitions, composition, asymptotes, and typed global
causality conditions.

## Covered

- arbitrary local `T`, `L`, and `beta` and all derivatives;
- complete-coframe modulation upstream of the induced pair metric;
- both null branches and both observer orientations;
- all smooth local time-orientation-preserving causal/conformal transitions in 1+1 dimensions;
- exact composition, inversion, and endpoint calibration bookkeeping;
- `phi=0`, `phi -> +infinity`, and `phi -> -infinity` limits;
- typed local/global and causal/signalling separations.

## Not covered

- selection of a global topology or physical complete solution;
- proof of global hyperbolicity for every complete branch;
- nonsmooth, null, rank-changing, or singular pair surfaces beyond boundary classification;
- a material principal symbol, source, coupling, response, or signalling theorem;
- an on-shell native evolution equation, action, boundary completion, or bootstrap fixed point;
- observational fitting or assignment of mathematical sectors to physical regimes.

## Solver posture

Exact symbolic and standard-library algebra only. No ODE/PDE or GPU work is justified unless the
causal classification first produces an owned evolution law, which is not assumed.
