# Lay report

The larger angular screen did not wreck the Hopf connection. It revealed a simpler conditional
route that had been obscured by concentrating on two specially aligned angular circles.

In the chosen closed `S3` geometry, UDT's stretched ruler is `exp(phi)` times a basic geometric
ruler. Since `phi` is already the founded amount of stretching, dividing that stretch back out
recovers the basic ruler exactly. That basic ruler is the standard twist running through the Hopf
fibration of `S3`. It remains the same even when the angular screen is squeezed or sheared in either
of its two independent ways.

So the conditional chain is now sturdier:

```text
founded ruler and founded dilation
    -> undilated geometric ruler
    -> Hopf circle on the chosen S3 geometry
    -> unit Hopf bundle.
```

But the words “chosen `S3` geometry” still matter. The metric has not shown that every physical UDT
universe must use this closed coframe. Nor does every possible angular screen respect the Hopf
circle well enough for its full metric to descend to the quotient sphere.

There is also an exact warning. A twisted contact structure is not automatically made of closed
circles; perfectly smooth examples have trajectories that wind forever without closing. The
founded-depth normalization is what recovers the standard closed Hopf circle in this particular
`S3` coframe.

The general toric route is even less unique. Smooth lens-space completions can have free circle
quotients with Hopf/Chern numbers `3, 4, 5, ...`, including an infinite exchange-symmetric odd
family. Thus mirror symmetry, smoothness, and screen shear do not select the unit case.

The net gain is real but conditional: we now know a unit Hopf bundle survives the complete screen
on the strongest existing `S3` branch. We still do not have a native reason selecting that branch,
the full screen's descent, a round carrier sphere, its deformation space, or its action.
