# G168 preregistration — ordered co-present pair-plane ownership

Date: 2026-08-18

## Whole question

Does one **local, calibrated, ordered co-present positional-comparison germ** already own the
rank-two clock/ruler plane needed by the primary UDT metric pullback, or must a path, complete pair
surface, ambient plane selector, or other independent mechanism be supplied?

The phrase above is deliberately stronger than two observer names. The test must determine the
smallest exact datum between those extremes.

## Bounded arena

- one event on observer A with a calibrated future timelike clock tangent;
- one regular nonzero co-present separation germ from A toward B;
- the declared primary static-spherical UDT metric;
- local tangent-space algebra only;
- both radial and nonradial separation directions;
- observer reversal, coincidence, coordinate/screen covariance, and relative-motion controls.

Excluded: a selected path, geodesic, signal trajectory, full pair immersion, global foliation,
`X_max`, fitting, source, action, bootstrap, mass, radiative transfer, protected packages, and a
general nonspherical or ambient-time-dependent metric law.

## Required exact tests

1. Given a timelike clock tangent `u` and a nonzero separation vector `s`, construct the unique
   clock-orthogonal separation
   `r=s+g(u,s)u/(-g(u,u))` and prove its uniqueness.
2. On the regular stratum `g(r,r)>0`, prove that `span(u,r)` is Lorentzian and its orthogonal
   complement is positive definite.
3. Decompose `u` and `r` in the primary spherical chart and recover the G167 `Y,Z` blocks without
   an independent screen or mixing choice.
4. Show explicitly how nonradial separation produces live angular `Z`, while a central radial pair
   has `Z=0`.
5. Prove invariance under angular-coordinate changes and positive rescaling of the raw separation
   germ.
6. Test reversal: direction orientation changes, while the unoriented local plane is unchanged only
   after both ordered germs are lawfully compared in one tangent space.
7. Test coincidence: `s=0` must be classified as a genuine rank-loss boundary, not silently given a
   direction.
8. Test relative motion: B's velocity need not lie in `span(u,r)`; do not promote the positional
   plane into a complete two-observer kinematic plane.
9. Give an exact counterfamily with the same observer worldline labels and metric but different
   event/separation germs, producing different planes or calibration states.

## Maximum conclusion

At most, G168 may prove that a **typed local ordered co-present pair germ** supplies the local
calibrated plane and therefore the G167 pullback inputs. It may not derive which events are paired
from two observer names, a global pair realization, a path, cross-query carry, a unique observer
network, a physical profile, `X_max`, dynamics, signalling, or a general complete metric.
