# G160 lay report

The changing orchestra now carries consistently from one observer to the next.

If observer A receives the comparison through B, or receives the same closed comparison directly
from C, the full changing pair metric agrees exactly. The reverse test is weaker: two different
carries can look identical when their difference is a Lorentz symmetry. A changing carry contributes a “motion of the
measuring frame” term, but that term cannot be separated from the metric change in an observer-free
way. Only their combined result is stable under changing notation.

One simple note survives every regular carry: the change in common area/volume scale. Reciprocal
clock-versus-ruler depth and shift have clean laws on the positive flag-preserving carry class. That
class is sufficient, not the only possible special case. If roles mix arbitrarily, the answer also
depends on the metric and transported clock direction.

So the kinematic transport machinery is no longer missing. The remaining open question is which
cross-observer carries and complete history Nature supplies—not how a supplied one evolves.
