## Independent argument

 nabla X=-(H_x/2)l du,
 nabla Y=-(H_y/2)l du.

1. The natural transverse screen span(X,Y) has A=0, F_screen=0, despite generally nonzero ambient curvature. More generally any supplied l-compatible screen has local orthonormal representatives X+a l,Y+b l (up to SO2 gauge), and A=0 in those frames for arbitrary smooth a,b. Therefore the natural/parallel-null-compatible reduction cannot reproduce a nonzero NR2 form; a change of frame inside it cannot repair that mismatch.

2. A different family of screen planes is span(e2,e3), with

For all real smooth a,b these vectors are orthonormal spacelike and independent; their orthogonal complement is Lorentzian. Nullness makes every derivative da,db contribution to g(e2,nabla e3) vanish. Direct connection calculation yields

This family is n-compatible, NOT the previous l-compatible one. Changing a,b changes the screen plane, not simply its SO2 gauge. The construction requires supplied n and functions a,b, not just a coordinate-free selected object of g.
