# G133 preregistration — fixed-`K` two-density and overlap descent

Date: 2026-08-16

## Whole question

G132 separated the common scale retained by a supplied complete pair metric from the common
multiplier excluded inside the fixed-`K` reciprocal transformation. This audit asks whether that
fixed pairing supplies a query-independent physical two-density that descends across overlapping
observer-pair descriptions, or only an internal invariant of each supplied reciprocal
representation.

The audit must distinguish four types:

1. the fixed symmetric channel pairing `K` on the abstract clock/ruler representation;
2. the determinant-line density preserved by the determinant-one reciprocal action;
3. the intrinsic Lorentzian area density of one supplied pair pullback `h=F* g`; and
4. any metric-derived object on spacetime capable of evaluating different timelike two-planes.

## Bounded regime

- real two-dimensional reciprocal channel spaces with fixed
  `K=[[0,1],[1,0]]`;
- positive reciprocal matrices `D(delta)=diag(exp(-delta),exp(+delta))`;
- regular timelike pair immersions and their full non-diagonal pullback metrics;
- ordinary orientation-free densities, with oriented forms treated separately;
- regular overlaps and triple overlaps of one supplied common pair atlas;
- distinct timelike observer planes at one regular Lorentzian tangent space;
- pointwise positive conformal rescaling of the ambient metric.

No action, source, bootstrap law, fit, matter model, physical history, query selector, branch
selector, `X_max`, signalling rule, or observational outcome will be introduced.

## Candidate tests

1. **Internal determinant line.** Compute `D^T K D`, `det D`, and the induced action on the
   one-dimensional determinant line. Decide exactly what is preserved without a spacetime solder.
2. **Pair-density covariance.** For a generic non-diagonal Lorentzian `h`, construct
   `nu_h=sqrt(-det h)|dy0 wedge dy1|`. Under an arbitrary invertible pair-coordinate Jacobian,
   verify density covariance and derive the transformation of the coefficient called
   `kappa_pair`.
3. **Overlap descent.** On one supplied common pair atlas, test whether the pullback densities agree
   under genuine transition maps and whether triple-overlap composition closes.
4. **Calibration mismatch.** Construct two independently normalized channel presentations whose
   transition is not `K`-orthogonal. Determine whether using the same numeric `K` in both charts
   defines one tensor/density or two unrelated trivializations.
5. **Cross-query object.** Derive the ambient metric area bilinear on bivectors
   `A_g(u wedge v,u wedge v)=g(u,u)g(v,v)-g(u,v)^2`, and classify its type and conformal weight.
6. **Universal two-form countertest.** In Minkowski four-space, test whether one alternating
   two-form can reproduce the positive metric area density on every oriented timelike
   clock/ruler plane.
7. **Three-observer carry.** Prove or refute that conditional `K`-isometric transition functions
   compose and descend, while arbitrary independently rebuilt middle calibrations require an
   explicit transition.
8. **Ownership ruling.** Separate a universal object over the bundle of timelike two-planes from a
   differential two-form or density on base spacetime, and from a physical valuation of the
   observer network.

## Certification and falsification contract

The strongest positive conclusion allowed is a type-separated overlap theorem. A valid positive
descent must establish:

- coordinate/frame covariance of the actual density object, not equality of coordinate
  coefficients;
- agreement on genuine overlaps;
- identity triple-overlap obstruction for one common atlas; and
- explicit hypotheses for extending the object across distinct observer queries.

The audit is falsified or downgraded if:

- the determinant-one reciprocal action fails to preserve its internal determinant line;
- the pair volume density fails ordinary tensor-density descent on a common atlas;
- `kappa_pair` is called a query-independent scalar despite a nontrivial Jacobian weight;
- fixed `K` is silently soldered to every spacetime pair plane;
- a metric area norm on bivectors is mislabeled a differential two-form;
- conditional `O(K)` transition closure is promoted into physical ownership of the transitions;
- conformal weight is erased or strong local CSN is restored; or
- any algebraic descent identity is said to determine the metric history or its numerical values.
