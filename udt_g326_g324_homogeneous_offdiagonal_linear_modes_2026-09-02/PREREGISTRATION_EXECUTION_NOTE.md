# G326 preregistration execution note

Date: 2026-09-02
Timing: before production computation

The phrase “nonzero linearized local curvature” in falsifier 3 means curvature change beyond the
Lie transport generated by the corresponding local cover-coordinate vector field. A raw coordinate
component of `delta Riemann` need not vanish under a pure coordinate change when the background
curvature is nonzero. The load-bearing test is therefore either

\[
\delta Riemann-\mathcal L_\xi Riemann=0
\]

for a claimed cover-coordinate mode, or an equivalent local invariant/operator-spectrum test.
This clarifies the already preregistered gauge-versus-geometry distinction; it does not change the
candidate count, landing, falsifiers, or permitted conclusions.

For the transverse logarithmic candidate, the intended witness is the first-order splitting of the
background-degenerate transverse tidal operator. Such a splitting cannot be produced by Lie
transport within that degenerate eigenspace. Scalar polynomial invariants may remain silent at
first order and are not required as the witness.
