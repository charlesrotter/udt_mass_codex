### Independent exact check: common coordinate time is not null correspondence

In Minkowski space let Y be at x=0 with proper time t, and let X be the
inertial receiver `x=L+vt`, 0<v<1, with proper time `tau_X=t/gamma`.
At equal coordinate increments, `(d tau_X/dt)^2=1-v^2`. For a signal emitted
by Y at t_e and received by X at t_r,

    t_r-t_e=L+v t_r,
    Z=d tau_X,r/d tau_Y,e=1/[gamma(1-v)]=sqrt((1+v)/(1-v)).

the actual received ratio is1/4 and Z=2. This exactly refutes a naive substitution

refute the compass reconstruction when the required time-transfer map and
normalization are supplied. The 2018 paper's different normalization must also
