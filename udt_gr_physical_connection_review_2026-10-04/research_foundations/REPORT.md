**EPS, 1972, inspected in the 2012 reprint.** Its inputs include smooth particles,
echoes/messages, a four-coordinate radar atlas (D1–D4), local light-incidence and
cone conditions (L1–L2), all timelike directions and pointwise inertial projective
coordinates (P1–P2), and light/free-fall compatibility C. It recovers conformal,

Lorentz metric locally, unique up to constant scale; global metric existence is
qualified by simple connectedness. The paper explicitly presents an incomplete
formalization. Dimension, smoothness and the free-fall law are not derived from
uninterpreted observations. [Primary reprint](https://bpfoundations.org/plans/ehlers2012.pdf).

**Matveev–Scholz, arXiv:2001.01494v1.** Definitions 1–2 and Theorem 1 concern
C² smooth structures, dimension at least three, indefinite nondegenerate metric,
and symmetric connections. If every conformal null geodesic is a projective
geodesic, the connection difference has the form
`D^i_jk=b^i g_jk+δ^i_j a_k+δ^i_k a_j`.
This establishes Weyl compatibility; it does **not** establish that the
projective class contains a Levi-Civita connection. Integrability is a separate
condition. Its proof fills the specific compatibility gap, not every operational
step of EPS. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.01494v1).

**Avalos–Dahia–Romero, arXiv:1611.10198v2.** In torsion-free Weyl geometry,
`∇g=ω⊗g`, they define standard clocks by
`g(v,∇_v v)=0`; Proposition 1 proves equivalence to EPS proper time.
Equation (15) compares identically calibrated clocks that separate and later
share a worldline: their subsequent rate factor depends on the difference of
the two `ω` line integrals. Requiring no such effect for all timelike alternatives
forces local exactness/closedness. Their global exactness conclusion adds simple
connectedness. The result uses this clock definition, not an unproved universal
identification of every material clock with transported vector length.
[Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.10198v2).

The literature's Weyl objects are comparison machinery here. None is introduced
as UDT physics. No atomic-clock experiment, matter action or empirical bound was
reviewed. The original Perlick paper was discovered bibliographically but was not

**Consequence and ceiling.** This is a cross-consistency requirement if light,

**Extra hypotheses.** An operational reconstruction needs sufficiently rich
identified trajectories, incidence and clock calibration, and the smoothness and
inertial assumptions listed above. No finite observing network was supplied by
this work. Replacing ordinary proper time with a Weyl standard-clock law would
be a changed object and requires separate authority.

**Adverse test / failure mode.** Do not replace absence of a second clock effect
with equality of elapsed proper times, zero distant redshift or path-independent
Lorentz transport. After reunion, identical ideal clocks sharing a worldline
have identical subsequent rates; their accumulated readings may differ. Metric

R7's directional comparison and ACI1's finite transport bound explicitly retain
that freedom. Those results are not refuted by norm integrability.

**Prior overlap.** R6–R7 already own the metric null-clock relation and its
direction-retaining composition; R8 owns metric recovery from a declared radar
protocol. R5 already separates reconstructing compatible full records from
selecting their values. EPS adds an operational framing and explicit assumptions

## Lead 2 — exact all-path rigidity: a conditional consistency guard

**Antecedent.** W4 plus the requirement that an additional effect be inside one
geometry motivates comparing a candidate `g` with a reference `g₀`. Neither the
owner clarification nor GR FILTER ONLY states that their complete trajectory
data agree. The following is therefore conditional, not a requirement already
derived for the physical UDT metric.

**Bridge (hand reconstruction).** On a connected regular domain with identified
events, suppose the two Lorentz metrics have the same null cones and the same
unparameterized timelike geodesics in every timelike direction. Cone equality
gives `g=e^(2f)g₀`. The connection difference obeys

    (∇^g−∇^0)_v v = 2v(f)v − g₀(v,v) grad₀ f.

Equality of each timelike geodesic as an unparameterized path requires this to
be parallel to `v`. Since `g₀(v,v)≠0`, the gradient must be parallel to every
timelike direction, hence vanish. Therefore `g=C²g₀`, with constant `C>0`.
This is the metric uniqueness underlying the EPS route, reconstructed here;
no Einstein equation enters. One shared proper-time calibration fixes `C`.

**Geometric consequence.** For the same actual endpoint curves, matched events
and regular null correspondence, both endpoint proper intervals acquire the
same constant factor. Their ratio `Z` is unchanged. Thus a proposed *additional
matched ratio* cannot coexist with exact agreement of all these geometric data
on the comparison domain. At least one premise of that complete agreement must
fail. This does not specify which trajectory or curvature changes, its size or
its distance dependence.

**Extra hypotheses / unsupported join.** The event matching, complete cone and
all-direction free-fall agreement, connected domain and clock calibration are
additional comparison hypotheses. Tested GR recovery is restricted and normally
approximate; it is not these hypotheses. Nor do current UDT sources attach the
positional effect to this particular reference matching. Consequently the lemma
does not close the requested physical assignment or establish a need for a new
postulate.

**Adverse test proposed to the parent.** Retain all null paths but require
agreement only of a single free-clock congruence: compare `g₀=η` with
`g=a²(η_time)η` and coordinate-rest clocks. This directly challenges any attempt
to replace all-direction free-fall information by one observer family. A parent
test should compute the connection, proper clocks and actual null arrival map,
not merely count conformal parameters. No scientific program was run in this
lane; this is a proposed discriminating test, not an already frozen parent test.
The main lemma's proof itself is analytic and does not need a finite sample.

**Prior overlap and novelty ceiling.** R5 already supplies full-record inverse
uniqueness, and R8's SGE1 already requires physical matching and warns that a
second redshift function cannot be added after `Z[g,Q]`. This version identifies
the particularly strong light-plus-all-free-fall data sufficient for uniqueness.
That operational data choice was not found by the bounded central search. This
is not a whole-repository novelty claim or a new law. Its best use is to reject
a purported invisible extra ratio under exact complete geometric agreement.
