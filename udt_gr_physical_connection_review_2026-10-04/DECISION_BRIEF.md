# GRL1 — return for discussion

The GR review found useful ways to test geometry, but did not yet find the
missing physical rule that selects UDT's geometry.

Three research contexts inspected ten primary papers on operational foundations,
clock/tidal measurements and joint cosmographic observations. Two additional
contexts independently challenged the proposed arguments. Their agreement is
AI review with stated exposure and limits, not a human physics endorsement.

Two findings are worth retaining:

1. If two geometries agree exactly about all light cones and sufficiently rich
   free-fall paths, their matched clock comparisons must also agree. UDT's
   additional effect must therefore appear in the shared geometry or comparison
   circumstances. It cannot simply be added to otherwise identical complete
   geometric data. This does not demand exact global agreement with GR or rule
   out small effects compatible with existing tests.
2. A changing redshift can reveal curvature, but changing relative motion and
   viewing direction also contribute. We derived the correction and checked a
   flat-space example that produces positive raw drift entirely from motion.
   This gives a diagnostic for a specified experiment, not a selected UDT law.

Much of the foundational literature recovers assumptions our current metric
framework already contains. It does not provide an extra curvature law for free.
We also kept coordinate clock rates separate from received tick rates and
geometric image areas separate from brightness assumptions. One disputed external
quadrupole formula was excluded; the retained scalar result was rederived.

The open question is now precise: for which actual clock comparison does the
additional positional commitment require a particular result, beyond what can
already follow from its motion and ordinary shared geometry? The asymptotic
distance aim by itself does not answer the distinct question of how one source's
redshift changes with receiving time. This review has not proved that the full
postulates are insufficient, or that a new one is necessary.

The next discussion should choose between developing a justified physical
comparison requirement and using independently defined observations to constrain
candidate geometries. Neither option is selected here. There is no proposal to
adopt Einstein dynamics, a new coupling, a cosmic source population or a fitted
distance law. Previous tools remain available. No further model search, data
campaign or new premise begins automatically.

The central R8GRL argument, R18, generated startup view and operational handoff
carry this return. Fixed initial reports, the two precision corrections and
actual review/check records retain the history. Commit preserves this work;
it does not canonize it.
