# Map and ponder — what could actually select a history?

The G60 audit showed that all declared instruments can vibrate together, but smooth-coframe
compatibility accepts every regular movie. Because little reliable work exists at this exact level,
the audit begins with mathematical type rather than historical filenames.

The key distinction is between making an object and imposing a condition on it. A metric makes
curvature tensors, causal cones, endpoint ratios, holonomies, and boundary data. None becomes an
equation of motion merely by existing. A genuine selector must contain an owned statement of the
form “admissible histories satisfy this relation.”

The likely sharp result is therefore structural: prove whether the complete chart carries arbitrary
metric jets. If yes, any nontrivial local restriction must enter through an additional owned law or
a global condition that truly excludes extensions—not through a more elaborate expansion of the
same metric parameterization.
