# Lay report — the instruments are not the score

We proved that the complete mathematical orchestra is genuinely complete in its regular local
arena. Its knobs give independent control of all ten parts of spacetime geometry, not a secretly
restricted subset.

That means no amount of repeatedly unpacking those same knobs will reveal the song the universe
must play. Curvature, light cones, endpoint dilation, holonomy, and boundary data are all things the
instrument can report. The familiar geometric identities merely ensure the instrument remains
assembled correctly.

What is still missing is one actual rule saying which otherwise possible performances are allowed.
It might ultimately be a global--local bootstrap condition, an evolution/response equation, or a
selected completion rule. But it must genuinely exclude some histories; it cannot be another name
for the metric or one of its automatic identities.
