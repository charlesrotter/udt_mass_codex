# G141 preregistration execution note

The first production implementation attempted to simplify complete radical-valued transition
matrices. It produced no outcome before being stopped and was refactored to certify the same theorem
through exact rational diagonal ratios and squared terminal invariants. No tolerance, witness, or
target conclusion changed.

During the ensuing type audit, the preregistered matrix `C_BA=R_B R_A^-1` was recognized as requiring
a supplied shared pair-coordinate carrier. On that carrier the distinct metric-matching map is

\[
D_{BA}=R_B^{-1}R_A.
\]

The production and independent routes therefore test both statements separately. `C` compares
calibrations on the model clock/ruler plane. `D` composes, reverses, and matches the endpoint pair
metrics on the original carrier. Its grading has the opposite sign convention,

\[
-\tfrac12\log\frac{(D_{BA})_{11}}{(D_{BA})_{00}}=\Phi_B-\Phi_A.
\]

The inverse carrier map `D_AB` and preregistered `C_BA` have the same positive diagonal
grading but generally different shifts. Only that shared grading enters the relative terminal
scalar. Neither is promoted to G123's full four-dimensional observer-chart differential. The final
report must not identify the two full matrices or omit the shared-carrier premise.

The repaired final landing returns to the preregistered `C_BA` theorem. The `D_BA` carrier-matching
identities were added after preregistration and are retained only as supplemental algebra, not as a
preregistered physical-transition theorem. Fresh review also established that the three finite
witness planes are pairwise distinct, so the witness is explicitly reclassified as independent
endpoint-state algebra rather than a realized common-event transition.
