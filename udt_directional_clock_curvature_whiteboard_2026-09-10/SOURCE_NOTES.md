[arXiv:1012.4806v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1012.4806v1), section II equations (1)–(9),
provides a primary account of the nongeodesic unit-timelike identity and norm conventions.

congruences is the relevant method; no matter model, field equation or energy condition
from the paper enters this package. DCI1 gives the direct commutator proof and separately
checks the curvature convention on explicit metrics. No quotation or novelty claim.

potential criterion. That criterion is not a premise of DCI1 and was not replayed here.
