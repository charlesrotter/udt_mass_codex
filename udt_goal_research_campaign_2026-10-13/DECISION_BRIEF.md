# GRC1 discussion return

We found a useful connection: a proposed universal form of additional clock
slowing also restricts how neighboring light rays separate. That lets us test
physical alternatives instead of merely choosing curves with the desired shape.
The connection is conditional. UDT has not yet selected its spacetime.

The starting clock-sign condition was already present in PRT1. This campaign
worked out its sharper optical consequences and tested alternatives. Under the
stated matching, the condition permits extra local spreading of light rays. If
we separately forbid that extra spreading, the allowed curvature difference
collapses to the isotropic class previously explored in PCC1. A weaker condition
on the average clock response in each frame also has a conditional consequence:
in a declared reference sector with zero Ricci curvature, and with a separate
requirement of nonnegative null convergence (Ric_g(k,k)>=0), it gives the familiar
Einstein Ricci class while leaving ordinary tidal structure free. The average
clock condition alone does not give that class. None of those physical conditions
has been adopted for UDT, and familiar GR-shaped mathematics is not evidence
that nature or UDT must select it.

There are concrete controls. A supplied nonconstant metric gives actual finite
slowing in both legs of a released-clock exchange, yet the same geometry gives a
blueshift when the distant clock is held fixed. Thus the clock preparation matters;
we have not removed ordinary velocity/gravity effects or identified a universal
positional component. Ordinary optical reciprocity also holds in a counterexample
that violates the proposed radial neutrality. A simple universal null-transport
idea fails its stated GR recovery test, and a naive projective-clock shortcut
already fails an inertial SR comparison. These failures narrowed proposals and
prompted redirection during the campaign.

The recommended discussion is about a physical meaning, not adoption of a curve:

1. Does the additional positional contribution constrain the directional clock
   response in every prepared frame, or the angular average in each prepared frame?
2. What actual comparison identifies that contribution amid ordinary SR/GR
   effects, and justifies a reference rather than assuming one?
3. Does it permit additional local spreading of light rays? If it does, the
   wider sign-constrained family survives; if an independent principle forbids
   it, the stronger curvature restriction follows.

These questions offer alternative research directions, not a claim that Charles
must invent another postulate. Keeping all trials unadopted is a valid outcome.
The next bounded campaign could seek a physical justification or counterexample
for one of these connections and inspect relevant observations after specifying
the readout. More arbitrary profile examples, a fitted curve, or a long GPU run
would not settle this particular gap. No successor is authorized automatically.

The campaign used two persistent researchers, primary sources, short exact and
numerical checks, and two fresh adversarial reviewers. It reached a substantive
review point before using the three-hour exploration allowance. Review included
independent original-metric and actual-arrival calculations. Same-model contexts,
floating-point checks and literature inspection are not human review, formal
proof certification, empirical confirmation or a full-corpus audit. Exact reports,
repairs and final attestations govern acceptance; all hypotheses remain explicit.
