# Lay report

We found a real piece of the hoped-for bridge, but only in a clearly labeled family of complete
geometries—not yet in the laws that choose the universe.

Picture the metric as supplying a compass at every point.  In six already-registered complete
twisted geometries, the metric itself identifies:

1. a clock direction;
2. a ruler direction; and
3. the two-dimensional screen perpendicular to that ruler inside ordinary three-space.

The new calculation asks whether that compass merely sits in a curved room, or whether the compass
field has its own genuine turning geometry.  It does.  In all six configurations, an exact
fractional calculation finds nonzero **relative projector curvature**.  In plain language, moving
the metric-selected ruler in two different directions and comparing the order of those moves gives
a real rotation on the remaining screen.  That is precisely the kind of path-versus-loop structure
needed by the earlier conditional `two-derivative plus four-derivative` geometric response.

This is stronger than inserting the old `S2` carrier by hand: the projector is recovered from the
complete metric on these configurations.  It is also weaker than a matter theory: the six metrics
are complete, smooth configurations, but no native UDT equation has selected them as solutions.
Nothing here selects the action, its coefficient, a particle, a mass, or stability.

The useful picture is therefore:

```text
complete metric configuration
        -> intrinsic clock and ruler
        -> rank-one ruler projector
        -> nonzero path/loop geometry
        -> conditional L2/L4-shaped response available

missing:
        native law/bootstrap selects the configuration
        + physical action/coefficient/boundary/source
```

So the conceptual joint is no longer hypothetical everywhere.  It exists in a nontrivial complete
configuration family.  The next honest question is whether it persists through that family's full
nearby deformation space and whether any metric-native on-shell admissibility rule selects that
family—without using the desired action to choose it.
