# Scope clarification — inherited twist amplitude

The committed preregistration `f362970` inherits the parent stationary twisted-`S3` family. In that
family `c_E`, `kappa`, and the twist amplitude `alpha` are constants; `alpha=0` is the frozen
twist-off control. Promoting `alpha` to a spacetime field was not part of the candidate universe.

This clarification records the inherited type explicitly. It does not change a candidate, outcome,
tolerance, acceptance condition, or conclusion. The exact descent conditions in this package are
necessary and sufficient only within that constant-`alpha` stationary family. A future field-valued
`alpha` would add its own fiber-invariance condition and requires separate preregistration.
