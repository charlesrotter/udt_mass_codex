# Next bounded question — proposal only

The next metric-led seam is the fiber-invariant branch's two-dimensional Abelian Killing plane.
The complete metric supplies a compact Hopf generator and a Lorentzian symmetry plane containing
the stationary clock direction, but the old rank-three uniqueness method cannot distinguish that
clock once the circle symmetry is present.

A bounded follow-up would classify, without assuming a desired answer:

1. the complete Gram, twist, curvature, orbit-topology, and fixed-point data of every Killing
   direction `K+Omega V`;
2. which data are invariant under a change of basis in the Killing algebra;
3. whether any metric-derived eigenline or global flow class uniquely identifies the founded clock;
4. counterfamilies with no unique line or multiple equally admissible lines; and
5. whether selecting a zero-winding representative would require an added global framing rather
   than follow from the metric.

The maximum possible result would be a metric-intrinsic clock-line selector inside the descended
branch, or a precise proof that the currently available metric data leave a residual framing class.
It would not derive the carrier, action, source, or physical branch.
