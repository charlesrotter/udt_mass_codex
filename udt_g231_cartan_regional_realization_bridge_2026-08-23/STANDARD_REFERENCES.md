# G231 standard-reference boundary

These sources are used only for standard Cartan-realization theorems. They do not supply UDT
curvature values, a history law, an action, or observer population.

1. Rui Loja Fernandes and Ivan Struchiner, *The Classifying Lie Algebroid of a Geometric Structure
   I: Classes of Coframes*, arXiv:1103.5850.
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.5850>

   Load-bearing use: a finite-dimensional Cartan realization problem is encoded by structure
   functions and anchor vector fields satisfying the Lie-algebroid identities; local realizations
   are obtained from the local integrating groupoid, and every realization is locally of this form.

2. Rui Loja Fernandes and Ivan Struchiner, *The Classifying Lie Algebroid of a Geometric Structure
   II: G-structures with connection*, arXiv:2107.01193.
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.01193>

   Load-bearing use: the classifying-algebroid formulation applies to finite-type `G`-structures
   with connection, the appropriate standard category for orthonormal frames and Levi-Civita carry.

3. Rui Loja Fernandes and Wilmer Smilde, *Relative algebroids and Cartan realization problems*,
   arXiv:2503.19233.
   <https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19233>

   Load-bearing use: a PDE/infinite-prolongation realization problem requires formal-integrability
   data; the general existence result cited here is conditional in the analytic category. G231 will
   not promote it into an unconditional smooth or global theorem.
