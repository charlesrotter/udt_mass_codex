# Lay report

We opened the whole angular “orchestra” instead of listening only to its round, equal-screen
subcase. All four parts were kept: screen size, both ways it can shear, and the rotation of the
screen frame.

That exposed a cleaner geometric object. The two old contact readings behave together like the time
and space parts of one two-number arrow. Observers who change frames **without changing which plane
is the reciprocal pair and which plane is the angular screen** disagree about the two separate
numbers but agree on one combined norm. That norm still carries the same depth-versus-angular-area
pattern found earlier.

But it is not yet a property of the metric with no extra viewpoint attached. If we tilt the
reciprocal pair into the angular screen, the combined norm changes even though the metric itself has
not changed. In plain terms: we found a sturdy joint inside the current skeleton, but the metric has
not yet told us that this is the one privileged joint every observer must use.

The encouraging part is that the real curvature contains this contact norm along with depth, area,
shear, and rotation. The limiting fact is that they arrive as an ensemble; curvature does not single
out the contact piece as a law.
