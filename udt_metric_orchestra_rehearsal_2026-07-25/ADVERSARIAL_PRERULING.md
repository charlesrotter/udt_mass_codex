Pure-gauge constant/gradient S jets must not generate curvature, although individual coordinate Ricci-component expressions can reshuffle under coordinate transformations; invariant combinations should depend on F and derivatives of F.

1. Calling the 16-by-16 scalar-curvature Hessian or a Ricci-component graph the full geometric response risks hiding nonlinear, angularly varying, boundary, spectral, global, and gauge-reduced channels.
2. A graph connected through coordinate Ricci components can be gauge/chart dependent. Connectivity must be qualified as an exact coordinate/component coupling census in the registered chart, not an invariant physical orchestra or selector.
3. Counting nonzero Hessian entries needs a frozen convention: upper triangle versus ordered matrix, self loops, exact simplification, and component aggregation. Counts alone are fragile.
4. A finite-difference reconstruction using the same coframe ansatz is independent code/method but not independent ontology or source provenance. It should not be described as independent proof of physical interpretation.
5. If the independent checker reads the production result to define expected sparsity/values, it is artifact verification, not independent derivation. Strong independence requires reconstructing curvature from the declared coframe without importing production code/formulas.
6. Pointwise Ricci components are coordinate-dependent. Gauge tests must use invariant scalar/tensor-zero controls or transform components correctly.
7. The neutral-point two-jet Taylor calculation is complete only for local two-jets within the torus-invariant ansatz, not for the full nonlinear coframe solution space.
8. The common domain may show instruments can coexist algebraically, but does not prove they coexist on any solution or globally complete finite cell.
9. Boundary volume formulas require the boundary causal character and chosen orientation; charges need a functional and polarization.
10. The direct response matrix must distinguish algebraic direct dependence, derivative dependence, global/set-valued dependence, and absence from the bounded slice. Zero Jacobian at a point is not universal independence.
11. Any source manifest mismatch, missing source read, unregistered output, silent output addition, or post-outcome alteration of the frozen universes is a hard failure.
12. The result should remain VERIFIED-WITH-CAVEATS or a bounded exact-atlas grade even if all algebra passes, because the chart, toric topology, neutral jet, scalar/Ricci diagnostic choice, and semantic review are premises rather than selected UDT physics.
