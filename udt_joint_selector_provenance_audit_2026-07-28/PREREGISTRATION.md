## Whole question

Do the two founding UDT postulates and the complete metric already supply one native typed
operation that jointly determines:

1. the physical observer/event/path comparison and signed depth;
2. the finite four-dimensional reciprocal lift, including transverse and mixing response; and
3. compatible global transition, descent, completion, and causal-interface data?

This is a provenance and entailment audit. It is metric-led and source-led, not a search for an
answer with desirable physics. It does not ask whether a candidate resembles GR, cosmology,
particles, Hopfions, or the desired universe.

## Source completeness rule

The prior P03 source omission forbids a hand-selected candidate list. The complete tracked tree at
the fixed base—9,926 paths—is the discovery universe. `DISCOVERY_RULES.json` freezes five broad
semantic buckets before scanning:

- founding reciprocity and observer comparison;
- signed depth and physical `phi` assignment;
- complete lift, coframe, angular, screen, and mixing response;
- global descent, cocycle, completion, seam, quotient, and causal interfaces; and
- joint selection, derivation, forcing, uniqueness, or whole-solution closure.

`discover_sources.py` reads blobs from the fixed Git commit, never the dirty worktree. A coherent
source group qualifies only if it has at least one hit in every bucket. Every hit-bearing textual
file and every preregistered evidence-named textual companion in a qualifying group enters the
frozen candidate manifest. Root files are groups individually. Normal audit directories are grouped
by top-level directory; archive, legacy, rescued, grok, and reorganization material is grouped by
its immediate containing directory so unrelated historical corpora cannot qualify one another.

Generated records from this audit cannot influence selection because the audit directory does not
exist in the fixed base. A discovery failure, undecodable source class needed by a qualifying group,
or mismatch with the fixed tree forces `SOURCE_DISCOVERY_INCOMPLETE_STOP`.

## Typed joint operation

A successful candidate must define one coherent operation `J`, not an assembly of attractive pieces
from unrelated packages:

```text
J : (complete Lorentzian coframe/metric, founded reciprocal observer data)
    -> (physical comparison arrows and signed depth,
        finite full-frame reciprocal response,
        global transition/descent/completion/interface data).
```

The three outputs must be obtained together or by an explicit internal dependency chain in the same
source lineage. A citation to another source is admissible only if the source explicitly imports the
typed operation and its premises; silent cross-package splicing fails.

`JOINT_OPERATION_OBLIGATIONS.tsv` freezes the exact input, output, covariance, composition,
nondegeneracy, global, and uniqueness gates. A rule that merely names a family, demands consistency,
or assumes the result is not a selector.

## Countertests

Every candidate must be tested against the already verified controls:

- the complete `R x S3` family with fixed domain and founded pair but arbitrary real `lambda`;
- arbitrary admissible smooth `phi` profiles on that same family;
- the endpoint cocycles `delta_f(p,q)=f(q)-f(p)` for arbitrary `f`;
- twist-on versus twist-off configurations, separating stationary depth from ruler selection;
- distinct registered global completions and causal-interface requirements; and
- off-shell complete configurations, separating construction from realized equations.

A candidate closes a joint only if it distinguishes the counterfamily by an operation derived from
its stated inputs. Adding trace zero, isotropy, trivial holonomy, a preferred path, a topology, a
profile, or field equations as an unstated convenience is a failed test, not a solution.
