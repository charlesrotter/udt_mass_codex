# G125 lay report

The SNe result now has a cleaner job.

It does not tell us the loudness of `phi` by itself. It tells us the exact note produced by three
instruments together:

```text
observed frequency depth
  = reciprocal depth
  + screen/areal expansion rate
  + source-clock relation.
```

The frozen SNe curve specifies how that combined note changes with areal distance. It therefore is
a real constraint on a history that already uses this same spherical observation setup, temporary
light-transfer assumption, catalog frequency coordinate, and frozen P1 curve: the three instruments
must add to this curve. It is not a universal condition on every possible UDT history, and SNe alone
cannot tell us which instrument played which fraction of the note.

This also explains why no new SNe fit is needed. The luminosity curve was already expressed using
the operational frequency ratio and areal radius. G124 changes the internal decomposition, not the
predicted curve, so the old Pantheon+ and DES likelihood numbers remain exactly the same.

In the orchestra metaphor, we have recovered one line of the score. We have not yet assigned that
line uniquely to the individual instruments or shown that its extrapolation beyond the observed
SNe range is physical. The instrument assignment must
come from complete-history consistency or another independently typed observation—not from adding
fit coefficients.
