## Whole question

Across every registered finite-cell completion class and every constant scalar-screen response
`lambda`, what global object does the conditional reciprocal family actually define?

The audit will distinguish:

1. a tensorial family on the ordered pair-frame/path groupoid;
2. a chosen global endomorphism field;
3. a field selected naturally by the metric;
4. a parallel field whose endpoint value is independent of path; and
5. a physical reciprocal comparison with a metric-native signed depth.

These are different gates. Passing an earlier gate must not be reported as passing a later one.
