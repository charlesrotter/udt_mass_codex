# PCC1 decision brief — a consistent addition with a reference-dependent meaning

The proposed isotropic additional clock effect can be stated precisely, once
two physically matched comparison experiments are supplied. Demanding the same
leading added effect in every freely falling frame fixes the difference of their
curvatures. It does not flatten or make all ordinary gravitational tides uniform.
A familiar nonuniform geometry provides an exact compatibility example.

Against a vacuum reference, the consequence is a constant curvature contribution
of the kind already allowed by GR with a cosmological term. Against a more general
evolving reference, the coefficient can vary; an explicit smooth example verifies
that distinction. The reference is therefore physical input, not removable wording.
The known example is compatibility evidence, not a new UDT prediction or selected
geometry. The leading local statement does not determine the entire finite clock
curve or its asymptote.

This completes the authorized trial without adopting it. Keep it as a reviewed
conditional connection and keep its reference/matching and positional attribution
explicit. The next decision is whether there is independent UDT justification
for that comparison, or whether the leading local assignment is too restrictive
for the intended finite/global positional effect. No such justification has been
invented in this test, and a new premise is not proved necessary.

No-change option: retain the conditional result and pursue another justified
consequence of existing premises. Adoption would instead add the all-frame leading
contrast hypothesis AND a physical reference/matching prescription; it would need
its own decision, predicted consequences and counterevidence. Simply choosing the
successful supplied metric, positive coefficient or a fitted curve would not
justify that adoption. No successor computation, empirical campaign or physical
promotion starts automatically at this return.
