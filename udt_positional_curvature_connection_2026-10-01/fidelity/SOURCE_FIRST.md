## Independent algebraic implication

Set G(X,Y,Z,W)=g(Y,Z)g(X,W)-g(X,Z)g(Y,W), so G(n,U,U,n)=-1.
B=A-kappa G is an algebraic curvature tensor with B(n,U,U,n)=0. For any
x and timelike v, split x into a component parallel to v and its orthogonal
spatial component; antisymmetry and homogeneity give B(x,v,v,x)=0. For fixed x,
this polynomial in v vanishes on the open timelike cone and therefore everywhere.
Polarization of sectional contractions for an algebraic curvature tensor then
gives B=0. Thus the pointwise condition is equivalent to A=kappa G.
The converse follows by direct contraction. This is not total-isotropy:
the shared/matched anisotropic component of R0 may be nonzero.

Consequences in the matched tangent space are Delta Ric=3kappa g,
Delta scalar curvature=12kappa, unchanged matched Weyl curvature, and leading
outgoing/return contrast coefficients kappa/2 and 3kappa/2. These are geometric
consequences of the trial condition, not E_response=Ric, DDR response closure,
Einstein dynamics or an action/source identification. One observer's isotropic
electric part is insufficient to determine A. One triad is weaker still.

## Regional integrability and comparison limits

Pointwise algebraic realizability is not a regional existence theorem. Let
S_ab be the reference Ricci tensor transferred by the supplied smooth matching.
If A=kappa G holds throughout a region, then Ric_g=S+3kappa g and R_g=tr_g S+12kappa.
The physical metric's contracted Bianchi identity requires

    3 d(kappa) = div_g S - (1/2) d(tr_g S).

This necessary condition does not close full Bianchi/integrability or select S,
matching, boundary data or a metric. In general the transferred reference does
not obey its reference differential identities with the physical connection;
naively subtracting two Bianchi identities is invalid. Do not infer an actual
variable-kappa solution merely because this formula does not force constancy.

There IS a valid stronger special case: if the matched reference is Einstein
Ric0=Lambda0 g0 with constant Lambda0 on its connected region, S=Lambda0 g.
Then the physical metric is Einstein with Lambda0+3kappa and its OWN contracted
Bianchi identity forces d(kappa)=0. This does not require the matching to preserve
connections. The anisotropic witness above uses a Ricci-flat reference and fits
this special case. Einstein/Ricci-flat restrictions remain explicitly conditional
comparison choices under G312, not admitted UDT equations.
