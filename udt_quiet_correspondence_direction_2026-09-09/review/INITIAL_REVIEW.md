## R1 — independent test information must include the residual bound

Defective seam: the draft requires supplied comparison data and warns about
unused test information, but does not explicitly connect that requirement to the
angular-residual control `epsilon` itself. Calling the eventual readouts predicted
could become circular if they supplied the fitted reference or the controlling
residual estimate.

Reason: a conditional implication between geometric quantities remains useful
mathematics even when all quantities are reconstructed from the same records,
but that reconstruction is not automatically an independent empirical test or
native prediction of the residual.

Survivor: the entire proposed geometric bound and potential future comparison
remain legitimate under their declared assumptions.

Smallest repair: state explicitly that any later empirical application must
document which independent information controls epsilon and the calibration,
with designated consequence records unused for those choices; otherwise the
result is a consistency/reconstruction relation. State that the bound supplies
neither a UDT-predicted residual nor an independent test by itself.

## R2 — do not presume coefficient uniformity across comparison data

Defective seam: the displayed coefficient
`B(K,positive margins,normalization)` can be read as a promised uniform bound
independent of the fixed reference/comparison data. Nonlinear lapse-gradient or
acceleration comparisons may require further dependence on those data or on
explicit reference bounds. A positive lapse lower margin by itself is not an
upper bound on a reference gradient.

Reason: sensitivity of a quotient such as a logarithmic lapse derivative can
depend on its reference numerator as well as the denominator margin. The
proposed question should allow the proof to expose that dependence instead of
silently promising it away. This is a scope observation, not a proved
counterexample or completed estimate.

Survivor: a bound for fixed admissible comparison data remains a credible
bounded question; no new physical assumption or selected scale is demanded.

Smallest repair: make B's displayed argument list explicitly illustrative;
include the fixed reference/comparison-data bounds actually required by each
readout, and require the future calculation to derive and disclose all such
dependence. Do not add an unstated uniformity claim.
