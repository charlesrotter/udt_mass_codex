# G86 preregistration — existing-condition completion-owner audit

Date: 2026-08-12

## Question

Does any condition already owned by the frozen source universe distinguish among the three regular
G85 completion families:

1. `SHIFT_SUPPORTED_NONUNIFORM_SEAM`;
2. `LAPSE_LIFTED_TIMELIKE_SEAM`; and
3. `TAPERED_UNIFORMLY_NULL_SEAM`?

This is an ownership audit, not a search for another completion and not a request to choose the
family that most resembles a desired horizon or physical `X_max`.

## Frozen source universe

`SOURCE_MANIFEST.tsv` freezes exactly 21 current controlling sources at base commit
`b8875ccffaaa422e3447c58895017e475202a804`. Generated G86 records may not become sources for
their own selection. No protected untracked source is in scope.

## Candidate conditions

The audit will classify, without adding to them:

- Lorentz regularity and nondegeneracy;
- exact preservation of the inherited G75 north cell;
- time orientation and bidirectional local causal-cone admissibility;
- observer-order reversal, Reciprocity, and matched middle-state carry;
- typed observer-query realization;
- co-presence as whole-solution membership;
- finite-domain/seam type distinctions;
- the working observer-pair `X_max` asymptote;
- observer re-centering/frame sharing;
- global topology and completion;
- geodesic or causal completeness;
- SNe compatibility-anchor preservation;
- Cartan/Maurer--Cartan/Bianchi compatibility; and
- an owned native history or global nonidentity restriction.

Each condition receives exactly one owner class:

- `IDENTITY_ONLY`;
- `KINEMATIC_ADMISSIBILITY`;
- `NECESSARY_NOT_SUFFICIENT`;
- `QUERY_CONDITIONAL`;
- `CONDITIONAL_CANDIDATE`;
- `OPEN_UNOWNED`;
- `INACTIVE_HYPOTHESIS`; or
- `OWNED_NONIDENTITY_SELECTOR`.

## Selection test

A condition selects a family only if all of the following are true:

1. it is present in a frozen source with the correct ownership type;
2. it is a nonidentity restriction, not a chart identity, readout, compatibility equation,
   necessary gate, or query-supplied condition;
3. it excludes at least one other G85 regular family without silently identifying the candidate
   seam with physical `X_max`, a material wall, a preferred center, or a variational boundary;
4. the exclusion is invariant under the condition's stated observer-order/re-centering scope; and
5. it does not require an action, source, matter law, bootstrap closure, fitted observation, or new
   history equation.

Passing the first four but requiring item 5 is `OPEN_UNOWNED`, not selection.

## Falsification contract

The audit fails closed if it:

- omits or duplicates one of the three regular G85 families;
- treats lapse-zero, uniform-null character, or preservation of an asymptote as merit rather than
  a typed property;
- calls `chi=pi/2` physical `X_max` by name;
- promotes co-presence, `c_E`, Reciprocity, a complete query, or Maurer--Cartan compatibility into
  a history law;
- uses SNe compatibility to rank continuations that are identical on the authoritative cell;
- claims geodesic completeness from complete tensor-channel regularity;
- activates bootstrap, action, source, matter, CMB fitting, or GPU work;
- lets a generated G86 record influence its own result; or
- changes any frozen source byte.

## Allowed landings

Exactly one primary landing is allowed:

- `ONE_EXISTING_OWNED_CONDITION_SELECTS_ONE_G85_FAMILY`;
- `EXISTING_OWNED_CONDITIONS_REDUCE_G85_TO_A_PROPER_SUBSET`;
- `NO_EXISTING_OWNED_CONDITION_DISTINGUISHES_THE_THREE_G85_REGULAR_FAMILIES`; or
- `SOURCE_CONFLICT_BLOCKS_ADJUDICATION`.

The result may separately state conditional implications such as “if the seam is additionally
identified with a uniform null observer asymptote, then ...”, but such implications cannot count as
owned selection unless that identification is already owned in the frozen sources.

## Scope and maximum conclusion

The scope is the exact 21-source ownership universe and the three G85 regular archetype classes.
It is not the continuum of all smooth completions, all topologies, all observers, or all possible
native laws. The maximum conclusion is a bounded ownership classification. It cannot derive a
physical continuation, `X_max`, `R`, CMB observable, action, source, matter, bootstrap closure,
signalling law, or dynamics.
