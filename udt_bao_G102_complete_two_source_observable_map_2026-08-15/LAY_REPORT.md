# Lay report

The machine is now assembled correctly at the measurement end.

One galaxy on the sky is one complete chord from us to that source. A second galaxy is another
complete chord. The observed angle is not a signal travelling between the galaxies: it is the angle
between those two chords where they meet at us. Each chord also carries its own accumulated clock
imbalance, which is read as redshift. Those two pieces are joined only because they belong to the
same chord; they are not the same local calculation.

The survey then repeats this for enormous numbers of source pairs and counts how often each angle
occurs compared with the survey mask.

What we have derived is the exact measuring machine. What we still do not have is the physical score
fed into it: the complete spacetime history and the original source-pair distribution. If that
source distribution is completely free, it can imitate any smooth invertible lens. Geometry becomes
testable only when it produces restrictions the source cannot absorb, or when a lawful source/history
relation is supplied.
