# G290 panel synthesis

Three independent perspectives were requested after the G290 question and decision tests were
committed at `22753c0a`:

1. differential geometry and conformal-history discrimination;
2. topology and full local-frame gauge; and
3. causal/time-live persistence and history-selection typing.

All three returned the same primary conclusion: the problem is layered. Pure topology can label a
sector and persist kinematically, but it cannot distinguish the connected G289 conformal twins.
Connection/holonomy can distinguish them, but it is continuous geometry and does not select a
history.

The geometry and topology perspectives independently nominated the same strongest replacement:
the differential Euler/Chern character of the metric-induced completed-pair screen connection. The
causal perspective independently nominated complete-pair closed-loop holonomy as the smallest
history-sensitive native object. These are the same bridge at two levels of packaging.

No panel member introduced or recommended a fixed round carrier, historical action, field equation,
source, boundary, observation, mass, scale, Planck cutoff, or `X_max`.

The whiteboard did not test an owned curvature-flux law and therefore did not select a physical
history.
