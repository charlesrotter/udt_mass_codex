## Decisive tests

1. **Gauge descent:** the object must be independent of local Lorentz/spatial-frame components.
2. **Conformal-twin separation:** a proposed discriminator must distinguish at least one preregistered
   regular pair such as `g_alpha=exp(2 alpha r^2) eta`, or state exactly why its mathematical type
   cannot do so.
3. **Connected-family test:** any discrete topological invariant proposed to distinguish the smooth
   regular `alpha` family must identify the necessary degeneracy, boundary change, or other failure
   of homotopy invariance.
4. **Persistence typing:** distinguish conditional invariance under a supplied smooth history with
   fixed regularity/boundary from a law that propagates or selects a history.
5. **Nonidentity:** a history selector must reject an otherwise regular history for a reason stated
   independently of the desired Hopfion or outcome.
6. **No import:** no action, source, field equation, carrier, boundary, observation, mass, scale,
   Planck cutoff, or `X_max` may enter.
