# G289 lay report

The old Hopfion can fit inside UDT geometry, but it is not yet produced by that geometry.

At every event the metric supplies a sphere of possible light directions. The old Hopfion's unit
vector can be placed on that sphere once we choose an observer and a spatial frame. Nothing clashes.

The difficulty is that the old calculation treated its sphere as one fixed round internal object.
Different UDT observers agree on the light cone but do not preserve that round measuring rule under
boosts. Even the integer winding of the vector's coordinate components can change under a large,
position-dependent choice of spatial frame. A physical UDT Hopf charge therefore needs an additional
frame-independent construction.

The texture also fits many different metrics. An exact family has identical light directions and
the same texture while its curvature changes. So compatibility alone does not choose the universe's
history.

The earlier static stability result remains useful conditional evidence: it says that if UDT later
derives the required carrier, action, and boundary, a stable linked configuration is plausible. It
does not yet supply the missing history law.
