# Goal-directed research

Help researchers reduce a named scientific uncertainty across several possible
approaches. A failed idea can redirect the inquiry; a useful conditional result
can justify a return before the budget is exhausted. Neither outcome by itself
selects UDT's physics.

## Authority and entry

Follow [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) and its bounded startup sequence. Scoped
agents attribute the parent's startup and perform their assigned checks. Read
the relevant sections of the [operating plan](../../../UDT_RESEARCH_OPERATING_PLAN.md)
for discovery permission, continuity, the research cycle and stopping criteria.
This skill is an execution aid for that plan, not another authority or a source
of physical premises. Current status, scientific grades and the maintained
argument retain the owners named by AGENTS.

Resolve these links from this skill's directory. Read live sources as needed;
do not copy a premise inventory, historical frontier or previous result into the
skill. A request to design a campaign authorizes its design. Execution uses the
actual user-approved scope; selecting this skill does not start a campaign.

## Establish the mandate once

Use the current written work order or construct the requested proposal. State
the objective and question family, source/premise snapshot, exploratory assumptions
allowed, methods, workspace, resources, exclusions, review/repair allowance,
return point and maximum conclusion. Carry forward existing user authorization;
resolve only material missing boundaries. Adapt staffing, duration and compute
to the task and available tools. A prior campaign's three hours or team size is
an example, not a default permission or requirement.

## Give researchers ownership of inquiry

Where authorized and available, give separate contexts the same objective and
source packet before exposing them to the coordinator's favored answer. Each
chooses a promising avenue and an inexpensive probe that could change a decision.
Keep the nearest prior attempt and the proposed difference visible. Researchers
can develop an idea enough to discover its test; an initial conjecture need not
already have a native derivation or complete proof.

The coordinator compares learning value, assumptions and cost, preserves useful
alternatives and exchanges findings when informative. Disclose later cross-exposure.
If delegation or a requested independence axis is unavailable, report that limit;
do not invent additional researchers or silently substitute self-review.

## Explore, learn and redirect

For each load-bearing conjecture, keep a compact record of its physical meaning,
source or invention, hypotheses, test and current disposition. Distinguish:
admitted consequences; borrowed mathematical methods with checked hypotheses;
and explicitly unadopted physical assumptions in conditional candidate chains.
Use primary literature when outside results become load-bearing. Check the
closest inspected predecessor before claiming novelty; disclose the search scope.

Attempt the informative next step. On failure, preserve the decisive evidence,
identify what survives and select another in-scope route when one is worth testing.
A missing connection is a research target; repeating its absence without a new
probe is not continued inquiry. A new conjecture inside the authorized family
does not require another permission request. Physical adoption, broader scope,
protected work and additional resources retain their existing boundaries.

Use one campaign notebook for decisions and continuity, with focused source and
check artifacts. Track what changed, what remains uncertain and what the next
probe can distinguish. At compaction or interruption, leave enough state to
resume the question without repeating closed routes. Keep failed runs and
original candidates recoverable.

## Review the argument and the next decision

At a substantive return, freeze the candidate with its discovery history and
have the authorized fresh reviewers challenge its strongest inference and
proposed continuation. Review should examine hypotheses, counterexamples,
observable preparation, closest prior work and relevant independent checks.
Distinguish fresh context, different model, independent argument and independent
implementation. Resolve objections through the included repair/re-review cycle;
retain any unresolved objection in the result's scope.

When compressing a result into a headline, lay brief or dependency graph, retain
every necessary assumption and quantifier. Check the compressed claim against
the proof, including adverse conclusions. A familiar mathematical class,
successful numerical example or reviewer agreement cannot adopt its physics.

## Return when the scientific decision warrants it

Use the work order's return point: a reviewed decision-ready result, exhausted
budget, actual scope/resource blocker or honestly exhausted informative routes.
Do not stop solely because the first idea failed, and do not fill remaining
time with examples that cannot change a decision. Judge progress by reduced
uncertainty, a justified restriction, a useful conditional tool or a concrete
rejected hypothesis; distinguish these outcomes explicitly.

When authorized by the work order, integrate the surviving argument and relevant
failures into the existing central development using the repository's
maintenance/review process. Keep fixed campaign
evidence and update operational continuity proportionately. Explain the result,
limits and next decision in lay terms. Checkpointing and publication preserve
the work; a successor campaign follows only from its actual authorization.
