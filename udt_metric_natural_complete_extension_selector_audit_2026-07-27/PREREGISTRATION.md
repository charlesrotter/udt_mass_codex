## Whole question

The founded reciprocal pair acts as
`diag(exp(-phi), exp(phi))`, while the registered positive-triangular
complete-coframe chart leaves a seven-parameter pointwise extension class.
This audit asks whether any already registered metric-natural construction
selects a unique local-Lorentz-equivariant section of that extension bundle,
including its transition semantics.

The frozen candidates are listed in `CANDIDATE_UNIVERSE.tsv`. Every candidate
will be tested against every gate in `GATE_SCHEMA.tsv` across generic,
degenerate, causal-type-changing, and nontrivial-holonomy controls. The audit
classifies what the registered geometry supplies; it does not demand a
spectator extension, Hopf structure, particle, action, source, or cosmology.

## Scope

Complete within:

- the twelve frozen candidate families;
- their cited registered source records;
- the founded pair and seven-parameter triangular extension class;
- exact pointwise, local-covariance, transition, degeneracy, and global-descent
  gates defined before evidence inspection.

Not complete for:

- future metric-natural constructions absent from the frozen source universe;
- arbitrary coframe parameterizations outside the registered chart;
- unknown complete UDT solutions or field equations;
- the separate physical observer/event/path variation domain;
- action, matter, source, boundary charge, mass, bootstrap feedback, or dynamics.

## Outcome vocabulary

- `SELECTED_DERIVED`: one section is forced by active UDT structure and passes
  every section gate.
- `SELECTED_CONDITIONAL`: one section is forced only after an explicit supplied
  premise; never promoted to native selection.
- `PARTIAL_CONSTRAINT`: the candidate reduces the extension family but does not
  select one member.
- `SET_VALUED_ONLY`: an invariant set/subbundle is selected but no member is.
- `AVAILABLE_CONDITIONAL`: a construction exists after choosing an auxiliary
  path, screen, eigenline, completion, or boundary.
- `OBSTRUCTED_ON_CONTROL`: the candidate fails an exact registered control;
  the negative remains control-scoped.
- `NO_ACTIVE_AUTHORITY`: the mathematical construction exists but no active
  UDT premise makes it the physical selector.
- `OPEN_SOURCE`: the registered source lacks the equation/global data required
  to evaluate the candidate physically.

## Falsification and certification contract

A candidate may receive `SELECTED_DERIVED` only if it:

1. is built from the complete metric plus the typed founded pair;
2. outputs a member of the complete extension class, not merely a line, plane,
   set, scalar, or diagnostic;
3. is local-Lorentz equivariant and chart independent;
4. introduces no unselected ruler, screen, path, eigenline, orientation,
   boundary, cap, quotient, or reset;
5. is unique on the generic stratum and has an intrinsic rule at degeneracies,
   causal-type changes, and nontrivial holonomy;
6. has explicit overlap/transition semantics and global descent; and
7. is authorized by an active registered UDT premise.

A set-valued result is not silently converted into a section. A rule that
works only on a chosen branch or completion is conditional. Failure of all
frozen candidates supports only
`CURRENT_REGISTERED_METRIC_NATURAL_SELECTOR_BOUNDARY`; it is not a universal
no-go theorem and does not license invention of a new premise.

The physical observer/event variation domain is separately typed. Even a
successful section cannot close a physical response or action without it.

## Maximum allowed conclusion

Either:

1. one frozen candidate selects a complete extension-bundle section within
   the stated scope; or
2. none does, and the exact unresolved equivalence/authority/transition gate
   is reported for each candidate.

No action, carrier, source, density, `X_max`, fit, matter emergence, boundary
charge, mass, time-live, GPU, canonization, startup-file edit, or repository
reorganization is authorized.
