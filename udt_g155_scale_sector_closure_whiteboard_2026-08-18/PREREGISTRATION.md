# G155 common-scale equation-role and principal-rank audit

Date: 2026-08-18

Status at registration: `PREREGISTERED__NO_OUTCOME_INSPECTED`

## Whole question

Within the exact frozen source universe in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.tsv`, does any currently active,
owned, nonidentity UDT statement constrain or propagate the common pair-metric scale
\(\kappa\), rather than merely define a representation, reconstruct or evaluate a supplied
metric, enforce consistency of independently supplied data, or evolve a query field on a supplied
history?

Equivalently, after writing a supplied regular pair metric as

\[
h=e^{2\kappa}\bar h,
\]

what is the principal rank of the presently owned physical-history equations with respect to the
jets of \(\kappa\)?

## Bounded regime

- regular, time-oriented, positive-screen complete-coframe charts;
- the founded reciprocal character on supplied ordered depth;
- supplied calibrated pair immersions and their complete pullbacks;
- the active G129--G154 relation-network, area, position, carry, chord, and asymptotic results;
- finite local jets and the explicitly registered network conditions;
- the exact 41 sources frozen by path, byte count, and SHA-256.

The audit is metric-led and source-led. It is not a search over candidate field equations.

## Excluded from this test

- the protected stopped native-on-shell draft and all other protected local packages;
- bootstrap, matter, mass, source, carrier, Maxwell-like, or radiative dynamics;
- an action, fixed-volume condition, strong local Common-Scale Neutrality, preferred conformal
  representative, constant-curvature rule, completeness rule, boundary rule, or fitted profile;
- SNe, BAO, CMB, or any observational target;
- singular, null, cut-locus, topology-changing, and unowned global-completion strata;
- the numerical value of \(X_{\max}\), an AM seam, or a desired quiet/finite/divergent tail.

No omitted object may be reintroduced after inspecting the result.

## Role classification fixed in advance

Every load-bearing statement will receive exactly one primary role:

1. `REPRESENTATION_CONSTRAINT`: restricts the presentation or algebraic character, not a metric
   history;
2. `DEFINITION_OR_EVALUATOR`: computes an output from supplied data;
3. `NETWORK_ADMISSIBILITY`: tests whether independently supplied relational data descend or agree;
4. `QUERY_EVOLUTION`: propagates a declared query, Jacobi field, or transport state on a supplied
   metric;
5. `CALIBRATION_OR_WORKING_FRAME`: observed calibration or explicitly working constitution;
6. `INACTIVE_OR_OPEN`: not an active equation;
7. `PHYSICAL_HISTORY_CONSTRAINT`: a presently owned nonidentity condition on \(g\), \(h\), or
   \(\kappa\);
8. `PHYSICAL_HISTORY_EVOLUTION`: a presently owned principal differential relation that propagates
   \(\kappa\) from finite data.

Only roles 7 and 8 contribute to physical-history principal rank. A formula that contains
derivatives of \(\kappa\) but merely evaluates the connection, curvature, normalized frame, or
response of a supplied metric contributes rank zero. A nonidentity condition on independently
supplied edge or area data is not silently promoted to a metric-history equation.

## Preregistered outcomes

Exactly one primary landing will be returned:

- `RANK_ZERO`: no active owned physical-history equation contains a nonzero principal coefficient
  on any \(\kappa\) jet in the frozen source universe;
- `CONSTRAINT_ONLY`: at least one active owned nonidentity condition restricts \(\kappa\), but none
  propagates it from finite data;
- `EVOLUTION_PRESENT`: an active owned principal differential relation propagates \(\kappa\);
- `MIXED_SCALE_CLOSURE`: distinct active owned conditions provide both constraint and evolution
  roles.

The landing is source-bounded. `RANK_ZERO` would not prove that no native UDT scale law exists; it
would prove only that the frozen active record does not yet own one.

## Certification and falsification contract

The result must pass all of the following:

1. exact verification of every source hash and byte count;
2. a complete equation-role ledger covering every G129--G154 controlling result and every upstream
   source in the frozen manifest;
3. symbolic conformal/common-scale checks showing separately which quantities are invariant,
   covariant, or contain supplied \(\kappa\) jets;
4. an independent implementation that does not import the production derivation;
5. mutation catches that fail if an evaluator is promoted to evolution, a source is omitted,
   strong CSN or fixed volume is activated, Jacobi propagation is called metric evolution, or the
   reported rank is changed;
6. the current scientific-premise verifier and package verifier must pass.

Any owned source statement satisfying role 7 or 8 with a nonzero \(\kappa\)-jet principal
coefficient falsifies `RANK_ZERO`. Any independently supplied smooth common-scale deformation that
passes every claimed history constraint falsifies that claimed constraint.

## Maximum conclusion

This audit may classify the equation role and source-bounded principal rank of the active common
scale sector. It may not adopt a new scale law, declare common scale gauge, select a universe,
derive dynamics, canonize a result, or infer observational physics.
