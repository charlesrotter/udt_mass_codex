## Result first

The whiteboard unanimously rejects **“select the one physical universe”** as the next mathematical
question. That phrase has been conflating three different jobs:

1. **admissibility** — which complete metric/relation histories obey the theory;
2. **evolution or continuation** — which partial data determine further data;
3. **actualization** — which admissible member is realized after calibration, boundary, initial,
   or observational data are supplied.

A predictive theory need not derive one unique universe. It must define a nontrivial lawful history
space and a way to propagate the data it claims are sufficient. The realized member may then remain
conditional on observations or boundary data.

The exact open object is therefore:

```text
SCALE_SECTOR_CLOSURE
```

not a universal history selector.

## The outside-box reframe

Write a supplied metric locally as

\[
g=e^{2\kappa}\bar g,
\]

where \(\bar g\) carries the conformal/reciprocal structure and \(\kappa\) records common scale.
The reciprocal skeleton owns weight-zero data such as

\[
\chi=\tanh\phi_{\rm pair}
\]

and its lawful composition. With a supplied fixed \(X_*\), \(\rho=X_*\chi\) is also fixed across
the G154 conformal twins. But a normalized metric-frame derivative has nonzero conformal weight:

\[
V(\rho)\longmapsto e^{-\omega}V(\rho)
\quad\text{under}\quad
g\longmapsto e^{2\omega}g.
\]

Therefore quiet, finite, divergent, and nonconvergent `V(rho)` classes are exact metric statements
for supplied histories, but they are not yet scale-carry-independent operational observables. They
become physically comparable only after the theory owns how clock/ruler scale is carried between
the relevant observer/query fibers.

This does **not** declare common scale gauge. The scale may be fully physical. The current
reciprocal skeleton simply does not determine it.

An economical mathematical type is a positive scale line or torsor \(\mathcal L\) over the
observer/query groupoid:

- reciprocal/projective quantities have conformal weight zero;
- normalized rates live in \(\mathcal L^{-1}\);
- pair areas and spacetime volume live in positive powers of \(\mathcal L\);
- a weightful value can be compared across endpoints only after an owned scale section or
  scale-carry connection is supplied.

This scale-line language is a type ledger, not a new physical field and not a gauge claim.

## What this changes

The common-scale gap is:

- **not** a blocker to constructing smooth Lorentz metrics or coherent complete observer networks;
- **not** automatically a defect because the theory need not actualize one unique history;
- a blocker to dimensionful normalized clock/ruler rates, proper intervals, absolute areas, and
  volume predictions;
- potentially a blocker to predictive evolution if no current nonidentity equation propagates
  \(\kappa\) from finite data.

Conformal/reciprocal predictions may proceed within their honest scope. Dimensionful claims may
not silently borrow a scale carry.

## Hostile audit of tempting shortcuts

### Reciprocal determinant

\[

\]

is `DERIVED` for the internal reciprocal comparison representation. It does not derive a fixed
terminal pair determinant, ambient metric volume, or \(\kappa\).

The founding simple metric has

\[

\qquad

\]

but these are conditional properties of its calibrated reciprocal block, spherical ansatz, and
areal chart. Promoting them to a universal complete-pair or ambient-volume law would add a
preferred pair density or volume form.

With the complete coframe,

\[
\det E=\det B\,\det Q.
\]

Mixing \(S\) does not change the ambient determinant, but screen scale \(Q\) does. A fixed volume
target could remove the pure common conformal direction without turning off all angular/mixing
data, but the target and its physical owner would be a new premise.

### Network reconstruction

The complete network reconstructs each supplied metric. Reconstruction is decoding, not
generation. A fixed point of “reconstruct your own network” is satisfied by every coherent twin.

### Causality

Causal order and cones are conformally invariant. They cannot fix common scale. Global
causal-geodesic completeness is scale-sensitive and coefficient-free, so it is a legitimate later
admissibility candidate; adopting it would nevertheless be a new global-completion `POSIT`, and
many complete twins would survive.

### Extremality, bootstrap, and information principles

An extremum of an already-zero consistency residual is vacuous. A new functional, endofunctor,
entropy, or bootstrap map can discriminate only after its measure, domain, variation, or
metric/query-generation rule is supplied. Those are possible law architectures, not derivations
from completeness itself.

## Three distinct mathematical homes

Let \(\mathsf K\) be the groupoid of coherent complete networks.

1. A physical **admissibility law** defines a proper subcategory

   \[
   \mathsf A_{\mathscr L}
   =\{\mathcal N\in\mathsf K:\mathscr L[\mathcal N]=0\}.
   \]

2. An **evolution law** has a principal part or continuation rule that propagates scale data rather
   than merely testing a whole supplied history.

3. Calibration, boundary, initial, and observational data \(d\) restrict admissible histories by

   \[
   R^{-1}(d)\subseteq\mathsf A_{\mathscr L}.
   \]

There is no requirement that \(R^{-1}(d)\) contain exactly one member.
