## Independent geometric argument

`g=-N(x)^2 c_E^2 dt^2+gamma_ij dx^i dx^j`, its stationary worldlines,
and proper-time clocks under W4's working physical-metric identification.
This symmetry slice is a declared supplied restriction, not a chosen Earth
solution or a consequence of G312/G313. With `u=N^-1 partial_t` normalized
by `g(u,u)=-c_E^2`, the spatial Christoffel coefficient is
`Gamma^i_tt=c_E^2 N gamma^ij partial_j N`. Hence
`a_i=c_E^2 partial_i log N` for the supported congruence. Along a spatial
curve of supplied proper length and unit tangent n,
`log[N(B)/N(A)] = integral_A^B (a.n)/c_E^2 dl` exactly.
The same Killing-norm acceleration identity extends to timelike Killing
observers in a stationary patch; this does not make arbitrary observers
static or automatically supply laboratory rotation/time corrections.

Killing energy is conserved and measured frequency is proportional to 1/N,
so received/emitted photon frequency has the reciprocal endpoint orientation.
Upward support acceleration gives faster clocks above. Zheng labels ensembles
1 to5 from TOP to BOTTOM and defines `delta f_ij=f_j-f_i` for j>i, so its
negative gradient with positive separation is consistent with this sign.
The conversion from synchronous atomic phases to those rates is a supplied
instrument/atomic/laser model; W4 does not derive the Ramsey estimator.
