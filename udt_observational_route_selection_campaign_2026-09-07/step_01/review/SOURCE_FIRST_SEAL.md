## Independent source-first argument

1. W4 already assigns clocks, free fall and null propagation to the same
   physical metric. This is a working premise, not a detector construction.
   Thus a conditional geometry-to-measurement comparison can be meaningful
   before a UDT atomic/light-counting theory exists, provided the actual
   metrological realization is explicitly supplied. G312's bounded vacuum
   law leaves local data and Weyl geometry free (G313); quiet-regime agreement
   is consequently shared consistency, not a unique UDT signature.
2. A stationary proper-time ratio depends on lapse ratios. It does not fix
   constant metric homothety. G276 requires a positive independently measured
   interval on the exact modeled segment to attach an absolute scale. The
   emitted-to-received photon-frequency ratio and local clock-rate ratio must
   have their endpoint orientation stated; they need not share a sign.
3. The controlled clock-height contrast is the most immediately legible
   connection among these sources: measured height change and a supplied
   local potential increment predict a rate difference. Chou2010 reports
   33cm and `(4.1 +/- 1.6)e-17`; Fig.3 calls its errors statistical. An
   independent total uncertainty and stability of systematic corrections
   cannot be inferred merely from this statistical bar. Inferring a height
   from those clock results and then using that height to retest them would
   be circular. Source: https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=905055
4. Cassini supplies a practical null-radio geometry link, but its stated
   result fits orbital/force quantities together with the geometric
   parameter. A published jointly fitted constraint is valid evidence in
   that model; it is not an unused-data prediction. The authors' presentation
   prints different uncertainties on its cover and result slide; these
   require explicit separation, not silent reconciliation. Source:
   https://www.oca.eu/Mignard/Grex/Presentations_pdf/Grex04_B_Bertotti.pdf
5. GW170814's calibrated strain channels are available, and actuator-based
   calibration is distinct from source fitting. Nevertheless the published
   polarization comparison assumes a GR phase model and only pure tensor,
   vector or scalar alternatives. It is neither arbitrary mixed-polarization
   reconstruction nor full eventwise curvature recovery. Original2017
   uncertainty numbers do not certify later GWOSC-v3 strain processing.
   Sources: https://dcc.ligo.org/public/0145/P170814/015/LIGO-P170814.pdf
   and https://gwosc.org/eventapi/html/GWTC-1-confident/GW170814/v3/
6. SNe reconstruction/scale attachment inherits G281's existing transfer,
   supplied-history and representation-sensitivity boundaries; this screen
   has no warrant for another fit. An ideal multiple-observer tidal theorem
   would be mathematical work with a later instrument bridge, so is not
   already a practical measurement route.
