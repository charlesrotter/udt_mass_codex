## Judgment before seeing the proposed recommendation

1. C is the best practical next route among the three inspected families
   for a small conditional published-summary benchmark. The reason is a
   documented local geometric query plus independent height/gravimetry and
   a published differential uncertainty/correction budget. Its roughly24%
   gradient sensitivity is modest. Precision across C/R/W is not a common
   scalar ranking; neither exhaustive coverage nor a unique optimum follows.
2. The mathematical bridge is proper-clock lapse comparison in a supplied
   stationary laboratory congruence: a=c_E^2 d ln N, then integrate along
   the declared proper-height path. A source-supplied local uniform-gradient
   approximation yields the signed clock gradient. This constrains a local
   combination under assumptions, not a full metric/source/history. W4
   supplies a working physical metric role; atomic/lattice/gravimeter and
   estimator/correction models remain explicitly supplied. G312/G313 do not
   provide an Earth source solution or extend vacuum dynamics into apparatus.
3. An executable next order can freeze one reported primary summary,
   independent geometric controls, sign/units, inherited errors and an
   elementary comparison before calculation. It may translate the observed
   ratio into a conditional lapse/potential constraint and compare against
   independently supplied geometry. It must label the result retrospective,
   since published outcome exposure is already complete. A new blind holdout,
   raw covariance certification or an experimental refit is unsupported.
4. The authors' control slopes do not tautologically fix the gravity-only
   intercept, but shared controls/records and large corrections remain
   load-bearing. Independent metrological purpose is not statistical
   independence. A new uncertainty combination must either retain the
   source's stated model or use explicit conservative bounds; it cannot
   invent a covariance guarantee or stronger significance.
5. Fig4 clock-inferred heights would circularly predict the same clock
   contrasts. Fig3a/3b use the same experiment and cannot be combined as
   independent confirmations. The geometric DDS/camera height in3H is the
   appropriate conditional calibration. Full raw shots are unnecessary for
   the narrower published-summary scope.

   acceleration can fix a scale in an identified family/segment: under
   g->s^2g, proper height scales as s and acceleration magnitude as1/s.
   Thus unrestricted scale after fixing every dimensional input is false.
   This experiment still does not infer an overall global metric/history.

   trajectory/nuisance estimation; W has accessible strain but a new
   predictive network comparison needs sky, waveform/response and matched

8. All three are shared geometric consistency/conditional parameter routes,
   with no established UDT-specific distinguishing signal. A discrepancy
   challenges the combined geometry/readout/correction assumptions; it is
   not automatically a universal UDT refutation or a new-mechanism discovery.
