## The precise clock connection

For observers following a timelike stationary Killing field K, set
g(K,K) = -c_E² N². Their proper support-acceleration one-form obeys

    a = c_E² d ln N,
    ln(rate₂/rate₁) = ln(N₂/N₁) = ∫ a·e dh / c_E².

Here h is proper distance along a local spatial path and e its unit tangent.
This requires the stated stationary observers and corrected clock-rate readout;
it is not a sourced Earth solution. With nearly constant upward support
acceleration, the upward rate change is approximately g Δh/c_E². The paper's
top-to-bottom ordering gives the negative sign. Conventional free-fall and
atomic readout connect the gravimeter/clock records to those geometric objects;
UDT has not derived those instruments.

The published uncertainty combination gives roughly 0.24 of the predicted
gradient. Large systematic corrections are retained, not dismissed or silently
certified. Source methods document control-variable measurements before
unblinding, so the target need not have been imposed by processing. This is
conditional use of published metrology, not independent reproduction of its
entire error budget. The campaign did not fit observations or calculate a new
observed discrepancy. [Main methods and Table 1](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-40629-8)

What remains free is substantial: spatial metric, unsampled lapse shape,
curvature and history. Five exact nodal clock rates give at most four lapse
contrasts, not ten independent gravitational quantities. Clock ratios alone
do not fix overall scale; independently fixed dimensional heights/accelerations
can fix a scale within an identified family. Legitimate initial/query data need
not all be uniquely selected. No global family or scale is inferred here.

The published clock-inferred heights cannot serve as independent height
calibration, and two analyses of the same experiment cannot become two tests.
If the clock gradient is used to calibrate potential, it cannot also test that
potential with the same datum. Published outcome exposure is already complete.

## Next decision

Approve a **two-step, two-hour maximum** published-summary clock benchmark,
including fresh review and one bounded same-premise repair/re-review per step.
Freeze the external calibration and source error assumptions, then translate
the published measurement into an explicitly conditional local-metric constraint
and a nuisance-sensitivity envelope—without refitting raw pairs or retuning
the calibration. A narrowed interval or an identified degeneracy is a useful
negative outcome. Full scope and stops are in the proposed work order.

There is no external-access blocker for that modest scope. Missing raw covariance
blocks only a broader joint reanalysis, not use of the published summary.

SNe assessments were reused; no fit repeated. Complementary ideal tidal
measurements could probe curvature the clocks miss, but remain mathematical
groundwork, not an available real-data experiment. They and further emergence
are alternatives, not prerequisites for this practical bridge.
