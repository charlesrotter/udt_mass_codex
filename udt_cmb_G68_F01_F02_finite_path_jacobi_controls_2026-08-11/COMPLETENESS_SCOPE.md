# G68 completeness scope

This package is one bounded observer-query tile, not an increment toward claiming full solver
completion.

1. **Fields:** supplied stationary F01/F02 metric profiles only; no varying matter or independent
   field solve.
2. **Action terms:** none selected or varied.
3. **Full equations:** full geodesic, parallel-screen, and Jacobi equations for the supplied metric;
   no metric field equation.
4. **Domain/coordinates:** all four coordinates are live in the central and perturbed geodesics;
   profiles are stationary and radial, and the central query is equatorial.
5. **Boundary/regularity:** declared control start/end, turning, caustic, affine-cap, `A>0`, and
   regular block tracked; no physical boundary completion.
6. **Topology:** no global topological sector classified.
7. **Dynamical character:** stationary background with affine geodesic/Jacobi evolution; no time-live
   metric.
8. **Branches:** the unique IVP branch for each registered profile is followed; other endpoint-
   connecting, cut-locus, non-equatorial, and global branches are not enumerated.
9. **Stability:** not tested.
10. **Regime:** dimensionless `r/R in [1/4,1]`, three analytic lapse controls, three mixing shapes,
    two amplitudes, and the stated auxiliary checks.

Every omitted criterion could contain additional structure. The package therefore characterizes
the exact frozen slice and nothing broader.
