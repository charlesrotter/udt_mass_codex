CPU work max120seconds/2048MiB per process, one parent scientific process at a
time, coordinated with review. The full406 premise audit has its separate
900second cap. No grid, relaxation, GPU or floating-point certification.

1. Build the original Cartesian metric second jet from C(x)^T S C(x), with
   general symmetric tracefree S, and derive curvature from its derivatives
   and the declared connection convention at the center. Check first derivatives
   vanish; compare all Riemann/Ricci entries, scalar and quadratic invariants,
   Weyl electric entries and signed null-screen entries.
2. Verify D x=0, its trace and tangent determinant, and exact sphere moments
   giving q's first nonconstant coefficient. The proof of full local analyticity,
   positivity and exact area uses the positive sphere integral, not a finite
   sampling check. Check the radial identities algebraically and S=0 recovery.
3. Check the diagonal witness's signed screen entries; include c=0 and both
   signs of c and S through symbolic identities. No finite sample implies
   completeness. Test the full directional density difference and source
   normalizations, retaining actual/scaled tide distinction.
4. A separate-context reviewer must recompute a load-bearing quantity from
   original definitions with distinct implementation/argument and inspect the
   full local metric existence proof and native-admission limit. No shared-code
   regression is advertised as independent science.
5. Hostile mutations: omission/sign reversal of D, omission of q's required
   area correction, false normalized-only reconstruction, and confusion of raw
   and comparison-scaled tide orders. Count only actual recomputations/catches.

Source hashes, exact registry/CANON/fixed-manuscript nonchange, preservation,
startup-navigation tests, final review exposure and publication are separate
integrity/documentation checks, not independent proofs of the mathematics.
