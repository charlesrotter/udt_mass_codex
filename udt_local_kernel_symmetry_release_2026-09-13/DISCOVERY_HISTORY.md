# Discovery history, before candidate freeze

The parent explored the geometry analytically after the work-order freeze and
before new executable mathematics. It first identified the danger of replacing
G288's metric by an unrestricted supplied Lorentzian envelope: that would discard
more than spherical symmetry. It then sought an angular-only perturbation with
exact radial block and areal normalization. The cross-product quadratic D and
the sphere-average q arose in that exploration, not from a preregistered formula.
Their proposed center tensor and invariant coefficients were worked out by hand
before computing. No observation, fitted profile or desired physical field was
used. No alternative numerical candidate was run or discarded.

The first fresh reviewer was dispatched source-first without exposure to this
construction, the proposed signs or expected landing. The initial candidate,
including unverified coefficient predictions, is preserved. Exact computation
and adversarial review follow its hash freeze; any defect is appended as a
repair record rather than erased from the initial history.
