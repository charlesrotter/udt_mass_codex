# G328 lay report

The sideways test passed, but it revealed more structure than the earlier axial test.

We allowed every small metric disturbance with the first wavelength around one transverse compact
direction. Most of those apparent components are coordinate freedom or constraint bookkeeping.
After removing those exactly, two genuine ripple patterns remain—the expected two gravitational
polarizations.

They do not behave identically. The G324 spacetime expands in two directions while contracting in
the third. A sideways-moving ripple has one polarization involving that contracting direction and
one that does not. The background therefore gives them different time equations: one uses
order-zero Bessel functions and the other order-three Bessel functions. Both become oscillatory
and decrease in relative amplitude like `T^(-1/6)` toward the expanding future. Toward the past,
their four branches behave differently: finite, logarithmic, proportional to `T`, and proportional
to `1/T`. We retained all four rather than choosing the pleasant ones.

This is a cohesive result for the provisional UDT spacetime equation. It was derived from the
current G324 metric with all ten perturbation components present; no matter model, action,
observational fit, new scale, or old scaffolded kernel was added. It does not prove that the entire
spacetime is stable. Oblique waves, combined harmonics, and nonlinear interactions remain open.
