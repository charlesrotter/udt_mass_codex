# Lay report

We have built the instruments and proven they can play together. We have also proven that the same
instrument wiring can play at least three very different songs: flat, steadily changing, or loud at
both ends with a quiet middle.

That means the orchestra is real, and your loud–quiet–loud picture is genuinely available. But the
current equations are still the rules for how instruments connect and stay in tune; they are not
the sheet music choosing which song the universe plays.

The failed SNe control tells us one chosen song is wrong. It does not automatically write the right
song. To keep building the house, we now either need one actual sheet-music rule or we explicitly
use observations to calibrate a provisional song and test it against different observations later.
