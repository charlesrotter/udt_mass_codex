# Lay report — what the complete-cell Cartan audit found

The earlier work found a possible two-instrument musical phrase: clock depth and angular area can
trace a loop together. But that phrase had only been written down mathematically; we had not shown
that the actual metric played it.

This audit found that the complete `S3` geometry really does contain a measurable geometric note,
called `t1`. It tells how the ruler leg twists through the angular two-dimensional floor. Its change
is exactly the clock-depth change minus the angular-area change. Combining that real Cartan note
with the founded clock-depth field reproduces the curl of the earlier two-instrument phrase with no
fitted coefficient.

That is genuine forward movement: the motif is not merely an attractive combination of two
variables. One actual complete metric family carries it in its first geometric derivatives.

But the result is still written for a chosen division between “ruler” and “angular floor.” Rotating
the two angular axes does not hurt it, but changing the entire ruler-versus-angular division may.
And the geometry has not said, “this phrase is the law.” It has only shown that the phrase is already
available in the instrument itself.

The mapping-torus family supplies the honest counterexample: its clock depth is constant, so the
phrase goes silent. The first exact curvature check also stays silent. Thus we found a real joint,
not yet the conductor, tuning rule, or matter equation.
