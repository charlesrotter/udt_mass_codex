## Whole question

data, does the metric/coframe differential algebra itself produce the cold-reviewed alternating
depth/angular-area object


pair/screen split, or does it descend to a presentation- and full-frame-natural object? Formal/free
rank and each fixed branch-pullback rank will be reported separately.

This is a **metric-led production and classification audit**, not a search for a desired response
law. `lambda`, zero, exact-only, multiple-response, and rank-collapse outcomes have equal standing.
