## Source obligations independently extracted

FRI1 supplies a conditional metric/history class and deterministic observation
interface, not an experimental specification already realized by an instrument.
It requires mu=mH in [.005,.01], A=aH in [.05,.1], E in [1,10], principal
limiting B_*=0, and y=1/(HR) in (0,.00002] across the whole support hull,
including all gaps. Independent algebra yields HR>=50000,
R/a=1/(Ay)>=500000 and R/m=1/(mu y)>=5000000. These are necessary admission
tests within the supplied coordinates/history; they do not prove that any
measured baseline is this R or any observed source size is this a.

External independent bounds on admitted m,a,R could constrain an H interval
without a desired target value: H must lie in [.005/m,.01/m] intersect
[.05/a,.1/a] intersect [50000/R,infinity), before the other shape/history
requirements. This is a conditional compatibility test. It is not a proof that
all geometric quantities are empirically supplied or a universal circularity
obstruction. The required observational map and geometry admission are open.

The timing observable is the arithmetic mean of
Y=log Z-log(nu_e/nu_ref)=-log(nu_o/nu_ref) over equal translated true-time
windows. It is not log of an arithmetic frequency mean. Source q is a uniform
bound on |dlog nu_e/dell_o| over the hull. If an emitter proper-second bound
Q_e is independently available, the map gives
|dlog nu_e/dell_o|<=Q_e/(c_E Z); a usable uniform q needs an independently
admitted positive Z lower bound over the hull. Unknown constant source
normalization drops out but arbitrary temporal variability does not.

The relevant angle is the signed position of the same pointlike emitter in the
specified parallel transported radial frame. FRI1 means theta arithmetically;
its angular record must not be substituted by mean log angle, source extent,
line centroid velocity, or a catalog direction in an unbridged sky frame.

## Failure witnesses and surviving interfaces

1. Phase endpoints determine an arithmetic frequency integral, not a log mean.
   Constant nu=1 and half-window nu=1/2, half-window nu=3/2 have identical total
   phase. Their mean log frequencies are respectively zero and (1/2)log(3/4).
   Smooth transitions can approximate this discrepancy while retaining the same
   total phase. Therefore a counter's finite phase difference cannot simply be
   relabeled FRI1 Ybar. Full resolved frequency reconstruction with a justified
   log transform and quadrature/window bound could supply the target. The
   witness rejects that substitution, not all phase-based experiments.

2. Finite samples alone do not imply uniform q: a smooth log-frequency bump
   supported strictly between sample times leaves all samples unchanged while
   its derivative can be made arbitrarily large by narrowing its support. A
   justified bandwidth/dynamics envelope or probabilistic regularity model can
   address this missing interpolation input; it must remain a supplied physical
   assumption rather than be inferred from non-detection at those samples.

3. Standard deviation, Allan deviation, a fitted residual rms, expanded
   uncertainty or a posterior credible interval is not automatically an
   absolute deterministic epsilon. A probabilistic adaptation can condition on
   a simultaneous event covering every needed mean, timestamp, drift and frame
   term, with its explicitly justified failure probability. Marginal intervals
   do not certify that event; correlations and systematic/model terms matter.
   A statistical statement cannot silently inherit FRI1's deterministic label.

4. Timing source variability enters with a minus sign in dY/dell. A fitted
   source drift that shares the time dependence of the sought scale cannot be
   used as independent q evidence without an additional separating observation
   or source constraint. It remains possible to provide such an independent
   constraint; no impossibility theorem is claimed.

5. A time-dependent sky-frame rotation or source-centroid displacement changes
   the angular record. It needs an allocation to the finite angular error or a
   separately bounded transfer to the stipulated radial frame. Fixing a catalog
   coordinate convention alone does not demonstrate the physical transport law.
   Finite angular measurements do not by themselves establish exact limiting
   B_*=0 preparation; a nonzero-tolerance extension would be a separate argument.

6. FRI1's derivative-to-difference argument crosses observation gaps. Disjoint
   successful windows cannot establish source/geometry conditions in the gaps.
   Conversely the use of finitely many pulses is not by itself fatal to a
   finite-window protocol: finite clock resolution must be included in its
   finite error. TSI1's finite pulse inventory limits infinite-tail sampling,
   and must not be promoted to a universal finite-experiment no-go.

## Three fixed protocol classes, pending primary-source exposure

Atomic optical link: controlled clock labels and monitored transfer offer a
plausible route to calibrated finite clock readout. Required separately are the
link's physical propagation map to the stipulated ray (including path/noise
correction), full error accounting and log-mean construction. Fiber transport
does not identify a free null ray automatically. Neither source stability nor
link precision establishes the FRI1 source orbit, radial escaping receiver,
principal preparation or tail. This is a candidate metrology interface, not an
admitted geometry.

Pulsar: a finite pulse arrival/phase sequence can provide calibrated time tags.
An intrinsic spin clock plus propagation corrections is not a labeled proper
clock unless its map is justified. Intrinsic evolution, pulse jitter/profile
changes, gaps and propagation corrections need distinct allocations; fitting
them from the same drift-sensitive record risks absorbing the scale signal.
The statistical interpretation and independent source bound must be checked
against primary methods. No astronomical source law is imported here.

Megamaser: spectral line centers and resolved angular spots are potentially two
channels, but neither is automatically a stable proper-clock frequency of the
same pointlike object. Blending, acceleration/velocity effects, spot identity
and spatial reference need explicit maps and error bounds. An external dynamical
or distance fit is comparison evidence with its own assumptions, not native
UDT metric/source admission or independent data merely because it has units.
