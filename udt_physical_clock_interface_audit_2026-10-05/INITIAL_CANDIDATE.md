# PIA1 initial interface argument — conditional, not empirical admission

Discovery history: the parent and two fresh source-first reviewers independently
identified extreme-tail and readout requirements. Their leads were exchanged
before this candidate was frozen. Published outcomes are exposed; no held-out
observational test, source fit or new physical premise is claimed. FRI1's
conditional mathematics is retained, not regraded or promoted.

## 1. A physical experiment must first inhabit the stated class

From FRI1, mu=mH∈[.005,.01], A=aH∈[.05,.1], E∈[1,10],
0<y=1/(HR)<=.00002, principal B_*=0 and actual small-B incidence.
The entire support hull, including gaps, is admitted. Since h=1-3mu/A<=.85,
B<=.0001, w<9 and alpha<=1.000000006,

 Z=(1-wB)/(sqrt(h)alpha y)
   >=.9991/[sqrt(.85)*1.000000006*.00002] >54180.        (P1)

This is an exact conservative implication, not an independently observed ratio.
It applies to total Z for these source/receiver histories, not an extra factor
to append to an already reduced redshift. A calibrated absolute source ratio
below this bound excludes THIS class for that clock pairing; an unknown source
normalization prevents that inference. Published systemic frame-reduced spectral
proxies must first be mapped to the actual pairing. A large measured Z alone
would not establish all the class's motion, geometry and incidence conditions.

Also HR>=50000, R/a>=500000, R/m>=5000000, m/a∈[.05,.2].
At the receiver f(R)=1-2mu y-1/y²<0: this is the exterior future region of
the supplied static patch, not an ordinary stationary laboratory pair in it.
R is areal radius in this conditional metric; none of these inequalities
identifies R with an observed distance, radar range or X_max.

Define K=c_E H and dimensionless monitoring duration C=K T_seconds.
The longer FRI1 control has C=1, the shorter C=.008, window K delta=.0005.
For any chosen finite physical T and C,

 a/c_E=A T/C,  R/c_E>=50000 T/C,  m/c_E=mu T/C.          (P2)

Thus changing H to shorten monitoring also changes the required geometry.
No number of years follows without an independent H; H is not set to an
observed Hubble constant. P2 does not interpret R/c_E as signal travel time.
The long control additionally requires q_seconds/K=.001 and sigma/T=.001.
These are design ratios, not achieved physical specifications; q must be

## 2. Which clock quantity needs calibration

Under the inherited proper-clock ray readout, nu_o=nu_e/Z and
Y=-log(nu_o/nu_ref). A continuously resolved receiver frequency record can
therefore supply the FRI1 mean of Y, provided frequency/reference drift, missing
samples, link effects, time calibration and source identification are budgeted.
FRI1 never derived those material/instrument conditions.

An emitter proper-time stability bound |dlog nu_e/dtau_e|<=Q_e maps to
q_seconds<=Q_e/Zmin because dtau_e/dtau_o=1/Z. This implication is useful
ONLY after independent admission of Z>=Zmin throughout the relevant interval.
Local source diagnostics or independently established source specifications
could support Q_e; fitting an unknown source drift from the same desired
clock slope cannot independently supply it. Finite frequency stability
statistics do not themselves bound the pointwise derivative across all gaps.
Nor does small amplitude imply a derivative bound: log nu_e=epsilon sin(omega
tau_e) has bounded amplitude and arbitrarily large derivative as omega grows.

For arbitrary two positive geometric histories Z1,Z2 on a finite interval,
choosing nu_e2(t)=nu_e1(t) Z2(t)/Z1(t) gives identical nu_o(t). Each function
can be composed with its own monotone emission map to define a source history.
This is an observational ambiguity if source drift is unrestricted, not a
new physical source law or a refutation under FRI1's stipulated q bound.

Finite pulses/cycles must actually resolve the window. A source proper period
whose entire corresponding reception interval lies in the class is stretched
by at least54180. This restricts cadence; it predicts neither photon flux nor
visibility, which need a separate physical source/detector interface.

## 3. Readout reduction and uncertainty

A phase-endpoint counter gives mean frequency, not mean log-frequency. Let
Y_c=-log(mean exp(-Y)); then J=mean Y-Y_c>=0. If |Y'|<=M on a window of
width delta in the same calibrated time coordinate,

 0<=J<=M² delta²/8.                                    (P3)

Proof: for X=-Y with range width L<=M delta, let g(t)=log(mean exp(tX)).
The tilted distribution remains supported on that range, so g''=Var_t X
<=L²/4 (distance to its midpoint bounds the variance). Twice integrating
g'' from0 to1 proves g(1)-g(0)-g'(0)<=L²/8. Jensen gives nonnegativity.
FRI1 provides M=H(1+eta)+q in proper length. Its longer-control values give
J<3.14e-8. This is a small conditional estimator correction, not a physical
instrument error bound. Include it in epsilon_z or correct it; do not silently
replace the measurement. To use it without circularly selecting H, certify an
independent M or retain H-dependent bias in the candidate compatibility test.

A uniform fractional frequency error |nu_hat/nu-1|<=r<1 implies log error
<=-log(1-r). Epsilon_z=.003 corresponds to r<=1-exp(-.003), about.29955%,
if it were the sole error. A physical budget must allocate that total among
source/reference calibration, transfer/readout, estimator, sampling and other
uncertainties; receiver reference drift can mimic geometry. Source drift q
is already separate and must not be double-counted. Timestamp-center error
sigma does not cover width, clock-rate or dead-time uncertainty automatically.

The angle tolerance5e-8rad is about10.313mas. It applies to a finite signed
angle mean in the declared parallel radial frame. Relative VLBI spot errors
or telescope pointing alone do not calibrate that frame. Attitude/radial-axis
registration, aberration/motion calibration, feature wander and window reduction
need a total budget. An unconstrained frame rotation beta(t) adds to theta(t)
and can conceal angular drift. Small positional error bars cannot rule it out.

FRI1's bounds are deterministic. Standard uncertainties, credible intervals
or Allan-deviation plots cannot be substituted without their probability model
and coverage. There is no need to demand absolute empirical certainty: if an
independently justified JOINT event gives all relevant bounds with probability
at least1-p, the deterministic implication yields an H interval with at least
that coverage, conditional on the geometry/source class. For separate valid
event bounds p_i, the union bound gives p<=sum p_i without independence. No
such joint calibration is supplied here, and pointwise coverage does not certify
an entire time hull. This is a route to a future statistical protocol, not a
likelihood adopted in this audit.

## 4. Three declared protocols

Primary-source provenance and limited supporting facts are in SOURCES.md.

1. Engineered atomic clock plus optical link: supports calibrated source/reference
   diagnostics and precise frequency transfer in its tested local setup.
   The cited BACON experiment does not supply FRI1's circular source, escaping
   receiver, extreme total Z or parallel angular frame. Importing its headline
   precision into the distant-tail case would be unsupported. It is useful for
   validating finite frequency reduction, not evidence that this scale experiment
   is physically prepared. Its local ordinary-clock success is consistent with UDT.
2. Pulsar timing: provides finite arrival records and explicit timing/noise models.
   The cited IPTA analysis must fit intrinsic spin and spin-down; it removes a
   quadratic timing component. Thus its residual precision is not independent
   knowledge of the secular source clock required here. No tail, circular-source,
   radial-free-receiver or bounded proper-cadence certificate is provided.
3. Megamaser spectra and resolved positions: the already audited MCP XI protocol
   is the closest existing two-channel interface. Its feature fits, disk dynamics
   and statistical errors are explicit but are not FRI1's means/frame. MCP XIII's
   six systemic proxies have1+z about1.0023–1.0340, not the P1 scale. Those are
   model/frame-reduced proxies, not direct total-Z inputs. They cannot certify
   the extreme tail by transplantation; source-to-receiver reduction remains
   necessary. No formal likelihood exclusion or new fit is asserted.

None of these three documented protocols independently supplies ALL required
FRI1 interfaces. The obstruction is concrete, not a universal nonexistence
claim: an extreme conditional regime plus unvalidated source/frame/error
admission. FRI1 survives as a conditional tool; its1.902% width is not yet an
experimental precision forecast. Matched GR remains identical. Native geometry,
event/path assignment, physical mass interface and X_max remain open.

## 5. Return and next question

Do not extend this tail calculation into a proposed astronomical fit. The next
useful bounded question is whether the SAME supplied metric can yield useful
finite-source readouts in a physically justified, moderate observed-ratio
regime, with source drift and motion retained, and whether any scale-sensitive
combination survives those nuisance freedoms. That requires its own stated
protocol/domain and finite checks; the present eta and error certificate do
not transfer. If geometry and source remain indistinguishable, return the
explicit ambiguity rather than fit it away. Native UDT selection is a separate
question and no additional UDT prediction follows merely from this comparison.
