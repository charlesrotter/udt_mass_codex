# PIA1 explicit operational limitations

The exact principal B_*=0 preparation is another conditional FRI1 input. Finite
angular data with nonzero errors cannot by themselves certify an exactly zero
limiting preparation. Any robust enlarged-class use needs new bounds; PIA1
does not supply them. Whole-hull geometry/source conditions cannot be certified
merely by finitely many observed points.

Receiver clock-rate scale, window width and dead-time calibration must be
independently accounted for. c_E converts a calibrated time; it does not
calibrate the clock. A reported time t_hat=(1+epsilon)t rescales an inferred
logarithmic rate by1/(1+epsilon). Center error alone is not a rate-error model.

The suggested moderate-regime successor is optional and requires a new
authorization. It would first test operational applicability/ambiguity within
the same conditional comparison family; it cannot be advertised as native UDT
selection or an experiment distinguishing that family from matched GR.
