# PIA1 primary-source audit ledger

Accessed2026-10-05 through web PDF/HTML text; no new observational data fitted.
Five sources, within the six-source budget. Paper outcomes were exposed during
discovery. These are metrology/comparison sources, not adopted native physics.
Short paraphrases below identify the evidence used, with locators for recheck.
No claim that the search exhausts experiments or represents newest performance.

**S1 BACON Collaboration, arXiv:2005.14694v1**,
[author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.14694v1), main text and supplement
sections1,3,4: compares Al+,Yb,Sr frequency ratios over fiber/free-space links.
The reported uncertainties concern corrected ratios with statistical/systematic
evaluation, clock shift corrections, filtering and correlation analysis.
Supports a physical frequency-transfer interface; does not supply a uniform
all-time source derivative, FRI1 distant-tail conditions or its angular frame.
This is the2020 manuscript, not a claim to current best performance.

**S2 Hobbs et al., arXiv:1910.13628**,
[IPTA timescale paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.13628), sections2,3.1, PDFpp3,6–8:
timing analysis uses noise models and fitted spin parameters. Spin period and
spin-down are unknown beforehand; fitting removes quadratic timing components.
The clock estimates/errors are correlated and statistically interpreted.
Supports usable timed records, not independently known secular source cadence
or deterministic uncertainty across a continuous observing hull. This is the
specific published protocol assessed, not every pulsar technique.

**S3 Pesce et al., MCP XI, arXiv:2001.04581v1**,
[author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.04581v1), section3.2 PDFp8,
section4 and appendicesA/B: fits Gaussian spectral features jointly over nine
epochs and uses a warped disk model with nuisance parameters and uncertainty
modeling. Positions are measured relative to an observational reference.
Existing ACP1 independently exposes its source/dynamics and angular-map costs.
Supports a concrete two-channel reduction; does not validate FRI1's window
means, physical radial frame or pointwise source-drift bound.

**S4 Pesce et al., MCP XIII, arXiv:2001.09213v2**,
[Table1 and note, PDFp4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.09213v2): six systemic optical
velocities in the CMB frame give near-unity1+z under the stated optical convention.
The medians and16th–84th percentile intervals come from disk modeling, not raw
paired-clock readings. Existing OEV1's six-row transcription is reused as
comparison provenance; no H0 stage, scale fit or posterior reanalysis here.

**S5 JCGM100:2008**, [official GUM](https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/2071204/JCGM_100_2008_E.pdf),
sections5.2,6.2–6.3 and annexG: uncertainty combination must account for relevant
correlation; expanded intervals require stated coverage and distributional
knowledge. Supports distinguishing uncertainty from absolute bounds. It neither
rules out empirical tests nor supplies the missing instrument probability model.

Our own algebra, not these papers, supplies P1–P3, the drift/frame ambiguity
and the joint-event propagation statement. The papers do not establish a new
UDT law, an additional matched-GR effect or physical X_max.
