## Why response-first receives priority—but is not selected

The response-first architecture has one UDT-specific foothold already derived: global completion
data change legal local endpoint and jet fibers. The bootstrap posit gives the correct relational
type for a complete return, and observer Reciprocity supplies its equivariance gate. These facts do
not provide the missing response formula, but they permit one bounded next test without first
choosing an action class.

The action-first architecture is not refuted. A legitimate action would define its own variation
ownership and could make bootstrap a property of complete solutions. Current known candidates do
not establish that route natively: `C^2`/Bach depends on inactive strong CSN plus further class
premises, EH depends on post-scale Lovelock/minimality premises, and the `S2` `L2+L4` model retains
carrier, boundary and coefficient premises. Removing CSN does not select EH or any other action.

Thus priority follows from **smaller current premise extension**, not convenience or expectation.
It is explicitly revisable if the response-first test reduces to identity/boundary bookkeeping or
if a genuinely metric-selected functional and variation domain are derived.

## Architecture rulings

The complete comparison is in `ARCHITECTURE_COMPARISON.tsv`.

### `R_FIRST`

The primary object would be an observer-natural nonidentity relation joining global data and
complete configurations. Legitimate variations would be tangents to that relation, and its
conormal could carry a response. Source would be a local projection/current role of the complete
response; mass would require a selected solution, generator/reference, boundary and normalization.
An action would be downstream and optional unless integrability is derived.

What is present: a derived partial completion-dependent correspondence, typed readouts, an
equivariance gate, and plural conditional response witnesses.

What is absent: a complete readout, a nonidentity interior return, physical boundary ownership,
rank-changing/query completion, dynamics and integrability.

### `A_FIRST`

The primary object would be a native functional on a declared complete configuration space with a
differentiable boundary completion. Its varied fields and tangents would define variation
ownership. Its Euler/boundary response would then govern complete solutions; source and mass would
remain downstream of coupling and boundary-generator normalization.

What is present: exact conditional action-class theorems and conditional model examples.

What is absent: a foundation-derived functional class, derivative order, coefficients, query
quantifier, complete boundary term, source coupling and normalization.

## The joint dependency result

`JOINT_DEPENDENCY.tsv` distinguishes prerequisites from outputs.

- A law and its variation ownership are one structural joint: the law's home, fields, query
  semantics, global moduli and boundary data determine the legitimate tangent directions.
- Source is not automatically a substance. It is the role by which one sector appears in another
  sector's response equation after the coupling/variation is defined.
- Physical mass is not any positive energy-like integral. It requires an admitted solution plus a
  generator/reference, boundary completion and normalization.
- Bootstrap is not a density optimizer. It requires a nonidentity same-solution relation between
  independently typed global and local data.

This order explains why earlier work repeatedly encountered the same open gates under different
names. It does not prove that geometry will close them.

## F01/F02/F04 as scoped witnesses

- F01 demonstrates that changing variation ownership can create or remove a nonzero branch. It is

- F02 demonstrates that local response, stiffness and completion conditions can coexist

None may be used to choose the parent architecture.

## Falsifiers and circularity gates

For response-first, the current foothold fails as a path to a native law if every candidate
conormal response is zero, purely presentation-dependent, boundary-only with no owned interior
channel, or becomes the identity after the readout `O=R[X]` is substituted. Defining admissibility
as “whatever the desired solution satisfies” is also circular.

For action-first, the current route fails native selection if the functional class or variation
domain is chosen by familiarity, desired equations, stability, mass, or observational success.
Multiple inequivalent complete functionals satisfying the same founded gates would also defeat a
claimed unique selection, though not the general possibility that an action exists.

The machine-readable catches reject promotion of either architecture, bootstrap-as-return,
source-as-substance, physical-mass promotion, CSN reactivation, conditional-family promotion and
loss of the current asymptotic/calibration distinctions.

The next justified work is a **partial-correspondence conormal response audit**, not construction
of a complete law. It should use only the already-derived completion-to-local constraints and ask:

1. whether their conormal response is nonzero and observer-natural;
2. whether it depends on both global and local variables rather than one side alone;
3. whether it has any metric-owned interior lift beyond endpoint/joint data;
4. whether F01/F02 conditional wall/seam responses are compatible pullbacks; and
5. whether the relation remains nonidentity under same-solution closure.

The maximum positive result is a partial response channel and a better-typed continuation. The
maximum negative result is that this foothold is boundary kinematics only. No complete response,
action, source or mass conclusion follows either way.
