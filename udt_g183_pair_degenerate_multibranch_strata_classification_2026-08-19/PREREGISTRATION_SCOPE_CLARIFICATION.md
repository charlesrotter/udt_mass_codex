# G183 preregistration scope clarification

The preregistered rank theorem was explicitly conditional on `h00<0`: with a valid timelike clock,
`det(h)=0` is equivalent to tangent rank loss. Before external review, the derivation was expanded to
record the complementary cases that this conditional was designed not to erase:

- a rank-two null plane can have `det(h)=0` while the induced metric has rank one; and
- a rank-two spacelike plane has positive determinant but no observer-clock direction.

This clarification adds no new premise or desired outcome. It prevents the conditional timelike-clock
theorem from being overgeneralized into the false statement that every zero Gram determinant is map
rank loss. The landing and conclusion ceiling are unchanged.
