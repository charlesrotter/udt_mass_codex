# Ponder map — equation ownership before evolution

The previous audit found most of the observer-relation skeleton but not the calibration reset.
The present question is deliberately prior to computation: do we already possess a lawful engine
that could turn the skeleton into one selected reset, or only drawings, controls, conditional
engines, and readout instruments?

The pure route is to audit all equation families against the same typed input/output contract.
The tempting shortcut is to choose the most time-dependent-looking equation and run it. That
shortcut is forbidden because a time derivative does not establish native dynamics, variation
ownership, R17 compatibility, or reset selection.

No physical value, boundary condition, profile, branch, path, winding, carrier, or action is fixed
by this audit. The candidate universe is characterized, never filtered for a desired result.
