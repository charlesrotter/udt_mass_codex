# P03 in plain language

We now know exactly why the next global map could not honestly be run.

The repository has a large box of well-described parts. It has local clock
geometry, angular geometries, possible ways to close a finite cell, exact rules
for what happens when `dphi` changes causal type, and two complete toy/control
geometries on `S3`.

What it does not yet have is one assembled object containing all the needed
parts at once. The two complete geometries have trivial clocks. The geometry
with the interesting clock is only local. Bolting the interesting local clock
onto a complete angular geometry would be our invention, not something the
metric has yet supplied.

There was also a procedural catch: the P03 preregistration froze the summary of
P02 but not P02's detailed row ledgers. That means we can quote the total of
7,897 locally constructive patterns, but we cannot reconstruct their promised
lossless family map inside P03 without changing the rules after seeing the
evidence. We did not do that.

So P03 stopped at the right gate. It did not fail numerically and it did not
find a no-go theorem. It identified the missing joint very sharply: a complete
finite-cell metric in which the nontrivial clock and angular structure are
already joined and the observer-pair depth comes from that same metric.
