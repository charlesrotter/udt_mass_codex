# G305 lay report

The positive G304 geometry does something important when we stop looking only from one stationary
observer's chart and unfold the whole regular spacetime.

Its apparent maximum-radius surface is not a wall or the edge of the universe. It is that
observer's horizon. The geometry continues smoothly beyond it, and each complete spatial instant
has the shape of a compact three-sphere, `S3`.

That matters for the old Hopfion work. Previously, we obtained an `S3` domain by putting the field
in a finite box and forcing its outer boundary to one value. In this positive geometry, the metric
itself supplies `S3`; no artificial outer boundary is needed. An explicit nontrivial Hopf pattern
can live on it, and its topological integer remains unchanged while the three-sphere grows and
shrinks through the regular time-dependent description.

The zero and negative standard completions instead have noncompact spatial slices shaped like
`R3`. They need an added condition at infinity before the same Hopf classification applies.

This is a real bridge and a reduction of scaffolding, but not yet a matter theory. The metric has
supplied the stage, not the actor or its equation of motion. It has not yet selected a physical
`S2`-valued field or target, supplied the old action, proved dynamical stability, created mass, fixed the
remaining curvature scale, selected a physical history, or determined physical `X_max`.

In one sentence: the positive sector naturally provides the compact space the old Hopfion needed,
but whether UDT actually populates that space with a stable physical Hopf field remains open.
