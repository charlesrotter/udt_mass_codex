## Three episodes

### 1. NGD1/TDS1/TPS1: broader numerical freedom under an admitted comparison law

**Question and input.** NGD1 explored nonlinear geometry with previously frozen
degrees of freedom released, using conditional Ric=0. TDS1 expanded the spatial
implementation; TPS1 ran a bounded survey. Their work orders already excluded
native equation selection. Sources: [NGD1 plan](../udt_gpu_time_live_discovery_2026-09-30/PLAN.md),
[TDS1 scope](../udt_three_spatial_smoke_2026-10-01/WORK_ORDER.md),
[TPS1 scope](../udt_time_live_production_survey_2026-10-01/WORK_ORDER.md),
[central R12N/R12T](../UDT_DEVELOPMENT.md#r12n).

**Actual learning.** Executable conditional metric histories, clock readouts and
wider explored freedom are retained gains. Failed numerical qualification and
bounded repairs remain part of the evidence. They do not identify the physical
response or make the supplied equation native.

**Then-known decision point.** At dispatch, the law's conditional provenance was
already explicit. A successor could therefore say whether it was investing in a
comparison tool or testing a particular native discriminator. The restricted
sign identity found during NGD1, and later failed numerical checks, were not
known reasons to reject the original investigation.

**Replay decision.** Continue authorized tool construction if its intended use
warrants the effort. Before a larger survey, name which unknown the extra domain
or accuracy could settle. If the intended claim is law selection, adding more
solutions of the same supplied law does not change that provenance. The proposed
workflow would make that allocation decision explicit; it does not condemn the
completed survey or require algebra instead of numerical discovery.

### 2. CPR1: a real conditional restriction with an open physical attribution

**Question and input.** Could existing commitments restrict CGE1's supplied
exterior? The fixed query used a circular source, finite-energy outward receiver
and actual connecting rays, within a conditional metric/equation. Sources:
[work order](../udt_candidate_commitment_restriction_2026-10-04/WORK_ORDER.md),
[decision](../udt_candidate_commitment_restriction_2026-10-04/DECISION_BRIEF.md),
[central R8CPR](../UDT_DEVELOPMENT.md#r8cpr).

**Actual learning.** That query's static-region clock ratio is bounded. Its
particular escaping divergent-clock continuation requires positive Lambda with
the other hypotheses retained. This is a genuine restriction on that proposed
realization. It does not establish that the owner postulate requires this
experiment or supplies its geometry. The physical attachment remains open.

**Then-known decision point.** The work order already separated realizing a
qualitative asymptote from identifying the additional positional effect. That
separation was available before the positive-sign result; its result was not.

**Replay decision.** Perform the bounded restriction test. At return, report
parameter restriction and physical attribution separately. Select a successor
only after explaining what could change the latter or why the former warrants a
specific further use. CPR1's real decision brief already recommended an
operational attachment investigation. ROD1 would improve how its value is weighed
against alternatives, not retroactively manufacture a missing caveat.

### 3. TSI1/FRI1/PIA1: inference precision and access to the required regime

**Question and input.** TSI1 supplied ideal timing-scale information; FRI1 asked
about finite noisy records, with explicitly synthetic histories and bounds.
PIA1 then tested physical admission in three clock/source protocol classes.
Sources: [TSI1 return](../udt_timing_scale_identifiability_2026-10-05/DECISION_BRIEF.md),
[FRI1 work order](../udt_finite_record_scale_test_2026-10-05/WORK_ORDER.md),
[initial domain](../udt_finite_record_scale_test_2026-10-05/INITIAL_CANDIDATE.md),
[PIA1 return](../udt_physical_clock_interface_audit_2026-10-05/DECISION_BRIEF.md),
[central R16FRI/R16PIA](../UDT_DEVELOPMENT.md#r16fri).

**Actual learning.** FRI1 retained a finite conditional enclosure and a short-record
factor-two ambiguity. PIA1 found no complete physical admission among the three
reviewed protocol classes and exposed the selected tail's total paired-clock
ratio greater than 54,180. Its measurement/error clarifications remain useful.
The synthetic 1.902% interval width is not an instrument precision forecast.

**Then-known decision point.** TSI1 had already separated ideal access from
practical observations. Once FRI1 chose its domain, its implied observable regime
could have been inspected before expanding precision studies. The numerical
threshold is a later PIA1 result: treating it as already known at FRI1 launch
would be hindsight. This retrospective does not rerun its derivation.

**Replay decision.** Keep the inverse problem if conditional inference is the
objective. If experimental usefulness is the objective, bring a small domain/
observable-admission screen forward. A moderate-regime successor can address
applicability within the supplied metric, but does not thereby select a UDT law.
Charles's current direction prioritizes research-process design before choosing
that successor. Its old recommendation is retained as history, not authorization.

## Prior methods that prevent a false novelty claim

[TC0](../udt_native_theory_research_plan_2026-09-13/execution/tc0/RESULT.md)
already compared candidate implications with earlier arguments and stopped when
none passed its bounded novelty test. That was a useful operational return,
not evidence of complete-postulate insufficiency.
[PCW1](../udt_physical_connection_whiteboard_2026-09-30/WORK_RECORD.md)
already used independent proposals, peer challenge and separate review.
[CPA1](../UDT_DEVELOPMENT.md#r16cpa) already distinguished real conditional
mathematics from the remaining physical admission and redirected generic branch
expansion. These are positive precedents, not additional failed episodes.

The proposed difference is a connected mandate with independent question
formation, visible allocation and continuation decisions, and a compact record
of what would justify reopening a route. Whether it improves discovery remains
untested. No route above is scientifically regraded; no old source is rewritten.
The three selected episodes do not quantify all research productivity or show
that procedural changes alone can close the physical gap.
