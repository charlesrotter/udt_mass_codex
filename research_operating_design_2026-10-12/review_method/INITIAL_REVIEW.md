## Initial report

The proposed team structure is mostly a relabeling unless it changes research
selection and continuation. TC0 already combined independent candidate
formation, comparison with previous arguments, a fresh challenger, and an
early stop. PCW1 already used three fresh proposers, frozen proposals, peer
challenges, coordinator synthesis, and two fresh reviewers. See
[TC0's return](../../udt_native_theory_research_plan_2026-09-13/execution/tc0/RESULT.md)
sections Question and actual investigation, Candidate screen, and Return;
and [PCW1's execution record](../../udt_physical_connection_whiteboard_2026-09-30/WORK_RECORD.md)
paragraphs beginning Three actual fresh proposing contexts and Two actual fresh
direct reviewers.

The real change should be a bounded research cycle that gives agents
responsibility for choosing informative questions, compares those questions
before substantial work, and measures progress by changed understanding or
decisions.

A minimal contract could work as follows:

1. **Authorize an objective and question family once.** The short charter
   states the scientific objective, existing premises, permitted exploratory
   hypotheses, methods, budget, protected exclusions, return point, and maximum
   conclusion. It explicitly includes question refinement, changing approaches,
   counterexamples, unsuccessful investigations, review, and bounded repair.
   These permissions largely exist already; the practical improvement is using
   them across several connected attempts without presenting each small
   continuation as a new permission request. New physical premises remain
   outside accepted dependencies. A charter may expressly authorize
   investigation of a named family of unadopted physical hypotheses; otherwise
   agents may propose such hypotheses but must return before making them
   load-bearing. [AGENTS bounded discovery](../../AGENTS.md) already provides
   this foundation.

2. **Let the explorers formulate questions independently.** Give both the
   objective, authority packet, budget, and relevant prior-work pointers. Do
   not initially give them the coordinator's preferred question, hypothesis,
   or anticipated answer. Each returns one short proposal identifying the
   unknown, closest previous attempt, substantive difference, first informative
   probe, possible useful adverse result, and assumption boundary. Differentiate
   their approaches through actual sources, arguments, or methods, rather than
   cosmetic role names. Reveal and compare their proposals only after these
   initial records exist.

3. **Choose by information value, with reasons visible.** The coordinator
   selects or combines work by explaining which uncertainty a probe could
   reduce and how its possible outcomes would affect the next decision. A
   proposal need not promise a native field equation, closed-form result, or
   positive finding. Improving an operational comparison, discovering a
   restricted obstruction, testing a numerical regime, and identifying an
   assumption-sensitive result can each be worthwhile. Record the strongest
   unchosen proposal and why it lost priority; preserve dissent. The
   coordinator's decision is an allocation judgment, not a scientific verdict.

4. **Run short probes before committing the remaining budget.** Explorers may
   revise conjectures, change permitted methods, and pursue a discovered lead
   within the charter. After a meaningful probe, record four things: what
   changed, what remains unresolved, what continuation could discriminate, and
   whether that continuation is still authorized. This is not an obligatory
   document after every calculation. A speculative approach may continue when
   the next step has a plausible informative outcome despite uncertain success.
   Stop spending on a route when the proposed continuation merely repeats the
   same premise-to-result relation and supplies no new domain, quantifier,
   diagnostic, or concrete reuse.

5. **Use the fresh skeptic on the strongest surviving claim and the continuation
   decision.** After the explorers finish, use a genuinely fresh context. It
   should reconstruct the central argument, examine assumptions and prior
   attempts, identify counterexamples or missing interfaces, and distinguish
   mathematical progress from progress toward the stated physical objective.
   Agreement is not a vote that establishes truth. Objections must name a
   defect or decision-relevant uncertainty; unfamiliarity or lack of the
   desired appearance is insufficient. Existing bounded repair and re-review
   authority applies.

6. **Return one compact research decision.** Report the strongest justified
   result, failed routes, unresolved objections, changes to the objective's
   dependency chain, remaining resources, and proposed continuation or stop.
   Retain reusable tools with their exact limits. For parked routes, record a
   reopening condition such as a new premise authorization, independently
   justified application, changed source, stronger method, or concrete
   discriminating idea. Use the existing central development and work record;
   do not create another maintained scientific summary.

A retrospective desk replay supports these distinctions, without showing that
this workflow would have produced discoveries:

- **TC0:** It correctly stops a bounded attempt that only recovers existing
  distinctions. Its candidate table already performs much of the proposed
  comparison. The updated contract should preserve that stop while avoiding
  "no source implication found" becoming "native closure is impossible."
  TC0 explicitly preserves that boundary.
  [Source](../../udt_native_theory_research_plan_2026-09-13/execution/tc0/RESULT.md).
- **PCW1:** It passes the hypothesis-exploration requirement and proves that
  fresh proposer teams are not the missing innovation. Its proposal review
  remains valuable without adopting the proposals. The new process must add a
  clearer reason to choose one discriminating investigation over another.
  [Central account, R18P](../../UDT_DEVELOPMENT.md#r18p).
- **CPA1:** It passes the useful-negative-outcome test: preserve the conditional
  gains, recognize the unchanged upstream physical connection, and redirect
  generic branch expansion. Reopening remains possible for an independently
  justified use. This is the clearest existing model of direction correction.
  [Decision brief](../../udt_completion_progress_audit_2026-10-04/DECISION_BRIEF.md).
- **NGD1:** It must remain eligible despite offering conditional numerical
  discovery rather than immediate native selection. Its explicit testbed,
  retained failures, actual clock readouts, and finite scope show why a
  "derive first" rule would be too narrow.
  [Plan](../../udt_gpu_time_live_discovery_2026-09-30/PLAN.md).

The adversarial concerns are substantial. Numeric novelty quotas reward
cosmetic differences and discourage useful confirmation or integration.
Premature demands for closed-form derivation suppress numerical,
observational, relational, and global approaches already permitted by the
roadmap. Consensus among related models can amplify shared assumptions;
separate contexts help with exposure but do not establish different-model
independence. A coordinator can bias both question formation and final
synthesis, so initial proposals and dissent must survive its selection.
Documentation can consume the research budget; use one charter, compact
decision entries, and the existing evidence structure, with checks proportional
to actual changes.

The strongest evaluation criterion is therefore qualitative but concrete:
**did this cycle change a scientific inference, narrow an uncertainty, expose
an assumption, enable a justified use, or provide a defensible reason to stop?**
Count neither reports nor passed checks as scientific convergence. That
distinction is already required by
[R16CPA](../../UDT_DEVELOPMENT.md#r16cpa) and the
[roadmap's next-question guidance](../../UDT_RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md).
