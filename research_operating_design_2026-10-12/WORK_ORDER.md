# ROD1 — research operating design

Authorized by Charles: “Proceed with the first deliverable. And decide if it may
not be better to start using ChatGPT desktop for Linux or continue in this
Codes/CLI terminal window. Same LLM models.” The preceding proposal was a concrete
operating plan, tested against several past episodes before another investigation.

Question: can a small semi-autonomous team improve question choice, continuity,
and decisions about further work without weakening UDT's scientific controls?
Compare desktop and CLI for this repository, holding model choice constant.

Scope is process design and source-relative retrospective assessment. Read the
central development and exact decision/work-order sources for three episodes:
numerical conditional exploration, CPR1's candidate restriction, and FRI1/PIA1's
measurement/interface chain. Check prior TC0/PCW1 methods to avoid claiming an old
workflow is new. This is an exposed retrospective, not a blinded experiment or a
reproof of those results. Existing scientific grades, hypotheses and failures stand.

Methods: bounded source inspection; two actual separate-context contributions;
parent synthesis; both contributors' direct adversarial review of the plan and
its historical support; at most two bounded repair rounds. A fresh final reviewer
may replace a contributor if unresolved self-review concerns require it. Record
contexts, exposure, checks and omissions. Same-model review is not cross-model
or human review. Product comparison uses current official OpenAI documentation
and minimal read-only local environment inspection.

Resources: parent plus at most three concurrent agents, at most four child
contexts total; no GPU, new solver, scientific numerical experiment, API service,
installation, OS upgrade or unattended scheduler. Existing CPU repository guards
may run under the existing capture utility's 2 GiB/one-BLAS-thread limits, without
a wall timeout. No multi-hour production is authorized. Stop discovery once the
three episodes, prior-method comparison and tool recommendation are resolved;
stop after a reviewed plan or a concrete unresolved design objection.

Deliverable: UDT_RESEARCH_OPERATING_PLAN.md, a compact retrospective and review
record here, and current operational routing. UDT_DEVELOPMENT.md remains the sole
scientific argument. No new scientific summary, premise, equation, fitted profile,
registry grade or CANON edit. No protected payload inspection, hashing or staging.
Preserve all 76 pre-existing untracked paths by names only.

Maximum conclusion: a reviewable process proposal with specified prospective
evaluation and a supported interface recommendation. Retrospective usefulness
does not establish that the process causes breakthroughs or saves a measured
amount of effort. Completion authorizes neither its research pilot nor desktop
installation. Banking this design preserves it; it does not adopt physics.
