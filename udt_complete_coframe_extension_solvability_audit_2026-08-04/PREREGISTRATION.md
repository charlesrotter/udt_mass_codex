## Whole question

Does metric-native global extension or solvability promote the already-derived partial
completion/join correspondence into a nonidentity relation on complete coframe interiors, without
presupposing the still-open native response, action, source, carrier, boundary law, or physical
completion?

This is a metric-led structural audit. It is not a search for a desired action, particle, mass,
cosmology, or unique universe.

## Bounded domain

The audit covers the current smooth fixed-rank complete-coframe architecture and its registered
completion/monodromy data. It classifies exactly these operation families:

1. two-sided coframe descent and cocycle compatibility;
2. extension of the positive screen metric and base-screen mixing over a supplied reciprocal split;
3. existence of a single global coframe;
4. coordinate-integrability of a coframe;
5. torsion-free metric-compatible Cartan connection reconstruction;
6. Cartan/Bianchi identities;
7. parallel-section or reduced-holonomy conditions;
8. bulk differential-equation solvability;
9. boundary-value/Fredholm solvability; and
10. rank-changing or stratified extension.

No unregistered operation may be silently used to close the result. The audit may identify an
operation as outside the bounded smooth tile; it may not interpret absence from the tile as a
physical negative.

## Circularity test

An operation counts as an independently supplied metric-native return only if it:

- is defined from the current metric/coframe/completion data without loading a native response or
  action as an input;
- has a nonidentity zero/admissibility set on otherwise supplied complete configurations;
- is invariant under presentation gauge, cover, and refinement;
- owns the complete interior and every boundary/global-modulus channel in its stated domain; and
- does not obtain its conclusion by renaming a chosen equation's solution set “solvability.”

If an operation first requires a differential operator, source, boundary polarization, connection
parallelism postulate, or action that the operation is meant to derive, it is classified
`CIRCULAR_PARENT_LAW_REQUIRED` or `CONDITIONAL_EXTRA_PREMISE`, not metric-native closure.

- `H1`: ordinary bundle extension supplies a nonidentity complete-interior return.
- `H2`: ordinary bundle extension is an existence/compatibility construction and is nonselecting.
- `H3`: a genuine topology or holonomy obstruction supplies a partial nonidentity return on a
