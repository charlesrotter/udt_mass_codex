## Two censuses

1. **Full exposure census:** every tracked text artifact containing `phi`, `dphi`, `depth`, or the
   Greek `φ`, including historical and frozen records. Generated records cannot select themselves.
2. **Active load-bearing universe:** exposed artifacts first introduced on or after 2026-07-01 that
   are not archive/reorganization records, plus current controls, current-frontier targets, and the
   exact 35-source founding-ownership chain regardless of introduction date.

The full census prevents silent omission. Individual regrade is required for every active
load-bearing artifact. Historical and frozen artifacts are classified but not rewritten.

## Frozen classification outcomes

Each active artifact receives exactly one primary disposition from `CLASSIFICATION_SCHEMA.tsv`.
Secondary dependency flags are recorded separately. The audit may not create a favorable new class
after reading outcomes.

`REDERIVATION_REQUIRED` is reserved for mathematics whose truth depends on treating pointwise
`phi` as an independently physical scalar, a universally owned section, or a universally valid
endpoint-difference law. A calculation performed with an explicitly supplied `phi` profile is not
invalid merely for being conditional.
