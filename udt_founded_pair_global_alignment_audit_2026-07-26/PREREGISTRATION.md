## Whole question

The founded reciprocal construction uses the dimension-matched covector pair

```text
q=(c_E dt, dr)
```

and, under its declared local Lorentzian readout, writes the transformed pair as the physical
clock/ruler coframe with reciprocal generator `H=diag(-1,+1)`. The immediately preceding calibrated
readout audit proved that this aligned causal readout cannot also admit a metric-isometric exchange
of the two reciprocal eigenlines. A genuinely different mixed metric/operator pair can admit such
an exchange.

This audit asks exactly:

> Does current UDT authority make the aligned clock/ruler pair an intrinsic local physical object,
> does it force that pair to be one global rank-two subbundle, or can a mixed two-channel readout be
> obtained merely by completing or changing the coframe without replacing a founding premise?

## Bounded regime

The calculation covers:

1. every real symmetric nondegenerate two-channel metric paired with the founded generator;
2. every passive invertible two-channel basis change, transforming metric and generator together;
3. every real symmetric four-dimensional Lorentz metric whose restriction to one supplied,
   nondegenerate founded pair is the calibrated pair metric;
4. every metric-self-adjoint complete reciprocal generator that leaves that supplied pair invariant;
5. arbitrary complementary-screen coordinates, including nonzero pair/screen cross terms; and
6. the distinction between an invariant-subbundle extension and an extension that preserves only a
   projection, compression, or quotient.

It does not construct a global pair field, choose an observer/path assignment, select a screen
response, or solve a field equation.

## Falsification and certification contract

The aligned local interpretation fails if a simultaneous change of basis converts the complete
metric/operator pair into a genuinely inequivalent mixed family while preserving all pair
invariants, or if the founding source does not in fact assign its transformed pair to the local
physical metric readout.

The mixed-readout-as-gauge interpretation fails if the invariant of the simultaneous
metric/operator pair differs, or if a basis change was applied to the metric while leaving the
operator artificially fixed.

The invariant-extension theorem fails if an exact Lorentzian counterexample exists in which a
nondegenerate invariant pair of a metric-self-adjoint generator has a non-invariant metric
orthogonal complement.

The maximum positive conclusion is a local/type-theoretic classification. A global alignment may
be declared derived only if current sources supply an observer/path assignment and compatible
transport, not merely a local adapted formula.
