# Lay report

We have separated three things that had been getting tangled together.

First, in the original UDT construction, the two reciprocal channels really are the local physical
clock and ruler once the recorded metric readout is applied. That is not just a convenient choice of
coordinates. The metric and reciprocal operation together identify which line is clocklike and
which is rulerlike.

Second, a complicated four-dimensional metric can still have clock-angle or ruler-angle cross
terms in its coordinates. Those terms do not automatically mean that the clock and ruler have
become mixtures of each other. Locally, one can choose the rest of the frame differently and remove
those cross terms while leaving the founded clock/ruler pair untouched. Globally, curvature and
holonomy may prevent one such choice from working everywhere, so the global problem remains real.

Third, the earlier “mixed readout” is mathematically valid but is not the same UDT clock/ruler pair
written in a different basis. It changes the relationship between the metric and reciprocity. Under
the current premises it is therefore a counterfactual alternative, not an active continuation of
the founded metric.

The good news is that the local meaning is no longer missing. The remaining gap is how every local
observer-pair comparison is carried and reconciled across the whole finite-cell geometry. The
angular sector may participate in that transport, but it no longer has to manufacture the local
clock/ruler identity from scratch.

This does not select the angular response, `lambda`, a global branch, an action, matter, density, or
`X_max`.
