# 100,000-foot overview and route map

## The shape that can honestly be banked

At the highest level, UDT currently looks like a theory with four visible layers and one missing
joint.

1. **Relational core.** Observer comparison has an exact reciprocal clock/ruler character. `phi`
   measures that logarithmic imbalance; `c_E` supplies an observed calibration.
2. **Geometric orchestra.** The reciprocal pair sits inside a larger coframe with angular area,
   shear, mixing, causal strata, connections and global joins. These parts interact and form a
   coherent configuration space rather than a random list of scalars.
3. **Conditional structures.** Different response and completion choices produce plural
   mass-bearing geometries, stable/unstable sectors, projector motifs and one conditionally stable
   3D Hopf model.
4. **Global architecture.** Completion data constrain local joins, and a global/local bootstrap
   relation could in principle tune local admissibility to the complete universe.

The missing joint is the rule that turns the allowed orchestra into played music: which complete
configurations are physical histories, how the whole constrains the local parts, what may vary, and
which disturbances persist.

This is a stronger and more encouraging position than “we need a definition of `phi`.” It is weaker
than a complete physical theory.

## What is probably not the missing piece

- another definition of `phi`;
- one universal pointwise reciprocal-plane selector;
- another composition or cocycle identity;
- a demand that one branch win before a law exists;
- a longer census of mathematical homes;
- a density scan before native mass and return variables are defined;
- blind adoption of EH, Bach or the Hopf action; or
- completion of C08 merely because it is computationally difficult.

Those routes have either been answered within scope or shown to require an upstream premise.

## The most plausible missing category

The evidence repeatedly points to a **realization principle**, not another local kinematic identity.
In plain language:

> A physically realized UDT universe may be a complete metric history whose global completion and
> local structures mutually satisfy one observer-natural consistency rule; persistent localized
> structures inside such histories are what we call matter.

This sentence is a synthesis, not an adopted postulate. It joins the stability and bootstrap
hypotheses without pretending to supply their equation. It also explains why the action might be
downstream: if a native response one-form is first derived, an action exists only if that response
passes integrability, gauge, boundary and global-period tests.

## Deduction and induction should now have different jobs

### Deductive lane

Deduction should protect the theory's spine:

- start with the founded reciprocal pair and the full coframe domain;
- demand observer/frame naturality and metric ownership;
- state a proposed realization/response principle at the weakest useful level;
- derive its allowed mathematical form and exact implications;
- test boundary, global, variation and causal-stratum consistency; and
- reject any candidate that imports the answer or collapses to a tautological readout.

### Inductive lane

Induction should compare consequences across the already banked families:

- Which candidate law keeps or removes F01, F02 and the projector families?
- Does it supply the two response components required by the F01 threshold surface?
- Does it own the F02 jet stiffness and its global seam law?
- Does it select or replace the Hopf projector/carrier and determine the `L2/L4` ratio?
- Does it produce a nontrivial global/local return rather than simply recompute outputs?
- Can completed survivors retain the conditional SNe readout?

These are cross-branch falsifiers. They must not be used to tune a law until it produces a desired
particle or cosmology.

## Three genuinely different closure routes

### Route A — derive a metric-native response on the full coframe space

Ask whether a covariant response/constraint one-form can be constructed from the founded pair,
complete coframe, connection/curvature and global completion data without assuming an action. This
is the strongest purely deductive route. It should use sparse exterior-form and bundle structure,
not one globally expanded polynomial system.

Failure condition: every candidate is either a geometric identity, depends on an unowned reduction,
or requires a new premise. A bounded failure would justify moving to Route B.

### Route B — adopt one explicit provisional realization principle

The smallest current candidate is the global/local mutual-admissibility principle typed by the
bootstrap audits. Adoption would not mean adopting a formula, optimizer, unique solution, density
value or action. It would authorize deriving and falsifying possible metric-native relations with
nontrivial dependence on both complete geometry and its global readouts.

This route needs Charles's explicit epistemic ruling because the current record classifies
bootstrap as a distinct coherent posit, not a consequence of Reciprocity or finiteness.

### Route C — a controlled GR-principle comparison

Survey the role played in GR by broad selector principles—diffeomorphism covariance, local
equivalence, conservation identities, locality, derivative order, initial-value well-posedness and
boundary differentiability—without importing Einstein's equation. Classify each as already present,
compatible, modified, rejected or genuinely optional in UDT.

The purpose is to discover a missing *kind of premise*, not to borrow GR dynamics. Observer
Reciprocity already covers part of covariance; adopting gravity/acceleration equivalence or a
derivative-order condition would be a new provisional premise and must be tested against UDT's
extreme-scale and plural-branch structure.

## Recommended balance

The next scientific move should be a short **premise-and-architecture decision**, not another large
solve:

1. freeze the exact realization/response requirements already implied by the branch evidence;
2. perform one bounded Route-A search across the complete coframe objects, with a preregistered stop
   after the distinct natural operator classes are exhausted;
3. if no nonidentity response survives, ask Charles whether to adopt the minimal Route-B principle
   as a `WORKING POSIT`;
4. use Route C only to audit candidate principle classes and consistency, never as affirmative UDT
   physics; and
5. only then return to F01/F02/Hopfion and numerical solution-space exploration as simultaneous
   cross-branch tests.

This restores balance. Deduction owns provenance and mathematical necessity. Induction owns pattern
recognition and falsification across plural branches. Neither is permitted to manufacture the
missing law from a preferred answer.

## When computation becomes justified again

- Resume C08 exact elimination only if its global curvature classification distinguishes candidate
  response laws or complete branches.
- Begin time-live ODE/PDE work only after the evolved variables, response, constraints and boundary
  posture are stated.
- Begin density/bootstrap scanning only after native global observables and a noncircular return
  relation exist.
- Use GPU continuation only after those equations and certification gates are frozen.

Compute duration then remains an engineering variable. Scientific progress is measured by domain
coverage, exact implications, branch events, residuals and falsification—not by whether an arbitrary
wall-clock limit was met.
