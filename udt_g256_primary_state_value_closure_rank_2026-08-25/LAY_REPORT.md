# G256 lay report

We tested whether all the gears we have already uncovered force the next note of the UDT score.
They do not—yet.

The good news is that the gears really interlock. If the reciprocal depth \(\phi\) and the two
angular responses are known at one distance, the metric lets us recover the local slope and
curvature of \(\phi\) exactly. The orchestra is one machine, not separate effects bolted together.

But this is like examining a musical score one measure at a time. The instruments tell us exactly
what chord corresponds to the notes written in that measure; they do not, from the equations tested
here, force which notes must be written in the next measure. Adding more observers adds more values
to learn: after one reference is fixed, \(N\) observers still carry \(N-1\) free depth values.

So we have not found a finite predictive SNe/BAO model in G256, and we should not fit observations
yet. We have sharply localized the remaining bridge: a genuinely native relation must propagate
the primary metric state from one separation/event to another. Until that relation is found, there
is no honest ODE or GPU solve to run.

A fresh external reviewer accepted this bounded conclusion after the sealed replay was made fully
dependency-free. That strengthens the result as an evidence package; it does not turn the missing
value-propagation relation into a theorem or authorize an observational fit of a free function.
