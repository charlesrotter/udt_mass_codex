# G120 lay report

We previously knew the supernova curve worked, but part of that curve was represented by a screen
shape we had supplied. G119 removed that extra screen: in the spherical observer problem, the
metric itself says the apparent transverse size is simply the areal radius `R`.

UDT still lacks a native theory of light. We therefore inserted one clearly labelled temporary
bridge: carried light is conserved and its energy changes with its measured frequency. With that
bridge, the old luminosity formula becomes simply

```text
observed luminosity distance = frequency factor squared times areal radius.
```

Both Pantheon+ and DES then reproduce their frozen results exactly, without tuning anything. The
old P1 screen is now just one empirically measured candidate curve for how `R` changes with the
observed frequency coordinate on the catalog's outward, redshifted orientation. It must not be
continued unchanged into blueshifted `Z<1`, where it would give an impossible negative radius.

This is simpler and less scaffolded, but it is not yet the whole spacetime. We still need to learn
whether global co-present observer consistency, Reciprocity, composition, and light-cone causality
force this radius curve to sit inside a particular complete metric history.
