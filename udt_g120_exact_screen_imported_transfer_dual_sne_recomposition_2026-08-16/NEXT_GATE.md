# Next gate after G120 — co-present reciprocal and causal history consistency

## Candidate principle to test, not assume

> A physical history is a complete metric configuration whose entire network of co-present
> observer-pair relations is globally reciprocal, composable, and mutually compatible with the
> metric's causal/light-cone structure.

The next program must decide whether this is a nonidentity restriction on histories or merely a
restatement of correct geometric bookkeeping.

## Correct typed network

For one supplied complete metric `g`, time orientation, typed observer-query domain, and each
regular branch, assemble:

- additive or endpoint-descended reciprocal/frequency channels where actually derived;
- full Jacobi phase carriers `(D,nabla_K D)` rather than inverse-only screens;
- orthogonal screen transport and any path-labelled holonomy;
- middle-observer calibration carry;
- the metric null cones and causal admissibility of each declared ray/surface query.

For triples `A,B,C`, test reversal and composition only on matched branches and middle states.
Do not demand scalar path independence where curvature legitimately produces holonomy.

## Decisive question

Does simultaneous satisfaction for all overlapping observer queries exclude at least one smooth,
regular, otherwise admissible complete metric history?

- If yes, identify the exact covariant differential/global condition and the first excluded witness.
- If no, classify co-present consistency as an identity/evaluator condition rather than a history
  selector, and locate the next genuinely nonidentity premise.

## Role of causality

Light cones are metric-derived admissibility structure. They may constrain which observer events,
branches, and compositions coexist, but they must not be confused with a native material-light or
signalling law. Co-presence means membership in one complete solution; it does not erase null-cone
typing or force instantaneous local transport.

## Boundaries

Do not use the G120 SNe curve to manufacture the condition. Do not import GR field equations,
Lambda-CDM evolution, a preferred congruence, transparent transfer, `X_max`, bootstrap, an action,
or a desired branch. G120 may later act only as an observational compatibility anchor after a
candidate nonidentity condition is independently derived.

## Recommended bounded first calculation

Construct the exact three-observer overlap equations for one generic central spherical time-live
history using frequency ratios, terminal pair blocks, Jacobi phase transfer, and null-cone typing.
Reduce them symbolically. Preregister two inequivalent smooth history witnesses before reduction.
The test succeeds as a history selector only if the residual vanishes for one witness and not the
other for a reason not equivalent to a definition, gauge choice, regularity condition, or supplied
query mismatch.
