and test stability of one realized configuration, or does it supply only formal compatibility and
conditional stability tests whose common on-shell closure remains missing?

The audit separates three notions that may not be merged:

1. `GEOMETRIC_PERSISTENCE`: preservation of metric/coframe admissibility under an allowed
   one-parameter family. This is kinematic unless a native evolution law selects the family.
2. `ENERGETIC_OR_SPECTRAL_STABILITY`: positivity/minimality for a specified functional and
   perturbation domain. This is conditional wherever an action, carrier, boundary, or norm is
   supplied rather than derived.
3. `BOOTSTRAP_SELF_CONSISTENCY`: a global background admits persistent local structures whose total
   response reproduces that background. A fixed-point schema is not a derived fixed-point map.

angular module embeddings must be realized by the same configuration, equation set, boundary data,
and premise stack. Formal pointwise module containment alone does not pass this gate.
