**PASS.** `STABILITY_REQUIREMENT_MATRIX.tsv` contains 17 complete rows split as 4 geometric, 8

- geometric persistence supplies only an admissible path unless a law selects it;
- energetic/spectral stability depends on a functional or response generator, a realized

- bootstrap persistence depends on a global-to-local arrow, a realized local solution, a
  local-to-global arrow, a fixed point, and a stability structure for that fixed point.
