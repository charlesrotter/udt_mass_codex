# Completeness map — UDT stability foundations

This is a whole-frame census, not a claim that every UDT solution has been enumerated.

1. **Fields and configuration objects.** The founded reciprocal depth and registered metric/coframe
   and P4 response alphabets are included. Complete physical field ownership remains open. The
   round-`S2` matter carrier is a `POSIT`, not a derived section of the celestial fiber.

2. **Action or response law.** Reduced P4 seam/cell functionals and the particle `L2+L4` functional
   enter only as conditional comparison slices. The complete native action, native response law,
   source, and mass remain open. No action is adopted by this audit.

3. **Equations and on-shell realization.** Formal static, time-live, and angular module embeddings
   are retained. A single fixed field satisfying all modules with nonzero time-live and angular-live
   sectors, one native equation, and one boundary problem has not been constructed. A purely static
   shared mode does not pass that live gate. Formal compatibility and realized coexistence are
   separated.

4. **Variation and perturbation domain.** P4 postures, pairings, parities, wall germs, and reduced
   perturbations remain explicit and branch-dependent. No complete native tangent space or gauge
   quotient is selected. No direction is excluded because it spoils a desired result.

5. **Boundary and finite-cell completion.** Static seal parity and registered finite-cell postures
   are included. Differentiable boundary/corner completion, physical wall response, and free
   higher-germ data remain open. Finite-box results are not promoted to infinite-volume claims.

6. **Topology and carrier.** The existing implementation is full 3D Hopf-capable and the
   conditional topology identities are retained. The conditional celestial `S2` fiber does not
   derive a fixed carrier, section, transport, action, or physical boundary.

7. **Dynamics and time-live scope.** P4 pointwise time-live response slots are included as formal
   modules. Physical evolution, integration-layer closure, well-posedness, and time persistence are
   open. A relaxation trajectory is not treated as physical time evolution.

8. **Branches and regimes.** All cited P4 census, pairing, posture, reading, and wall-germ branches
   keep their original scopes. The audit does not select a massive, stable, particle-like, macro, or
   micro branch. `c_E` and `G_obs` remain observational anchors; no values enter this audit.

9. **Stability.** Geometric persistence, energetic/spectral stability, and bootstrap persistence are
   separately typed in all 17 requirement rows. Exact conditional P4 and Hopfion results remain
   intact. Native whole-solution stability remains open because realization and persistence joins
   are missing.

10. **Global/bootstrap closure.** The global-to-local map, selected local solution, local-to-global
    response, fixed point, linearization, and stability norm are individually censused. The
    two-arrow schema and conditional density quotient variation are retained without turning the
    working bootstrap interpretation into an equation or optimizer.

## Scope ceiling

The map supports `FOUNDATIONS_PARTIAL_MINIMAL_JOIN_IDENTIFIED` and the current operational status
`CONDITIONAL_STABILITY_ONLY`. It does not support a native stability solve, T4, action or carrier
selection, matter emergence, or a bootstrap parameter-selection theorem.
