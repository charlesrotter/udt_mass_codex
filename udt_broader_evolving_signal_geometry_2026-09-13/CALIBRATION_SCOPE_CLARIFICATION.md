Write c_j=cos(k_j xi_A+phi_j). Equation(8) remains exactly valid. For fixed known
phases and modes, the amplitude calibration is the linear map

 (a_1,...,a_m) -> Q_A=sum c_j a_j.

If at least one c_j is nonzero, its rank is one and each compatible fixed-Q_A
level is an affine set of dimension m-1. In particular the two-mode family
leaves one amplitude direction ONLY under (c_1,c_2)!=(0,0).

If all c_j=0, its rank is zero. Q_A=0 is then automatic and leaves all m
amplitudes free; a nonzero supplied target Q_A has no compatible member.

phi_j=pi/2-k_j xi_A. Both coefficients vanish exactly. Thus the original
unqualified one-direction assertion is false even with known phases.
