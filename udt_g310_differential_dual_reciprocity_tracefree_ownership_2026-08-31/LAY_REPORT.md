# G310 lay report

We found a clean candidate for the missing bridge.

Ordinary Dual Reciprocity tells us that a clock/ruler pair changes shape in a perfectly balanced
way: one side tightens while the other loosens, without changing their combined scale. When every
possible pair orientation is included, those changes cover all nine ways the shape of a
four-dimensional metric can change while leaving its overall size alone.

G310 asks for one additional rule: the physical curvature response must be balanced against every
one of those reciprocal shape changes. The math is exact. A response satisfying all those balances
can only point in the one untouched direction—the common scale. Within the carefully classified
G301 arena, that statement is exactly the trace-free Ricci equation.

Conditional on that proposed rule and G301's explicitly chosen operator arena, the equation then
leaves one curvature scale while removing the arbitrary history curve. This gives a direct
mathematical connection to the determinant-preserving character of Dual Reciprocity; it does not
derive the proposed rule from the older postulates.

But the new balance rule is still a proposed postulate. The original postulates describe reciprocal
comparisons; they do not say that curvature must be stationary against every reciprocal change.
Calling the new rule “differential Dual Reciprocity” does not make it already derived. The honest
result is therefore: we may have found a particularly economical missing postulate, and within the
conditional G301 arena it produces the promising trace-free law exactly. It does not yet choose a
particular history or scale; Charles has not adopted it, and the narrow repair follow-up is still
needed.

The first outside review agreed that the bounded mathematics works. It found two shortcomings in
how the evidence was demonstrated, not in the result: the scripts used half of the displayed
tangent (which has the same span), and the independent script stated the final one-dimensional
answer instead of deriving it from its own matrix. Both have now been repaired. A narrow external
follow-up still has to verify those repairs.
