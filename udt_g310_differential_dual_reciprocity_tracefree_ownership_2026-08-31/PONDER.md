# G310 ponder

G309 makes another attempt to squeeze a field equation out of the existing compatibility identities
circular: a smooth counterfamily already satisfies those identities while violating G301. The useful
question is therefore not “can more algebra rename the same identities?” but “what is the smallest
new physical statement that joins reciprocal shape to curvature response?”

Dual Reciprocity supplies determinant-preserving pair deformations. Across every pair orientation,
they cover nine metric-shape directions and omit precisely the common scale. A balance law against
all nine directions would naturally constrain shape while allowing one scalar curvature datum to
survive. That is the exact structural signature of G301's exceptional trace-free class.

The danger is verbal smuggling: calling the balance “Dual Reciprocity” does not derive it. G310 must
keep the new differential statement visible, prove the full-plane span rather than use one radial
pair, catch the scalar-only vacuous case, and show that the common scale remains free.
