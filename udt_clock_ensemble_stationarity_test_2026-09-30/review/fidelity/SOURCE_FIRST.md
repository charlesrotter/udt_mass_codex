## Strongest physical objections and their disposition

1. **An operational population is not a native response law.** The ensemble is
   a collection of repeated ideal experiments, not a gravitating material
   distribution. Its count weights, laboratory, rapidity interval and response
   identification remain supplied. Writing C[g] suppresses these additional
   arguments. Diffeomorphism covariance with the apparatus carried does not
   make C a natural functional of the metric alone or establish off-shell
   conservation. W4 and DDR do not supply these choices.

2. **A geometry-dependent measure cannot be discarded.** Here the abstract
   label measure is fixed by definition. In physical query space its pushforward
   changes with the arrival/ray map Q_g. Integration in labels legitimately
   accounts for that dependence through the full derivative of Z[g,Q_g]. A
   second separately invented measure term would double-count this coordinate
   description; holding the pushforward fixed would describe a different
   experiment. This does not claim every ensemble measure is metric-independent.

3. **Fixed rapidity must be physical.** The flat preparation neighborhood makes
   eta and L exactly the same physical quantities for all epsilon in this
   witness. The earlier constant-metric shortcut changed their proper meanings.
   This calculation does not repeat that defect. For a future *full* functional
   under variations changing g at p, the continuation of the chosen unit
   laboratory frame must also be specified. That completion is not needed to
   refute stationarity on the present subset of variations, which leave g near p
   unchanged, and it cannot be used to claim metric-only naturality already.

4. **The central laboratory is not a preferred cosmic center.** Its event/frame
   is supplied query data and moves under coordinate changes. The shell is a
   compact test-function support about that laboratory. No X_max, material wall,
   physical cutoff, universal frame or finite cosmic boundary appears. Isotropy
   is only in that laboratory frame; no finite uniform all-boost distribution is
   asserted.

5. **A test strain is not an actual field solution.** The smooth shell metric
   is Lorentzian, has temporal t, is exactly Minkowski near the center and
   outside compact spacetime support, and has an exactly determinant-minus-one
   t-r block. Its derivative is f H(partial_t,partial_r), including G310's
   factor two, with zero Minkowski trace. Angular directions are retained.
   Radial coordinate singularities do not affect the tensor because f vanishes
   near r=0. The candidate tests arbitrary compact reciprocal variations;
   that admits this direction without asserting that g_epsilon solves UDT or
   GR. Restricting after failure to variations satisfying a new field equation
   would change the tested law. Conversely, this probe cannot prove a realized
   UDT solution has the derived response.

6. **Nonstationarity is not local-SR failure.** Baseline received ticking is
   exactly the ordinary SR receding-clock result, Z0=exp(eta). Perturbed clocks
   remain ordinary proper clocks and the metric retains local Lorentz form.
   Local SR does not imply extremality of an arbitrarily proposed global
   contrast functional. The nonzero derivative rejects the conjunction of
   this functional/preparation and its exact Minkowski-stationarity demand.
   It does not reject W4, owner-provisional DDR or UDT. Exact flat stationarity
   is a declared diagnostic, not a criterion already derived from filter-only
   GR. Controlled recovery, observed tolerances and empirical compatibility
   have not been tested here.

7. **No locality theorem follows.** A finite received observable can depend on
   an interior ray while the physical field equation is local. Averaging a
   continuum may change support, but proves neither a smooth four-volume
   response nor dependence on a finite local metric jet. Local Metric
   Sufficiency concerns the identified local physical response, which remains
   open. This benchmark failure ends the authorized next locality test for

   response architectures.
