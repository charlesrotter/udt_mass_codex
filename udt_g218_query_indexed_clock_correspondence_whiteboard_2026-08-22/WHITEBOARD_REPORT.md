## 1. The simpler object

Let `gamma_A` and `gamma_B` be supplied future-timelike observer curves with metric proper-clock
coordinates `tau_A` and `tau_B`. A typed comparison query `Q` supplies or derives a regular
correspondence

\[
R_Q\subset\gamma_A\times\gamma_B.
\]

Locally write the correspondence as

\[
F_Q(\tau_A,\tau_B)=0,
\qquad \partial_BF_Q\ne0.
\]

The implicit-function theorem gives one local clock map and its positive slope:

\[
\frac{d\tau_B}{d\tau_A}
=-\frac{\partial_AF_Q}{\partial_BF_Q}>0.
\]

Under the G176/G216/G217 premises,

\[
\boxed{
\delta_Q
=-\log\frac{d\tau_B}{d\tau_A}
}
\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
\boxed{
\frac{d\tau_B}{d\tau_A}=e^{-\delta_Q}.
}
\]

Therefore event incidence, depth, and the positive first jet are not three scalar mechanisms. They
are the graph, logarithmic coordinate, and derivative of one observer correspondence. This
compresses the description without selecting the physical correspondence.
