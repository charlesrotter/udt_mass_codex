The proposed rule “the extra directional area correction never increases in
any prepared laboratory” is exactly as strong as the previous two clock/optical
conditions together. That is a clearer physical proposal, not a derivation from
the founding interpretation. If imposed on total spacetime, it removes the Weyl tides
needed for Schwarzschild recovery and fails that precise recovery test. If imposed only
on an additional contribution, we still need an actual physical comparison that
identifies that contribution.

Averaging the area measures scalar curvature, but a scalar reading does not tell
us what caused it. A concrete geometry has positive scalar curvature while its
static clocks exchange unchanged ticks. The stronger checked statement concerns
all finite frames' leading coefficients and nonnegative averaging; it does not
rule out other measurements or an additional contribution canceled in the net
reading. Requiring the scalar-subtracted part to obey Bianchi on its own under the same
physical metric connection is another optional law and gives less than Einstein
geometry.

tools; do not adopt the universal total-area law or identify scalar curvature

budget. More scalar tomography or a larger arbitrary-profile survey would not
resolve this assignment by itself.
