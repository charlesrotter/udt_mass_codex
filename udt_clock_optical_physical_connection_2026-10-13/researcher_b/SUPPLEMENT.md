## What the area is

S_l is the codimension-two EDGE where two null cones intersect. It is not a
maximal-area slice of a null cone, not the maximal-volume spacelike filling,
and not a geodesic sphere of proper radial length l. l is exactly half the
central freely falling clock's round-trip proper duration. At a regular edge,
the two independent future null normals span a timelike normal plane; a suitable
normalized positive sum gives a local measuring observer orthogonal to the edge.
Proper local screen/ruler measurements in that rest frame read its induced area.
This describes ideal query data permitted by W4, not guaranteed naturally
available instruments or a physical population. A frequency-only record cannot
substitute for proper area and source angular calibration.

The recovery B3 determines the leading source-normalized null Ricci contraction;
it does not reconstruct an entire finite affine parameter along a distant ray.
Acceleration, imperfect relay delay, uncertain local rulers, finite sampling and
finite-l remainder all require their own controls before real data use. No such
finite accuracy is claimed here. If normalized-area error is epsilon(l), its
contribution to the coefficient scales as epsilon(l)/l^2, in addition to O(l).

## Total versus additional RA1: a physical rejection

**RA1-total, separate UNADOPTED trial:** set D=A_g, without any reference and
demand nonpositive TOTAL directional radar-area coefficient in every laboratory.
This is a tempting attempt to eliminate reference choice. The same B6 argument
and GRC1's algebraic rigidity force A_g=kappa B_g with kappa>=0. On a smooth
connected region, Ric=3kappa g and Scal=12kappa; contracted Bianchi gives
3d kappa=6d kappa, hence kappa is constant. All Weyl curvature vanishes.

Thus the universal exact RA1-total proposal cannot recover a nonzero Schwarzschild
exterior in C^2 on a common regular region: Weyl is continuous in the metric2-jet,
zero for every member of that proposed class, and nonzero for Schwarzschild away
from infinity. This is a scoped rejection of that proposed universal optical law,
not of UDT, actual positive/negative finite observations or a contrast law.

RA1-CONTRAST avoids that particular algebraic exclusion because equal reference
and physical Weyl may survive. Its physical reference and attribution then remain
essential. This makes the physical tradeoff concrete: removing the reference
from the STRONG directional hypothesis discards ordinary tidal geometry; keeping
only total/mean area preserves more geometry but loses the strong clock implication.
The ultrastatic control already proves the latter cannot by itself mean received
slowing. These are distinct outcomes, not an exhaustive menu of possible UDT laws.

## Finite witnesses behind the boost argument

The proof uses limits of extracted coefficients but violations need not require
an infinite boost. If e<0 and rho<0, any exp(2eta)>4|e|/|rho| makes
a_eta=(4e-exp(2eta)rho)/6 positive. If e>0 and rho>0, choose
exp(2eta)<4e/rho instead. Zero/other sign cases are immediate. Each is a finite
laboratory frame followed by its own sufficiently small-l experiment; no uniform
finite-l validity across unbounded rapidities is inferred. Measurement error or
a fixed minimum physical laboratory size would bound an empirical test, not
establish the exact all-frame law.
