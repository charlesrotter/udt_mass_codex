does not isolate an additional positional physical cause. A total scalar readout
is already available in CMF1; this campaign must not present its reconstruction
as new. The useful additions here are two discriminators:

1. Pointwise null invisibility does not give independent regional freedom. For

   coefficients in every frame and direction. It is therefore not obtainable
   as a positive average of those actual net slowing coefficients, and cannot
   be source-separated merely by calling the algebraic projection positional.

The first supplies a sharp conditional restriction and the second rejects a
concrete proposed identification. Neither establishes whole-theory insufficiency.

It follows that full calibrated local null screens determine A modulo K B.
The null traces Ric(k,k)=S(k,k) likewise determine S uniquely once the tangent
metric and source normalization are supplied. To see the latter without an
inverse theorem, a symmetric tensor annihilating all null vectors has zero

## 3. Regional coupling through one connection

The physical metric's contracted Bianchi identity gives

    ∇^a S_ab=(1/4)∂_b R_scalar=3∂_b K.                 (A2)

Thus, if g and its Levi-Civita connection are independently known, ideal
spatially resolved calibrated null Ricci data determine the gradient of K.
On a connected region one may write

    K(q)−K(p)=(1/3)∫_p^q (div_g S)_b dx^b.             (A3)

The one-form must be exact for data arising from the stated geometry. This is a
consistency condition, not a new field equation. Null optical data ALONE do not
provide the metric connection or its derivative calibration, and pointwise
samples do not provide a derivative. Finite noisy use needs independent metric,
transport, sampling and derivative-error control. No empirical inversion claim
or sufficiency theorem for arbitrary candidate S is made. Even compatible ideal
data leave one additive scalar constant unless a scalar value is supplied.

The full differential Bianchi residual of A_vis is minus the cyclic covariant
derivative of K B, since ∇B=0 and A obeys Bianchi. Consequently

    A_vis satisfies differential Bianchi under ∇g
        if and only if dK=0.                           (A4)

Necessity follows by contraction: A_vis has Ricci S and zero scalar, so its
Bianchi identity would give div S=0; combine with (A2). Sufficiency follows
directly from ∇(K B)=0 when K is constant. This establishes only a tensor
identity. It does not assert that A_vis is the Riemann tensor of another metric.
Indeed the same g has a unique Levi-Civita curvature; treating A_vis as that
curvature would force K=0. A different reference metric has a different
connection, exactly the issue already controlled by PCC1's regional matching
caveat; its Bianchi identity cannot be substituted into (A4).

**AUTONOMOUS-COMPLEMENT, separately UNADOPTED trial:** demand that the
scalar-free optical complement be independently Bianchi-closed using ∇g. Its
maximum consequence is constant scalar curvature (and a sign only if separately
required). This is a genuine proposed restriction, not implied by one geometry,
no preferred observer, metric null propagation or ordinary clocks.

Constant R is not the Einstein condition. On the supplied regular comparison
g=−dt²+sinh(2t)(dx²+dy²+dz²), t>0, in free units, direct original-metric
calculation gives R=12 and S00=3/sinh²(2t)≠0. This control was chosen solely
to test that implication; it is not a preferred profile or UDT scale. More
generally, for flat-spatial FLRW and y=a², R=3y''/y, so constant R=12K gives
y''=4K y; choosing the positive sinh branch merely makes nonzero S explicit.

Accordingly, demanding independent scalar closure would supply a new physical
choice while still leaving trace-free Ricci and Weyl freedom. It cannot be
advertised as a derivation of the Einstein family or automatic GR recovery.

## 4. Concrete physical-attribution failures

### 4.1 Ordinary GR trace enters the same extracted scalar

Use the Einstein equation ONLY as a familiar comparison assumption,

    Ric−(R_scalar/2)g+Λg=κ_GR T.

Its trace gives R_scalar=4Λ−κ_GR tr(T), hence K=Λ/3−κ_GR tr(T)/12.
For a perfect-fluid comparison, tr(T)=−ρ+3p. Thus the local null-invisible
scalar includes ordinary traceful gravitational curvature. This does not import
matter into UDT, require a native T, or establish that UDT rejects GR matter
sectors. It refutes the general inference that an extracted scalar is thereby
an additional contribution beyond whatever ordinary GR curvature is present.

The standard flat-spatial dust comparison a=t^(2/3), t>0, verifies the point
without relying on a Friedmann substitution: original-metric differentiation
gives

    G_ab=diag(4/(3t²),0,0,0),
    K=1/(9t²)>0,  M(∂t)=−2/(9t²)<0,
    N(∂t)=4/(3t²),  div S=(−2/(3t³),0,0,0)=dR/4.

K>0 therefore need not mean that even this ordinary prepared clock mean slows.
The GR interpretation is comparison provenance, not a new UDT source law.
The scalar value itself does not tell how much to label positional and how much
to attribute to the comparison's matter trace or Λ.

### 4.2 Stronger metric-only control: no frame has a positive leading net effect

After the first attribution failure, test another explicit proposal:
**POSITIVE-AVERAGE, UNADOPTED trial:** isolate the positive positional clock
contribution by a nonnegative average of actual prepared net clock coefficients
over frames/directions, with no reference metric. Such averaging would avoid
counterfactual subtraction if it worked.

Use CMF1's already published off-equation ultrastatic control

    g=−dt²+h_b,   h_b has3D sectional curvature b>0.

Ric00=0, Ricij=2b δij and R_scalar=6b, so K=b/2>0. For any unit timelike
U=(sqrt(1+w²),w,0,0), obtained without loss by spatial rotation, choose the
orthonormal rest basis e1'=(w,sqrt(1+w²),0,0), e2,e3. A unit perpendicular n
has the form n=a e1'+b2 e2+b3 e3, a²+b2²+b3²=1. The spatial space-form
curvature gives

    A(n,U,U,n)=b[|U_sp|²|n_sp|²−(U_sp·n_sp)²]
              =b w²(b2²+b3²),
    C(U,n)=−b w²(b2²+b3²)≤0,
    M(U)=−(2b/3)w²≤0.                                  (A5)

This covers every finite frame and direction by spatial isotropy and rest-frame

This does NOT refute a positive additional algebraic component canceled by a

## 5. Actual return and next informative decision

The original ambition of extracting an additional physical cause merely by
projecting onto the null-invisible scalar is not established. The geometry fixes
the total projection, not the identity of a source or the counterfactual
ordinary contribution. Requiring the projection to be independently closed
would add constant-R physics and can fail in ordinary traceful comparison
regimes; its compatibility must be scoped before adoption, not silently inferred.

What survives is a useful separation of tests:

- The metric readout K is reference-free at the ideal local algebraic level;
  CMF1 already supplies a prepared-clock route. Measurement/calibration remains
  a real operational burden, not an additional native field equation.
- The regional relation (A2) tests consistency of a proposed independently
  evolving scalar contribution with its optically visible Ricci complement.
  Arbitrary scalar variation cannot be added while preserving that complement
  and the same metric connection's Bianchi identity.
- The ultrastatic all-frame sign control rejects positive averaging as a way
  to turn K into observed additional slowing. Ordinary GR traceful controls
  prevent calling it a clean subtraction of all GR contributions.

The next informative step inside this campaign is fresh challenge of (A4)/(A5)
and the actual-readout construction from researcher B. A better optical
calibration can validate a measurement but cannot by itself repair this
attribution. Further attractive scalar metrics or an extra solver run would
not resolve the issue. A proposed physical replacement must explain why its
selected comparison isolates positional physics while admitting the concrete
controls, rather than add a scalar-free-reference or separate-Bianchi convention
without physical justification. This return rejects named local identifications;
it does not demand a new postulate or exhaust finite/global UDT possibilities.
