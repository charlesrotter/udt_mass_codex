
5. global persistence on every registered finite-cell family.

The audit must determine whether any of those requirements selects
`lambda`, whether different requirements select incompatible values, or
whether selection remains branch-conditional.

6. Separate centralizer descent from line-normalizer/twisted descent.
7. Cross all twelve finite-cell families with restricted holonomy, discrete
   monodromy, singularity, seam, and path-multiplicity requirements.
