# Completeness map

## Covered

- All seven corrected parent-atlas family partitions.
- Twelve existence/stability/completion/readiness cells for every family.
- Separate computation-readiness and derivation-queue axes.
- Exact source and premise closure for the single F01 CPU candidate.
- Controls against survivor, time, bootstrap, carrier, and GPU promotion.

## Not covered

- Unregistered configuration families.
- A new lambda-Schur calculation or wall-germ derivation.
- Joint-realization, native-time, boundary, action, response, carrier, source, or bootstrap closure.
- A common stability operator, particle spectrum, physical mass, or observational identification.

## Quantifier ceiling

`SURVIVOR_MAP_COMPLETE_WITH_CPU_CANDIDATE` means exactly one conditional algebraic cell can be
separately preregistered for a CPU certification attempt. It does not mean a family is a particle,
fully stable, time-persistent, physically completed, or selected by the universe.
