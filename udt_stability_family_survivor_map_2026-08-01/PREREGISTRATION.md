## Whole question

For each of the seven configuration/evidence families frozen by the cross-family atlas, what is the
current evidence-backed state of existence, conditional survival, stability coverage, and missing
closure? Which missing cells, if any, are sufficiently specified for a later bounded computation?

This is a CPU-only evidence map. It does not search parameter space, solve an equation, or select a
family resembling a desired particle.

## Frame and scope

- **Order:** `MAP -> OBSERVE -> PONDER -> DERIVE`.
- **Orientation:** metric-led family census, not Hopfion-led and not a particle template.
- **Bounded object:** exactly the seven disjoint effective family partitions already verified by the
  parent atlas.
- **Source universe:** the parent's effective 1,469-source freeze plus all 44 tracked files in the
  complete parent atlas package at this base; expected exact union **1,513 paths**.
- **Excluded:** no ODE/PDE, relaxation, GPU, T4, fitting, new carrier, action, response, source,
  boundary, mass law, time equation, bootstrap law, or observational identification.

## Frozen cell universe

Every family receives exactly one row for each of the twelve cells in `CELL_UNIVERSE.tsv`:

1. configuration/existence;
2. stationary or realized background;
3. response/functional ownership;
4. boundary/completion ownership;
5. perturbation-domain ownership;
6. existence pruning;
7. static/sector stability;
8. full energetic or spectral certificate;
9. physical time persistence;
10. physical finite-cell completion;
11. bootstrap membership/selection;
12. computation readiness.

`empty`, `unstable`, `nonisolated`, `sector-stable`, `static finite-box stable`, and `time-persistent`
must remain different states. A missing cell is not a negative physical result.

## Readiness contract

The audit may use only the labels in `READINESS_LABELS.tsv`. In particular:

- `CPU_EXACT_CHECK_READY` requires an already owned exact object, response, domain, and bounded open
  algebraic cell; it does not authorize the computation.
- `CPU_BOUNDED_SOLVE_READY` requires an already owned equation, background/configuration domain,
  boundary, residual gate, and bounded parameter range.
- `GPU_READY` additionally requires demonstrated CPU anchors, an owned high-dimensional numerical
  object, memory/output/timeout controls, and a stated independent validation route.
- If any physical equation, carrier, response, boundary, perturbation domain, or fixed realization
  would have to be invented, the family is blocked rather than ready.

Readiness is operational, not scientific merit. No family may be promoted because it is visually
particle-like, historically interesting, or likely to give a favorable result.

## Certification and falsification

The verifier must reject:

1. a missing or duplicate family/cell row;
2. overlap between the corrected F01/F05/F06 partitions;
3. transfer of a P4 response to the Hopfion or vice versa;
4. promotion of a massless control, structural ring, or empty family to a survivor;
5. promotion of sector/static stability to time persistence;
6. promotion of the round `S2` carrier, `L2+L4`, a physical boundary, or bootstrap selection;
7. `CPU_BOUNDED_SOLVE_READY` without all five ownership prerequisites;
8. `GPU_READY` without every CPU and GPU prerequisite;
9. treating a formal module as a realized solution;
10. ranking by hoped-for physics rather than closure and verification cost;
11. omitting an unresolved blocker or premise;
12. using a source outside the frozen 1,513-path universe.

## Fixed outcomes

Exactly one overall outcome must be returned:

- `SURVIVOR_MAP_COMPLETE_NO_COMPUTATION_READY`
- `SURVIVOR_MAP_COMPLETE_WITH_CPU_CANDIDATE`
- `SURVIVOR_MAP_COMPLETE_WITH_GPU_CANDIDATE`
- `FAMILY_EVIDENCE_CONFLICT`
- `SOURCE_OR_SCOPE_BROKEN`

## Maximum conclusion

The audit may produce a complete present-tense survivor ledger, blockers, dependency closure, and a
ranked recommendation for at most three later tests. It may not run those tests or derive a particle
catalogue, common stability operator, time persistence, bootstrap selection, matter, or mass.

Banking requires deterministic primary verification, a fresh cold adversarial review of the
load-bearing readiness ruling, the repository gates, and explicit reporting of all four evidence
gates. No navigation update or `grok` integration is authorized.
