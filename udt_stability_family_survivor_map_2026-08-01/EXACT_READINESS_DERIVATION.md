# Exact readiness derivation

## Two independent axes

For each family `F`, define:

```text
R_compute(F) = whether a bounded computation already owns its object, law, domain, boundary,
               certification gate, and validation route.

Q_derive(F)  = whether a missing prerequisite is itself a legitimate metric-led derivation object.
```

`R_compute(F)=blocked` does not imply `Q_derive(F)=abandoned`. The two axes answer different
questions and are recorded separately in `READINESS_LEDGER.tsv` and `DEVELOPMENT_QUEUE.tsv`.

## F01 readiness

The registered P4 S-i crease branches already own:

- a conditional stationary background and exact reduced/joint second variation;
- the supplied free-versus-odd angular parity domains;
- the `ell=1` chosen normalization and root equation
  `F(s)=integral_-1^1 log(w_s(x)) dx=0` on `s in (1,3)`, where
  `w_s(x)=(s^2/2)x^2+(s^2-s)x+1+s^2/2-s`;
- the germ-Hessian-flat witness branch on which the lambda-Schur block is defined;
- a prior free-`f/h`-trace joint Galerkin index result that is explicitly corroboration, not
  certification and supplies no evidence for the supplied-odd zero-`f/h`-trace branch.

The sources establish at least one massive root but not uniqueness, so a later check must isolate
every root in the registered interval. The unresolved lambda-Schur sign is still a bounded
scalar/index question on an existing conditional object. A rigorous root/integral enclosure can be preregistered without choosing a new
field equation, carrier, boundary, or stability functional. That meets `CPU_EXACT_CHECK_READY`.

Its conclusion is local to the named single-cell Schur block and branch; it does not automatically
extend to a whole mixed chain. The free second-wall-germ curvature is not part of that readiness ruling. It is unpinned by the
registered N2 layer and prevents the lambda result from becoming a full certificate. It remains in
development queue Q02.

## Why no other family is computation-ready

- F02's existing exact inequality closes its registered sector. Any stronger question changes the
  object to a joint realized solution, which is not owned.
- F03 is deliberately a degenerate control.
- F04's static finite-box operator cannot be promoted to physical time evolution; the time equation
  and physical boundary are missing.
- F05 has no stability response or perturbation domain.
- F06 is empty in the exact registered massive scope.
- F07 lacks the realized field on which a stability operator would act.

Thus the counts `CPU_EXACT_CHECK_READY=1`, `CPU_BOUNDED_SOLVE_READY=0`, and `GPU_READY=0` follow from
ownership closure rather than a judgment about which family is interesting.
