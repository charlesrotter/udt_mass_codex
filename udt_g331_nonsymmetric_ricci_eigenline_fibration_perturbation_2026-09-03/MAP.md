# G331 map — nonsymmetric Ricci-eigenline and Hopf-fibration perturbation boundary

Date: 2026-09-03
Mode: metric-led bounded classification

## Whole question

G330 found two properties on supplied non-round Berger-`S3` data:

1. the spatial Ricci tensor has a simple, metric-defined eigenline; and
2. Berger symmetry makes the line's leaves the closed Hopf circles.

G331 asks whether these are equally robust under genuinely nonhomogeneous, nonsymmetric smooth
metric perturbations. It separates four questions which must not be conflated:

- does a uniform simple Ricci eigengap preserve a smooth global line;
- does the topology of `S3` permit a global unit representative of that unoriented line;
- do the line's integral curves remain closed circles forming a fibration;
- what, if anything, carries conditionally through local evolution under the active provisional
  vacuum response equation?

This is an observing question. It does not require the perturbation to produce a particle, a stable
lump, a selected universe, or the historical fixed-target Hopfion.

## Pure frame and bounded tile

The pure full problem would construct and classify the entire nearby nonsymmetric solution set of
the Einstein constraint equations and then follow every maximal development. That is a separate
constraint-manifold and nonlinear-evolution program.

This bounded tile instead does two things:

1. classifies the geometry of every sufficiently `C2`-close smooth spatial metric whose Ricci
   spectral cluster remains uniformly separated; and
2. applies the result conditionally to every constraint-compatible Cauchy datum in that
   neighborhood and its smooth local development.

An explicit compactly supported conformal bump is a geometric witness that tests whether the old
Berger fibre direction is forced to remain an eigenline. It is not silently classified as a
constraint-compatible physical history.

## Method route

1. Put the Ricci endomorphisms of nearby metrics on one fixed inner-product bundle and apply the
   finite-dimensional spectral-gap estimate pointwise, uniformly on compact `S3`.
2. Construct the continued rank-one spectral projector and audit its frame covariance and
   smoothness.
3. Use `H1(S3;Z2)=0` only as an imported topology theorem to distinguish a global line from a
   globally signed vector representative.
4. Apply the exact three-dimensional conformal Ricci transformation to a nonhomogeneous bump with
   a vertical-horizontal Hessian entry at one point.
5. Use an arbitrarily close nonvanishing flow on `S3` with irrational generic torus slopes as a
   logical control: closeness and line-bundle topology alone do not imply closed leaves. This
   control is not claimed to be a Ricci eigenline of the bumped metric.
6. State the conditional short-time consequence of smooth local Cauchy evolution while the uniform
   eigengap remains open.

## Maximum possible conclusion

At most G331 may establish that the metric-native Ricci eigenline is structurally open under a
uniform `C2` eigengap condition, while the closed Hopf-fibration property and G330's period-normalized
integer require additional orbit closure or retained symmetry. It may not establish a complete
constraint-data census, energetic or nonlinear long-time stability, global topology conservation,
physical occupancy, matter/mass, scale, physical `X_max`, or canon.
