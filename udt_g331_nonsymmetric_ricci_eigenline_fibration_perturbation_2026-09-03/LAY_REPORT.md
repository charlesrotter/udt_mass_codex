# G331 lay report

G330 found a direction written into the curvature of a squashed three-sphere. Because that special
space is highly orderly, following the direction takes you around closed Hopf circles.

G331 separates those two facts.

The direction itself is robust: make any sufficiently small smooth change to the metric, and—as
long as the relevant curvature eigenvalue remains separated from the others—a unique nearby
direction still exists everywhere. On `S3` it can be represented by a global arrow, although the
metric does not choose which sign the arrow has.

The closed circles are less robust. We constructed explicit smooth metrics arbitrarily close to
the squashed sphere. Their Ricci curvature still selects one exact direction, but for an irrational
choice of two nearby weights, most paths following it wind forever on a torus and never close.
So a curvature-selected line is more durable than a Hopf circle fibration.

That also limits G330's integer. Its normalization used the common length of the closed fibres. If
most curves do not close, that common length does not exist, so the same normalized integer cannot
be claimed. This does not prove that every topological quantity disappears; it says a different
quantity would need a new definition and audit.

For actual time evolution, the result is conditional: any independently valid vacuum initial data
in the small gap-open neighborhood keeps the smooth line for a short time. We have not shown that
the explicit irregular examples satisfy the vacuum constraints, and we have not shown energetic or
long-time stability.

The practical result is useful and narrowing: G330's metric-native **direction** survives broadly,
but its closed Hopf **fibration** is a special additional structure. No old carrier or action was
restored, and no universe, matter sector, mass, scale, or `X_max` was selected.
