# G308 completeness map

## Covered

- every point of the positive G305 standard `R x S3` completion;
- every finite global time and every `X>0`;
- both G306 chiral components;
- both G307 members reconstructed from an arbitrary regular supplied directed germ;
- pair reversal;
- every oriented orthonormal transverse screen;
- full `O(4)` versus orientation-preserving `SO(4)` metric equivalence;
- smooth connected topology-preserving time carry;
- metric and light-cone noninterference.

## Not covered

- nonspherical complete metrics;
- nontrivial quotients or disconnected domains;
- singularities, cuts, caustics, rank loss, boundaries, or topology change;
- arbitrary locally varying non-Hopf fields;
- a physical population of germs, routes, screens, or members;
- field equations, actions, stability, matter, mass, observations, scale selection, or physical
  `X_max`.

## Negative-result scope

If both sectors are coherent, the negative conclusion is only that the bounded unoriented metric
and its causal structure do not select between them. It is not a no-go against parity-sensitive
dynamics, supplied oriented data, global quotients, or observational selection.
