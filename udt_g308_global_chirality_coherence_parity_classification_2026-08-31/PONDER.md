# G308 bounded ponder

The two G307 members agree on the supplied route plane and differ only on its transverse oriented
screen. There are three distinct questions that must not be conflated:

1. **Existence:** does each local member extend to a smooth global field?
2. **Coherence:** can a connected regular family change chirality from place to place?
3. **Selection:** does the unoriented metric prefer one globally coherent sector?

Round `S3` already admits both left- and right-handed global Hopf fields. This makes one-sided
failure unlikely but does not settle the time-live or query-preserving equivalence questions. The
decisive tests are therefore:

- extend both candidates through the exact G305 product slicing;
- compute whether a reflection fixing the directed route plane conjugates one member to the other;
- separate full `O(4)` metric equivalence from orientation-preserving `SO(4)` equivalence;
- test whether pair reversal changes chirality or only fiber orientation;
- prove or refute that a continuous regular chirality label is constant on a connected domain;
- verify that both candidates leave the same spacetime metric and light cones.

If both candidates pass, choosing positive rather than negative signed helicity would be a new
orientation/parity premise unless another active UDT structure supplies the sign.
