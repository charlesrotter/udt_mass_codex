# G277 map — observational scale-anchor ownership

Date: 2026-08-26

## Whole question

G275 leaves one positive constant homothety scale after a complete dimensionless metric history and
regular relation family are supplied. G276 proves that one independent calibrated proper-clock
record on the exact modeled segment can fix that scale. G277 asks a narrower, outcome-blind
question:

> Do the already held Pantheon+ calibrator fields, the DES-Dovekie release, or the previously typed
> `cmb_temp` object contain an independent datum of the right mathematical type to attach that one
> scale without changing the UDT metric or reciprocal kernel?

This is an ownership and identifiability census. It is not a fit and does not ask which candidate
gives the desired value.

## Metric-led frame

The metric/kernel remain fixed. On a supplied dimensionless history,

```text
g_ell = ell^2 g_bar,  ell > 0.
```

An eligible direct anchor must be an independently calibrated observation attached to the same
modeled geometric object and must transform with a known nonzero power of `ell`. A candidate that
requires a supplied detector, source, luminosity, or operational-distance bridge is classified as
conditional rather than native. A candidate with a free catalog offset or weight zero is not an
absolute-scale anchor.

## Candidate families

1. Pantheon+ `CEPH_DIST` on rows marked `IS_CALIBRATOR=1`.
2. Pantheon+ `MU_SH0ES` or corrected apparent magnitude without the Cepheid-host attachment.
3. DES-Dovekie `MU`, whose release normalization states `H0=70` and whose global nuisance values
   were obtained from its likelihood analysis.
4. The already typed `cmb_temp` temperature field or a source/receiver temperature ratio.
5. A separately supplied same-segment proper-clock record of the exact G276 type.
6. A separately supplied direct geometric length/Jacobi/area/curvature record of the G250 type.

## Explicit omissions

- no SNe residual, likelihood, chi-square, profile, or coefficient fit;
- no inspection of BOSS outcomes;
- no angular-sector coefficient or post-readout orchestra term;
- no Lambda-CDM distance curve, acoustic ruler, `H0` import, or standard-ruler interpretation;
- no source temperature, emission spectrum, native radiative-transfer law, or detector law;
- no new history, field equation, action, matter model, operational distance, `X_max`, or global
  completion;
- no protected local work.

## Maximum possible conclusion

G277 may classify a route as direct, conditional-on-explicit-imports, relative-only, or not
scale-typed. It may authorize a later preregistered calibration experiment. It cannot supply a
numerical `ell`, validate a history, modify the kernel, or identify `ell` with `X_max`.
