# G299 lay report

The metric gives us one rich relationship between the observers. A two-dimensional pair plane is
not that whole relationship. We have at least two natural pair planes, and neither contains all the
frame and directional information needed for general composition.

One view keeps the ruler carried from the source. Another uses the ruler measured locally at the
target. Both are legitimate and both return the same reciprocal clock dilation, but they retain
different directional information. Neither view contains the complete path and screen relation by
itself.

The provisional W5 premise says physical normalized pair position retains the **complete**
projective/frame information needed to compose nonradial relations. That blocks us from calling
either bare pair plane the whole physical relation, and no current premise uniquely selects one.

A simple architecture is available: keep the richer relation and treat pair planes as views chosen
by typed questions. But the present premises have not yet defined which such views are lawful. That
architecture is compatible with UDT, not yet derived as the unique kernel domain. The pullback
formula and reciprocal kernel are unchanged.

This substantially narrows the local architecture question without closing it. It also does not
yet tell us which observer relations the universe populates or how the metric history evolves.
