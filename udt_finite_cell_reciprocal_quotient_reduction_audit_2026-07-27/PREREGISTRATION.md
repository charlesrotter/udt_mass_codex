## Whole question

Across every registered complete or completion-structural finite-cell branch, does the complete
metric and its transition data supply:

1. an intrinsic rank-two screen subbundle complementary to the founded clock/ruler pair;
2. an exact finite reciprocal quotient lift on that bundle;
3. a metric-independent-of-frame finite lift despite the screen-rotation fiber found in the parent
   audit;
4. a global screen flag or rotation law; and
5. a path-independent/parallel quotient reduction compatible with finite-cell transitions and
   holonomy?

These are five different gates. A pointwise screen projector is not a screen flag; a global screen
bundle is not a parallel subbundle; an isotropic screen metric is not an endpoint-independent
transport law; and a global coframe supplied by an ansatz is not automatically a metric-selected
physical section.

## Observing rather than targeting

The audit freezes all registered concrete witnesses, controls, degenerations, killing strata, and
twelve completion classes before constructing new outputs. It will not select the twisted `S3`
family because it is the most developed, discard incomplete/degenerate branches, demand a Hopf or
particle interpretation, or treat full holonomy as failure. Every branch is characterized by the
structure it actually carries.

## Bounded source universe

## Maximum conclusion

At most this audit may derive a branchwise global screen/quotient reduction, prove exact finite
metric descent through the parent screen-rotation fiber on a stated stratum, and classify whether
metric transport preserves or mixes that reduction. It may not promote a configuration to an
on-shell UDT solution, choose `lambda`, a screen flag, observer/path semantics, completion,
variation domain, action, source, carrier, boundary law, bootstrap closure, density, `X_max`, mass,
or dynamics.
