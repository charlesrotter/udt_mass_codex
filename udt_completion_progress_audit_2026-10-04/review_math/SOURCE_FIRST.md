checked rather than inferred from bounded smooth motion. First arrival and echo
domains differ. Interior bumps preserve endpoint data while changing history.

justified candidate geometry/preparation. They do not supply such a candidate.

diverge. A nonzero net redshift/pole consequently does not isolate an additional
positional effect. State/query freedom also need not be removed by a universal
law; demanding a unique history or universal population would add an unwarranted
target.

My provisional direction recommendation is to retain these results as reusable
conditional tools and pause extension of the RG asymptotic branch as a claim of
native progress. Further sharpening of H2, generality or asymptotic coefficients
would be additional conditional mathematics, unless a concrete use requires it.
The physical progress target is a specific justified connection from admitted
UDT constraints to a candidate geometry or its class, with the resulting actual
clock query and additional-effect attribution checked. It need not be an Einstein
equation, a unique universe, or a newly adopted premise. CPA1 should report the
inspected RG admission join as open, not fill it by a proposed assumption and
