## 2. Independent logical checks of the load-bearing gains

### FCL1: a velocity assumption genuinely disappears

With g=x^-2 b and a normal collar b=-N^2 dx^2+h, a physical unit clock u=xT
has T^x=-gamma/N and spatial covector w=hT_spatial. The lowered geodesic equation
for P=w/x cancels all explicit x factors on its right side. Dividing by
dx/dtau=-x gamma/N gives w_x=w/x+F, where
F_i=N_i gamma-N h_jk,i T^j T^k/(2gamma). This checks the sign and the
inhomogeneous lapse/spatial-metric forces: no homogeneity has been inserted.
On a compact regular collar gamma is comparable to 1+|w| and
|T_spatial|^2/gamma is O(|w|), so |F|<=C(1+|w|).

For s=log(x0/x), w_s=-w-xF. The norm comparison has damping -|w| with an
integrably decaying perturbation; its integrating factor yields the displayed
O(x[1+log(x0/x)]) estimate from arbitrary finite entry velocity. This derives
T->n rather than putting bounded T into the hypotheses. Integrating y_x=O(x log x)
and subtracting -N(p)/x from tau_x leaves an integrable remainder. These are
substantive new consequences beyond the homogeneous/comoving calculation.

The clock step still needs actual source/branch data. For k=x^2 kbar,
-g(k,u)=x[-b(kbar,T)]. A regular nonzero limiting null K and T->n give a
strictly positive finite B*. Hence Z=1/(xB) diverges for that one receiver.
