# CPA1 lay decision brief

The recent mathematics is real. We proved that free clocks behave as claimed
inside the proposed geometry, connected the behavior to curvature and actual
clock readings, and worked out when it becomes a curve against initial distance.
We also found real limits on those statements. The audit found no false or
circular load-bearing step at those declared scopes.

But the physical starting point is still supplied. The geometry was introduced
openly as a stronger trial condition; the recent calculations have not derived
that condition from UDT's founding commitments. Part of the curve's asymptotic
form comes from that choice, and the distance exponent also depends on how the
clocks are prepared. Correct consequences do not establish why this is the
geometry nature uses or isolate an additional UDT effect.

The fair assessment is useful conditional mathematical progress with an unchanged
upstream physical connection. Treating it as demonstrated convergence on UDT's
field equations or a distinctive cosmological prediction would overstate it.
This does not refute the founding positional interpretation, show the complete
postulates inadequate, or require an added physical premise.

Recommendation: keep the results as tools, and redirect the next physical task
toward a specific existing UDT commitment and a quantitative restriction that
may follow from it. Another generic completion example or another lookup of
this same admission chain would not answer that question. A concrete independent
geometry or observational/operational question could justify using the tools.
A separately chosen provisional model would be a modeling decision, not a
derivation of the missing implication; none is adopted here.

No specific new native selector was found in this bounded audit. The next
discussion should choose a named implication to investigate, a concrete use,
or deliberate retention of this branch without further extension. A unique
universe, every field equation and microscopic light physics are not required
for every useful question. No new research campaign begins automatically.

The central development records the gains, assumption costs and unresolved
connection together. Two fresh reviewers separately reconstructed and attacked
the audit, with source-proof exposure disclosed. Their agreement is evidence
of scrutiny, not physical confirmation. Required repository checks and final
banking are separate operational evidence.
