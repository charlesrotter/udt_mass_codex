# G159 lay report

We have now connected the orchestra to what a supplied calibrated pair reports.

The ten-part coframe score and the observer's own changing measurement setup first combine into one
two-dimensional pair metric and its rate of change. Clock/ruler imbalance, common scale, shift, and
the conditional `c_eff` ratio are then different readouts of that one object. Nothing is bolted on
afterward, and observer motion is not silently frozen.

Rotating or boosting the internal coframe changes the written musical notation but not the pair
metric or what it is doing. Changing the observer's clock/ruler calibration is different: it changes
the numerical notes being reported. Such calibrations must therefore be fixed or carried consistently
between observers.

This closes the local path from a supplied changing complete metric to its terminal pair response.
It still does not tell us which history Nature realizes or what the family parameter physically
means.
