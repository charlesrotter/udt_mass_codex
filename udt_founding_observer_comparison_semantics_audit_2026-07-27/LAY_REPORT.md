# Lay readout

We found the exact line between what UDT already says and what it does not yet say.

UDT already gives a clean rule for comparing two clock/ruler states: if their reciprocal depth is
known, the comparison is fixed, reversing the two reverses the comparison, and comparisons can be
chained. That rule does not need us to draw a route through space first.

But the postulates do not yet tell us how the real universe assigns that depth to two actual
observers. There are two live possibilities:

- only the two observers matter, so every route between them gives the same complete comparison; or
- the route can matter because the angular frame is carried differently around curved geometry.

The mathematics can consistently handle either possibility. The founding words do not choose one.
So we must not use the endpoint version to declare `lambda=1`, and we must not use the path-groupoid
version to declare paths physically real.

In short: **the comparison rule is derived; what physical data you feed into it is still open.**
