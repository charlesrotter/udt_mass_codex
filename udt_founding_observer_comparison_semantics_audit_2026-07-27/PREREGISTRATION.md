## Whole question

Does the currently registered UDT foundation require the reciprocal comparison to be one
parallel, path-independent endpoint endomorphism, require a physical path-labelled observer-pair
arrow, or leave that realization choice open?

This is a source-first semantic implication audit. It does not ask which realization is more useful,
more familiar, or more likely to select `lambda=1`.

## Frozen alternatives

Exactly one primary ruling is allowed:

1. `ENDPOINT_ONLY_DERIVED` — an exact controlling statement forces physical path independence;
2. `PATH_LABELLED_DERIVED` — an exact controlling statement makes the path label constitutive of
   physical reciprocal comparison; or
3. `SEMANTICS_OPEN` — the foundation derives the abstract ordered comparison but does not decide
   whether its complete-metric realization is endpoint-only or path-labelled.

A more precise secondary ruling may distinguish what *is* founded from the unresolved realization,
but may not evade the three-way primary ruling.

## Observing frame

The audit begins with the original reciprocal-c derivation, the frozen Stage-I statement of
positional comparison, current premise precedence, owner corrections, and every current layer that
separates abstract comparison, metric transport, observer-pair depth, path-groupoid assembly, and
parallel endpoint collapse. Later mathematics may clarify logical alternatives but may not rewrite
the earlier postulates.

The terms have fixed types:

- `ordered comparison`: an abstract arrow with source/target roles and additive depth;
- `endpoint-only`: the physical comparison depends only on its ordered endpoints and is independent
  of connecting path, endpoint-frame gauge having been resolved;
- `path-labelled`: two paths with the same endpoints may be distinct physical comparison arrows;
- `metric transport`: Levi-Civita transport of a supplied metric, not the founding dilation itself;
- `abstract composition`: composition through a matched intermediate comparison object; it is not
  silently a spatial path concatenation;
- `covariance`: equality of law under allowed frame changes; it is not path independence.

## Success and falsification contract

`ENDPOINT_ONLY_DERIVED` requires a cited controlling statement that rules out two physically
distinct same-endpoint path arrows, plus a valid implication from the statement to path
independence. Abstract group composition, existence of an endpoint factorization when a global
section is supplied, or mathematical convenience is insufficient.

`PATH_LABELLED_DERIVED` requires a cited controlling statement that makes the connecting path or
its homotopy/transport class part of the physical comparison. The availability of a consistent
path groupoid, curvature, holonomy, or cut-locus path multiplicity is insufficient.

`SEMANTICS_OPEN` is required if the source derives ordered relational comparison but leaves the map
from a complete observer pair to depth, event pairing, path assignment, or endpoint collapse open.
It must survive the strongest charitable readings of difference, composition, reversal, no
preferred observer, and “through an intermediate position.”

All catches in `FALSIFICATION_CONTRACT.tsv` must be exercised. Every frozen source claim and every
semantic route must receive exactly one outcome. Generated audit records may not change the frozen
universes.
