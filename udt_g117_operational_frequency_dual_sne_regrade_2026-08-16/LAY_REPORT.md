# G117 in lay language

The rebuilt kernel did not break the supernova result. Pantheon+ and DES return the same curves and
the same fit numbers without changing a parameter.

The simplification is conceptual but important. The releases give processed redshift coordinates,
rooted in spectral frequency measurements and frame corrections. For this bounded test we place
those coordinates in the frequency slot. We had been treating them as if they also directly told us the terminal
clock/ruler imbalance `phi_pair`. G116 showed that those are equal in the quiet pure-reciprocal
regime, but a live metric can also contain relative drift and optical focusing.

The SNe calculation never needed that extra identification. It needs the measured frequency ratio,
the apparent sky-area response, and the still-conditional light-transfer rule. Once wired that way,
the old P1 curve reappears exactly.

Metaphorically, the same microphone recording survives, but we have stopped claiming that it heard
one instrument in isolation. It heard a conditional release-frequency-and-area chord. This SNe
interface alone cannot tell us how
much of the underlying terminal relation came from positional dilation, live relative drift, or
optical focusing.

This is a real removal of scaffolding. It preserves the macro anchor while making fewer claims than
the observations support. The next useful step is the proposed multiagent whiteboard: inspect the
remaining P1 screen representative, transfer closure, and history parameterization for any other
scaffolding that can be replaced by metric-native relationships.
