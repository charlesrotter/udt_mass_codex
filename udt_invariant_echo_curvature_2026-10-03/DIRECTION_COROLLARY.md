# Opposite preparation directions — direct coefficient corollary

This exposed same-premise consequence of the candidate d4 is separated from its
initial freeze. No additional computation or physical condition is imposed.
Hold o,U fixed and physically prepare the two experiments in directions n and
−n, each at positive L with its own future first reception and later return.
Curvature multilinearity gives C→−C, A→−A, B→B, T→T, V→−V and W→W.
Consequently the two independently measured quartic coefficients satisfy

    d4(n)+d4(−n)=4|V|²,
    d4(n)−d4(−n)=2<V,W>.

Their sum is nonnegative; both can vanish only if V=0, and V=0 indeed makes
both quartic coefficients vanish. This statement is at a fixed event/frame and
direction pair, conditional on the locally symmetric metric and PSW protocol.
It is not an all-frame classification, a complete curvature reconstruction, a
finite-distance equality or a physical UDT law. When V=0 the sixth-order term
remains T|W|²/3; a zero coefficient there need not remove W when T=0.

The analytic signed-L evenness in DERIVATION_COMPLETION is a different operation:
its continued root becomes past-directed when L changes sign. It therefore
does not identify the two future-directed experiments just specified. Existing
positive/negative tilt controls do not by themselves test n→−n; this corollary
is proved by tensor multilinearity, without relabeling those controls.
