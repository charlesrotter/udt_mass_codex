# Mathematical method sources and scope

Inspected primary author/university sources on2026-10-03, after the parent wrote
its exploratory transvection plan. These credit standard mathematical methods;
they supply no UDT physical premise or field equation. The new echo coefficients
are the package's own calculation. No historical physicist endorsement is claimed.

- Daniel Monclair, [Globally and locally symmetric spaces; geometry of symmetric spaces](https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~daniel.monclair/chap4.pdf),
  §§13.2,14.1–14.3, especially Proposition13.2.9, Lemma14.1.2,
  Corollary14.1.4 and Theorem14.3.1. Inspected the Jacobi proof, transvection
  transport/geodesic formulas and bracket sign. These notes state a Riemannian
  framework; DERIVATION_COMPLETION rederives the needed local facts using
  indefinite metric Jacobi fields and local group splitting. No positive-
  definite global theorem or classification is transplanted.
- Ines Kath and Martin Olbrich, [The classification problem for pseudo-Riemannian symmetric spaces](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0612101),
  introductory symmetric-triple/transvection discussion. Consulted to verify
  that the Lie-algebraic method includes indefinite signature. No classification
  list, physical metric admission or completeness theorem is used in IEC1.
- Vincent Pecastaing (July28,2023),
  [Lie groups and Riemannian symmetric spaces](https://math.univ-cotedazur.fr/u/pecastaing/symmetric_spaces.pdf),
  §5 consulted as supplementary method context. No additional load-bearing
  result beyond the explicit local derivation is taken from these notes. Authorship/date were checked on its title page.
