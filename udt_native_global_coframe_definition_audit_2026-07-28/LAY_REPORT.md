# Lay readout

We found that the UDT skeleton is real, but the joints are not all installed.

The two founding ideas do force a clock part and a ruler part to respond oppositely. They also tell
us how that pair must be described without choosing a preferred observer. The angular part is not
random either: under a natural, explicitly stated symmetry test, all of its remaining freedom
collapses to one number, called `lambda`.

Most importantly, an earlier audit missed an already-existing example. We do have complete,
non-static-in-the-clock-sense geometries in which the reciprocal clock/ruler structure and the
angular structure coexist smoothly. So the pieces are not mutually incompatible.

But the metric premises do not yet tell us:

1. which real observer-to-observer comparison supplies the depth `phi`;
2. how strongly the two angular directions participate—the remaining `lambda` choice; or
3. how the local pieces must be joined through a whole finite universe, including unusual causal
   interfaces.

Those are three different hinges. The existence of one complete example cannot choose them. After
they are fixed, another question remains: what whole-universe equation selects the configuration
that is actually realized rather than merely mathematically allowed?

So this audit did not close UDT, but it replaced a vague missing “table leg” with a precise parts
list. It also corrected a misleading obstacle: the clock and angular sectors can already coexist in
one complete geometry. The next productive search is therefore for a native law that links the
three hinges, not another isolated ansatz or a numerical hunt through an undefined system.
