# Lay report

The cold reviewer agreed that the two-instrument pattern is real mathematics, but tightened what
we are allowed to say about it.

There is one special “harmony direction” when depth and angular area are treated as two freely
independent instruments. But a particular song may not use it at all. If angular area simply
copies depth, the special harmony collapses to zero. So we found a possible building block, not a
guaranteed extra component in every universe or every solution.

The reviewer also confirmed that the tilted-ruler examples are legitimate mathematical tests, but
they are not yet physical UDT frames. They tell us what can go wrong if we extend the old
lower-triangular result too broadly. They do not tell us that UDT itself makes that extension.

The useful result survives: two metric sectors can jointly carry a global loop signal that one
real scalar cannot. What remains missing is still the same crucial joint—the complete metric must
actually generate and select that response before it becomes physics.
