## Purpose

Conduct a read-only smoking-jacket whiteboard review before launching another derivation, then
write one cold-start consultation document, `path.md`, that reconstructs the current problem from
the two founding postulates through reciprocal `c_E`, complete observer-pair geometry, `X_max`,
SNe, and the G66--G86 CMB arc.

The goal is to determine whether the work is:

1. a linear narrowing of a correctly typed problem;
2. a useful but exhausted exploration of a conditional ansatz;
3. a category-error loop; or
4. a mixture that requires a precise pivot.

## Whiteboard protocol

Two independent read-only theorists receive distinct roles:

- foundational minimalist;
- geometric synthesist.

The root agent acts as hostile chair. The first memos are written independently. Each theorist is
then shown the other's conclusions and asked to attack the strongest claim, identify agreement,
and revise only where the record warrants. The chair adjudicates disagreements against the frozen
sources rather than by majority vote.

## Frozen evidence spine

`SOURCE_MANIFEST.tsv` freezes the exact 20-source spine. It is deliberately selective rather than
repo-exhaustive: each source owns one necessary stage in the cold-start chain. Historical records
may be cited for provenance, but they may not overrule these sources or `LIVE.md`.

The protected untracked stopped draft is excluded and must remain unread, unmodified, and unstaged.
