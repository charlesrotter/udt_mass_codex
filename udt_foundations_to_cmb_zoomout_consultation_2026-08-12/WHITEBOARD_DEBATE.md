# Smoking-jacket whiteboard debate

Date: 2026-08-12  
Mode: independent read-only memos, followed by directed cross-examination  
Status: conceptual synthesis; no new physics law or branch selection

## Question put to the room

Is the current foundations-to-CMB arc a linear deduction, a useful but exhausted conditional
exploration, or a rabbit hole caused by asking the wrong object to provide physical selection?

## Independent memo A — foundational minimalist

The minimalist classified G83--G86 as technically linear inside one bounded AM case study but
ansatz-conditioned at program level:

```text
chosen G75 stationary family
-> sole explored lapse ansatz with a positive zero
-> free continuation beyond the owned x<=1 domain
-> conditional doubled-S3 completion
-> constructed completion archetypes
-> search for a selector among surviving archetypes.
```

Its diagnostic gains are real. It separated a chart horizon, spatial diameter, seam causal type,
and physical `X_max`; it also proved that the current premise set selects none of the three regular
G85 classes. The minimalist judged further seam-selector drilling to be a rabbit hole.

The earliest open dependency was placed here:

```text
(metric, observers, paired events, route/branch) -> signed ordered depth delta.
```

The founding derives the reciprocal character after `delta` is supplied. A supplied calibrated
pair immersion then permits the terminal metric readout. Neither step selects the physical pair
relation.

The minimalist initially proposed a strict dependency order:

```text
history owner g
-> query/relation owner F on g
-> Xmax test on (F* g, separation_F, phi_F).
```

It recommended treating the G85 completions as a fiber of unowned continuations over the same
inherited local data rather than as rival physical histories.

The synthesist reconstructed a coherent multichannel pipeline:

```text
complete metric/coframe history g
        +
typed observer query Q
        |
        v
pair immersion F_Q and induced metric h_Q=F_Q* g
        |
        +--> kappa, phi_pair, beta
        +--> extrinsic geometry
        +--> Jacobi response D
        +--> screen/normal/ambient transport U
        |
        v
terminal reciprocal readout
        |
        +--> SNe projection
        +--> CMB observer-sky projection.
```

It proposed that the missing history law and missing relation owner might be aspects of one global
observer-relation object. In this picture `X_max` is more naturally an ideal boundary of calibrated
observer-relation space than a named spacetime seam.

The synthesist also sharpened the status of the G85 classes. Their seam causal types are invariant,
so they are not gauges or mere observer descriptions. Yet the pointwise regularity domain

```text
4 u_H - b_H^2 < 0
```

appears to permit continuous paths from shift support to lapse lift and to tapered mixing through
overlap regions. They may be distinguished strata or phases in one larger regular kinematic arena,
not disconnected candidate laws. This is a motivated inference, not a banked global-connectivity
theorem; common-chart smoothness and the zero-shift Kruskal quotient still require care.

## Cross-examination of the synthesist

The minimalist accepted the typed-bundle picture but rejected a premature merger of proof
obligations. If histories form a base and admissible observer-relation families form fibers, then
a fiber-valued assignment

```text
g -> R_g
```

assigns relation architecture to each supplied history; it does not by itself select which
histories are physical. A single nonidentity law could constrain typed `(g,Q,F)` triples jointly,
but naming one global functor does not create that law.

It also warned that a lawful relation object must cover all admissible ordered observers rather
than choose a preferred observer/query. Connectedness of off-shell metrics weakens the language of
discrete branches but supplies neither gauge equivalence nor physical selection.

## Cross-examination of the minimalist

The synthesist accepted that its one-object language had merged distinct current ownership types.
It also agreed that AM entered the program because it was the sole explored lapse continuation
with the desired positive zero. That is a lawful witness search, but it creates real program-level
selection pressure if the witness is then treated as the likely physical `X_max` realization.

The synthesist nevertheless rejected a mandatory sequential order. Query types can be stated
before a physical history is selected, and the working `X_max` frame is an admissibility condition,
not merely an after-the-fact diagnostic. The more neutral architecture is a joint constraint space:

```text
admissible triples (g,Q,F)
subject to history, query-coherence, and Xmax gates.
```

Whether this space factorizes into `select g`, then `construct F`, remains open.

## Exact shared ground

Both theorists agree that:

1. G83--G86 exhausted the current AM seam-selector drill.
2. Their registered seam properties differ invariantly under seam-preserving identifications.
   Full equivalence under all allowed global diffeomorphisms, query relabellings, and completion-
   chart changes has not been classified.
3. Current premises select none of them.
4. The AM seam is not identified with physical `X_max`.
5. `c_E` is a calibration and terminal reciprocal evaluator, not a route or history selector.
6. The founding character acts on supplied ordered depth; the physical relation assigning depth is
   open.
7. The complete coframe is a configuration/history arena, not an on-shell law.
8. More regularity archetypes, stationary continuations, or ownership tables will not generate the
   missing nonidentity relation.
9. A future global law may constrain history and observer relations jointly, but their proof
   obligations must remain separately visible.

## Remaining disagreement, now sharply bounded

The live question is not which G85 family wins. It is whether the complete theory has:

- a history-first factorization;
- a query/relation-first construction that restricts histories;
- one coupled admissibility law on `(history, relation-family)`; or
- only a kinematic relation framework until an additional physical premise is supplied.

No current evidence chooses among those architectures.

## Whiteboard recommendation

Pause further AM seam excavation. First perform a no-solve object-type/factorization audit of the
space

```text
C = {(g,Q,F): g is a candidate history, Q is a typed ordered query,
                F is a regular calibrated realization of Q on g}.
```

State separately the quotients by spacetime diffeomorphism, query reparameterization, endpoint
screen gauge, and route equivalence. Express `X_max` invariantly on evaluated pair relations before
naming a manifold seam. Only then decide whether AM is still an informative witness.
