# G166 lay report

The UDT metric and the reciprocal kernel are not two machines.

For observers A and B, the UDT metric already tells their completed clock/ruler relationship. The
kernel is just the exact dial that reads the imbalance between those two parts. We should not first
choose a road between A and B, calculate an ordinary distance, and then add positional dilation.

The recent “infinitely many metrics” problem came from temporarily allowing the complete coframe
to represent every possible Lorentzian metric. That was useful for proving what a general
calculator can and cannot see, but it was broader than the founded UDT metric.

What survives is substantial:

- the reciprocal kernel follows directly from the founded pair metric;
- the orchestra must alter the one completed pair metric before the kernel is read;
- `X_max` is not an input to that local kernel;
- G165 remains a warning about what happens if arbitrary extra metric freedom is admitted.

What remains is not “which universe do we select?” It is the cleaner UDT question:

> How does the primary reciprocal metric generate its complete angular, mixing, and time-live form
> as one inseparable geometry?

That is the next derivation.
