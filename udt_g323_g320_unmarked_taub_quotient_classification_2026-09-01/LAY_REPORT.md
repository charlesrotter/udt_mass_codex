# G323 lay report

The whiteboard found that the earlier alternatives were neither completely different universes nor
merely coordinate tricks.

Imagine one flexible spacetime tube whose cross-sections are three-dimensional tori. The different
`psi` curves tell us how a chosen “now” surface wiggles through that tube. Locally, every registered
curve sits in the same Ricci-flat spacetime geometry. Much of the apparent free profile was therefore
freedom to choose a differently shaped snapshot.

But the tube must also close around its compact direction. The total distance needed for that
closure is fixed by the complete snapshot—both its intrinsic shape and how it is embedded in time.
For modes 1, 2, 3, and 4 that closure number is strictly different. It cannot be removed by a
coordinate relabeling or by changing the unattached common scale. So the modes are different
explicit compact globally hyperbolic spacetime quotients even though they share the same local
spacetime form.

The two signs of `K` are simpler: they are the same underlying spacetime with its direction of time
reversed. If we ignore the time arrow, they are one metric, not a universe/anti-universe pair.

This is meaningful linear progress. It answers the exact marked-versus-unmarked question that was
left after G322 and explains what the `1, 4, 9, 16` result was seeing. It does not choose which
compact quotient Nature occupies, and it does not alter the UDT metric, reciprocal kernel, angular
cancellation, field equation, physical scale, or `X_max`.

One global boundary remains deliberately open: we have not proved that each explicit quotient is
already the largest possible globally hyperbolic development promised conditionally by G322. The
result classifies the explicit spacetime developments we constructed, not every possible extension
of them.
