# G332 lay report

G331 found smooth three-dimensional shapes whose metric-defined direction does not run around neat
closed Hopf circles. The unanswered question was whether those shapes were merely mathematical
drawings, or whether they could be legal snapshots of a UDT spacetime under the provisionally
adopted vacuum rule.

G332 finds an exact legal completion. A spacetime snapshot needs two things: its spatial shape and
how that shape is bending into time. The first is the G331 metric; the second is called extrinsic
curvature. For every metric in the weighted family, we can assign that time-bending quantity from
the metric's own curvature so that both required vacuum consistency checks vanish exactly.

This works even when the two weights have an irrational ratio and the metric-defined lines never
close. So the vacuum constraints do not secretly force those lines back into perfect Hopf circles.

That is real progress, but it is deliberately narrow. We now have lawful initial snapshots, not a
proof that Nature occupies one, not a proof that the irregular pattern survives for all time, and
not a new matter, mass, scale, or `X_max` result. The metric and reciprocal kernel were not changed.

A fresh hostile reviewer independently retained that result. Its two evidence repairs were then
preregistered, completed, and accepted in a sealed repair-only replay; neither repair changed the
mathematics or conclusion.
