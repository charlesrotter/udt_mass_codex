The tile is complete for its declared existence question if one exact smooth irregular-flow datum
satisfies both constraints. It is not a full constraint-manifold census. Any obstruction claim must
quantify over every smooth symmetric extrinsic curvature and therefore has a higher completeness
burden than an existence claim.
