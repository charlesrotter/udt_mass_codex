# G332 map — weighted-contact metrics on the active vacuum constraint manifold

Date: 2026-09-03
Mode: metric-led bounded existence/obstruction test

## Whole question

G331 exhibited exact smooth weighted-contact metrics on `S3` whose metric-defined simple Ricci
eigenline has nonclosed, generically dense orbits when the two positive weights have irrational
ratio. Those metrics were geometric configuration-space controls only: no extrinsic curvature had
been supplied, so they were not yet points of the active provisional vacuum constraint manifold.

G332 asks whether that boundary is real or merely unfinished bookkeeping:

- can a genuinely unequal-weight, nonhomogeneous weighted-contact metric be paired with some
  symmetric extrinsic curvature satisfying both the Hamiltonian and momentum constraints;
- can this be done for a fixed connected `Lambda`, without changing the active equation;
- or do the complete constraints force extra orbit rigidity, such as equal weights or closed
  Ricci-line leaves?

This is an observing question. Closed or irregular orbits are outcomes to classify, not acceptance
criteria.

## Pure frame and bounded tile

The pure full problem is the complete constraint-data space near G331 with all six components of
extrinsic curvature free, followed later by its developments. G332 does not replace that census by
an ansatz classification.

The bounded tile is an exact existence/obstruction test on the complete G331 weighted-contact
family. The allowed extrinsic curvature is unrestricted in the question. A single exact lawful
construction is sufficient to refute universal orbit rigidity; failure of one construction is not
an obstruction unless all symmetric extrinsic curvatures are ruled out.

## Metric-led route

The displayed G331 metric has a global unit Killing field `xi`, equal to its Ricci eigenline, and
its scalar curvature is constant along `xi`. G332 tests the covariant momentum tensor

```text
P = K - tr(K) gamma = -C gamma + b xi tensor xi,
```

where `C` is a free constant with inverse-length dimension and `b` is to be fixed by the
Hamiltonian constraint. This is a candidate witness inside the unrestricted `K` space, not a
postulate or a claim that every solution has this form.

## Maximum possible conclusion

At most G332 may establish exact smooth active-constraint data with unequal irrational weights and
irregular Ricci eigenflow, thereby showing that the provisional vacuum constraints do not by
themselves force G330's Hopf-circle orbit rigidity at the initial-data level. It may not classify
all extrinsic curvatures, prove evolution or stability of the irregular flow, select topology or
occupancy, or infer matter, mass, scale, physical `X_max`, observation, or canon.
