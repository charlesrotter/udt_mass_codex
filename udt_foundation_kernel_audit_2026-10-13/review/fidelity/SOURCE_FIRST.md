## Operational objects and first analytic discriminators

Use a Lorentzian metric, ordinary proper clocks on specified future timelike
worldlines, and specified connecting future null signals as a conditional
geometric realization. This is a declared test frame, not a claim that the
owner words uniquely select all of it. For received ticks define a future
arrival map `tau_receiver = F(tau_emitter)` on a regular interval. The received
rate per emitted unit is `R = 1/F'` when differentiable and monotone. A second
future signal experiment in the opposite direction has its own map `G`.
`G` is not in general `F^{-1}`: inverse F recovers an earlier emission event
from an already chosen reception, whereas a new future signal reaches a later
event. A valid review must preserve these event choices.

For a phase description with geometric-optics covector k, measured frequency
is proportional to `-u.k` and a ratio needs endpoint u and the propagated k.
This standard geometric construction is a possible method, not an imported
UDT field equation. The arrival-map argument alone suffices for the initial
tests; no external theorem or literature claim is needed at this stage.

**Difference/composition test.** If the intended object really is a continuous
positive scalar `D(x)` on a signed additive interval containing zero, with
`D(x+y)=D(x)D(y)`, `D(0)=1`, then `log D` is continuous additive and hence
`D(x)=exp(k x)`. The scalar codomain, multiplication law, signed domain,
continuity and normalization must all be stated. This result does not select k,
the physical assignment of x, a full metric, or what experimental map D denotes.
Changing the additive coordinate's scale trades off against k. Without
regularity, exponential uniqueness is not established by this argument.

If both `D(x)<1` and `D(-x)<1` are required for nonzero x in this *same*
representation, composition gives `D(x)D(-x)=1`, a contradiction. That defeats
only this signed scalar formalization of bilateral slowing. A positive-distance
semigroup, two different future maps, a path-dependent transport or another
well-specified comparison need not have that contradiction. Each alternative
owes its actual composition domain and operational connection; it cannot be
inserted solely to make a target result true.

**Metric-versus-clock test.** Begin more generally with
`ds^2 = -A(r)c_E^2 dt^2 + B(r)dr^2 + r^2 dOmega^2`, A,B positive on a regular
interval. Static clocks satisfy `d tau = sqrt(A) dt`. At fixed endpoints,
time-independent radial signal travel implies equal coordinate emission and
reception spacings, so `R(A_to_B)=sqrt(A_A/A_B)`. A reverse static future signal
has the reciprocal ratio. Both cannot be below one; equality is possible.
This is a conditional result for static observers in a stationary branch,
not a no-go for arbitrary time-dependent geometry or moving observers.

The benchmark gives `A=e^{-2 phi}`, `B=e^{2 phi}` and hence `AB=1`. Its radial
coordinate null slope is `|dr/dt|=c_E sqrt(A/B)=c_E e^{-2 phi}`. Local radial
proper ruler length is `d ell=sqrt(B)dr`; dividing by a static observer's
`d tau=sqrt(A)dt` gives local null speed c_E. These distinct quantities must
not be renamed the same observed speed. The algebra can survive even if its
proposed source derivation or a physical clock interpretation fails.

**Metric selection test.** Even if exponential lapse ratios follow from an
additive scalar comparison, they do not alone constrain B or the angular
geometry. Staticity, spherical symmetry, areal radius and absence of a shift
are additional restrictions unless independently justified. In the areal
chart, `AB=1` is a substantive restriction rather than an arbitrary radial
coordinate choice still freely available. No preferred governing observer law
also need not mean spatial homogeneity or absence of locally different gravity.
An argument from conformal null cones alone cannot determine proper-clock
normalization: conformal changes preserve cones but generally change clock
comparisons. A metric paired with observer/path/calibration data is a legitimate
query; data sufficient to evaluate that query need not be selected by bare
geometry or by the founding statements.

All static/generic metrics here are free-and-explored analytic controls. Only
the benchmark equation is pinned to a supplied source branch, not derived
foundational necessity. c_E retains the packet's calibration meaning. No
boundary, physical profile, new coupling or independent physical degree of

## What would justify a positive, negative or unresolved return

A derivation needs a premise inventory with dated provenance and actual
operational meaning, then every restriction and normalization accounted for.
Its equations must connect owner statements to the supplied metric and finally
to a specified received-clock experiment without cycling back through the
target formula. A useful positive result may be conditional scalar algebra;
it need not be inflated into full geometry selection.

A negative result needs an exact failed implication: either a regular witness
satisfying *all premises relevant to that implication* but not the conclusion,
or a contradiction under explicit hypotheses. Merely exhibiting arbitrary
metrics does not refute a selection theorem with additional admitted premises;
the control must match them. A missing derivation warrants “not established,”
not a whole-theory impossibility claim. Ambiguous operational composition or
unverified owner provenance may remain a located unresolved join.

Continue work when another in-scope formulation can answer a material meaning
question, when a proposed counterexample does not satisfy its claimed premises,
or when the candidate has not tested its strongest interpretation. A reviewed
return is warranted when the surviving derivation and exact failed/unresolved
joins are explicit and remaining progress needs an owner meaning decision or
a new physical selection input. Resource allowance alone creates no reason to
invent additional calculations. Later review must challenge this stopping
judgment as well as the candidate's mathematics.
