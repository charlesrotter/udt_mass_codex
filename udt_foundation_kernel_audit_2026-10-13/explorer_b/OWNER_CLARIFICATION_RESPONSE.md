## A clock-first mathematical formulation, without independent light physics

A useful starting object is a stipulated relation between ordinary proper-clock
records. When an event correspondence is regular it can be written

    tau_B = F_AB(tau_A),       Z_AB=F_AB'>0.

The reverse physical experiment has its own F_BA. It is not forced to equal
F_AB^{-1}; both may have Z>1 without contradiction. This statement needs no
independent propagation law. The metric realization would subsequently have
to represent these clock correspondences and the associated causal order.

For example, as an algebraic template only, F_AB(tau)=a tau+b and
F_BA(sigma)=a sigma+d with a>1 have mutual interval slowing. Their composition
has slope a^2, not one. This is not a complete metric model, causal-history
proof, UDT solution or proposed physical law. It demonstrates why an inverse
scalar argument alone cannot reject the postulate.

The template also distinguishes derivative information from offsets: a fixes
the interval-rate comparison, while b and d enter the event alignment. Changing
an offset leaves the derivative unchanged. If the postulate identifies what we
call travel time with the same underlying clock relation, a mathematical theory
must relate these aspects through its geometry or a stated correspondence law.
It is wrong to demand a second independent propagation delay in addition to
that law. It is also wrong to claim that a bare rate number already supplies
all event alignment data. This is an operational distinction inside the desired
single account, not an objection to Charles's identification.

## What the metric then supplies, and what it does not supply by itself

For a supplied regular pair geometry,

    h=-T^2(dy+beta dσ)^2+L^2 dσ^2,

the metric's null boundary obeys

    T(dy+beta dσ)=+/- L dσ.

This is an equation of the geometry itself. It need not be interpreted as the
law of a separate underlying transport mechanism. The physical construction
still needs to show how the specified mutual clock correspondence is represented
by this geometry and which measured clocks/rulers give observed c_E. Using c_E
to dimension-match channels records the observed calibration; it does not prove
that the geometry was derived from it.

There remains a precise distinction between the ratio and product conditions.
Write arbitrary positive factors as

    T=Omega exp(-psi),       L=Omega exp(psi).

Then L/T=exp(2psi), while TL=Omega^2. Cone-slope information alone fixes a
ratio, whereas reciprocal normalization TL=1 fixes the common factor in this
calibration. Therefore identifying a cone ratio does not by itself derive the
product condition. This is only a test of that limited attempted inference.
Full proper-clock comparisons can constrain Omega too; I do not claim that
arbitrary conformal changes preserve the complete clarified owner postulate.

The strongest favorable conditional route remains:

    specified mutual clock relation and calibrated time/length channels
      + declared dual action on their evaluation pairing
      + regular additive composition and nonidentity
      -> reciprocal exponential character
      + stated Lorentzian readout / metric realization
      -> reciprocal metric block
      + completed-pair premise and chosen scalar readout
      -> chi=tanh(Phi).

If the owner intends dual action as part of the precise postulate, the argument
may start from it without deriving why it was postulated. It should then be
identified as the postulate's mathematical content. If the claim is that mutual
clock slowing alone derives that dual action, the inspected argument still
needs the implication rather than a substitution of one wording for the other.
The clarification does not erase later provisional G176 adoption; its

## Exact mathematical definitions still needed for the physical implication

These are outstanding equation-level joins, not demands for a different
foundation or repeated permission before inquiry.

* **Comparison object and preparation:** which separated-clock records and
  event correspondence represent the positional relation, and how other
  GR/SR contributions appear in the combined comparison. The supplied metric,
  actual clock worldlines and calibration are inputs of a concrete experiment.
* **Quantifier and direction:** which pairs/preparations must exhibit mutual
  slowing, whether the assertion concerns the positional contribution or the
  net record, and which operations are actual future comparisons rather than
  mathematical inverses of the same correspondence.
* **Composition:** whether the additive parameter belongs to signed endpoint
  comparisons, ordered nonnegative separation, or accumulated paths. The June
  18 words name difference-dependence and composition but do not alone equate
  these distinct domains. Full signed inverse composition cannot simply be
  applied to two different future experiments.
* **Time–length pairing:** which ruler/clock channels the relation identifies
  and why their positional action preserves the particular dual evaluation
  form K, rather than merely a ratio or an ordinary conversion map.
* **Metric realization and readout:** how that relation produces or constrains
  one common metric and its physical pair germ; which calibration is fixed;
  and why the normalized contrast is the scalar intended by the kernel. Once
  that readout is stipulated, tanh is exact, not a further adjustable profile.

An informative continuation would start from the clock relation and derive one
of these missing equations, or demonstrate that a later explicit adopted
premise already supplies it. It would not start by imposing a familiar static
signal-redshift formula as the definition of the founding effect. Numerical
surveys cannot substitute for this logical connection.
