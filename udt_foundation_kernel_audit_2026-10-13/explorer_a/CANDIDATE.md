## 2. What the completed-pair theorem actually fixes

Supply a full Lorentz metric g and an ordered calibrated pair germ with columns
J₀,J₁. Form `h=JᵀgJ`. Impose `h00<0` and `det h<0`. Direct square completion
gives

`T²=-h00`, `β=h01/h00`, `L²=h11-h01²/h00>0`,
`h=-T²(dy+βdσ)²+L²dσ²`, `-det h=T²L²`.

At fixed clock leg and calibration, G176's provisional working clarification
requires reciprocal completed factors `T(L/m)=1`, where `m>0` grades the ruler.
Therefore

`m=TL=sqrt(-det h)`, `L_s=1/T`, `Φ=-log T`,
`χ=(L_s-T)/(L_s+T)=(1-T²)/(1+T²)=tanh Φ`.

Uniqueness is exact for this supplied, calibrated query and positive density.
It is not uniqueness of g, the germ, the physical clock, a population, a
distance-to-depth law, or every possible scalar statistic. The normalized
contrast is the specified terminal readout; its tanh shape then is an identity,
not a fitted response. Other calibrated curves remain legitimate different
queries; they are not residual ambiguity at the same completed input.

This proof applies to any regular pair of the stated type, including Lorentz
metrics supplied as controls. Thus normalization alone does not select the
ambient UDT geometry. It can acquire physical force as the adopted rule for a
named measurement type without being an independent metric field equation.

## 3. Positive operational construction from a smooth null family

### Exact hypotheses and construction

Use proper time in length units, so `c_E=1` is only a units convention. Supply
a smooth time-oriented Lorentz metric, ordinary future unit proper-clock curves
`z_e(y)` and `z_o(b)`, and a smooth regular future null correspondence
`b=b(y)` with `Z=b'>0`. A sufficient local construction is to put both endpoint
curves in a convex normal neighborhood, assume the null incidence branch is
regular, and set

`V(y)=exp⁻¹_{z_e(y)} z_o(b(y))`,
`F(y,λ)=exp_{z_e(y)}[λ V(y)]`, `0≤λ≤1`.

Alternatively the query may directly supply a smooth branch of affinely
parametrized connecting null geodesics. The endpoint-normalized affine λ is
dimensionless and may have a different affine scale on each ray. Set

`K=F_λ`, `J=F_y`.

Then `∇_K K=0`, `g(K,K)=0`, and `[K,J]=0` as variation fields. Metric
compatibility and torsion freedom give

`∂_λ g(K,J)=g(K,∇_KJ)=g(K,∇_JK)=½J[g(K,K)]=0`.

At emission, `J_e=U_e`; at reception, `J_o=Z U_o`. Define the positive endpoint
contractions `ω_i=-g(K_i,U_i)>0`. (Their absolute scale follows the affine
parameter; the physical ratio does not.) Constancy yields

`ω_e=Z ω_o`, hence `Z=ω_e/ω_o`.

No completed normalization was used to obtain Z. This first computes the
observable from original null incidence and ordinary clock geometry.

### The actual endpoint pair

At reception the induced pair entries are

`h00=-Z²`, `h01=-Zω_o`, `h11=0`, `det h=-Z²ω_o²<0`.

J is timelike and K is nonzero null, so they are linearly independent. Smoothness
gives a regular immersed Lorentz pair in a neighborhood of that reception point.
The general theorem does not guarantee `g(J,J)<0` on the entire finite ray;
interior failure, caustics, multiple branches and global embedding need separate
checks. This is a local endpoint result, with a local strip where regularity
persists, not a canonical global plane. Both the clocks and null branch are
part of the supplied query.

The positive shifted factors at reception are

### Calibration and integrability boundaries

The result is invariant under ambient coordinate changes and coframe gauge when
g,J and the physical clocks are transformed together. Under an orientation-
preserving rescaling of the auxiliary ray coordinate at the endpoint, K and
its contractions rescale by the same positive factor: m transforms as a ruler
density, while T,Z,Φ and χ do not. A reparameterization depending on y must
retain the endpoint labels; at a fixed normalized endpoint this keeps J_o
equal to `Z U_o`. A generic interior change can mix the clock leg with K and
then must be treated as a different coordinate representative with its physical
clock leg explicitly retained.

The clock label y is source proper time. Replacing it by an arbitrary increasing
label y' changes the raw coefficient T by the label derivative. If both endpoint
clock coefficients are retained, `T_o/T_e=Z` still holds. Proper source
calibration sets `T_e=1`. Using target proper time instead gives another clock
leg, not an ambiguity in the fixed source-clock query. Common changes of physical
time units cancel from Z; independent uncorrected clock-rate calibration does not.

Completion is a pointwise coframe/density operation and a one-family ruler

term and retain the same T. This is precisely current R4/G180's integrability
qualification. No global `ds=m dλ` coordinate with the original clocks is claimed.

## 4. Independent exact shifted control in the original metric

Let source `A(y)=(y,0)` and receiver `B(t)=(t,b+vt)`, with `b>0`, `|v|<1`.

`t_o=y+d(y)=(y+b)/(1-v)`, `x_o=d(y)`.

`Z=sqrt((1+v)/(1-v))`.

`K=(d,d)`, `J=(1+λa,λa)`,

`h=[[-A,-d],[-d,0]]`, `A=1+2λa`, `det h=-d²`.

Here A interpolates between `1` and `Z²>0`, so this control is regular along

## 5. A further alternative to the static obstruction

An evolving reciprocal coordinate metric can itself support mutual redshift
with fixed-coordinate proper clocks. As an explicitly supplied control, use

`h=-dt²/t²+t²dx²`, `t>0`, and two clocks at `x=0,L`, `L>0`.

No field equation or UDT admission is assigned. Proper time is `τ=log t` up
to origin. Original radial nullness gives `|dx/dt|=1/t²`, so an emission at
`t_e` reaches the opposite clock at

`L=1/t_e-1/t_o`, `t_o=t_e/(1-Lt_e)`, `0<Lt_e<1`.

Therefore `Z=dτ_o/dτ_e=t_o/t_e>1`. Reflection gives the same result for the
independent reverse future experiment. A later immediate return also redshifts
when `2Lt_e<1`; it samples later endpoints and has its own ratio. This control
is not merely constant delayed ticking. In proper-time coordinates it is
`-dτ²+e^(2τ)dx²`; direct Christoffel contraction gives scalar curvature 2 in
these chosen units, so it is not the flat moving-clock control in another chart.

Its presentation depth is `φ=log t`, so `Δφ=log Z`, whereas the actual
comparison-clock depth is `Φ_clock=-log Z`. This matches G220/current R7's
time-only limit and shows exactly why a universal equation between the same
presentation-potential difference and clock depth is unjustified. It supplies
no physical scale, recovery law, isolated positional contribution, global
completion or geometry selection. Its purpose is to reject an overbroad stop
based on stationary clocks, not to install a replacement theory.
