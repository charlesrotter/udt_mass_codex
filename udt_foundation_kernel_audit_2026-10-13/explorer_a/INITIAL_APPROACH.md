## Question and first probe

What minimum operational chain connects the founding statements to a metric,
and a supplied metric to what two observers actually count from each other's
clocks? My first approach starts from an emission/reception map rather than a
desired kernel formula. A dimensionless algebraic contrast and a received clock
rate can coincide only after their objects, events and calibration are matched.

The inexpensive first probe is exact: choose two stationary radial clocks on the
supplied regular static branch, integrate the radial null relation, and compare
successive emitted and received pulses. Favorable outcome: reciprocity in the
intended two-future-signal sense follows without added readout assumptions.
Adverse outcome: algebraic inversion differs from two physical future-directed
exchanges. Ambiguity: unspecified clock worldlines or distance assignments
prevent a unique prediction. Each outcome is useful only within this branch and
preparation; none alone resolves all founding postulates.
