## What was learned

G249's remaining absolute freedom is one positive number after a full dimensionless history and
regular branch are supplied. Any independent, same-object metric observation with nonzero
homothety weight conditionally fixes that number. A second anchor is more valuable as a
falsification test: it must return the same scale.

The direct eligible classes are registered proper-time intervals, lengths/Jacobi amplitudes,
areas, volumes, and nonzero curvature or tide invariants. Their eligibility does not claim that a
specific physical instance has already been selected or measured.

`c_E`, `G_obs`, reciprocal redshift, causal cones, normalized Jacobi shape, and the current
zero-point-free G236/G237 SNe state do not fix absolute scale. Adding an observed mass, mass
density, or energy density creates the expected dimensional length combinations, but those remain
candidates until a lawful bridge attaches one to a particular metric quantity. G99's `X_eff` is
dimensionful but retains its historical P1, external-`M_B`, and imported-transfer conditions, so it
is not a native G249 construction input.
