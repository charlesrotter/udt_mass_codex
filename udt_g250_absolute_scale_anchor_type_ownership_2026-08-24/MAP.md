# G250 map — absolute-scale anchor type and ownership

Date: 2026-08-24

## Whole question

G249 leaves one positive constant homothetic scale `ell` after a complete dimensionless metric
history, a labelled regular branch, and its vertex-normalized Jacobi problem have been supplied.
Which anchor types already authorized by the current evidence can calibrate that one number, and
which merely have suggestive units or retain historical observational normalization?

This is an outcome-blind ownership audit. It will not inspect or fit an anchor value.

## Metric-led frame

For one supplied dimensionless history and matched query object, let a scalar observable obey

```text
Q_ell = ell^w Q_bar.
```

The audit classifies whether a registered observation of `Q_ell` can determine `ell`. It does not
choose the history, observer population, branch, feature, source, or transfer law.

## Candidate classes frozen before calculation

1. dimensionless reciprocal and angular-shape data: `c_E`, `phi`, redshift, clock ratios, causal
   cones, and unit-determinant Jacobi shape;
2. direct metric observables on a specified matched query object: proper time, length/Jacobi
   amplitude, area, `k`-volume, and nonzero curvature or tidal scalars;
3. dimensional composites authorized as candidates in G132/G202: `G_obs M/c_E^2`,
   `c_E/sqrt(G_obs rho)`, and `c_E^2/sqrt(G_obs epsilon)`;
4. the current G236/G237 relative SNe state;
5. the historical G99 `M_B`-conditional `X_eff` calibration.

## Whole-arena conditions

A candidate closes `ell` only if all of the following hold:

- its homothety weight is nonzero;
- its dimensionless model value is nonzero where required;
- the observation and model refer to the same identified event, pair, screen, branch, or invariant;
- the observation is independent of the scale being inferred;
- every source, detector, or radiative-transfer bridge it needs is already owned or is explicitly
  retained as a conditional import.

## What is omitted

- all numerical observational outcomes;
- selection or fitting of a physical history, profile, branch, or anchor value;
- P1, G116/G189, `X_max`, Lambda-CDM distances, BAO/CMB outcomes, protected work, and source or
  matter dynamics;
- G248 finite-fiber aggregation and caustic completion.

## Maximum conclusion

G250 may classify necessary and sufficient conditions for calibrating the single G249 homothety
parameter and grade the frozen candidate anchor types. It may not turn a dimensional combination
into a UDT law, identify a preferred anchor, assign `ell`, or claim an observational prediction.
