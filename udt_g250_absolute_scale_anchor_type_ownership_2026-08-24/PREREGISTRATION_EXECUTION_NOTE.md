# G250 preregistration execution note

Date: 2026-08-24

The frozen G250 calculation is outcome-independent: no observational value, residual, covariance,
catalogue, or outcome artifact enters any formula, candidate grade, or test.

Strict reviewer blinding was not preserved during the pre-computation ownership map. The mandatory
startup surface displayed already-banked SNe statistics, and a provenance search displayed the
historical G99 `X_eff` value while establishing that its absolute normalization depends on external
`M_B`, P1, and imported transfer. Those values will not be copied into the G250 package, read by its
scripts, or used to choose any classification.

Accordingly, G250 may claim `OUTCOME_INDEPENDENT` and `NO_OUTCOME_USED`; it may not claim that the
driver was numerically blind to every historical summary before preregistration.
