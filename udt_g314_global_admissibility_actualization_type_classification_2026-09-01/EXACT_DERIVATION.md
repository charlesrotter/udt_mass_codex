## 1. Three different mathematical jobs

Let

\[
\mathcal M=\mathrm{Sol}(R_{ab}=\Lambda g_{ab})/\mathrm{Diff}
\]

denote complete admitted histories modulo diffeomorphism. A global admissibility rule has type

\[
\mathcal A:\mathcal M\longrightarrow\{0,1\}.
\]

Its accepted set is `M_A={m in M:A(m)=1}`. Three cases are distinct:

1. `M_A=M`: identity/compatibility, with no further global restriction;
2. `empty != M_A proper subset M`: a nonidentity admissibility restriction;
3. `|M_A|=1`: unique acceptance modulo the declared equivalence.

Physical actualization is a choice of a member of `M_A`, or more generally a population measure
`mu` supported on `M_A`. A nonidentity predicate need not provide either. If more than one member
survives, the delta measure on each survivor is a different population consistent with the same
admissibility predicate.

If `M_A` is a singleton, no logically separate choice is needed. Thus G314 does **not** prove that
two new postulates are required. One sufficiently strong, independently owned predicate could in
principle both restrict and uniquely select. Current UDT supplies no such predicate in the
registered types.

## 2. Why complete-network reconstruction is not a selector

Let `N` assign the complete relation network of a supplied metric history, and let `Q` reconstruct
the metric class from a faithful complete network. On the reconstructible image,

\[
Q\circ N=\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal M}.
\]

Therefore the condition

\[
Q(N([g]))=[g]
\]

is satisfied by every reconstructible history. It certifies faithful description, not physical
selection.

A bootstrap fixed-point proposal instead has the form

\[
B:\mathcal M\to\mathcal M,
\qquad B([g])=[g].
\]

The syntax alone supplies no restriction because `B=id` is allowed and fixes all histories. The
independent finite census exhausts all 27 endomaps of a three-element control moduli set and obtains
all 8 possible fixed-point subsets. Hence the fixed-point set is determined by the **specified
map**, not by the phrase “bootstrap” or “fixed point.” A selective `B` is itself the missing
nonidentity structure.

A global admissibility/actualization law is needed only for the stronger bootstrap ambition that
UDT predict the realized universe without freely supplied initial/boundary data. The absence of
such a law is not, by itself, a refutation or incompleteness of a conditional field theory.
