## Why a two-constant invariant form would be exhaustive

1. In Riemann normal coordinates with the initial orthonormal frame, expand the
   original geodesic, parallel-transport and null-incidence equations. Rescale
   the positive flight/proper distances by L. At L=0, the full null endpoint
   map has linearization (delta flight,delta angle) -> delta flight*n+delta angle
   and is invertible in angular charts. There is no inversion of curvature or
   an equation singular at zero curvature. Formal coefficients are universal
   polynomials in metric jets and the initial frame. Denominators at the base
   are fixed flat normalization/flight factors, not curvature invariants.

2. The normal-coordinate metric jets through degree4 consist of Riemann,
   its first and second covariant derivatives, and quadratic Riemann terms.
   This follows recursively from the curvature definition and radial normal-
   coordinate condition; no field equation enters this coordinate expansion.
   After angle integration, a degree4 scalar can therefore contain only
   quadratic curvature or a contraction of two covariant derivatives of
   curvature with g and U. Odd orientation scalars are excluded: the original
   experiment and unsigned full-sphere measure contain no spatial orientation.
   One may equivalently integrate polynomial n-components using isotropic
   Kronecker-delta moments; it cannot manufacture a preferred spatial tensor.

3. On a Ricci-flat neighborhood, Riemann=C and all covariant derivatives of Ric
   vanish. Contracted Bianchi gives divergence-free Weyl. Classify a scalar
   contraction of nabla_e nabla_f C_abcd with g and U by the number of U's:
   with no U, either Weyl is traced or it is antisymmetrically self-contracted;
   with two U on distinct Weyl pairs, the only trace-free possibility has
   both derivative indices contracted into the remaining Weyl slots, i.e.
   U^a U^c nabla^b nabla^d C_abcd (or the reversed derivative order). Each
   vanishes by the corresponding inner divergence. If either U instead occupies
   a derivative slot, the remaining Weyl slots force a trace. With four U,
   either three occupy Weyl slots, giving an antisymmetric zero, or two occupy
   derivative slots and the remaining Weyl slots force a trace. Six U also
   vanish by antisymmetry. Relabelings and curvature convention do not alter
   these zero statements. Symmetrized covariant derivatives are covered by
   linearity; any conversion of nonsymmetrized normal jets has already placed
   commutator terms in the quadratic-curvature sector.

4. At a fixed U in dimension4, Weyl is algebraically equivalent to E and B.
   The rotation-invariant quadratic scalars of two symmetric trace-free spatial
   rank2 tensors are E², B² and E:B. The last is odd under spatial reflection,
   since B uses one dual, and is absent here. Thus for each first-signal sense
   the quartic mean has the form a_± E²+b_± B² with universal constants. The
   same reasoning makes the vacuum spherical quadratic and cubic coefficients
   vanish: their available contractions are Ricci traces/derivatives or traced/
   divergence/antisymmetric Weyl contractions. The exact PSW trace already owns
   the quadratic statement.

This argument requires the actual polynomial natural-jet property, not simply
dimensional analysis or a fit to controls. A defect in that property or a missed
second-derivative contraction defeats the claimed classification. Review should
especially challenge those steps before using the constants below.
