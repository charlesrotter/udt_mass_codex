## A. The independent reverse experiment has the same leading trace

PSW1 proves log p_+=-T_o(n,n)L²/2+O(L³) for the fixed parallel-prepared free
worldlines and a regular first null branch. Reversing the initial spacelike
preparation geodesic gives exactly the same two initial events and velocities:
at B0 the new direction is minus the parallel transport of n and the new
reference velocity is the transported U. Reversing that preparation transports
U back to its original value. Thus PSW1 can be applied with B0 as reference
event and A as receiver. It evaluates an independent first emission at B0,
not the echo at a later B event or an inverse arrival map.

Because B0=o+O(L) and the vectors are parallel-related, the reversed tidal
coefficient equals T_o(n,n)+O(L). Smooth compact directional data and a common
rescaled normal tube give a uniform-in-n remainder for a fixed finite frame.
Both means, weighted on the original preparation sphere, therefore satisfy

    M_±(U,L)=-Ric_o(U,U)L²/6+O(L³).

This does not claim the two finite means agree, identify their cubic/quartic
coefficients, or assert uniformity over unbounded observer boosts.

## B. A geometric shrinking-domain witness

Choose any smooth Lorentz4 Ricci-flat comparison g0 near p with a smooth local
time coordinate t, t(p)=1, dt_p=-g0(U,·), g0(U,U)=-1. A Gaussian time coordinate
is available locally. Kasner's coordinate t and central U=partial_t meet this
condition at its reference event. For epsilon>0 define the supplied family

    f_epsilon=(epsilon/2)(t-1)²,
    g_epsilon=exp(2 f_epsilon) g0.

Units fix the chosen reference time; epsilon has inverse-length-squared units.
The profile, sign and normalization are free-and-explored control choices, not
a selected UDT response or empirical fit. At p, f=df=0, so g_epsilon and g0
share tangent calibration and U. Prepare clocks independently using each
metric's own geodesics and parallel transport. Do not hold the old free-fall
paths fixed under conformal scaling.

The conformal connection difference is

    D^a_bc=delta^a_b f_c+delta^a_c f_b-g_bc f^a.

At p, D=0 and H_ab=nabla_a nabla_b f=epsilon (dt)_a(dt)_b. Direct contraction
of its derivative in the Ricci definition gives, in dimension4,

    Ric(g_epsilon)_ab-Ric(g0)_ab=-2 H_ab-g0_ab tr_g0(H).

Here tr H=-epsilon, hence Ric(g_epsilon)(U,U)=-3 epsilon exactly at p. Part A
then yields for each fixed epsilon>0

    M_±(epsilon,L)=epsilon L²/2+O_epsilon(L³).

Each mean is strictly positive on some sufficiently small interval. The same
interval can be chosen for both means at this one laboratory. This does not
establish a uniform radius, an all-event/all-observer law, or positive individual
directions. Nonzero Weyl curvature of g0 is not removed by the positive conformal
scaling; on each fixed compact regular region g_epsilon tends to g0 in C^k for
every finite k as epsilon tends to zero.

## C. What an adverse finite GR mean would and would not exclude

Suppose a regular GR control has M_+(0,L0)<0 or M_-(0,L0)<0 at a fixed L0>0.
Under smooth convergence of metrics and physically matched preparations on a
common compact normal tube, ordinary smooth ODE and transverse null-incidence
dependence make these finite means continuous. Compactness of the direction
sphere controls the integral; future endpoint velocities make the null incidence
transverse, with a positive lower bound on a compact regular branch. Therefore
the corresponding mean stays negative at L0 for sufficiently small epsilon.

This conflicts with AMS only if AMS applies to that same finite L0 throughout
the recovery family. The conjunction of AMS, regular smooth recovery and the
common query is the conditional target. None of those applicability/uniformity
conditions is silently derived from the owner's solar/cosmic expectation.

If the adverse control is found, B simultaneously provides positive very-short-
range means for every epsilon>0 and convergence to the adverse GR control at
fixed L0. Consequently any initial interval of positivity must fail before L0
for small enough epsilon. If the limiting mean is negative for all sufficiently
small L>0, there can be no epsilon-independent positive initial interval. This
is an actual smooth-metric escape from an overbroad uniform no-go, not a fitted
redshift curve. It does not supply a distant positive branch or a native scale.

Conditional quantitative refinement, to use only if the calculations justify
the required coefficients and joint regularity: if

    M(epsilon,L)=epsilon L²/2+b L^4+O(epsilon L³+L^5+epsilon L^4), b<0,

then at L=s sqrt(epsilon), M/epsilon² tends to s²/2+b s^4 uniformly on compact
s-intervals. Its nonzero crossing tends to s=(-1/(2b))^(1/2); an analytic joint
implicit-function argument can establish a nearby actual crossing. This scaling
is not claimed before checking the cubic cancellation and remainder. In
particular a polynomial surrogate alone is not proof of a geometric clock map.

## Physical decision boundary

Neither mean automatically isolates the positional contribution. Both are total
clock records for a deliberately prepared laboratory. A negative local control
cannot exclude an additional effect restricted to a distant-onset regime or a
nonuniform recovery law. A positive mean common to all controls would not select
geometry. The source-to-interpretation join must therefore remain explicit in
the campaign's eventual decision, independently of the arithmetic outcome.
