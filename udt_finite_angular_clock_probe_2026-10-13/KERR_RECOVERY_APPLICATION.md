# Applying the preserved recovery argument to the Kerr control

Additional candidate consequence, recorded after INITIAL_CANDIDATE/SCIENCE_FREEZE
and before final review/integration. It changes no frozen initial artifact. This
uses the general setup already in RECOVERY_CANDIDATE; it does not introduce a new
conjecture or a Kerr clock simulation.

Assume the FAC-Q proof and the nonextremal Kerr example survive review. At its
regular event q choose a local analytic time coordinate tau, tau(q)=0 and
d tau_q=-g0(U,·), for the declared initial Carter tangent U. The coordinate
is a laboratory construction, not a preferred physical time or observer field.
For a supplied small epsilon>0 put

    g_epsilon=exp(epsilon tau²) g0.

At q its calibration and first metric derivatives agree with g0, and the
conformal-connection calculation of RECOVERY_CANDIDATE gives exactly
Ric_epsilon(U,U)=-3epsilon. Re-prepared free clocks therefore have

    M_±(epsilon,L)=epsilon L²/2+O_epsilon(L³)>0

for all sufficiently small L>0, separately for each fixed epsilon and this
laboratory. The family tends smoothly to the same Kerr control on fixed compact
normal tubes. Its undeformed mean is -L^4/540+O(L^5), so both means are negative
at any fixed sufficiently small L0 once epsilon is small enough. This provides
an actual smooth-metric family realizing the shrinking-positive-interval escape
from a common-fixed-query contradiction. It does not claim AMS on a fixed region
for all observers, a distant positive branch or native admission.

The vacuum cubic term vanishes, and joint analytic dependence gives

    M_±(epsilon,L)=epsilon L²/2-L^4/540
                       +O(epsilon L³+epsilon L^4+L^5).

At L=s sqrt(epsilon), division by epsilon² tends to s²/2-s^4/540. The nonzero
simple root is s0=sqrt270=3sqrt30. Analytic implicit dependence in
h=sqrt(epsilon) gives separate actual crossings

    L_±(epsilon)=3sqrt30 sqrt(epsilon)+O(epsilon).

These are local sign crossings, not globally unique roots. At scales below/above
that root with fixed s separated from s0, the signs follow for sufficiently
small epsilon. Restoring units, epsilon is an imposed curvature-scale amplitude;
the numerical constants use M=1 in the supplied Kerr control. There is no
physical selected scale, source law or modification of UDT's founding premise.

This application reinforces the quantifier distinction. An actual negative GR
mean is compatible with positive sufficiently-short means along a smoothly
recovering family whose positivity domain shrinks. A universal all-small-query
law with a common nonzero recovery interval would still conflict with this Kerr
limit. Which regime UDT requires remains a separate physical question.
