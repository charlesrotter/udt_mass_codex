# G209 lay report

The shift behaves like a moving floor under the observers.

The existing spatial geometry determines the size and shape of the local light-cone opening. The
shift does not independently stretch that opening; it moves its center. That is a clean interlock,
not a bolted-on aftereffect.

Three things were learned:

1. Turning on all three shift directions does not damage the metric locally. Its signature and
   volume determinant remain correct, and the same time coordinate remains a valid clock ordering.
2. Moderate, controlled shifts preserve the good G205 causal and null-completeness behavior.
3. A smooth shift can still become enormous relative to the shrinking outer light cone even when
   its coordinate value stays bounded. Then the spacetime can remain globally causal while some
   nonradial light paths reach the outer end in finite affine time.

The observer-pair kernel hears the shift before producing its final reciprocal reading, except for
a special observer moving exactly with the shifted cone. That special silence is geometry, not an
instrument being switched off.

So another major component has joined the same metric machinery coherently. We have mapped what it
does; we have not selected Nature's shift profile or claimed an `X_max` result.
