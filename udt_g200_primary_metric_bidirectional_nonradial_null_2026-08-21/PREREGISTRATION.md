## Required derivation

1. Parameterize the ruler by a source angle `alpha` and derive both initial null vectors.
2. Prove that the two branches have common conserved energy, opposite signed angular momentum,
   and opposite initial radial velocity.
3. Derive the common radial potential and the branchwise frequency law.
4. Propagate each natural G187 screen and derive both diagonal tidal functions from the metric.
5. Prove that the two tides are equal whenever evaluated at the same `r`, while the branchwise
   functions differ only through `r_plus(lambda)` versus `r_minus(lambda)`.
6. Derive the vertex series far enough to expose the first possible accumulated directional
   difference.  The preregistered target is order `lambda^4`, controlled by the radial derivative
   of the common tide.
7. Recover the strict radial G199 limit, the flat control, and the tangential/reflection control.
