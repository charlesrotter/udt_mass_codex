## What is actually measured

Supply ordinary proper clocks A and B and a regular future-null correspondence
`b=f_AB(s)`. Their local readings are s and b. Matching transmitted labels to
received labels determines pairs `(s,b)`; a clock origin convention is harmless
once the matched events are recorded. In a differentiable idealization,

    p(s) = db/ds = omega_A/omega_B > 0,
    omega_i = -g(k_i,u_i),
    Delta b = integral_[s0,s1] p(s) ds.

G220 and FSL1 own this result; it is conditional on the supplied ordinary clocks,
metric, regular ray correspondence and affine transport. A finite interval ratio
is an interval average of p, generally not its point value. TRI1's pushforward
of labelled tick measures handles actual discrete records; a nonzero atomic
count record has no ordinary instantaneous density. With supplied continuous
emission cadence nu_A, received cadence is nu_B=nu_A/p under its stated
regularity. Absolute source cadence, detector protocol or energetic luminosity
does not follow from a dimensionless clock ratio.

Knowing p(s) on an interval determines f_AB only up to one arrival constant:

    f_AB(s) = b0 + integral_[s0,s] p(v) dv.

One matched event `(s0,b0)` supplies that constant. Knowing p at one event
supplies much less. This is TRI1's existing distinction, not a new result.

For an ideal immediate relay at B, let the later reception be

    a=f_BA(b),  q(b)=da/db>0,
    F(s)=f_BA(f_AB(s)),  F'(s)=q(f_AB(s)) p(s).

For a finite relay, supply the proper-time re-emission map `r(b)`:

    F(s)=f_BA(r(f_AB(s))),
    F'(s)=q(r(f_AB(s))) r'(f_AB(s)) p(s).

The outgoing and incoming direction, later event, endpoint clock, and emitted
label protocol are distinct. A relay's absolute emitted frequency may be supplied
separately; the displayed arrival derivative is a labelled-clock correspondence,
not an assumed coherent-frequency transfer law. ICN1 already owns this structure.

If F(s)>s in the declared chronological domain, the observing clock A can report
radar delay and distance, using the observed calibration c_E:

    T=(s+F)/2,  R=c_E(F-s)/2,
    beta_rad=(pq-1)/(pq+1)

for the immediate relay. This radar drift need not equal material speed or a
local propagation speed, and positive drift does not imply both p>1 and q>1.
Ordinary local clocks remain ordinary throughout.

## Inversion and an actual return are different operations

The inverse map of this same first correspondence has

    (f_AB^-1)'(b)=1/p(f_AB^-1(b)).

It maps B's reception back to A's original emission. An actual return maps that
reception/re-emission to a later A event. Reversal in the founding reciprocal
algebra therefore cannot be substituted for a future causal echo.

For full oriented endpoint tetrads and transport, FSL1/R7 use

    Lambda=E_o^-1 P E_e,
    Lambda(1,n_e)=f(Lambda,n_e)(1,n_o),  f=1/Z,
    f(Lambda2 Lambda1,n)
      =f(Lambda2,A(Lambda1,n)) f(Lambda1,n).

The intermediate direction is essential even for subdivision of one ray. A
relay changes the ray and its launch direction; it must be represented as that
new protocol, not passed through the unchanged-ray composition formula. The
inverse multiplier is reciprocal on the same arrow with the transformed
direction. The future return has its own arrow. FSL1/G274 already own this
content; it is not an additional UDT selecting law.

Likewise, from the transported clock column `(gamma,s_vec)` one obtains

    Z=gamma-s_vec dot n_o
     =(1-chi_vec dot n_o)/sqrt(1-|chi_vec|^2).

One Z compresses this directional information. Even the projective clock vector
alone is not full nonradial composition data. W5 and the matched scalar
`chi_clock=tanh(-log Z)` have different types. None of these facts calls for
changing the founding interpretation or introducing abnormal intrinsic clocks.

## One analytic illustration: identical outgoing data, different echoes

This is an independently written conditional illustration of the future-data
distinction, not a native-admitted countermodel or a new selecting result. It
does not challenge ESR1's locally symmetric theorem. It is the sole analytic
control explored in this contribution; no numerical checks were claimed.

Use c_E=1, L>0, and a supplied smooth metric

    g=-dt^2+a(t)^2 dx^2+dy^2+dz^2,
    A(t)=(t,0,0,0),  B(t)=(t,L,0,0).

Take a(t)=1 for t<=5L/4, a(t)=C for t>=3L/2, and a smooth monotone positive
transition in between, with 1<C<=2. L, C, the transition and the metric family
are `free-and-explored` controls, not physical UDT inputs or selected scales.
The flat control has a=1 everywhere. A smooth plateau transition can be
constructed with a standard smooth step; no field equation is asserted.

Both clocks are unit geodesics because g_tt=-1, g_ti=0, and Gamma^i_tt=0.
At t=0 the metric is exactly flat in a neighborhood; the length-L connecting
spacelike geodesic parallel-transports A's velocity to B. Thus the initial
preparation matches the free parallel-clock prescription. For every emission
|s|<L/8, the direct outward branch lies entirely before 5L/4, so

    b=s+L,  p=1.

The whole outgoing clock correspondence in that window, endpoint frames,
transport, and any local metric derivatives supported on its neighborhood are
identical to the flat control. This is a full outgoing record in the R6/R7
sense, not a claim about an unspecified globally complete W6 relational state.

The immediate return time a_R is determined by its distinct incidence equation

    integral_[b,a_R] dt/a(t)=L.

At t=3L/2 the accumulated integral is at most 3L/2-b<5L/8, so the return
occurs after the transition. Since 1<=a<=C, it exists at finite time and
obeys a_R>b. Differentiating the actual return incidence yields

    q=da_R/db=a(a_R)/a(b)=C,

whereas the flat control has q=1. The selected direct branches are ordinary
smooth null branches; no inverse/past root was used. The change is in a future
part of the supplied geometry, not in intrinsic clock rates or a second light
speed. No metric equations, source law, empirical fit or physical admission are
being inferred from this construction.

This example makes one narrow point: enlarging the record of the outgoing
experiment does not automatically supply all data needed for its future return
in the unrestricted conditional interface. It does not prove that the full UDT
premises admit these two histories, that an admitted evolution law could not
distinguish them, or that new physics is necessarily needed.

## Scalar FC, physical selection, and the recommended stop

FC says one smooth q=Q(p) applies across all its declared prepared events,
frames, directions and separations. That is an extra sufficiency hypothesis:
it declares omitted circumstances irrelevant after p is fixed. It is not the
same statement as covariance, one geometry, no preferred observer, or a common
governing law. ESR1 gives its real mathematical cost within its explicit
parallel-curvature class: total curvature must be constant sectional curvature.
It selects no sign/scale, and it does not classify generic variable gravity.
ULC1 neither requires nor categorically excludes such a stronger condition.

FNA1 has already assembled a full pair and transport for the specified finite
example. ICN1 has already reconstructed a clock-sheet metric value/gradient in
its extra totally geodesic2D sector and exhibited a finite-network inverse
ambiguity. TRI1 has already separated ratios, absolute cadence and matched
arrival origins. Repeating these as “the full-data universal law” would rename
existing conditional evaluation/reconstruction, not advance physical selection.

SGE1 gives the appropriate boundary. The measured net Z is fixed by a physical
(g,query). A further positional consequence must concern physically admissible
geometry or its physical comparison assignment. The auxiliary identity
`log Z=integral(H+sigma(n,n)+a dot n)dell` redistributes under a change of
intermediate observer field; it cannot identify a unique local positional
density without another physical argument. An operationally matched GR
contrast is a diagnostic, not automatic attribution. GR remains FILTER ONLY.

**Recommended return: park the scalar-FC physical-selection lead. No new
operational derivation is recommended from this contribution alone.** The
authorized whiteboard permits this outcome. There is no source-backed additional
relation in this lane that I can responsibly identify as the ONE next selector.
A proposed direction-aware law must state a nonidentity restriction on physical
records beyond the known transport/readout identities, preserve ordinary motion
and nonuniform gravitational comparisons, and say how the restriction realizes
the positional requirement. Writing an arbitrary multivariable Q is not enough.

Alternatives remain legitimate but distinct: a variable-curvature FC theorem
would extend mathematical classification without by itself justifying FC;
FCW1's regular-completion lead is another explicitly unadopted hypothesis and
requires its own physical motivation; a genuine source-derived relational
restriction could be assessed when specified. None starts here. No operational
uniqueness, universal underdetermination, empirical incompatibility or founding
refutation is claimed. The present stop is a source-relative physical-meaning
audit and a decision against inventing a successor connection for momentum.
