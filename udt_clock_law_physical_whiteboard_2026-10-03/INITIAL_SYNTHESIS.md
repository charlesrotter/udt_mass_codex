    p(P1)=p(P2) implies q(P1)=q(P2).

p-image; smooth extension is an additional condition, not proved by this
observation. FC does allow different p values for different circumstances.
The issue is whether other circumstances can affect q after p has been fixed.

normalization, ordered-depth composition, a common governing law, ordinary local

necessary, and physical countermodels to full UDT have not been constructed.

all-preparation smooth FC iff constant sectional curvature, including flat and

Recommendation: retain FC/ECS/IEC/ESR as diagnostics and park extending FC as the

## 4. One alternate target tied to the founding asymptote

FCW1/R16FCW already poses a different UNADOPTED trial RG: a regular spacelike

The new recommended question is **clock/protocol robustness of that existing
trial in nonuniform geometry**. Does the pole follow for actual freely falling
receivers from stated finite initial data, or did the simple result depend on
the special comoving/conformal clock choice? This tests the hypothetical bridge's
content before any physical adoption. It does not yet justify the bridge.

The minimal exact bookkeeping can be derived without Einstein dynamics. Put
l=log Omega. Direct metric compatibility/torsion-free computation gives

    nabla^g_X Y = nabla^bar_X Y - X(l)Y - Y(l)X
                 + gbar(X,Y) grad_bar l.

For affine null kbar, k=Omega^2 kbar is affine for g: its derivative contributes
2Omega^3 kbar(Omega) kbar and the connection difference contributes its negative,
with the remaining gbar(kbar,kbar) term zero. For an ordinary unit physical clock
u, put ubar=u/Omega, which is unit for gbar. Its frequency is exactly

    omega=-g(k,u)=Omega[-gbar(kbar,ubar)]=Omega omegabar,
    Z=(Omega_e/Omega_o)(omegabar_e/omegabar_o).                 (C1)

This is a conditional representation of the prior R6 observable. It is not an
extra propagation mechanism, a newly selected geometry or a new physical law.
Affine normalization must be consistent along each ray; a common constant ray
rescaling cancels from the ratio. In a family, arbitrary inconsistent endpoint
normalizations would invalidate (C1).

If along the chosen family A=Omega_e omegabar_e and B=omegabar_o have finite
strictly positive limits A*,B*, then Omega_o Z -> A*/B*>0. Two-sided positive
bounds alone give only two-sided bounds on Omega_o Z, not existence of a limit.
The physical Z is invariant under a smooth finite positive nonzero conformal
gauge Omega->w Omega,gbar->w^2 gbar; omegabar->omegabar/w. The residue expressed
in Omega changes with gauge. Omega is not automatically measured distance,
and this is not yet a pole in distance or identification of X_max.

The substantive missing step is whether actual prepared timelike geodesics
supply these endpoint limits. They must not be assumed merely to obtain the
desired asymptote. A pointwise control illustrates the distinction: in gbar=eta,
kbar=(1,1,0,0), ubar=(cosh rho,sinh rho,0,0), omegabar=exp(-rho).
Choosing rho=log Omega makes physical omega=1 despite Omega->0. Each vector
is timelike unit, but this is not a demonstrated freely falling receiver family
or a counterexample to the proposed geodesic statement. Proper normalization
alone is insufficient; the free-clock dynamics/branch hypotheses are the target.
