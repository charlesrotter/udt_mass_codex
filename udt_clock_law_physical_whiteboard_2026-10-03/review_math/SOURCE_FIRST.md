completion ansatz, not a new physical law or a solution of the proposed successor.

kbar be an affine null tangent for gbar and set k=Omega^2 kbar. The connection

    nabla^g_X Y = nabla^bar_X Y - X(log Omega)Y - Y(log Omega)X

Consequently nabla^g_k k=0: the derivative of Omega^2 cancels the two
log-Omega terms, while the null term vanishes. Thus, using compatible affine

    omega = -g(k,u) = Omega * omegabar,
    omegabar = -gbar(kbar,ubar) > 0,
