## Exact finite relation, retaining the return direction

Consider the immediate relay protocol A(s) -> B(b) -> A(a) on actual regular
future null branches, with b=f_+(s), a=f_-(b), p=f_+'(s)>0 and q=f_-'(b)>0.
Use signature(-+++), c_E=1 length units, and endpoint orthonormal frames whose
time columns are the actual clocks and whose spatial orientations are compatible.
Let Lambda_+ transport the outgoing vector components from A(s) to B(b), and
Lambda_- transport return components from B(b) to A(a). These are different
paths with different endpoint events. They use the Levi-Civita connection of
the supplied metric, not an independent physical transport law.

For l(n)=(1,n), |n|=1, define

    Lambda l(n)=f(Lambda,n) l(A(Lambda,n)),
    f(Lambda,n)=[Lambda l(n)]^0>0.

If n_0 is the outgoing direction at A, n_1=A(Lambda_+,n_0) its arrival
direction at B, and r_1 the actual future return launch direction at B, then

    p = 1/f(Lambda_+,n_0),
    q = 1/f(Lambda_-,r_1),
    (f_- composed with f_+)' = p q.                       (1)

All three statements are R6–R8/FSL1 identities. In particular, r_1 is not
silently n_1, minus n_1, or the sky direction of an inverse/past arrow; it comes
from the specified returning branch. Merely knowing p does not specify these
returning geometric data.

One can express the same bookkeeping as a single Lorentz product. Choose any
spatial rotation R_B in B's rest frame satisfying R_B l(n_1)=l(r_1), and let

    M = Lambda_- R_B Lambda_+.

Then direct substitution, without a transport theorem import, gives

    M l(n_0)
      = f(Lambda_+,n_0) f(Lambda_-,r_1)
        l(A(Lambda_-,r_1)),

and hence

    p q = 1/f(M,n_0).                                    (2)

R_B is a mathematical way to retain the two actual directions, not an adopted
mirror/reflection law or an emitted-frequency rule. Different rotations with
the displayed action give exactly the same scalar on this one ray; their full
matrices can differ. This product includes redirection and unequal endpoint
events, so it is not automatically ordinary connection holonomy or an inverse
of the outgoing map. A delayed relay has different B events and its own proper
time map; the timing chain acquires that map's derivative. Equation(2) is stated
only for the immediate timing protocol, with no optical-device inference.

The corresponding radar relation is already R8:

    beta_rad=(p q-1)/(p q+1).

No distance-only curve, homogeneous geometry, tidal isotropy, observer
population, reference subtraction or selected scale has been introduced.
Spatial/directional variation is allowed through the supplied connection,
worldlines, paths and n_0,r_1. The same formula at every preparation is the
appropriate kinematic sense of universality; replacing all these inputs by

The matrix derivation is elementary algebra verified independently here.
It recovers prior composition mathematics; it does not create a novel physical
prediction. Full data make the evaluation well-defined but do not by themselves

with T=g(R(n,U)U,n), V=R(n,U)U-Tn and W=R(n,U)n-TU, the sourced IEC coefficient
is d4=2|V|^2+<V,W>. Under n -> -n, direct substitution gives T -> T,
V -> -V and W -> W, so d4(n)+d4(-n)=4|V|^2. Positivity of the transverse
space then turns vanishing of both coefficients into V=0. This short algebra
has been checked independently; the general IEC coefficient itself is sourced,
not rederived here. ESR's supplied all-frame quantifier is doing real work.

With variable curvature, extra derivative terms may enter the echo expansion;
the locally symmetric formula cannot simply be reused. A new theorem there
would test an enlarged mathematical scope for the *same proposed scalar
sufficiency*. Whether it excludes or admits particular spatially varying
metrics would not decide the separate implication from the founding positional
requirement to FC. No generic rigidity claim is made here, and no empirical
GR incompatibility follows from ESR's restricted mathematical selection.

The concrete decision gate for any later FC proposal is therefore: identify
an owned argument that requires the returning net clock ratio to depend only
