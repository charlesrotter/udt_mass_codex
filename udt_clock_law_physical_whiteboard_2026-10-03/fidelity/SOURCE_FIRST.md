## The precise missing implication

Write a complete declared comparison as \(\mathcal P\): metric, event, clocks,
physical preparation, direction, ray branches and immediate-relay rule. In the
PSW experiment the geometry is supplied, the initial second clock is reached by
a short spacelike geodesic, its velocity is parallel transported, and both
clocks subsequently fall freely. This is an explored query preparation; the
founding sources do not make it the unique physical comparison population.

R6/R8 give two actual arrival maps

\[
 b=f_{AB}(s),\quad a=f_{BA}(b),\qquad
 p=f_{AB}'(s)>0,\quad q=f_{BA}'(b)>0,\quad
 (f_{BA}\circ f_{AB})'=pq.
\]

Each ratio is fixed by its own endpoint contractions
\(Z=\omega_e/\omega_o\), with \(\omega=-g(k,u)>0\). The first and echo
legs occupy different future event pairs. Inversion of the first arrow is a
different operation. A physical finite relay changes the data again.

Let \(\mathscr S\) be the declared ensemble of these preparations. FC is the
factorization \(q=Q\circ p\) on \(\mathscr S\). Its essential necessary
condition is

\[
 p(\mathcal P_1)=p(\mathcal P_2)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad q(\mathcal P_1)=q(\mathcal P_2).
\]

Constancy on these equal-p sets is sufficient for a set-theoretic function on
the attained p-image. The additional smooth-extension requirement near 1 still
needs its own hypotheses; no quotient regularity is inferred here. On one
monotone family, eliminating its parameter may create Q without establishing
this cross-preparation property.

Covariance instead says that changing auxiliary coordinates/frames for the
**same physical experiment** preserves its observable. Changing the actual
observer, direction, separation or environment changes the experiment. No
preferred observer does not identify those experiments, and ordinary local
proper clocks do not erase their different transport. FC itself does allow p
to vary; the extra demand is that no other information affects q once p is
fixed. It would be inaccurate to summarize FC merely as identical observations
for every observer.

In R7, direction-aware transport already makes the extra data explicit:

\[
 f(\Lambda_2\Lambda_1,n)=
 f(\Lambda_2,A(\Lambda_1,n))f(\Lambda_1,n),\qquad
 Z={1-\boldsymbol\chi\cdot n_o\over\sqrt{1-|\boldsymbol\chi|^2}}.
\]

These are established conditional relations, not new CPW1 laws. They retain
the actual direction, full path morphism and compatible clocks. The matched
scalar relation \(\chi_{clock}=\tanh(-\log Z)\) faithfully normalizes one
supplied clock leg. It does not claim that this scalar determines a different
future leg. Parking FC therefore leaves the scalar kernel intact.

## What ESR1 does and does not decide

ESR1's quantifier is every event, future unit observer, perpendicular unit
spatial direction, both physical directions and sufficiently small positive L,
with preparation-dependent intervals, in a connected supplied Lorentz4 region
with parallel curvature. It is not a theorem about all physical matter or
arbitrary nonuniform gravity. It derives the constant-sectional-curvature class
under FC, then the rational relation on attained records. It does not assume
that rational formula as the physical law.

The theorem imposes an informative cost on total-clock FC in that restricted
class. It cannot turn that cost into its physical motivation. Conversely,
anisotropic supplied controls are not established native UDT solutions and do
not logically disprove the complete founding interpretation. Extending ESR1 to
variable curvature could answer a mathematical question, but would not itself
prove the source-to-FC implication. Applying FC to a purported positional part
also adds an attribution map absent from these sources; writing a subtraction
or factor does not supply that map.
