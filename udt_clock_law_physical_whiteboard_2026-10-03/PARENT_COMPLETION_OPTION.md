# Parent alternative considered during CPW1 discovery

Unreviewed option, not an adopted connection or executed successor. After reading
R16FCW, the parent identifies the already-open completion/protocol robustness
lead as a potentially more direct target than enlarging scalar-FC rigidity.
This is not a third candidate: if retained, it competes with at most one
contributor-selected approach in the final two-option synthesis.

FCW1's UNADOPTED RG condition supplies a regular spacelike conformal completion
g=Omega^-2 gbar with Omega=0 and nonzero timelike dOmega. Its solved homogeneous
comoving case gives a simple received-clock pole. A useful new question would
be whether that growth survives physical free-clock motion and nonuniform
geometry, and what regularity/branch assumptions are actually needed. This
would test whether the old asymptote is tied to its special clock preparation.
It would not establish that UDT demands RG, choose the interior history or
derive X_max/sign/scale. R16FCW already recommends this as a possible next target.

For a fixed declared regular null branch, write k=Omega^2 kbar and u=Omega ubar,
so g(u,u)=-1 corresponds to gbar(ubar,ubar)=-1. Direct contraction gives
omega=-g(k,u)=Omega[-gbar(kbar,ubar)]=Omega omegabar, hence

    Z=(Omega_e/Omega_o)(omegabar_e/omegabar_o).

The affine-null rescaling is conditional on the usual conformal connection
identity. It must be independently checked before use. This formula alone
does not prove finite positive limits of omegabar for actual clocks, control
caustics, make Omega a distance, or establish the physical receiver family.
Assuming bounded conformal clock state merely to obtain the desired pole would
leave the crucial physical-data join unproved. A follow-on question could ask
whether actual timelike geodesic preparation supplies the required limit within
the declared completion class; otherwise return a counterexample or exact gap.

The additional regular-completion hypothesis remains FCW1's unadopted physical
trial, not a consequence of founding divergence. Beta=2 and compact-interior
counterexamples retain their force. Conventional conformal mathematics is a
method on supplied geometry, not an imported Einstein equation or second
signal mechanism. No such geodesic-limit theorem is proved in this note.
