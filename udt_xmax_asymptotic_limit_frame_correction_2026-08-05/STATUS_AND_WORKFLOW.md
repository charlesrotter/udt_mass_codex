# Xmax asymptotic-limit status and workflow

Date: 2026-08-05
Grade: owner-directed status correction; no new derivation

## Controlling meaning

`X_max` is the frame-shared positional-dilation asymptote for observer pairs. It is the separation
limit approached as inter-frame dilation becomes infinite, structurally as `c` is the relative-speed
limit approached at divergent Lorentz dilation in SR/GR.

Writing `s(p,q)` only as the still-open nonnegative observer-pair separation type, and `delta` as
the signed ordered comparison depth, the required realization has the limiting behavior

```text
0 <= s(p,q) < X_max                         for every finite comparison,
s(p,q) -> X_max from below  => |delta| -> infinity,
comparison dilation exp(|delta|) -> infinity.
```

This is a requirement on the missing physical realization. It is not a derivation of `s`, `delta(s)`,
a tanh or fractional-linear formula, the value of `X_max`, or their angular/global completion.

## Epistemic split

- `WORKING_FOUNDATIONAL_FRAME`: the meaning of `X_max` as the positional-dilation asymptote.
- `DERIVED`: reciprocal action on a supplied ordered depth.
- `OPEN`: the general observer/event/path-to-depth and separation law.
- `OPEN`: proof that the complete transformation law gives the same `X_max` to every admissible
  observational frame.
- `OPEN`: numerical `X_max`, local-WR-L/global join, angular modulation, bootstrap/density selection,
  native mass relation, and complete global solution.

The asymptote is relational. It is not a material wall, a preferred center, a radial edge, a
finite-cell seal, or a terminal boundary of the manifold. A selected branch may represent the limit
by a chart endpoint or regular causal horizon, but that identification must be derived separately.

## Boundary consequence

`X_max` alone supplies no boundary completion. It specifies a limiting behavior that a physical
observer-pair law must realize. It does not specify what variables are varied, what data are fixed,
which global completion is selected, or which surface term makes an action differentiable.

Therefore future boundary work must first distinguish:

1. the observer-pair asymptote;
2. a chart endpoint representing it in a chosen solution;
3. a causal horizon or observational-domain limit;
4. an actual manifold boundary or seam; and
5. the variational boundary data and functional.

No two of these may be identified by name alone.

## Required workflow gate

Every proposed general depth/separation law must be evaluated against the whole complete-metric
question before observational fitting or downstream action/matter work:

1. Keep nonnegative separation distinct from signed orientation.
2. Demonstrate the `X_max` asymptote without installing an ordinary wall or preferred center.
3. Preserve neutral self-comparison and unchanged local physics.
4. Demonstrate observer exchange/re-centering behavior and state whether the same limiting scale is
   proven or only supplied.
5. Retain angular, path, cut-locus, and global-completion data where load-bearing.
6. Recover ordinary-regime calibration by observed `c_E` without importing SR/GR dynamics.
7. Compare tanh, fractional-linear, WR-L, and other profiles only as candidates; do not choose them
   from familiarity or endpoint behavior alone.
8. Keep local WR-L `X` distinct from global `X_max` until the diameter/realization join is derived.
9. Do not infer a numerical value, mass divergence, action, source, bootstrap law, boundary term, or
   charge from the limit definition.

Failure to realize the asymptote falsifies a candidate general UDT depth law. Passing the asymptote
is necessary but not sufficient: many functions can share the same limiting behavior.

## Effect on the current scientific frontier

The current general-depth-law decision remains the right scientific seam, but its candidate space
is now constrained. The next metric-led work should characterize observer-pair realization families
that satisfy the asymptotic gate, rather than treating finite maximum separation as optional or
searching for a physical outer wall. The stationary Killing law remains a branch-local anchor; the
WR-L/SNe branch remains a conditional observational anchor.

No old algebra is promoted by this correction. Historical statements that call the limiting role
itself `OPEN` are superseded only on that semantic point; their open law, value, global-join,
boundary, action, source, and mass conclusions remain controlling.
