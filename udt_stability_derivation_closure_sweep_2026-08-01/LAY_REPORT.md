# Lay report

We checked whether five unfinished stability families were waiting on equations that could already
be extracted from the metric record. The answer is useful but mostly negative: the missing pieces
are real missing joints, not merely paperwork or overlooked algebra.

- The static, time-like, and angular pieces can still be laid beside one another, but we do not yet
  have one actual nonzero object governed by all of them at once.
- At the P4 wall, the existing rules fix the wall's position and slope, while stability also feels
  its curvature. Two walls can match in position and slope yet bend differently. Nothing currently
  fixes that bend.
- A closed ring tells us which configurations fit around the loop. It does not tell us how a fitted
  configuration reacts when nudged. Fit is not response.
- The Hopfion calculation tells us a chosen static model can sit stably in its computational box.
  A still photograph does not determine the movie, and the box edge is not yet a physical edge of
  the universe.

So the sweep did not unlock a large numerical scan. It did something more protective: it showed
exactly why such a scan would currently be answering assumed equations rather than UDT's equations.
All five families remain on the map, and the one already bounded F01 local CPU check remains valid.
