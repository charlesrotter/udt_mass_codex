## Group methods and falsifiers

### Q01 — F02/F07 joint realization

Test the compatible pullback/fiber-product gate. A constructive pass requires one nonzero field
assignment satisfying the static, time-live, and angular-live modules on shell, one native whole
equation, one compatible differentiable boundary, one tangent/variation domain, and one premise
stack. Formal embeddings, shared zero modes, or separately satisfied modules do not pass.

Falsifier: an exact source-derived common field/equation/boundary witness changes the parent
`OPEN_REALIZATION_JOIN`; otherwise countermodels may establish underdetermination but not
nonexistence.

### Q02 — F01 second-wall-germ ownership

Trace every registered N4, period, holonomy, seal, parity, and complete-cell condition to the second
wall germ entering the F01 Hessian. A constructive pass requires an explicit equation fixing that
germ on the same branch. First-germ or first-variation constraints cannot silently become second-germ
constraints.

Falsifier: two admissible higher-germ completions with identical frozen lower-order data but different
second germ establish nonuniqueness in that exact premise scope.

### Q03 — F05 response and variation domain

Test whether the metric and cyclic completion identities supply a stationary response, perturbation
domain, and second-variation/stability object for the ring family. Period balance or mass exclusion
alone is not a response law. No P4 or Hopfion operator may transfer.

Falsifier: inequivalent response laws compatible with the same frozen ring/completion identities show
underdetermination; they are logic controls, not candidate UDT actions.

### Q04 — F04 native time and physical boundary

Conditioned on actual upstream results, test whether the metric derives a physical time response,
physical finite-cell carrier boundary, carrier-section ownership, time perturbation domain, and
topological persistence statement for F04. A static chosen functional and computational mask do not
define physical time or boundary.

Falsifier: inequivalent time flows or boundary completions sharing the same static data establish
underdetermination. No external dynamics may be imported affirmatively.
