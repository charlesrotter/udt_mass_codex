# G137 exact derivation

## 1. Input owned by the complete pair

For a supplied regular calibrated complete pair metric, after angular, screen, and mixing data have
entered the pullback, let its positive clock and ruler densities be `T` and `L`. Then

\[
q_{AB}=\frac{T}{L}=e^{-2\phi_{AB}}.
\]

G136 proves that Charles's adopted continuous, ordered, same-native-Mobius physical-position
constitution with unit slope has the unique normalized coordinate

\[
\xi_{AB}=\tanh\phi_{AB}.
\]

Consequently,

\[
\xi_{AB}
=\frac{L-T}{L+T}
=\frac{1-q_{AB}}{1+q_{AB}}.
\]

On the conditional inter-observer `c_eff` branch,

\[
q_{AB}=\frac{c_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(AB)}}{c_E}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\xi_{AB}
=\frac{c_E-c_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(AB)}}{c_E+c_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(AB)}}.
\]

This does not redefine `c_eff` as a local signal speed.

## 2. Signed position and nonnegative separation

With positive working asymptotic scale `X_max`, define

\[
x_{AB}=X_{\max}\xi_{AB}
=X_{\max}\tanh\phi_{AB},
\]

and discard orientation only afterward:

\[
\sigma_{AB}=|\xi_{AB}|,
\qquad
s_{AB}=X_{\max}\sigma_{AB}
=X_{\max}|\tanh\phi_{AB}|.
\]

Thus observer reversal gives

\[
\phi_{BA}=-\phi_{AB},\qquad
x_{BA}=-x_{AB},\qquad
s_{BA}=s_{AB}.
\]

At coincidence `phi_AB=0`, both `x_AB` and `s_AB` vanish. For every finite real depth,

\[
|x_{AB}|<X_{\max},\qquad 0\le s_{AB}<X_{\max},
\]

and the two signed limits are

\[
\phi_{AB}\to\pm\infty
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x_{AB}\to\pm X_{\max},
\qquad
s_{AB}\to X_{\max}^{-}.
\]

The inverse relations on the regular open interval are

\[
\phi_{AB}=\operatorname{artanh}\!\left(\frac{x_{AB}}{X_{\max}}\right),
\qquad
q_{AB}=\frac{X_{\max}-x_{AB}}{X_{\max}+x_{AB}}.
\]

## 3. Composition requires orientation

For matched calibrated ordered comparisons whose depths add,

\[
\phi_{AC}=\phi_{AB}+\phi_{BC},
\]

the native law is

\[
\xi_{AC}
=\frac{\xi_{AB}+\xi_{BC}}
       {1+\xi_{AB}\xi_{BC}},
\]

or dimensionally,

\[
x_{AC}
=\frac{x_{AB}+x_{BC}}
       {1+x_{AB}x_{BC}/X_{\max}^{2}}.
\]

There is no corresponding single-valued law on nonnegative magnitudes alone. Take both input
magnitudes to be `1/3`. Equal orientations give

\[
\left|\frac{1/3+1/3}{1+1/9}\right|=\frac35,
\]

whereas opposite orientations give

\[
\left|\frac{1/3-1/3}{1-1/9}\right|=0.
\]

The same unsigned inputs therefore have different unsigned outputs. Ordered signed position is the
compositional object; ordinary nonnegative separation is its magnitude.

## 4. Scope ceiling

The derivation does not determine `X_max` numerically or identify `x` or `s` with path proper
length, areal radius, signal-travel distance, a spatial diameter, a material wall, or a preferred
center. It evaluates a supplied regular complete pair and does not select the pair realization or
complete metric history.
