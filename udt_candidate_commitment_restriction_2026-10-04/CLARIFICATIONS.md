# Same-premise exposed-review clarifications

INITIAL_CANDIDATE.md is preserved. The following wording/hypothesis details
apply to its reviewed reading and the central integration:

1. The source and receiver constants/preparations stay fixed. Each individual
ray conserves its b; different emitted rays have different b(R). The phrase
“ray constants remain fixed; b varies” must be read with that distinction.
2. The endpoint t_* is finite. It is receiver proper time, not emission time,
that tends to infinity. This is a causal reception-horizon limit, not yet the
owner's physical separation limit or a finite X_max realization.
3. For r>=a>3m, (f/r²)'=-2(r-3m)/r^4<0, independent of Lambda. The strict
source bound on |b| consequently gives s²(r)>=s²(a)>0 along every outward ray.
For b near a strict limiting root the bound is uniform; the integral tails and
their derivatives used in the implicit-function argument are regular. This
does not prove a branch at arbitrary early epochs or winding numbers.
4. The squared proper orbital-rate recovery comparison alone does not certify
orbital stability, a disk, or the whole GR filter. The candidate never assumed
such a conclusion. A stable-orbit application would additionally require the
effective-potential condition from CGE1.

Items1–2 were requested by the fidelity reviewer; items3–4 by the mathematics
reviewer. They change neither the equations nor the physical admission status.
