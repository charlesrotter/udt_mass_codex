# G318 lay report

G317 held the spatial scale pattern fixed. G318 lets that pattern vary from place to place.

The old G317 arrangement cannot simply be reused: once the spatial scale varies, it would violate
the momentum constraint. Instead, the curvature profile and spatial scale must change together by
an exact power relationship. So the pieces continue to interlock, but they rearrange themselves.

That rearrangement creates real branches. Some power/sign combinations cannot close around a
periodic space. Another combination does close and gives a whole family of smooth repeating
geometries. Direct curvature checks show those repeating members carry genuine tidal geometry;
they are not merely different coordinates on the zero-tide G317 member.

This is meaningful progress in extending the lawful-data surface. It is still one deliberately
restricted mathematical family, and it does not choose which member Nature uses or supply a full
evolving universe. The metric and reciprocal kernel were not modified.
