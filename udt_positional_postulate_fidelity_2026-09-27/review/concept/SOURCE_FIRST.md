Finite optical travel time is `Delta t=(1/c_E) integral |dr|/f`; proper slice length instead integrates `|dr|/sqrt(f)`. These are different functionals of the same metric. Their existence does not require another signal speed or a second fitted dilation curve. This also identifies how much the phrase “clock dilation” compresses: the founded metric changes both clock and radial-ruler calibration, and physical readouts use both.

G220 gives the general supplied-null-query formula `r_AB=(k_A.U_A)/(k_B.U_B)` and distinguishes inverse correspondence from a later causal return.
