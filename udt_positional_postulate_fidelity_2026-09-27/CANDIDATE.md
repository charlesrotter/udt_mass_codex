## 1. Answer at the present evidence boundary

The construction represents substantial parts of the postulate. It supplies a
single clock/ruler geometry, reciprocal comparisons, conditional redshift and
causal timing, and a working projective notion of pair position. The reciprocal
kernel is not shown defective by this audit. Its conditional algebra is sound
at the reviewed source scopes.

Equivalence between the entire founding physical statement and the mathematical
construction has not been established. The original statement does not by itself
specify the operational comparison, distance notion or full relation/history
restriction needed to prove that equivalence. Later working clarifications fill
some of this content; they must not be erased. In particular, no specific extra
metric restriction follows merely from replacing the word "aim" with "postulate."
Conversely, lack of a value law is not evidence against taking the postulate as
foundational, and does not prove that another physical postulate is necessary.

The concrete boundary is between specifying how a supplied geometry expresses
positional clocks and specifying which complete geometries meet all of UDT's
physical requirements. Ordinary initial and query data are legitimate; selecting
a unique universe is not required.

## 3. A crucial distinction: normalization versus a metric restriction

For a completed regular pair,

    h=-T^2(dy0+beta d sigma)^2+L_sigma^2 d sigma^2,
    m=T L_sigma,  L_normalized=1/T.

G176 proves uniqueness after W1 specifies reciprocal completion. The local
normalization can mathematically be carried out for any such positive regular
pair. That fact does not establish all ambient metrics as physical UDT metrics.
The rescaled ruler differential is generally a coframe; a regional coordinate
interpretation additionally needs integrability. Full geometry has not disappeared
merely because the terminal scalar is -log T.

There is also an actual restriction in the PRIMARY STATIC SPHERICAL AREAL branch:

    ds^2=-A(r)c_E^2 dt^2+B(r)dr^2+r^2 dOmega^2,
    A=f, B=1/f, f>0.

Here r is tied to sphere area. A reciprocal change of an auxiliary pair ruler
does not freely enforce B=1/A while preserving that same areal metric form.
For the Levi-Civita Einstein tensor, used strictly as a geometric diagnostic,

    G^r_r-G^t_t = [A'/A+B'/B]/(r B).

Consequently the reciprocal primary branch has G^r_r=G^t_t. Conversely their
equality on a connected regular positive-radius interval makes AB constant;
constant time normalization sets that constant to one. This equivalence is
scoped to the displayed static areal chart, not to arbitrary metrics or all
observer pairs. It is not a new field equation or an identification of physical
stress. It demonstrates that the primary ansatz has structural content, beyond
the availability of pair normalization.

For B=1/f the complete mixed tensor is

The angular component is essential. Equality of the radial and clock components
does not make them vanish or fix the angular component. These formulas recover

## 5. How a GR-modification exploration could help

GR already represents position-dependent clock rates through its metric. A
position-dependent lapse alone therefore does not define a modification of GR.
The useful question is which geometries or responses the UDT postulate requires
that a specified GR comparison would exclude, or conversely. The primary
reciprocal ansatz includes the familiar vacuum comparison family; its exponentials
do not by themselves distinguish an equation from GR.

There are three different routes, and the audit does not choose between them:

1. Same dynamical equations, new physical interpretation: useful conceptual
   reconstruction, but identical metrics and instrument rules give identical
   records. No distinctive prediction follows from interpretation alone.
2. Additional UDT restrictions on GR solutions: a possible subset/selection route,
   if the restriction follows from the postulate. A restricted solution class
   is not automatically a modification of the differential operator.
3. A changed metric-response law: a possible extension route when a specific
   independently motivated UDT requirement needs a different response. Its
   extra mathematical commitments must remain explicit and unadopted while explored.

Native deduction and counterfactual exploration have different entry conditions.
A later explicitly bounded investigation may openly choose a response modification
as an unadopted hypothesis before deriving it from UDT. It must name the altered
assumptions, motivation, domain, tests and return ceiling; passing those tests would
not make it native, unique or adopted. The present work order does not choose such
a modification. The requirement for an owned deduction must not be used to forbid
Charles's separately authorized exploratory approach.

For the third route, the schematic external comparison

    G_ab+Lambda g_ab+H_ab = kappa T_ab

can label what differs. H is only a placeholder, not a proposed correction;
Lambda, kappa and T are external comparison data, not native source/scale claims.

the contracted Bianchi identity requires covariant conservation of H on solutions;
an off-solution identity is a stronger additional requirement. Defining H after
choosing a desired metric, or calling G/kappa a discovered source, does not provide
