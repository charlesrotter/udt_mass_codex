## Independent argument before computation

Let ell=c_E t be calibrated receiver proper time in length units. Suppose a
declared record class supplies |d log Z/dell-H| <= beta uniformly over every
record window and timestamp uncertainty envelope. If intrinsic source frequency
has |d log nu_e/dell_e| <= D_e and Z >= Z_min there, then

    q=-log(nu_o), |q'-H| <= eta=beta+D_e/Z_min.

The source derivative is with respect to emitter proper LENGTH time. A bound
in emitter seconds must first divide by c_E. Receiver drift is not simply D_e;
the arrival Jacobian gives d ell_e/d ell=1/Z. A declaration made using an
unknown inferred H is circular unless replaced by uniform bounds over a
declared admitted range or an independently justified membership condition.

For recorded nominal centers ell_0,ell_1, D=ell_1-ell_0>0, center errors at most
delta and half-width w, any positive normalized averaging of q inside the
actual windows differs from q at its nominal center by at most L(w+delta),
L=H_max+eta. Log of a positive average of frequency obeys the same min/max
bound, but its exact numerical forward prediction is a different functional.
With deterministic per-record additive log errors at most epsilon,

    |(qbar_1-qbar_0)/D-H| <= eta+2[epsilon+L(w+delta)]/D.

This conservative enclosure needs no differential record, Gaussian noise,
independence assumption, known source normalization or differentiation of
noisy samples. Invert a strictly positive H interval with reversed endpoints
to enclose 1/H. Intersecting with a supplied compact parameter class is a
conditional restriction, not evidence for the class. The theorem says all
class members compatible with the stated record lie in the enclosure; it does
not establish injectivity of all nuisance parameters or of arbitrary records.

For angular tracking, set q=-log|theta| and replace eta by a justified uniform
angular-rate error. Absolute angular errors do not become uniform log errors
as theta tends to zero. Supplied log-angle errors assume relative resolving
power. Unknown frame rotation, centroid motion, wrong image identification,
arbitrary source drift, or an unverified tail remove the premise. They are not
bounded merely by listing them.

CPR1's principal preparation has d(R)=int_0^(1/R) dq/[V(Eq+V)] and
P-Omega U=Omega d, where P=int_(1/R)^(1/a) b dq/s and
U=int_(1/R)^(1/a) b^2 dq/[s(1+s)]. These original incidence equations support
an independent implementation. For E=1 an exact proper-time radius relation is

 R(ell)=[sqrt(2m)/H*sinh(asinh(H*R0^(3/2)/sqrt(2m))+3H ell/2)]^(2/3).

Under factor-two homothety, corresponding initial Z and theta agree, but at
the SAME calibrated ell the second history is the first evaluated at ell/2.
Thus a sufficiently short finite record can admit both within declared errors;
an indefinitely late derivative is not needed to show this ambiguity. A
midpoint record is admissible only if each complete finite record, including
its declared windows, lies within the per-history errors. The midpoint witness
is synthetic, not a data fit or proof that a real instrument supplies it.
