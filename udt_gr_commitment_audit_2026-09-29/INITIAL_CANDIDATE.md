## 3. Four different conservation statements

(i) The identity div G=0 follows from Levi-Civita Bianchi geometry for every
smooth metric. (ii) A matter stress tensor may be conserved when its matter
equations hold, under the stated matter-coupling assumptions. (iii) Requiring
div E[g]=0 identically for an unidentified metric response is a new constraint
on an operator, not a synonym for (i) or (ii). (iv) A conserved label measure
such as G351 has a different object type. None automatically selects E.

For a hypothetical metric-plus-scalar action, fix conventions
delta I=integral sqrt(|g|)[E_ab delta g^ab+P delta psi]. A compactly supported
diffeomorphism gives delta g^ab=-2 nabla^(a X^b), delta psi=X^b partial_b psi.
After integration by parts,

    2 nabla^a E_ab + P partial_b psi = 0.

Thus setting the scalar equation P=0 is a real hypothesis if one concludes
div E=0. The metric-only case lacks the scalar term. This comparison introduces
no UDT scalar field and chooses no matter action. With multiple fields the
corresponding Euler terms must be retained. It also says nothing about a global
conserved energy of an arbitrary cosmological spacetime.

Under DDR, E=lambda(x)g on solutions; div E=0 then implies d lambda=0.
It does not establish an off-shell identity or determine lambda's value.
Within the pre-existing conditional class E=a Ric+b Rg, constants a,b and a!=0,
DDR already gives Ric=Rg/4 and Bianchi already gives dR=0. Off-shell conservation
only imposes b=-a/2 in that two-term class. Allowing an added constant trace gives
E=aG+Cg. DDR still tests TF(E)=0; replacing it with E=0 would add an equation.
In particular E=0 would fix R=4C/a whereas DDR leaves connected-region R free.
No physical source, absolute scale or new clock prediction is obtained here.

## 4. A weaker question about a representative

DDR sees S=TF(E), not an arbitrary trace addition. As CRV1 established, for
a specified S on a FIXED metric, write E=S+qg and j=div S. Then div E=0 iff
dq=-j. On a contractible patch this is solvable iff dj=0; globally j must be
exact. Passing this fixed-metric test does not ensure q is a local natural
finite-jet operator for all trial metrics or an Euler response.

This suggests a careful exploratory criterion, NOT an adoption:
"Does the identified response class S admit a smooth local natural conserved
representative on its declared metric domain?" It does not prescribe a trace,
an action or a derivative order. The fixed-metric curl test is a necessary local
diagnostic for this stronger operator-level question, not a sufficient proof.
It must be assessed off shell; on S=0 solutions its curl test is vacuous.

For S=a(Ric-Rg/4), the known completion is q=-aR/4+C. CRV1's explicitly
unadopted S=TF(R Ric) has a fixed-metric obstruction; that rules out completing
that comparison on its witness patch, not UDT. Nonvanishing multiplication of
S can preserve its zero set while changing its divergence/variational properties.
Hence conservation can constrain response representatives more than metric
solutions. Physical normalization/meaning cannot be inferred from a zero set.
