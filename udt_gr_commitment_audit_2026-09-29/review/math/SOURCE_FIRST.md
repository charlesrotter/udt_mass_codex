## 1. The conservation statements must retain their quantifiers

Let E[g] be the specified symmetric response and S=TF_g(E). On the registered
regular all-pair Lorentz4 domain, DDR states S=0 on an admitted solution.
Equivalently E=λ(x)g there. Metric compatibility then gives div E=dλ.
Thus arbitrary DDR alone neither says E=0 nor ensures conservation of that
representative. If div E=0 is required only on DDR solutions, it says λ is
constant on each connected solution region. It says nothing yet about E on
metrics that do not solve DDR.

The stronger identity div E[g]≡0 for every metric in the operator's domain is
off shell. It can restrict the formula for E. It is not implied by tensorial
coordinate transformation, by choosing normal coordinates, or by the all-pair
balance statement. Curvature remains in normal coordinates; Bianchi is an
identity of that curvature, not an identity for every natural response.

For the conditional two-term class with universal constants,

    E=a Ric+b Rg,
    TF(E)=a(Ric-Rg/4),
    div E=(a/2+b)dR.

If a≠0, DDR gives Ric=Rg/4. Bianchi then gives dR/4=0, so R is constant and
Ric=Λg on a connected regular solution region. Consequently div E vanishes
on those solutions for EVERY constant b. Requiring only on-solution conservation
there adds no restriction on b. Requiring the off-shell identity instead gives
b=-a/2, provided the domain admits varying-R metrics as the classification
domain does. No action is needed for this calculation.

This distinction also prevents a change of equation: G301 classifies zero sets
of E=0. On symmetric Ricci data the trace-free eigenvalue is a and the trace
eigenvalue is a+4b. For a≠0 and a+4b≠0, E=0 is Ric=0; for a≠0 and a+4b=0,
E=0 is TF(Ric)=0. DDR, in contrast, imposes TF(E)=0 for EVERY b when a≠0.
Thus the conserving representative aG belongs to G301's generic E=0 class,
but conservation plus DDR still allows the connected constant Λ. Adding E=0
would be a separate stronger field equation.

For S=a TF(Ric), scalar completion gives

    div(S+qg)=(a/4)dR+dq,
    q=-aR/4+C,
    E=aG+Cg.

On a DDR solution, E=(C-aΛ)g. DDR does not set C-aΛ to zero. E=0 would set
Λ=C/a for a≠0. This is precisely the distinction between completing a
representative and selecting the solution's scalar datum. A fixed Cg is excluded
from full-E weight zero, but allowed as an invisible trace representative in
RMS1's weaker shape-only route. A constant found separately on each supplied
metric is not automatically a universal operator coefficient.

Unoriented naturality is retained in the sufficient route. It should not be

already gives a two-dimensional invariant space under all infinitesimal Lorentz

The GRS1 limiting argument is reconstructible without a finite-degree search.
For normal jets J2,...,Jr and g_s=s^-2 D_s* g, naturality and weight zero give

    F(s²J2,s³J3,...,s^r Jr)=s²F(J2,...,Jr).

The zero jet gives F(0)=0. On each admitted fixed finite jet ray, its norm is
O(s²), so differentiability gives a remainder o(s²). Divide by s² and let
s→0. Only D2F(0)J2 remains, exactly. If the lowest nonzero jet has higher order,
the remainder is even smaller; r<2 gives zero. Flat regularity and the entire
ray are essential to THIS limit, but this does not prove them necessary for
every route to Einstein geometry. The remaining linear contraction step agrees
with [Navarro–Sancho, Theorem 5.1](https://arxiv.org/html/0709.1928v2).
The tensor's precise regular/natural domain must accompany that theorem;
the paper's GR stress-energy interpretation is not imported into UDT.

RMS1's reading distinction also survives: a covariant tensor of weight w has
unit-timelike readings of weight w-2 when U rescales by ell^-1. If a quadratic
form vanishes on every timelike vector it vanishes identically, since that cone
is open. Null contractions instead leave the metric line, as n and -n remove
mixed entries and a constant quadratic form on the unit spatial sphere fixes
the spatial matrix. Hence shape readings can determine S's weight only AFTER
the measured object is identified as S. The DCI1 curvature readout does not
perform that response identification. No DCI1 dynamics or detector model was
introduced in this review.

## 3. Conservation completion and action converse

On one fixed smooth metric, every tensor with specified trace-free part S is
E=S+qg. Setting j=div S gives dq=-j. A smooth local q exists on a contractible
region iff dj=0. Necessity is d²q=0 and sufficiency is Poincare's lemma. On
general domains exactness, including periods, is required. This does not supply
a natural finite-jet primitive or an action; it is a scalar PDE on supplied
geometry. Testing only S=0 makes j=0 and cannot certify an operator identity.

Independent curvature construction on the different local diagnostic metric

    g=diag(-(2+x²+y⁴)²,1,1,1)

gives R=-4(6y²+1)/(x²+y⁴+2). For S=TF(R Ric), direct covariant divergence
agrees with j=Ric(grad R,·), and (dj)_xy at x=y=1 is -27/4. Thus no smooth
scalar trace completion exists near that event. This is a comparison tensor
outside the weight-zero/nondegenerate flat-response route, not a UDT
countermodel or failed empirical GR candidate. Ric itself has div Ric_x=7/4
at that event while div G is identically zero, a nonvacuous representative
control. This independently confirms the type of CRV1 obstruction using another
metric, without reusing its 16/3 witness implementation.

For a metric-only local diffeomorphism-invariant action defined by
δI=∫sqrt(|g|) E_ab δg^ab, compactly supported diffeomorphisms have
δg^ab=-2∇^(a ξ^b). Integration by parts gives div E≡0. This assumes the
action's variational interpretation; tensor covariance alone does not give it.
Changing variation variable for the SAME action gives

    T^ab=-sqrt(|g|)E^ab

as the covariant-metric Euler density. This is CRV1 repair 1, not the initial
candidate's plus sign. Both signs can satisfy an action-existence theorem, but
they belong to opposite actions.

The primary [Anderson–Pohjanpelto theorem](https://arxiv.org/html/1202.5811v1)
was inspected at its definitions and Theorem 1. Natural symmetric metric
tensor-density operators of order at most three satisfying the off-shell
density-divergence identity are locally variational. Its everywhere-smooth
case supplies a natural Lagrangian in dimension four. Fixed arbitrary signature
is allowed. This is a conditional literature result, not my proof and not an
arbitrary-order converse, uniqueness result or globally defined action theorem
on every manifold. Finite jet locality alone supplies no order-three bound.
These hypotheses mean one must not claim that an action always needs a new
independent physical postulate. Conversely, invoking the theorem cannot identify
the physical response or justify its unproved hypotheses.

## 4. Matter conservation and carried-measure conservation

An explicitly unadopted scalar-matter comparison makes the quantifier visible.
For L_m=-1/2(∇ψ)²-V(ψ), define
T_ab=∂aψ∂bψ-g_ab((∇ψ)²/2+V). Product differentiation gives

    ∇^a T_ab=(Box ψ-V'(ψ))∂bψ.

Thus matter equations imply conservation in this model. It is not the
metric-only off-shell divergence identity for arbitrary E[g]. The total
diffeomorphism identity contains the independent matter Euler expression before
that equation is used. This comparison adopts no matter field, action, coupling
or physical source for UDT. A flat-metric exact control with ψ=t²+x³/3 and
V=ψ³/3 has div T_t=-32/9 at t=x=1, so the identity does not falsely assert
off-shell conservation. A metric equation E=κT, if separately supplied, and an
off-shell div E identity would require compatible source conservation; neither
selects E or κ by itself.

cuts cannot by itself identify a symmetric matter tensor, response E, energy
per crossing or matter Euler equation. Only that typing distinction is reused
