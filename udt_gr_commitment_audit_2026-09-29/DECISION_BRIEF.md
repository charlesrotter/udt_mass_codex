# GCA1 decision brief — provisional GR commitments

This is a decision aid for the scoped audit, not an adoption record. Review state
and any correction belong to REVIEWED_RESULT and the actual separate reviews.

## What changes in our understanding

The question "should we accept conservation?" is too broad to decide responsibly.
Ordinary matter conservation, a geometric identity, and requiring the unknown UDT
response to be conserved for every trial geometry are different commitments.
The last one can strongly restrict a law without being forced by the first two.

Several useful starting commitments are already explicit: one shared geometry,
ordinary local clocks, local SR form and metric causality. They retain their
working labels. Current GR agreement is a filter on candidates; it does not
silently supply a response law. These need no fresh adoption in this audit.

## Recommendation and alternatives

Recommended next use of conservation: for an independently motivated candidate
response, investigate whether its shape part admits a local covariant conserved
representative. Use this as a declared conditional diagnostic. It has a concrete
local curl test, with the stronger natural-operator and global questions exposed.
This is not yet a mandatory UDT postulate and cannot replace identifying the
physical response being balanced. Without adopting it, all present conditional
results remain available at their existing scope.

A stronger option would provisionally postulate that conserved-representative
property for the physical response on a specified domain. Its gain would be a
new elimination rule for candidates; its cost would be excluding candidates
without a presently established native reason. Any such adoption needs an exact
response definition/domain and a separate owner decision. This audit does not
make that decision or presume the resulting rule is true.

Still stronger choices—second derivative order, exact response scaling, a chosen
action, or full Einstein dynamics—can already select Einstein-type responses.
They must be considered separately with their consequences visible. Within the
existing conditional Einstein shape class, conservation adds no new positional
clock prediction. It completes a response representative; imposing its full zero
would add an equation that reciprocity did not supply.

## Counterevidence and what it means

An exact unadopted comparison includes an Einstein term and conservation yet has
a different reciprocal shape equation because a curvature-squared term remains.
This shows those broad requirements alone do not select the response in that
comparison arena. It does not identify a viable UDT alternative or establish a
new physical field, parameter, empirical recovery or stable model.

Exact linear curvature response does not mean linear metric behavior. Conversely,
the expected nonlinear positional behavior does not justify inserting a selected
curvature correction. Neither analogy closes the physical identification.
