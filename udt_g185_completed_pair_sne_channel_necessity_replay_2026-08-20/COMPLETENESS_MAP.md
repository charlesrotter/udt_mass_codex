- full primary spherical metric pullback for the supplied radial pair plane;
- completed density/depth ordering;
- separate central-spherical sky Jacobi area;
- Pantheon+ and DES-SN5YR observed tables and covariance products;
- frozen P1 radius-frequency calibration;
- imported transfer `eta epsilon=1/Z`;
- one declared magnitude offset per catalog;

## Omitted by design

- displaced or nonspherical observers;
- nonradial pair germs and ambient mixing;
- caustic and multiple-image branch aggregation;
- polarization, absorption, scattering, and native carrier dynamics;
- derivation or selection of `R(Z)` and `phi(r)`;
- global completion and numerical `X_max`;
- BAO, CMB, dynamics, sources, matter, bootstrap, and signalling.

## Maximum conclusion

G185 can establish bounded channel compatibility and frozen observational non-regression only. It
cannot establish a native light law, a unique physical history, a global pair family, or a complete
cosmology.
