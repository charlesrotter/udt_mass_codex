# G243 map — reciprocal SNe radial representation

Date: 2026-08-24

## Lay question

SNe redshift gives reciprocal depth directly. Their processed brightness information then gives a
conditional estimate of radial area distance. Can the two de-overlapped SNe releases supply one
smooth observational representation of that radial relationship, including derivatives, without
using angular data or asking BAO to choose the curve?

## What this is

This is a statistical measuring surface placed over the SNe observations. It is not a new UDT
equation and its spline coefficients are not physical constants. The representation is selected by
SNe-only generalized cross-validation across a preregistered numerical census.

## What this is not

It does not use an angular orchestra to create redshift. It does not fit BAO, select a physical
history, derive radiative transfer, or infer `X_max`. If the representation is nonmonotone, that is
reported rather than repaired.
