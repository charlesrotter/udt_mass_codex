# G158 lay report

The gears were already assembled.

The registered regular gauge does not contain ten instruments that must be synchronized by a new
external director. Its ten coframe channels form one machine:

- three clock/ruler instruments;
- three screen or angular instruments;
- four instruments describing how those two sectors mix.

The first six automatically change how the four mixing instruments are carried. Their relative
volumes may vary with regime while the gauge-fixed relation still composes exactly. This does not
yet classify every coframe gauge or make the component volumes observable.

The simplest new object is the coframe's logarithmic rate of change—roughly, the chord being played
at that instant. When a complete history is supplied, this score comes directly from the changing
coframe. The observer query contributes a second, separately typed motion term. Both enter before
the final reciprocal readout.

So we no longer need to hunt for a rule forcing the orchestra into one fixed balance. What remains
is to determine the actual time/distance history and the physical observer query. The algebra tells
us how their music is organized, not yet which composition the Universe plays.
