## Candidate exact statements

### A. Scalar reciprocal-generator theorem

Let `delta` carry the founded additive reciprocal-depth group. Supply an additive parameter `s` and
require a continuous composition-compatible map

```text
gamma(s+t)=gamma(s)+gamma(t).
```

The preregistered target is that `gamma(s)=k s`, hence

```text
D(s)=diag(exp(-k s),exp(+k s)),
chi(s)=tanh(k s).
```

The constant `k` changes inversely under positive rescaling of the supplied parameter and therefore
does not attach `s` to physical distance or time. This is to be classified as a conditional
reparameterization theorem, not a physical history law.

An equivalent autonomous-generator formulation will require the vector field on the additive depth
group to commute with every depth translation. The target is that the generator is constant. If
translation equivariance is dropped, an arbitrary function remains and the lane is not finite.

### B. Scalar-plus-Euler nonselection theorem

On an oriented fixed-rank screen sector, the Euler period fixes only the integral of the curvature.
The preregistered same-sector family is

```text
q_a(theta,s) = 1 + a(s) P_2(cos(theta)),
P_2(x)=(3x^2-1)/2,
```

where `a(s)` is any smooth supplied function small enough for any auxiliary positivity condition
one elects to impose. The sphere integral of the `P_2` term is zero for every `s`, so the total
Euler flux remains fixed while the local flux profile varies. The scalar reciprocal flow and the
Euler period do not constrain `a(s)`.

### C. Architecture funnel

The already-banked G259 result will be treated as an inherited theorem, not re-proved or widened:

```text
local + metric-only + two-jet + identity-divergence-free + rank two
    -> a G_ab + b g_ab

```

G293 will test whether A and B close the alternatives. The preregistered expectation is that they do
not: scalar composition only fixes parametrization, and topology only fixes a sector. A genuinely
departing UDT law must then explicitly supply at least one architecture outside the G259 class:

1. higher metric differential order;
2. a larger covariant complete relation state with a nonidentity generator;
3. a specified global/nonlocal admissibility or propagation law;
4. or Einstein metric propagation plus an independently owned source/population history.

This list is an architecture partition, not a list of candidate mechanisms and not a proof of
exhaustiveness over every conceivable mathematical formulation.
