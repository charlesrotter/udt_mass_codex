## Independent positive argument and precise hypotheses

    sup_a |w(x,a)| <= exp(C x0)(x/x0)[M+C x0 log(x0/x)]

if receivers actually enter and remain in a common compact normal collar, the same coefficient bound C applies, and their entry momenta are uniformly bounded by M. Transport to this collar needs a common smooth finite transit/crossing and no loss of the admitted domain; it must not be assumed from compactness alone. Likewise endpoint existence or a common collar for every member is not established merely by compact initial data.

The bound gives uniform T=u/x alignment to the normal and |y(x,a)-p(a)|=O(x^2(1+|log x|)). As (x,a)->(0,a*) along that family, xZ->1/B*. This is useful uniform control beyond the single-clock theorem, while retaining its supplied geometry.

The pointwise FCL estimate alone proves C1 in x along each receiver, not continuous differentiability in all preparation labels. ## Exact positive and adverse controls derived independently

Use supplied g=x^-2(-dx^2+dy^2+dz^2+dw^2), x>0, future decreasing x. Initial surface x=1 has Euclidean proper distance, origin y=0, and a radial receiver starts at y=L>0. Fix emission e=(1,0) with the comoving ordinary emitter. For finite constant geodesic momentum P,

    u^x=-x sqrt(1+P^2 x^2), u^y=P x^2,
    y(x,L)=L+[sqrt(1+P^2)-sqrt(1+P^2 x^2)]/P,
    A(P)=[sqrt(1+P^2)-1]/P,
    Y(L)=y(0,L)=L+A(P).

Expressions extend continuously at P=0. The unit normalization and conserved physical spatial momentum check the free-clock equations. Null incidence is y=1-x, equivalently

    H(x,L)=x+Y(L)-1+[1-sqrt(1+P^2 x^2)]/P=0,

H(1,L)=L>0 and H(0,L)=Y(L)-1. Hence for these receivers there is one actual interior outgoing reception iff Y(L)<1 (for L>0 and this preparation). Its frequency ratio is

    Z=1/[x(sqrt(1+P^2 x^2)-Px)].
