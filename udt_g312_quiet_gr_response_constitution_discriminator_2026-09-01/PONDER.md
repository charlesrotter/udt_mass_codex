# G312 ponder — what must “reduces to GR” mean?

Date: 2026-09-01

Three meanings must not be conflated:

1. **Solution overlap:** familiar Ricci-flat GR metrics are admitted.
2. **Principal overlap:** small local perturbations have GR's nondegenerate causal principal system.
3. **Complete quiet response germ:** the regular local response near the quiet curvature origin has
   the GR first variation and no additional scale, field, or nonlocal carry.

Solution overlap alone is weak: a curvature-squared response vanishes on every Ricci-flat metric but
has zero first variation at flat space. Principal overlap excludes that degeneracy, but nonlocal
nonlinear corrections can retain the same quiet first variation. The derivation must identify the
smallest exact strengthening that excludes those competitors without importing Einstein's equation
by name.

Candidate closing language, not adopted here:

> The vacuum UDT metric response is a regular local natural response of the complete metric whose
> quiet response germ has the GR causal principal part and introduces no independent operator scale.

The test must decide whether this is already owned, is one coherent new premise, or still hides
multiple independent choices.
