# G312 lay report

We found the next gap, and it is much smaller than “we need an unknown field equation.”

The present record proves that familiar GR vacuum shapes fit inside UDT. That is like proving a car
can sit on a road. It does not yet prove that, when nudged, the car accelerates and steers exactly as
GR predicts. A curvature-squared rule can accept all the same parked GR solutions while having the
wrong small-disturbance behavior. So the intended claim “UDT becomes GR in the quiet regime” must
explicitly include GR's local dynamical response, not only shared solutions.

Even after making that clarification, a second choice remains. A law can use only the metric and
respect the light cones while still remembering a whole earlier region through a retarded global
calculation. That would match GR locally near quiet conditions but would not be a local equation.
Nothing currently says UDT must reject such history-dependent laws; co-presence makes that question
particularly real rather than merely technical.

If Charles provisionally supplies both statements—full quiet dynamical GR overlap and a local
finite-jet vacuum response—then the remaining freedom collapses to the already classified G301
family. Universal Reciprocity immediately reduces that family to the trace-free Ricci equation.
No fitting or new coefficient is needed for that closure.

So G312 is progress, but not an automatic field-law derivation. It replaces a vague missing-law
problem with two plain questions:

1. Does “UDT reduces to GR” mean the full local dynamics, not merely shared GR solutions?
2. Must the UDT metric response be local, or may causal co-presence carry nonlocal history?

The metric and reciprocal kernel were not changed.

Fresh external review agreed. After one packaging-only repair, the sealed package replayed from an
empty temporary directory and G312 was accepted with this exact two-premise boundary.
