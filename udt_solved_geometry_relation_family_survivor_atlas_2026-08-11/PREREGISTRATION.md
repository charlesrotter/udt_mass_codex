## Whole question

When the currently banked endpoint-descended and path-labelled observer-relation homes are carried
through actual metric-derived comparison, transport, geodesic/Jacobi, and holonomy equations on
matched complete regular geometries, which structures persist under symmetric perturbations and
which become singular, branch-valued, route-sensitive, or globally incomplete?

This does **not** ask which family resembles a desired universe, particle, spectrum, or known
theory. It does not assume that one family must win. It does not call perturbation persistence
physical or dynamical stability.

## Bounded regime

- source-owned regular complete stationary/global witnesses where the frozen sources expose enough
  geometry to reconstruct them;
- source-owned complete time-live local coframe histories, explicitly off-shell when no native
  history law is owned;
- endpoint-descended and path-labelled comparison homes evaluated on matched geometry whenever
  typing permits;
- regular Lorentzian strata, with null/rank loss, cut loci, conjugate points, and global failure
  retained as classifications rather than filtered out;
- CPU integration only, with exact or high-precision anchors and a separate implementation.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- no action, source, matter carrier, bootstrap target, GR field equation, or imported observer
  mechanics;
- no physical stability, dynamics, signalling, particle, mass, CMB, or SNe claim;
- no shortest/smoothest/most symmetric branch selection;
- no setting angular, shift, mixing, route, or time dependence to zero as a verdict;
- no use of the protected curvature atlas or stopped native-on-shell draft;
- no physical filtering by expected shape or outcome.

## Preregistered landing classes

Exactly one primary landing will be used:

1. `BOUNDED_GEOMETRIC_DISCRIMINATOR_OBSERVED` — one typed relation home loses regular/global
   admissibility throughout a preregistered matched family while another retains it; this remains
   witness-family evidence, not universal physical selection.
2. `MULTIPLE_GEOMETRIC_SURVIVOR_FAMILIES` — endpoint and path-labelled homes both retain regular
   perturbation-open witnesses, so geometry in the tested class does not select between them.
3. `REGIME_SPLIT_GEOMETRIC_SURVIVORS` — different relation homes persist on different declared
   strata or global branches without a universal winner.
4. `INSUFFICIENT_OWNED_SOLVED_GEOMETRY_DATA` — the frozen sources do not own enough metric/query
   data to construct the promised matched solve without adding a premise.
5. `TYPE_FAILURE_IN_MATCHED_COMPARISON` — the homes cannot be evaluated on one matched declared
   query without an unowned identification.

Maximum allowed conclusion: a bounded geometric persistence/survivor classification and a precise next
missing owner. No native evolution law, action, source, bootstrap selection, or physical stability
may be inferred.
