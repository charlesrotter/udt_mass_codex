# Ponder map — from a gear pile to running geometry

The G62 result showed that the pieces can assemble in two mathematical machines. This phase turns
their handles on explicit complete geometries instead of deriving another identity.

The observer-network analogy is:

- the complete metric/coframe is the machine body;
- the calibrated observer query chooses where the shafts attach;
- endpoint descent is a direct gear train;
- path-labelled transport is a gear train that can retain a rotation after a loop;
- geodesic/Jacobi evolution tests whether nearby shafts focus, separate, or encounter a caustic;
- perturbation persistence asks whether the construction survives a small machining tolerance.

The test must not confuse a machine that runs with the physically selected machine. It also must
not confuse geometric persistence with dynamical stability, which requires an owned evolution law.

The potentially informative result is an open region of complete geometries on which a relation
home remains regular, causal by its declared local type, globally composable, and numerically
well-conditioned—or a rigorous account of where it stops doing so.
