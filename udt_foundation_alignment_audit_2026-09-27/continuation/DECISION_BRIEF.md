evaluator. UDT's intended positional-clock interpretation is now explicit in

analogy. W4 provisionally supplies one local geometry for clocks, rulers, free
fall and null propagation. Given a complete metric and a regular signal query,
the existing mathematics links clock comparisons, sky angles and round-trip

coefficients; other records can then test that same conditional model.

One distinction needs to remain visible: **clock-rate dilation and elapsed
signal time are different records**. In the existing flat comparison, clocks
can run at equal rates while a round trip takes positive time. That rules out
the added reading that all flight time must vanish with endpoint rate mismatch.
It does not refute your broader positional interpretation or prove that another
physical propagation mechanism is needed. The diagnostic reading is not being
attributed to you or adopted as a UDT premise.

- **Can a supplied geometry predict measurements?** Yes, conditionally. A real
  test still needs justified apparatus, layout, probe/relay assumptions and errors.
- **Does UDT itself require that geometry or response?** The existing native
  response-class membership route remains unclosed. A GR equation cannot fill it
  merely because it gives familiar answers.
- **Would the result distinguish UDT from GR?** Only if owned predictions differ.
  Shared metric and measurement rules give shared records.
