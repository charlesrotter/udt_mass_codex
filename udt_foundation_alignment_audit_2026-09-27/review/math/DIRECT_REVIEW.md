## Objections, survivors, and smallest repairs

### D01 — null-frequency depth is not automatically terminal or endpoint depth

Location: AUDIT A05's interlock formula and THEORY_BRIEF's statement that the
null-transport construction “relates them” after naming terminal, directed and
mutual quantities.

Defect: the formula reuses `delta` without defining it as the independently
constructed null-frequency depth. The brief's antecedent also suggests a
universal connection to terminal `Phi`. G269 owns an identity between the
frequency ratio of endpoint clocks on a supplied affine null branch and an
independently transported mutual scalar. It does not assert that this frequency
depth equals every G170 endpoint-potential difference or G176 terminal value.
The sources match these on their declared primary reduction. Moving observers
already supply a failure case: a flat metric's fixed static primary profile does
not determine the Doppler frequency depth for arbitrary endpoint clocks.

Survivor: `Gamma_PT=cosh(delta_null)+(exp(-delta_null)/2)|W|^2` and the sharp
mutual-clock inequality remain correct, as does matched static agreement.

Minimum repair: define `omega_X=-g(k_X,U_X)>0` and
`delta_null=-log(omega_A/omega_B)` in A05; use that symbol in the interlock.
State the matched-primary restriction before identifying it with endpoint depth.
In the brief name the directional frequency and independently transported mutual
outputs actually related, and avoid a universal terminal-scalar join. This is a
load-bearing type repair, not an algebra change.

### D02 — the brief overbroadens the reciprocal pair and scalar W5 reduction

Location: THEORY_BRIEF's second paragraph, beginning with a supplied ordered
depth and saying a regular calibrated pair has `T=exp(-delta)`, `L=exp(delta)`.

Defect: a general regular calibrated Lorentzian pair need not satisfy `TL=1`.

Survivor: the displayed formulas are exact in the matched reciprocal reduction;
W5's physical interpretation remains owner-adopted working, not derived/canon.

Minimum repair: explicitly say matched planar/radial completed reciprocal
reduction, in declared units, and add that W5's general relation retains the

### D05 — state the weak-field qualifier in the lecture comparison

Location: AUDIT A08's limited static, slow-motion account of “curvature of time.”

Defect: the familiar Newtonian reduction invoked for that description also assumes
a weak field. Leaving out that qualifier makes the comparison less precise than
the primary comparison reference used by the candidate. This is a small repair.

Survivor: the critique of a universal time-only description and of lapse variation
as sufficient evidence of curvature. Independently, flat Minkowski coordinates

`ds^2=-a^2 x^2 d tau^2+dx^2+dy^2+dz^2`; the clock coefficient varies while curvature
is zero. A periodic spatial projection likewise need not close its spacetime
worldline because time continues to advance.

Minimum repair: say “weak-field, static, slow-motion approximation.” Carroll's
definition lists those three limits. The equivalence-principle finite-region
