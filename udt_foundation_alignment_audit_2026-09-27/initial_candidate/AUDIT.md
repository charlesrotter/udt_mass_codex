## A02. Reciprocal structure is legitimate conditionally

For positive diagonal action `P=diag(u,v)` and the off-diagonal bilinear form `K`,
`P^T K P=(uv)K`. Preservation of that particular dual form gives `uv=1`.
This is the content of Dual Reciprocity, an independent physical premise. Ordinary
preservation of a clock/ruler ratio would instead constrain `u/v`; it cannot be
substituted for the dual action. Continuity and positive one-parameter composition
give exponential form after the declared nontrivial generator/sign/unit choices.
Neither step alone derives a spacetime field equation or metric history.

The reciprocal kernel is therefore not arbitrary algebra, but its mathematical
consistency is not proof of its physical adequacy or of the non-transfer thesis.

## A04. Nonlinear redshift is present; the physical assignment is open

`chi=tanh(delta)` gives `exp(delta)=sqrt((1+chi)/(1-chi))` for `-1<chi<1`.
Under the matched static conserved-frequency redshift attachment, orientation
matters: `delta_z=log(1+z)=-delta_source,observer`. Defining
`chi_z=tanh(delta_z)` yields `1+z=sqrt((1+chi_z)/(1-chi_z))` and the nonlinear
climb without a second fitted response.
It does not supply that attachment in every geometry, identify `chi` with proper,
radar, luminosity or areal distance, prove physical access to `chi=1`, or select
`X_max`. A redshift profile versus physical distance needs the missing assignment.
Those distinctions reconcile “no second response profile” with “depth data open.”

## A08. The lecture helps intuition, with limits

The complete supplied text was read: 415 lines, ending mid-sentence. It is a
simulated lecture, not authenticated Feynman testimony. Its useful viewpoint is
operational clocks, free fall and geometry. It supplies no adopted UDT postulate.

Its finite rocket/elevator comparison overstates local equivalence; tidal effects
matter across finite regions. A varying clock coefficient alone does not establish
curvature (accelerated coordinates in flat spacetime are a counterexample).
“Curvature of time” alone describes a limited static, slow-motion approximation,
not general gravity. Proper-time extremization requires specified endpoints and
local qualifications; the drifting-to-maximize-age story omits them. A closed
spatial orbit is not a closed timelike spacetime geodesic. The lecture explicitly
describes disturbances propagating at c, so it is not evidence for the proposed
non-transfer ontology. Its quantum/entropic suggestions are speculative material.

Einstein already treated positional gravitational clock rates and redshift. The
UDT novelty should be the proposed reciprocal/positional interpretation and its
specific new consequences, not a claim that Einstein missed ordinary gravitational
time dilation. External comparison references, not UDT premises:
