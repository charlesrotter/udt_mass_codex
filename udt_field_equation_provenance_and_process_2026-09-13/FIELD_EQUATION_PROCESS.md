# A process for deriving or proposing UDT field equations

FEP1 process proposal, subject to source-fidelity review. This document supplies
no scientific premise. Charles authorized this planning/recovery task, not adoption
of an action, response formula or law. Current sources and owner decisions control.

## Objective

Produce a specified, testable law for the metric's allowed configurations and
development, with a clear argument for each physical ingredient. The first target
can be a regular local source-free domain. It need not select one universe, derive
all matter or prove global completion before becoming a useful scoped theory.
Source-free scope itself must be stated; it is not inferred from empty notation.

These are reviewable deliverables rather than fixed calendar promises. A stage may
return a defect, an unowned premise or a decision packet. No further computation
can turn a missing physical assertion into an accepted one by accumulating examples.
Source recovery is now complete at its named scope; this is not a mandate for
another whole-history audit before every proposal.

## Rules that the recovered history makes necessary

**Specify a law, not only a notation.** Naming E[g] or setting a residual equal to
a source reconstructed from that same residual does not constrain a geometry.
Identify what would fail the proposed equation and why that is physically relevant.

**Specify the law and its variables/variations together.** The full metric and
completed pairs retain density, angular/screen, mixing and shift information.
Do not treat terminal phi as a new independent field or reciprocity normalization
as a global fixed-volume constraint. If a constrained law is deliberately proposed,
declare its constraint and what equations it retains; unrestricted variation is
not automatically more physical. Actions and variational principles are choices
unless independently justified. Response-first need not become action-first later;
it is a pragmatic recommendation. Action-first remains eligible on the same
transparent proposed-premise footing, with no exemption from the motivation gate.

**Reuse conditional classifications.** G259, G301 and G310/G311 already do major
parts of the algebra. G301's exact and RF1's leading-limit scopes differ. Once
class membership is justified or explicitly proposed, use the matching theorem;
do not rederive a familiar result to hide the premise choice. A mathematical
lowest-order option is not automatically the physical law. Exact curvature-weight
homogeneity is not implied merely by c_E or by the absence of a chosen length.

**Compare laws, not cosmetic formulas.** Track the equation/solution class under
the exact allowed equivalence. G301's invertible trace changes and DDR's invisible
trace additions demonstrate real redundancies. Do not globalize that equivalence
to source coupling or an arbitrary differential transformation. Conversely,
agreement on selected backgrounds does not prove equal perturbations, nonlinear
developments or predictions; ND1/ND2 already provide the relevant warning.

**Handle dimensions and scope directly.** Mixed curvature orders may require
dimensionful coefficients; pure higher-order actions do not all introduce a new
length merely by being higher order. Every proposed coefficient, domain, scale
and physical normalization must be accounted for. c_E/G_obs are observed anchors;
X_max supplies no local cutoff, seal, boundary term or chosen operator length.

**Separate proof from proposal and testing.** Use DERIVED, OWNER_PROVISIONAL,
CONDITIONAL, PROPOSED_UNADOPTED, CONTROL or OPEN at every dependency. A proposed
principle can be developed transparently without being called native derivation.
External comparison success can support it empirically at that scope; it cannot
retroactively make an added premise a theorem of the original metric/kernel.

## Normal verification cycle

For an authorized equation construction: explore and record alternatives; freeze
the first candidate and exact scope; check original equations and hypotheses;
obtain fresh separate-context adversarial review; preserve objections and use a
bounded same-premise repair/re-review. Return the reviewed conditional candidate,
refutation or unresolved issue and a lay premise/decision packet. Promotion and
physical adoption remain separate decisions. Failed numerical searches are not
nonexistence proofs; old static successes are not time-evolution/stability proofs.

concrete dynamical-principle proposal for Charles, rather than launching a large
solve from an equation that has not yet been justified or adopted.
