## Independent initial assessment

1. **The two law orders deserve a fair comparison.** G20 explicitly keeps
   response-first and action-first open. Response-first must define a response
   relation with its object and legitimate tangents; it does not receive an
   arbitrary tensor by notation. Action-first must supply a functional and its
   variation domain together; stationarity, unrestricted variation, locality,
   derivative order and boundary treatment must not enter invisibly. An action
   can be tested later as an integrable generator, but that ordering is a
   process proposal rather than a proved UDT requirement.
2. **The surviving mathematical classifications are useful and conditional.**
   G259 fixes the nonzero Einstein zero set only in its specified natural
   metric two-jet identity-divergence-free class with quiet-flat inclusion.
   G301 fixes two residual classes only in its smooth, exactly weight-one,
   unoriented natural symmetric rank-two curvature-order/principal lane.
   Neither says UDT owns its entire lane. Matching GR mathematics is not a
   defect; importing the class because it guarantees that answer is.
3. **Current G312 authority is decisive.** DDR and Local Metric Sufficiency
   remain owner-provisional; the stronger quiet GR principal-response overlap
   does not. DDR gives TF(E)=0 for a supplied symmetric response on the admitted
   all-pair domain. It does not identify E with Ricci. The historical G312
   class-membership route is unclosed under the current filter-only GR reading.
   That does not prove independent closure impossible or a new premise necessary.
4. **Historical defects need separate bins.** Arm C's sign errors and
   restrict-before-vary loss of normal equations are mathematical issues.
   Unadopted action/field/order hypotheses are conditional scope. Strong-CSN
   and finite-cell ownership in that report are superseded authority. These
   should not be collapsed into “the old equations were wrong.” Its full-cell
   countermodel gates cannot be imported as present prerequisites for every
   local field-theory result.
5. **The next proposal must have a constructive return.** A useful bounded
   task should work through at least one explicitly defined candidate relation
   or action/variation pair and return its actual conditional equation,
   response obstruction, narrowed result, or unresolved substantive objection.
   Re-listing the G259/G301 missing assumptions or rediscovering their already
   known class result is insufficient unless the new work establishes a
   previously open implication from owned UDT premises. A proposed physical
   hypothesis may be discussed as unadopted; execution and adoption are distinct.
6. **Local progress must retain a realistic ceiling.** A law may define a
   local solution space without selecting the realized universe, physical
   source/mass, global data or X_max. Those omissions limit claims; they do not
   automatically invalidate a sound local conditional construction. Conversely,
   a witness or scoped reconstruction cannot establish complete UDT dynamics.
