## Starting material and comparison discipline

Use the calibrated complete metric and pair kernel, completed reciprocity, current
owner-provisional DDR and Local Metric Sufficiency. Use the recovered G20 distinction
between a law and its variational representation. The full regular metric/pair
domain is the starting configuration arena, not proof all metrics are UDT solutions.

The existing conditional trace-free Ricci equation is the **reference candidate**:

    Ric(g) - (R(g)/4)g = 0.

It already follows from DDR inside the full G301 class with a!=0. It is not adopted
as native by this proposal and need not win. Its purpose here is to make the cost
of one fully specified law concrete. Compare the exact G301 route with the genuinely
weaker GL1/RF1 leading-limit route; the latter does not by itself define a complete
equation. Reuse those arguments instead of recalculating their classifications.

For each property needed by a proposed response, give one of: current source
derivation; current owner adoption; a stated new physical hypothesis and why it is
UDT-motivated; or unsupported. Mathematical simplicity/lowest order can motivate a
hypothesis but is not proof that nature chooses it. GR resemblance, observational
success of GR, absence of a chosen length, or an attractive solution is insufficient
to re-label class assumptions as UDT consequences.

An alternative to the reference is eligible only if the proposal identifies its
independent physical motivation and exact difference. At most ONE new hypothesis
package is developed; every constituent assumption is listed separately. No generic
scan of arbitrary actions or extra terms. Do not revive strong CSN/Bach, independent
scalar/carrier, optional source or Maxwell branches as fallback candidates.

## Deliverables and work stages

1. Define the physical question, response, metric/query quantifier and domain. State
   what would violate the proposed law. Specify any independent fields, constants
   or background structures; an unowned extra one stops that candidate's native claim.
2. Construct the smallest motivated principle packet and map it to its equations
   or a rigorously classified equation class. Specify law and variations together
   if a variational route is used. No compulsory action, fixed determinant, preferred
   frame or early symmetry restriction. No source reconstructed from its own residual.
3. Reuse the existing full-metric classification and development evidence where
   hypotheses match. Identify one distinguishing consistency/prediction obligation
   not already answered by a shared background or vacuous linearization. Do not
   execute an observational fit or use its answer to choose the principle.
4. Freeze the candidate and premise delta. Independently check the load-bearing
   argument, covariance, nonidentity/degenerate cases and declared equation
   equivalence. If no new calculation is needed, say so; do not manufacture checks.
5. Obtain a fresh separate-context adversarial review, repair at the same premise
   scope if possible, and return a lay decision packet. No scientific promotion or
   physical adoption is included. Charles may accept, reject or revise the hypothesis.
