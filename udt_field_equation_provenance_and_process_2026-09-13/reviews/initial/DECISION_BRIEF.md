# FEP1 — lay recovery and next direction

We found real earlier field-equation work. Its useful mathematics was not all
invalidated. The problems fall into different categories:

- The conformal/Bach route depended on treating local common rescaling as a
  physical gauge freedom. That was an added postulate and is now inactive.
- Some early action calculations restricted the geometry before varying it and
  therefore lost equations of the proposed full theory. Correct restricted
  mathematics could be mistaken for complete dynamics.
- Some carrier-action signs required genuine correction. Corrected identities
  survive conditionally; they do not establish a native source or mass law.
- The later curvature/reciprocity arguments give strong conditional field equations.
  Their route to native ownership was weakened when GR was clarified to be a
  comparison filter rather than a law-building input.

Removing an old assumption can remove an old objection as well. For example,
inactive strong common-scale gauge no longer rules out considering an EH action;
it also does not make that action a consequence of UDT.

The most useful recovered clue is that **the dynamical law and what is allowed
to vary must be specified together**. An action is one possible formulation.
We can start from UDT's provisionally adopted reciprocity balance and ask what
physical response it governs, then ask whether an action generates that response.

The revised process is: formulate the dynamical principle; state its exact
assumptions and equations; establish consistent development; derive linked
predictions; test them; then extend the theory. We retain the existing conditional
spacetime results and do not demand one uniquely selected universe.

I recommend one bounded **dynamical-principle design packet** next. It may derive
a connection from existing UDT commitments, or propose one clearly labeled new
hypothesis with a UDT-specific reason, alternatives and ways it could fail. You
would see and decide on that exact hypothesis before it enters accepted theory.
No Einstein/Maxwell equation, action or extra field is adopted by this direction.

This changes the process from repeatedly asking the current metric to yield an
unspecified law to constructing a precise, accountable physical proposal. The
proposal must earn its place; an arbitrary mechanism or desired answer is not
sufficient motivation. If no such proposal is found, we report that limit.
