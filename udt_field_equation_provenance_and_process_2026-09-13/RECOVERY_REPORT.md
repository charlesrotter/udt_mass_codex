## 2. A real equation-loss mechanism was already diagnosed

For the restricted reciprocal spherical metric with radial factor A, the early
EH radial density is a total derivative:

    r^2 R = d/dr[-r^2 A' - 2r A + 2r].

Consequently, varying that already restricted bulk action cannot recover the
whole unrestricted metric equation. Restricting variations yields projected
equations; varying a justified full theory and then evaluating its equations on
a restricted metric asks a different question. A multiplier formulation also
needs its own equivalence analysis. One cannot restore a missing equation merely
because it is familiar from GR.

This does not invalidate constrained variation in general. A genuinely constrained
physical theory may intentionally have the projected equations. The law and its
variation domain must be specified and justified together. The conceptual audit
already warns against asking kinematics to choose a universal variation domain
before a candidate law exists. Nor does this make an action compulsory.

This clue affects the process immediately: distinguish pair calibration/readout,
metric identities, a solution ansatz, gauge and a physical constraint. Completed
pair reciprocity retains the ruler density and complete metric information; it
does not automatically impose a fixed ambient metric determinant or select an
off-equation variational theory.

honest routes kept distinct:

1. Derive a law-level relation from current UDT commitments. Every step retains

2. Develop a concrete UDT-motivated dynamical hypothesis if derivation does not

import a familiar equation or invent a mechanism to obtain a desired answer.
