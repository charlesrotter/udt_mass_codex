## Owner words and the interpretation boundary

Charles's exact recorded words concern an interframe observable that “doesn't
affect local physics at all,” followed by ordinary at-rest/geodesic clocks as
in GR. W4 already supplies ordinary locally calibrated metric clocks and one
geometry. The current transcription into ordinary proper clocks is supported
by that context, but cannot be treated as proof that it exhausts his intended
statement about all local physics.

Distinguish: ordinary proper-time clocks; the same locally expressed physical
laws; and equality of all finite-region experimental outcomes between different
geometries. The first does not prove the third. The audit neither upgrades
strong local CSN nor assumes curvature/tidal readings vanish. The optional
owner clarification is pending; without an answer the broader interpretation
is explicitly unresolved, not silently narrowed or adopted.

No-clock-anomaly has an exact foothold in the old calculations. CRD1 starts
with ordinary proper clocks and u=N^-1 partial_t in an orthonormal frame.
FCV1 defines N=sqrt(-g(v,v)), u=v/N and Z=N_o A'/N_e. N converts a supplied
curve label into proper time; varying it is not a postulate that a clock runs
abnormally relative to its own proper time. RMS1 explicitly distinguishes fixed
query K from co-normalized K and actual received-clock ratios. Consequently
the asserted correction does not, on its ordinary-clock reading, falsify these
definitions. Their applicability still needs their actual geometry/query data.

## Dependency and negative-result propagation

The local-response branch is CRD1 -> GRS1 -> RMS1 -> CRV1 as an investigative
sequence, not a theorem that all later work must pass through it. FSL1 recovers
earlier null-clock geometry; FCV1 extends the finite comparison rather than
only RMS1's local coefficient; ICN1 uses actual two-way records. MGC1 asks a
particular global mass/scale question. These dependencies explain why a change
in research priority need not falsify an equation, but must change its current use.

Retain specific negative results only with their quantifiers:

- CRD1 excludes one derivative-free forced scalar equation as standalone
  hyperbolic evolution; it leaves constraints and other closures open.
- GRS1 distinguishes actions in a broad comparison arena, not two native UDT
  theories passing all current premises and observational constraints.
- RMS1 excludes particular direct kernel/readout identifications, not all
  relational ways of constraining geometry.
- CRV1's curl witness excludes trace completion for its supplied S, not UDT.
- FCV1's support argument rejects one smooth-volume representation for a
  single query; it leaves local dynamics and multi-query constructions open.
- ICN1's finite timing ambiguity leaves full pair/screen/tidal information and
  native admission distinct. MGC1's external examples have the same admission
  boundary. Neither establishes full-postulate underdetermination.

The corresponding positive claims also keep their conditions. In particular,
ordinary clocks do not select an E, homothety symmetry, conservation identity,
Einstein dynamics or unique history. No failure in a chosen route proves a new
physical postulate necessary. Legitimate initial/query data can remain free.
