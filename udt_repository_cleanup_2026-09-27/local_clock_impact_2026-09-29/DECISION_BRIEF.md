# What this correction audit changes

**Your concern exposed a real maintenance failure.** We updated the current
explanation, but left older reports steering later work back toward a particular
local field-response problem. Six reports needed that guidance narrowed or
withdrawn as a general prerequisite. Ten reading entry points now carry notices.

The audit distinguishes what the math proves from what we should do next.
The inspected calculations already used ordinary local proper-time clocks.
That is why their specific equations can survive your clarification. It does
not justify keeping every earlier interpretation, obstacle or recommendation.
Any blanket assurance that nothing needed revision is withdrawn. The earlier
written reviews themselves stated limited scopes; they did not establish a
complete impact audit.

We retain the usable clock-comparison calculations, the scoped reconstruction
results, and the genuinely conditional tests. Local field-response selection,
conservation/action construction and the Machian matter–geometry connection
are optional routes with their own unfinished steps. None becomes a compulsory
detour for all work on positional dilation.

We also have **not established that UDT's full clarified postulates cannot
select its geometry**. Several examples demonstrate limits in supplied test
geometries; they were not established as UDT solutions satisfying every current
constraint. Their negative conclusions must stay within those limits.

Your [clarification](OWNER_CLARIFICATION.md) settles “no local physics” in the GR
sense: ordinary local laws and clocks, with geometric effects in comparisons
between observers. It confirms the interpretation used in this audit. It does
not require all finite-region measurements to be identical. Reciprocal inversion
of a mathematical map still does not by itself represent two observers actually
receiving each other's ticks.

The original scientific bodies, old reviews and evidence are preserved. The
notices withdraw obsolete guidance where future sessions will encounter it;
the current index, program and handoff point to the corrected use map. This is
a focused audit of ten packages, not a claim that the entire corpus is now free
of interpretation errors. No further derivation automatically starts.

The [reviewed audit](REVIEWED_AUDIT.md), [impact ledger](IMPACT_LEDGER.tsv) and
[closeout](CLOSEOUT.md) give the exact scope, substantive review and checks.
