## Why some mathematics survives while guidance changes

CRD1 and CRV1 explicitly assumed ordinary proper clocks before the clarification.
RMS1 distinguishes the fixed query K from its proper normalization. FCV1 defines
N=sqrt(-g(v,v)), u=v/N and Z=N_o A'/N_e. N converts a chosen curve label into
proper time; its variation does not postulate a locally abnormal physical clock.
Its interior-geometry witness actually leaves both clock neighborhoods unchanged
while the received comparison changes. That is compatible with the ordinary-clock
reading of the clarification.

The audit inspected the actual relevant arguments, including the full FCV1
support proof, GRS1 flattening hypotheses, RMS1 response types and CRV1 primitive
criterion/sign repair. It found no clarification-dependent algebraic defect in
those inspected steps. The independent reviewer also reconstructed seven exact
argument anchors using a separate implementation. This warrants retaining the
specific conditional results; it does not warrant saying all previous mathematics
is correct or that no other part of the corpus needs revision.

The parent's separate RMS1 pairing-supplement read followed the candidate freeze;
the reviewer inspected its reviewed account, not that supplement or producer code.
The original ledger records cumulative coverage; REPAIR_1 makes the timing explicit.

Negative conclusions require the same care. A supplied control that has not
been shown to satisfy all applicable UDT premises cannot establish that those
premises underdetermine geometry. These studies leave native prediction open.
They neither prove a new physical postulate necessary nor supply the missing
constraint by themselves. Legitimate initial/query data can remain free.
