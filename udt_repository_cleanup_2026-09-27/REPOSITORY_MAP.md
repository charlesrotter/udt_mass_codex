## What staying at root means

A root path is not a scientific endorsement or an instruction to resume work.
Some paths are explicitly fixed by current guards; others support executable
imports, source pins, canonical/foundational provenance or coherent evidence.
Historical working instructions that can safely move are classified individually.
Do not treat a file's age, a claim of “complete,” or a search result as current
authority. Do not infer that a retained package's science was newly reviewed.

The closed baseline census covers 33,380 tracked paths, including 1,147 root
paths. Most receive a preservation disposition from metadata, not a content audit.
The content-reviewed working-surface decisions and literal-reference checks are
separate evidence. The census is not a complete graph of dynamically constructed
paths or external references, and excludes untracked/ignored/external payloads.

## Recover history without reviving its instructions

Use the exact old path in the appropriate archival manifest; inspect the saved
source commit and preserved document. For a historical execution whose source
contract pins an old tree, use that tree. Current source grades and later owner
decisions still control interpretation. A superseded claim, failed calculation
or contrary control remains evidence and is retained alongside its explanation.

Protected local packages remain outside this cleanup. Missing or present directory
shells establish neither permission to inspect them nor successful preservation
of unbanked payloads. Backup completeness and external storage readiness are not
being certified by this repository cleanup.
