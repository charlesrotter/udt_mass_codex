# Review findings, repairs and limits

This is maintenance discovery/review history. None of these checks proves a
scientific claim, source grade, complete dependency graph or backup completeness.

## Before archival construction

The initial inventory generator allowed override fields to replace immutable
Git metadata. The separate dependency reviewer identified that defect. The
repaired generator accepts only named disposition fields, protects current and
excluded interfaces, and requires safe unique destinations, full content review
and two existing distinct review references. Actual reviewer contexts are recorded
in their reports; reference-file existence alone does not establish independence.
The independent inventory auditor imports no production classifier. Its finite
corruption controls and separate tests of the real builder are preserved.

The initial source table retained three transfer/synchronization records on
metadata-level coherence caution. Exact consumer tracing showed the apparent
pins addressed historical Git/intake paths. Full prose review then cleared all
three. The initial table and report remain unchanged; a separately sealed
supplement owns that revision. All 34 final eligible paths were moved, leaving
56 deliberate source/guard/code/scientific-interface retentions in the 90-path
closer review, with 49 unique full prose reads.

Static review of the parent integrity checker found that matching archive source
sets alone did not match destination fields and that read_bytes/exists could
follow or miss symlinks. Destination equality, regular-file mode and old-path
symlink checks were added, along with the maintenance-packet size cap.

The first construction attempt required filesystem permission0644, while this
workspace's regular non-executable originals were0664. It stopped during preflight
before writing a manifest or moving anything. Its stdout/stderr/exit receipt is
retained. The repair permits0644/0664, records observed permission metadata, and
still checks Git100644, regular files, no symlinks and exact original bytes.
The separate repaired construction receipt records success. No source content
was changed to satisfy a check.

## Final delivery checker review

The first staged/commit delivery checker trusted its required-file list without
requiring that every archive destination appear in it. A malformed list could
therefore omit preserved originals while still passing its internal comparisons.
The dependency reviewer caught this before staging. The repair requires every
archive destination, mandatory navigation/manifests/verifier, exact membership of
all owned artifacts, regular non-executable files and an exact single-parent
commit. Staged and committed blobs must each match the sealed files. This is a
delivery check, separate from the earlier construction/content checks; the
reviewer's isolated Git fixtures preserve the objection and repair evidence.
