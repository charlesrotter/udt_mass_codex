# G315 lay report

G315 answers a practical version of “what is a history?”

The adopted bounded UDT equation does not require us to hand it a complete universe for all time.
We can give it a starting snapshot: the geometry of space and the rate at which that geometry is
initially changing. Those numbers cannot be arbitrary. Four geometric consistency conditions must
hold. Once they do, the equation determines the local continuation, apart from our freedom to label
coordinates.

After removing the four consistency conditions and coordinate freedom, two genuine local shape
degrees of freedom remain, together with their initial rates. Different lawful starting snapshots
therefore produce different valid local histories. That is normal conditional dynamics, not the
old failure to determine a universal `phi(r)` profile.

There is a parallel light-cone description. We can give data on two intersecting lightlike sheets.
Their screen distortion or shear can be supplied, while the change in cross-sectional area must
obey a transport rule. The same-ray focusing equation does not contain the regional constant
`Lambda` directly; the relationship between the two directions does contain it.

The reciprocal kernel remains downstream. First the metric evolves; then a supplied observer pair
pulls back that metric and the reciprocal readout evaluates it. No fitted profile, extra kernel,
matter model, scale, or `X_max` was introduced.

This result is local and conditional. It does not decide the universe's topology, starting data,
global completion, scalar magnitude, population, or unique actual history.
