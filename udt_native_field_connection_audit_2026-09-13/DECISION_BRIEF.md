# NFCA1 — lay return and direction correction

Directly reviewed conditional audit; final fidelity is separately recorded in
review/. This audit clarifies where the current work connects to UDT.

The metric/kernel connection is real at its established conditional scope.
Complete reciprocal records, with their calibration information and shared
markings retained, can describe the metric. We can therefore learn about that
metric using general geometry. Mathematics shared with GR or optics does not
make that work an import of their physical laws. My earlier warning was too
broad if read as a reason to discount this existing connection.

The newer field construction has a different status. We chose a way of building
a mathematical field from a supplied geometry and additional data. We proved
useful facts about it, including survival after the original symmetry fails.
But the current sources do not identify that field as UDT's native response,
physical light, energy or something a detector measures. That is the open join.

A direct check makes the distinction concrete: an existing metric change can
change a reciprocal record while leaving our added field unchanged. Changing
the supplied field's strength can also leave the entire metric unchanged.
These are ideal, explicitly calibrated mathematical comparisons. They show
which inputs the construction uses; they do not prove that physical emergence
is impossible or that another postulate is required.

The examined work retained inconvenient cases and failed checks. It remains
valid conditional exploration. The correction is to its role in the program:
preserve it as a field-construction/comparison branch, and stop treating another
such example as automatic progress toward native UDT electromagnetism.
The metric-only blind-spot and neighboring-tide results remain directly useful.

The roadmap now requires a concrete connection before proposing the next
extension: name the UDT relation or established metric/record bridge being used,
the exact new question, the nearest completed result, and what the proposed
step would add. A conditional example may qualify; a demand for unique initial
data or familiar physics is not the criterion. The previously proposed
transverse-field expansion is paused for this decision.

This audit has not established a new native response law or identified a
distinct next theorem beyond the already-documented response gates. Relisting
those gates would repeat work. The next discussion should choose a precise
metric/kernel implication or an explicitly justified comparison question under
this corrected framing. No further scientific campaign or promotion is launched.

Original NR1/NR2 evidence and all accepted gains remain preserved. Scientific
