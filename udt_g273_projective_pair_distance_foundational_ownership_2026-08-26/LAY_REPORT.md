# G273 lay report

The original idea said that distance itself makes two observers' clocks and rulers respond
reciprocally. It did not say exactly what mathematical quantity the word “distance” meant. That is
why the earlier derivations could not honestly claim a unique distance formula.

The metric now gives us a particularly natural candidate. Think of the reciprocal clock and ruler
readings as two sides of a balance. Their normalized imbalance is

\[
\tanh(\Delta\phi).
\]

That formula is not merely a nice curve: it is the unique scale-free projective contrast of the two
reciprocal sides. When the relation is not radial, the metric gives a whole bounded vector whose
other components are the native screen response. Nothing is turned off or added afterward.

However, another smooth bounded distance label could satisfy the original verbal description. So
the exact prompts did not logically force the projective identification. One small provisional
clarification would close the gap:

> Physical normalized observer-pair position is the metric's complete projective relation state.

If adopted, the radial law follows exactly:

\[
\Delta\phi=\operatorname{artanh}(x/X).
\]

Only the scale `X`, global realization, and evolving history would remain. This is much smaller than
inventing an arbitrary profile or field mechanism.
