# Ponder map — derive the tape-carrying rule, not another old equation census

In lay terms, we already know how the finished measuring tape is read: reciprocal `c_E` reads the
clock/ruler imbalance at the end. We also know that the angular and mixing sectors help shape that
tape before it is read. The unanswered question is what mathematical kind of object carries the
tape's calibration from one observer state to the next.

This calculation lays every possible kind of carrier in the declared class on the table. It asks
which pieces come automatically from the metric and which would require a director—an observer
query, a global consistency rule, or a genuine equation of motion. It does not begin by selecting
the carrier we hope will work.

The pure route is the full time-dependent complete coframe and its exact bundle geometry. The easy
route would be the stationary R17 formulas alone. R17 is retained only as a recovery check because
using it as the whole arena would freeze precisely the degrees of freedom we are trying to audit.
