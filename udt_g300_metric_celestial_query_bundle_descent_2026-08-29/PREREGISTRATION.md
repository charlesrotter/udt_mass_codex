## Frozen candidate construction

Let `P_gamma:T_XM->T_YM` be the full metric isometry of one supplied regular path-labelled relation,
and let `U_X,U_Y` be metric-unit future clocks. Define the oriented source celestial sphere

```text
S_X = {n : g(n,U_X)=0 and g(n,n)=1}.
```

For every `n in S_X`, define

```text
K_X(n) = U_X+n
Omega_gamma(n) = -g(P_gamma K_X(n),U_Y)
r_gamma(n) = 1/Omega_gamma(n)
A_gamma(n) = P_gamma K_X(n)/Omega_gamma(n)-U_Y
J_gamma,n = (r_gamma(n) U_Y, A_gamma(n)).
```

The construction is tested as a family. No `n` is privileged.

## Registered derivations

1. Prove `Omega_gamma(n)>0` and `A_gamma(n)` is unit spacelike and orthogonal to `U_Y` for every
   source direction.
2. Prove `A_gamma:S_X->S_Y` is bijective, with inverse supplied by the mathematically reversed
   full relation.
3. For `P_AC=P_BC P_AB`, prove exact composition
   `A_AC=A_BC o A_AB` and the multiplicative clock cocycle
   `r_AC(n)=r_AB(n) r_BC(A_AB(n))`.
4. Prove every `J_gamma,n` is a regular rank-two W1 input with pullback
   `diag(-r_gamma(n)^2,1)` and terminal depth `Phi=-log r_gamma(n)`.
5. Prove the family of oriented clock-containing Lorentzian two-planes is canonically `S^2`; after
   forgetting ruler orientation it is `RP^2`. Every regular pair plane containing the comparison
   clock line must occur.
6. Show the G298 target-local plane occurs directly and the transported-source plane also lies in
   the full plane family after pair-domain orthogonalization. Neither is selected.
7. Prove covariance under endpoint Lorentz frame changes, diffeomorphisms, positive affine
   rescaling, and pair-domain gauge.
8. Prove the family construction uses the full relation morphism for transport and therefore does
   not replace G274 composition by plane-only or scalar-only composition.
9. On the identity/quiet relation, use the spatial isotropy action to test whether a gauge-natural
   individual section can exist. If the isotropy acts transitively with no fixed direction, a
   unique query cannot be derived from metric-plus-clock data alone.
10. Keep supplied multiroute branches set-valued and state separately the derived query domain,
    supplied query value, supplied route population, and open global history.

## Certification and falsification contract

Landing 1 requires all of the following:

- exact all-direction proofs of positivity, unit norm, reversal, and composition;
- exact clock-cocycle and W1 recovery;
- a canonical plane-bundle identification, not a finite list of convenient projections;
- explicit inclusion of both registered G298 plane classes;
- full frame/path carry retained as the composition owner;
- no preferred direction, route, branch, history, or observation inserted;
- an implementation-distinct exact verifier over noncollinear active-screen cases;
- hostile catches for inverse, normalization, composition order, scalar collapse, plane omission,
  and false unique-section claims;
- a fresh zero-context adversarial review before banking.

Landing 2 is mandatory if the endpoint plane bundle exists but direction/frequency transport fails
composition or reversal. Landing 3 is mandatory only if a source-owned, gauge-natural unique
section survives the isotropy test. Landing 4 is mandatory if even the full family requires a new
operational structure not contained in the metric clocks and full relation morphism.

## Hostile catches

Reject any result that:

- chooses `J_T`, `J_L`, the radial axis, or a screen basis as the unique physical view;
- checks only the actual route tangent and calls that the full query family;
- treats a direction query as physical route population;
- composes pair planes or W1 scalars after discarding the full morphism;
- identifies mathematical reversal with a later causal return;
- erases ruler orientation without recording the `S^2 -> RP^2` quotient;
- imports observation, action, source, matter, field equation, scale, distance, profile, `X_max`,
  or protected work;
- promotes W1 or W5 beyond their working/conditional grades.
