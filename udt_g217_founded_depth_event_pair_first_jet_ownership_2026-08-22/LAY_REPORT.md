# G217 lay report

The gap is smaller than “we need another observer-pair rule.”

Once we specify which event on A is being compared with which event on B, and we supply their UDT
depth, the relative tick rate is no longer free. It is fixed:

```text
B clock rate relative to A = exp(-depth).
```

That is the complete first-order clock connection between those two events. There is no extra knob
to fit.

What remains open is different: the equation does not choose which events are paired, and it does
not describe every detail of how the pairing bends or changes nearby. In a road metaphor, UDT fixes
the slope at a marked connection once the two marked points and depth are known; it does not choose
the two addresses or draw the entire road.

So this is genuine narrowing. The scalar first-jet joint closes conditionally. The remaining work
is event-incidence and full-germ ownership, not another scalar coefficient.
