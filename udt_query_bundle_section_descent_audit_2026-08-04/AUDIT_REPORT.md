## What section-free really means

Section-free does not mean observer-independent. A query law may legitimately answer a question
about a supplied observer/ruler pair and transform naturally when the pair changes. It is not one
tensor at the base event.

Conversely, an ambient law can be independent of the pair and still leave observer-pair readouts as
downstream query observables. Therefore the absence of a universal natural `N` does not block every
possible native metric law.

## What still requires a section or equivalent reduction

A single physical screen, mixing response, pair-projected connection/curvature, pair-resolved
boundary polarization, or section-evaluated source needs one of:

- a realized reciprocal field whose physical ownership is declared;
- a branch-derived intrinsic selector on its regular stratum; or
- a new, explicitly derived aggregation/quotient rule.

None is currently universal. The zero mixing map is a trivial basic value but does not select a
plane or establish physical zero mixing.
