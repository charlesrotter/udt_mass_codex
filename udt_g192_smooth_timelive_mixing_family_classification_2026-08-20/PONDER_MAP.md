# G192 ponder map

## Plain frame

G191 showed that one steadily changing clock/ruler scale and one constant mixing strength can be
played by the same metric without bolting one response onto the other. G192 removes those two
constant-volume restrictions while preserving the same complete-coframe architecture.

The intended observation is not “make the desired loud/quiet/loud pattern.” It is:

```text
arbitrary smooth common scale a(eta)
+ arbitrary smooth signed mixing mu(eta)
    -> one completed pair
    -> one affine null branch
    -> frequency and full two-dimensional screen
    -> classify every turn and caustic the family permits
```

## Why this is one tile rather than the whole metric

The family keeps only time dependence in two coframe functions and holds the central pair/query
architecture fixed. It frees exactly the two constants whose special status limited G191. It does
not activate every function in a general complete coframe. A successful classification therefore
separates G191 identities from G191 control behavior; it does not select a universe.

## Regression alarms

- Do not assume `a` is monotone or `mu` has one sign.
- Do not replace the matrix screen with its determinant or trace.
- Do not append a mixing coefficient after frequency propagation.
- Do not insert P1, a redshift/radius curve, transfer, an observational anchor, or `X_max`.
- Do not call a property universal beyond the displayed two-function family.
