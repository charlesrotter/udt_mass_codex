# G191 lay report

## What happened

We switched on time evolution and angular mixing inside the same complete metric.

The result did not fall apart. The metric produced one outgoing ray, and along that same ray it
produced both the changing frequency and a two-direction image response. The image response has a
real cross-term: stretching in one screen direction feeds the other. Nothing was appended after
the calculation.

The relative strength also changes with depth automatically. Very near the source the cross-effect
starts quietly; farther along this particular test geometry it grows relative to the unmixed
response. No regime switch or fitted orchestra coefficient was inserted.

## What this does not mean

This analytic geometry is a stress test, not our universe. It does not predict supernova
brightness, choose the physical UDT metric realization, derive light transfer, or calculate
`X_max`. It shows that the G190 engine can carry a genuinely time-live and mixing-active orchestra
without returning to P1, G116, G189, or a separately chosen radius/redshift curve.
