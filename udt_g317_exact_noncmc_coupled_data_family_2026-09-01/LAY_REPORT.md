# G317 lay report

G316 gave us a method for building possible starting geometries. G317 tests that method when the
geometry varies from place to place and its two constraint equations can no longer be treated as
independent.

The result is positive and informative. An unlimited variety of smooth nonconstant profiles is
allowed in this exact test family, but several other pieces must adjust together: the longitudinal
correction follows the varying profile, one constant tensor component follows its average, and
the remaining curvature constant is tied to a transverse parameter.

There are two subclasses. With the transverse parameter zero, the nonconstant data are a different
slicing of locally flat spacetime. With it nonzero, the Weyl tensor detects a real tidal field, so
the effect cannot be dismissed as coordinates.

This shows genuine interlocking, not physical selection. The equations admit the family but do not
choose its profile, size, transverse strength, or which member Nature realizes. The metric and
reciprocal kernel were not modified.
