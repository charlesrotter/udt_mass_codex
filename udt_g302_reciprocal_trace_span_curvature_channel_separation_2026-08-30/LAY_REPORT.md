# G302 lay report

The calculation found a real structural connection, but not yet the missing law.

Dual Reciprocity controls the **shape** of the metric response.  When every possible pair direction
is included, those shape changes account for nine of the metric's ten local degrees of freedom.
The remaining degree is the common size of clocks and rulers.  The complete metric retains that
size, bringing the count back to ten.

So no part of the metric was accidentally turned off—but Dual Reciprocity by itself does not choose
the trace-free candidate equation left by G301.

When that candidate is examined conditionally, the exact spherical metric contains

\[
f(r)=1+\frac b r-\frac{R_0}{12}r^2.
\]

The two additions do different jobs:

- `b/r` dominates toward small distance and creates the Weyl/angular tidal response;
- `R0 r^2` can dominate toward large distance and changes reciprocal clock/position behavior;
- both can be small in a middle interval if their scales are sufficiently separated.

The striking result is that the `R0` contribution cancels exactly from the registered angular
tides while remaining present in reciprocal position and in non-null geometry.  This preserves the
quiet angular behavior previously found near the GR-like regime; it was not discarded to obtain the
new family.

But `R0` is not yet mass, and `b` is not yet a derived mass parameter.  A nonzero `b` is singular at
an ordinary smooth center, so the result describes an exterior family rather than a completed
microphysical object.  No observational value, fitted curve, source, matter model, or imported GR
equation entered.

The honest conclusion is: the extra constant-curvature datum can support a native large-distance
channel while the angular sector remains quiet, and a second geometric datum can support the
small-distance channel.  Current UDT premises still do not select their values or adopt this
conditional class as Nature's equation.
