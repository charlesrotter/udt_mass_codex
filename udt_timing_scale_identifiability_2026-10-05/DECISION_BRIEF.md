source/receiver histories, the late rate of increase of redshift on an ordinary
receiver clock determines H. The observed c_E converts that inverse-time rate
into the length1/H. No known source size is needed. For the principal preparation,

rate. These are consequences of the metric and actual incidences, not inserted

The distinction from untimed data is exact: all lengths and clock durations can
be scaled together while the ordered redshift/angle curve stays unchanged. A
calibrated clock rate breaks that scale freedom. It does not determine every
other parameter or show that UDT selects this conditional geometry.

The important limitation is physical access to the ideal record. A finite
remaining emitter interval contains finitely many fixed-spacing ticks; it

Finite practical accuracy has not been established. Matched GR still predicts
the same records, and no actual data have been fitted.
