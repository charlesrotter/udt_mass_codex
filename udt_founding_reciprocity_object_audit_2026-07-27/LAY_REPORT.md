# Lay report

We had accidentally been asking Reciprocity to do too much.

The founding idea already gives a precise rule for comparing a clock-like direction with a
ruler-like direction. It tells us how one shrinks while the other grows, and how successive
comparisons combine. That rule is real and derived.

But it does **not** say that the same clock/ruler arrows must point in one rigid direction everywhere
in a curved universe. Curvature can rotate the larger frame as it is carried around, while every
observer still uses the same reciprocal comparison law. We proved this with a small exact example
and then confirmed that the full twisted finite-cell example behaves the same way.

So one apparent missing strut was actually an unnecessary demand: UDT does not presently need a
single globally parallel reciprocal frame.

The real missing joint is narrower. We still need the complete geometry to tell us how two actual
observers, their chosen events, and the angular directions produce the signed depth fed into the
already-derived comparison rule. That final physical comparison may turn out to depend only on the
endpoints, or it may retain path information. The founding postulates do not yet decide.
