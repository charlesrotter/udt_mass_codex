## Answer to the framing question

Yes, the program's conversational goal repeatedly leaned toward a bad framing: lawful composition
was too often discussed as though the complete orchestra should collapse to one preferred balance.
But the current banked mathematics did not actually impose that lockstep.

The positive-triangular clock/ruler/shift transition has three independent coordinates,

\[
C=e^\sigma D(\delta)U(\mu),
\]

and their coordinate Jacobian has nonzero rank three everywhere. Their exact composition law is

\[
(\sigma_2,\delta_2,\mu_2)*(\sigma_1,\delta_1,\mu_1)
=\left(\sigma_2+\sigma_1,\delta_2+\delta_1,
\mu_1+e^{2\delta_1}\mu_2\right).

These three channels therefore play together but do not carry fixed relative strengths. Reciprocal depth
modulates accumulated shift; common scale remains a separate central channel.

This exact semidirect theorem is bounded to the \(B^+(2)\) transition. It is already a counterexample
to universal orchestral lockstep, but it does not classify the full `B,Q,S,Y,Z` screen/mixing
composition. That larger composition is the next bounded algebraic question.
