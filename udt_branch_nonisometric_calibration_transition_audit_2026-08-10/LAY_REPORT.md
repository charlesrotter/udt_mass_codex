# Lay report

We found one branch where the metric already appears to contain all the parts needed to build a
working non-rigid measuring tape between observers.

The metric supplies:

- its own clock direction;
- its own ruler direction;
- the amount of reciprocal clock/ruler dilation between the endpoints; and
- the path transport that carries the whole clock/ruler/angular apparatus through the geometry.

Putting those owned parts together gives a comparison that composes correctly from A to B to C.
The comparison is genuinely non-rigid: the clock and ruler scale reciprocally. The angular sector
is not discarded; it appears as rotations and holonomy of the complete carried apparatus.

The remaining issue is very specific. At B, should physics use the calibration apparatus carried
from A, or should B rebuild a fresh apparatus from its local geometry? The metric currently
provides both objects but has not yet told us how they are physically identified. That is the
remaining joint, not another missing formula for reciprocal dilation.

This result is only for six named complete off-shell configurations. It does not say that the
universe selects them, and it does not derive an action or matter.
