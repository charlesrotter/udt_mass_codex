# Lay report

We found one branch where the metric contains all the parts needed to build a mathematically
working non-rigid measuring tape between observers—but not the instruction that says nature uses
that particular assembly.

The metric supplies:

- its own clock direction;
- its own ruler direction;
- the amount of reciprocal clock/ruler dilation between the endpoints; and
- the path transport that carries the whole clock/ruler/angular apparatus through the geometry.

If we put those owned parts together in the tested order, the comparison composes correctly from A
to B to C.
The comparison is genuinely non-rigid: the clock and ruler scale reciprocally. The angular sector
is not discarded; it appears as rotations and holonomy of the complete carried apparatus.

The external reviewer caught the decisive distinction: owning all the parts is not the same as
owning the assembly rule. The formula remains a strong conditional candidate, not a derived branch
law. The remaining issue is very specific. At B, should physics use the calibration apparatus carried
from A, or should B rebuild a fresh apparatus from its local geometry? The metric currently
provides both objects but has not yet told us how they are physically identified. That is the
remaining joint, not another missing formula for reciprocal dilation.

This conditional result is only for six named complete off-shell configurations. It does not say that the
universe selects them, and it does not derive an action or matter.
