# G225 ponder — the image-plane handoff

Date: 2026-08-22

In lay terms, each null direction gives observer B a tiny two-dimensional image sensor held
perpendicular to that direction. If the incoming and outgoing directions differ, the metric can
compare how those sensors are tilted inside B's three-dimensional rest space.

There is an obvious least-turning rigid comparison away from exactly opposite directions: rotate
the incoming sight direction into the outgoing one while leaving their common perpendicular axis
fixed. That construction uses only the metric and the ordered directions.

But a sequence of least turns need not equal one direct least turn. Moving through three sight
directions can leave the image sensor rotated even though every individual handoff was rigid. That
would not be a defect. It would mean the angular channel naturally remembers the route through
direction space, while G224's one-dimensional amplitude channel remains exactly composable.

This is a preregistered expectation, not the result. The exact algebra must decide it, including
the opposite-direction stratum where “least turn” may cease to be unique.
