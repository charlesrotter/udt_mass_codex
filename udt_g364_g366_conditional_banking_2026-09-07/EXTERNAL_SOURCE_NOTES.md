Consulted2026-09-07 through read-only web search and direct HTML retrieval.
No observational payload, fits, measured outcomes, likelihood or data selection
was performed. These notes support engineering plausibility only, not the
scientific identities, adoption of an instrument relationship or data eligibility.
Short paraphrases, no preserved full copyrighted pages. URLs are live sources;
these notes are not a cryptographic freeze of the changing external pages.

- ESA, GOCE's payload, https://www.esa.int/Applications/Observing_the_Earth/FutureEO/GOCE/Instruments
  documents six accelerometers arranged into three orthogonal paired arms for
  gravity-gradient measurements, with drag/attitude control and tracking. This
  supports a real multi-axis gradiometry precedent, not a ready UDT test interface.
- ESA, Calibrating GOCE, https://www.esa.int/Applications/Observing_the_Earth/FutureEO/GOCE/Calibration
  distinguishes pre-flight, internal and external calibration; controlled
  motion and thruster signals address response parameters, while external
  calibration can use ground data or existing models. This motivates checking
  target-relation circularity. It does NOT prove that any particular released
  channel has a trace constraint imposed, or that independent calibration is adequate.
- ESA, Data products, https://www.esa.int/Applications/Observing_the_Earth/FutureEO/GOCE/Data_products
  describes Level1b as converted/calibrated/validated measurement series with
  gradients, orbit and attitude information; Level2 adds processed gravity
  products. Raw or suitably unconstrained channels, covariance and accuracy for
  the proposed test remain UNVERIFIED. No download or access entitlement tested.
