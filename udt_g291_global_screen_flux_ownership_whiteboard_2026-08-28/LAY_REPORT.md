# G291 lay report

The conference found a real middle answer.

The screen geometry has something like a fixed topological wiring class. On a complete spherical
sky, for example, that wiring cannot be made globally flat, and its total twist is fixed. A smooth
evolution cannot change that integer without the screen becoming singular or changing topology.

But the wiring class does not determine the detailed field. The same total twist can be distributed
over the sky in infinitely many smooth ways, all compatible with the metric identities and observer
composition rules. The reciprocal clock/distance reading can even remain unchanged while this
angular distribution changes.

So topology gives UDT a genuine global skeleton, but not yet the evolving body. The next exact
calculation should separate the universal twist of the observer's complete sky from the additional
screen curvature that records the particular metric history. That is a narrower and clearer target
than asking topology to select the whole universe.
