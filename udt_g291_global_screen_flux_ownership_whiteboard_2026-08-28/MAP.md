# G291 map — global screen-flux ownership whiteboard

Date: 2026-08-28
Mode: metric-led, exploratory multi-agent whiteboard

## Whole question

G290 externally establishes a conditional metric-native discriminator. On a supplied smooth regular
complete-pair screen carry, closed-loop screen holonomy hears representative-metric curvature, and
its time-live change equals curvature flux through a supplied loop worldtube. Every smooth regular
`alpha(t)` in the registered family remains admitted, so G290 does not select or propagate history.

G291 asks a broader, deliberately non-binary question:

> When all lawful complete-pair screens, loops, overlaps, reversals, compositions, and worldtubes are
> considered together, does already-owned UDT structure impose any nonidentity restriction on the
> screen-curvature flux, its global compatibility, or the histories that realize it?

The answer may be a full restriction, a partial or stratum-dependent restriction, a reconstruction
theorem, an obstruction only at topology change or degeneracy, or no restriction in the present
premises. These outcomes are not to be collapsed into a premature bifurcation.

## Candidate sources of structure

- global screen-bundle topology and integral characteristic classes;
- holonomy composition, thin homotopy, and differential-character descent;
- overlap, reversal, middle-observer calibration, and complete-network cocycles;
- Bianchi, Cartan, metricity, and Ambrose-Singer-type curvature/holonomy relations;
- causal loop/worldtube availability and global hyperbolicity restrictions;
- regularity, orientation, nonorientable reflection sectors, caustics, and singular strata;
- any already-owned reciprocity or completed-pair condition that couples distinct loops or regions.

## Excluded shortcuts

- proposing an action, field equation, source, constitutive law, conservation law, or boundary merely
  because one would restrict flux;
- fitting or importing observations, mass, Planck scale, operational distance, or `X_max`;
- calling Bianchi identities, Stokes transgression, or holonomy reconstruction history selection;
- choosing a preferred loop population, orientation, screen framing, or topology to force closure;
- treating the G290 conformal witness as the entire complete-metric solution space;
- claiming that a negative bounded result proves no future UDT history law can exist.

## Maximum whiteboard conclusion

G291 may identify an already-owned global restriction, classify partial restrictions by stratum,
prove a bounded nonselection result, or sharpen the exact mathematical type of the remaining gap. It
cannot adopt or canonize a new dynamics, physical history, loop population, matter model, scale, or
boundary.
