# G291 preregistration — global screen-flux ownership

Date: 2026-08-28
Outcome status: `FROZEN_BEFORE_PANEL_RESPONSES`

## Decisive tests

1. **Ownership.** Every claimed restriction must be traced to an active UDT premise or a theorem of
   the supplied metric/network structure. A useful new proposal is not an owned result.
2. **Nonidentity.** A selective condition must reject at least one smooth regular G290-compatible
   network/history for a reason independent of the desired result.
3. **Globality.** Distinguish local differential identities from restrictions arising only after
   lawful overlaps, loop composition, topology, or global causal completion.
4. **Reconstruction versus restriction.** Showing that holonomies reconstruct a connection or
   curvature does not by itself restrict their values.
5. **Partial outcomes.** Record quantization, topology-change obstruction, orientation dependence,
   or singular-stratum restrictions even if no complete history law follows.
6. **Complete-space guard.** Do not generalize solely from the conformal `alpha(t)` witness; either
   state the tested stratum or reason on the general supplied screen connection.
7. **No import.** No action, field equation, source, boundary, observation, mass, scale, Planck
   cutoff, physical loop population, or `X_max` may enter as an owned premise.

## Candidate landings

1. `OWNED_GLOBAL_SCREEN_FLUX_RESTRICTION_FOUND__NONIDENTITY_WITNESS_SEPARATED`
2. `PARTIAL_TOPOLOGICAL_OR_STRATUM_RESTRICTIONS_ONLY__NO_COMPLETE_HISTORY_SELECTION`

Hybrid landings are allowed when different strata support different conclusions.

## Required panel return

Each perspective must provide:

- its strongest theorem or obstruction;
- the exact supplied structures and active premises used;
- one explicit separating witness or counterfamily where possible;
- what survives full gauge and global descent;
- whether the result reconstructs, constrains, propagates, or selects;
- the smallest next exact calculation, if any.

## Maximum conclusion

At most this whiteboard can identify or refute, on a stated bounded class, an already-owned global
restriction on the G290 screen-curvature flux. It cannot manufacture the missing law or elevate a
conditional geometric identity into physics.
