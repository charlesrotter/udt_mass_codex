# G257 lay report

The UDT metric already has an exact GR lane running through it.

For the empty exterior around a spherical body, imposing the GR vacuum condition does not require
changing the UDT metric or adding a response function. It simply selects one exact family already
inside the primary metric. The completed clock/ruler comparison and angular response then follow
from that same geometry.

The important correction is that “GR is quiet” does not mean “nothing is happening.” A Solar-System
GR field still changes clocks, rulers, and angular geometry. Those effects are merely weak, and
they satisfy the exact GR balance. Flat space is the point where everything goes completely silent.

We now have a better way to ask where UDT departs from GR: do not look only at whether `phi` is
nonzero. Instead, measure whether the metric leaves the exact GR balance. The present work writes
that balance down, but it still does not tell us what native UDT law pushes the geometry away from
it at the extreme regimes.
