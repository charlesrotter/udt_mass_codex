# G246 exact derivation — two-observer null-incidence descent

Date: 2026-08-24

## Scope

Let `(M,g)` be a supplied smooth time-oriented Lorentz four-manifold. Supply two future-timelike
proper-clock worldline germs

\[
z_A:I_A\to M,
\qquad
z_B:I_B\to M,
\qquad
g(U_A,U_A)=g(U_B,U_B)=-1.
\]

Declare the bounded query type to be future-null incidence from A to B. This declaration does not
make null incidence the universal UDT comparison protocol. The physical metric history and
observer population remain supplied.

## 1. The full incidence relation is metric-defined

In a convex normal neighborhood let `sigma(p,q)` be Synge's world function. Define

\[
\mathcal I^+_{AB}
=\{(a,b):\sigma(z_A(a),z_B(b))=0, z_B(b)\in J^+(z_A(a)), z_A(a)\ne z_B(b)\}.
\]

This is a relation, not initially a chosen function. It contains every future-null incidence pair
between the supplied worldline pieces. It can be empty, have one connected local branch, or have
several separated branches. No ray is selected in its definition.

At `(a_0,b_0) in I^+_{AB}`, convex-normal uniqueness gives one nonzero future null affine tangent
`K` from A to B. With affine span `Delta lambda>0`, the endpoint gradients are

\[
\sigma_a=-(\Delta\lambda)K_{A\,a},
\qquad
\sigma_{a'}=(\Delta\lambda)K_{B\,a'}.
\]

The derivative in the target-clock direction is

\[
\partial_b\sigma
=(\Delta\lambda)g(K_B,U_B).
\]

It cannot vanish. The orthogonal complement of a timelike vector is positive definite, so it
contains no nonzero null vector. Therefore a nonzero null `K_B` cannot be orthogonal to `U_B`.
This proves that cone-worldline incidence is transverse on every such branch.

The implicit-function theorem consequently gives a unique smooth local correspondence

\[
b=f_{AB}(a)
\]

through every element of `I^+_{AB}`. Choosing the element specifies which relation germ is being
described; it does not add a ray, sheet, or source law. Returning all such germs preserves the full
local solution set.

## 2. The metric owns the positive clock arrow

Implicit differentiation gives

\[
r_{AB}=\frac{db}{da}
=-\frac{\sigma_aU_A^a}{\sigma_{a'}U_B^{a'}}
=\frac{-g(K_A,U_A)}{-g(K_B,U_B)}
=\frac{\omega_A}{\omega_B}>0.
\]

This is exactly the G220 clock arrow. Thus the metric-owned G245 cone family, intersected with the
target timelike worldline, removes the separately supplied local null branch from G220. It does not
remove the supplied observer curves or the declared null query type.

Define

\[
\delta_{AB}=-\log r_{AB}.
\]

No additional scalar coefficient appears.

## 3. The incidence branch owns the G222 pair ribbon

Convex-normal uniqueness and smooth dependence of the geodesic on its endpoints give

\[
F(a,\lambda)
=\operatorname{Exp}_{z_A(a)}
\!\left(\lambda\operatorname{Log}_{z_A(a)}z_B(f_{AB}(a))\right),
\qquad 0\le\lambda\le1.
\]

Set

\[
K=F_*\partial_\lambda,
\qquad
J=F_*\partial_a.
\]

Then

\[
g(K,K)=0,
\qquad
\nabla_KK=0,
\qquad
[J,K]=0,
\]

and the endpoints satisfy

\[
J_A=U_A,
\qquad
J_B=r_{AB}U_B.
\]

Exactly as in G222,

\[
\mathfrak a=-g(J,K)>0,
\qquad
K(\mathfrak a)=0,

\[
F^*g=
\begin{pmatrix}

-\mathfrak a&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det(F^*g)=-\mathfrak a^2<0.

The pair plane is therefore regular and Lorentzian even where `J` is not timelike. On a
clock-regular patch, the G176 working completed-pair clarification gives

\[

\]

In the calibrated vertical coframe `vartheta=mathfrak a d lambda`,

\[

\qquad

At B, `T_B=r_AB`, and hence

\[

The complete local null pair ribbon is thus downstream of the metric, two observer germs, and the
declared null query. It is not a separately bolted-on surface.

## 4. Mathematical reversal is not physical future return

Because `r_AB>0`, the local correspondence is a diffeomorphism onto its image. Reversing the same
geometric relation gives

\[
a=f_{AB}^{-1}(b),
\qquad
r_{AB}^{-1},
\qquad
\delta_{BA}^{\rm inv}=-\delta_{AB}.
\]

This reversed generator is past-directed when viewed from B. A physical future return is instead a
new relation

\[
\mathcal I^+_{BA}
\]

generated by B's future cone intersecting A's worldline. It generally has a different event map
and is not the inverse arrow.

### Exact moving-flat control

\[

\qquad

\qquad

\[

\qquad

\[

\qquad r_{AB}=e.

\[

\[

\qquad \frac{dy_+}{db}=e.

## 5. Why cone-cone transversality was the wrong proposed test

Take null-separated flat events `A=(0,0)` and `B=(L,L)`. Along their shared generator `P=(s,s)`,
the two cone functions have gradients

\[
dQ_A=(-2s,2s),
\qquad
dQ_B=(-2(s-L),2(s-L)).
\]

Their wedge is zero. The future cone of A and past cone of B share the connecting generator, so
their direct-null intersection is nontransverse. This does not obstruct the observer relation:
cone-worldline incidence is transverse because the target tangent is timelike.

For timelike-separated endpoints, future-A and past-B cones can instead intersect in a sphere of
intermediate events. That is a different two-leg query, not a unique direct arrow.

## 6. Global branches remain a relation, not a selection

On flat `R times S^1` with circumference `C`, put static observers at spatial separation `L`.
Lifting to the cover, every integer winding `n` gives

\[
b-a=|L+nC|.
\]

Every branch has

\[
r_n=1,
\qquad
\delta_n=0,
\]

but the delay, affine density, and route label differ. The metric and topology own the entire
branch-labelled relation. The reciprocal scalar cannot select one winding.

Outside a convex normal neighborhood, the correct object is therefore the set/groupoid of regular
branch germs. At conjugate points the G188 position block can lose rank while the full G226 phase
remains lawful. At cut points or self-intersections, distinct direction labels must not be erased.

## 7. Degenerate strata

- **No incidence:** `I_AB^+` is empty on the supplied worldline pieces.
- **Coincidence:** `A=B` has zero affine span and is not a nontrivial null ribbon; identity is typed
  separately.
- **Regular null incidence:** the local clock map and ribbon are uniquely metric-derived.
- **Several regular incidences:** return every local branch.
- **Cut/self-intersection:** retain direction and route labels.
- **Conjugate/caustic:** retain full G226 phase; do not invert the position block.
- **Incomplete generator:** the branch ends at its maximal affine domain.
