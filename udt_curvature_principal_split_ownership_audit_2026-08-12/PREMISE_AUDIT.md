# Premise and anti-imposition audit

## Question ownership

The question is metric-led: given supplied metrics and a supplied registered `2+2` split, what do
their pointwise Weyl and Ricci curvature operators say about that split? Petrov type is used as a
classification, never as an acceptance criterion. No row is filtered for looking spherical,
particle-like, cosmological, or physically desirable.

## Choices retained

- The founding spherical readout is conditional and has no native equation for `phi`.
- The 14 G63 witnesses and their `p/q/r` tiles are inherited bounded controls. The R17 `phi`
  profiles and all five time-live movies are off-shell.
- G85 uses all 196 frozen nonzero-mixing profile identities and all three regular constructive
  completion classes. `B=0.6`, `L=0.45`, `omega=1.1`, the three time controls, and the off-axis seam
  point are `pinned-by-HABIT` coverage witnesses, not UDT constants.
- The shift-supported A05 witness sets mixing to zero on an equatorial band exactly as G85 permits.
  Its 588 atlas rows represent only three distinct local metric jets; profile identities are retained
  for provenance, not counted as independent geometric evidence.
- The tested split is the registered coframe split. Replacing it by a Weyl-principal split would be
  a new construction and was not done.

## What is characterized, not filtered

Every robust class is retained: D, I, alignment, Ricci ownership with finite Weyl candidates,
misalignment, Ricci agreement, and Ricci failure. Near-degenerate cases would have returned
unresolved. No physical ranking is
assigned to any Petrov type.

## Completeness

This is one pointwise-curvature tile. It covers complete metric jets through second order at 1,221
distinct preregistered local jets, represented by 1,806 provenance rows. One separately registered
zero-shift A05 subcase remains `INSUFFICIENT_OWNED_JET` and is not part of that numerical census. It
does not cover:

- every point or global eigenbundle continuation;
- eigenvalue crossings, cut loci, or global split topology;
- higher curvature derivatives or nonlocal invariants;
- a native action, field equation, source, bootstrap law, or boundary completion;
- physical history, query, realization, `X_max`, SNe, or CMB selection.

## Maximum honest interpretation

Curvature can recover the registered reciprocal/angular split on some metric strata, but it does not
do so universally in the tested complete metric arena. This narrows the split-owner question. It
does not turn Petrov classification into an admissibility law and does not remove the separate
history/query/realization ownership problem.
