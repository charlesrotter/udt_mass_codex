# G204 lay report

The metric does not want one steadily rising volume knob from the center to infinity.

A smooth center must begin quietly. The reciprocal response can then dip into a strong inner
negative regime, return to the quiet overlap, and grow into the outer positive regime. That entire
shape occurs inside one metric profile; no instrument is switched on or off by hand.

We found an exact infinite family with this behavior. That is strong evidence of cohesion, not yet
a unique score. Global regularity tells us what the score must do near the center, but it still does
not choose the crossing order, scale, loudness, or outer completion.
