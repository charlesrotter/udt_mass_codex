## Criteria (Q1 verdict vocabulary — exhaustive, one per consumer)

- **DESCENDS** — output invariant under member swap (all inputs transformed covariantly), or
  well-defined directly on the orbit/pair. Requires an explicit transformation argument, not an
  assertion.
- **EQUIVARIANT** — output changes but covariantly (`C(σs) = τ(C(s))` for a stated involution τ
  on outputs): the pair structure propagates downstream; not a uniqueness consumer, but its
  downstream inherits the question (name the downstream).
- **CONSUMES-UNIQUENESS** — a banked number, observable, or stamped claim genuinely differs by
  member with no covariant identification; the choice is load-bearing. Cite the exact claim.
- **INDETERMINATE** — not decidable from disk evidence; state the exact missing object.
- **OUT-OF-SCOPE** — construction references no exchange-orbit member at all (logged, dropped).

Q2 verdicts: **RECIPROCITY-ROOTED / DISTINCT-ORIGIN / MIXED / INDETERMINATE**, each with the
exact group, action, and the mapping (or failed mapping) to the G01/G02 swap.
