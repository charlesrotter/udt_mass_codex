## Independent mathematical tests to apply

1. **Angular measure is load-bearing.** For one fixed Lorentz arrow with
   boost rapidity `r`, rotate source angles so its frequency multiplier is
   `f(mu)=cosh(r)+sinh(r) mu`. Direct integration over `mu` gives

       <log f> = r coth(r) - 1 >= 0,
       <log Z>_source = 1 - r coth(r) <= 0,  Z=1/f.

   The limit at `r=0` is zero. Positivity follows from
   `r cosh(r)-sinh(r)>=0`, whose derivative is `r sinh(r)>=0`.
   The received-angle expression in R7 is instead
   `Z=cosh(r)-sinh(r) mu_received`, so its *uniform received-sky* mean of
   `log Z` has the opposite sign. The celestial map has area factor `f^-2`;
   a transported source measure is not uniform received measure. These are
   fixed-arrow mathematical comparisons, not FAC1's varying physical arrows.
   A proposed positive angular sign obtained this way may be a measure/boost
   identity, with no restriction on geometry or identification of positional
   physics. Conversely this does not make the FAC1 sign trivial or impossible.

2. **Covariance versus state invariance.** Rotating a laboratory cannot change
   its full-sphere average. Changing its timelike unit tangent changes the
   prepared experiment and the electric/magnetic split. A proposal equating
   their values in all frames needs an additional hypothesis. If only a common
   inequality is proposed, test its actual weaker quantifier; do not replace
   it by PCC1's constant curvature-difference equality.

3. **Inverse composition and ordinary transport.** A law for `log Z` must retain
   the intermediate direction in R7's cocycle. Additivity of log for actual
   successive correspondences alone does not give a sign, preferred weight,
   distance assignment or factorization over endpoint positions. Reversal of
   the preparation may preserve leading coefficients while changing finite
   records; combining the two means can conceal failure of one.

4. **Local-to-distant inference.** A distant slowing requirement does not by
   itself prescribe the first nonzero local Taylor coefficient. The PRT1 cubic
   example already prevents an automatic quadratic requirement. A candidate
   using local FAC1 signs must name the positivity domain and recovery domain,
   with a common finite query before interchanging recovery and `L->0`.
   Distant onset, finite-tolerance recovery and a shrinking admissible radius
   must remain unless separately excluded. An angular pointwise-in-direction
   asymptote also needs a uniform lower/integrability bound before passing to
   an average; compactness alone does not bound a varying family.

5. **Prepare and differentiate correctly.** The emitter/receiver histories
   remain fixed in differentiating nearby emissions. Releasing geodesics in a
   changed metric need not retain histories; a same-history conformal endpoint
   factor cannot be applied to independently re-prepared clocks. Null-screen
   averaging and spatial preparation averaging require their actual map and
   Jacobian. Check clock normalization, null incidence, and branch regularity.
