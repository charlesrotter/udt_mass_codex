## P03: global coframe compatibility and completion atlas

P02 shows that the unrestricted local two-jet arena is too flexible for a
screen-curvature condition to select a branch: all three symmetric tidal
components can be prescribed at every frozen P02-B base.  The next map should
therefore change scale of question rather than choose another local target.

The whole P03 question should be:

> Which P02 local motif families can occur as parts of one smooth complete
> coframe on a finite cell, across every already registered completion class,
> and which fail only because of genuine global compatibility, transition,
> topology, or regularity constraints?

The design should:

1. start from the exact complete-extension class ledger rather than silently
   selecting the triangular representative as physical;
2. freeze the complete set of registered finite-cell completions, transition
   maps, caps, seams, and chart domains before construction;
3. organize the 7,897 constructive P02 strata into exact equivalence families
   without discarding any family for failing to look physical;
4. build smooth global representatives or record explicit compatibility
   obstructions for every family/completion pairing;
5. carry causal type, rank changes, pair/screen mixing, repeated tides,
   holonomy, determinant, and chart-invariant curvature as readouts rather than
   acceptance filters;
6. test chart/coframe covariance and transition consistency independently;
   and
7. stop before introducing an action, source, carrier, density, bootstrap
   equation, boundary functional, or physical time evolution.

P03 should begin with a design and source-availability audit.  GPU work is
appropriate only after the global function families and completion universe
are frozen.  A repeated tide is one motif to transport, not a preferred goal.

If the registered sources do not define a complete enough global coframe or
completion family to pose this atlas without new choices, the correct P03
return is `OPEN_MISSING_GLOBAL_DEFINITION`, not an invented completion.
