# G189 lay report

We successfully removed P1 from the light-screen machinery. The metric now tells us how a beam
spreads, and the temporary light rule tells us how that beam area becomes brightness.

What P1 was still supplying was simpler: it told us which radius corresponds to each observed
redshift. We tested the most obvious replacement—make radius directly proportional to the native
reciprocal position `chi`. That does not work. It is not smooth at a central observer, and its SNe
curve is poor in both catalogs.

This is a useful negative, not damage to the kernel. It says pair position and sky radius are two
different metric outputs and should not be identified by hand. The next clean route is the
time-live metric: let the evolving reciprocal geometry jointly determine redshift and screen size,
then compare that derived relation with SNe. P1 can remain only as a reference curve.
