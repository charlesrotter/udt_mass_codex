# G237 lay report

We had two de-overlapped, processed SNe maps of the same middle-distance terrain. G236 showed that
their shapes were compatible after removing known duplicate objects. G237 has now combined them
into one sharper map, with each survey weighted by its reported within-release uncertainty.

This was not a new fit to a cosmological formula. We did not choose a smooth curve, force it to rise,
insert P1, or use `X_max`. We froze eleven relative measurements on a twelve-point grid. A completely
separate calculation went back to the released survey rows and rebuilt the same combined map to
about twelve decimal places.

The important caveat is that the two surveys may share calibration or processing errors that no
public joint covariance describes. We removed known duplicate supernovae, but then had to treat the
remaining survey errors as separate. So this is a trustworthy conditional observational state—not
yet a native UDT law or prediction.

If external review accepts it, the next honest test is to carry this frozen map into a genuinely
different observation without adjusting it. That will test whether the same relational state can
organize more than SNe.
