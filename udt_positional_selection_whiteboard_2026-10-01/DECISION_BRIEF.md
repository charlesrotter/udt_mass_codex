# PSW1 decision brief — geometry can be measured; physical selection is still open

The three-perspective whiteboard found a concrete clock experiment that reads a
particular part of spacetime curvature. Prepare two freely falling clocks with
initially matched motion, exchange neighboring ticks, and immediately return
them. At sufficiently small separation the change in tick rates is fixed by
the local tidal curvature. The return has a different coefficient because it
arrives at a later event; it is not an inverse replay of the outward signal.

Averaging the leading effect across three perpendicular directions measures
Ricci curvature. The Ricci-flat equations supplied to our numerical survey
already set that particular average to zero. Individual directions can still
redshift or blueshift. An independent nonflat vacuum example confirms both.
This explains a constraint built into that comparison family; it does not turn
its directional plots into a failed test of UDT or explain every finite result.

The physical connection we made concrete is: could this clock-measured tensor
be the complete response governed by reciprocity? As an explicitly unadopted
hypothesis, it returns the familiar conditional Einstein family with an unfixed
constant. It does not select an additional positional effect. An earlier volume-
response proposal reached the same equation; the new gain is its operational
clock meaning, not a new field law. Measuring something does not establish
that it is what reciprocity balances.

Recommendation: retain the prepared-clock test, keep that response hypothesis
unadopted, and do not expand the same Ricci-flat survey merely to seek a nonzero
leading average that its equation forbids. The next useful question is whether
UDT's intended positional effect actually constrains this prepared comparison,
or is instead a finite-separation/global relation invisible to its leading
coefficient. The whiteboard has not proved which answer the founding premises
give. A local positive coefficient is not to be imposed just to close the math.

The endpoint-only alternative was also checked. It adds an observer-family
restriction already studied in NCI1, admits either sign and supplies no new
selection. Repeating it alone would not advance this gap.

What changes: a precise conditional discriminator, its derivation, counterchecks
and the response-identification comparison enter the central development.
What survives: ordinary local clocks, the founding interpretation of c_E,
the kernel's existing scope, the survey's actual successes and limits, and all
current scientific grades. No source, scale, matter law or preferred frame is
adopted. The small-separation result neither predicts cosmic redshift nor the
asymptote. All mathematical controls are supplied comparisons.

Three actual separate contexts challenged the synthesis. Their82 total symbolic
checks share Python/SymPy; analytic cross-checks use different arrangements but
the same model. A reviewer found an incomplete domain condition, now repaired
with the initial version preserved. No GPU or long solve was used. Final
attestations and repository check receipts bind the completed integration.

Return for discussion. There is no adoption request hidden in this packet and
no automatic next run. A later bounded derivation should name its physical
implication and discriminating calculation before spending additional compute.
