## The specific requirement, before proposing a test

The admitted interpretation requires **one physically meaningful assignment of
the common geometry to actual comparisons that realizes a mutual positional
slowing contribution while preserving the ordinary local clock and causal
interface**. An implementation must say which compared events, clocks, paths
and calibration instantiate this claim, and what justifies calling the effect
positional when ordinary motion and gravitational contributions coexist.

This is more specific than asking for an arbitrary extra equation: it is a
typed restriction on `(g,Q)`, with `Q` the physical comparison assignment.
Because R6 fixes Z[g,Q], changing neither g nor Q and appending a second
redshift factor cannot implement it. TPS1 imposed Ric=0, supplied geometric
initial data, and supplied query clocks. It did not impose this additional
physical-assignment/attribution condition. Multiple initial data are legitimate;
their multiplicity is not itself a missing-law proof.

Four distinctions are load-bearing.

1. **Mutual reception is not inversion.** F3 says D(-delta)=D(delta)^-1 for
   reversing the same ordered comparison. Two future null legs have slopes
   p=f_AB'(s), q=f_BA'(f_AB(s)); q need not be 1/p. On them the matched depths
   are -log p and -log q. Neither a universal q=1/p nor a universal p=q follows.
2. **Ordinary local physics is not zero finite effect.** The owner's clarification
   retains local proper clocks, not exact equality of all finite tidal or timing
   experiments with Minkowski/GR. A controlled O(L^2) effect is compatible with
   an ordinary local clock at L=0. It is not automatically an additional UDT effect.
3. **Positional is not net.** The tentative “probably” net-redshift expectation
   is not a theorem that every boosted, falling, or returning clock has Z>1.
   SGE1's matched log-ratio difference is a diagnostic, not an already derived
   arithmetic separation of physics.
4. **No preferred observer is not no physical clocks.** A specified lab, initial
   tangent U, and covariant preparation are ordinary query data. Transforming
   them with g leaves the experiment invariant. Replacing U changes the physical
   experiment. No uniform probability on all observers, cosmological rest frame,
   or population selector follows from observer covariance.

DDR does not replace that requirement by E=Ric. Local Metric Sufficiency does

## One concrete chain: a prepared two-way tidal clock test

### Calculation and sign convention

Use proper time in length units (c_E=1) and the SGE1/DCI1 convention
R(X,Y)=[nabla_X,nabla_Y]-nabla_[X,Y]. Put

    E(n,n)=g(R(n,U)U,n),        tr E=Ric(U,U).

This E is the **electric tidal quadratic form**, not DDR's response tensor E.
Call the latter E_response whenever both occur. Jacobi relative acceleration
is -E; this matches G358's recorded tide convention.

In Fermi coordinates around A, write e=E(n,n) at preparation. To the needed
order along the initial radial direction,

    g_tt=-(1+e r^2)+O(L^3),   g_tr=O(L^3),   g_rr=1+O(L^3).

Here t,r=O(L). The quadratic mixed/spatial curvature contractions vanish on
the radial line by Riemann antisymmetry. Time dependence of E contributes at
the next order. The prepared receiver has zero radial initial velocity;
possible transverse O(L^2) velocity from frame transport affects no displayed
quadratic coefficient. Geodesic motion and proper normalization give

    r_B(t)=L-(e/2)L t^2+O(L^4),
    d tau_B/dt=1+(e/2)L^2+O(L^3).

For neighboring emissions s=O(L), outward null arrival t_b satisfies

    t_b-s=r_B(t_b)-(e/6)r_B(t_b)^3+O(L^4),
    t_b=s+L-(e/2)L(s+L)^2-(e/6)L^3+O(L^4).

Thus at s=0, dt_b/ds=1-eL^2+O(L^3), and

    log p=-(1/2)e L^2+O(L^3).                        (P)

The future return obeys t_a=t_b+r_B(t_b)-(e/6)r_B(t_b)^3+O(L^4).
Dividing dt_a/dt_b=1-eL^2+O(L^3) by d tau_B/dt_b gives

    log q=-(3/2)e L^2+O(L^3).                        (Q)

The factor three concerns the later future return with a receiver that has
continued to fall. Replacing that return by the inverse outbound map would
give the wrong coefficient and sign. The emission parameter varies one prepared
worldline, rather than resetting B at each emitted tick. Resetting would be a
different comparison.

The remainder is a local smooth-geometry statement: fixed compact template,
bounded curvature/derivatives and transverse regular incidence give controlled
Taylor remainders and their emission derivatives for small enough L. It is not
a uniform error bound over TPS1 or arbitrary metrics; no finite instrument
accuracy is claimed. These analytical coefficients are initial candidates for
adversarial verification, not a numerical certification.

### Finite controls and a second analytic check

Reuse DCI1's actual lapse metric

    g_kappa=-N(r)^2 dt^2+dx^2+dy^2+dz^2,
    N=1+(kappa/2)r^2>0

on a small patch. The central A is geodesic; the receiver released from rest at
r=L has E(n,n)=kappa and physical radial speed
v_B=-kappa L^2+O(L^3) at first arrival. Killing photon energy and the ordinary
local Doppler contractions independently give

    p=N_B/[gamma_B(1-v_B)] =1-kappa L^2/2+O(L^3),
    q=gamma_B(1+v_B)/N_B  =1-3 kappa L^2/2+O(L^3).

This verifies the leading signs and coefficient ratio without the arrival-map
derivative. kappa<0 gives both leading redshifts; kappa>0 gives both leading
blueshifts; kappa=0 gives exact p=q=1 for this preparation. This metric is a
supplied control, not a native UDT solution. Its static metric does not conflict
with SGE1's stationary-clock exclusion because B is freely falling, not a
Killing clock. Holding B on a Killing orbit instead gives reciprocal lapse
ratios and pq=1, a deliberately different protocol.

Another necessary control is ordinary Minkowski recession: choose instead
A at x=0 and inertial B at x=L+vt, 0<v<1. Proper-time differentiation gives
exactly p=q=sqrt((1+v)/(1-v))=exp(eta)>1. Thus mutual net slowing alone does not
discriminate an additional positional effect. The zero-initial-motion protocol
specifically removes this freely supplied leading Doppler explanation.

### What distinguishes candidates

Repeat the preparation along three orthogonal directions in the same initial
frame, with equal initial L. These are three repeated test-clock experiments,
not an assumed physical global congruence. Equations (P),(Q) give

    sum_i log p_i=-(L^2/2)Ric(U,U)+O(L^3),
    sum_i log q_i=-(3L^2/2)Ric(U,U)+O(L^3).          (T)

The trace is independent of the chosen orthonormal triad. Three directions
recover the trace, not the full six-component E or an all-direction sign bound.
Six linearly independent quadratic-form directions can recover E's six
coefficients; positive/negative definiteness then needs an eigenvalue argument,
not a finite sign sample pretending to cover the sphere.

If Ric=0, E is trace-free. It cannot be negative definite. Hence a Ric=0
metric cannot supply **strictly positive leading quadratic coefficients in
every direction** for this preparation. Nonzero E has opposite-sign eigenvalues;
E=0 leaves higher-order effects undecided. A zero trace does not exclude
positive finite shifts in selected directions or effects at higher order or
larger separation.

Match the same preparation geometrically in g and g_0, with independently
specified initial frames, proper L, units and emission/relay rule. Then

    Delta log p=-(L^2/2)Delta E(n,n)+O(L^3),
    Delta log q=-(3L^2/2)Delta E(n,n)+O(L^3).

This is a useful comparison diagnostic. If both metrics are Ric=0, Delta E is
trace-free under the matched initial frames, so it cannot produce a strictly
positive all-direction quadratic excess. If a proposed implementation explicitly
requires such an excess, it needs a different Ricci contraction, a different
physical preparation, or a different leading-order requirement. Changing any
of those is a disclosed alternative, not a repair silently adopted here.

This does **not** say the founder requires a nonzero Ricci tensor, excludes GR
vacuum, demands all clocks slow, or specifies an isotropic L^2 positional law.
The diagnostic's net/GR-matched curvature shift is not automatically isolated
positional physics. Standard gravitational tides remain in it.

## Lay conclusion

UDT asks for more than a valid empty-space geometry with some redshifted clocks:
it asks that the same geometry give a physically identified positional slowing
alongside ordinary motion and gravity. The existing survey did not enforce that
identification. A small, carefully prepared pair of freely falling clocks gives
a concrete way to test one proposed identification: the outward and return
shifts expose a particular tidal curvature, and several directions reveal its
trace. That test can rule out a specified local implementation; it cannot yet
rule out UDT or turn the positional interpretation into a new field equation.
