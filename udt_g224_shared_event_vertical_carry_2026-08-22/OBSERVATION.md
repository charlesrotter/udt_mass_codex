# G224 observation

G223's negative boundary was exact: the G216 clock chain alone does not identify the vertical
lines of distinct ribbons.  But a genuinely composable pair of relations supplies more than two
abstract clock ratios.  At their shared middle event it supplies:

1. one ambient tangent space;
2. one metric-unit middle-observer tangent `U_B`;
3. two future-null vertical lines incident at that event; and
4. on each line, the positive metric functional `v -> -g(U_B,v)`.

Every nonzero linear functional on a one-dimensional real vector space is an isomorphism to
`R`.  Therefore the two metric functionals may provide the unique comparison

\[
S_B=\mu_{BC,B}^{-1}\circ\mu_{AB,B}.
\]

This possibility was not available to G223's deliberately abstract cross-ribbon question.  It
appears only after the shared event and its actual observer clock are made part of the type.

The same construction cannot compare different middle events without additional transport, and
it says nothing by itself about the two-dimensional normal screens.
