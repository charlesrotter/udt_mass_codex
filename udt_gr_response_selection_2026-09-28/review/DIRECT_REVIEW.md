## Load-bearing argument review

TF(E)=0 for a specified symmetric response. They do not identify E, assert
variational integrability or choose an action. Compactly supported inverse
metric variation gives the displayed f(R) Euler derivative, with the correct
signs of the volume, Hessian and box terms. The formula agrees with the primary
metric-variation reference [De Felice and Tsujikawa, section 2.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1002.4928).
The work imports that differential-geometric method only. Its source, matter
couplings, normalization and physical f(R) models are not UDT inputs.

Trace-free variation of EH gives S=0 and allows a connected constant scalar
curvature. It does not fix that constant, and does not uniquely identify even
an action giving the same projected derivative: rescalings, compact-support
boundary ambiguities and pure volume terms supply ordinary variational
nonuniqueness. The R-squared witness gives the stronger concrete separation of
two different projected equations, in the explicitly larger action-comparison
arena. The candidate correctly says the witness is outside G301 and makes no
claim that it passes the current GR filter or all UDT premises.

**Finite-jet classification.** The normal-coordinate dilation calculation is
sound. Naturality contributes the two covariant output factors, exact weight
zero removes the explicit homothety, and differentiability at the flat jet
controls the o(s^2) remainder. The fixed finite-order assumption and admitted
flattening rays make this a valid limit, not an illicit truncation. Higher jet
directions vanish after division by s^2. The remaining linear second normal jet
is equivalent to algebraic curvature. The attributed G301 invariant basis
then gives E=a Ric+b Rg, with universal constant coefficients in the stated
natural metric-only arena. This also matches [Navarro and Sancho, Theorem 5.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0709.1928).

The arbitrary finite-order conclusion comes from that analytic proof. The
producer's enumeration through order eight is correctly labeled a finite
regression. It does not prove the general classification and is not being used
to do so. The full historical invariant-basis rank census was not rerun here.

**Nondegeneracy and conservation.** For a!=0, TF(E)=aS and Bianchi implies
constant R on each connected regular solution. For a=0, DDR is vacuous. The
candidate correctly preserves this exception. Off-shell divergence freedom
would set b=-a/2 within the class, but is not inferred from DDR. In particular,
the trace-free equation's differential consequence does not prove an off-shell
conservation identity for whichever E was supplied.

**Ownership.** The current September 9 GR authority record controls historical
