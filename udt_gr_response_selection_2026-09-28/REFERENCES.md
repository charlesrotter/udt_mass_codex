# GRS1 mathematical references and exposure

Primary sources inspected through web tools on 2026-09-28. They supply methods,
not UDT premises. No external data or copied full text is included in this package.

* J. Navarro and J. B. Sancho, *On the naturalness of Einstein's equation*,
  arXiv:0709.1928v2, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0709.1928 . Definitions2.1–2.2,
  Theorem4.1/Corollary4.8 and Theorem5.1 give a natural-tensor classification
  including the covariant rank-two weight-zero case. Sections6–7 additionally
  use physical assumptions about stress-energy and scaling; those are not UDT
  inputs here. Our finite-order flattening proof exposes the narrower hypotheses
  actually used. The source is known mathematics, not a novel UDT result.
* A. De Felice and S. Tsujikawa, *f(R) Theories*, arXiv:1002.4928v2,
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1002.4928 , section2.1 equation(2.4): metric variation
  of f(R) supplies the response formula used in the comparison. Its physical
  models, observational discussion and source assumptions are not imported.
* David Tong, *General Relativity*, section4.1,
  https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/gr/grhtml/S4.html : Einstein–Hilbert
  variation and cosmological term, consulted as a primary pedagogical reference.
