## Two pairings must not be silently identified

not preserve the declared orthonormal metric eta2=diag(-1,1):

    D^T K D=K,
    D^T eta2 D=diag(-exp(-2 delta),exp(2 delta)) != eta2.

Lorentz boost. For N=[[1,-1],[-1,-1]], N^T K N=2 eta2 and

    N^-1 D N = [[cosh delta,sinh delta],

physical path. In an actual oriented planar transported comparison, the matching

## Independent restricted geometric calculation

A useful conditional bridge is also directly recoverable in a supplied smooth
Lorentz2 reciprocal chart. This is an explicitly restricted mathematical control,
not a physical symmetry selection or a statement that every G176 pair germ

    g2 = -exp(-2 phi(t,x)) dt^2 + exp(2 phi(t,x)) dx^2,
    theta0=exp(-phi)dt, theta1=exp(phi)dx,
    U=exp(phi) partial_t.

U is the ordinary unit rest-clock field in this chart; it need not be freely
falling. Independent coordinate Christoffels, transformed into this orthonormal
frame, give the single Lorentz connection one-form

    w = -exp(-2phi) phi_x dt + exp(2phi) phi_t dx.

Transport obeys dc + w J c=0 with J=[[0,1],[1,0]]. On a future null branch with
fixed sign s=+/-1, dx/dt=s exp(-2phi), its null eigenvector has J eigenvalue s.
Consequently d log f=-s w. In coordinates both signs yield

    d delta_clock = -phi_t dt + phi_x dx,
    delta_clock = phi_o-phi_e - 2 integral(phi_t dt).       (* )

An independent derivation uses omega=exp(-phi)k^t and the affine geodesic equation:
its derivative gives d log omega/dt=-phi_t+(dx/dt)phi_x, agreeing with (*).
This is a transport/readout consequence of the supplied metric, not an equation
selecting phi. Static phi gives the established Killing-clock endpoint depth;
a time-only presentation gives the opposite endpoint sign. Therefore an
identification delta_clock=phi_o-phi_e on every arbitrarily short null segment
would restrict this particular chart/clock sector to phi_t=0. On one finite path,
integral cancellation suffices, so stationarity must not be inferred from a
single agreement.

The one-form alpha=-phi_t dt+phi_x dx has

psi=-a(t)+b(x) when phi=a(t)+b(x), and delta_clock=psi_o-psi_e for these null legs.

connected regions, vanishing periods also matter. Scalar potential exactness
