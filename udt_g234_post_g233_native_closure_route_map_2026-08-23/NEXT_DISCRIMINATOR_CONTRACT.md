# G234 next-discriminator contract

No solve follows G234 until a candidate condition is named before outcomes are inspected.

## Allowed candidate types

1. **Invariant family cut:** a diffeomorphism-natural condition `C[g]=0` that reduces the admitted
   metric family without defining membership by the desired profile.
2. **Global relation law:** a natural condition `G[g,R]=0` on a complete valued observer-relation
   network that is stronger than overlap, composition, pullback reconstruction, and regularity.

The time-live/nonspherical route qualifies only after it supplies one of these two condition types.

## Mandatory preregistration

Before evaluation, record:

- the exact condition and its provenance;
- its domain, regularity, topology, orientation, branch, and query assumptions;
- every fixed value as `free-and-explored`, `pinned-by-THEORY`, or `pinned-by-HABIT`;
- one accepted control and one rejected control chosen independently of the desired profile;
- the exact invariant used to distinguish them;
- the maximum conclusion if the test passes or fails.

## Nonidentity gate

The candidate must reject at least one regular member already admitted by the current evaluator.
For a local/family condition, use a candidate-satisfying seed and a G233-type higher-order
perturbation. For a global condition, construct two globally admissible valuations that agree on
the declared local and calibration data but differ in the candidate global invariant. For a
time-live/nonspherical condition, distinguish at least one registered G206--G210 supplied
extension from another.

## Automatic failures

The candidate fails as a selector if it is only:

- a coordinate, gauge, or calibration convention;
- metricity, Bianchi, Cartan, pullback, overlap, or cocycle compatibility;
- faithful reconstruction of already supplied values;
- smoothness, completeness, or causality without a demonstrated family cut sufficient for the
  stated conclusion;
- declaration that the physical profile belongs to a preferred ansatz family;
- a fit, observational target, `X_max` insertion, action, source, matter, bootstrap, transfer, or
  protected-package import.

## Maximum possible next landing

A passing test may establish only that the named condition narrows the declared arena and survives
its exact controls. Physical ownership requires a separate premise audit; uniqueness requires a
full survivor classification.
