# G301 lay report

Date: 2026-08-30

We asked whether the remaining law could still be almost anything, even after requiring it to use
the whole metric, behave smoothly where UDT overlaps GR, propagate locally, and introduce no hidden
ruler.

Inside that deliberately narrow lane, the answer is **no**. Almost all of the apparent choices are
just different ways of writing the same rule. The freedom collapses to two serious possibilities:

1. the law fixes a certain overall curvature quantity to zero; or
2. the law lets that quantity be one constant belonging to the universe's initial/global data.

Both treat the full metric, including angular and screen behavior. Both have the same local
short-wavelength behavior around the quiet regime. They differ only in whether one uniform
curvature value is forbidden or carried as a constant.

That is genuine progress: the local scale-free question narrowed from an unlimited formula hunt to
one precise fork. But it did **not** yet tell us which fork UDT owns. Requiring the familiar
divergence-free form chooses the first, but UDT has not derived that requirement. Allowing one
global curvature datum gives the second and could make an observational anchor relevant only
after that class is independently justified.

Nothing in G301 changed the UDT metric or reciprocal kernel, discarded the cancelling angular
sector, fitted observations, or adopted a GR field equation. Global/nonlocal relation laws,
higher-order laws, loud-regime dynamics, sources, and the realized universe remain outside this
bounded result.
