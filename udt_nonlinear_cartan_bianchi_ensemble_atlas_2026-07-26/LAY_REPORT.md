# Lay readout

Within the chosen torus-invariant triangular coframe, the exact metric
component atlas really does have an orchestra-like structure.

We let all of the clock/distance and angular parts vary together, kept every
nonlinear term, and calculated how the complete geometry bends. The result is
not a collection of isolated instruments. The clock/distance field `phi` has
direct curvature-component couplings with three kinds of angular deformation:
overall angular size, reciprocal angular shape, and angular shear. Those angular
deformations in turn connect to the two twist/connection channels. So every
instrument belongs to one connected geometric network.

There is also a useful restraint: `phi` does not have a direct component-graph
edge to either twist channel in this map. The angular geometry is the bridge.
This is not yet a frame-independent physical coupling theorem. Within its
stated slice, it supports your old “the angular orchestra modulates phi”
intuition more clearly than the earlier one-instrument-at-a-time calculations
did.

What we have not found is the conductor. The Cartan and Bianchi relations say
how any chosen metric must remain internally consistent. They do not say which
combination the universe chooses, how matter supplies it, or how background
density retunes it. In other words, we now have a much better wiring diagram,
not yet the tuning law.

The later density experiment is therefore well framed but not ready to run.
We can center a broad exploratory bracket on the usual Lambda-CDM density
estimate, clearly labeled as an outside comparison. First UDT must supply the
socket into which density plugs: a native rule connecting total proper density
to these metric channels on a complete global branch.
