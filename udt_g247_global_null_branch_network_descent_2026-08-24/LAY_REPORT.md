# G247 lay report

The metric now tells us how to organize the whole family of lightlike observer connections without
pretending there is one preferred route.

Think of each direct null connection as one flight leg. Two consecutive flight legs do not usually
turn into one direct flight: the first may go east and the second west, leaving the start and finish
timelike rather than lightlike separated. So the direct legs are a network, not a group whose legs
can always be multiplied into another direct leg.

What does compose is the itinerary. Along that itinerary:

- the reciprocal clock depth adds exactly;
- the inverse ruler factor multiplies exactly;
- the angular/focusing state composes as an ordered matrix and remembers the route;
- a caustic can squash the visible position map without destroying the complete phase state.

This removes a category mistake and preserves all metric-owned information. It does not yet choose
which global route or observer population is physically realized, and it does not fit data.
