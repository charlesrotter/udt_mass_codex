## Whole question

rank-two pair germ underdefined. G298 asks the narrower constructive question: does each declared
regular directed null leg already contain the calibrated clock direction and transported ruler
direction needed by the existing completed-pair evaluator?

  regular leg;

## Pure and reduced options

The least-imposed option retains the entire route-indexed family and full transported vectors. A
smaller scalar-only option would keep only radar rate or reciprocal depth, but that discards the
screen/path information already shown active by G269 and is not an admissible complete transfer.

## Exact bounded regime

1. regular future-timelike observer clocks;
2. regular future-directed null legs with positive endpoint frequencies;
3. declared route labels and no sampled caustic/focal rank loss on the leg;
4. local one-jet output at the target endpoint;
5. full spacetime vectors retained before the pair metric and terminal readout;
6. flat planar, active-screen, static-lapse, homothety, reversal, and isometry-exchanged
   multibranch controls.

Coincidence, null-degenerate pair planes, sampled caustics, topology-changing strata, and a unique
higher embedded pair surface are outside the regular theorem and must be classified rather than
discarded.

from supplied directed causal legs to calibrated regular pair one-jets and show that the existing
kernel recovers the same clock depth. It may not select observer events, routes, branch population,
