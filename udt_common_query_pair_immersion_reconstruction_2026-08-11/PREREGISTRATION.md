## Whole question

When endpoint depth, pair-metric shift, geodesic variation, ambient transport, and normal transport
are generated from one fully typed calibrated observer query, which channels become identities or
compatibility-linked data of one pair immersion, and which remain genuinely additional query or
extrinsic data?

This test does **not** search for a universal preferred path, physical branch, scalar conductor,
action, source, matter law, bootstrap selector, or observational fit. It characterizes the geometry
returned by two declared queries.

## Frozen witness/query universe

Exactly two query classes are admitted.

### Q1 — exact stationary R17 leaf control

- G63 witness: `R17_P1_P`, hence `lambda=+1`, `epsilon=+0.12`, twist `a=0.4`.
- Initial chart point inherited from G63: `(t,theta,varphi,psi)=(0.07,1.08,0.31,0.44)`.
- Pair immersion: the intrinsic Hopf-cylinder leaf through that point,
  `F_R17(y,s)=(0.07+y,1.08,0.31,0.44+2s)`.
- This query owns a pair surface and its tangent/normal geometry. It does not silently own a
  geodesic ruling; a Jacobi channel is reported only if a declared geodesic variation is actually
  constructed on this same surface.

### Q2 — generic complete time-live Fermi query

- G63 witness: `TL_P2`, hence `epsilon=+0.15`, with all registered `kappa`, `phi`, `beta`, `Q`, `S`,
  coordinate, and time dependencies active. It remains local and off-shell.
- Initial chart point inherited from G63: `(t,x,y,z)=(0.12,-0.18,0.23,-0.14)`.
- Initial observer tangent and ruler are the first two vectors of the inverse complete coframe at
  that point, normalized and oriented by the registered coframe presentation.
- The observer germ is the timelike geodesic through the initial point. The ruler is parallel
  transported along it. The pair immersion is the local Fermi/exponential surface
  `F_TL(y,s)=Exp_{z_A(y)}[s n(y)]`.
- Its `s`-rulings are affinely parameterized spacelike geodesics. The `y` variation is therefore a
  query-owned Jacobi field.

The two queries are controls of different type. Q1 tests an exact source-owned integrable leaf;
Q2 tests a generic complete local time-live exponential query. No conclusion may be generalized
from these two witnesses to the full metric solution space.

- Production integration: adaptive DOP853, `rtol=1e-11`, `atol=1e-13`, `max_step<=2.5e-3`.
- Local query domain for Q2: `|y|<=0.06`, `|s|<=0.06`.
- Derivative/convergence scales: `0.004`, `0.002`, `0.001` in query coordinates where applicable.
- Small-loop half-widths: `0.04`, `0.02`, `0.01` when the loop remains in the regular query tile.
- Independent verification must use a separately implemented fixed-step RK4/centered-difference
  path or exact symbolic control; it may not import the production geometry evaluator.

These are bounded numerical controls, not physical scales or boundaries.

## Required outputs from the same query

For each typed query, report when defined:

1. induced pair metric `h` and `(kappa_pair,phi_pair,beta_pair)`;
2. tangent and positive normal bundles;
3. second fundamental form `II` and normal connection `D_perp`;
4. Gauss, Codazzi, and Ricci compatibility residuals under one declared curvature convention;
5. the Jacobi propagator generated by the same geodesic variation, or `NOT_OWNED_BY_QUERY`;
6. ambient and normal holonomy around loops lying in the same pair surface;
7. small-loop curvature-generator comparisons;
8. regularity, rank, causal inertia, focal/cut warning, and numerical-convergence classifications.

Any G63 channel whose path, loop, or variation is not generated by the declared immersion remains
additional query data. It may not be retrofitted into the common object.

## Preregistered landing classes

Exactly one primary landing will be used:

1. `COMMON_QUERY_CHANNELS_COMPATIBILITY_LINKED_WITH_RETAINED_EXTRINSIC_DATA` — the defined channels
   satisfy common-immersion compatibility, while `h` alone still does not determine `II`, normal
   connection, or route-labelled transport.
2. `COMMON_QUERY_OWNS_ONLY_A_STRICT_CHANNEL_SUBSET` — one or more proposed channels cannot be typed
   on the same query without additional data.
3. `QUERY_CLASS_DEPENDENT_CHANNEL_ARCHITECTURE` — Q1 and Q2 own different channel sets for exact
   geometric reasons, with neither promoted to a physical regime.
4. `TYPE_FAILURE_IN_COMMON_IMMERSION_CONSTRUCTION` — a promised channel was assigned the wrong
   mathematical home.
5. `NUMERICALLY_UNRESOLVED_COMMON_IMMERSION_TEST` — convergence or residual gates do not certify a
   classification.

Maximum conclusion: a two-query, bounded geometric ownership/compatibility classification. No
preferred physical query, UDT field equation, dynamics, stability, action, source, bootstrap law,
signal law, or downstream physics follows.

## Falsification and certification

The package fails closed if:

- either frozen witness/query is silently changed;
- angular or mixing fields are zeroed in Q2;
- a G63 path is called common without being generated by the declared query;
- `beta` is treated as the event-pairing protocol or an additive character;
- an arbitrary immersion variation is called Jacobi without a geodesic variation;
- the pair metric is claimed to determine extrinsic geometry;
- Gauss/Codazzi/Ricci residuals fail to converge or use inconsistent signs;
- production and independent implementations share the load-bearing evaluator;
- a singular or focal branch is discarded instead of classified;
- the result is promoted beyond the two registered witnesses.
