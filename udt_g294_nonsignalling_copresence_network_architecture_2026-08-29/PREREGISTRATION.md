## Frozen question

Does a metric-native, non-signalling formulation of co-presence exist in the bounded current UDT
arena, and does it select or propagate physical values?

## Frozen tests

### T1 — reversal and distance typing

Verify that positive pair magnitude can coexist with oriented depth,
`rho_AB=rho_BA>=0`, `delta_BA=-delta_AB`, an odd projective channel, and an even mutual channel.
The `sech` equality may be used only in G267's provisional planar scope; the complete metric theorem
must retain G269's `M_PT<=sech(delta)` and screen mismatch.

### T2 — dimensional closure

Represent dimensions as exact exponent vectors. Test whether any power of `c_E` alone has length
dimension and whether one independent proper-clock datum supplies `ell=c_E T_*`. A dimensionless
depth-to-length map must retain an unspecified dimensionless function or an independently owned
attachment.

### T3 — relation versus foliation

Give an exact finite symmetric nontransitive co-presence relation. Prove that symmetry alone does not
produce global “now” leaves. Separately test a timelike one-form with nonzero Frobenius obstruction
to show that a time direction need not define hypersurfaces. A universal foliation must therefore be
supplied or derived by a stronger condition.

### T4 — connectedness versus signalling

Construct exact finite examples in which equal-slice variables are correlated while the intervention
response is diagonal/causal. Separately show that instantaneously re-enforcing a cross-node
constraint after a freely chosen local intervention produces a remote response. Conclude only that
correlation is compatible with no-signalling and that a bare constraint is insufficient to certify
it.

### T5 — metric nonselection

Construct at least two regular positive static-spherical primary metrics sharing the same supplied
`t=constant` co-presence foliation and passing reciprocal evaluation, while a curvature invariant
differs. Co-presence existence alone then does not select the profile.

### T6 — architecture classification

Classify at least:

- pair-relative relation/groupoid without global foliation;
- integrable global co-presence foliation;
- complete-network constraint with separately causal response/evolution;
- literal controllable instantaneous response.

Record which are coherent, additional premises, nonselective, or causally rejected.

## Preregistered falsifiers

The candidate architectural landing fails if any exact check shows that:

- positive pair magnitude is inconsistent with directed reciprocal reversal;
- `c_E` alone supplies an absolute length without another datum;
- symmetry alone forces transitive global leaves;
- correlation mathematically entails remote controllable response;
- co-presence plus metric cones rejects one registered regular profile for a nonidentity reason; or
- the complete metric universally forces `M_PT=sech(delta)` with active screen mismatch.
