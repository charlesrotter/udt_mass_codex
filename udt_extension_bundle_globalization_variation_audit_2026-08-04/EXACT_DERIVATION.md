# Exact extension-bundle globalization and variation derivation

Date: 2026-08-04

## 1. Scope and conventions

This derivation asks only whether the registered local complete-coframe charts fit into coherent
global geometric containers, and what their exact infinitesimal compatibility rules are. It does not
choose a global topology, a reciprocal field, an action, a source, a boundary condition, a carrier,
or a physical branch.

On a chart `U_i`, let `bar_theta_i` be a reference coframe and `theta_i` a physical coframe. Write

```text
theta_i = E_i bar_theta_i,

E_i = [[A_i,       0],
       [D_i S_i, D_i]].
```

Here:

- `A_i=diag(exp(-phi_i),exp(phi_i))` is available only after a local reciprocal realization has
  been supplied;
- `D_i` is a local positive-triangular representative of the rank-two screen metric and has three
  chart directions;
- `S_i` is a local `2 x 2` base-screen mixing representative and has four chart directions.

The `3+4` count is a local chart count. No field equation has been loaded, so it is not a count of
propagating modes.

## 2. Two-sided overlap rule

Let the physical and reference coframes transform on `U_i intersect U_j` by

```text
theta_j     = L_ij theta_i,
bar_theta_j = R_ij bar_theta_i.
```

Then

```text
E_j R_ij bar_theta_i = L_ij E_i bar_theta_i,
```

and therefore

```text
E_j = L_ij E_i R_ij^-1.                                  (2.1)
```

For `i -> j -> k`, the coframe cocycles have the ordered products

```text
L_ik = L_jk L_ij,
R_ik = R_jk R_ij.
```

Substitution into (2.1) gives

```text
E_k = L_jk L_ij E_i R_ij^-1 R_jk^-1
    = L_ik E_i R_ik^-1.
```

Thus compatible left and right cocycles are sufficient for associative two-sided gluing. This is a
compatibility identity for a supplied cover and supplied transitions. It does not generate the cover,
its incidence, its topology, or its physical transition functions.

Equation (2.1) is an identity in the ambient complete-coframe bundle. Arbitrary `GL(4)` choices of
`L_ij` and `R_ij` need not preserve the seven-dimensional positive-triangular slice used on one
chart. Globalizing that slice therefore requires a supplied smooth rank-two split and its associated
transition subgroup, followed by local re-triangularization. The exact tensor-level construction and
transition laws are given in Section 6; the generic two-sided identity alone is not a proof of that
reduction.

## 3. Exact block inverse and local variation

For invertible `A` and `D`,

```text
E^-1 = [[ A^-1,       0],
        [-S A^-1, D^-1]].                                (3.1)
```

The determinant factors as

```text
det(E) = det(A) det(D).
```

Therefore the registered smooth tile is fixed-rank whenever `A` and `D` are invertible. Defect or
rank-changing configurations are not rejected; they require a different stratum and chart.
An ambient path of complete metrics or potentials may cross such a stratum, but its crossing vector
is not a tangent vector **within** the fixed-rank bundle tile. Calling it a tangent therefore always
requires the ambient/stratified configuration space to be named.

Differentiate the block matrix:

```text
delta(E) = [[delta(A),                         0],
            [delta(D) S + D delta(S), delta(D)]].
```

Multiplying by (3.1) produces an exact cancellation of the two `delta(D) S A^-1` terms:

```text
delta(E) E^-1 = [[delta(A) A^-1,                 0],
                 [D delta(S) A^-1, delta(D) D^-1]].       (3.2)
```

For the local reciprocal representative,

```text
delta(A) A^-1 = diag(-delta(phi),delta(phi)).
```

Equation (3.2) keeps the depth, three screen-metric chart directions, and four mixing chart
directions algebraically distinct. It supplies neither their physical ownership nor a kinetic or
constraint operator.

## 4. Variation of the overlap data

Define right logarithmic variations

```text
e_i    = delta(E_i) E_i^-1,
ell_ij = delta(L_ij) L_ij^-1,
r_ij   = delta(R_ij) R_ij^-1.
```

Differentiating (2.1) and right-multiplying by `E_j^-1` gives

```text
e_j = ell_ij + Ad(L_ij)e_i - Ad(E_j)r_ij.                 (4.1)
```

If the transition representatives are fixed, (4.1) reduces to

```text
e_j = Ad(L_ij)e_i.
```

If transitions vary continuously, differentiating the two cocycles gives

```text
ell_ik = ell_jk + Ad(L_jk)ell_ij,
r_ik   = r_jk   + Ad(R_jk)r_ij.                          (4.2)
```

Coboundaries generated by changes of local trivialization are presentation gauge. Cohomologically
nontrivial solutions of (4.2) may describe continuous global moduli after quotienting by those
coboundaries, but only after a cover and completion family are supplied. A change of topology,
orientation class, or discrete reversal class is another sector, not an infinitesimal tangent.

## 5. Reciprocal transition algebra

The registered constant reciprocal algebra uses

```text
G_a = diag(a,a^-1),
F_b = [[0,b],[b^-1,0]].
```

Exact multiplication gives

```text
G_a G_d = G_(ad),
F_b F_c = G_(b/c),
F_b diag(exp(-phi),exp(phi)) F_b^-1
    = diag(exp(phi),exp(-phi)).
```

Consequently any reciprocal transition product that is required to equal the identity—such as an
identity-required triple-overlap cocycle product—has even reversal parity. This does **not** require
every noncontractible loop to have even monodromy. Odd `Z2` monodromy around a noncontractible loop is
precisely a possible reversal-twisted reciprocal bundle. The algebra permits such an unoriented or
reversal-twisted object on a supplied cocycle. It does not prove that the constant swap is a physical
Lorentz transition: for the diagonal two-channel readout

```text
eta_2 = diag(-1,1),
F_1^T eta_2 F_1 = -eta_2,
```

not `eta_2`. A physical reversal seam therefore remains conditional on a complete metric lift,
screen/angular action, cover incidence, and boundary rule.

When a local nonnull `dphi` realization exists, the scalar

```text
s_phi = g^-1(dphi,dphi)
```

and the corresponding unoriented rank-one projector are unchanged by `dphi -> -dphi`. This supports
sign-insensitive local data; it does not solve the null/zero-gradient strata or select an oriented
global depth.

## 6. What globalizes after a reciprocal reduction is supplied

There are three distinct architectures, and this derivation does not choose between them:

1. In the relational-query architecture, the ordered observer-ruler pair lives in an associated
   query bundle. No global observer or ruler field is required. The reciprocal plane and its screen
   exist on the pullback to that query bundle.
2. In the realized-field architecture, a global reciprocal pair or equivalent rank-two reduction
   over spacetime is additional physical field data. Its existence and ownership remain open.
3. In the branch-derived architecture, a parent metric solution may construct a reciprocal section
   from an intrinsic object on a bounded branch. Such constructions are not universal.

The two global bases must be distinguished.

- In the query architecture, let `pi:P->M` be the **total** ordered pair-frame query bundle over a
  supplied regular Lorentzian metric `g`. The tautological reciprocal plane `N` is a smooth rank-two
  subbundle of `pi*TM` over `P`, and its screen is

  ```text
  Q = N^(perp_(pi*g)).
  ```

  A single query point gives only one fiber. The global query-bundle claim and the gluing argument
  below live over the entire paracompact total space `P`.
- In a realized-field architecture over spacetime, the input must be a supplied smooth nondegenerate
  rank-two reduction together with a smooth split

  ```text
  TM = N direct_sum Q.
  ```

  If a physical metric `g` has already been supplied as a readout, one may take `Q=N^(perp_g)`.
  When constructing a configuration before selecting `g`, the split itself is part of the supplied
  reduction; using `N^(perp_g)` must not be used circularly to claim that `g` was derived.

On either base, once smooth rank-two bundles `N` and `Q` have been supplied, coordinate-free
extension data consist locally of

```text
h in Sym^2_+(Q*),
sigma in Hom(N,Q).
```

The fiber `Sym^2_+(R^2*)` is the convex cone `SPD(2)` and is contractible. `Hom(R^2,R^2)` is a
four-dimensional vector space and is contractible. Their product is therefore a contractible
seven-dimensional local extension fiber.

Choose the following explicit component convention on an overlap:

```text
x_N,j = P_ij x_N,i,
x_Q,j = Q_ij x_Q,i.
```

Then a screen metric matrix and a mixing map transform as

```text
h_j     = Q_ij^-T h_i Q_ij^-1,
sigma_j = Q_ij sigma_i P_ij^-1.                         (6.1)
```

Both laws obey their induced cocycles. Positive definiteness is preserved by the congruence in
(6.1), and the zero mixing section is invariant. A local triangular matrix `D_i` with
`h_i=D_i^T D_i` generally does not transform triangularly under `Q_ij`; a compensating local `O(2)`
rotation/re-triangularization restores the chart. Thus `D_i` and the block coordinate `S_i` are local
representatives of the tensor-level data, not global matrices. This is the reduced transition law
that the generic ambient `GL(4)` formula in Section 2 did not supply by itself.

On a paracompact smooth base, choose local positive screen metrics `h_i` and a subordinate partition
of unity `chi_i`. Then

```text
h = sum_i chi_i h_i
```

is positive definite wherever the weights sum to one, because for every nonzero screen vector `v`,

```text
h(v,v) = sum_i chi_i h_i(v,v) > 0.
```

Thus a screen bundle admits a global positive metric without admitting a global screen frame. The
local triangular `D_i` is only a Cholesky/polar representative of `h`; it is not a global scalar
matrix unless additional triviality is supplied.

Similarly, `Hom(N,Q)` is a vector bundle with a canonical zero section. Therefore its existence does
not add a topological obstruction. This does **not** select zero mixing; an arbitrary nonzero section
may be physical if a future law selects it.

The conclusion is precise and conditional:

```text
Once smooth rank-two bundles N and Q are supplied over the total query bundle, or over spacetime by
a supplied split, the positive screen-metric and mixing extension fibers add no independent
existence obstruction.
```

The hard global join is not removed. It is localized to the reciprocal reduction or query semantics,
the actual transition/cover data, completion topology and boundary glue, rank-changing strata, and
the native law.

## 7. Global `phi` is not silently supplied

The founded reciprocal character fixes the local algebraic meaning of depth. It does not by itself
make every `phi_i(x)` one global scalar. Preserving transitions may shift or rescale the local
representative, and reversal transitions change its sign. Depending on the supplied cocycle, the
global object could be a scalar, an affine/line-bundle section, an unoriented line, a path-dependent
comparison, or only query-relative data. Selecting among those possibilities is still open.

Accordingly, `phi` is not an arbitrary eleventh placeholder field, but neither is its physical
event-by-event assignment already derived.

## 8. Exact conclusion boundary

The derived result is an equivariant configuration-container and tangent-compatibility result. It
does not derive:

- a globally realized observer-ruler field;
- a preferred global coframe or parallel split;
- a unique topology or boundary completion;
- an action, equation of motion, response, source, carrier, or bootstrap rule;
- a propagating degree-of-freedom count;
- an initial-value or time-live system.

Those omissions are represented explicitly in the ledgers rather than repaired by an ansatz.
