# Lay report

We checked whether the clock-and-ruler structure we found was like balancing a pencil perfectly on
its tip: mathematically possible, but destroyed by the slightest change.

It is not like that. It is more like a valley. You can change the detailed shape of the metric a
little, change the twist a little, or change the way the angular sector responds a little, and the
same basic structure remains:

- the metric still identifies one clock direction;
- the turning of that clock direction still identifies its reciprocal ruler direction; and
- the complete geometry remains regular in the tested sense.

That is a real advance. We have found a stable *geometric motif* in the space of possible metrics,
not one miraculous formula with exactly tuned numbers.

But “stable” here means stable under small mathematical changes, not stable matter moving through
time. We still do not know what law makes the universe choose this family, how large the viable
region is, or whether a physical solution lives in it. Co-presence remains our working big-picture
interpretation; this test neither proved nor needed it, and it did not prove instantaneous
communication.

The most useful next move is to map where these valleys join or end, then ask whether UDT's existing
founding premises select one. We should not invent a new action merely to force that choice.
