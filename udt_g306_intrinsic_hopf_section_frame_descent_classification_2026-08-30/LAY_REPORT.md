# G306 lay report

G305 found that the positive completed geometry has a genuine compact three-dimensional spherical
space. G306 asked whether that space also tells us which Hopf-like pattern must exist.

The answer is halfway between yes and no.

The geometry naturally contains two handed families of exceptionally orderly global direction
patterns. Their lines close into circles, they twist without stretching or shearing, and
collapsing each circle to one point produces a two-sphere. So the old Hopf shape is no longer merely
an arbitrary object drawn inside an artificial box. A closely related family is genuinely present
in the completed metric's own geometry.

But the round space is perfectly symmetric. It cannot choose one pattern from either family any
more than a perfect ordinary sphere can choose one longitude as special. Choosing a coordinate
frame can make one pattern look preferred, but changing that frame can even change the raw
coordinate Hopf count. That coordinate count is therefore not physical by itself.

Once one geometric member is supplied, there is a frame-independent measure of its handed twist,
equal to \(+1\) or \(-1\). It does not depend on the size of the three-sphere. This removes more old
scaffolding—the invariant no longer needs a fixed component target—but it still does not explain
why Nature chooses or populates a member.

So this is linear progress. The remaining question is no longer “can this geometry host a Hopf
pattern?” It is “what, if anything in the complete UDT relation or dynamics, turns one member of
this metric-defined family into a physical field?” No action, matter model, mass law, scale, or
\(X_{\max}\) was inserted here.

The outside reviewer independently reproduced that mathematical result. It found only that our
sealed replay package was inconveniently assembled: some repo-wide checks were listed as if they
belonged inside the small intake, one verifier looked in the wrong folder, and the first derivation
script expected SymPy. Those defects have been repaired without changing a scientific byte of the
result. The exact production calculation now needs only ordinary Python. The repair-only reviewer
reran the sealed package and accepted every repair with no defects. G306 is therefore externally
closed in its bounded scope: the metric supplies the two Hopf families and their geometric
invariant, but still does not choose or populate a physical member.
