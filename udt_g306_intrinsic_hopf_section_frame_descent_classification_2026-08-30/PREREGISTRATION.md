# G306 preregistration — intrinsic Hopf-section and frame-descent classification

Date: 2026-08-30
Status: `PREREGISTERED_BEFORE_OUTCOMES`

## Frozen question

On every positive-radius round `S3` spatial slice supplied conditionally by G305, determine whether
the metric, orientation, complete spatial-frame gauge, celestial direction bundle, and projected
screen connection derive:

1. a unique physical `S3 -> S2` map or unit direction section;
2. no intrinsic Hopf structure beyond existence of arbitrary maps; or
3. an invariant metric-defined family of geometric Hopf congruences without a selected member.

The result must separately grade component-map topology, geometric-vector-field topology,
frame-gauge descent, isometry naturality, target ownership, and physical population.

## Frozen candidate landings

### A — invariant family, no selected physical section

The round metric intrinsically characterizes one or more isometry orbits of unit geodesic Killing
fields with pure screen rotation. Each supplied member defines a Hopf fibration and admits a
frame-independent normalized geometric invariant, but isotropy prevents the metric from selecting
one member. Physical field/query population, target ownership, dynamics, history, mass, magnitude,
and `X_max` remain open.

### B — unique intrinsic section

A unique metric/screen-natural section survives the full round-slice isometry group and complete
local frame gauge, with no observer, axis, query, target, or complex structure supplied.

### C — no intrinsic family

Even a metric-defined equivalence family of Hopf congruences fails; G305 supplies only a domain on
which arbitrary maps exist.

### D — derivation or certification failure

Any load-bearing algebra, topology, symmetry, independent replay, or premise gate fails.

No outcome-dependent landing may be added.

## Frozen exact derivations

1. **Isotropy test.** At a point of round `S3`, exhibit the full `SO(3)` tangent isotropy. Prove
   that an isometry-natural tangent section must be fixed by that action and hence must vanish;
   a metric-natural unit section is impossible.
2. **Scalar/curvature controls.** Show that the reciprocal scalar alone has contractible target,
   normalized gradients are undefined at zeros and Frobenius-restricted, and constant curvature
   supplies no nondegenerate spatial curvature eigendirection.
3. **Radial control.** Show that a supplied-observer radial direction is not globally smooth at the
   observer and antipode/cut locus and therefore is neither universal nor a Hopf section.
4. **Large frame-gauge test.** On `S3 ~= SU(2)`, construct a smooth basepoint-fixed local rotation
   `R(q)=Ad_q`. Show that it sends a constant component direction to the standard Hopf component
   map. Therefore the raw component Hopf number does not descend through full local `SO(3)` frame
   gauge.
5. **Geometric family construction.** For every orthogonal complex structure `J` on oriented
   `R4`, define `V_J(x)=Jx/a` on the radius-`a` sphere. Verify unit norm, tangency, Killing property,
   geodesic flow, closed great-circle fibers, zero expansion/shear, and pure screen rotation.
6. **Full family census.** Classify the orthogonal complex structures as two `S2` components
   `SO(4)/U(2)` distinguished only after orientation. Verify the isometry group acts transitively
   on each component, so no member is preferred. Without frozen orientation retain only the
   orientation-blind union/absolute class.
7. **Intrinsic invariant.** For `alpha_J=g(V_J,.)`, derive
   `d alpha_J = +/- (2/a) * alpha_J` and the normalized helicity
   `(4 pi^2 a^2)^(-1) integral alpha_J wedge d alpha_J = +/- 1`, with sign tied to orientation and
   chirality. Test independence from `a` and carry along G305's supplied regular slicing.
8. **Emergent target typing.** Show that after `V_J` is supplied, its closed-circle orbit space is
   an `S2` and the quotient map is a Hopf fibration; neither the member `J` nor a fixed cross-history
   target identification is selected by the metric.
9. **Screen/query audit.** Reconcile the result with G290/G292/G300: connection/Euler data descend
   conditionally, the celestial control fiber is available, but neither supplies an individual
   global direction field or lawful population.
10. **Scope audit.** Prohibit export of the maximally symmetric no-section result to nonspherical,
    quotient, singular, route-conditioned, or dynamically populated cases.

- the normalized intrinsic helicity by a method distinct from the production derivation;
- isotropy fixed-vector nonexistence;
- all premise and maximum-conclusion flags.

1. one coordinate Hopf map is the unique metric-owned physical field;

4. constant curvature selects a unique curvature eigendirection;
5. orientation or time orientation selects a member of an `S2` moduli family;
6. existence of the intrinsic family populates a physical field or query;

8. kinematic carry along a supplied slicing is dynamical conservation or stability;
9. the old `L2+L4` action, fixed target, box, or boundary is metric-derived;
10. the round-slice isotropy no-go applies unchanged to nonspherical or route-conditioned cases.
