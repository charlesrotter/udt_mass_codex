# Ponder map — panel, instruments, and score

This is a question map, not a result.

The complete metric now resembles an instrument panel with four different kinds of information:

- common size/scale (`kappa`);
- reciprocal clock-versus-ruler imbalance (`phi`);
- event-pairing or synchronization lean (`beta`); and
- angular-frame carry (`U_gamma`).

The founding postulates may define one specific instrument—the reciprocal clock/ruler comparison—
without declaring the other readings nonexistent. The audit asks whether that is the exact
relationship: one founded microphone inside a larger geometric orchestra.

The central alternatives are:

1. the founding query owns the complete panel;
2. it owns one unique reciprocal projection while enriched queries can access other channels;
3. several scalar projections satisfy the founding gates, leaving even the founding microphone
   nonunique; or
4. the source signature is too incomplete to own any realized projection.

Regime-dependent orchestration is a later question. No geometric activity stratum will be renamed
micro, ordinary, or cosmological here.
