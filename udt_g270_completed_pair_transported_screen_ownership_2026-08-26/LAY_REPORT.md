# G270 lay report

Completed reciprocity makes sure each local two-dimensional clock-and-ruler sheet is internally
balanced. It does not say how that sheet is tilted relative to the sheet carried from another
observer.

The exact counterfamily is like several identical pieces of graph paper embedded at different
angles. Measurements made only within each sheet are identical, but comparing their orientations
through the surrounding spacetime reveals the difference.

So `W` is not a new knob to fit. Once the complete metric, light path, and two observer clocks are
given, the metric computes it. But completed-pair reciprocity by itself does not turn every physical
comparison into the planar `W=0` case.

This closes the immediate question raised by G269. Further algebra on the intrinsic determinant
cannot select `W`; doing so would repeat the same question in different notation.
