## Whole question

Do the seven inherited `F01`–`F07` labels denote genuinely distinct mathematical solution
families, sectors of shared parent systems, conditional models, structural completion classes,
formal modules, controls, or exact empty scopes? Is the inherited seven-way partition a valid
physical or mathematical ontology, or only an operational evidence map?

This audit treats every inherited label as a hypothesis. It does not assume that a current
"family" is matter, a particle species, a realized solution set, mutually disjoint, or collectively
complete.

## Orientation and method

- **Question type:** evidence-and-metric-lineage-led, observing rather than targeting.
- **Order:** `MAP -> OBSERVE -> PONDER -> DERIVE`.
- **No solve:** no ODE/PDE, stability calculation, response completion, T4, scan, relaxation, or GPU.
- **No adoption:** no action, carrier, source, boundary, time law, bootstrap operation, or family
  partition is adopted.
- **Classification only:** statuses describe the frozen record and may be regraded if a controlling
  premise or native parent equation changes.

## Partition-validity test

The inherited seven-way map counts as a mathematical solution-family partition only if the frozen
record supplies:

1. a defined object/set for each nonempty family;
2. an owned equation/response and domain for each solution family;
3. pairwise disjointness or an exact overlap/quotient convention;
4. coverage relative to a stated parent universe;
5. consistent premise stacks and boundaries;
6. a distinction between realized solutions, formal modules, constraints, controls, and empty sets.

Failure means `OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE_MAP_NOT_SOLUTION_PARTITION`, not that UDT has no natural family
structure.

## Falsifiers and anti-steering

1. A frozen native field/equation/domain certificate can promote a label to a native realized
   family.
2. An exact shared parent equation plus injections/projections/intersections can merge or relate
   labels without assuming physical identity.
3. A conditional carrier/action family stays conditional even if it is the strongest stable
   exemplar.
4. A period/completion class is not a stability family unless it owns a response and varied domain.
5. Formal modules are not realized families without one common nonzero on-shell object.
6. Controls and exact empty scopes remain in the evidence map but cannot be counted as candidate
   matter families.
7. No classification may be chosen because it makes the stability hypothesis look stronger,
   simpler, or closer to particles.
8. Every premise-scoped negative must be regraded if its controlling premise changes.

## Fail-closed verifier requirements

Reject:

1. a missing/duplicate family, axis, or pair;
2. ontology inferred from a filename or prefix;
3. a label receiving multiple primary ontologies;
4. a structural/control/formal class promoted to a realized family;
5. a conditional model promoted to native;
6. separate sectors declared disjoint without an exact set relation;
7. overlap or identity inferred from shared notation alone;
8. a physical partition claim without both disjointness and coverage;
9. an inherited negative surviving a changed premise without regrade;
10. a source outside the 1,605-path freeze;
11. a readiness/GPU/stability promotion;
12. a family silently discarded;
13. a countermodel presented as affirmative UDT physics;
14. a conclusion stronger than the primary family and pairwise ledgers.
