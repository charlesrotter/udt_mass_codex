# G229 ponder — what would realization change?

Date: 2026-08-23

The key distinction is between a **consistent description of curvature** and an **actual metric
piece that produces it**. G228 closed the first question at one derivative order. G229 tests the
second without adding physics.

If every compatible `(R,nabla R)` has a normal-coordinate metric 3-jet, then no hidden local
obstruction remains at this order. The metric and the reciprocal/Jacobi machinery really do fit
together locally. What remains open would move outward: how those local pieces overlap, which
values are populated, and whether one global evolving history results.

If an extra obstruction appears, then differential Bianchi was not the complete local bridge and
the missing relation must be stated exactly. Either outcome is useful. No observational merit or
expected universe is used to choose between them.

The simplest-looking conclusion is not assumed. The calculation retains every quadratic and cubic
metric coefficient first, identifies the complete coordinate kernel, and only then applies normal
gauge. This prevents a normal-coordinate formula from hiding an omitted mode.
