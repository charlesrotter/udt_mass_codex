# G229 next-gate recommendation

The point-jet realization question is closed. Do not repeat it at another basis or chart.

The next step should begin with a brief PONDER that distinguishes two possible extensions:

1. one-order-higher point-jet compatibility; or
2. finite-neighborhood compatibility of a smoothly prescribed curvature field and its derivatives.

The second is closer to the remaining “history” bridge, but it must not be framed as value
generation. Its first bounded question would be whether the identities already exposed are
sufficient for local overlap of neighboring realized jets, or whether new higher differential
compatibility appears.

No solve is preregistered by this recommendation.
