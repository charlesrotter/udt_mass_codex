# F02 global-completion admissibility audit — preregistration

Date: 2026-08-01  
Base: `2149ec9`  
Mode: CPU-only exact completion and seam algebra; no GPU or new physics

## Whole question

Does any already registered complete finite-cell/global branch admit the conditional F02
P1-4D landing with `p=0`, `lambda=0`, at least one nonzero affine angular slope, and `E0!=0`?

The previous package proved only a nonperiodic local background on a supplied open/acyclic
posture. This audit tests whether that local object can survive the complete registered closure
census. It must not turn an open terminator, unregistered toric class, or supplied fold into a
physical completion.

## Frozen candidate census

Every candidate receives exactly one machine-readable row:

1. mirrored/quotient completion;
2. one-cell two-sided cyclic completion;
3. homogeneous multi-cell cyclic completion;
4. piecewise/cross-cell cyclic completion, including possible slope cancellation;
5. open/acyclic crease- or open-terminated chain;
6. banked two-cap `c=1` toric completion with `R-A` supplied;
7. banked two-cap `c=1` toric completion without `R-A`;
8. mixed-posture or cross-census/cross-pairing joins;
9. the package-introduced same-closer toric class, recorded only as `UNREGISTERED_EXCLUDED`.

The detailed source census may refine names or prove two rows identical, but may not silently omit
or merge a candidate. Generated audit records cannot create a new candidate.

## Frame and premises

- `DERIVED`: founded metric/coframe arena and reciprocal depth.
- `CONDITIONAL`: the P4 fields census, P1-4D response, F02 affine landing, jet-quadratic member,
  registered completion/seam classes, and their inherited chart/sign conventions.
- `free-and-explored`: cell count, positive cell lengths, affine slope vectors, allowed registered
  transition maps, supplied seam jumps, and every completion datum not fixed by source evidence.
- `pinned-by-THEORY`: only exact metric/coframe and registered completion identities with source
  anchors.
- `pinned-by-HABIT`: none.
- `OPEN`: physical boundary, selection of completion or `R-A`, native response/action/source,
  bootstrap return, time persistence, physical mass, and species.

The question is metric-led within a conditional inherited response class. It is not a complete-UDT
field-equation or matter theorem.

## Required algebra

For every candidate, distinguish and test:

- endpoint/cap parity constraints;
- field single-valuedness and allowed transition action;
- value and first-jet seam matching;
- whether multiple F02 cells can cancel total field periods without violating local seam laws;
- whether `p=0` makes a transition weight compatible but leaves an unfilled J07/J11 obligation;
- whether `R-A` is derived, supplied, absent, or inapplicable;
- whether the result is complete, merely acyclic/open, or outside the registered arena;
- the effect on `E0` and on the three nonzero candidate readings versus `M_WALL=0`.

The audit must independently challenge the inherited shorthand “cyclic completion forces slopes
zero.” If that statement was only one-cell or globally affine, it must be narrowed; multi-cell
slope cancellation and transition-twisted closure must be tested rather than assumed away.

## Certification and falsification

A positive complete witness requires an explicit registered global branch, exact cell fields and
transition/seam data, all background Euler rows, all endpoint/seam/cap constraints, and a nonzero
`E0` on at least one F02 cell. The conclusion remains conditional and cannot promote mass or
stability.

A no-witness result requires exhaustion of all nine rows and exact elimination of every free
multi-cell slope/transition possibility. If registered seam/transition laws are missing or do not
close the equations, the honest result is `OPEN_INCOMPLETE_REGISTERED_CLOSURE_DATA`, not a no-go.

Independent verification must not import the primary classifier. Catch-proofs must reject:

- omission or duplication of a candidate;
- use of the unregistered same-closer class as a witness;
- promotion of an open endpoint to physical completion;
- smuggling `R-A` into a no-`R-A` row;
- one-cell reasoning presented as a multi-cell theorem;
- ignoring possible slope cancellation or transition twisting;
- loss of the dissenting zero wall reading;
- promotion to native mass or global stability.

Repository premise guards, all six frozen manifests, package hashes, and the test baseline must
pass before banking.

## Maximum conclusion

At most one of:

- `CONDITIONAL_REGISTERED_COMPLETE_F02_WITNESS_EXISTS`;
- `NO_REGISTERED_COMPLETE_F02_WITNESS_IN_EXHAUSTED_CENSUS`; or
- `OPEN_INCOMPLETE_REGISTERED_CLOSURE_DATA`.

No result selects a physical completion, response law, boundary, bootstrap state, mass, or species.
