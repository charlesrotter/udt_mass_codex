## Numerical adjudication

Production used float64 automatic differentiation through the third metric jet. Its tensor
identity verifier passes every one of the 1,221 rows, with worst normalized residual
`1.46e-14`.

The independent route rebuilt curvature with fourth-order finite differences and then used a
separate outer finite-difference derivative ladder. Its worst full-tensor relative error is
`1.2738e-4`, below the frozen `5e-3` gate, and its worst ladder difference is `1.9297e-4`.

It reproduces every derivative-Gram classification. It reproduces 1,211 scalar-gradient classes
exactly and places ten rows in the preregistered rank-uncertainty band. The independent script
therefore correctly returns `FAIL` for exact all-row classification. `adjudicate_derivative_atlas.py`
does not hide that failure or change a threshold: it retains the production evidence and marks the
ten disputed rows `SPI_DEGENERATE_OR_NUMERICALLY_UNRESOLVED` as required by the frozen contract.

A cold adversarial review then identified a package-completeness defect: the initial atlas had not
recorded the promised full Gram spectra, Jordan/rank diagnostics, or alternative invariant
subspaces. Outcome-independent repair controls were committed before these saved tensors were
evaluated. NumPy and separately coded SciPy routes now map all 3,663 objects. They agree exactly on
3,650 complete diagnostic rows; the remaining 13 cross-route disagreements and every route-local
threshold-band case contribute to the 3,266 adjudicated `SPECTRALLY_UNRESOLVED` rows. The maximum
eigenvalue discrepancy is `6.54e-5`; the maximum matched candidate-plane projector defect is
`2.43e-4`, both inside their frozen gates. The repair also adds Ricci-from-Riemann and Weyl
trace-free contraction checks, which pass near `1e-15`, and replaces non-standard JSON infinity
with explicit unmatched-projector counts.
