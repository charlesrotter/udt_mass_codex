# Completeness and scope map

This is a finite local-atlas calculation, not an ODE or time-evolution solve.

## Covered

- every one of the 1,221 distinct third-order metric jets owned by the parent package;
- all seven preregistered scalar gradients;
- all three preregistered derivative-Gram tensors;
- all 3,663 full Gram spectra, rank/Jordan diagnostics, spectral blocks, and intrinsic spectral
  two-plane candidates, with 397 independently resolved and 3,266 explicitly unresolved;
- all tensor components, contractions, rank classes, alternative planes, degeneracies, and
  preregistered uncertainty bands;
- independent full-tensor replay on all 1,221 jets.

## Not covered

- the 196-fold repeated A05 provenance identities as independent evidence;
- the zero-shift A05 subcase, because no owned third jet exists;
- the complete scalar polynomial invariant algebra;
- second or higher covariant curvature derivatives;
- nonlocal holonomy, global topology, boundaries, or complete physical histories;
- a solve of any action, source, matter, bootstrap, SNe, BAO, CMB, or `X_max` equation.

## Maximum conclusion

The atlas can classify only the declared local first-curvature-derivative structures on this
bounded supplied arena. It can falsify universal ownership by those objects. It cannot prove that
no other metric-natural local or global structure exists.

Within the 1,194 parent pointwise-misaligned jets, 1,187 are robustly resolved nonowners and seven
retain a derivative-Gram ownership uncertainty. The latter cannot be counted as negative evidence.
