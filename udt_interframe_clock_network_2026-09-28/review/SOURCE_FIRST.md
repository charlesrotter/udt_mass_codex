## Reconstructed relations

Let proper-time outbound map be b=F(s), and let G(b) give A's reception time
for a distinct B-to-A future null leg emitted at b. Require F'>0 and G'>0 on
the selected regular branches. An ideal instantaneous relay gives

    R(s)=G(F(s)),     P(s)=R'(s)=F'(s) G'(F(s)) > 0.

Each derivative is G220's actual frequency/clock ratio on that leg. G is not
F inverse. For an ordinary echo define, on an ordered-return domain R(s)>s,

    T(s)=(s+R(s))/2,   D(s)=c_E (R(s)-s)/2.
    (1/c_E) dD/dT=(P-1)/(P+1),  -1 < (1/c_E)dD/dT < 1.

The subluminal radar-coordinate bound follows from P>0; it is not an additional
metric field equation, global speed theorem, or selection of a physical pair.
Future time orientation alone does not exclude global chronology pathologies:
the operational radar interpretation needs the ordinary ordered-return domain
(or a sufficiently causal local domain), not an unstated global causality theorem.

For actual linked legs satisfying F'>=1 and G'(F(s))>=1, D is nondecreasing.
The converse is only P>=1, not redshift of each individual leg. This is a
necessary observational restriction once those extra inequalities hold; the
inequalities do not follow merely from free fall. General Doppler/positional
separation is not furnished by a name.

The matched G220 clock-leg depth has Phi_out=-log F' and
Phi_back=-log G'(F(s)). Thus

    (1/c_E)dD/dT = -tanh((Phi_out+Phi_back)/2).

This is compatibility with the scalar reciprocal algebra on matched clock
legs. It does not produce W5's full projective vector, the transverse screen,
pair germ, path population, G176 full pair plane, or a native selected metric.
Each Phi is a clock readout; a general transported rapidity is not thereby
identified with it. The full vector cannot be reconstructed from two slopes.

## Independent exact controls and adversarial seams

1. Flat geodesic A at rest and B with v=3/5, gamma=5/4, initial x=L:
   F(s)=2(s+L), G(b)=L+2b, R=4s+5L. Actual ratios are 2 and 2,
   P=4, dD/dT=3/5. The inverse outward slope is 1/2, so identifying a
   causal return with inversion fails. Changing L changes D while all clock
   ratios stay the same. Both worldlines have ordinary proper time and are
   geodesic. Fixed recession already permits D to grow without P diverging.

2. The same echo has endpoint Lorentz frame maps Lambda_AB and Lambda_BA
   whose product is identity in flat geometry. Each actual null leg's
   frequency factor is 1/2 and the echo ratio product is 4. The relay reverses
   the null direction in B's frame. FSL1's cocycle for one transported ray
   would instead give factor 1 on the unchanged ray. Thus a candidate must
   retain the reset direction: a product of separate-leg clock slopes is not
   automatically the frequency factor of the composed frame morphism.
   This is a direction/protocol mismatch, not a failure of FSL1.

3. Three stationary flat clocks at A=(0,0), B=(1,0), C=(0,1): direct A-to-C
   arrival is s+1, while instantaneous relay A-to-B-to-C arrives at
   s+1+sqrt(2). Their derivatives both equal 1. Derivative consistency cannot
   identify actual event maps or direct and broken paths. General curvature
   and holonomy are not excluded by the chain rule.

4. Supplied time-dependent Lorentz4 geometry
   g=eta^2(-deta^2+dx^2+dy^2+dz^2), eta>0, with comoving clocks at separation
   ell>0: clocks are geodesic and tau=eta^2/2. Radial null legs have
   eta_1=eta+ell, eta_2=eta+2ell. Hence ratios
   Z1=(eta+ell)/eta, Z2=(eta+2ell)/(eta+ell), P=(eta+2ell)/eta.
   At eta=2, ell=1, P=2; using the wrong return emission event gives 9/4.
   Radar D=eta*ell+ell^2 grows without bound as eta grows, while P tends
   to 1. This refutes a generic inference from unbounded radar distance to
   infinite redshift on supplied geometries; it is not a claim about native
   UDT cosmology or physically admitted populations. No field equations are

5. In independent base dimensions (M,L,T), c_E has (0,1,-1), G_obs has

   pure length, time or mass target gives rank 3. In particular a monomial
