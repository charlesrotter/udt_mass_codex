## Stage gates

### Stage 1 — whole-system equation

Pass only if a source-led derivation supplies an operation `E_native: U_full -> Y` such that:

- its domain owns every field it varies;
- it is not an identity true for every admitted configuration;
- it covers the complete static/time/angular coframe rather than a chosen slice;
- its provenance is metric-native rather than imported or conditionally adopted; and
- its zero set is meaningful without an unspecified downstream action, source, pairing, or map.

### Stage 2 — boundary/completion

Pass only if the same problem supplies `B_native` with:

- field and variation domains matching `E_native`;
- seal, corners, caps/seams/quotients, and global moduli addressed or explicitly absent by theorem;
- differentiability/flux closure established; and
- no solver wall or parity value promoted into a physical boundary law.

### Stage 3 — shared nonzero-live solution

Launch only if Stages 1 and 2 both pass. Before any computation, record the complete fields, domain,
coordinates, boundary data, topology, dynamical character, free branches, and raw residual gate.
Characterize every branch in the bounded regime; do not filter for a desired shape.

### Stage 4 — certificate

Assemble only if one identical `u` has nonzero time and angular content and passes all restrictions,
`E_native[u]=0`, `B_native[u]=0`, and one compatible premise stack. A problem without a solution,
or clauses spliced from conditional branches, cannot pass.
