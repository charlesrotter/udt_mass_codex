# G244 lay report

The angular orchestra now has a clean native output.

Imagine sending a tiny circular bundle of neighboring sight lines toward a distant screen. The
metric may make that circle arrive larger, smaller, or stretched into an ellipse. The Jacobi map
already records all of this.

- Its determinant tells us the area change.
- Its shape tensor tells us the stretch and squashing.
- No adjustable “angular volume” knob is needed.

A response that is the same in every direction disappears when the sky map is normalized. A
direction-dependent response can leave an angular pattern. That is the mathematical kaleidoscope
effect in its cleanest current form.

What is still missing is not another kernel mechanism. We have not yet supplied the one complete
metric history that gives the real sky values, nor proved how physical galaxies and a detector
sample this geometric area field. So this is a native angular forward query, not yet a BAO fit or
prediction.
