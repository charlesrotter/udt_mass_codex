## What Charles added provisionally

We now consider a supplied causal phase with evenly spaced marks, without declaring that it is
light. The phase, marks, and their population are supplied; the metric does not create them.

Charles provisionally defined one particular readout: how many of those crossings pass per unit of
an observer's own clock time and per unit of metric sheet area.

## What follows

For the bounded calculation we use the smooth rate at which the phase itself changes, measured by
an observer's clock. We also choose the same conserved label amount on every phase slice, without
extra phase-dependent weighting. G351 tells us how that amount spreads over area. Combining these
explicit ingredients gives

```text
clock-rate density transfer = frequency change / area change.
```

In G350's notation, this readout has `p=1` and `q=-1`.

This is not a fitted choice. The one frequency factor appears because the adopted quantity is
explicitly realized as a continuous rate per observer clock time. Rescaling the arbitrary phase
numbering changes the measured phase rate and the spacing between marks together, so it cancels.
Changing observers changes the rate exactly once, as expected for a clock rate.

The external audit caught an important distinction. If we literally count separate marks, the count
jumps one at a time: between marks its instantaneous count rate is zero, and at a mark it is a spike.
That atomic count is not the smooth phase-rate used here. The smooth branch is a chosen mathematical
realization of Charles's provisional readout; averaging or identifying it with literal events would
need another premise.

## What it does not mean

We have not discovered one universal value of `p`. `p` labels the kind of readout:

- the conserved amount per area remains the separate `p=0` density;
- the chosen continuous phase rate per clock time has `p=1`;
- anything assigning additional value to each crossing would require another premise and could
  have a different observer weight.

The result does not identify the crossings as light, assign them energy, emit them, populate their
paths, or say what a detector does. It is therefore not yet brightness, luminosity, distance, or a
supernova prediction.

At a focus, the amount per area and the continuous rate density may become infinite or cease to be an
ordinary density. The finite weighted measure can still be carried on the permanent labels when
the observer frequency is integrable. That retains the same careful caustic boundary as G351.

The advance is precise: Charles's clock-first premise supports a coherent conditional `p=1`
continuous rate readout once the continuous phase measure and phase-independent label product are
stated openly. The external repair-only reviewer accepted that bounded conclusion. It still does
not import an energy or light-transfer formula.
