# G307 lay report

The round metric by itself allowed two whole families of equally valid twisting direction fields.
G307 asked what happens once we stop looking at an empty symmetric universe and supply one actual
directed observer relation.

The answer is sharply simpler:

- a location plus a direction reduces the many possibilities to two mirror versions;
- both mirror versions follow exactly the same route, so watching only the route cannot tell them
  apart;
- they twist the surrounding two-dimensional screen in opposite directions;
- one signed measurement of that sideways twist identifies one version.

So the local geometric ambiguity has shrunk from two continuous families to one binary handedness
choice, and then to one member when the transverse handedness is supplied.

The remaining gap is not another formula inside the kernel. Current UDT premises do not yet say
which observer routes and transverse handedness Nature actually populates. G307 tells us how to
reconstruct the unique field from such a completed relation; it does not claim that the physical
relation has already been selected.

No metric term, reciprocal-kernel term, fitted coefficient, mass rule, or scale was added.
