# G307 bounded completeness map

## Covered exactly

- the positive-radius standard connected simply connected round `S3` completion supplied by G305;
- both orientation-relative G306 Hopf-congruence families;
- every regular point and ordered unit tangent pair `(p,v)` by the quaternionic isometry theorem;
- every regular oriented rank-two screen complementary to `span{p,v}`;
- the full great-circle route generated by `(p,v)`;
- every positive slice radius, because radius enters only as the nonzero factor `1/a` in the
  transverse first jet;
- member counts after point, tangent, route, and signed transverse-screen data.

The 36 exact rational frames and five explicit positive radii are executable witnesses of the
general identities, not a finite sampling claim. The independent numerical replay uses 1,000
unrelated random oriented frames as a coding and sign check.

## Not covered

- nonspherical deformations, quotients, singular/cut/caustic strata, or topology change;
- ownership or population of physical observers, events, routes, queries, or screens;
- an action, dynamics, stability, backreaction, source, matter or mass law;
- observational calibration, a dimensionful scale, history selection, or physical `X_max`;
- protected local packages.

The theorem reconstructs a family member from supplied germ data. It does not derive that Nature
supplies or populates those data.
