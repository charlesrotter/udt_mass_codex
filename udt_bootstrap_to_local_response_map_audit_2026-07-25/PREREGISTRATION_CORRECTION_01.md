bootstrap may be a coupled, vector-valued global closure involving native

  type, not merely an optional mathematical enlargement;

  other metric-derived components without treating any one as selected;

  `B_A[X]` and `alpha=D B[X]^* lambda`, including cross-responses among
  observables;

  angular response even when the density-volume projection cannot; and
- no parameter's exact functional, local/global averaging, target value,
  multiplier, weight, or coupling is supplied by naming it.

The original preregistration remains historical evidence. This correction is
additions-only and does not change the nine candidate map identities or the
maximum allowed conclusion.
