# Preregistration correction 03: “optimize” means mutual tuning

Recorded after correction 02 and before final adjudication.

The owner uses *optimize* in the physical sense of tuning an instrument: the
matter, energy, density, curvature, and macro geometry adjust together until
the complete configuration is mutually compatible. This is the working
`TUNING_OR_CLOSURE` hypothesis represented by `GL01-GL03`.

It must not be misread as requiring a scalar objective, minimum, or maximum.
That narrower mathematical construction is renamed
`SCALAR_EXTREMIZATION_REALIZATION` and remains `OPEN_STRONGER_PREMISE` under
`GL04`.

This clarification changes terminology, not candidate gates. Mutual tuning is
compatible with a vector closure `B^A(O,X)=0`; scalar extremization would be
only one possible way to generate such a closure.
