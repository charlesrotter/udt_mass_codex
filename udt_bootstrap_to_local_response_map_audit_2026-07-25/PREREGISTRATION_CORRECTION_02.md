Recorded after the original preregistration and parameter-channel correction,
but before the final adjudication and package freeze.

## Owner hypothesis

- the amount and organization of matter and energy determine total density,
  curvature, and the macro geometry;
- that global geometry and curvature determine the parameters or admissible
  regime in which matter can exist;
- a realized universe is a mutually consistent solution of both directions.

This hypothesis is `WORKING`, not `DERIVED`.

premise-neutral mathematical type is a coupled self-consistency or fixed-point
closure. Optimization is one possible stronger realization and must remain
