## Three tempting but invalid shortcuts

**Counting three conditions as three frequency probes.** F4's comparisons do not establish
the fixed-frequency indexing, shared nuisance class or unused prediction specified above.
Matching a detector control does not by itself establish cross-frequency phase calibration.
This is an object/condition mismatch in the proposed reuse, not criticism of the source's
different experimental question.

**Reading phase from a displayed magnitude without its reference.** A generic unreferenced
complex amplitude A and exp(i alpha)A have the same |A|. Therefore a magnitude alone is not a
unique signed-phase record. Extra reference/branch/transfer information can restore phase
access; a phase-capable instrument is not ruled out by this elementary ambiguity. What is
missing here is the documented reconstruction and uncertainty for the actual OB1 quantities.
The abstract ambiguity is a standard information-map check, not a simulation of this detector
or an assertion that every gyroscope readout is a simple cosine.

**Using the modulation label or a sensitivity figure as the required calibration.** Electrical
drive frequency, optical-frequency excursion and optical carrier frequency have distinct roles.
Even when units share 'Hz', substituting one for another needs an actual transfer and registration
argument. Likewise, a response/noise statement in another output variable does not directly bound
the wrapped phase residual. It would need the documented transfer, statistical/finite-error
meaning, domain, nuisance support and calibration provenance. No such conversion is invented.

Broadened or driven light can still produce interference; we do not infer that loss of a
monochromatic description destroys every usable phase measurement. It requires a justified
replacement or reduction to the declared contract. This assessment does not authorize that
new transfer-model derivation or silently expand OB1 to it. The proposed fully integrated
architecture is also not substituted for the actually documented loop (F6 versus F1).
