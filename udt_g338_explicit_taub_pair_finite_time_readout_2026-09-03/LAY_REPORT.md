# G338 lay report

We took one complete, already-lawful evolving spacetime from the recent work and followed a fully
specified clock-and-ruler pair through it. We did not add a new field equation, choose a scale, fit
data, or alter the reciprocal kernel.

The result is positive and concrete: the kernel responds coherently through finite time. Its raw
clock, ruler, and mixing pieces remain mutually consistent, and the completed-pair normalization
does not erase the evolution.

The most useful finding is that “quiet now” need not mean “quiet forever.” There is one direction
that has exactly zero first-order change at the chosen instant, yet the exact solution shows that it
becomes nonzero on both sides of that instant. So the higher-order behavior uncovered in G337 can
correspond to genuine finite-time evolution, at least in this explicit lawful example.

There is also an important warning. If the pair has no initial boost, its final one-number
reciprocal readout stays zero even while its ruler-density changes. The full pair kernel sees the
change; that one terminal number does not. This confirms why UDT must retain the complete pair
metric rather than treating `phi` alone as the entire physical state.

What remains open is unchanged: this spacetime was supplied as one lawful member of the adopted
equation's solution space. G338 does not show that Nature selects it, does not select a physical
observer population, and does not set an absolute scale or `X_max`.
