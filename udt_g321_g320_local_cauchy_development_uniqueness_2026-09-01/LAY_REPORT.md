G320 found many genuinely different legal starting geometries. G321 asked whether the current UDT
equation knows what to do next once any one complete starting geometry is supplied.

Inside the present bounded vacuum framework, the answer is conditionally **yes**.

Each starting geometry contains both its spatial shape and its initial rate of change. Those data
satisfy all the constraint equations and fix the remaining regional curvature constant to zero.
The active equation then gives one immediate response. The standard mathematical theorem for this
kind of causal metric equation says that the response continues into one local spacetime, apart
from harmless changes of coordinates.

The two sign choices are not two futures produced from the same starting point. They reverse the
initial rate and therefore describe time-reversed starting data. Likewise, the `1,4,9,16` curvature
pattern means the four spatial-wave examples remain different when their initial surfaces are
kept marked.

This is the normal structure of a dynamical physical theory: a law plus complete initial data gives
one local evolution. It does not say which initial data Nature chose, whether the solutions remain
regular forever, or whether apparently different slices could belong to one larger global
spacetime. The local uniqueness conclusion also remains conditional on a standard imported
mathematical well-posedness theorem. No metric or reciprocal-kernel formula changed.

The first outside review agreed with that scientific result. It did catch that some of our test
machinery was claiming more rigor than it delivered. We repaired the machinery: it now damages
copies of the real package and confirms the verifier rejects each damaged copy, while clearly
separating what the scripts check from what the imported mathematical theorem supplies.
