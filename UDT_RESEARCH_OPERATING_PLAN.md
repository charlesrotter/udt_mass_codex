## Recommendation

Organize research around a persistent goal: find a physically defensible
connection through which UDT's positional clock-dilation interpretation restricts
the shared spacetime geometry or its physical clock comparisons, retaining the
source-qualified SR/GR recovery requirements. A useful partial restriction can
advance this goal; one unique geometry or a complete field equation is not the
entry requirement.

Give two researchers responsibility for developing lines of inquiry over multiple
ideas, with a coordinator able to redirect effort within one bounded mandate.
They may form conjectures, learn from literature, work out consequences, find
counterexamples, revise or discard a proposal, and move to another informative
idea. Each individual calculation serves the goal. Review both the strongest
result and the reason to continue or stop. Keep one scientific account in
[UDT_DEVELOPMENT.md](UDT_DEVELOPMENT.md).

The objective is better research decisions, not a promise that more autonomy or
agents will produce a breakthrough. A stalled program can reflect a difficult
scientific problem even when its methods are sound. We have not established that
UDT's clarified commitments are insufficient, or that another postulate is needed.

For now, use **this Codex CLI workspace for execution and the first pilot**.
Consider ChatGPT desktop as a later interface for supervising the same repository,
after a small compatibility trial. The interface decision is explained below.

## Discovery permission and scientific fidelity

Charles's clarification permits ideas and techniques from primary GR literature,
other physics and pure mathematics. Search and inspect the original argument
before using a named result; check its mathematical hypotheses. A useful analogy
can motivate a conjecture without proving it. Broad literature mining is a
discovery method, not automatic adoption of the source theory.

Keep three distinctions explicit in the campaign notebook:

- A borrowed mathematical method, whose hypotheses must hold for its application.
- A proposed physical connection or equation, labeled unadopted and used only in
  the declared conditional candidate chain.
- A consequence actually derived from admitted UDT commitments, at their source
  grades and within the proved domain.

A researcher may flesh out the second kind before knowing whether it can become
the third. Before using a conjecture, name its assumption and provenance, explain
what UDT uncertainty it might address, and choose a first test that could change
the decision. The campaign scope may authorize this generation and testing of
conjectures; it need not enumerate every equation before discovery begins.
Changing the founding interpretation, silently replacing the metric/kernel,
adding physical objects outside that scope, promoting a fit, or accepting a new
premise does not follow from permission to explore.

An idea need not arrive with a theorem or assured success. A short exploratory
development can reveal its useful test. After that initial development, continued
effort needs a plausible learning target. Missing proof is work to attempt;
repeatedly restating a missing join without a new attempt is not a research plan.
Preserve a falsified conjecture and its surviving lessons rather than repairing
it by concealing new assumptions. A finite unsuccessful search proves only its
bounded result. There may or may not be a viable connection; agents must be free
to find adverse evidence.

## Continuity and campaign boundaries

Keep researchers on the goal across failed ideas. After a decisive failure, each
identifies what failed, what remains usable, and its next promising in-scope
approach. The coordinator may request a revision, exchange perspectives, reassign
effort or retire a line. An empty proposal queue should prompt an explicit search
for alternatives within the remaining budget, not automatic certification of
the first two dead ends. That search need not manufacture a replacement when no
informative direction emerges.

Return to Charles at a meaningful scientific decision, a genuine scope or resource
boundary, or the agreed campaign checkpoint. Routine switches of mathematical
method or in-scope conjecture do not need another authorization. Do not relaunch
an exhausted route under a new label. A global chronology question receives no
automatic priority merely because GCP1 exposed it; its proposed connection to the
goal must be assessed alongside alternatives.

Each campaign still names its scope, current premise snapshot, workspace,
computational limits, output/checkpoint budget, stops, review and repair allowance.
The budget bounds the campaign, not the number of ideas it is required to produce.
Production computation keeps its existing smoke-test and dispatch gates. This
method update launches no unattended service or unspecified long computation.
It authorizes the research style and agenda development; execution remains tied
to a concrete written campaign with resources stated before computation.

Use one shared notebook to retain candidate status, decisive observations,
assumption changes and next actions. Preserve source-specific evidence where
needed, but do not close and publish a whole package after every idea. Draft
checkpointing is allowed before final review. At a substantive return, independently
challenge the strongest argument and the continuation decision, repair within
scope and integrate once. Researcher continuity is continuity of the inquiry and
record; it does not require an AI process to remain alive indefinitely.

Judge the campaign by what uncertainty changed: a justified restriction,
discriminating prediction, viable conditional connection, or a concrete rejected
physical hypothesis. Record unresolved candidates honestly. Documents, agent
count, apparent personality and agreement are not evidence of scientific progress.

## What changes from the existing practice

TC0 already used independent candidate formation and a novelty stop. PCW1 already
used three fresh proposers, peer challenge and separate reviewers. CPA1 already
distinguished conditional mathematical gains from native-selection progress.
These are precedents to reuse, not new features of this proposal.
The [retrospective](research_operating_design_2026-10-12/RETROSPECTIVE.md) identifies
three concrete decision points and the limits of the comparison.

The proposed change is how those practices govern a connected investigation:

| Existing capability | Proposed operational use |
|---|---|
| Bounded work orders and autonomy | Authorize a question family and several connected probes, including revision and review, rather than habitually returning after each small calculation. |
| Multi-agent whiteboards | Have agents formulate questions from the objective before seeing the coordinator's favored approach; preserve the strongest alternative. |
| Premise and novelty checks | Use them to decide what to work on and whether to extend it, not only to qualify the result afterward. |
| Independent result review | Also challenge whether the recommended continuation addresses the named uncertainty. |
| Central documentation | Maintain a short decision history and reopening conditions, so another session does not relaunch the same route because it forgot why it stopped. |

This is a proposed improvement in use and continuity, not a claim that these
permissions were absent. Repeating the same warning at the end of another paper
would not implement it.

## One research cycle

**1. Set the question and authority once.** Charles approves a short charter with
objective, question family, premise/source versions, methods, workspace, resource
budget, exclusions, stop conditions and maximum conclusion. It includes
construction, relevant checks, fresh adversarial review and bounded repair.
An objective can concern geometry selection, a physical interface, a diagnostic,
or a reusable computational method; its actual purpose must be visible.

Explorers may change approaches, revise conjectures, construct counterexamples,
and pursue unexpected connections within that scope. They do not need approval
for each algebraic step. They may develop and test new physical hypotheses inside
the conjecture family or goal scope authorized by the charter, naming and labeling
each before it becomes an assumption in an exploratory chain. A hypothesis outside
that scope requires an extension. Ordinary mathematical methods
remain available with their hypotheses checked. Deriving a new equation as a
consequence is allowed; introducing an unadopted governing physical equation
requires the campaign's explicit exploratory authority and candidate labeling.
No automatic premise adoption, scientific
promotion, or spending beyond the mandate follows.

**2. Form questions independently.** Give two fresh explorer contexts the same
objective, source packet and budget, without the coordinator's preferred solution
or each other's proposals. Each writes a short initial proposal containing:

- The uncertainty and why resolving it matters to the objective.
- The closest previous attempt and a specific difference, or a reason to replicate it.
- One inexpensive first probe and what favorable, adverse, or ambiguous outcomes would teach.
- The premise boundary and the strongest reason the route may be unproductive.

Different sources or methods matter more than names such as “relativist” and
“quantum expert.” These are AI analytical roles, not human credentials or simulated
endorsements from named physicists. Initial proposals stay recoverable when the
agents subsequently exchange ideas. A useful speculative idea need not arrive
with a complete proof or a forecast of success.

**3. Compare and allocate.** The coordinator explains which uncertainty each
proposal could reduce, which assumptions it consumes, its likely effort, and why
one first probe deserves priority. Keep the strongest alternative and objections.
Do not decide physics by vote or numerical novelty scores. Question selection is
a judgment under uncertainty, open to correction by the first results.

**4. Explore, then reconsider at meaningful milestones.** Run the initial probes
within the approved budget. Agents can continue a developing argument, change
permitted method, or switch to a promising alternative in the question family.
They should return at an actual decision point, not after every calculation.
Use four short sentences: what changed; what remains unresolved; what the next
probe could distinguish; whether it remains inside the mandate.

Every proposed extension should make its intended decision visible. Increasing
resolution, sample size or mathematical generality can be valuable when it settles
a named uncertainty. It does not acquire a new physical justification merely
because the calculation is larger. Conversely, a conditional tool may deserve
further investment for a concrete use even though it does not select geometry.

If an outcome looks physically applicable, check its implied observable regime
early: actual clock records, preparation, scales, calibration and uncertainties
as relevant. Identify missing interfaces without inventing them. This screen is
proportional to the intended claim; a pure conditional theorem need not wait for
an instrument. Numerical and empirical discovery remain legitimate approaches.

**5. Review a frozen candidate and its continuation argument.** Use fresh contexts
separate from the explorers for substantive candidate review. Distinguish context,
model, argument, implementation and exposure. Same-model agreement is limited
evidence; it is not cross-model independence. Review should try to break the
strongest inference, test the load-bearing hypotheses, compare the nearest prior
result, and challenge the proposed use. A source change requires the existing
positive and negative descendant review. Preserve initial claims and repairs.

The coordinator cannot discard an objection by majority vote. Resolve it within
the authorized repair budget or return the strongest surviving result and exact
unresolved issue. Human specialist review is valuable for major claims when
available; this plan does not pretend that agent roles supply it.

If no candidate emerges, review the reason to stop and its load-bearing sources;
do not manufacture a candidate-certification stage to fill the staffing plan.

**6. Return and integrate once.** Explain the result in lay terms, its contribution
to the objective, surviving conditions, failed approaches, unresolved objections,
resource use and the reason to continue, redirect, or stop. Integrate reviewed
content at its actual epistemic status into the existing central chapter with
its required reviews;
save an unresolved candidate at its honest status. Commit and synchronize under
[BRANCH_WORKFLOW.md](BRANCH_WORKFLOW.md). No successor starts merely because a
cycle ended.

## What counts as progress, and when to stop

Record the actual change, rather than assign a score:

| Type of gain | Evidence needed for the claim |
|---|---|
| Restriction from an existing UDT commitment | The implication, its hypotheses, and what previously admitted freedom it removes. A partial restriction can be valuable. |
| Physical identification or applicability | A justified link between a geometric quantity and a specified observation/preparation, with remaining conditions visible. |
| Conditional mathematical or computational tool | A checked result and a concrete intended use; the supplied geometry or law remains supplied. |
| Informative negative or clarification | A scoped counterexample, failed identification, assumption sensitivity, or resolved ambiguity that changes a decision. |

Report categories separately. Passing checks, producing documents and agreement
among agents are not substitutes for any of them. Initial data and measurement
calibration can remain free in a successful theory; the objective is not to force
one unique universe or eliminate every constant.

Redirect or stop a route when its next proposed step has no articulated new
inference, uncertainty, domain question or concrete reuse, or when an unresolved
premise boundary blocks the proposed inference. A source search that finds no
argument proves only that limited search unsuccessful. An unfamiliar result is
not a reason to reject valid admitted mathematics.

Do not use rigid novelty quotas or insist on a closed-form law before discovery.
Preserve an unexpected lead if it has a plausible informative probe inside the
mandate. At budget exhaustion, save an honest return, including incomplete work.
For each parked route, state what could reopen it: a changed source, new argument,
stronger method, justified application, or an explicitly authorized hypothesis.

## Team, continuity and minimal records

Use one coordinator, two exploratory contexts, then two fresh review contexts
sequentially as needed. At most four agents, including the parent, run concurrently.
Do not start a permanent swarm or a custom orchestration service for this trial.
The coordinator owns integration; agents write only assigned work areas and never
edit the same central files concurrently. Protected work stays excluded.

Use the repository as durable memory. Conversation memory and role prompts are
working aids, not authority. Each resuming agent receives the approved question,
commit/source versions, what has been tried, exact unsettled issue, remaining
budget and next permitted action. A changed input flags the relevant assumptions
and positive and negative dependencies for review; their recorded status is
preserved until that review determines the consequence. Applicability under
changed inputs stays unverified until checked. A stale summary cannot overrule
disk evidence.

The minimal cycle record is one work order and one working notebook with initial
proposals, decisive observations, failed routes, decision changes and dissent.
Keep computation/review artifacts when evidence requires them. Use existing
central development and operational adapters at return. Do not create a parallel
scientific summary, per-agent knowledge base or a new database of all historical
results. Existing mandatory evidence requirements still apply; reducing them is
a separate reviewed workflow change, not an implicit permission in this plan.

Unattended execution, if later useful, must operate only within a written scope,
persist checkpoints and respect resource/stop controls. A chat window, desktop
project or scheduler does not itself authorize continual research. No background
service or automatic successor is installed by ROD1.

## Original first-pilot design — executed subsequently as GCP1

Try one connected cycle before adopting this as routine practice. Its objective
would be to identify and test one specific, previously unextracted consequence
of an existing UDT commitment for allowed geometry or physical comparison. The
precise question is chosen from the independent proposals, not prescribed as
another variation of the current conditional astronomical example.

The initial charter should admit source/argument exploration and short checks,
with two initial proposals, at most three substantive probes across their shared
question family, two fresh reviewers, and at most two bounded repair rounds.
Use at most four concurrent agents, no GPU or multi-hour solve. Before launch,
the charter must name the exact source set, permissible hypotheses, and measured
CPU/memory settings, output allowance, checkpoint/restart and interruption controls
appropriate to its actual workload; do not inherit stale numerical limits.
These are workload limits, not a countdown
that kills a valid running computation; manual-stop authority remains.

If neither proposal yields a defensible probe, return the concrete obstacle and
the best alternative research question. Do not fill the budget with another
compatible metric or promote a failed search into a theorem of insufficiency.
Explorers may identify a worthwhile conditional tool, but a change in the cycle's
main objective must be presented as a decision rather than disguised as closure.

Evaluate the pilot against its recorded starting uncertainty: did it change an
inference, expose an assumption, support a justified use, or justify a particular
stop? Also record total effort, time spent on discovery versus administration,
unplanned scope changes, repeated requests for already-authorized steps, and
whether another context can resume from the compact record. Do not claim a causal
productivity improvement from one trial. If it merely produces more paperwork
and the same unexamined next-step recommendation, revise the process.

ROD1 supplies this proposal and an exposed retrospective. It does not execute the
pilot. The next discussion should settle its charter and whether its scope is the
best use of the retained tools.

## Desktop or this terminal

**Decision: continue here for the first pilot.** This environment already operates
on the repository with shell commands, version control, separate agent contexts
and captured verification. The documented CLI supports local work and repeatable
command execution. Keeping the same model does not guarantee identical results
across interfaces: instructions, tools, context and permissions also matter.
We have not measured a scientific reasoning advantage for either interface.
[Official CLI documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/codex/cli).

ChatGPT desktop is a real alternative for overseeing parallel chats and reviewing
files; it includes Codex and can open a local folder. It need not replace Git or
turn this into a document-upload workflow. That could make Charles's supervision
more comfortable, but it is a usability judgment to test, not improved physics
inference. [Desktop overview](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/app).

The Linux app is currently a preview. Its listed Ubuntu support is 24.04 LTS and
26.04 LTS; this workspace reports Ubuntu **22.04.5 LTS, x86_64**. That is outside
the listed support, not proof it cannot run. Linux Computer Use is not yet
supported, and native Wayland is experimental. Those features are unnecessary
for our proposed repo workflow. Do not upgrade this research machine merely to
change the chat interface. [Linux requirements](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/linux/linux-app).

A later supported desktop trial should open this repository as the primary local
folder in **Codex**, verify actual model/effort and project instructions, and
reproduce a read-only orientation and a small already accepted check. Then test
two bounded read-only agent tasks and review the returned evidence. Verify the
same HEAD, tools and files and that protected work is untouched. Keep one writer
while both interfaces are open. No reliance on automatically shared history:
official docs distinguish Work and Codex histories and explain that synced chats
do not upload the local folder. [Project behavior](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/projects).

Prefer Local for the trial. Automatic worktree handoff can move Git state and
does not carry all local files; this repo has protected and local-only artifacts.
Do not let a convenience handoff switch the active scientific checkout or copy
those payloads. Worktrees can be introduced deliberately when needed for isolated
editing. [Worktree behavior](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/environments/git-worktrees).

If that trial preserves reproducibility and makes multi-agent oversight easier,
use desktop as the main interface and retain CLI for exact commands and recovery.
Until then, the terminal supports the proposed method. This comparison is based
on official pages accessed 2026-10-12 and read-only environment inspection; no
desktop installation, account-access test or cross-interface benchmark was run.
