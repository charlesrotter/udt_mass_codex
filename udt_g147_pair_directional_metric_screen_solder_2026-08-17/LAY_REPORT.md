# G147 lay report

## What we found

One conditional local connector simplifies completely.

For one fully specified observer-pair comparison, the metric gives:

- the observer's clock direction;
- the ruler pointing toward the paired observer;
- the two sideways directions perpendicular to both.

Those two sideways directions are the metric's pair screen.

Now picture the paired observer as a point inside the bounded ball of possible positions. The two
directions in which that point can move while keeping the same radial separation are also exactly
the directions perpendicular to the clock and ruler. They are the same two-dimensional plane.

If we represent normalized pair position inside that observer's own rest-space ball, there is no
missing adapter and no coefficient to tune: we have given the same plane two names.

That “if” matters. This does not yet prove that the rest-space ball is UDT's physical
multidirectional position carrier or a spacetime displacement. An independently defined position
ball would still need its orientation soldered into the metric screen.

## What remains

The physical position carrier remains open. Even if the rest-space representation is later owned,
an observer turning from comparison with B to comparison with C has two screen planes at different
directions, and G147 does not carry one to the other.

The result also does not select either trial multidirectional composition rule, determine
`X_max`, choose a universe, or change local physics.
