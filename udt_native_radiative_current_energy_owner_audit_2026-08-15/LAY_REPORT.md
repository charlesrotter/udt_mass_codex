# Lay report — what the metric gives before Maxwell

The metric has supplied more than “nothing,” but less than a complete electromagnetic theory.

Think of the complete coframe as providing a network of grooves and rotating gears. Once we specify
which angular gear is being followed, the metric tells us its curvature and automatically produces
a Maxwell-shaped accounting system. The accounting always balances: geometric response cannot
appear or disappear inconsistently.

But balanced accounting is not the same as knowing what is in the boxes. The geometry has not yet
said that this particular response is light, that it carries the supernova's energy, or that none of
it can be absorbed or exchanged.

The metric also provides a natural conveyor belt: null covectors follow its canonical geodesic
flow, and the conveyor preserves phase-space volume. But the metric does not place physical cargo on
that belt. A distribution of cargo and the rule that it travels without collisions are still extra.

One useful simplification emerged. If a physical carried unit is identified with the same null
covector already used by the observer query, its energy ratio is automatically `1/Z`. The absolute
conversion constant cancels, so Planck's constant is unnecessary for this ratio.

The remaining step is therefore smaller than “derive all of Maxwell”:

1. identify the physical radiative unit with the query's null covector; and
2. derive or provisionally state that its populated measure is conserved on the transparent regular
   branch.

Those two statements would close the familiar `d_L=Z^2 d_A` propagation law. This audit has not
adopted them. It has located exactly where they would enter and shown that the existing geometric
identities do not secretly provide them.

A fresh outside reviewer reproduced the load-bearing counterexamples and agreed with that boundary.
It also clarified that the package verifier checks whether the evidence record is internally
consistent; the independent mathematical support comes from the separate exact-arithmetic replay
and the reviewer's fresh reconstruction.
