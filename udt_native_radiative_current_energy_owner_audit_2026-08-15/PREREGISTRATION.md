## Whole question

observer/null-screen query, and only the already conditional toric/screen geometry where it exists,
determine the strongest current, carried-measure, and endpoint-energy law that follows from the
metric and its natural differential geometry.

The load-bearing targets are the two open G94 factors

```text
eta     = surviving carried amount / emitted carried amount,
epsilon = observer energy per carried unit / source energy per carried unit.
```

The audit asks separately whether the current authority derives `eta=1`, `epsilon=1/Z`, both, or
neither.

## Falsification and certification contract

A claimed derivation of `eta=1` must exhibit a metric-owned transported measure/current whose flux
through matched beam sections is exactly the G94 carried amount, not merely define a closed form.

A claimed derivation of `epsilon=1/Z` must exhibit a metric-owned physical energy functional on the
same carried unit and prove its endpoint ratio, not merely rename frequency as energy.

Every identity must be catch-tested against an arbitrary smooth field that violates the proposed
inhomogeneous/source-free equation. Every current claim must survive independent exterior-calculus
or coordinate verification. A nonzero field selected by an action, boundary condition, or carrier
premise cannot be called metric-only.
