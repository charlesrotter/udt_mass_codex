# G69 observational-anchor policy

Observational anchors are permitted in a later separately preregistered phase; none is used in
G69.

Before any fit:

1. declare the exact metric/profile family and physical query;
2. declare each coefficient and whether it belongs to profile, endpoint, source/state, or readout;
3. require the coefficient count not to exceed the identified independent readout rank;
4. state which observable primarily anchors each coefficient;
5. retain at least one independent observable, channel, or regime as a held-out check;
6. do not add coefficients after inspecting residuals;
7. preserve source freedom explicitly rather than absorbing it into geometric coefficients;
8. keep the conditional P1 SNe anchor in its low-redshift observer-pair role unless a derived map
   connects it to the CMB query.

Peak positions, heights, scalar temperature, and polarization are not interchangeable data points.
They read different parts of the geometric and source/state machinery. A successful fit is an
`OBSERVED` compatibility result until its premises are independently justified.
