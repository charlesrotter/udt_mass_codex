# G198 in lay language

We sent two lightlike test directions through the exact same metric.

One direction hears the full screen orchestra: stretching, shear, rotation, and their variation.
The opposite direction hears only the common background scale. That quietness was not added by a
switch or fitted coefficient. It comes from the fact that this particular metric family contains
one null-direction screen coupling and not the other.

This is good evidence that the machinery is beginning to interlock naturally. It is also bounded:
we have learned what this metric family plays, not yet proved that every complete UDT metric must
contain only that one directional coupling.

The next clean question is whether the primary UDT metric itself forbids the missing opposite
coupling, or whether G196 froze a legitimate degree of freedom for convenience.
