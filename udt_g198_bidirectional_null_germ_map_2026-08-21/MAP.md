## Why this is not automatically a mirror test

\[

\[

opposite, or reciprocal screen responses. The direct Levi-Civita reconstruction must decide

belongs to another exact class.

## The minimally less-frozen adjacent frame

Only after the same-metric opposite-germ result is understood should the next family be considered:

\[
\theta_{\rm screen}=a\left[dX+C_\eta X\,d\eta+C_zX\,dz\right]
=a\left[dX+M_+X(d\eta+dz)+M_-X(d\eta-dz)\right],
\]

where

\[
M_+=\frac{C_\eta+C_z}{2},\qquad
M_-=\frac{C_\eta-C_z}{2}.
\]

G196 chose `C_eta=C_z=M`, hence `M_-=0`. Allowing independent `C_eta,C_z` would unfreeze a
configuration-space degree of freedom; it would not add a force or physical mechanism. That frame
is recorded now to prevent a negative opposite-germ control from being misread as a general UDT
law, but it is not authorized for derivation in G198.

## Reconstruction ceiling visible before computation

Even two crossing germs cannot determine an arbitrary two-variable field away from their union.
For example, a perturbation proportional to

\[

\]

and its first directional derivatives vanish on both central rays while remaining nonzero
elsewhere. Thus G198 can classify complementary restrictions and local compatibility, not recover
the full off-ray metric history.
