# G198 lay map

G196 listened in one direction through the metric. The next clean test is to turn around and listen
in the opposite direction **without changing the metric**.

There is an important catch. The particular G196 family was written with one directional channel
already active. So the opposite ray may be naturally quiet—not because UDT says one direction is
physically silent, but because this mathematical family contains only one of the two possible
directional screen couplings.

That is exactly what we should measure first. If the opposite ray is quiet, we have learned the
boundary of G196 rather than discovered a universal law. The adjacent, less-frozen family would
turn on both directional couplings and let the two rays read the two halves of the same local
screen connection.

Even then, two rays are not the whole room. They can reveal more of the shared geometry at their
intersection and along their paths, but many different surrounding fields can agree on both rays.
So this step tests whether the pieces interlock; it does not claim to reconstruct the universe.
