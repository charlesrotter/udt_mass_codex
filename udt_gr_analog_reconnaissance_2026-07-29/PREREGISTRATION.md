## 0. Interrogation declaration

"which GR results should UDT adopt" (none; that is the named smuggle) and not "which analogs
support the expected physics" (merit-blind survey).

## 3. Falsifiers (frozen)

- **F-G1 (answer import).** Any row or prose recommending adoption of a GR result's ANSWER
  (an equation form, boundary term, charge formula, uniqueness conclusion) as UDT's. Fires →
  that row is RED-stamped and the recommendation VOID; method+failure-surface only.
- **F-G2 (correspondence overclaim).** Any claim that a P4 problem "is" the corpus problem
  rather than shape-analogous; every row must carry its DISANALOGY column filled with the
  UDT-specific difference. Fires → row void until the disanalogy is stated.
- **F-G3 (fabricated/misattributed scholarship).** Any load-bearing attribution that is wrong
  or unverifiable. Every attribution carries VERIFIED or MODEL-KNOWLEDGE status; a
  MODEL-KNOWLEDGE row may not enter TG3's shortlist rationale without a flag.
- **F-G4 (merit filter).** Any prioritization or selection by whether an analog would deliver
  the expected/desired physics. Prioritization is by gate-relevance and transformability only.
