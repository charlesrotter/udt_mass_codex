# Next justified question — proposed, not launched

This audit did not open the density-bracketing gate. It did identify the precise joint a future
metric-native relation must fill:

```text
local exact reciprocal depth dphi
        -> observer-natural, locally varying response with zero harmonic mean
        -> global harmonic/metric channel alpha, theta1, I, and angular area D.
```

The next bounded metric-led audit should therefore test the robustness and possible ownership of
that exact-to-harmonic response in a broader coframe class:

1. release the lower-triangular restriction far enough to include upper-right pair/screen mixing;
2. retain non-torus-invariant base/screen dependence where compact Hodge decomposition remains
   exact or CPU-tractable;
3. enumerate every metric-derived one-form or scalar coefficient produced by the complete coframe,
   connection, and curvature—without choosing one because it resembles the desired bridge;
4. decompose each into exact, coexact, and harmonic parts and test observer naturality, descent,
   and the required zero-period condition;
5. keep the no-response outcome and stop if the expanded metric supplies none.

Only if a nonidentity same-solution relation survives should `rho_tot` be introduced as an explicit
working control. At that later stage, `sqrt(G_obs rho_tot)/c_E` is dimensionally capable of supplying
an inverse length, but its placement must come from the derived relation rather than a fit.

No follow-on, density scan, GPU work, action, carrier, matter solve, or canonization is launched by
this package.
