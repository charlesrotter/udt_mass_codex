# G202 lay report

We now know exactly what “quiet middle” means.

It is not enough for the reciprocal depth merely to pass through zero.  The profile must also be
locally level and unbent there.  In the music metaphor, the conductor, the tempo change, and the
tempo curvature must all pass through neutral together.

A cubic crossing is the simplest smooth example, but it is not the only one.  Infinitely many
metric-native profiles can be quiet in the middle and grow strongly in both directions.  A few
observations can calibrate a chosen small family, but they cannot manufacture the entire global
score unless the theory first tells us why that family is the right one.

Also, `c_E` and `G` need a mass or density anchor before they can even form a length scale.  That
clarifies the legitimate role an electron mass, cosmic density, or another observed anchor could
eventually play without pretending the anchor derived the kernel.
