A route is `DERIVED_METRIC_NATIVE_SIGNED_DEPTH` only if all six requirements hold without an extra
physical choice:

1. `METRIC_NATIVE`: complete metric plus the already typed pair/path inputs suffice;
2. `SIGNED`: reversal changes the sign;

4. `FRAME_COVARIANT`: coordinate/coframe gauge changes do not change the scalar assignment;
5. `NONTRIVIAL`: nonzero depth is available on at least one admissible arrow; and
6. `FOUNDING_ALIGNED`: the result labels the founded reciprocal character rather than a merely

normalization is supplied is classified `AVAILABLE_CONDITIONAL`, not derived.
