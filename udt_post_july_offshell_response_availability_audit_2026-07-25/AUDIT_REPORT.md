## Interpretation

The current architecture is coherent but divided across three levels:

1. native UDT geometry says what reciprocal depth, angular structure, and
   finite-cell alternatives are available;
2. conditional actions show examples of how a chosen local system would
   respond to variation; and
3. the working bootstrap judges completed solutions after the fact.

What is absent is the joint that turns level 3 into an off-shell response at
level 1. This makes the previously identified bootstrap-to-local response map
the smallest justified next derivation target. It does not make a guessed
action, a density sweep, or the conditional Hopf carrier native.
