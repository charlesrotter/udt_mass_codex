# Next gate after G121 — mixed causal/co-present commuting square

## Question

For one complete metric construction, does the direct co-present observer-pair edge agree with the
edge reconstructed by carrying the two observer states to a common causal source and back?

## Smallest diagram

Use one source `S`, two observers `A,B`, and their supplied regular branches:

\[
R_{BA}^{\rm causal}=P_B^{-1}C_{BA}P_A.
\]

Construct independently from the same metric/query data the direct co-present pair immersion and
its complete differential. The causal edge acts on four-dimensional Jacobi phase data; the direct
pair differential is not yet typed on that carrier. Before outcome evaluation, declare and
preregister candidate common fibers `W_A,W_B`, metric-derived maps

\[
q_A:V_A\to W_A,\qquad q_B:V_B\to W_B,
\]

and a direct pair arrow `R_BA^pair:W_A->W_B`. The typed square to test is

\[
q_B R_{BA}^{\rm causal}=R_{BA}^{\rm pair}q_A.
\]

Every `q` must be derived from the common metric/query construction and fixed before evaluation.
Do not choose a convenient or “largest” quotient after seeing the result; such a quotient could
erase the very defect being tested.

## Required channels

- terminal pair metric and reciprocal readout;
- source/observer frequency contractions;
- full Jacobi phase, including caustics;
- middle calibration carry;
- path-labelled angular/screen transport;
- null-cone and time-orientation typing.

At low radius the scalar junction must reduce to G116. It must not impose
`zeta=phi_pair` outside the pure stationary reciprocal branch.

## Success condition

A history restriction exists only if one preregistered typed-square residual vanishes for some regular
histories and not others, without postselecting a query, branch, screen path, source state, or
flat-holonomy rule. If the defect vanishes identically once all types match, bank it as the next
metric identity and do not call it selection.

No SNe, transfer, `X_max`, bootstrap, action, matter, or signalling input enters this gate.
