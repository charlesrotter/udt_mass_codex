## Independently reconstructed possible theorem

Fix a four-dimensional Lorentz vector space (V,eta). Let A be its algebraic
curvature space and let D_j be finitely many declared Lorentz tensor modules
for derivative order j>=1. The simplest sufficient domain is an invariant open
neighborhood of zero in the direct sum A plus these D_j, with a FIXED map
F into symmetric covariant rank two. For a germ it suffices to require local
equivariance whenever both transformed arguments belong to its domain. Require
Frechet differentiability at the origin, not merely directional derivatives.
No exact homogeneity is required. These are CONDITIONAL mathematical class
assumptions, not consequences of Local Metric Sufficiency.

Choose fixed calibrated component units and positive finite-dimensional norms.
They are numerical/mathematical controls, not a preferred physical observer or
a positive Lorentz-invariant norm. Write z=(K,D_1,...,D_m), and

    F(z) = F(0) + L_0 K + sum_j L_j D_j + r(z),
    ||r(z)|| <= omega(||z||)||z||,  omega(t)->0.

Lorentz invariance forces F(0)=c eta. Equivariance of the derivative plus the
reused G301 algebraic-curvature intertwiner lemma gives
L_0 K=a Ric(K)+b R(K)eta. Thus, for a fixed trace-free projector P,

    P F(z) = a S(K) + sum_j P L_j D_j + P r(z).

For full O(1,3), -I acts on D_j as (-1)^j and acts trivially on the output,
so the linear odd-derivative terms vanish. This sharpened parity claim must
not silently replace the assumption by SO+(1,3) without another argument.
The leading estimate itself does not need parity: it survives bounding all
j>=1. The G301 basis also holds for the connected group by its independently
certified full intertwiner census, but parity and that census are distinct.

Suppose, uniformly over a declared family of points/normalized data,

    ||K|| <= M_0 epsilon,
    ||D_j|| <= M_j epsilon delta^j,
    epsilon -> 0, delta -> 0.

Then ||z|| <= M epsilon for delta<=1, and

    ||P F(z)-a S(K)||/epsilon
      <= sum_j ||P L_j|| M_j delta^j

This tends to zero. Under full O parity its first derivative contribution
starts at j=2 if that slot is present. For F in C^(1,alpha) with a stated local

## Attempts to break stronger conclusions

1. **No nonzero coefficient from regularity.** A scalar-only response f(z)eta,
   the zero response, or a smooth curvature-quadratic trace-free response has
   a=0. They obey the conditional class conditions while leaving nonzero S
   possible. They cannot be excluded by historical principal gates that this
   campaign deliberately does not assume. A quadratic response may possess
   nontrivial zero sets, so even a small exact response says no universal S
   estimate without nondegeneracy.
2. **No slow variation from small curvature.** Even-order linear responses
   such as the trace-free Hessian of scalar curvature are allowed on the
   declared derivative slots. If these slots are O(epsilon), they contribute
   at leading order. Small curvature by itself does not suppress them. A
   candidate must expose the long-variation hierarchy, not silently put it
   into a generic word such as quiet.
3. **No O(epsilon^2) from Frechet differentiability.** Scalar functions can have
   remainders epsilon/|log epsilon|. Smooth tensor examples require appropriate
   invariant construction, but the abstract Frechet estimate alone already
   cannot give a power rate. Gating a power claim needs the stated stronger
   regularity, not passing polynomial witnesses.
4. **No relative curvature claim from an upper amplitude bound alone.**
   ||S||=o(epsilon) does not entail ||S||/||Rm||->0 if ||Rm|| is itself much
   smaller than epsilon. A normalized curvature lower bound or a different
   hierarchy expressed relative to actual ||Rm|| is needed for that ratio.
5. **No frame-uniform estimate over arbitrary boosts.** Fixed component norms
   are equivalent only with constants depending on frame change. The Lorentz
   group is noncompact; arbitrary epsilon-dependent boosts can spoil smallness.
   Tensor identities remain covariant. A uniformly bounded family of frame
   changes is a sufficient additional quantitative control.
6. **No hidden dimensional scale.** In response units L^-2, a linear derivative
   order-j correction requires coefficients of dimension L^j, and a degree-q
   curvature term requires L^(2q-2). A chosen reporting unit is not an adopted
   physical operator scale. Allowing arbitrary such fixed coefficients defines
   a conditional mathematical class beyond G301's scale-free class; it cannot
   be called admitted UDT response architecture. If exact weight-one scale
   freedom is restored, the old G301 theorem remains the stronger exact result.
7. **No automatic finite-slot representation theorem.** Metric finite jets,
   curvature jets, and a full direct-sum tensor neighborhood are different
   objects. Realizable derivative jets obey Bianchi and commutation identities,
   some involving quadratic curvature. An ambient equivariant differentiable
   extension is an explicit sufficient assumption, not something locality
   alone proves. If the domain is instead a constrained jet manifold, the
   derivative/tangent decomposition must actually be justified there.
8. **No metric or dynamics limit.** An algebraic response estimate is neither
   existence of DDR developments nor compactness/convergence of normalized
   metrics. Small C0 trace-free Ricci does not on its own bound its derivatives,
   control accumulated scalar variation, or establish a connected constant
   scalar by Bianchi. The exact G301 Bianchi argument cannot be transferred to
   an approximate equation without derivative/domain controls.
