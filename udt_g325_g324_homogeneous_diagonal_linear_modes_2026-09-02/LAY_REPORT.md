# G325 lay report

The G324 spacetime is not an isolated mathematical needle. In the complete “same everywhere on
each spatial slice, with no cross-terms” perturbation sector, nearby possibilities exist—but they
have four different meanings.

- One apparent change only moves the zero point of the time coordinate.
- Three changes alter the lengths and shape of the compact wraparound space. Locally they look
  like coordinate rescalings, but they cannot be removed by a well-defined coordinate change on
  the fixed torus.
- One change makes the two previously equal transverse directions expand at slightly different
  rates. It changes the local tidal field and is therefore genuine geometry.
- One change moves the spacetime into a neighboring constant-curvature branch already allowed by
  Universal Reciprocity's provisional trace-free equation. Its value remains free.

The local shear difference grows logarithmically relative to the chosen symmetric background, and
the constant-curvature mode grows quadratically toward the expanding end. Consequently this is
evidence of real neighboring UDT histories, not proof that the G324 spacetime is stable.

This result changes no UDT metric, reciprocal kernel, angular cancellation, premise, scale, or
physical `X_max`. Spatially varying waves and cross-direction modes remain to be classified.
