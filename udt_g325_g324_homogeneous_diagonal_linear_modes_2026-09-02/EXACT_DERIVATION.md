## 2. Direct metric equations

For a diagonal Bianchi-I metric let

\[
\alpha_i=\log C_i+p_i\log T+\epsilon u_i,
\qquad H_i=\dot\alpha_i,
\qquad \theta=\sum_iH_i.
\]

A direct Christoffel and Ricci calculation gives

\[
R_{00}=-\sum_i(\dot H_i+H_i^2),
\qquad
\frac{R_{ii}}{g_{ii}}=\dot H_i+\theta H_i.
\tag{4}
\]

The background satisfies both Kasner identities, `sum p_i=sum p_i^2=1`, and is Ricci-flat.
The trace-free equation plus the contracted Bianchi identity makes any connected regular solution
Einstein with a spacetime constant. At first order write that free constant as `lambda`:

\[
\delta R_{ab}=\lambda (g_0)_{ab},
\qquad \delta R=4\lambda,
\qquad d\lambda=0.
\tag{5}
\]

This does not add a cosmological source or select `lambda`; it retains the regional scalar datum
already present in G303/G310--G313.

With `v_i=dot u_i` and `V=sum v_i`, direct linearization of (4) gives

\[
\dot v_i+\frac{v_i}{T}+\frac{p_iV}{T}=\lambda,
\tag{6}
\]

and the time equation is

\[
\sum_i\left(\dot v_i+\frac{2p_iv_i}{T}\right)=\lambda.
\tag{7}
\]

Equivalently, the linearized Hamiltonian constraint is

\[
\frac{V-\sum_i p_iv_i}{T}=\lambda.
\tag{8}
\]

The direct tensor verifier reconstructs (4)--(8) from (3), rather than importing the production
ODE.
