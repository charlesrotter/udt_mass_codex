## Preregistered classification axes

Every major object or question must receive exactly one primary dependency role:

- `FOUNDING_CONSTRAINT`: enters directly through the current founding premise ledger;
- `METRIC_DERIVED_STRUCTURE`: established downstream geometry in its stated regime;
- `GLOBAL_COMPLETION_JOINT`: needed to turn local/coframe geometry into complete spacetimes;
- `DYNAMICAL_CLOSURE_JOINT`: needed for time-live evolution, variation, or source response;
- `WORKING_BOOTSTRAP_HYPOTHESIS`: proposed global-local consistency, not yet a law;
- `CONDITIONAL_MATTER_BRANCH`: depends on a carrier, action, response, boundary, or completion;
- `OBSERVATIONAL_READOUT_OR_ANCHOR`: comparison/calibration, not native dynamics;
- `CERTIFICATE_SIDE_LEMMA`: exact local algebra that does not currently block the spacetime model;
- `DEFERRED_NONBLOCKING`: legitimate question whose answer is not required for the next model step.

Record separately:

- evidentiary status;
- scope/regime;
- direct prerequisites;
- downstream consumers;
- whether its failure blocks kinematics, complete geometry, dynamics, bootstrap, or matter;
- the least expensive honest method appropriate to it.

No file is to be called scientifically irrelevant merely because it is expensive. Conversely, no
question is load-bearing merely because substantial computation has already been invested in it.
