# UDT spacetime-model objectives

## Course correction

The scientific goal is not to make a particular symbolic computation finish. A timeout, RAM ceiling,
or successful Gröbner calculation says how a representation behaved on this workstation. It does not
say whether UDT spacetime is true, false, complete, unique, or physically selected.

The goal is to construct the smallest complete mathematical model in which the two founding UDT
postulates have an unambiguous four-dimensional realization, all metric degrees and global transition
data are visible, identities are separated from physical laws, and every later cosmological or matter
claim can be traced to a stated equation and premise.

## What the repository already establishes

1. **Relational kinematics.** The founded scalar `phi` is the additive logarithmic depth of the
   reciprocal clock/ruler pair. The reciprocal two-slot action is exact. This is not a placeholder
   scalar and is not derived from an adopted action.
2. **A nontrivial complete-geometry arena.** The founded pair admits a general triangular
   four-dimensional coframe extension. The stationary general-screen atlas contains genuine angular
   shape, shear, gauge, causal-class and Cartan structure.
3. **Some physical comparison structure.** On a stationary branch, a Killing norm supplies a
   metric-native endpoint depth and the complete coframe supplies path-labelled transport. Arbitrary
   observers and the physical path family remain open.
4. **Global configurations exist.** Finite-cell completion families and transition constraints have
   constructive witnesses. They are an atlas of possible completed geometries, not a selection
   theorem.
5. **Matter consequences exist conditionally.** The adopted `S^2`, `L2+L4`, finite-box branch is full
   3D and Hopf-capable. It does not derive the carrier, native source, unconditional mass or parent
   spacetime law.

## The corrected four-layer model

### Layer 1 — founded relational kinematics

Objects: events/observers, the reciprocal pair, founded `phi`, observer comparison arrows, and the
observed calibration anchors `c_E` and `G_obs`.

Question answered: what relationship must reciprocal observations obey?

Question not answered: which complete metric profile is realized or how it evolves.

### Layer 2 — complete configuration geometry

Objects: manifold or finite cell, coframe and metric, all angular-screen and mixing variables,
connections, causal classes, transition functions, seals/joins, and global topology.

Question answered: what complete geometries and geometric identities are admissible?

Question not answered: which admissible geometry solves the physical UDT law.

### Layer 3 — native law and global closure

Missing objects: a covariant response/constraint/evolution law on Layer 2, a boundary/completion law,
and—if the working bootstrap idea is correct—a type-correct global-local return operation.

This is the genuine open joint. It must not be replaced silently by EH, Bach, a carrier action, a
density ansatz, a desired soliton, or the demand that the founding postulates choose one local metric.

### Layer 4 — readouts and matter consequences

Objects: observer-pair distance and `Xmax`, redshift/SNe comparisons, possible background-density
windows, carrier emergence, sources, persistence, particles, forces and masses.

These are scientifically important, but their unconditional interpretation is downstream of the
Layer-3 law. Conditional branches remain useful tests of consequences.

## The principal conceptual finding

Earlier work often treated “no unique local selector” as a prompt for another elimination or
uniqueness calculation. That demand is too strong. A spacetime theory can define a configuration
space and a law whose many solutions are distinguished by initial, boundary and global data. Its
kinematic postulates need not select one metric at each point.

Therefore the zero selector rank of the current registered premises is not itself a failure of UDT.
It is evidence that those premises are kinematic or constitutive. The missing table leg is an equation
or response on the complete configuration space—not another local certificate showing that one
algebraic branch is unique.

## Immediate bounded objective

Build a **factorized whole-spacetime configuration skeleton**. It must contain every currently
derived slot without choosing its value:

- manifold/cell and chart cover;
- reciprocal pair and founded `phi`;
- full coframe/metric extension, screen, shear and off-block mixing;
- observer/event arrows and coframe comparison data;
- connection, torsion/curvature and metric identities;
- transition, seam/seal, boundary and topology data;
- calibration anchors and their dimensional roles;
- explicit empty slots for response, evolution, constraint, boundary and global-return equations.

The skeleton is not a new ansatz and must not be expanded into one giant polynomial system. It is a
typed ledger of the theory's variables, gauge freedoms, identities, equations and open choices.

## Maximum conclusion of the next program

The next program may show that the existing results fit into one internally coherent factorized
configuration model and may identify the minimum missing equation slots. It may not claim a native
action, selected carrier, bootstrap theorem, unique universe, numerical `Xmax`, matter source or
stable mass.
