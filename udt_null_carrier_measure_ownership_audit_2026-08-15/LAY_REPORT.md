# Lay report — the numbered hooks are bookkeeping

The previous audit said the metric gives us a conveyor belt but not the physical cargo. This audit
looked more closely at the belt.

The complete observer query naturally puts numbered hooks on it: source time and sky direction
identify each ray. As the angular sector, mixing, and focusing squeeze or stretch the bundle, those
hooks spread out or crowd together, but none of the hook labels disappear. The metric calculates
the exact crowding automatically.

So we have proved an exact bookkeeping statement: **the query's geometric labels remain labels.**
That is true by construction, not a newly discovered UDT conservation law. The full orchestra still
determines how crowded those labels become, without a frozen radial shortcut.

What we have not proved is that physical light is attached to those hooks and cannot jump off,
scatter, be absorbed, split between branches, or be created. Calling the hooks "photons" would
simply assume the missing step.

This makes the category boundary clearer but does not make the physical joint smaller than G94/G95
already left it. We do not need all of Maxwell merely to relabel a regular ray bundle. We still need
either to derive or provisionally state that the physical radiative amount follows this bookkeeping
map with physical zero side flux. A separate identification ties each carried unit's energy to the
transported null covector and gives the `1/Z` energy ratio.

The metric precisely describes the spacing of the hooks, but neither the boxes nor their survival
are owned by the metric.
