## Analytic check

For each o,U,n, PSW1 gives

    log p_L(U,n) = -T_U(n,n)L^2/2 + O(L^3).

If T_U(n,n)>0 then the right side is negative for all sufficiently small L,
contradicting p_L>1. Hence T_U(n,n)<=0. Strict finite positivity does NOT justify
T_U(n,n)<0: the coefficient may vanish and higher orders may matter.

T_U is a symmetric quadratic form on the positive-definite rest space. Its trace
is Ric(U,U)=0, so a nonpositive T_U has every eigenvalue zero and T_U=0.
This deduction holds for every future unit timelike U at each point of O.

rescale to arbitrary future timelike U. T_U=0 gives R(X,U,U,X)=0 for X orthogonal
to U; replacing any X by its spatial projection gives the same contraction by
curvature antisymmetry. Thus for every X the quadratic polynomial in U given by
R(X,U,U,X) vanishes on the open future timelike cone and hence on all U.
All sectional numerators vanish. Polarization with the algebraic curvature
symmetries and first Bianchi identity gives Riemann=0 at this point. This is a
standard algebraic consequence, not G212's unadopted isotropy condition.
