# G255 scope reconciliation

Date: 2026-08-24

## The apparent contradiction

G166 correctly says the reciprocal kernel is algebraically downstream of the primary UDT metric
and that an arbitrary conformal Lorentz family must not be promoted into the derived UDT solution
space. G212 correctly says a fully valued rank-complete completed-pair network can itself be the
metric state, so no second selector is needed after faithful reconstruction. G254 correctly says
that the current evaluators do not provide a time-live metric residual.

These statements are compatible once three levels are kept separate.

## Level 1 — founded reciprocal form

On supplied ordered depth, the founded character fixes the reciprocal clock/ruler form. G166 then
shows that the kernel is the algebraic readout already carried by that completed ordered-pair
metric. There is no second response profile to bolt onto it.

This fixes the **form of the response after depth is supplied**. It does not determine the values
of depth across all separations or their time-live continuation.

## Level 2 — complete valued relation state

G176 fixes the positive completed ruler calibration after the full pair pullback. G212--G215 show
that a sufficiently rich, consistently valued relation network can reconstruct and therefore be
the metric state.

No second history selector is required after those values are known. But reconstruction consumes
the values; current overlap, carry, reciprocity, and rank identities do not generate them from
finite anchors.

## Level 3 — complete-coframe configuration arena

The full coframe is a lawful arena in which to test whether candidate time-live, screen, mixing,
shift, and volume functions remain compatible with the native evaluator. It is not a derived list
of physical UDT histories.

G254's family

\[
g_b=-dt^2+e^{2bt^2}(dx^2+dy^2+dz^2)
\]

was explicitly registered as a diagnostic family, not a physical ansatz. Its purpose is limited
but decisive: completed-pair normalization and the current compatibility/evaluator identities do
not, by themselves, generate ambient values. It does not prove that every member is a physical UDT
universe.

## Corrected wording of the open gate

The open problem is not:

> Choose one arbitrary Lorentz metric from the set of all Lorentz metrics.

It is:

> Derive or otherwise own the complete numerical depth/profile and time-live relation state of the
> primary UDT metric, or reduce that state to a finite family whose remaining constants can be
> calibrated by declared observations.

That bridge could be a local invariant condition, a genuinely global relation law, an algebraic
finite-family closure, or another metric-native nonidentity restriction. A conventional evolution
equation is one possible mathematical form, not an ontological requirement.

## Consequence for the roadmap

G254 supplies a valid stop gate for ODE/GPU work: there is no owned residual to solve yet. G255
narrows the constructive target. The next derivation must act on the primary UDT valued relation
state, not on an unrestricted metric zoo, and must do at least one of the following:

1. generate or propagate the depth/profile and time-live values;
2. reduce them to a finite metric-native family;
3. impose a genuinely global completed-relation constraint that rejects one regular valuation.

Only after that closure is explicit can a reduced algebraic, ODE, PDE, or GPU solve be typed. Finite
observational anchors may then calibrate remaining constants and test consilience; they must not be
used to manufacture an unrestricted function while calling it a derivation.
