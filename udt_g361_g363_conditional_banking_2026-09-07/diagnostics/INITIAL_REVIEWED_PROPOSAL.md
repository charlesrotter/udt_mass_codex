## Proposed adaptive tests

At most three substantive reviewed steps, selected in this order only while
useful. Freeze each step's exact question, class, free/fixed inputs, criterion,
resources and possible outcomes before its computation.

1. **Find genuine directions out of the sufficient class.** On a declared
   regular local complete-data patch, examine variations of gamma,K and the
   auxiliary null section that satisfy the ORIGINAL linearized Hamiltonian/
   momentum constraints, while testing the full seed and full curvature-root
   compatibility. Separate coordinate/frame changes and constant auxiliary
   seed rescaling from genuine changes; a nonconstant rescaling of a chosen
   seed is not by itself evidence that no suitable parallel seed exists.
   Keep Lambda fixed for the first test; repeating the known nonzero-Lambda
   seed/root failure would add little. Outcome: only demonstrated class-tangent
   freedom, a candidate lawful direction leaving this seed route, or an
   obstruction in the explicitly examined variation sector. Linearized
   compatibility is NOT an exact nearby Einstein family or nonlinear stability.

2. **Test whether departure is real and whether the same recipe survives.**
   For the most informative preceding direction, seek an exact admitted
   local family or a justified local-development construction from EXACT
   constrained data. Then check the unchanged full B=beta tensor4 domain,
   full ambient recurrence and chosen current, not only selected components.
   Ask whether a suitable parallel null representative exists, rather than
   penalizing one badly normalized seed. A lawful case without such a seed
   but with the recipe would disprove necessity of that route. A proved
   obstruction could establish necessity only in its stated class. If exact
   realizability or the relevant integrability remains unproved, return that
   limit: no off-equation example or formal jet becomes an admitted development.

3. **Measure the remaining joint data/query freedom, if the preceding result
   permits it.** Test deformations of the coupled initial alignment and product
   equations on the resulting admitted class. Compare two explicitly different
   experiments: fixed phi,z,Delta,mu while metric data vary; and a declared
   supplied phase variation with z,Delta,mu still fixed. Can nontrivial lawful
   changes satisfy both equations, or is there a precise obstruction?
   Computing a new matching mu is a different query and cannot rescue a
   fixed-mu failure. Do not claim a codimension from naive component counts,
   or call a formal tangent family exactly realizable. Skip this step if it
   only reproduces the known fixed-product criterion or needs broader authority.

No new recipe coefficient, phase/current law, physical counting rule or
measurement identification may be introduced to force a favorable outcome.
Mathematical methods with checked hypotheses are allowed; a new physical
premise is neither assumed necessary nor pre-authorized.

## Keep the outcomes separate

1. **Within the preserved class:** full admitted-data, seed, recipe and query
   conditions hold. Apply only the already proved local conditional preservation.
2. **Outside this construction but still lawful:** the original equations hold,
   but seed, recipe type, positive branch or chosen query matching fails.
   Identify which boundary was crossed. This is NOT evidence against UDT.
   Leaving the seed theorem alone does not even prove recipe failure.
3. **Original-equation failure:** a proposed datum/development violates an
   actual original constraint/equation after implementation and error checks.
   Reject that candidate as not admitted; do not silently repair it. This is
   distinct from showing the admitted equations inconsistent or UDT false,
   neither of which follows from a failed trial. Numerical ambiguity stays
   unresolved rather than being assigned to any successful outcome.
