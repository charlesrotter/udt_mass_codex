Both favorable and adverse uses narrow together: missing raw-table/estimator
access blocks a full source replay, not the algebraic construction; the source
summary remains useful within its actual source model. The published count
inconsistency does not prove the distance fit wrong. A scalar-map defect cannot
reject a general geometry using the same scalar summary. Constant-factor drift
cancellation does not erase spectral redshift, local source motion or a varying
external factor. No old result is regraded from these audits.

R6/R7/R13 are the actual proof dependencies. OEV1 observation products and the
MCP conventional readout are explicit observational hypotheses. Old controls,
postulates and trial candidates route context only. New source/reference joins
are open boundaries, never proof inputs. Whole-corpus reproof, raw spectra,
full covariance recovery, native matter/evolution and protected lanes omitted.
