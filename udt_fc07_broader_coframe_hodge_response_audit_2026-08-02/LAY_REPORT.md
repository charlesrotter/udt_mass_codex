# Lay report

The useful result is that one instrument really cannot play the missing global note by itself.

If the depth field `phi` rises and falls around a closed cell, all of its changes cancel. Changing
the volume of that same instrument does not help; mathematically it is still the same tune written
differently.

But depth and angular area together can do something genuinely new. Imagine graphing depth on one
axis and angular area on another while walking around the cell. If the two change out of step, the
graph can trace a loop. The signed area inside that loop survives as a global residue. That is the
precise mathematical version of the orchestra metaphor: the joint timing of two instruments can
carry information that neither instrument has alone.

We also learned that the old statement “the physical ruler and the global harmonic ruler are the
same line” depended on the coframe being lower-triangular. Once a legitimate mathematical
upper-right mixing control is allowed, the physical ruler can contain a circulating screen part,
while the global harmonic direction remains different.

What we have **not** learned is that the universe must use the new joint response. The metric makes
the motif available, but we have not yet found the piece of geometry that plays it with a fixed
coefficient or turns it into a law. Density is therefore still premature.
