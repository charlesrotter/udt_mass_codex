# G75 in plain language

The previous sky test used 21 example geometries. Twelve of them had a mathematical defect at the
very center, so they could not honestly tell us what happens across the whole sky.

G75 built a new box of center-safe shapes from scratch. Think of them as 49 different smooth ways an
axial distortion can turn on, persist, cross through zero, or taper away. We tested each shape at
four strengths and with three broad lapse controls, plus three undistorted controls. That makes 591
exactly catalogued geometries.

The useful finding is that center regularity does not force everything to look the same. Several
different kinds of smooth global profile survive. We now have a clean vocabulary for the next sky
map rather than having to repair a broken example or choose whichever profile gives an attractive
picture.

What we do **not** have is the universe's actual profile. Nothing here predicts CMB peaks or chooses
the size of the universe. This is a carefully checked parts catalogue, not the assembled physical
instrument.
