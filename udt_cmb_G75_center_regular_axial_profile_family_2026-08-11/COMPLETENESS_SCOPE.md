# G75 completeness scope

G75 is one bounded algebraic tile. It maps every member of the preregistered 49-ray projective
quadratic family, but not every center-regular complete metric.

| completeness axis | covered | deliberately not covered |
|---|---|---|
| fields | all four coordinates in the stationary axial control metric | generic ten-function coframe, time-live fields, matter |
| metric terms | lapse, spherical areal block, axial time-angle mixing | other angular shear/twist/mixing channels |
| equations | exact metric regularity and algebraic profile identities | action, Euler-Lagrange equations, source equations |
| domain | complete control interval `0<=r/R<=1` and full angular chart with Cartesian center audit | physical endpoint, exterior continuation, cut-locus atlas |
| boundary/regularity | exact center and endpoint orders | selected boundary law or finite-cell seal |
| topology | ordinary axial spherical control chart | alternative global topologies and branch gluing |
| dynamics | stationary profiles only | time-live evolution and stability |
| branches | all 49 frozen coefficient rays, four amplitudes, three lapses, plus zero controls | all smooth even profiles or generic complete-metric branches |
| response | none | geodesic, Jacobi, sky map, source, spectra |
| scale/regime | symbolic positive `R`; dimensionless full control cell | physical `R`, `X_max`, CMB endpoint, bootstrap range |

Every fixed physics-relevant choice is visible in `PREMISE_LEDGER.tsv`. The coefficient lattice and
amplitudes are `CHOSE_CONTROL`, not theory-selected. Classification records behavior and never filters
a member for resemblance to a desired sky.
