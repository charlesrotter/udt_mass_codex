## 1. Scalar curvature from finitely many clock-frame contractions

PSW1 gives c(U)=Ric(U,U)=-2 lim sum_i log p_L(e_i)/L^2. Here each triad is
orthonormal in U-perp and every clock experiment uses that frame's full prescribed
parallel preparation. A finite L output is an estimator, not this exact limit.

In one orthonormal frame e0,e1,e2,e3, set U_i^+-=gamma(e0+-v e_i),
0<v<1, gamma^2=(1-v^2)^-1. For a completely arbitrary symmetric Ricci matrix C,

    c_i^+ + c_i^- = 2 gamma^2 (C00+v^2 Cii),
    R = (1-v^2)/(2v^2) sum_(i,+-) c_i^+- - (1+3/v^2)c(e0).

Thus seven supplied timelike frames suffice; no Einstein condition, spatial
isotropy or scalar response equation was used. Opposite boosts cancel C0i.
This design also identifies C00,C0i,Cii, but leaves all three spatial off-diagonal
components undetermined. Scalar identification does not equal full Ricci or full
Riemann recovery. Seven is a convenient sufficient design, not a minimality claim.

One frame's complete electric tidal form still does not determine R. The product
g=-dt^2+h_K, where h_K is a local 3D constant-sectional-curvature metric, has
T_ij=0 for U=partial_t but R=6K. Its prepared clocks have zero leading quadratic
shifts in every spatial direction, for every K. This explicit kinematic family
also shows why trace-of-electric-tides is a timelike Ricci contraction rather
than the Lorentzian scalar trace. It is not a native-admitted UDT family.

If each c estimate has error at most delta, the displayed seven-frame R error
is at most (6/v^2-2)delta. For v=3/5 the exact weights are 8/9 on each boosted
contraction and -28/3 on c0, giving 44 delta/3. This is a deterministic worst-case
linear propagation bound, not an independent-error statistical estimate.
Small v is ill-conditioned; high boosts require their own preparation/regularity
bounds and are not an optimum justified here.

If each measured outgoing log p has absolute error epsilon and each PSW remainder

## 2. What independent space/time response testing requires

Scalar R at one event is insufficient to measure its differential response.
Supply an independently calibrated smooth operational geometry/connection and a
nearby spacetime array of scalar reconstructions; no response equation is used
to generate those records. At the central event choose normal coordinates with
orthonormal axes, x0=c_E tau. Then

    Box_g R = -partial_0^2 R + sum_i partial_i^2 R.

In a general chart one must instead include measured inverse-metric and
connection terms in g^(ab)(partial_a partial_b R-Gamma^c_ab partial_c R).
Metric/connection reconstruction is extra information: the seven scalar
contractions alone do not supply a full spacetime chart or its connection.
An ideal normal/exponential chart is a specified geometric construction, not a
claim that arbitrary distant astronomical data already realizes it.

For a common central difference step h, use R at o and o+-h e_a, with signs
s=(-1,1,1,1). The estimator sum_a s_a[R(+h e_a)-2R(o)+R(-h e_a)]/h^2 has noise
bound 12 delta_R/h^2 if all nine values have error <=delta_R. Its truncation
bound is h^2 sum_a sup|partial_a^4 R|/12 on the four coordinate segments.
Sufficient regularity is R in C4 (for metric curvature, g in C6 suffices).
The center's total coefficient is -4/h^2, not -8/h^2. Correlated errors do not
invalidate this worst-case bound; probabilistic bounds need their covariance.

Consequently a convergent joint idealization needs h->0 and delta_R=o(h^2).
With only the PSW1 O(L) normalized bias and log-clock error epsilon, sufficient
conditions are L=o(h^2), epsilon=o(L^2 h^2), and comparable control of preparation
and chart errors. The example L=h^3, epsilon=o(h^8) is mathematical scaling,
not an instrument specification or feasible resource estimate. Estimating fourth
metric derivatives from noisy observations is materially harder than measuring
the leading local tidal term. More efficient estimators may exist; none is
certified by this bounded study.

For the UNADOPTED quadratic vacuum candidate, the exact necessary trace equation

## 3. Weak pole and available backgrounds

An independently reconstructed small perturbation about an actual admitted
constant-curvature background can test (Box_0-M^2)delta R=0. Spatial and temporal
samples must belong to the same declared regime, with independent background,
source exclusion and truncation/error control. Flat homogeneous oscillations and
static radial exp(-Mr)/r profiles give a useful comparison, but plane-wave or
Euclidean Yukawa formulas do not automatically apply on an arbitrary curved
background. One may instead evaluate its actual Box_0.

The cubic f=R+alpha R^2+beta R^3 has M^2=(1-3 beta R0^2)/(6 alpha+18 beta R0).
At R0=0 all beta share 1/(6alpha); measuring this pole cannot select beta=0.
At fixed Lambda, constant-curvature vacuum backgrounds obey
beta R0^3-R0+4Lambda=0, so they do not provide a freely variable open interval.
More generally an open interval of roots of FR-2f+4Lambda=0 gives
R F_R-F=0 and therefore M^2=0 wherever F_R!=0. The stronger positive-pole
invariance premise cannot be operationalized by changing vacuum curvature at one
fixed Lambda. The exact two-event quadratic trace test above avoids such a knob,
but is only a candidate-specific necessary test on variable-curvature geometry.
