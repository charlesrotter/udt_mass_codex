# CMF1 lay return

The measurement connection works in ideal mathematics. Carefully prepared clocks
in several moving frames can recover the scalar curvature without assuming the
candidate field equation. A single observer's clock/tidal records are insufficient.
Measuring how that curvature changes requires additional independently calibrated
space/time observations and stricter accuracy than the first clock comparison.

There is also a simpler necessary test of the quadratic candidate. Its exact
trace requires a fixed straight-line relationship between measured curvature
and a particular combination of its changes through space and time. This test
does not require manufacturing a family of different background universes.
It does require informative variation, independent measurements, error control
and applicability of the source-free candidate. Two points merely fit the
constants; additional data must challenge the relationship. Even a perfect scalar
pass does not verify the full field equation, as an explicit false-pass geometry
demonstrates. No candidate has become UDT's governing law.

The practical gap is now specified: parallel/free-fall preparation, independent
proper distances and moving-frame calibration, geometric placement of nearby
measurements, a usable signal, and errors small enough to survive derivative
estimation. None has been shown achievable by existing instruments here. No
physical source/excitation or source-free certificate is supplied. The clock
reading also does not by itself separate an additional positional contribution
from the total geometry.

Recommendation: retain this as a conditional measurement interface. The next
bounded test could feed independently generated prepared-clock/ruler records
from a known smooth control geometry into the reconstruction, with declared
finite separation and noise, and test parameter recovery/rejection without
giving the estimator the equation or curvature answer. Include a scalar false
pass and check original tensor residuals separately. That would test numerical
recoverability; it would not establish physical feasibility or native selection.
Use a small CPU experiment first. No hours-long GPU campaign, data fit or new
physical premise is justified by this return alone. This successor has not run.

Alternative: stop at the ideal protocol and return to the native physical
selection/positional-attribution question. That question remains open regardless
of better instruments or a synthetic pass. No-change keeps current founding
postulates and all comparison equations unadopted. Source/horizon programs remain
paused. The current decision is discussion, not adopting a new law.
