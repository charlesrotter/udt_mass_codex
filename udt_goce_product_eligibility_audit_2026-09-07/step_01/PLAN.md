# PE1 — release identity and raw-support provenance

Question: for the official EGG_NOM_1B release associated with the 2018
reprocessing, which versioned method, calibration inputs and raw temporal
support are actually documented? The processing version itself is to be
established, not guessed from Level-2 release numbers or publication year.

Assumptions: prior TM1/TM2 conditional mathematics at unchanged reviewed scope;
primary technical sources are evidence of described processing, not proof
of flight performance or implementation fidelity. No observation payload.

Scope: official ESA catalogue/release notes/technical notes plus legitimate
primary-author institutional copies. Old/preflight sources are contextual
controls only. Maximum search is bounded by the campaign deadline, with an
early stop once the decisive documentary gap and safe alternatives are clear.

Possible outcomes: (a) identified release and reconstructible target/calibration
and support graph, enabling PE2; (b) a specific documented projection makes
the proposed signal unavailable; (c) an identified but incomplete dossier
leaves eligibility OPEN. Document unavailability is not (b), and model-assisted
calibration alone proves neither independence nor complete signal erasure.

Checks: verify publisher/domain/version/page and PDF versus HTML bytes;
cross-link independent official metadata where feasible; inspect load-bearing
method layouts; preserve retrieval failures and precise omissions. Fresh
source-first reviewer then direct source-map challenge, one repair allowed.
No new general theorem, fitted nuisance model or physical adoption.
