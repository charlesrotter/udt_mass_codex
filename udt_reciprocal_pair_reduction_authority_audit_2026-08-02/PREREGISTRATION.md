## Authority tests

A physical equivariant reduction is counted as supplied only if the current record provides all of:

1. an intrinsic rank-two subbundle/projector/slot rule in the complete metric or a clearly stated
   founding postulate that owns it;
2. a transformation law under observer changes (equivariance, not fixed components);
3. compatibility across the active complete-metric branches claimed by the source;
4. separation from a merely chosen block-screen ansatz or calibrated readout convention; and
5. no reliance on inactive CSN, a posited carrier, GR field equations, or a desired contact result.

Branch-conditional reduction data will be recorded as such and cannot establish universal founding
authority. Conversely, a covariant physical structure is not rejected merely because its
components change with observer frame.
