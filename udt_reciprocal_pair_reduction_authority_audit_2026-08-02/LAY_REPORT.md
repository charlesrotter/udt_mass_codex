# Lay report

The answer is neither “yes, completely” nor “no, it was only coordinates.”

UDT really does begin with a clock/ruler pair whose two sides change oppositely. That pair is not an
accidental mathematical placeholder. In the simple local metric used in the founding derivation,
an additional stated readout identifies those two directions with a physical clock and ruler.

But the complete four-dimensional metric has more directions, twists, and angular structure. The
two postulates tell us how the clock/ruler pair behaves **once its place in that full structure is
known**; they do not yet identify that place in every possible complete branch.

Some branches help. A unique stationary time direction can identify the clock, and twist can point
to the ruler. We have not yet put both facts together in one complete configuration where every
required uniqueness and global test passes.

So the new combined contact quantity is like a reliable reading made by an instrument whose mount
is known in the current test rig. The reading is invariant when observers change coordinates while
carrying the rig properly. We have not yet proved that the complete metric always builds that same
rig for us.
