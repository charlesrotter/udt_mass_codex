# Descriptive atlas scope

This is outcome-exposed characterization, not a preregistered observational
confirmation or fitted law. The original numerical outcomes and a few original
clock readouts were already visible when this plan was written. It adds no
evolution or ray query. Preserve every sign, dataset and failed qualification.

Tabulate all78 original datasets using their three frozen resolution controls,
and show the13 repaired realizations separately using old24half/new24quarter/
new32half. Each of the four supplied initial ray directions has its own row.
Retain each triple's logZ values, maximum finite resolution difference,
original field status, repaired field status where applicable, and source hashes.
Field and clock qualifications are distinct. A successful clock comparison
cannot erase an original field failure or qualify a repaired field on its own.

Plot logZ against the supplied initial amplitude multiplier in four panels.
Display the original32coarse values, all twelve supplied phase/polarization
recipes, and the axial recipe. Show repaired32half values at amplitude3 with
distinct markers; unqualified fields have distinct open markers. These markers
are finite supplied examples, not samples from a probability distribution.
Resolution differences are numerical diagnostics, not statistical confidence
intervals or certified continuum error bars. Avoid connecting unrelated initial
recipes into a distance or time trajectory; any recipe lines only guide the eye.

The horizontal coordinate is neither source distance nor elapsed physical time.
The plotted clocks use the unchanged short late-window query, fixed chart
positions/directions and supplied conditional Ric=0 geometry. No population,
selected physical frame, cosmological redshift curve, asymptotic scale, preferred
center, UDT law or universal redshift/blueshift assertion is inferred. Include
all unresolved numerical cases in the table and figure.

Build from authenticated compact original/repair reports after both clock stages
and repair equation checks return. Review the joins, computed differences and
plotted labels in separate context before banking the descriptive artifact.
No new fit, threshold, interpolation query or exclusion is authorized here.
