## Semantic definitions that must survive intact

**Positional c_E:** the owner interpretation is substantive, not just reciprocal
units. 1/c_E is time per separation. Zero received contrast does not remove the
geometry responsible for finite timing. x, dx/dt and d²x/dt² express the owner's
hierarchy of effects; no additive equation or independent correction factors follow.

**Ordinary local clocks:** proper time remains ordinary, including freely falling
clocks. Coordinate/query lapse coefficients are not intrinsically abnormal local
clock behavior. The owner's GR-sense clarification does not impose equality of
all finite-region experiments, tidal neutrality or strong local CSN. Strong CSN
remains CHALLENGED_OWNER_POSTULATE_NOT_DERIVED and inactive.

**Depth and redshift:** if Z=d tau_o/d tau_e, then 1+z=Z. On the same matched
clock leg Phi=-log Z. With delta_z=log Z and chi_z=tanh(delta_z),
Z=sqrt((1+chi_z)/(1-chi_z)). This does not identify every presentation phi,
completed Phi, transported rapidity magnitude, projective norm or physical distance.
Every sign convention should be explicit at the first equation.

**Three meanings of reciprocity:** (i) inversion of one comparison map;
(ii) two actual future signal routes; (iii) DDR stationarity of a specified
response under reciprocal shape variations. Neither of the first two is silently
the third, and the inverse of an outward map is not a physical causal return.

ICN1's actual outward b=f_AB(s), return a=f_BA(b), slopes p,q>0 give F'=pq at
the actual relay event and beta_rad=(pq-1)/(pq+1). Both p,q>1 imply increasing
radar separation, but beta_rad>0 alone does not imply both redshift; this is not
a derivation of Hubble expansion or native positional geometry. Chronology or a
verified ordered return is needed for F>s, not time orientation alone. In the
extra totally geodesic Lorentz2 sector, Omega_B=sqrt(p/q) and the gradients use
B's geodesic equation. This reconstructs limited metric data from observations,
not a general 4D evolution law.

**Asymptotes:** FSL1 requires the matched propagation direction as well as r=|chi|.
For r→1, Z→infinity iff (1-mu)/sqrt(1-r)→infinity. Saturating projective norm
alone is insufficient. Actual two-leg divergence requires S-|K|→infinity,
S=(log p+log q)/2, K=(log p-log q)/2. Neither criterion constructs physical
X_max, a population or an operational distance law.

**Native versus mathematical method:** standard geometry, variational calculus
and existence theorems can be methods with checked hypotheses. A supplied Einstein
metric or imported detector/optical response is not thereby a UDT derivation.
Conversely use of familiar mathematics does not invalidate a genuinely derived
consequence. An accepted conditional theorem and physical class membership are
different dependency edges.

## Review hazards and maintenance tests

1. A single polished narrative can hide unsupported arrows. Each cross-chapter
   arrow needs object/domain/protocol compatibility, inherited provisional and
   supplied premises, and its actual conclusion ceiling.
2. Historical source headings can disagree with later acceptance; preserve
   original bytes and explain the controlling overlay. Conversely older strong
   GR adoption language is superseded by current FILTER ONLY authority.
3. Negative results must inherit premises too. Controls not admitted under full
   UDT cannot prove full-UDT underdetermination. Changed premises flag positive
   and negative descendants; they do not automatically reverse either verdict.
4. Source-led completeness cannot mean reciting registry rows in prose. The main
   text should actually derive central steps; supporting coverage can explain why
   controls and optional branches sit outside that derivation. Deferred derivations
   must be named as documentation omissions.
5. Centralizing science tokens must not weaken startup guards. A reviewed central
   excerpt/hash plus strict adapter role validation can replace duplicated prose;
   merely allowing missing tokens or a link cannot. Reject stale strengthening in
   adapters and modified central science with stale review pins. LIVE remains the
   operational status owner; registry/source grades remain scientific owners.
6. Maintenance needs explicit scientific dependency versus review/control edges,
   no silently accepted cycle, changed-source and changed-premise descendants,
   stable IDs and versioned review pins. An absence of cycles or 406/406 rows is
   not semantic proof. Test stale GR-input wording, c_eff signal-speed promotion,
   local-E universal gates, optional-source adoption and restricted-no-go expansion.
7. Cold-reader test: explain positional geometry/local clocks, derive the
   reciprocal character and shifted complete-pair normalization from the central
   text, distinguish actual two-way timing from inversion, and locate the exact
   remaining physical joins without jumping into package directories. This is
   a readability/coverage test, not scientific promotion.
