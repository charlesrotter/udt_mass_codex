## Bounded interpretation

One governing law applies to every observer. Its clock-comparison predictions
may depend on the physical geometry and observer circumstances, including
acceleration and nearby gravitating matter. Universality alone does not require
an identical additional leading clock-curvature coefficient for every frame and
direction at an event. It also does not exclude that stronger property if some
separate physical argument or hypothesis supplies it.

Local proper clocks retain their established meaning. Acceleration of a clock
concerns its worldline and comparisons; this wording does not identify acceleration
itself with curvature or change an ideal clock's intrinsic proper-time law.
The GR analogy does not adopt GR field equations as a UDT source law. No new
matter model, matter-curvature formula, observer population or causal cone is
specified here.

PCC1's stronger all-frame leading contrast remains UNADOPTED and optional.
Its pointwise implication, regional compatibility conditions and supplied
examples retain their original mathematical hypotheses and conclusions. A
reference/matching prescription remains necessary for that diagnostic; it is
not established as a prerequisite for every possible native UDT law. The
statement neither adopts nor refutes PCC1, supplies a finite positional curve,
selects a scale, nor proves that existing UDT premises cannot close the open join.

The maintained meaning belongs in UDT_DEVELOPMENT.md D1/R8/R18. This fixed
source preserves the clarification and does not become another maintained
