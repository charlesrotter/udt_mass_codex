# G329 lay report

G329 turned on a wave that points partly along the special G324 direction and partly across it.
This is the first tile where the direction seen by the changing metric rotates with time, so it is
a stronger test than either earlier axis calculation.

The result held together. Starting with every possible small metric change, the four legal
coordinate freedoms remove four descriptions of the same geometry, and the field constraints
determine four more quantities. Exactly two genuine wave patterns remain. Each has two independent
time solutions and both cosine and sine placements on the compact space, for eight real constants
in this one Fourier tile.

The two patterns do not drive one another because reflection across the unused `z` direction
separates them exactly. But both equations still know about both pieces of the tilted wave. In
particular, they contain extra terms caused by the fact that its physical angle changes as the
background stretches. A shortcut that freezes that angle fails the hostile checks.

At early time the two patterns reduce to the same finite/logarithmic and linear/inverse-linear
families found in the transverse test. At late time both approach the axial oscillatory behavior.
No branch was thrown away. The earlier axial and transverse equations reappear as exact component
limits, with one necessary change of polarization normalization at the singular transverse edge.

So this is another positive, linear extension of the provisional spacetime analysis. It says the
G324 background and adopted trace-free response equation remain coherent for one genuinely tilted
mode. It does **not** establish full stability, choose a universe, determine a scale, add matter, or
change the UDT metric, reciprocal kernel, angular sector, or field equation.
