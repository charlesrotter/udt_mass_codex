## Whole question

```text
M=sech(delta)
```

## Plain-language decision

If every old relation enters the bounded state uniquely and every bounded state returns to one old
relation, the semicircle is a faithful new coordinate system rather than a selector. If some old
relation cannot enter, or some lawful network identity changes, it is a genuine restriction.

If `M` is independently measured, agreement between that reading and the signed clock arrow may be
new testable content. That possibility must not be confused with choosing which universe or which
observer relations Nature realizes.

## Maximum conclusion

G268 may classify finite regular relation-space equivalence, finite-network equivalence, the status
of the compact endpoints, and the conditional epistemic type of an independent `M` measurement. It
may not adopt `M` as canon, derive its measurement protocol, or close distance, population,
history, dynamics, or `X_max`.
