# G268 preregistration

Date: 2026-08-26

## Frozen question

Does the provisional full signed state `(M,chi)=(sech(delta),tanh(delta))` impose a nonidentity
constraint on the existing regular reciprocal relation space or its arbitrary finite composable
networks, beyond bounded reparameterization?

Separately: if `M` is declared an independently operational readout, what constraint does the
candidate place on the enlarged data type `(r,M)`?

## Frozen mathematical domains

1. `R`: finite ordered depths `delta in real numbers` with addition and reversal.
2. `K`: positive reciprocal clock ratios `r=exp(-delta)` with multiplication and inversion.
3. `H`: pairs `(M,chi)` with `M>0`, `-1<chi<1`, and `M^2+chi^2=1`, equipped only with the G267
   two-channel composition law.
4. Arbitrary finite directed relation graphs whose edge depths are actual composites of supplied
   endpoint potentials. Independent direct arrows remain independent unless supplied as the same
   composite, per G217.
5. The two ideal endpoints `(0,+1)` and `(0,-1)` are tested only as a mathematical closure, not as
   regular relations or physical `X_max` states.

## Preregistered relation-space landings

- `R0__EXACT_EQUIVALENCE_ONLY`: the maps among `R`, `K`, and `H` are bijective and intertwine
  identity, reversal, composition, and every finite endpoint-potential network. No admitted regular
  relation or network is rejected.
- `R1__GENUINE_RELATION_RESTRICTION`: exhibit an admitted regular reciprocal relation or finite
  lawful network with no unique lawful lift to `H`, or an `H` state with no reciprocal inverse.
- `R2__BOUNDARY_ONLY_CHANGE`: finite interiors are equivalent, while adjoining ideal endpoints
  changes or partially breaks the composition domain. This is not by itself a finite physical
  relation selector.
- `R3__TYPE_OR_ALGEBRA_FAILURE`: one of the registered maps, signs, inverses, or network laws is
  inconsistent.

`R0` and `R2` may both hold because they answer interior and closure questions, respectively.

## Preregistered operational landings

- `O0__DEFINITIONAL_ONLY`: `M` has no independently supplied operational data type, so the circle is
  only a coordinate identity.
- `O1__CONDITIONAL_CROSS_READOUT_LAW`: if an independent mutual-clock protocol supplies `M` on the
  same calibrated correspondence as signed `r`, the candidate predicts
  `M=2r/(1+r^2)` or equivalently `M^2+chi^2=1`. This rejects inconsistent enlarged `(r,M)` data but
  does not reject an existing reciprocal relation or select a history.
- `O2__OWNED_OPERATIONAL_PROTOCOL`: an existing premise independently derives the physical `M`
  protocol and thereby activates `O1` without a new premise.

## Exact certification contract

Production must verify symbolically:

1. all forward and inverse maps among `delta`, `r`, and `(M,chi)`;
2. the nonvanishing differential rank throughout the finite interior;
3. identity, reversal, composition, associativity, and kernel reconstruction;
4. equivalence of additive cycle closure and bounded-state cycle closure;
5. arbitrary endpoint-potential network lift and reconstruction up to one reference;
6. whether the compact endpoints support all compositions, including opposite ends;
7. the exact enlarged-data cross-readout residual.

An implementation-distinct verifier must use exact rational arithmetic on at least 1,000 positive
rational ratios, at least 5,000 ordered compositions, and at least 1,000 finite networks of varied
size. It must import no production module or production result.

Hostile mutations must catch at least:

- loss of the sign of `chi`;
- an incorrect inverse formula for `r`;
- composing `M` multiplicatively;
- deleting the G267 denominator;
- accepting an off-circle state;
- treating opposite ideal endpoints as a regular composite;
- claiming a history rejection from a pointwise bijection;
- upgrading the conditional operational law to an owned protocol.

## Frozen maximum conclusion

Even a complete `R0+R2+O1` result establishes only faithful bounded presentation of every finite
regular reciprocal relation, a non-group ideal closure, and a conditional coefficient-free
cross-readout test if `M` is independently operational. It does not derive the physical `M`
protocol, distance, scale, observer population, history, dynamics, source, matter, transfer,
observation, `X_max`, or canon.
