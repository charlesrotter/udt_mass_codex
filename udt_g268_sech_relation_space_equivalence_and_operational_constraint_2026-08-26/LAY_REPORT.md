# G268 lay report

The semicircle is a perfect translation of the reciprocal kernel, not an extra rule choosing which
relations exist.

Think of Celsius and Fahrenheit: every finite reading in one system has exactly one reading in the
other, and converting a whole list of readings does not remove any possible weather. Here the
bounded pair `(M,chi)` retains every bit of the original signed reciprocal relation and every lawful
finite observer chain.

The translation may still become physically useful. If we can measure the symmetric mutual-clock
effect independently of the ordinary signed clock/redshift ratio, the two readings must land on one
exact curve. A mismatch would disprove the provisional `sech` interpretation.

We do not yet have that independent measurement protocol. So G268 clarifies where a real test could
live, but it does not choose the universe, define distance, or generate `X_max`.

The two ends of the semicircle are also only ideal infinite-depth limits. Trying to combine opposite
ends gives an undefined expression, so they cannot simply be treated as ordinary physical states or
a completed universal boundary.
