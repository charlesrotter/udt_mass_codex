## Falsification and certification

The strong claim is certified only if the complete metric supplies a reference-independent,
local-Lorentz-equivariant, path-complete pair map with both requested outputs and no added physical
choice. It is falsified by any exact same-metric/same-observer-data counterexample with different
depth or separation, or by two admissible physical profiles obeying all active gates.

A split result is allowed: exact derivation on the founded reciprocal subgroup together with an
obstruction to universal extraction from a bare complete metric.

## Maximum conclusion

At most this audit may derive or refute a complete-metric kinematic identification. It cannot derive
an action, source, boundary, carrier, bootstrap return, mass, stability, signalling law, cosmology,
or dynamics. Any owner-supplied statement that reciprocal magnitude *is* physical separation must
remain explicitly distinguished from a consequence of the frozen metric sources.
