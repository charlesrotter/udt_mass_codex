## Recommendation in plain language

Test whether a chosen geometric construction that fits on an initial slice
continues to fit when that same lawful metric develops. This could reveal a
real additional restriction on compatible initial data, a preserved subclass,
or a failure of automatic preservation. It should not produce another special
compatibility example unless that example answers this different question.

We now have local existence for a freely supplied noncritical phase (G359)
and a special-class criterion for matching a CHOSEN curvature current to a
phase-independent product (G356). Neither says their relationship persists
on admitted metric developments. Conservation of the current is already
known NOT to imply the fixed-label product even within the harmonic class.
Repeating that counterexample alone would not answer the new question.

The proposed target is conditional geometric compatibility, not photons,
energy, particles, physical counting or another familiar mechanism. A
successful persistence result would not identify physically carried content.
Failure would not imply that a new physical premise is necessary.

## Precise question and supplied versus imposed conditions

Supply complete lawful metric data (Sigma,gamma,K and any fixed admitted
sector/marking data), their actual conditional smooth development, a local
real spatial phase phi with dphi!=0, initial label registration, retained
patch/support and positive spacing Delta. These are ordinary initial/query
data, free within their admitted conditions; they need not be uniquely chosen
by the metric. G359 fixes n(Theta)=-|dphi|_gamma and locally extends Theta
while its characteristic graph remains regular. That normal derivative is
not independently free once phi and the future-null type are fixed.

Hold the old curvature recipe fixed. Wherever its full root/recurrence
operations actually exist, distinguish two already different routes:

- ORIGINAL-ROOT phase: require dTheta=beta as a FULL ambient covector identity.
  Within G355's harmonic class, closure of this root forces q_rec=0. Do not
  require this same identity while claiming G356's positive q_rec route.
- DIFFERENT-PHASE weighted current: beta need not be closed; require the
  separately supplied/extended future-null k=dTheta to align with D, with
  D=rho k# for an appropriate smooth positive rho on the retained domain.
  The root remains beta, not a silently rescaled fourth root.

For the positive-current route, ask additionally whether the conserved current
has a well-defined ray-quotient intensity Xi that agrees with
(|dTheta|/Delta) tensor mu for ONE phase-independent finite label measure
on the frozen labels. Any extension of the quotient construction beyond the
accepted special class must itself be proved with its hypotheses, not inserted
as a definition claiming equality. Product equality, finite/integrable measure,
current conservation and phase alignment are distinct conditions.

If mu is supplied independently, recipe equality is a compatibility restriction
on it. If mu is computed by requiring equality with a fixed smooth intensity,
it is no longer an independently free second choice in that comparison.
Neither case selects physical population. Relabeling across phases, changing
the initial phase or replacing the recipe during a fixed-input test changes
the test; it is not a repair of its original conclusion.

The central quantifier is: on an explicitly stated class of ACTUAL admitted
developments satisfying the recipe/domain and full compatibility conditions
initially, do those conditions hold on some nonzero local interval?
An initial residual R|Sigma=0 is not a proof that R=0 nearby.
A computed nonzero normal derivative of a required residual on a complete
lawful compatible datum could disprove automatic preservation; vanishing
finitely many derivatives cannot prove general smooth preservation.
For a positive result, derive a propagation argument from admitted evolution
and the stated initial conditions, or prove a genuinely invariant subclass.
These are proposed methods and outcome criteria, not new results asserted here.

1. **Domain and admissible-data gate.** Specify the unchanged recipe, each

   Outcomes: a usable conditional class; a scoped incompatibility/obstruction;
   or an unsupported join that must remain open.

2. **First loss or a propagation identity.** On the surviving class, use
   admitted metric evolution to examine the first unconstrained derivative
   of alignment/root/product defects. Seek either a lawful initially compatible
   counterexample with rigorously nonzero departure, or a closed propagation
   argument. Special solutions count only as controls or as a rigorously
   identified subclass, not evidence for generic preservation.
   A recurrence/type-domain loss and a product mismatch are different failures.

3. **Only if informative: boundary of the surviving result.** Check whether an
   identified extra compatibility condition is preserved or just delays failure,
   or establish the exact freedom left by a reviewed preservation result.
   Include a distinguishable compatible/failed control only where it tests that
   new boundary. Do not invent a third task after an adequate negative answer.

## What the alternatives would establish

A positive general propagation result would make initial compatibility
sufficient on its proved local class: an additional connection between the
admitted metric evolution and this chosen construction. A narrower positive
result would identify a compatible invariant subclass, with its remaining
initial data and optional restrictions stated explicitly. Neither selects
that subclass as the physically occupied one or derives a counting law.

A lawful initially compatible departure would refute automatic preservation
for this fixed recipe/relationship and stated class, and isolate the failed
condition. It would not refute UDT, all G352 realizations, free local phases
or all metric-derived constructions. Failure of recipe admissibility already
on the initial slice would instead limit the candidate's domain; it would
not be a dynamic counterexample. An unresolved interface means OPEN, not
nonexistence or evidence that a new premise must be adopted.
