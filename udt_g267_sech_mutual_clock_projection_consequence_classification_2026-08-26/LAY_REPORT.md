# G267 lay report

The candidate works as a coherent new piece of the provisional model.

Think of the reciprocal pair as having two linked readings:

- `chi` says which direction the comparison points;
- `M` says how strong the mutual clock slowdown is, without favoring either observer.

Together they always lie on one unit semicircle. Near the quiet GR-like middle, the directional
redshift changes immediately, but the mutual slowdown begins only at second order and is therefore
much quieter. Far toward either extreme, the mutual clock rate falls toward zero symmetrically.
This is the exact loud–quiet–loud pattern, with no fitted coefficients.

The important qualification is that the symmetric number cannot stand alone. If direction is
discarded, two identical-looking pair magnitudes can combine in different ways. The directional
and mutual channels are therefore two faces of one kernel, not separate instruments that can be
turned on and off.

What this does not yet provide is miles, meters, or a complete universe. It tells us absolute
reciprocal depth, but converting that into physical distance still needs a metric-owned operational
protocol and a scale. It also evaluates every supplied history rather than choosing which history
Nature realizes.
