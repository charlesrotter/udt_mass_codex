| axis | covered here | not covered / deliberately dropped |
|---|---|---|
| metric block | stationary `(A,q_AB,b_A)` envelope | time dependence; radial-angular and direct pair/screen off-blocks |
| screen | round, area, two shears, full, degenerate control | a selected physical screen profile |
| mixing | zero, axial, two-component, twist-free and causal controls | a selected physical mixing field |
| dependence | radial, axisymmetric, fully angular, conditional S3 controls | complete WR-L/S3 join |
| symmetry | SO3, U1/reflection, discrete/none, enhanced controls | a universal symmetry or universal `m` |
| operator | exact scalar `Box_g` diagnostic | native UDT action, source, vector/tensor or polarization operator |
| regularity | basis requirements classified | physical center, seam, wall or asymptotic realization selected |
| spectrum | no solve | all eigenvalues, convergence, source weights, CMB comparison |
| compute | exact CPU algebra | FD2, GPU, time-live work |

The map is complete relative to the preregistered stationary scalar envelope and the registered
C01-C18 control universe. The full five-axis Cartesian census contains 2,800 uniquely dispositioned
cells in `AXIS_CROSS_PRODUCT_DISPOSITION.tsv`. It is not a complete map of all UDT coframes or
dynamics.
