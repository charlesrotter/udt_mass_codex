selected UDT completion, the scalar wave operator is not a native UDT dynamical law, and no screen,
boundary, population, or spectral family is physically selected here.

Use coordinates `(t,r,y^1,y^2)` and

```text
g_tt=-A,  g_rr=1/A,  g_tA=b_A,  g_AB=q_AB,
```

with `A>0` and positive screen matrix `q`. Define

```text
v=q^-1 b,    Lambda=A+b^T q^-1 b,
S=sqrt(det(q) Lambda/A).
```

The Schur complement of `q` is `-Lambda`. Consequently this envelope is Lorentzian wherever the
stated positivity conditions hold. Exact block inversion gives

```text

g^tA = v^A/Lambda
g^rr = A
g^AB = (q^-1)^AB-v^A v^B/Lambda
det(g) = -det(q) Lambda/A.
```

The symbolic matrix identity and determinant were checked for arbitrary two-by-two symmetric `q`.
An independent standard-library implementation checked three nonsingular rational matrices by
permutation determinant and exact rational matrix multiplication.

## Exact stationary scalar operator

For `Psi=exp(-i omega t) u(r,y)`, direct expansion of
`Box_g Psi=S^-1 partial_mu(S g^munu partial_nu Psi)` yields

```text
S^-1 partial_r(S A partial_r u)
+ S^-1 partial_A[S((q^-1)^AB-v^A v^B/Lambda) partial_B u]
+ omega^2 u/Lambda
- 2 i omega (v^A/Lambda) partial_A u
- i omega S^-1 partial_A(S v^A/Lambda) u.
```

The final line is load-bearing. It is a frequency-dependent zero-order term generated by the
angular divergence of the shift density. It vanishes for special divergence-free axial shifts,
but it is nonzero for a general angular shift. Therefore the earlier axial
`omega-m Omega`-style expression is a special case, not a complete-screen formula.

The explicit witness `Lambda=1+epsilon^2 sin^2(x)`, `S=sqrt(Lambda)`,
`g^(tx)=epsilon sin(x)/Lambda` gives

```text
S^-1 d_x(S g^(tx)) = epsilon cos(x)/Lambda^2,
```

which equals `epsilon` at `x=0`.

## Controls

```text

D=A r^2+h^2 sin^2(theta),     Lambda=D/r^2.
```

The general formulas recover the prior C1 inverse and full volume exactly. When `b=0`, they recover
the ordinary inverse screen. The three independent symmetric screen directions are area,
diagonal shear, and off-diagonal shear.

Multiplication by a nonaxisymmetric coefficient such as `cos(psi)` couples Fourier labels
`m -> m+1` and `m -> m-1`. Thus `m` is a good label only in a certified axial family. The atlas has
three practical complexity tiers:

1. `SO(3)`: radial ODEs indexed by fixed `ell` multiplets.
2. axial `U(1)`: fixed `m` (and reflection parity when present), with coupled `ell` content and a
   two-dimensional or harmonic-matrix radial problem.
3. discrete/no continuous angular symmetry: both `ell` and `m` mix in a full spatial problem.

These are operator/basis facts, not a population or branch-selection rule.
