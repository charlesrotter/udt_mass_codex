## Passing rules

### `HOPFION_DEPENDENT`

Passes only if removing every Hopfion source destroys the logical or algebraic basis of the July 31
stability hypothesis, rather than merely removing one supporting example.

### `MULTI_FAMILY_SUPPORT`

Passes only if at least two object-inequivalent families independently contribute load-bearing
evidence to the weak architecture `discrete family x stability pruning`, with their incompatible
premises and stability notions retained. This does not derive a universal law.

### `SHARED_NATIVE_GRAMMAR`

Passes only if the same metric-native object and implication—not a verbal analogy—operates across
at least two object-inequivalent families without importing or identifying their actions, carriers,
or boundary conditions. Otherwise the status is `ARCHITECTURAL_PATTERN_ONLY` or `OPEN`.

### `BOOTSTRAP_SELECTION`

Passes only if the frozen record supplies an explicit global/local membership or return rule that
selects the family/basin catalogue. The parent premise audit's nonselection result is controlling.
