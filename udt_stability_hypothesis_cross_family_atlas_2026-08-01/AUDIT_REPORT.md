## What each family contributes

### P4 `S-i`: original non-Hopf algebraic spine

The free angular-wall branch is unstable. Supplied odd angular parity removes the unique negative
direction from the zero-trace reduced core, but free higher wall germs and the lambda-Schur sector
leave the complete certificate open. This is exact conditional pruning evidence, not a fully stable
particle.

### P4 `S-ii`: strongest non-Hopf dichotomy

The no-modulus-jet class is unstable for nonzero `E0`; the jet-quadratic sector is stable exactly
when its registered mass-size-stiffness inequality holds. The cold P4 review retains this as sector
stability, not full dynamics or a realized universe.

### P4 massless controls

The controls are positive-semidefinite with the expected flat directions. They validate the
calculation and show nonisolation; they are not isolated stable basins and are not counted as a
third survivor stream.

### Conditional full-3D Hopfion

This is the strongest complete three-dimensional conditional example. Its carrier, functional,
finite computational boundary, staticity, and operator remain explicit premises. It is an exemplar,
not the definition of the hypothesis or a carrier derivation.

### Rings, empty branches, and formal live modules

Uniform all-definite cyclic rings are forced massless in the registered period arena. No stability test was performed
on that family. Massive cyclic one-cell and double-crease postures are
empty in their stated scopes; nonexistence is not instability. Static/time/angular modules embed
formally, but no one common nonzero live on-shell configuration exists in the record. These families
shape the catalogue and its missing joins without adding stability certificates.

## Reframed development program

The stability hypothesis should be developed as a catalogue-and-pruning program:

1. keep configuration families and their premises separate;
2. map existence, emptiness, instability, sector stability, and full conditional stability as
   different outcomes;
3. compare only invariant requirement patterns until a common metric-native response is derived;
4. use prospective bootstrap quantities as free conditional axes, never as an inferred selector;
5. ask later whether an explicit global/local closure law selects a mutually compatible survivor
   catalogue.

The immediate computational program is therefore not “stabilize the Hopfion.” It is to maintain a
family-by-family survivor map and close only those missing cells for which the necessary response,
boundary, and perturbation domain are already owned. At present the realization and persistence
joins remain the hard gates to any unconditional stability theorem.
