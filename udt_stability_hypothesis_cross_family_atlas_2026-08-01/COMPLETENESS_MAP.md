# Completeness map

## Covered

- Exact lineage of the July 31 stability hypothesis across the effective 1,469-path frozen universe.
- Seven preregistered configuration/evidence families and eight hypothesis claims.
- Separation of existence, emptiness, mass classification, sector Hessian stability, conditional
  static finite-box stability, time persistence, and bootstrap selection.
- Exact Hopfion-removal and P4-removal dependency tests.
- Ten-component grammar matrix with one row per family/component pair.

## Not covered

- Unregistered metric solution families.
- A new P4 or Hopfion calculation.
- Complete time-live equations, physical finite-cell carrier boundary, or infinite-volume limit.
- A common action, response operator, carrier, source, mass functional, or perturbation norm.
- A bootstrap membership relation or survivor-selection theorem.
- Particle identification, mass ratios, observational fitting, or canonization.

## Quantifier ceiling

`HYPOTHESIS_MULTI_FAMILY_SUPPORTED_NOT_DERIVED` means that two object-inequivalent evidence streams
support a weak catalogue-plus-pruning architecture under different premise stacks. It does not mean
two UDT matter species have been derived, that their stability notions are equivalent, or that every
family belongs to one physical universe.
