# PRT1 lay return and next action

Steps1–3 now have a reviewed, concrete formulation. We chose an existing pair
of freely falling clocks with a defined starting separation and initial motion,
then examined what mutual slowing would require of that comparison. We obtained
conditional restrictions and tested their limits. The connection assigning the
additional positional effect to this exact reading is still unproved.

Two lessons matter for the next stages. Requiring slowing to have the same sign
does not require the same amount in every circumstance; the latter was a stronger
earlier trial. Also, the effect can start more weakly than the usual quadratic
term while still growing without bound at a distant receiving limit. An existing
mathematical example demonstrates both a cubic onset and a distant asymptote
with regular future curvature. It is a control, not a selected UDT universe.

This means we should let the justified physics and observations constrain the
shape instead of building a chosen onset, constant-curvature form or singular
endpoint into the next calculation. Free clocks still respond to the full
geometry; their preparation alone does not isolate an extra positional piece.
The review does not prove that a new postulate is necessary.

The next task is **step4A: an observation-to-comparison map**. For at most three
promising measurement channels, identify what is actually measured, which
distance information is independent, what motion/source/gravity assumptions
enter, and what uncertainties and previous exposure exist. Astronomical sources
need their own actual comparison; they are not automatically our prepared lab.
Then choose one bounded, frozen analysis capable of constraining a declared
candidate. This design work need not wait for complete native field equations.

**Step5 has two levels.** We can compute received clock behavior on a supplied
geometry now, using the existing equations for clocks, lightlike paths and
transport. A broad search over evolving UDT geometries still needs specified
equations or explicitly conditional constraints. Observational fits do not
supply a native law automatically. Actual workloads need smoke, load and
checkpoint/restart checks before a long run.

give the entry gates, outputs and equations. The sole maintained account is
