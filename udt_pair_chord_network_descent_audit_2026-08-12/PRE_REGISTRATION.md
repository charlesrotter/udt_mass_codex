# Preregistration — zero-order pair-chord network descent

Date: 2026-08-12

Mode: metric-led, exact algebra, CPU only

## Whole question

Determine whether the exact zero-order A-terminal observer-pair chords already carry a coherent
three-observer composition law, without importing derivative evolution, a preferred path, an
action, or a physical branch selector.

The audit must keep two candidate structures separate:

1. the positive-semidefinite Gram relation `h_j-h_i>=0` inherited from the local
   base-plus-orchestra decomposition; and
2. the invertible calibrated endpoint transition reconstructed from the unique terminal coframe
   of each A-calibrated Lorentzian chord.

It must determine which structure has identities, composition, reversal, scalar reciprocal
descent, nontrivial loops, and middle-observer calibration requirements.

## Exact bounded arena

- one common oriented A-calibrated pair-domain covector basis;
- all real symmetric Lorentzian `2 x 2` terminal forms with `h00<0`;
- positive terminal densities `T,L` and arbitrary real `beta`;
- all ordered triples of such terminal states;
- all positive-semidefinite Gram increments between them when present;
- no coordinate freezing of `beta` and no diagonal-only reduction.

The analytic classification is load-bearing. A rational triple atlas must contain at least 400
distinct controls spanning identities, strict and rank-one Gram order, incomparable states,
nonzero shift, composable chains, reverse transitions, and attempted nontrivial directed loops.

## Premise classification

- `pinned-by-THEORY`: exact terminal decomposition of a supplied A-calibrated Lorentzian pair
  metric; the parent base-plus-`PSD(2)` theorem; ordinary matrix composition and congruence.
- `free-and-explored`: symbolic positive `T,L`, arbitrary `beta`, rational endpoint triples, Gram
  ranks, and relative endpoint ordering.
- `pinned-by-HABIT`: none.
- `OBSERVED`: `c_E` remains only the clock/ruler calibration anchor and cancels from the
  dimensionless network algebra.
- `CONDITIONAL`: existence of one common calibrated terminal-state family and literal equality of
  the middle state used by adjacent edges.
- `OPEN`: physical history, query, relation family, independent-chart solder, branch/path
  ownership, and the universal physical identification of a terminal family.

## Required derivations

1. Reconstruct the unique positive upper-triangular terminal coframe `B(T,L,beta)` from each
   regular chord.
2. Classify `R_ij=B_j B_i^-1`, including identity, inverse, exact composition, determinant,
   reciprocal/common-scale characters, and the correct nonlinear shear composition.
3. Classify the Gram/Loewner relation `h_i<=h_j`, including transitivity, antisymmetry, absence or
   existence of nontrivial inverses/loops, cumulative Gram rank, and compatibility with the exact
   reachability inequality.
4. Determine whether `Delta phi`, `Delta kappa`, and any shift coordinate descend and telescope on
   a common family.
5. Re-run the middle-observer test when the incoming and outgoing B calibrations are independently
   rebuilt; do not silently set their transition to identity.
6. State exactly whether chords are sufficient for conditional scalar/network composition and
   what remains unowned physically.

## Falsification and certification contract

The proposed chord-only closure fails if any of the following occurs:

- the terminal coframe is not uniquely reconstructed in the declared calibration;
- `R_BC R_AB != R_AC` for a matched triple;
- reversal fails for the calibrated transition;
- endpoint `Delta phi` fails to telescope on a common state family;
- the PSD relation is incorrectly promoted to an invertible groupoid;
- two PSD increments compose to a non-PSD increment;
- a nontrivial PSD-directed loop survives;
- an independently rebuilt middle state composes without an explicit equality or transition;
- an A-chart boundary, physical `X_max`, signal speed, history, or dynamics is inferred.

Certification requires exact symbolic proofs, an independent standard-library rational replay,
hostile controls for every listed category error, and a fresh adversarial review.
