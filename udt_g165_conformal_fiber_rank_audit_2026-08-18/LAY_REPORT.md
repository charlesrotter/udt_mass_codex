# G165 lay report

We asked whether the pieces already on the table secretly tell the whole UDT metric how large to be
from place to place.

They do not—at least not in the current banked equations.

The reciprocal kernel determines the **shape of the clock-versus-ruler imbalance** for a supplied
pair. Causality determines which directions are lightlike. Co-presence checks that many observer
relations belong to one coherent geometry. But all three can remain unchanged while the entire
metric is smoothly enlarged or shrunk by different amounts in different regions.

This is like knowing a musical score's intervals, harmonies, and timing while still being free to
turn the master volume knob differently in every room. A few calibration microphones cannot fix
an arbitrary volume field between them.

The good news is that we did not uncover another missing local kernel formula. The remaining issue
is much narrower: does UDT possess a native way to carry relative scale through the whole
co-present network? If that carry reduces the freedom to one overall volume setting, one additional
physical anchor could calibrate it. That possibility remains open.
