# CGE1 lay return

We constructed and checked a restricted conditional model in which one geometry
supports orbiting clocks and predicts their received shifts and sky directions.
The geometry and clock motion are consistent; we did not attach an independent
redshift-distance curve. This supplies a concrete candidate for the astronomical
interface, although it is not a complete model of the observed maser galaxy.

The distinction matters: this is the familiar mass-plus-Lambda exterior inside
the equation class already retained conditionally in the repository. We have
not derived that class from UDT's founding postulates or shown an extra prediction
beyond GR. Ordinary clocks and causality remain intact. No central singularity
is used as a reference clock, and no cosmic center or X_max is introduced.

The construction exposes a useful limitation precisely. The same one redshift
can result from different curvature parameters when the receiver's allowed
motion/preparation changes. Full angular and time records can contain additional
information; this result does not say they are all degenerate. A fitted systemic
redshift alone cannot select the geometry or isolate UDT's positional effect.

Original-equation checks and finite actual-arrival tests pass. A wrong extra
division in the initial checking code was caught and preserved, then corrected
without changing the candidate or tolerances. The two fresh-context reviews and
their distinct checks establish the stated bounded review, not empirical proof.

The next substantive gate is physical discrimination: determine whether an
existing UDT commitment admits/restricts this conditional family or points to
another one, and define a physically matched comparison before claiming an
additional positional effect. More tuning of this family would not establish
that selection. Full source environment, image map and data reduction are later
astronomical requirements. No fit, large run or physical adoption is automatic.

The maintained scientific argument and next gate are in central R8CGE/R10/R18.
This fixed package records the derivation and review history, not a competing
maintained account.
