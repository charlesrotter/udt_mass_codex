## 1. Construct the metric inside the retained conditional branch

Use R2's supplied static spherical areal presentation, c_E=1 (time in length
units), f>0, r>0, with regular angular charts:

    g=-f(r)dt²+dr²/f(r)+r²(dtheta²+sin²theta dphi²).

Static/spherical symmetry is free-and-explored for one exterior environment,
not a universal center or a consequence of the founding postulates. R10 gives
Ric=Lambda g only on its full conditional response-class premises. With its
curvature convention direct connection contraction gives

    Ric_tt=f(f''/2+f'/r), Ric_rr=-(f''/2+f'/r)/f,
    Ric_theta theta=1-f-rf', Ric_phi phi=sin²theta(1-f-rf'),

and zero other components. The angular equation integrates (rf)'=1-Lambda r²;
the remaining equations then hold for

    f=1-2m/r-Lambda r²/3.                         (1)

m is a length integration parameter, Lambda an inverse-length-squared constant.
m>0 is the explored mass-like branch, not a matter derivation. Identifying
m=G_obs M/c_E² is a conventional external-source calibration if used; it is not
needed here. The excluded interior has not been constructed. Test clocks have
negligible backreaction; a disk's material physics/self-gravity is not supplied.
For f>0, phi_presentation=-log(f)/2 realizes the R2 radial chart. It is not
the received clock depth, nor is its coordinate divergence a physical X_max.

## 2. Actual orbiting clocks and a regular receiver

At fixed r=a, theta=pi/2 choose an oriented circular test geodesic:

    h=1-3m/a>0, Omega²=m/a³-Lambda/3>0,
    u_e=h^(-1/2)(partial_t+Omega partial_phi),
    phi_e(t)=phi_0+Omega t, tau_e=sqrt(h)t.        (2)

Require f(a)>0. Both normalization and the radial geodesic equation give (2),
not a Newtonian orbit appended to another metric. Although h contains no Lambda,
Omega and existence do. With fixed angular momentum ell, the effective potential
V=f(1+ell²/r²) has ell²=a³ f'(a)/(2f(a)-a f'(a)); radial linear stability is
V''(a)>0 at that fixed ell. This is a test-particle radial criterion, not disk or
nonlinear metric stability. The original geodesic equations own verification.

For an outward radial free receiver use a fixed E>0 and initial event (0,R0,0):

    u_o=(E/f)partial_t+v partial_r,
    v=sqrt(E²-f)>0, dt_o/dr=E/(fv), d tau_o/dr=1/v. (3)

These are supplied query data; no cosmological observer population is selected.
All emitter and receiver clocks are regular finite-radius clocks. No fictitious
emitting clock at r=0 is used. A static receiver would be a different experiment.

## 3. One null incidence predicts timing, frequency and angle

On a direct outward equatorial branch normalize the positive Killing energy to
one (affine calibration), let b be its signed conserved angular momentum and
s(r,b)=sqrt(1-f b²/r²)>0. Then the affine tangent is

    k=(1/f, s, 0, b/r²).                         (4)

For a source emission t_e, solve simultaneously for reception radius R>R0 and b:

    t_e + integral_a^R dr/(f s) = integral_R0^R E dr/(f v),
    phi_0+Omega t_e + integral_a^R b dr/(r² s)=0. (5)

The winding and monotonic outward branch are specified; no turning/reflected ray
is included. Equations (2)-(5) specify actual same-metric histories/incidence.
The receiver proper arrival is integral_R0^R dr/v. For each nearby emission keep
the emitter/receiver histories fixed; b and R must change with the incidence.

Put A_o=(E-v_o s_o)/f_o>0. Endpoint contractions yield

    omega_e=(1-Omega b)/sqrt(h), omega_o=A_o,
    Z=(1-Omega b)/(sqrt(h) A_o).                 (6)

For proof from arrival rather than endpoint contraction, I=integral_a^R
dr/(r²s³)>0. Differentiating the two incidence equations with respect to (R,b)
gives determinant -I A_o/v_o<0. Their t_e derivative gives
R'=v_o(1-Omega b)/A_o; dividing tau_o'=R'/v_o by sqrt(h) gives (6).
This proves regular local dependence and equality to the actual arrival ratio,
not global uniqueness across other branches. Finite numerical examples check it.

The receiver's unit radial and azimuthal tetrad vectors are
e_r=(v_o/f_o,E,0,0), e_phi=(0,0,0,1/R), with e_theta=(0,0,1/R,0).
The propagation direction components are

    n_r=(E s_o-v_o)/(E-v_o s_o),
    n_phi=f_o b/[R(E-v_o s_o)], n_theta=0.        (7)

Their squared norm is one; the observed source sky direction is -n. This gives
the actual finite angular position for each equatorial spot. It is not the full
two-dimensional disk Jacobi map or a scalar angular-diameter distance. General
inclination/warping and ACP1's finite observation reduction still require work.

With a stable supplied transition cadence ACP1 gives v_opt=c_E(Z-1) and
d v_opt/d tau_o=c_E Z'/Z (prime is emitter proper time). No independent systemic
factor is appended. A finite monitoring-window fit must be generated from the

## 4. A precise remaining degeneracy, not a universal impossibility

For a radial ray b=0 emitted by the actual circular clock, (6) reduces to
w=Z sqrt(h)=E+v_o. At fixed a,R,m and any Lambda satisfying the above orbit/
domain conditions, a chosen w with w²>f_o yields a regular outward receiver:

    E=(w+f_o/w)/2, v_o=(w-f_o/w)/2.              (8)

E²-v_o²=f_o exactly. Thus an open interval of Lambda values can share one
radial spectral ratio by different allowed receiver histories. Their local
null connections exist; this does not hold the original R0/time preparation
fixed. It is a same-single-observable construction, not a claim that their full
angular/time data agree or an astronomical parameter-estimation theorem.
No single source-summary Z selects Lambda or supplies native positional attribution.

Ric(k,k)=Lambda g(k,k)=0 in this entire branch, but m generally gives nonzero
Weyl optical tides. Null Ricci cancellation does not mean a flat angular map,
absence of lensing, or Lambda-independent clock/angle records. Likewise changing
Lambda changes orbit Omega² by -Lambda/3: relative to its same-a,m zero-Lambda
value the exact fractional change is -Lambda a³/(3m). Weak metric corrections
alone do not bound that fractional orbital change. No empirical recovery is claimed.

## 5. Meaning and limits of the advance

This explicitly constructs a source-clock/null comparison in an existing
conditional equation class, joining metric, orbital motion, received ticking
and finite sky direction. It extends the massless supplied examples toward the
astronomical interface without using a fitted Z(D). It is not a full CGCG model,
native field-equation derivation, new theory of light/matter, empirical success,
large-distance asymptote or demonstration of an effect additional to matched GR.
Identical geometry and physically matched queries in GR give identical records.

The conditional response-class admission, sign/scale, real source environment,
receiver preparation and source/reference/data reduction remain distinct. The
explicit family and equations identify where to test the next physical connection;
they do not prove the whole UDT postulate set underdetermined or require a new one.
Stationary-clock exclusion survives because (2)-(3) are not two static clocks.
Historical controls remain controls; no other conditional branch is ruled out.
