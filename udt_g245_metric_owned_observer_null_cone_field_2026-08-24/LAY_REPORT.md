# G245 lay report

The earlier result said: if we hand the metric a sheet of light paths, it tells us exactly how that
sheet changes area and shape.

G245 removes that handoff locally. Give the metric one observer—where they are and which direction
their clock points—and the metric itself draws every possible local light direction leaving that
observer. The same calculation automatically supplies the area and distortion field found in
G244. Nothing is bolted on afterward, and no angular tuning knob is added.

The easiest picture is a flashlight shining in every direction at once. The metric owns how the
whole expanding shell stretches and squashes. It does not yet say which distant galaxies occupy
which beams, which global route survives after rays cross, or which complete evolving universe is
physical. Those are later questions, not defects in this local derivation.
