# G188 lay report

G187 showed that the spherical UDT metric could carry a tiny image patch along one angled light
route. G188 shows the simpler general rule underneath it.

Once the complete metric and one route are supplied, the metric itself determines how the two
directions across that image patch are carried and bent. In a spherical setting those two
directions separate cleanly. In a general setting they can feed into one another, so the response
is a two-by-two matrix rather than two isolated scalars.

An exact test with genuine coframe mixing produces a nonzero cross-image response. Removing the
mixing returns ordinary flat spreading. No extra angular strength or orchestra coefficient was
added.

This means more of the music is now emerging from the metric. It still does not tell us which
light routes are populated or how image area becomes measured brightness; those remain separate
physical interfaces.
