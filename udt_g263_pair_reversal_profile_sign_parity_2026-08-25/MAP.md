\[
ds^2=-e^{-2\phi(r)}c_E^2dt^2+e^{+2\phi(r)}dr^2+r^2d\Omega^2,

1. `R_pair`: exchange the two endpoints of one supplied consistently calibrated observer-pair

regular branch `f=exp(-2 phi)>0`; it does not select a profile.
