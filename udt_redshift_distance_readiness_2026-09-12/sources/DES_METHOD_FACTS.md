# Bounded DES comparator facts

Source: White et al., arXiv:2406.05050v2; exact version/cache in DES_SOURCE.json.
These are paraphrased published-method facts, not our empirical results.

- Introduction: the first method searches the time-axis exponent by scatter;
  the second builds its reference using the usual dilation and is explicitly a
  consistency check.
- Sections2.1–2.2: matching emitted wavelength bands and source-population width
  variation matter; luminosity-related selection can affect width comparisons.
- Section3: SALT3 is used to locate/normalize peak flux and time, not wholly absent.
- Abstract: the quoted precise exponent comes from the second method; its
  statistical/systematic errors are not hard instrument-error bounds.

The source supports a complementary duration question. This checkpoint supplies
neither an independent reanalysis nor a new UDT observational result.
