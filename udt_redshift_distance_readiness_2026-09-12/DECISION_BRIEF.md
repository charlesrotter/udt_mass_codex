Two useful connections are now explicit. First, the general beam-area geometry,
combined with declared conventional light assumptions, yields the familiar relation

Second, the metric determines how corresponding emission and arrival intervals
relate on a supplied light path. For a whole event, the stretching accumulates
over its duration. Using one instantaneous redshift for the whole event needs a
justified error treatment; bounding its variation is one sufficient route.
An actual flat-space example and the reviewer's separate
time-dependent geometric example expose that distinction without invoking a
cosmological mechanism. Physical supernova widths additionally need source,
wavelength, selection and propagation support.

that distinction and did not reanalyse its data or turn a published result into
fresh UDT confirmation.
