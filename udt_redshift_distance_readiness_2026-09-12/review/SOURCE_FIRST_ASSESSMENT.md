### Orientation and clock duration

Let `e` emit and `o` receive on a smooth unique regular future null correspondence.
Define positive measured geometric frequency `omega=-g(k,U)` and
`Z=omega_e/omega_o`. Differentiating null incidence gives
`d tau_o/d tau_e=Z`. Thus G220/G402's oriented depth on the same causal edge is
`delta_eo=log(omega_o/omega_e)=-log Z`. A catalog convention writing `Z=exp(Delta phi)`
uses the opposite depth difference for this edge. A synthesis must never silently
identify these signs merely because both source packages use the word redshift.

For any matched finite source interval `[a,b]` along that same regular branch,

`Delta tau_o = integral_a^b Z(tau_e) d tau_e`.

Constant `Z=Z_*` gives exact interval dilation `Delta tau_o=Z_* Delta tau_e`.
If `sup |Z/Z_*-1|<=epsilon` throughout the interval, the relative error of that
constant approximation is at most epsilon, by integrating the pointwise bound.
No measured numerical epsilon is supplied by the sources.

This integral cannot in general be replaced by the spectroscopic value at one
epoch. An exact supplied conformal metric `g=eta^2(-deta^2+dx^2+dy^2+dz^2)`, eta>0,
