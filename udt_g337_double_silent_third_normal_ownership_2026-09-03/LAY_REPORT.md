# G337 lay report

G336 found special directions that were flat through the first two instants of response. G337 asks
whether the already adopted metric equation knows what happens next.

It does—provided we retain the complete starting geometry around the point. The third response is
fixed by the metric, its initial bending, and how those fields vary through the immediate
neighborhood. No new force, observational fit, or extra rule is needed.

But five numbers written at one point are not enough. We built two exact lawful starting
geometries with the same five local labels and the same first two silent responses. Their nearby
curvature changes differently, so their third responses differ. In plain language: a point and
its immediate surroundings are not interchangeable. The full initial fields contain the needed
information; the short pointwise summary does not.

On the simplest homogeneous control the answer becomes especially clear: the third response is
`8 × branch value × direction mix`. It is nonzero and reverses sign between the two lawful
branches. Thus “silent twice” does not mean permanently silent.

This is useful base work for evolving a candidate UDT spacetime. It does not choose which initial
geometry Nature occupies, set a scale, prove stability, or complete a universe. It also does not
alter the UDT metric or reciprocal kernel. It tells us exactly which already-present initial data
the next local response needs.
