# MAP / PONDER — what “different instrument volumes by regime” can mean

The complete pair surface has two complementary descriptions:

1. its intrinsic two-metric, already encoded by common scale `kappa`, reciprocal imbalance `phi`,
   and tilt `beta`; and
2. how its oriented tangent plane sits across the reciprocal and angular sectors of the complete
   coframe.

The second question is the new one. Its natural raw object is not an invented mixing coefficient.
It is the area element `V_0 wedge V_1`. Its six components say how much oriented pair-area lies in
the reciprocal plane, the angular plane, and the four mixed planes.

Because the metric is Lorentzian, these contributions are signed. They may reinforce or cancel.
Calling their absolute magnitudes “importance” would be a diagnostic choice, not automatically a
physical law. The audit therefore maps the exact signed orchestra first and asks only afterward
whether any positive weighting is actually forced.

No sector will be called microscopic, terrestrial, cosmological, particle, force, or background in
this bounded atlas. Those interpretations require an independently owned branch and scale map.
