# Completeness map

## Included

- arbitrary local time-live `4 x 2` pair Jacobian;
- all clock, ruler, angular, shift, and mixing entries;
- exact induced pair metric;
- simple-bivector sector decomposition;
- split-preserving frame covariance;
- regular Lorentzian, null, rank-loss, and split-loss algebraic strata;
- exact and sampled CPU checks.

## Not included

- selection of the physical pair surface or path;
- evolution equations for the Jacobian;
- global chart compatibility, cut loci, or boundary completion;
- action, matter source, carrier, bootstrap density selection, or `X_max` value;
- empirical fitting or physical interpretation of dominance regions;
- a claim that the conditional R17 split is universal;
- GPU work.

This bounded scope is complete only for pointwise algebraic sector-volume classification relative to
a correctly typed `2+2` split.
