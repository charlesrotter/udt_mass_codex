## What the calculation clarified

separation and the positional geometry determine how far apart the ticks arrive.
The existing mathematics can calculate that rate once the geometry and clock
paths are supplied.

This pass makes the connection explicit in one metric: its clock calibration,
the observers' motion and the change in the ray's time-per-distance relation
together determine received ticking. We checked that calculation against a
separate null-geodesic calculation and directly timed neighboring pulses.
The division into these pieces is a calculation convention; it does not prove
that Nature supplies three independent factors.

A reciprocal geometry can accommodate mutual received redshift. But the example

There is also a conditional nearby limit: for a smoothly matched observer family

This does not predict when an instrument could detect it, or show that fixed
relative-velocity shifts vanish with separation. No solar-distance cutoff follows.
