# G320 lay report

G319 found that the constraint equations allow many smooth spatial shapes. G320 asked whether those
were real differences or merely different labels for the same shape.

The answer, inside the narrow G319 test setting, is: **some of the freedom is definitely real.**

We compared shapes that use exactly the same range of `psi` values and have exactly the same total
volume. One shape makes a single wave around the torus; the others make two, three, or four waves.
Their coordinate-independent, ruler-independent total curvature changed in the exact pattern
`1, 4, 9, 16`. No change of coordinates or conformal bookkeeping can alter that number. Therefore
these are genuinely different possible initial geometries, not duplicate descriptions.

The safety controls worked too. Sliding a wave around the torus or reflecting it produced the same
geometry, as it should. Rewriting the same physical data with a visibly different conformal seed
also produced the same metric and extrinsic curvature. Both sign branches continued to satisfy the
constraint equations.

What this does **not** do is choose one universe. It proves that the admitted initial-data space has
real breadth after the obvious bookkeeping freedom is removed. It does not classify every possible
shape, evolve them through time, or say which one Nature occupies. The metric and reciprocal kernel
were not changed.
