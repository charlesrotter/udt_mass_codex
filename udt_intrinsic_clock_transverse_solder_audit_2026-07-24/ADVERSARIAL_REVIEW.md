# Adversarial review

## Hodge overclaim

Hodge duality is parallel and maps longitudinal area to screen area, but
both reciprocal and rotational plane-area characters are trivial. It loses
`delta` and has the wrong target bundle for Jacobi phase transport.

## Preferred-direction overclaim

A component map can always be written after choosing a screen basis. The
independent endpoint `SO(2)` gauge test forces a basis-free linear map to
zero unless another geometric reduction selects a screen line or matched
representation.

## Generator overclaim

`K=-a^2` is an exact pointwise natural-frame similarity condition after a
parallel tidal-invariant line has been supplied. It is not a new field
equation, and it does not make an arbitrary path-dependent basis intrinsic.

## WR-L overclaim

`R=X(1-Q^-2)` is a genuine exact scalar profile identity. A scalar
relationship between two readouts does not intertwine their complete
transport representations. WR-L's positive radial screen curvature fails
the pointwise generator condition.

## `dphi` overclaim

The field-assisted `3+3` split lives in spacetime two-forms. It does not
select one rank-two phase subbundle, and it degenerates at null/zero strata.
Kato transport preserves a supplied moving reduction but does not choose a
physical one.

## Branch overclaim

B19 completeness and WR-L dilation cannot be combined. No registered branch
has both a complete arbitrary-observer geometry and a nontrivial intrinsic
clock/transverse solder.

## Disposition

The exact area duality, invariant-line criterion, negative-curvature
generator condition, and WR-L scalar relation survive. An intrinsic
irreducible solder remains open, while the reducible same-path direct sum
remains the strongest current assembly.
