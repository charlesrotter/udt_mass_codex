# Lay report — what the solder audit learned

We now know more precisely which piece is missing.

Imagine UDT already gave us a perfectly designed two-sided ruler: one side measures clock change
and the other measures distance change. The reciprocal-flag work showed exactly how to read that
ruler if someone hands it to us in a physical comparison.

This audit asked: **does the ordinary local metric automatically put that ruler between two
observers?**

The answer is no—not by the obvious local routes.

- The metric can tell us which directions are “clock” and “toward the other observer” on a regular
  branch.
- It can transport those directions perfectly.
- But that transport preserves their size. It therefore reports zero UDT positional dilation.

Trying to compare coordinate grids instead can produce the desired exponential change, but the
audit built a flat-space example where that apparent change is created entirely by rewriting the
coordinates. It is not physics.

The important positive clue is the stationary case. If the whole geometry supplies one common
time-flow direction—a Killing direction—its length can genuinely differ between two locations.
The ratio of those lengths gives an exact reciprocal clock comparison. That works because the
geometry supplied a shared, **unnormalized standard**. Two separately normalized local clocks do
not contain that information.

So the missing piece is not another clever way to move local frames. It is the rule by which a
complete UDT universe supplies a shared relational calibration state. That rule may be visible only
in a complete global branch, in boundary/global completion data, or eventually in dynamics.

In the lock metaphor: we have derived the shape of the keyway and can construct the moving guide
rails. The remaining tumbler is the common calibration standard that tells the two ends how much
relative dilation has accumulated.

This is progress because it tells us where **not** to keep drilling. The next search should examine
complete metric branches for intrinsic unnormalized lines or density states, not invent more local
connections.
