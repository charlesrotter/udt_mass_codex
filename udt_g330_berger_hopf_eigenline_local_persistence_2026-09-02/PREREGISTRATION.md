# G330 preregistration — Berger Hopf eigenline and local metric persistence

Date: 2026-09-02

## Frozen question

On the full positive non-round Berger-`S3`, pure-trace-data stratum admitted conditionally by G313,
does the spatial metric itself define a frame-gauge-independent Hopf line whose normalized
topological integral persists on a nonzero interval of the owner-provisional local Einstein
development?

This is a metric-led classification. It does not ask for a particle, stable lump, selected universe,
or confirmation of the historical Hopfion.

## Candidate outcomes

1. `BOUNDED_METRIC_NATIVE_HOPF_LINE_WITH_LOCAL_PERSISTENCE`: every `a!=c` Berger metric has a
   simple spatial-Ricci eigenline; its leaves form the Hopf circle fibration, its orientation-blind
   normalized helicity has magnitude one, and smooth symmetry-preserving local development retains
   the line and class on some nonzero interval.
2. `METRIC_NATIVE_LINE_WITH_EXTRA_TOPOLOGICAL_OR_DYNAMIC_PREMISE`: the line descends from the metric
   but its Hopf normalization or local carry needs a supplied carrier, frame, connection, or
   non-metric transport law.
3. `NO_METRIC_NATIVE_HOPF_LINE`: the apparent axis is degenerate, frame-dependent, non-Hopf, or fails
   immediately under the admitted local dynamics.

No outcome may be promoted beyond the bounded stratum.

## Exact gates

1. **Metric/Ricci gate.** Starting from `[X_i,X_j]=2 epsilon_ijk X_k` and
   `gamma=a^2(sigma_1^2+sigma_2^2)+c^2 sigma_3^2`, independently derive
   `lambda_h=4/a^2-2c^2/a^4`, `lambda_v=2c^2/a^4`, and verify that the gap is nonzero exactly when
   `a!=c`.
2. **G313 witness gate.** At `(a,c)=(1,3/2)`, recover `Ric=diag(-1/2,-1/2,9/2)` and scalar `7/2`.
3. **Gauge-descent gate.** Express the line through the Ricci spectral projector and verify covariance
   under arbitrary orthogonal frame changes. A raw component direction is not accepted.
4. **Hopf gate.** Prove the line leaves are closed circles, construct the period-normalized metric
   connection, and compute orientation-blind normalized helicity magnitude one for every `a,c>0`.
5. **Sign/scale controls.** Verify invariance under `V -> -V`, common metric homothety, and both
   orientations with only the signed representative changing as declared.
6. **Round control.** At `a=c`, require the Ricci projector to become undefined through eigenvalue
   degeneracy and recover G306's no-selected-member boundary.
7. **Local persistence gate.** Use only smoothness, the initial nonzero eigengap, and symmetry
   inheritance from conditional Cauchy uniqueness to prove existence of a nonzero time interval on
   which the metric-defined line and its integer class persist.
8. **Nonuniversality gate.** Exhibit at least one admitted current-arena history on which this
   metric-native line is absent. The response equation must not be called a universal Hopf selector.
9. **Provenance gate.** No historical carrier field, fixed target `S2`, `L2+L4` action, finite box,
   boundary collapse, source, matter, mass, observation, scale, or `X_max` may enter the derivation.

## Falsification contract

Outcome 1 is rejected if any exact gate 1--7 fails, if the line or integral changes under a local
frame rotation, or if persistence requires an independently supplied field/transport law. Any
failure is reported without replacing the metric or adding a mechanism. Outcome 1, if supported,
still fails as a universal UDT law if gate 8 succeeds, and it remains only a conditional bounded
geometry theorem.
