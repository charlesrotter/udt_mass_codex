# PSC1 lay decision packet

This checkpoint identifies one precise extra physical claim that could select
the quadratic equation inside a restricted family. It also shows why the weaker
claim does not select it. Neither is derived from the founding UDT interpretation.

ProposalS asks whether curvature disturbances have a common response scale,
visible in how they vary through space and time. That is a concrete geometric
question and does not require treating the disturbance as a matter particle.
The weak version fits multiple nonlinear equations. Our cubic-curvature control
shares the weak response but changes a higher-order clock term. The strong
version additionally demands the same linear response scale across different
background curvatures; within the assumed metric-f(R) family it selects a
quadratic function. Both the family restriction and that extra invariance are
new commitments. The owner's universal law does not imply them.

The backgrounds in that selection theorem generally lie in different integration-
constant sectors. They are not automatically available as different locations in
one universe. Source-supported environments would need a justified source model,
which remains absent and paused. Independent lengths, times, curvature readout,
mode preparation and error control are required before a practical test exists.

ProposalH relates geometry to energy/entropy balance on local causal horizons.
Its provenance is established GR/quantum thermodynamic work, not native UDT.
It needs quantum temperature, a physical heat/source tensor, entropy per area,
conservation and production. The entropy law already determines which equation
results. Choosing its curvature dependence to recover our candidate would merely
move the original choice into a new function. No independent UDT entropy
observable or supporting measurement has been supplied.

No-change option: keep the founding interpretation and current provisional
premises, retaining R² only as a labeled comparison. AlternativeS: separately
consider a geometric response premise, with explicit class/background costs and
possible falsifiers. AlternativeH: undertake a substantially broader entropy/
source-foundation program, requiring new authority and independent motivation.
Neither alternative is adopted by this checkpoint.

Recommendation: do not adopt constant-pole invariance now, and do not extend the
R² survey automatically. RetainS as a possible geometric diagnostic; stop treating
H as an independent selector of R². A useful next proposal would be a bounded
measurement-feasibility analysis: can an independently reconstructed curvature
response be isolated with existing ordinary clock/ruler/tidal observables, in a
declared preparation, without presupposing the candidate law? It must name actual
information requirements and sensitivity limits before any data or GPU campaign.
If that interface cannot be supplied, report that limit rather than adding an
invented source or repeatedly fitting another compatible curve.

Counterevidence/limits are concrete: one weak response admits nonlinear completions;
one scalar equation can pass while the full tensor fails; fixed-Lambda vacua do
not furnish the required nonzero-pole interval; and horizon balance does not choose
entropy density. These are bounded findings, not a proof that full UDT is
underdetermined or needs a new postulate. Initial conditions remain lawful state
freedom. Native geometry, source relation, scale and empirical recovery remain open.
