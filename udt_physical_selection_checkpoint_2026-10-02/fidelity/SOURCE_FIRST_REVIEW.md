## S: what a shared scalar-curvature scale does and does not select

In the diagnostic metric-f(R) class, the original tensor trace is

    3 Box F + F R - 2 f + 4 Lambda = 0, F=df/dR.

The established metric formalism and source-coupling convention are documented
in [De Felice–Tsujikawa, section 2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1002.4928v2).
Our no-source use is conditional; this reference supplies no UDT authorization.

For f=R+alpha R² and Lambda=0, a first variation about Minkowski gives
(Box-m²)delta R=0, m²=1/(6alpha). On alpha>0, a plane wave has
omega²=|k|²+m² and the static spherical decaying branch has
delta R=A exp(-m r)/r. Thus the measured spatial decay length and temporal
frequency could share m in independently calibrated geometric units. The
decaying branch, spherical static sector, flat linear regime and disturbance
preparation are additional diagnostic restrictions. The mode equation is not a
full metric tensor law, and positive m² does not prove nonlinear stability.

A mathematical control is f=R+alpha R²+beta R³, beta free. Exact trace:

    -R+6alpha Box R + beta[R³+18R Box R+18(grad R)²]+4Lambda=0.

Its flat first variation and full first-order tensor response are independent
of beta. Indeed the additional tensor is
beta[3R² Ric-R³g/2+3(g Box-Hess)R²], which starts at second order about flat.
Therefore S's weak near-flat shared scale can constrain alpha within the stated
class without selecting its nonlinear completion. This control is not a third
physical proposal or a viable-UDT claim.

The stronger version mentioned by parent before the source-first freeze asks
for the **same pole across an open interval of constant-curvature backgrounds**.
It is a genuinely stronger UNADOPTED physical premise within S. For an actual
constant-R0 solution and fixed Lambda under each perturbation, linearizing the
trace gives

    [Box_0 - M²(R0)]delta R=0,
    M²(R0)=[F(R0)-R0 F'(R0)]/[3F'(R0)], F'(R0)!=0.

Metric variation of Box acting on the constant F0 vanishes. If M²=m² is exactly
constant on an open interval where F'!=0 and R+3m²!=0, then
(R+3m²)F'=F; integrating yields F=C(R+3m²) and
f=(C/2)R²+3Cm²R+D. With F(0)=1, m²!=0 this is
f=R+R²/(6m²)+D on that interval. This is a restricted conditional selection,
not unique response normalization or a derivation of the diagnostic class.
Finite approximate measurements cannot establish an exact open-interval premise.

Crucial background caveat: one fixed-Lambda vacuum theory generally does not
provide that open family of constant-curvature solutions. Its roots obey
R F-2f+4Lambda=0. If every R in an interval is a root, differentiation forces
R F'-F=0, hence M²=0 wherever F'!=0. Nonzero constant mass across an interval
therefore needs distinct Lambda integration sectors, an explicitly specified
source-supported family, or an openly off-shell comparison. The first is
mathematically allowed in the DDR completion but is not an experimental knob
or ordinary spatial environmental variation in one fixed sector. Source support
would require additional authority; none is supplied here.

For the cubic control,

    M²(R0)=(1-3beta R0²)/(6alpha+18beta R0),
    dM²/dR0 at 0 = -beta/(2alpha²).

Its nonconstant response scale does not violate a universal law: the same f
operates at every R0. Equating universality of law with constant linearized
response across circumstances would be the defective physical inference.

## H: independent local calculation and premise ledger

[Jacobson 1995](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/9504004) assumes area entropy,
quantum acceleration temperature, matter boost-energy heat flux and local
equilibrium for all null directions; conservation fixes the trace up to a
constant. This is a physical package, not a consequence of metric kinematics.
The curvature-entropy extension suggested there is explicitly corrected in
[Eling–Guedens–Jacobson 2006, equations 9–21 and note 12](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0602001).

The following algebra was independently reconstructed in this review. Write
S=s integral F(R)dA, with constant s>0, future affine generator k, endpoint
lambda=0, past segment lambda<0, and shear zero at the endpoint. A prime below
means affine derivative, not derivative with respect to R. Since A'=theta A,
the entropy integrand is F theta+F'. Heat with chi=-kappa lambda k and
T=hbar kappa/(2pi) starts at order lambda, so matching requires

    theta0=-F'/F, F!=0.

Using theta'=-theta²/2-Rkk at the endpoint gives directly

    (F theta+F')'_0 = -F Rkk+F''-(3/2)(F')²/F.

This is the coefficient of lambda in the local entropy integrand. It is also
the second derivative of F A divided by endpoint area, because the first
derivative vanishes there. With local shear retained there is additionally
-F sigma²; this calculation deliberately sets it to zero, not to a universal
property of all horizons.

Without production, null matching requires the tensor term

    F Ric-Hess F+(3/2)F^{-1}dF tensor dF + Psi g.

The first two terms have divergence d(f/2-Box F), for df/dR=F. In contrast,
the last explicit gradient tensor generally has non-gradient divergence.
Let X=(grad F)². Its divergence is
(3/2)[(Box F/F-X/F²)dF+dX/(2F)]. Its exterior derivative equals
(3/2)[d(Box F)/F-dX/(2F²)] wedge dF and need not vanish.

This is not merely an arbitrary-scalar witness: on the smooth local Lorentz
metric g=exp(2w)diag(-1,1,1,1), w=x²y+x⁵, take F=1+2R from the actual metric
curvature. At the origin R=0, F=1, R_y=-12, (Box F)_x=-1440 and
partial_x(div T)_y-partial_y(div T)_x=51840 for
T=(3/2)F^{-1}dF tensor dF. Since F=1 there, F remains positive on some small
neighborhood. Thus no scalar Psi can absorb this obstruction as an identity
over that metric domain. This off-shell local witness is not a physical vacuum
solution and does not claim equilibrium impossibility on every special metric.

Add the specifically prescribed entropy-production integrand

    d_i S/(d lambda dA) = -lambda (3s/2)(F')²/F.

For lambda<0, s>0 and F>0 it is nonnegative to the retained local order.
Subtracting production from the entropy change cancels the obstruction and
leaves F Rkk-Hess(F)kk proportional to Tkk. If this holds for all null
directions, the symmetric tensors differ by a multiple of g; with conserved
T, integration gives

    F Ric-(f/2)g+(g Box-Hess)F+Lambda g = [2pi/(hbar s)] T.

This is leading local horizon balance leading to a pointwise conditional
equation, not exact finite-horizon thermodynamics. Fixed F=1 gives Einstein;
chosen F=1+2alpha R gives the R² comparison; other F give other responses.
The balance and this cancellation therefore do not independently select F.
F>0 is needed for this entropy/production interpretation, although the original
conditional metric equation is not thereby forbidden on all F=0 strata.

The added physical costs are separate: quantum temperature and its vacuum/state
assumptions; a physically identified conserved heat/source tensor; horizon
entropy with its density s F and a specified microscopic or measured meaning;
endpoint entropy stationarity; a universal local balance; and the specified
production law. The 2006 construction also acknowledges higher-local-order
ambiguity in approximate boost fields. No physical entropy, heat source, value

## Distinguishing tests and feasibility

1. **S weak cross-regime test.** Independently reconstruct metric curvature from
   adequate clock/ruler/tidal data; one clock ratio alone does not determine
   general4D R. Prepare small disturbances with independent lengths, wavevectors
   and proper times, and separate source/boundary influence. Compare static
   decay m with temporal dispersion and independently held-out RCD1 clock
   curves. A single generated curve or alpha refitted for each regime is not
   confirmation. Identifiability, error control, derivative noise and preparation
   feasibility remain untested; this does not distinguish beta at linear order.
2. **S stronger/background or nonlinear discriminator.** If separately justified
   admissible backgrounds exist, measure M²(R0) independently; the cubic control
   predicts the displayed slope. Alternatively reconstruct original nonlinear
   metric residuals sensitive to beta on varying-curvature data. The constant-
   Lambda issue prevents presenting arbitrary R0 as freely available vacua.
   These designs can falsify restricted comparisons; finite data do not prove
   global functional uniqueness or the full tensor law from its scalar trace.
3. **H independent test.** Measure or derive horizon entropy/state, temperature,
   heat and production independently, then compare entropy density across
   curvature. Computing entropy from F chosen to match the desired equation
   and feeding it back into balance is circular evidence for selection. No such
   independent interface exists in this checkpoint. The immediate useful return
   is the explicit premise bill, not an observational claim.

Recommended usefulness: retain S as a sharply testable conditional comparison
and possible explicit premise for later consideration. Its stronger version is
mathematically selective only with its additional class and background-family
assumptions. H clarifies how a chosen entropy law corresponds to an equation,
but should not be treated as an independent R² selector at this checkpoint.
