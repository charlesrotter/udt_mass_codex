## Argument and attempted breaks

1. **Near-flat S does not select the nonlinear equation.** The permitted cubic
   completion leaves every original flat linear tensor component unchanged and
   has the same scalar dispersion/static scale. The full-tensor conformal mode
   and exterior checks in my sealed report guard against treating a scalar trace
   equation as sufficient. The candidate explicitly retains that distinction and
   its R=0, E00!=0 control. A source-free region and its static mode do not derive
   a matter source or boundary amplitude. Positive scalar mass is correctly
   bounded to a linear property. The actual local clock solutions establish
   inequivalence beyond linear order; this is more than comparing formal traces.

2. **The quartic and quintic coefficients survive.** The original geometric
   E00/Eii formulas, initial constraint, Cdot=-4HC and -C+3D=0 justify the local
   full homogeneous solution. The needed f'' is nonzero at the initially flat
   event because alpha!=0; no H division occurs. P0!=0 is required for the stated
   coefficient ratios. The same initial a,H,R,P is admissible in both models,
   but b4=-beta P0²/(48alpha) and the beta² correction to b5 differ. Arrival-time
   conversion affects these coefficients only above order5. Dimensional powers
   and the beta=0 RCD1 limit are correct. The result is local, with neither a
   practical estimator nor a full-history theorem inferred from finitely many
   coefficients.

3. **Strong S has a real conditional selection theorem and a large premise
   cost.** On the stated smooth regular interval, the scalar pole equation
   (R+3mu²)F_R=F yields the displayed quadratic f after F(0)=1. R+3mu²!=0,
   F_R!=0 and normalization imply F!=0 on the connected branch. The additive
   constant remains free. The conclusion holds on the interval, within f(R),
   and does not choose a unique physical response under RCD1's equivalence.
   Most importantly, the candidate correctly fixes deltaLambda=0 in each
   perturbation while allowing Lambda to differ between backgrounds. Holding
   Lambda fixed over an open interval instead forces M²=0. This prevents the
   theorem from being misdescribed as an immediately available multi-environment
   experiment in one universe. Changing matter environments requires a source
   law and background analysis absent here. No owner universal-law statement is
   strengthened into a universal pole requirement.

4. **H exposes its required physical inputs.** The local Raychaudhuri coefficient
   q'=F''-F Ric(k,k)-3(F')²/(2F) is correct at q=0 and zero shear. With s0>0,
   F>0 and a past affine segment lambda<0, the stated production is nonnegative
   and removes the extra gradient term. This local construction does not extend
   to arbitrary finite horizons. Once the all-null relation and conserved flux
   are stipulated, the completion F=df/dR gives E_f up to a cosmological term.
   Supplied F, source flux, temperature, conservation, normalization and production
   are explicit new interfaces; the geometric metric alone does not establish
   their physical meaning. Constant and nonlinear F controls demonstrate that
   the horizon principle by itself does not select the target entropy function.

The stated direction judgment is reasonable inside this budget: S requires fewer
new physical interfaces than H, but even its first experimental step lacks a
demonstrated independent mode preparation and sensitivity. Neither proposal is
ready for physical adoption. This is a scoped usefulness judgment, not proof
that every other native route fails or that a new postulate is necessary.
