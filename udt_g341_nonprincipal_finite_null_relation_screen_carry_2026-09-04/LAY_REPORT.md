# G341 lay report — slanted finite rays and screen carry

G340 showed that the metric itself could connect separated observers with exact light-cone routes,
but it solved the straight-axis cases most completely. G341 fills in the slanted routes on that
same spacetime.

For each chosen way of unwrapping the compact space, a departure event and a destination determine
one slanted future route and one arrival time. Neighboring routes do not fold together into a
caustic before arrival. The compact space can still provide several routes because it can be wound
around in different ways; those are separate labelled routes, not ambiguity in one route.

The receiving observer's full comparison plane generally does not line up with the plane carried
from the sender. That mismatch is exactly determined by the metric and is nonzero for every truly
slanted route. At the same time, the two-dimensional screen carried perpendicular to the route has
no intrinsic rotation in its proper null quotient. Those statements are compatible: one concerns
the full clock-and-ray plane, while the other concerns screen directions after ignoring a harmless
component along the null ray itself.

There is also one slanted direction whose endpoint frequencies are equal. Its reciprocal depth is
zero, but its screen mismatch is not. In plain terms, a quiet redshift channel does not mean the
whole observer relation is quiet.

This is still one exact conditional spacetime. It does not say which route Nature populates, add a
model of light emission or brightness, choose a physical distance convention, set a scale or
`X_max`, establish stability, or select our universe. The metric, reciprocal kernel, angular
sector, and provisional field equation were not modified.
