# G341 completeness map

## Included in this one tile

1. **Fields:** the complete supplied four-metric, null tangent, endpoint normal clocks, and two
   screen directions needed by the directed relation state.
2. **Action terms:** none selected or varied; G341 evaluates the already supplied metric.
3. **Equations:** full nonlinear null-geodesic Hamiltonian and full Levi-Civita vector transport.
4. **Domain:** all `T_r>T_e>0`, every nonzero spatial lift, every momentum direction, and both
   principal limits on the universal cover.
5. **Boundary/regularity:** cone vertex, `T=0` singular boundary, zero momentum, projective-axis
   chart boundaries, endpoint rank, and compact cut/branch crossings are separately typed.
6. **Topology:** every supplied compact-lattice winding is retained; no lattice is selected.
7. **Dynamics:** exact propagation along finite future-null legs on one explicit time-live metric.
8. **Branches:** per-lift endpoint branches and cross-lift ties are counted rather than filtered.
9. **Stability:** omitted; no persistence under metric perturbation is claimed.
10. **Regime:** one exact conditional Taub--Kasner spacetime and its fixed-label normal observers.

## Still excluded

Accelerated/nonnormal observers, generic G332 developments, metric perturbations, nonlinear
stability, Jacobi intensity/area beyond endpoint-rank classification, physical route population,
sources/detectors/transfer, matter/mass, observations, absolute scale, physical `X_max`, and canon.

Any positive result remains one-spacetime, supplied-query, branch-labelled, and conditional.
