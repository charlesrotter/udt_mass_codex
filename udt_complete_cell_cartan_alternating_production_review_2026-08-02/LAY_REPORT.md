# Lay report

The central geometric pattern survived the hostile review, but its interpretation became cleaner.

The metric's chosen ruler/screen split contains a real one-number contact pattern linking the
distance-dilation field to screen-area change. That pattern is reproducible on a complete test cell.
It is best understood as a way of **reading and reconstructing** information already present in the
geometry—not as the metric spontaneously writing a new law of physics.

The review also caught an important overstatement. The full curvature is not silent about the two
sectors: it contains many mixed terms. A specially aligned measurement of those terms is zero, but
that does not mean all mixing is absent. This makes the next audit more worthwhile: look at the full
two-dimensional screen without forcing it to be round or isotropic, then ask which patterns survive
honest changes of frame.
