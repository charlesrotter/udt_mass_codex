# Lay report

## What changed

We were asking for a formula that tells every instrument in the UDT metric how loudly to play as
the observer-pair separation regime changes. The whiteboard found that we already know how all the
instruments are physically assembled:

```text
complete metric frame E
        times
observer-pair realization J
        equals
the actual pair chord V
```

The terminal clock/ruler dilation is then read from that one chord. Nothing is bolted on afterward.

We can now also write exactly how the whole chord changes:

```text
change of V = E times (change of J + change carried by E)
```

That is the grammar of the orchestra. It keeps the metric instruments and the observer relation in
one equation and prevents us from confusing a change of coordinates with a physical change.

## What is still missing

The equation above does not yet say which performance Nature chooses. It accepts many lawful
histories because the motion of the observer relation is still free. In the metaphor, we have the
orchestra, its seating plan, and the rule saying how a musician's motion affects the combined sound.
We do not yet have the sheet music that fixes the performance.

## The best next move

The founding reciprocal law already assigns opposite exponential weights to clock and ruler. We
should first ask whether that same one-parameter law has any nontrivial, gauge-independent extension
to the complete coframe. This is a finite algebraic classification, not an observational fit.

If paired positive and negative weights survive the exact complete carry, a natural quiet-middle /
loud-ends envelope can arise as a consequence of reciprocity. If the weights cancel after the
observer realization is carried correctly, the apparent score was only a frame change and the idea
is falsified.

Only after that census should the surviving finite basis be connected to a metric-derived
Jacobi/Riccati propagation problem. Observational fitting can then estimate a small number of
remaining coefficients and test them on held-out windows. An unconstrained brute-force fit now
would find many equally good formulas and would not reveal the physical score.

## Honest landing

This is a substantial simplification and a linear next step. It is not yet the symphony formula.
It identifies exactly where such a formula would have to live and gives a cheap test that can either
produce a small native candidate family or kill the most tempting idea before a large solve.
