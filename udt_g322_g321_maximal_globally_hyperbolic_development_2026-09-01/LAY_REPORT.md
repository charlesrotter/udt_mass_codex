G321 showed that one complete lawful starting geometry has one local continuation. G322 now uses a
standard global mathematics theorem to extend that statement: each fixed starting geometry has one
largest causally predictable spacetime continuation, up to coordinate description.

That is meaningful progress. Inside this bounded arena, we are no longer missing a rule for how a
chosen starting geometry continues locally or throughout its globally predictable domain.

But “largest causally predictable continuation” does not mean eternal, nonsingular, stable, or
chosen by Nature. Different lawful starting geometries still produce different possible histories,
and UDT has not yet selected which one is physically occupied. The theorem is imported mathematics;
the UDT metric, reciprocal kernel, and angular behavior were not changed.
