# G132 preregistration — common-scale owner and observational-anchor audit

Date: 2026-08-16

## Whole question

G131 proved that the complete field of terminal reciprocal scalars on all known labelled
clock-ruler planes determines exactly the positive conformal class of a Lorentz metric. This audit
asks what that remaining conformal kernel means in UDT:

1. Is it a physical freedom already absent from the founded reciprocal comparison?
2. Is it information discarded only by the terminal `phi_pair` projection and retained by the
   complete pair metric?
3. Does an already-derived query-independent norm, area, volume, or areal datum fix it?
4. What can the observed anchors `c_E` and `G_obs` fix alone or with one additional observational
   mass-, density-, energy-, or curvature-bearing datum?

## Bounded regime

- pointwise regular Lorentz metrics and positive conformal rescalings;
- the founded dimension-matched reciprocal base with fixed pairing `K`;
- supplied calibrated regular pair metrics and their exact `(kappa_pair,phi_pair,beta_pair)`
  decomposition;
- the conditional central-spherical areal-radius branch;
- dimensional monomials made from `c_E`, `G_obs`, mass, mass density, and energy density;
- finitely many point anchors versus an arbitrary smooth positive conformal field.

No action, field equation, source equation, bootstrap law, fit, cosmological solution, `X_max`
value, matter model, or observational outcome will be derived.

## Candidate tests

1. **Fixed-pairing normalization.** For
   `P_A=A diag(exp(-phi),exp(+phi))`, compute `P_A^T K P_A`. Exact preservation of the same `K`
   must either force positive `A=1` or leave a survivor.
2. **Founded-base determinant.** Compute the determinant of the reciprocal base metric and test a
   positive common rescaling against the same calibrated reciprocal form.
3. **Terminal decomposition weight.** Under `h -> Omega^2 h`, compute the transformations of
   `kappa_pair`, `phi_pair`, and `beta_pair`; reconstruct `h` from the triplet.
4. **Network consequence.** Join exact triplet reconstruction to G129's rank-ten full-pullback
   theorem without assuming physical ownership of the query family.
5. **Areal datum.** On an identified spherical orbit, compute the conformal weight of orbit area
   and areal radius. Keep the result conditional on spherical symmetry and orbit identification.
6. **Volume datum.** Compute `vol_{Omega^2 g}` and decide whether metric volume is an independent
   owner or merely the missing full-metric datum restated.
7. **Dimensional anchors.** Solve the exponent equations for a length made from `(c_E,G_obs)`
   alone and after adding `M`, `rho`, or energy density `epsilon`.
8. **Finite-anchor obstruction.** Exhibit a smooth nonconstant positive `Omega` equal to one at
   two prescribed anchor points.

## Certification and falsification contract

The strongest positive conclusion allowed is a type-separated theorem identifying exactly where
common scale is already fixed or retained, and exactly where an external anchor or further law is
still required.

The audit is falsified or downgraded if any of the following occurs:

- exact fixed-`K` preservation admits positive `A != 1`;
- `(kappa_pair,phi_pair,beta_pair)` does not reconstruct every regular calibrated pair metric;
- `c_E` and `G_obs` alone generate a dimensionally valid length;
- a finite number of point normalizations uniquely fixes an arbitrary smooth `Omega(x)`;
- an areal or volume datum is promoted universally despite depending on a supplied branch, query,
  orientation, orbit structure, or full metric;
- a conditional observational calibration is called a derived UDT law.

## Maximum wording

No result may be called a selected universe, native action, source law, bootstrap closure,
`X_max`, or canon. Any conclusion about absolute scale must distinguish:

- founded reciprocal-base normalization;
- scale retained by the complete pair metric but discarded by `phi_pair`;
- conditional geometric area/volume scale;
- global or pointwise scale calibration from observations.
