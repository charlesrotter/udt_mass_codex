1. **Fields.** P4-03's 16 objects are not 16 physical fields: they mix varied fields,
   anchors, open forks, gauge, chart registration, boundaries, and completion labels.
   T1/A1 add shift and angular-mix rows at the posing level.  T2/A2 add response slots at

   `IF-ADOPTED`.  No package derives a final physical field census.
2. **Action terms.** P4-02 compares two conditional action equation sets.  P4-07 uses a
   reduced conditional action/ODE and integrated pairing; P4-08 uses the cell-energy
   first integral and conditional action-derived mass definitions; P4-16 varies the
   seam functional at `N=2`; P4-18 tests reduced and wall-germ Hessian sectors; and
   P4-20 tests an `IF-ADOPTED` theta-coupling menu.  These 26 exploded rows are tagged
   explicitly.  No action, coupling, boundary posture, or mass definition is selected;
   Helmholtz, boundary integrability, and complete action ownership remain open.
3. **Equations/operators.** Static pointwise algebra, Noether strata, reduced ODEs,
   first wall variation, real/circle periods, and reduced Hessian blocks are covered in
   their packages.  Whole-solution response equations, integration-layer slack identities,
   nonlinear time/angular on-shell systems, and T4 dynamics are absent.
4. **Domain.** Every result is tied to its registered chart, jet/order layer, comparison
   family, or smooth target class.  "Full cell," "all modes," and "full S3" are not a
   license to leave those domains.  P4-02's negative is equation-set inequivalence on a
   CHOSE stationary domain, not proof of no common solution anywhere.
5. **Boundary/corners.** Seam and wall packages reach the `N=2` first-germ layer.  Higher
   germs activate in the second variation and are unpinned.  Corners, `N=4`, and a complete
   global boundary action are typed or queued, not solved.
6. **Topology/completion.** Quotient, two-sided, open, cyclic, torus, registered two-cap
   `S3`, and the banked Hopf transition are distinguished.  No posture or completion is
   selected.  Exotic non-Hopf completions, singular configurations, mapping-torus time
   identifications, and the massive-carrier-to-two-cap-`S3` joins remain open.
7. **Dynamical character.** Most of the arc is map, posing, pointwise/off-shell response,
   topology, or kinematic period data.  Relaxation or cycle labels are not physical time.
   There is no time/angular-live massive on-shell solve and no T4 dynamics.
8. **Branches.** Constants/fields census, integrated/pointwise pairing, P1-4D/P1-triad,
   quotient/two-sided/open posture, lock coordinate/projected, spatial coordinate/projected,
   parity signs, topology forks, and registered/unregistered completion classes must travel.
   The cold review adopts none.
9. **Stability.** P4-18 computes reduced stationary second variations.  The dichotomy is a
   sector PSD criterion.  The absorption result closes the germ-independent zero-trace core,
   while free wall-germ curvature and the lambda-Schur block stop a full joint certificate.
   No dynamical or physical-particle stability follows.
10. **Regime/limits.** All results remain conditional on the declared stationary/time-live/
    angular-live layer, finite cells, regularity, jet bounds or all-finite-jet contraction,
    and exact premise stack.  No continuum-limit, singular-field, empirical, source,
    spectrum, carrier, mass, or canon conclusion is supplied by this review.
