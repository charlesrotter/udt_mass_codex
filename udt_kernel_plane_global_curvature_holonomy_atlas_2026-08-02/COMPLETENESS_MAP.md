## Whole-space rule

Every exact zero component, singular locus, nonzero region, failed saturation, numerical ambiguity,
loop value, sign, cancellation, and branch difference is retained. No solution is rejected because
it does not resemble a particle or because a holonomy is zero.

## Maximum conclusion

At most a verified or honestly incomplete global curvature/singularity and finite-loop atlas for
the declared family. Most of the ten-criterion theory space remains blank.
