# Lay report — what was blocking us conceptually

The clearest finding is that we had sometimes confused the **toolbox**, the **blueprint**, and the
**finished machine**.

The metric already contains Einsteinian `c`. That gives UDT a real clock-versus-distance calibration;
UDT is not currently a physically scale-free theory. But `c` is like knowing how many seconds go with
how many miles—it does not by itself tell you how large the universe is. `G` adds another conversion,
but we still need the universe's native mass/geometry relation to calculate a length such as `X_max`.

CSN was the largest confirmed wrong turn. It said that enlarging every clock and ruler together was
not physical. It was adopted after that assumption made one attractive action calculation close.
The calculation was honest **if CSN was assumed**, but it did not prove CSN. That assumption is now
inactive. We keep the mathematics as a conditional branch, but we no longer make the whole theory
pass through a scale-free stage.

Several other terms need equally strict labels:

- `phi` is the bookkeeping potential for a reciprocal relationship; the law assigning the
  relationship to real observer pairs is still missing.
- `X_max` is the place an observer-pair comparison approaches infinite dilation, not a wall around
  the universe.
- the complete coframe is the set of parts the metric allows us to assemble, not the rule saying
  which assembly nature uses.
- the angular sector is part of that geometry; it is not automatically the Hopfion's `S^2` carrier.
- a finite cell, a mirrored seam, and the boundary term needed for a variation are three different
  things.
- bootstrap is the demand that the whole universe and its local structures consistently determine
  one another; it is not yet a dial that optimizes density.
- a source is the role that drives a field equation; it need not be a separate cosmic substance.
- mass is probably an output of a completed, normalized solution; a mass-like geometric number is
  not yet physical mass.
- co-presence means events belong to the same complete universe. It does not mean they can exchange
  information instantly.

The most useful reframe is this: we may have been trying to choose the **rules for varying the
machine** before finding the **native rule the machine obeys**. Variation ownership may come with the
response law instead of being a separate missing object. Likewise, the action may be the compact
mathematical summary of a deeper global-local consistency rule, rather than the first selector.

That is not closure yet. But it narrows the next question to a genuine fork instead of another
open-ended hunt: does UDT first supply a global-local response whose action is downstream, or does it
first supply an action whose solutions realize bootstrap? We can now compare those two architectures
without CSN silently predetermining the answer.
