## The three most likely remaining conceptual blockers

### 1. Law and variation ownership are probably one joint

The project has sometimes asked kinematics to choose a variation domain before it has the physical
law whose variations are being defined. The current type atlas suggests a cleaner order: derive or
adopt the smallest metric-native global/local response relation, then ask whether it is variational
and what its legitimate tangents are. This is a reframing, not a derived response law.

### 2. Source and mass are downstream roles, not necessarily extra substances

The metric may ultimately make matter a stable globally admissible geometric response. If so, the
source is the response role and mass is a normalized global output of the selected solution. The
repository has conditional examples consistent with that picture, but no theorem. Treating either
as an input too early can create a false missing-substrate problem; assuming geometry must produce
them would be the opposite error.

### 3. Bootstrap needs one nonidentity return, not a tuning slogan

Global/local mutual admissibility is now cleanly typed. What is missing is not another inventory of
readouts, but one metric-owned relation that nontrivially joins complete global data to complete
local configurations and survives same-solution closure. Density, total mass, curvature, and
`X_max` may participate, but none may be installed as the objective in advance.
