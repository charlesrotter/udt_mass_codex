1. strong local CSN is called derived solely because a common factor cancels algebraically;
2. CSN's historical motivation is asserted without a cited source chain;
3. the original validation is called retired without an explicit current status or dependency test;
4. the presence of `c` in the founding metric is omitted, or is used to claim a complete absolute-scale
   closure that the sources do not derive;
5. an inactive premise is allowed to retain active blocking or selecting authority without disclosure;
6. `phi`, ordered depth, and their physical assignment are merged;
7. `X_max`, a finite-cell seal, and a variational boundary are merged;
8. a conditional carrier/stability or geometric mass-bearing result is promoted to native matter;
9. bootstrap is promoted from working mutual admissibility to a derived optimizer/equation;
10. a conceptual reclassification silently changes algebraic results that remain valid conditionally;
11. historical or frozen evidence is modified;
12. affected current claims are not enumerated with before/after status and controlling evidence.

Certification requires:

- a source inventory with hashes and roles;

- an independent standard-library verifier with exercised semantic mutation catches;
