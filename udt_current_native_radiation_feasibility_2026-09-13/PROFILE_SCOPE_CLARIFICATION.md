# NR1 C1 — controlling profile-scope clarification

This controls INITIAL_CANDIDATE section B where its wording suggests every
allowed q produces a varying field, and where the epsilon-family sentence could
be read as making every member nonconstant. The reviewer identified both issues
in direct review. Original candidate, freeze, code and outputs remain unchanged.

For general smooth positive q on the stated local patch, the two-form coefficient
2q' depends only on u; its derivative is 2q''. It varies where q'' is nonzero.
It is a nonzero null two-form exactly where q' is nonzero. Constant q gives zero.
A positive affine q on a suitable local patch gives a constant nonzero null
form when its slope is nonzero; it does not give a varying pulse. For example,
q=1+u on 0<u<1 gives A=0 and F=2du wedge dx.

For the illustrative family q=1+epsilon exp(-u²), epsilon>-1 ensures positivity.
It is nonconstant only when epsilon!=0. The specified epsilon=1/4 member is the
nonzero bipolar pulse-shaped example. Epsilon=0 gives the flat metric and F=0.
The original36 checks and their samples/equations are unchanged. This is a
bounded scientific wording/quantifier repair, not an extension of the construction,
a new physical premise or a claim that every admissible geometry radiates.
