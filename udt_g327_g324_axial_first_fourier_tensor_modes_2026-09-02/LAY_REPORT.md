# G327 lay report

We have now turned on the first genuine ripple across the compact G324 universe instead of letting
every point change in lockstep.

For a ripple traveling around the special axial direction, the metric permits the familiar two
independent sideways distortions. Neither is a coordinate illusion, and neither secretly forces
another part of the metric to switch on. Each ripple has two possible placements around the
circle and two independent time behaviors, giving eight real constants in this deliberately
bounded sector.

Toward the expanding future, both time behaviors oscillate faster while their size relative to the
background metric falls like `T^(-2/3)` in the declared first-order diagnostic. Toward the past
singular end, one remains finite and the other grows logarithmically. We kept both; no boundary
rule was imposed to select the nicer one.

This is a positive first inhomogeneous result, not proof that the whole spacetime is stable. Other
ripple directions, scalar and vector disturbances, higher modes, and nonlinear interactions have
not yet been classified. Nothing was added to or changed in the UDT metric, reciprocal kernel,
angular behavior, or adopted bounded equation.
