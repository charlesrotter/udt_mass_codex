# G284 lay report

The light-cone idea really does add shape—but in two stages.

`c_E` fixes the opening of the local light cone. That is like fixing the conversion between the
clock markings and ruler markings on the tape. Reciprocal `phi` then tells us the directed redshift
between the observers.

When we compare nearby light cones, their changing slope tells us how the tape bends sideways. That
sideways bending is exactly the tidal/angular matrix that focuses and shears neighboring light
paths. So the causal structure and orchestra are genuinely one metric object.

The remaining problem is that many differently bent tapes are internally consistent. The complete
light-cone network can reveal which bending was supplied, but ordinary causality does not yet say
which bending Nature must use.

This narrows the missing law. We are not looking for a separate mechanism attached to the kernel.
We are looking for a native rule connecting the redshift/longitudinal markings of the causal tape
to its transverse curvature. No formula for that rule has been adopted or fitted here.
