# Exact pairing control for the kernel derivative

This supplements INITIAL_CANDIDATE section2 before direct review. The reviewer
suggested the explicit nonzero pairing after its source-first seal and before
candidate exposure. The author adopted it as an additional mathematical check;
independence from that suggestion is not claimed.

For covariant metric variation h, the fixed-query kernel derivative is
delta Phi=<P,h>_g with P_ab=(1/2)U_a U_b. Its own reciprocal tangent is
H(U,n)=2(U_flat tensor U_flat+n_flat tensor n_flat), where U is unit timelike,
n unit spacelike and g(U,n)=0. Direct contraction gives

    <P,H(U,n)>_g = g(U,U)^2+g(U,n)^2 = 1.

For the inverse-metric coefficient -P the same metric pairing is -1. The signs
refer to different variation conventions; neither result is zero. Thus this
single-query first derivative cannot itself be a response satisfying all-pair
DDR on an eligible solution: its own reciprocal plane already fails the balance.
It is supposed to change when the clock/ruler shape changes. This rejects that
direct identification, not a possible further justified construction from full
metric data. No physical observer population, action or response is supplied.

The identity is pointwise and general for orthonormal U,n. A separate exact
rational-boost check supports the contraction/sign implementation; it is not a
new proof of native dynamics or a continuum physical observation.
