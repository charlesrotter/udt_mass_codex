# Lay result

The repository had not actually forgotten what `phi` is as broadly as our
discussion feared. Its current foundation already says that `phi` is the
amount of reciprocal clock/ruler imbalance: increasing it slows one member
of the pair by exactly the factor that it enlarges the other. Combining two
such comparisons adds their `phi` values.

What had become muddled was the next step. Knowing how this pair behaves does
not yet say how the other two directions of a four-dimensional frame must
join it. The simplest possibility is to leave those directions alone. That
works exactly, but it is not the only possibility. The angular directions can
also dilate reciprocally, and cross-links can grow between the clock/ruler
pair and the angular pair, while preserving the same founded two-channel
rule.

So the honest picture is:

- `phi` is not a random placeholder;
- its clock/ruler action is already derived in the stated founding scope;
- its physical profile and observer-pair assignment are still open;
- its unique extension through angular and cross-linked directions is still
  open; and
- no action, matter source, carrier, or boundary law follows from this audit.

The active-record audit found five files whose wording can blur these layers.
Their calculations survive. They need a future append-only clarification,
not deletion or wholesale rederivation. The older broad configuration atlases
also survive because they explicitly chose an independent scalar branch; they
must continue to be read as maps of that enlarged possibility space, not as a
derived count of native UDT fields.
