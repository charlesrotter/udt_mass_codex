# Exact primary mathematical sources consulted

- Thomas Jurke, “On future asymptotics of polarized Gowdy T3-models,”
  arXiv gr-qc/0210022v1, archive stamp 7 October 2002 (PDF body displays a
  different later typesetting date): https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0210022 .
  Equations (1)–(5), printed page2, supply a comparison method for the metric
  reduction. Here W=P and 4a=lambda-log t. We recompute original Ricci rather
  than adopt the reduction on authority alone. No physical identification,
  global maximality or geodesic-completeness theorem is being borrowed.
- NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, equation10.5.2:
  https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.5.E2 . Canonical J0/Y0 Wronskian is 2/(pi z).
- NIST DLMF, equations10.17.3–4 and derivative section10.17(ii):
  https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.17 . Fixed real order, real positive z=kt->infinity;
  k=3/4 is fixed. The first omitted term supplies the O(t^(-3/2)) errors
  in the profile and its derivative. This is not a uniform k->0 estimate.
