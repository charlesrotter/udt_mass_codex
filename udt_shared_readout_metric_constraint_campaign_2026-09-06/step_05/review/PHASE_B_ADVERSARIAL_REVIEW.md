## Direct argument and quantifier attack

G351 owner-provisional conservation supplies one finite nonnegative countably
additive mu on labels. G352's CHOSEN continuous phase-independent product
supplies the weighted preimage integral. Its mathematical measure-valued
query includes singular LABEL measures without claiming a smooth atomic
PHASE/time rate or misusing a regular density exponent. Exact registered
windows, positive reference rates and bounded measurable active frequencies
are supplied conditional query data. No measured calibration accuracy,
source terms, instrument noise, registration error or physical device law is
hidden in e. Finite mu and bounded weights guarantee integrability.

For a=1-e>0 and b=1+e, the stated pointwise certificates are sound. On P,
b f1-a f3>=ba-ab=0; on Q the third kernel vanishes and the other two are
nonnegative; on R, b f2-a f3>=0. Integration gives

    a z3 <= b(z1+z2),

and permutation gives the other two inequalities. Nonnegativity of the
records is separate and retained. Neither smoothness, cellwise constancy,
weight-content independence nor fixed total mu enters this proof. It holds
for EVERY fixed measurable kernel triple in the bound class, even if some
class members have no supplied smooth geometric realization. A failed
certificate rejects the conjunction of assumptions, not any particular
assumption or the observational truth of UDT.

The candidate's converse is valid across the uncertainty class. For a
nonnegative feasible z, L=z/b and U=z/a obey L_i<=U_j+U_k. If U is already
a triangle triple it is an admissible s. Otherwise exactly one U_i exceeds
the sum of the other two; replacing it by that sum preserves its lower bound,
lies below its upper bound, and produces a nonnegative triangle triple. The
other entries stay within their intervals. The SC2 inverse then gives finite
nonnegative masses. A finite sum of point-evaluation LABEL measures realizes
those masses on nonempty measurable cells. Each positive s_i gives
f_i=z_i/s_i in [a,b]; if s_i=0 its interval bounds imply z_i=0 and choosing
f_i=1 is legal. Original integrals therefore recover z exactly.

This argument answers an existential question in BOTH kernels and measure.
It does not assert feasibility for any already fixed actual kernel triple,
metric development, observer or supplied measure. The reviewer independently
used a different converse: choose f=1 if z is an ordinary triangle; otherwise
let z_m dominate the sum s of the others, choose f_m=b and the other weights
t=b*s/z_m in [a,b], put zero mass on the cell invisible to m, and put
z_j/t on each cell shared by m and j. This gives the same class without
reusing the author's interval-construction code.

The phase/observer realization also survives. In the SC2 flat geometry,
Theta=alpha(z-t), nonzero future-raised null gradient, common Delta and exact
label cuts remain supplied. For any positive constant constructed f_i and
reference r_i, d_i=Delta*r_i*f_i/alpha yields future unit U_i and
omega_i=alpha*d_i, so omega_i/(Delta*r_i)=f_i. Constant observers are smooth

## Threshold, degeneracies and counterexamples

For nonzero nonnegative z with S=sum z, the residual is
S-2z_i+e*S, giving exactly

    e_* = max(0, 2*max(z)/S - 1).

This is the class threshold for 0<=e<1. The zero record is separate. A
one-positive record has e_*=1 and is excluded throughout the retained range;
at e=1 the positive lower bound and the author's U construction fail, and
no extension is claimed. e=0 recovers the exact SC2 triangle cone. For any
e>0 the class is strictly enlarged; no absolute claim that all positive
records become feasible is made.

For (1,1,3), the exact lower condition is -1+5e>=0, so e_*=1/5. The
measure with (P,Q,R) masses (5/4,0,5/4) and constant weights
(4/5,4/5,6/5) evaluates through the actual windows to (1,1,3). It is legal
for all e>=1/5 below1. Fixed f_i=1 still excludes that triple at any nominal
e. Thus only uniform exclusion across the allowed class is lost; no
fixed-query sufficiency or empirical20-percent accuracy follows.

For e>0, omitting z>=0 would be wrong. The reviewer checked
z=(-1/10,1,1), e=1/5: all relaxed triangle inequalities hold, but no
nonnegative measure realizes the negative first record. This supports
the candidate's explicit nonnegativity requirement rather than refuting it.
Boundary masses, zero smaller entries and zero mu are all admitted. The
finer measure distribution stays free; changing weights can also change
cell masses. No probability normalization or support-generation rule enters.
