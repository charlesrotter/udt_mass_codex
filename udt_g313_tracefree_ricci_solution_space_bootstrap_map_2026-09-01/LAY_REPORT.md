# G313 lay report

We now have a provisional local rule telling empty UDT spacetime how it may evolve. That is a major
improvement over merely having a consistent measuring kernel.

But the rule does not draw one unique universe. It is more like a rule for which complete shapes
are legal. The round expanding-and-contracting cosh universe is one legal shape. Exact checks show
that differently shaped universes, including ones with real tidal structure, can obey the same rule.
Even keeping positive curvature and `S3` spatial topology does not by itself force the round initial
shape. The equation also cannot choose its own absolute size.

So “who writes phi?” now has two answers:

1. the adopted local equation tells metric data how to continue;
2. an initial/global selection rule still has to choose which allowed continuation Nature occupies.

That second rule may be a bootstrap condition on the whole universe. This is compatible with Local
Metric Sufficiency: the whole universe may select the local metric, but once that metric is present,
its local response does not consult a second invisible history variable.

The remaining hole is therefore narrower and better defined. We are no longer missing every local
evolution rule. We are missing the global admissibility rule and the value of the surviving scale.
Neither is supplied by the kernel, Hopf geometry, observations, or `X_max` in this package.
