## 6. What this does and does not integrate

Two endpoints on the same Hopf fiber lie on one intrinsic reciprocal leaf. Two endpoints with
different Hopf-base labels do not. Nevertheless the shared intrinsic Killing state defines the
endpoint coboundary `delta_K` across the whole R17 configuration. This endpoint scalar is not by
itself a connecting pair-surface Jacobian.

Within one leaf, different windings give the same endpoint scalar. They need not give the same
ambient or normal-bundle transport. For cross-leaf comparisons a connecting query/path must leave
`E`; neither the metric nor this audit selects one.

The vertical factor


remains a pointwise conditional metric class modulo screen `SO(2)`. The screen normal bundle has
its metric-projected connection, while full ambient R17 holonomy mixes the pair and screen and does
not preserve the grading. Therefore a complete four-dimensional comparison still needs a supplied
path/carry and a carried-to-intrinsic endpoint reset. The foliation closes the pair-surface seam; it
does not erase the normal-bundle seam.
