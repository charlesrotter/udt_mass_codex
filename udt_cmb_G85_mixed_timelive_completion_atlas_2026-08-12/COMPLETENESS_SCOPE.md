# G85 completeness scope

This package is one bounded solution-space tile.

1. **Fields:** the AM lapse/spatial sector, axial mixing, clock-radial shift, and clock-norm lift
   are live. The other complete-coframe channels are not needed for the constructive existence
   proof and remain unclassified here.
2. **Action terms:** none; no action is selected.
3. **Equations:** exact metric regularity and smooth-extension identities only, not equations of
   motion.
4. **Domain:** complete `R x S^3` candidate for the shift/lift witnesses and a local bifurcate
   neighborhood for the zero-shift taper witness.
5. **Boundary/regularity:** inherited G75 cell is fixed exactly; equator, axes, poles, shift-zero
   times, and taper order are audited. Geodesic completeness is not proved.
6. **Topology:** only the G84 minimal doubled candidate is tested; it remains conditional.
7. **Dynamical character:** time-dependent kinematic witnesses are included; the zero-shift taper
   is restricted to coefficients smooth in the regular completion chart; no on-shell dynamics or
   characteristic operator exists.
8. **Branches:** all `196` frozen mixed profiles and five preregistered archetypes are classified;
   the continuum of all smooth completion functions is not exhausted.
9. **Stability:** not tested and not relevant to this metric-completion gate.
10. **Regime:** transparent continuation outside `0<=x<=1`; no physical cosmological regime or
    endpoint is assigned.

The calculation characterizes regular, degenerate, uniformly null, nonuniform-causal, and
nonnull seam classes. It does not filter by desired physical appearance.
