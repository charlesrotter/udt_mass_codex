## Method and team

Two fresh, same-model explorer contexts receive this identical packet/objective
without a coordinator-favored hypothesis or each other's proposal. Each freezes
an initial question, closest prior result and substantive difference, inexpensive
probe, possible outcomes, premise boundary and strongest objection. They write
only their assigned explorer directories. Coordinator then allocates at most
three substantive probes across the shared question family; revisions within
each declared probe are allowed and recorded. Mathematical discovery may precede
candidate freeze. Do not force a positive outcome or an algebraic closed form.

Two further fresh contexts, separate from explorers, review the strongest
candidate and continuation argument, or the scoped reason to stop. Up to two
bounded source-preserving repair/re-review rounds are included. No new permission
is needed for those stages. At most four active agents including coordinator.
Different model, human specialist and empirical confirmation remain untested.
Retain initial proposals, counterexamples, dissent, source/exposure records and
actual reviews. Coordinator alone edits central files and operational adapters.

Standard mathematics, exact algebra, limits and short symbolic/CPU checks are
methods. State hypotheses, quantifiers and controls before computation. Physical
choices use free-and-explored / pinned-by-THEORY (source) / pinned-by-HABIT tags.
Conditional diagnostic examples test an implication, not an adopted universe.
No new observational dataset fitting, microscopic light theory, action, source,
carrier, coupling, boundary law, or GR field equation is silently introduced.
