    g = -dtheta^2 + dx^2 + dy^2 + dz^2.

Here dtheta denotes the global one-form descending from dt on the covering

event after s=L. Thus it is a closed future timelike curve. Local proper time
and the metric null cones are ordinary. These are direct quotient/local-metric
calculations, not an adopted physical topology or a full UDT model.

has its derivative supported on the diagonal. Every event is in its own causal
past (also strictly chronologically in this control), so the literal support
inclusion holds. More strongly, any two events are timelike related in both

restriction in this control. No physical field, source, action or response law
