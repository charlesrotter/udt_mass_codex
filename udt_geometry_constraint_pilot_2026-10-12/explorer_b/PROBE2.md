## 2. The implication with its quantifiers exposed

Let Q denote the complete actual comparison: events, ordinary endpoint clocks,
null branch and compatible affine normalization. Let Z_g(Q) be R6/FSL1's
received proper-interval ratio. Consider a family Q_s and any independently
proved theorem of the form

    H(g,{Q_s}) => sup_s Z_g(Q_s) < infinity.

H is the theorem's actual domain and uniform hypotheses. Then elementary
contraposition gives

    [Q_s physically realizes the target as Z_g(Q_s) -> infinity]
       => not H(g,{Q_s}).

The second line is a valid candidate-realization test. Its first bracket must
not be replaced by the phrase "the positional asymptote" without an argument
showing that this same net observable/family realizes it. For ACI1, H consists
of a qualifying sweep plus bounded preparation, accumulated acceleration and
transported curvature budget. Its original proof, not a new GCP1 bound, owns
that specific implication.

For the initial proposal H would instead involve a compact region in a temporal
neighborhood, confined actual rays and compact endpoint velocity data. The
compact-region lemma was only proposed, not proved here. Consequently probe 2
claims no new mathematical exclusion even at that conditional scope.

Calling a condition necessary for *any eventual physical realization* is sound
only with those qualifications retained. It does not make all realizations lie
in H, turn one selected mathematical family into the physically relevant family,
or show that every geometry failing a selected preparation must fail UDT. An
existential requirement that some physical family attain the asymptote cannot
be negated by showing one diagnostic family has bounded Z.

This distinction also preserves useful negative science: if a later concrete

and also supplies H, the contradiction is substantive. It rejects that claimed
realization. It is not vacuous merely because it uses a stated protocol; ACI1
