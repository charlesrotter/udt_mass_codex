# GCP1 decision brief

The pilot did not find a new rule selecting UDT's geometry. Two independent
explorers and two fresh reviewers tested two particular routes; that bounded
result is not proof that the complete commitments are insufficient.

The causality route exposed a precise distinction: requiring responses to follow
the metric's causal connections does not, by itself, forbid those connections
from forming loops. Ordinary local clock behavior does not fill that global
step. A mathematical control checks this inference; it is not an admitted UDT
universe or a claim that physical clocks can travel around such loops.

The asymptote route retained the existing tests for a specified physical clock
realization. It did not generate another bound while leaving the same assignment
unresolved. Conditional tests remain useful without selecting an entire universe.

Keep the existing tools and stop this pilot. One useful next discussion is whether
UDT's intended causality explicitly forbids closed future-directed timelike paths
in every physical spacetime, beyond the currently written response-support rule.
That clarification could exclude a global class, but would not by itself choose
a response equation or a unique geometry. The alternative is to retain the present
scope and seek a different source-supported implication. No stronger global
condition, preferred present, field law or physical clock assignment is adopted.

The record is [NOTEBOOK.md](NOTEBOOK.md); the maintained scientific explanation
is [central D1GCP](../UDT_DEVELOPMENT.md#d1gcp). Actual final attestations and required
check receipts own completion. No automatic follow-on calculation is authorized.
