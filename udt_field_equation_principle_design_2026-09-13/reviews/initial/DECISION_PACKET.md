# FE1 decision packet — initial candidate for review

The metric already defines a precise way to compare tiny spacetime volumes near
an event with the corresponding flat tangent-space volume. Because metric volume
combines clock and ruler calibration, it offers a concrete candidate for what
reciprocity could balance as geometry changes from event to event.

The proposed addition is: **that local volume response is the physical response
governed by reciprocity.** The geometric quantity is well defined without an extra
field. Its identification as the physical response is not established by UDT.
There is also a second constituent choice: its infinitesimal coefficient is the
complete response, rather than one part of a richer response.

The mathematics has a definite consequence: this proposal gives exactly the
trace-free Ricci equation, and hence the previously studied conditional Einstein
spacetimes. It preserves varied tidal geometry and initial data. It is not a new
solution class or a prediction beyond that existing branch. We knew this likely
outcome before drafting the proposal; it is not evidence that UDT independently
selected it.

Why consider it: it gives reciprocity's unspecified response a local geometric
meaning tied to clock/ruler calibration, without introducing a material substance,
a preferred observer, a finite cutoff or an action. Why be cautious: the metric
also defines shape distortion and richer curvature information. We have not shown
that volume alone deserves the physical role, or that its infinitesimal coefficient
exhausts UDT response. A passing algebra check cannot resolve that choice.

No change is a legitimate option: preserve the completed kernel and conditional
results while leaving response constitution open. Another legitimate direction
would require a separately motivated response; FE1 does not build or choose one.
There is no automatic fallback to an action or another familiar field equation.

The exact decision after review is whether this response identification merits
further treatment as a declared physical hypothesis. Accepting it would add a
constitutive principle; it would not establish emergence from the original metric,
prove empirical truth or make canon. No such adoption is included in this task.
The fresh reviewer must first assess whether the proposal meets FE1's motivation
gate at all. Its outcome and the final recommendation belong in the reviewed return.

Further application would need its own bounded question, matching development
hypotheses and independent domain/measurement assignments. Source/mass/Maxwell,
large cosmology fits and an autonomous successor remain outside this packet.
