volume density back to the tangent space. Dividing by the metric density at
the event produces an intrinsic scalar density ratio on a sufficiently small
normal neighborhood. Using absolute determinant avoids Lorentz-volume sign
ambiguity; a compact Lorentz-invariant unit ball is not available and should
not be invented. No frame or physical radius is necessary for the infinitesimal
coefficient. RF1's h2_ij(x)=-(1/3)R_iajb x^a x^b gives this ratio as
1-(1/6)Ric_ab x^a x^b+O(|x|^3) in a fixed reporting frame. Its log has the same
quadratic coefficient. Thus the second separation derivative at zero is
-(1/3)Ric_ab, up to the stated curvature convention. This is standard local
metric geometry, not a UDT dynamical derivation or a small-curvature equation.

## Motivation threshold for this work order

A proposed hypothesis need not follow from existing premises: new hypothesis
design was explicitly authorized. It need not first acquire experimental proof,
and the work order explicitly permits simplicity/lowest-order motivation. An
eligible proposal must nevertheless state a physical assertion whose rationale
can be understood without treating the target equation as desired. It must say
what feature of the complete metric this response measures, why that feature is
a plausible subject of Universal Reciprocity, and why this infinitesimal datum
is hypothesized to be the COMPLETE response rather than one geometric readout
or only its leading approximation. Each of those assertions must be tagged as
owned, geometric, or newly proposed. The fact that a candidate is local, simple,
metric-only and requires no chosen length is a reason to consider it, not a
reason it is required. Review cannot adjudicate truth or adoption from elegance.

A volume-based candidate can therefore pass as an explicit speculative design
only with an honest physical motivation and constitutive sufficiency assertion.
It fails the motivation gate if its only remaining reason is that its Hessian
is Ricci and DDR then delivers the desired equation. A failure of this one
proposal cannot prove no independently motivated principle is possible.
