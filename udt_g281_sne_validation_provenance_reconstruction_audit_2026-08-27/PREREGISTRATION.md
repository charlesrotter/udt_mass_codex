# G281 preregistration — retrospective SNe provenance audit

Date: 2026-08-27

## Chronology disclosure

This is not an outcome-blind experiment.  Historical SNe scores and many later regrades were known
before this package was opened.  The preregistration freezes the classification and verification
rules before the complete census and final landing are assembled.

## Acceptance rules

A historical claim may be labeled `NATIVE_PREDICTION` only if all of the following are evidenced:

1. `phi` or the complete metric history used by the prediction was determined independently of the
   SNe outcomes;
2. the physical SNe query/null branch was specified;
3. the optical/Jacobi area was derived from that same complete history, not equated to a fitted or
   reconstructed radial function;
4. every luminosity/transfer premise was native or explicitly left as a conditional import;
5. no fitted profile family, basis coefficients, observational reconstruction, or post-readout
   angular factor determined the prediction shape;
6. any quoted holdout was not used to choose the history, representation, scale, or transfer.

Failure of any item caps the result below native prediction.  A good chi-square cannot repair a
provenance failure.

## Required hostile questions

For every major lineage tile, ask:

- Did the data enter before `R(phi)` or `d_A(phi)` was frozen?
- Was `d_A=r` a theorem for a supplied central spherical history, or was `r(phi)` itself supplied?
- Was the one-factor or two-factor luminosity law used, and what later authority controls it?
- Did a profile ansatz, P1/P2/P3 menu, polynomial basis, spline/hat basis, or scale bridge enter?
- Did angular/screen variables execute, or were they only documentary siblings?
- Was the result a prediction, an evaluator replay, a reconstruction, a calibration, or a holdout?
- Was the release already standardized or bias-corrected using external conventions?
- Does the claim survive the G280 same-depth/different-area counterexample?

## Falsification and repair rules

- Finding one SNe curve whose complete history and native optical area were fixed before data is a
  positive native-prediction witness.
- Finding that every good curve depends on a chosen/reconstructed `R(phi)` or imported transfer
  supports `NO_COMPLETE_NATIVE_SNE_PREDICTION_IN_AUDITED_LINEAGE`.
- A historical numerical result remains valid as a number when replayed, even if its physics label
  is demoted.
- A later correction controls an earlier contradictory label; history is preserved rather than
  rewritten.
- The audit must distinguish an arithmetic error from a conditional premise and both from a fitted
  empirical state.

## Representative replay contract

Subject to dependency availability and bounded runtime, rerun:

1. the July `n=2` optics verifier;
2. the August M3/P1 frozen-result verifier or a registered package-level equivalent;
3. the G236/G237 reconstruction verifier;
4. the G278 scale/holdout verifier;
5. the G279 native-provenance and G280 optical-area verifiers.

The purpose is regression/provenance confirmation, not new observational inference.  Existing
registered tolerances and no-write paths control.  Any unavailable dependency is reported rather
than silently replaced.

## Maximum conclusion

A source-bounded provenance and epistemic regrade of tracked non-protected SNe work.  No new metric,
profile, transfer law, physical history, scale, `X_max`, or canonization can follow.
