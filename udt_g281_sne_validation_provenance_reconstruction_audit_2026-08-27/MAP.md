# G281 map — SNe validation provenance reconstruction

Date: 2026-08-27

## Whole question

The repository has accumulated several distinct objects under the informal label “SNe
validation.”  G281 asks which of those objects were genuine native predictions, which were exact
conditional evaluations, which were empirical reconstructions or calibrations, which were
regression controls, and which carried an overclaim or a later-corrected scaffold.

The audit starts at `udt_canonical_geometry.md`, follows the March/May SNe lineage, the July optics
correction and pair-space work, the August P1/full-pair retyping, the dual-release reconstruction,
the Cepheid/DES attachment, and the G279/G280 provenance and optical-area results.

## Frame

- Mode: `MAP -> OBSERVE -> PONDER`; no fit, profile solve, or observational retuning.
- Route: metric-led provenance audit.
- Native ceiling: a direct SNe prediction requires one metric-owned or physically selected complete
  metric and null-bundle history, frozen before SNe outcomes, to determine both reciprocal redshift
  and optical/Jacobi area. A merely supplied control remains a conditional evaluator. A native
  radiative-transfer law would strengthen the result; an explicitly imported transfer may support
  only a conditional comparison.
- G280 discriminator: endpoint depth or projective pair position alone does not determine optical
  area.  In the primary spherical class, equal depth can occur at different areal radii.

## Layer ledger

Every historical result is decomposed into these layers:

1. reciprocal character and endpoint redshift;
2. primary metric form and profile/history values;
3. observer/event/null-branch query;
4. Jacobi or areal-distance response;
5. radiative transfer and luminosity relation;
6. absolute-scale attachment and magnitude nuisance;
7. observational release processing;
8. numerical representation or fitted family;
9. holdout status and independence.

## Candidate classifications

- `NATIVE_PREDICTION`: all load-bearing geometric shape layers were fixed by native premises before
  SNe data; no observation-shaped function entered.
- `NATIVE_CONDITIONAL_EVALUATION`: exact metric/Jacobi/redshift operator evaluated on a supplied
  history/query or with a declared imported transfer.
- `EMPIRICAL_RECONSTRUCTION`: SNe outcomes determine a relative area/profile/state function.
- `EMPIRICAL_CALIBRATION`: observations fix finite constants inside an independently frozen state
  or finite family.
- `REGRESSION_OR_COMPATIBILITY_CONTROL`: an old curve is replayed or a supplied control is scored;
  no new prediction follows.
- `IMPORTED_PHYSICS_COMPARISON`: a declared light/flux/source or catalog convention is load-bearing.
- `SCAFFOLDED_OR_OVERCLAIMED`: the contemporary claim called a result native, derived, validated,
  or predictive although a load-bearing shape, transfer, history, or area law was chosen, fitted,
  reconstructed, or mathematically erroneous.
- `SUPERSEDED_OR_REPAIRED`: later evidence explicitly corrected the scientific or epistemic label.

One artifact may legitimately receive several compatible labels at different layers.

## Protected and omitted scope

The protected stopped draft, curvature atlas, unbanked pair-response package, and G88 package are
not inspected, cited, or reconstructed.  Their exclusion is a provenance boundary, not a negative
finding.  Raw arrays and full scripts are opened only when a registered replay makes them
load-bearing.

## Proposed bounded output

G281 will produce a chronological claim census, a layer-by-layer provenance ledger, a stale-active-
claim scan, representative no-retuning replays, and a concise regrade report.  It will not select a
new SNe curve or promote an observational reconstruction into the kernel.
