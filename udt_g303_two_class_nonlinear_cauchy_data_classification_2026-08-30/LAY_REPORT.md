# G303 lay report

The two remaining candidate metric laws both pass the local evolution test.

The GR-like candidate says one particular curvature quantity must be zero. The broader trace-free
candidate says the same quantity may instead have one constant value throughout each connected
region. It cannot vary freely from place to place or moment to moment.

Once that number is fixed, the GR-like candidate and that completed fixed-number sector evolve the
metric with the same local causal wave structure and the same light cones. The raw trace-free
equation by itself has only nine output directions; the tenth is restored only after consistency
forces that one number to be constant. Their starting information is otherwise the same. If the
number is not supplied, the initial geometry must make the relevant curvature quantity constant
throughout the connected starting slice. That determines one additional number, not another
arbitrary function.

The reciprocal kernel can read either evolving geometry, but it does not choose between them. A
direct formula audit confirms that these pair readouts do not secretly create another metric
evolution equation.
Therefore local causal consistency has narrowed the missing-law problem but has not closed it:

```text
Is the extra constant forced to zero, or may the initial geometry choose it?
```

This result is local and conditional on standard mathematical wave-evolution theorems; G303 did not
re-prove those theorems. It says nothing yet about global boundaries, singularities, matter, mass,
observations, populated observer paths, or which complete history Nature realizes.
