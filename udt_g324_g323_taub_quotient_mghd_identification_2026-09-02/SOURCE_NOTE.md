# G324 theorem source note

Date: 2026-09-02

G324 imports one mathematical extension-boundary result from Gregory J. Galloway, Eric Ling, and
Jan Sbierski, *Timelike completeness as an obstruction to C0-extensions*, arXiv:1704.00353v4,
related DOI `10.1007/s00220-017-3019-2`.

The primary author-posted full text states the one-sided theorem needed here: for a smooth `C2`
globally hyperbolic spacetime in a `C0` extension, future timelike geodesic completeness leaves no
future boundary. Its preceding endpoint theorem and time dual say that a nonempty remaining past
boundary is reached by a past-directed timelike geodesic.

G324 uses this only as an `IMPORTED_MATHEMATICAL_METHOD`. The Taub metric's future completeness,
finite-interior continuation, past curvature blow-up, and application to the G322 MGHD are derived
separately. The paper is not a UDT premise or field equation, and G324 does not claim a standalone
proof of past `C0` inextendibility.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.00353
