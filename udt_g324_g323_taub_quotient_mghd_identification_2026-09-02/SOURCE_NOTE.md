# G324 theorem source note

Date: 2026-09-02

G324 imports one mathematical extension-boundary result from Gregory J. Galloway, Eric Ling, and
Jan Sbierski, *Timelike completeness as an obstruction to C0-extensions*, arXiv:1704.00353v4,
related DOI `10.1007/s00220-017-3019-2`.

The load-bearing import is the paper's orientation-neutral Theorem 2: a smooth, at least `C2`,
time-oriented, globally hyperbolic Lorentzian manifold that admits a `C0` extension contains a
timelike geodesic with an endpoint on the extension boundary. The exact source identity, PDF hash,
theorem number and page location, two exact fragments totaling 23 words, and a formal transcription are recorded
in `GLS_PRIMARY_SOURCE_EVIDENCE.json`.

G324 does not import a separate past-boundary or time-dual theorem. It orients the theorem's
geodesic internally: future timelike completeness excludes a future endpoint, so any endpoint
provided by Theorem 2 must be past-directed. This is the only direction needed before the exact
`R`-monotonicity and curvature arguments take over.

G324 uses this only as an `IMPORTED_MATHEMATICAL_METHOD`. The Taub metric's future completeness,
finite-interior continuation, endpoint orientation, past curvature blow-up, and application to the
G322 MGHD are derived separately. The paper is not a UDT premise or field equation, and G324 does
not claim a standalone proof of past `C0` inextendibility.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.00353
