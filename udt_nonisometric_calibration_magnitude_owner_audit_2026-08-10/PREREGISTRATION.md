Date: 2026-08-10
Branch: `grok`
Base: exact committed source state frozen in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.tsv`
Hardware: CPU only

## Whole question

Across the already-mapped 24 stable complete-branch identities, does any structure already owned by
the UDT metric generate the non-isometric reciprocal calibration magnitude, rather than merely
transporting, representing, or reading a magnitude supplied elsewhere?

This is a metric-led ownership audit. It does not search for a formula that produces a desired
particle, cosmology, `c_eff`, `X_max`, source, or action.

## Bounded universe

The complete bounded universe is:

1. all 24 stable identities in the frozen G41 branch universe;
2. the five preregistered candidate-owner families below;
3. the regular strata and exact ownership distinctions already frozen in G35--G44.

The five families are:

- `F01_PAIR_SURFACE_JACOBIAN`: a non-isometric Jacobian generated by a lawful, branch-owned
  observer-pair surface;
- `F02_ENDPOINT_STATIONARY_CLOCK`: an endpoint-exact clock normalization on branches with an
  intrinsic unique timelike Killing line;
- `F03_PATH_GLOBAL_COMPLETION`: path, connection, development, or holonomy data supplied by the
  complete metric/global completion;
- `F04_NATIVE_DYNAMICAL_BOOTSTRAP`: a calibration-line magnitude supplied by a future native
  dynamical or bootstrap law;
- `F05_NO_CURRENT_KINEMATIC_OWNER`: the explicit null hypothesis that the present kinematic
  foundation owns transport/readout but not magnitude generation.

No additional family may be added after outcomes are inspected. A newly discovered type can only
force `TYPE_FAILURE` and a new preregistration.

## Ownership gates

A family/branch row may be called `OWNER_DERIVED` only if frozen evidence shows all of:

1. **existence:** the branch itself supplies the candidate object without an unowned observer,
   path, section, action, or bootstrap choice;
2. **non-isometry:** the object can generate a nonunit clock/ruler density ratio;
3. **typing:** source, target, flag/calibration state, and intermediate update are defined;
4. **composition:** the generated magnitude telescopes or composes on its declared groupoid;
5. **gauge descent:** the result is independent of the regular screen representative;
6. **branch ownership:** no conditional ansatz is promoted merely because its algebra closes;
7. **terminal compatibility:** the supplied magnitude can reach the normalized reciprocal terminal
   readout without redefining `c_eff`.

Failure of any gate must be recorded explicitly. `OWNER_CONDITIONAL_BRANCH_ONLY`,
`TRANSPORT_OR_READOUT_ONLY`, `BLOCKED_MISSING_PHYSICAL_QUERY`, and
`BLOCKED_MISSING_DYNAMIC_LAW` are not aliases for `OWNER_DERIVED`.
