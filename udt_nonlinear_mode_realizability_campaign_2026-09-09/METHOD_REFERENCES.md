- Y. Choquet-Bruhat, *Beginnings of the Cauchy problem*, arXiv:1410.3490v1,
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.3490, sections2--3, pages2--5.
  Describes the analytic noncharacteristic Cauchy theorem, why unreduced Einstein
  equations do not directly meet it, and spatial evolution in Gaussian coordinates
  with constraints recovered by contracted Bianchi. This is method provenance,
  not a theorem automatically applying to an unchecked candidate.

  and first-order system (4.1), printed pages38--40:
  https://www2.math.upenn.edu/~qze/notes/pde2.pdf.
  Supplies the analytic coefficient/data and noncharacteristic hypotheses and
  local analytic uniqueness. Joint parameter dependence will be justified by
  treating epsilon as an extra mathematical independent variable with no epsilon
  derivative in the evolution equations, not as an extra spacetime dimension.
