+# G236 preregistration repair — hostile redshift reassignment
+
+Date: 2026-08-23
+Status: pre-outcome correction
+
+Before any G236 state reconstruction or observational statistic was evaluated, implementation
+planning exposed a defect in the originally written shuffled-redshift hostile. A complete reversal
+of a sorted monotone coordinate can remain a one-dimensional function and therefore be fitted by a
+reversed piecewise-linear state with little or no residual penalty. It is not a reliable hostile.
+
+The registered hostile is replaced by a deterministic half-cycle roll of the sorted redshift
+coordinate while the observed-vector and covariance order remain fixed. This breaks the
+single-valued magnitude/redshift relation at the roll seam. The original factor-of-two raw
+chi-square requirement is unchanged.
+
+No observational reconstruction, fitted shape, state coefficient, or outcome statistic was viewed
+before this correction. All scientific classifications and other gates remain unchanged.
