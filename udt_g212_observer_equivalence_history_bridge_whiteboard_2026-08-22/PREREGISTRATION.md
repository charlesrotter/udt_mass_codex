# G212 preregistration — observer-equivalence/history bridge whiteboard

Date: 2026-08-22

## Whole question

Does the current native chain

```text
UDT reciprocal founding
-> supplied complete metric
-> supplied completed bidirectional observer-pair pullback
-> exact reciprocal calibration and G211 local mode basis
```

already contain a nonidentity condition that determines how the completed pair response evolves
with separation, at least up to finitely many observational calibration constants? Or does it only
evaluate an arbitrary smooth metric/pair family after that family has been supplied?

The investigation must keep three types separate:

1. a local completed pair germ;
2. a separation-labelled family of such germs;
3. a globally compatible observer network or metric history.

## Frame and scope

- Whole frame: the current metric-led G166--G211 chain.
- Bounded slice: exact scalar/carry consequences of completed pair composition and the strongest
  local symmetry consequence of requiring one separation-only radial law about every observer.
- No numerical solve, fit, source model, action, transfer law, matter law, bootstrap law, or
  `X_max` constitution.
- The determinant-one spatial remainder is not silently frozen. If it is needed to decide the
  bridge, the return must be `REMAINDER_REQUIRED`, not an assumed closure.

## Premise typing

- `DERIVED_CONDITIONAL`: completed pair normalization on a supplied regular pair pullback.
- `DERIVED_CONDITIONAL`: G211 rank-two diagonal scalar basis after a supplied calibrated `1+3`
  reference.
- `SUPPLIED`: complete metric, observer/event pair germ, calibrated split, and any separation
  parameter used before it is derived.
- `OPEN`: physical population of germs, evolution of sector functions, global history, and
  `X_max`.
- `CANDIDATE_EXTRA_HYPOTHESIS_TO_AUDIT`: the same separation-only completed radial law is isotropic
  about every observer, with no preferred center.

## Competing landings

1. `CURRENT_IDENTITIES_FORCE_ONLY_ENDPOINT_POTENTIALS__FUNCTIONAL_HISTORY_FREEDOM_REMAINS`
   if composition/descent give exact potentials but allow arbitrary smooth functions.
2. `ALL_OBSERVER_ISOTROPY_REDUCES_HISTORY_TO_CONSTANT_CURVATURE_FINITE_MODULI`
   if exact, stated symmetry hypotheses force a constant-curvature family.
3. `OBSERVER_EQUIVALENCE_BRIDGE_NOT_OWNED_BY_FOUNDING__CONDITIONAL_FINITE_MODULI_ROUTE_ONLY`
   if landing 2 is mathematically correct but its load-bearing symmetry premise is not currently
   derived from Reciprocity or completed-pair semantics.
4. `VALUED_RELATION_NETWORK_IS_THE_STATE__RECONSTRUCTION_NOT_HISTORY_SELECTION`
   if a complete pair network faithfully determines the metric but supplies rather than predicts
   its evolving values.
5. `DETERMINANT_ONE_SPATIAL_REMAINDER_REQUIRED_BEFORE_BRIDGE_DECISION` if an exact overlooked
   compatibility equation can only be assessed after the next local mode census.

## Preregistered tests

### T1 — cocycle-to-potential test

Classify additive real depth and multiplicative positive scale carry on a connected thin endpoint
pair groupoid. Determine whether exact composition fixes a functional form or only gives endpoint
potentials and possible loop holonomy.

### T2 — smooth regrading/counterfamily test

Exhibit or exclude two inequivalent smooth separation-labelled metric/pair families that satisfy
the same local reciprocal normalization, reversal, composition typing, signature, and overlap
identities while agreeing at finitely many calibration anchors.

### T3 — geometric-identity test

Determine whether Levi-Civita metricity, Cartan structure equations, Bianchi identities,
Gauss--Codazzi--Ricci compatibility, or smooth pullback descent impose a nonidentity equation on
the sector functions without an additional curvature, holonomy, symmetry, boundary, or source
condition.

### T4 — all-observer isotropy theorem

State exact hypotheses under which one separation-only spherical law about every observer implies
local homogeneity/isotropy and constant sectional curvature (or a larger finite-dimensional
family). Distinguish spherical symmetry about one observer from isotropy at every point. Audit
whether the current UDT founding owns the stronger hypothesis.

### T5 — anchor rank

If T4 reduces the history to finite moduli, determine the type and number of remaining constants
and whether `c_E`, `G_obs`, or one additional observed dimensionful datum can calibrate them without
being promoted into a field equation.

### T6 — network-as-state test

Determine whether a globally valued, rank-complete network of completed pair pullbacks reconstructs
the ambient metric and whether that reconstruction is a selection law or merely a faithful encoding
of supplied values.

## Falsification contract

- The claim that current identities determine a history is falsified by one arbitrary-function
  counterfamily passing every currently owned nonidentity condition.
- The constant-curvature bridge is falsified under its declared hypotheses by one regular
  non-constant-curvature metric satisfying the same all-observer isotropy/separation-only law.
- Anchor sufficiency is falsified if an infinite-dimensional functional kernel survives after all
  currently owned global conditions and anchors are imposed.
- Network selection is falsified if every regular metric produces a lawful network and no
  independent condition distinguishes two such networks.

## Evidence gate and maximum conclusion

This is a no-solve mathematical whiteboard. A positive conclusion requires exact hypotheses,
proof or counterexample, provenance typing, and independent adversarial reconstruction. It may
identify a conditional bridge or the exact missing premise type. It may not claim a physical
history, spacetime solution, or `X_max` realization.
