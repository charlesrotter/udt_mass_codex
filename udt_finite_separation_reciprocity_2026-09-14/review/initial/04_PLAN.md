establishes, narrows or refutes. More pages, examples, symmetry releases, agents,
passing assertions and elapsed hours are not themselves advancement toward that
goal. A mathematical result may be valuable without completing such a connection;
retain and describe it accurately, with its separate purpose.

## 2. What is allowed to supply an ingredient

| Kind | Permitted use | What it cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| Current derived relation | Use at its exact hypotheses, domain and quantifier | Become a broader theorem without a new argument |
| New UDT-motivated dynamical hypothesis | Develop a concrete proposal with premise delta, motivation, alternatives and failure criteria | Enter accepted dependencies without Charles's decision or be called derived |
| Existing owner-adopted provisional postulate | Derive consequences with the provisional label attached | Be described as derived, empirically proved or canon |
| Ordinary admissible initial/query data or calibration | Specify a prediction within an owned class; disclose free parameters | Secretly supply a new law or tune each prediction separately |
| Mathematical technique | Coordinates, exact algebra, PDE methods or numerical analysis with hypotheses checked | Import a physical equation through the method's assumptions |
| Restricted ansatz | Test a named implication, construct a valid scoped witness, or diagnose a restriction | Supply a missing interaction, source, response or universal claim |
| Borrowed physical model | Explicit isolated comparison/control only, if the work order permits it | Become a dependency of a claim labeled native UDT |
| Instrument/measurement bridge | Enable a declared conditional test with independent justification and uncertainty | Count as a derived UDT detector, photon, source or matter theory |

Every ansatz has an entry card: question, origin, what it fixes, why it is useful,
what survives without it, scope of its result and retirement/extension condition.
A valid solution ansatz may remain as a restricted example; it need not be erased
to retain its theorem. Its special assumptions cannot be generalized away by
wording. An added physical mechanism remains unadopted even if convenient or fitted.
If a claimed native conclusion depends on that addition, the native conclusion
has not been established. No successor may silently inherit the addition.

GR/LCDM/other physics may define comparison targets or provide explicitly labeled
calibration and instrument models. They may not prescribe the native equations.
Recovering familiar mathematics by a valid derivation is welcome; unfamiliar valid
solutions are retained. Neither resemblance nor novelty is an acceptance test.

## 5. Gravity, cosmology and later physics: test obligations

These are proposed comparison categories, not numerical benchmarks or claims
that the present theory meets them. Before an actual experiment, obtain primary
data/product documentation and freeze domain, precision, covariance, exclusions,
calibration parameters, nuisance terms and unused confirmation outputs.

- Gravity: linked clock/bending/delay and orbital/tidal behavior; then time-dependent
  propagation and nonlinear regimes. A conditional vacuum Einstein family is not
  already a full matter-coupled replacement for GR.
- Cosmology: first the jointly predicted redshift, durations, angular/beam area and
  distance relation for a lawful evolving family. Later structure growth, lensing,
  background-radiation and material-history tests require their own justified
  native or explicitly external interfaces. Do not assume expansion or its absence
  because either makes a preferred story easier.
- Mass: define what is measured and conserved before attaching the word mass to
  curvature, an eigenvalue, a topological number or an integral. If an energy or
  Noether construction requires an action not owned by the branch, that dependency
  remains open. Static finite-box stability, linear stability, nonlinear persistence
  and physical time evolution are separate milestones.
- Electromagnetism: distinguish representing a field with freely chosen geometric
  data from generating its states and interactions through native equations.
- Quantum/particle claims: define preparations, outcome probabilities and joint
  measurements before comparison. Hilbert-space/Born/commutator/gauge assumptions
  may be named as targets or isolated controls, never silently adopted as UDT.

Each comparison has an exposure ledger: which observations informed the model,
which set calibration, which are held for confirmation, and which outcomes would
reject the claim. Calibration does not erase its origin. A measured c_E or G_obs
remains an observational anchor; neither generates a new absolute scale by name.

## 6. Autonomous work must have a bounded contract

Every execution packet records question, sources/premises, workspace, methods,
allowed data, ansatz ledger, exclusions, time/CPU/memory/output budgets, review,
stops, maximum conclusion and allowed successor tasks. Reuse existing capture
and verification utilities. Do not build another software framework merely to
manage a research question that a task table and checkpoint can handle.

The loop is:

1. Read the current checkpoint and verify actual Git/source state; perform mandatory
   startup on a new top-level session. Preserve prior history and unrelated work.
2. Choose only a task whose dependencies and authorization are satisfied. State the
   exact previously unexamined implication. If none exists, stop that queue.
3. Explore mathematically within that scope; freeze a candidate before verification.
   Record abandoned conjectures and the discovery history.
4. Check the original argument/equations and saved artifacts. Error control must
   match the claim; repeated shared-code passes are not independent proof.
5. Obtain fresh separate-context adversarial review and bounded same-premise repair/
   re-review. Preserve first versions and objections. Report unavailable independence.
6. Save the result with exact scope and status, update affected dependencies, commit
   and push only authorized paths after required checks and synchronization.
7. Continue only to a pre-authorized successor with satisfied gates. An unproved or
   unpromoted premise may enter only an explicitly conditional candidate chain.
   A commit or review does not supply adoption, scientific promotion or canon.

Checkpoint after every substantive change and at least every30minutes of active
work: current claim, evidence/result files, actual resource consumption, objections,
remaining budget, next eligible action and precise stop reason if any. Keep normal
concise live progress updates too; the durable checkpoint is not a substitute.
Completion is based on the declared return, not using up the allocation.

The control files should be small: one WORK_ORDER, one current CHECKPOINT, one
TASKS table, one RESULT/DECISION_BRIEF per logical finding, source/ansatz records
and the relevant raw checks/reviews. Stable result IDs and Git preserve history.
No new scientific premise belongs in dispatch code, tests or status files.

## 7. Dead ends and mandatory stops

| Outcome | Honest disposition | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| A valid derivation closes the stated implication | Reviewed result at the exact premise scope | Continue only within the authorized dependency graph |
| A valid counterexample defeats the proposed implication | Refuted conjecture, with strongest survivor | Stop that derivation; a distinct already-authorized implication may continue |
| Inequivalent completions satisfy every relevant well-defined premise but disagree on the target | Premise-relative underdetermination, only as broadly as proved | Do not choose one for resemblance; report remaining predictive content or decision boundary |
| A necessary term/domain is unspecified in the current sources | Specification incomplete for this question | Name the missing definition/ownership; no invented join |
| No proof found within the budget | Unresolved | Do not call it impossible or run indefinitely |
| Numerical checks fail or convergence is absent | Implementation defect or numerical ambiguity unless stronger evidence exists | One bounded diagnostic/repair cycle, then the declared stop |
| A eligible frozen observation contradicts the scoped prediction | Bounded empirical incompatibility with uncertainty and model/data scope | Preserve it; no after-the-fact extra term, cutoff or source profile |
| Further progress requires a new physical premise, excluded package or larger resources | Authorization/scope boundary | Save a concrete decision brief and stop the dependent work |

A finite checklist is not an independence proof. A failed finite search is not a
no-go theorem. A branch failure is not failure of every UDT completion. Conversely,
renaming an unclosed join, adding a desired law, excluding an inconvenient valid
solution or fitting a new free function does not repair the theory.

Reopen a stopped branch only with a changed relevant source/premise, a specific new
argument not previously tried, or new eligible evidence, and the required authority.
That rule prevents repeated rediscovery while preserving honest future possibilities.
