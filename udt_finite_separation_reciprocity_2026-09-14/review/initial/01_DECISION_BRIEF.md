# FSR1 — what the finite-separation check found

The existing UDT work already includes comparisons across finite separation.
It does not stop at infinitesimal comparisons. Those comparisons retain clock,
ruler and angular/frame information and obey exact reversal and composition rules.

One detail matters: reversing the comparison also requires carrying its direction
between the observers' frames. Ignoring that frame information can make a valid
reciprocal comparison look inconsistent. We have written that rule explicitly;
it follows from the existing complete comparison mathematics.

The reviewer also highlighted a distinction between changing reciprocal clock/
ruler factors and transporting unit frames for a comparison. They are different
operations. Treating them as the same symmetry would introduce an unjustified
step into a field-equation derivation.

These rules tell us how to compare a supplied geometry consistently. They do
not require the physical surroundings of every observer to be identical.
The same rules can apply to observers in different surroundings. Turning observer
exchange into a symmetry that makes whole neighborhoods match would add a
requirement we did not find in the current postulates.

The proposed finite-separation route therefore has not yielded a new field
equation. The current reciprocity response condition remains substantive; its
connection to a specific response calculated from the metric remains open.
The previously proposed volume response remains unadopted.

This is a bounded negative return about this attempted route, not a proof that
UDT cannot supply field equations. It also does not prove that another postulate
is necessary. Our suggestion that a finite-separation gap might open a fresh
route did not survive the source check at the examined scope.

The useful outcome is a firmer stopping record: we should not keep restarting
the same search under new wording. A successor must bring a particular new
deduction, an overlooked source assertion, or an explicit proposal for a changed
premise. No such proposal is adopted or launched by this packet.
