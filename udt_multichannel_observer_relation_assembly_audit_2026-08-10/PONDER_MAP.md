# Ponder map — orchestra, score, microphone, and conductor

This metaphor is a question map, not a result.

| metaphor | mathematical candidate | ownership question |
|---|---|---|
| orchestra | complete coframe and its irreducible comparison channels | which entries are genuinely independent? |
| score | connection, composition, reversal and overlap laws | is the assembly direct, semidirect, extended, or stratified? |
| microphone | ordered observer query and its readout/projection | which projection is founded rather than chosen? |
| tuning standard | `c_E`, later possibly `G_obs` and `m_e` | calibration only, or does a banked law give more? |
| conductor | on-shell/global/bootstrap selection candidate | is any such owner already present, or merely hoped for? |
| musical movements | invariant metric strata and limiting contractions | can physical regimes be identified without a hand switch? |

The central alternative is not “one scalar or failure.” It is:

1. one scalar really is the complete invariant;
2. a typed multi-channel object is complete and scalar observations are projections;
3. several candidate channels exist but no minimal assembly is selected; or
4. the declared arena is too small to decide.
