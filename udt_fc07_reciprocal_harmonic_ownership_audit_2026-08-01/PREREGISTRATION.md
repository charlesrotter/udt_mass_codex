# FC07 reciprocal–harmonic ownership and background-window audit — preregistration

Date: 2026-08-01  
Mode: metric-led, CPU exact/symbolic, no physical selection

## Whole question

On every registered FC07 completion with one-dimensional first cohomology, does the complete
metric's unique harmonic one-form line coincide with the founded reciprocal ruler line after:

1. constant `phi` is released; and
2. the registered lower-triangular pair–screen mixing is released?

If an ownership relation exists, determine whether it holds across the complete nondegenerate
background family or only on a limited geometric/curvature stratum. This is a geometry-first test
of one possible bootstrap component. It does not assume that matter, density, or a native equation
uses the relation.

## Exact bounded family

Retain the four FC07 orientation-preserving `b1=1` completions:

```text
M_MINUS_IDENTITY, M_ORDER4_ROTATION, M_ORDER6_ELLIPTIC, M_HYPERBOLIC.
```

On the universal-cover chart `(t,s,y1,y2)`, use the complete torus-invariant lower-triangular
coframe class

```text
theta0 = p(s) dt,                       p(s)=c_E exp[-phi(s)] > 0,
theta1 = a(s) ds,                       a(s)=L exp[+phi(s)] > 0,
thetaA = u_A(s) dt+b_A(s) ds+P_Aa(s)dy^a,

```

`P(s)` is invertible and all coefficients obey the exact mapping-torus descent law. The induced
spatial metric is the full torus-invariant positive metric generated by

```text
theta1=a ds,   thetaA=b_A ds+P_Aa dy^a.
```

The class releases arbitrary smooth finite periodic `phi(s)` and all registered lower-triangular
clock/ruler-to-screen mixing functions `u_A(s),b_A(s)`. It retains the founded base pair as the
upper block of the already registered E02 footing. It does not cover an upper-right screen-to-pair
extension, non-torus-invariant coefficients, time-live fields, a different completion, or a
selected equation of motion.

## Observing frame

The audit characterizes every member of the bounded family. It does not search for a branch with a
particle, density window, preferred completion, smooth profile, or expected cosmology. Background
curvature and completion data are outputs of the arbitrary coframe functions, not fitted inputs.
Any loss of nondegeneracy, divergence of the harmonic normalization, or failure of descent is
recorded as a boundary stratum, not discarded as a failed solution.

## Distinct ownership questions

The following must never be merged:

1. `LINE_OWNERSHIP`: the harmonic covector and founded ruler covector span the same line.
2. `RESCALED_FORM_OWNERSHIP`: the harmonic representative equals a globally determined positive
   scalar times the founded ruler form.
3. `RULER_ITSELF_HARMONIC`: the unrescaled founded ruler is closed and co-closed.
4. `NORMALIZATION_CLOSURE`: the global normalization supplies an equation restricting the local
   coframe rather than merely reading it.

Only item 4 could begin a same-solution bootstrap return. Items 1–3 alone are kinematic geometry.

bootstrap window.

## Required calculations

1. Derive the full spatial metric, inverse, determinant, and volume form with arbitrary `a,h,b`.
2. Solve the harmonic one-form equation in the base cohomology class without setting `b=0` or
   `phi=constant`.
3. Prove descent and unit-period normalization on all four monodromies.
4. Compare the resulting harmonic line, metric-dual vector, and rank-one projector with `theta1`.
5. Determine the exact condition under which `theta1` itself is harmonic.
6. Classify dependence on `phi`, screen area/shear, both mixing doublets, completion, and every
   nondegeneracy/normalization boundary.
7. Determine whether any curvature invariant or complete-cell modulus restricts ownership to a
   finite interval. Do not translate curvature to density without a native source bridge.
8. Retain constant-screen and varying-screen controls and the hyperbolic forced-varying row.

## Falsification and certification

- Universal bounded-family line ownership fails if any smooth descending nondegenerate member has
  a nonzero wedge between the harmonic and founded ruler covectors.
- Mixing independence fails if either `u` or `b` enters the normalized harmonic solution or its
  projector comparison after exact simplification.
- A claimed window fails if it is only the already required positivity/nondegeneracy domain or if
  it depends on a chosen coordinate profile rather than an invariant condition.
- A bootstrap claim fails unless a nonidentity condition maps global data back into a restriction
  on the same local coframe family.
- A density statement fails because no native density–curvature/source equation is supplied here.
- The load-bearing algebra must be independently reconstructed without importing production code.
- Semantic catch-proofs must reject equality of line ownership with harmonicity, a kinematic
  normalization promoted to an equation, a nondegeneracy bound called a bootstrap window, a
  density/curvature identification, a preferred monodromy, and a matter/source claim.

## Maximum conclusion

At most this audit may derive a global metric compatibility/normalization relation between the
founded reciprocal ruler and the unique harmonic line throughout a specified complete FC07
coframe family, and determine whether that relation has a nontrivial geometric validity window.
It may not select a completion, derive a native field equation, equate curvature with density,
close bootstrap, determine `X_max`, or claim matter, mass, stability, carrier emergence, an action,
or a source.
