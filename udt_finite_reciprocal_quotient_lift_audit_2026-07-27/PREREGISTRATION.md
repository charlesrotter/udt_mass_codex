# Finite reciprocal quotient-lift audit — preregistration

Date: 2026-07-27
Branch: `grok`
Mode: `MAP -> OBSERVE -> PONDER -> DERIVE`; CPU/exact algebra only

## Whole question

Given the already derived additive reciprocal clock/ruler pair

`B(phi) = diag(exp(-phi), exp(+phi))`,

classify every finite complete-coframe lift that preserves this pair exactly as the action induced on
a two-dimensional quotient. Determine separately what follows from quotient preservation alone, what
follows only after the complete lift is required to be a continuous one-parameter representation,
and what remains after the first metric response is fixed.

The audit must decide whether the familiar block-triangular representative is forced, a convenient
flag choice, or only a first-jet chart. It must also determine when the remaining screen rotation is
metric-invisible and when it changes finite metric data beyond first order.

## Observing rather than targeting

This audit does not seek a preferred triangular lift or a positive closure. It will classify smooth
quotient lifts, one-parameter quotient representations, fixed-response lifts, both triangular flag
choices, isotropic and anisotropic screen strata, screen mixing, phi-dependent fixed-metric frame
changes, reversal, and finite-cell/global cases. A candidate cannot win because it resembles the
desired universe, the previous seven-parameter chart, GR, or a Hopf construction.

## Exact bounded regime

- real four-dimensional coframe vector space `V` with signature `(-,+,+,+)`;
- a supplied ordered reciprocal quotient `V -> B`, with two-dimensional kernel/screen `S`;
- regular finite real `phi`, including `phi=0` and reversal `phi -> -phi`;
- smooth invertible finite lifts, with the group law tested rather than assumed;
- first and second metric jets, and exact finite metric equality where tractable;
- local algebra plus the explicit global-bundle conditions needed to use it over a finite cell.

No action, source, matter carrier, field equation, physical observer/path section, boundary
functional, bootstrap selector, `X_max`, or dynamics is supplied.
