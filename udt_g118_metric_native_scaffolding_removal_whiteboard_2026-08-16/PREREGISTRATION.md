# G118 preregistration — metric-native scaffolding-removal whiteboard

Date: 2026-08-16

Status: `PREREGISTERED_BEFORE_ROLE_RETURNS`

## Whole question

Starting only from the founded reciprocal character, the supplied complete metric/coframe, and the
banked G94/G107--G117 derivations, which pieces of the current SNe observer interface are metric-owned
and which are temporary scaffolding? Can one exact metric/query object replace two or more remaining
conditional inputs without adding a fitted coefficient, a chosen history, or a new mechanism?

This is `METRIC_LED`: uncover what the existing metric does. It is not a search for a desired SNe,
BAO, CMB, `X_max`, loud--quiet--loud, bootstrap, or matter outcome.

## Bounded arena

The audit covers the dependency chain

```text
supplied ordered depth -> reciprocal character
supplied (g,Q,F) -> full observer differential dF
-> terminal pair / frequency / screen / source-junction channels
-> conditional G117 SNe brightness interface.
```

It does not solve a complete history, continue G116 beyond its local jet, fit data, derive an action
or source law, or inspect protected local packages.

## Four adversarial roles

1. **Metric reconstructor:** rebuild the shortest exact dependency chain and identify redundant
   intermediate objects.
2. **Category-error hunter:** challenge every equality between depth, frequency, screen, transfer,
   observer relation, and history.
3. **Scaffolding hunter:** list every ansatz, representative, normalization, and provisional closure;
   seek a metric-owned replacement but reject convenient invention.
4. **Minimal-structure defender/synthesizer:** try to prove each surviving conditional is genuinely
   required at its stated type, and adjudicate the other roles.

The first three roles use fresh contexts. The primary agent performs role four and synthesis.

## Required outputs

1. A typed dependency graph with every edge stamped `DERIVED`, `CONDITIONAL`, `OBSERVED`, or `OPEN`.
2. A scaffolding ledger stating whether each item is removable, replaceable, or irreducible at its
   current type.
3. A category-error ledger.
4. A ranked list of at most three simplifications.
5. Exactly one bounded next derivation selected by this rule: it must be exact, metric-led,
   outcome-blind, coefficient-free, and remove or sharply type at least one current conditional.

## Falsification and certification contract

- A proposed simplification fails if it needs a new physical postulate, selected history, fitted
  coefficient, unowned carrier law, or observational target.
- A claimed redundancy fails if two regular supplied metric/query realizations can vary the allegedly
  redundant objects independently.
- A claimed metric owner must be exhibited as an exact construction or identity and type-check under
  reversal/composition where applicable.
- Conflicting role returns remain visible and are adjudicated rather than averaged away.
- Any banked result requires a fresh blind adversarial review and the repository premise/test gates.
