# G118 in lay language

We had been treating the supernova calculation as though it needed a separately chosen angular
instrument. In the exact static case and the time-live near-center expansion already banked, it
does not: the apparent circular patch grows with the areal radius. That replaces a whole little
matrix of assumed behavior with one geometric quantity, `R`, inside those scopes. The next proof
must determine whether this remains exact at arbitrary finite distance in a time-live metric.

But the telescope does not observe `R` alone. It observes brightness. Brightness combines:

- the frequency change `Z`;
- the geometric spreading `R`;
- whatever physical amount and energy the light-like carrier retains, called `T` here.

The metric supplies the first two for a fully specified observation. It does not yet supply the
third. Consequently the SNe data currently hears the combination `R/sqrt(T)`, not `R` by itself.

So this is genuine scaffolding removal, not complete closure. We have reduced an apparent angular
law to geometry and exposed the one remaining transfer joint more cleanly. The next derivation is
to prove that geometric reduction exactly at finite distance in an arbitrary time-live spherical
metric, rather than only in the static and near-center cases already banked.
