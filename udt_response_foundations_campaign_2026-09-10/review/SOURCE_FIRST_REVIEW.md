## Independent reconstruction: an intrinsic normal-jet domain is available

Fix the reporting tangent space (V,eta), signature (-+++), and finite r>=2.
For each k>=2 let N_k be symmetric matrix-valued homogeneous degree-k
polynomials H_k(x), subject to the LINEAR identity H_k(x)x=0 (lower/upper
coordinate placement understood via evaluation of the covariant matrix).
Let N=N_2 direct_sum ... direct_sum N_r.

Every tuple in N is an actual local metric jet: set

    g_H(x)=eta+sum_{k=2}^r H_k(x).

For a sufficiently small neighborhood it is nondegenerate Lorentzian because
g_H(0)=eta. There is no claim of one neighborhood uniform over unbounded H.
The radial identity g_H(x)x=eta x implies Gamma_i,jk(x)x^j x^k=0:
differentiate g_ik x^k=eta_ik x^k, contract with x^j, and separately differentiate
g_jk x^j x^k=eta_jk x^j x^k. Both derivative terms entering the lower Christoffel
contraction vanish. Thus radial lines are affinely parametrized geodesics and
these coordinates are exponential normal coordinates near the origin.

Conversely, Gauss' lemma in normal coordinates gives that radial identity.
Its Taylor coefficients give H_k in N_k; symmetry eliminates the degree-one
coefficient. The metric r-jet modulo coordinate changes with identity linear
part is represented by the finite normal tuple. This uses actual metric germs,
not arbitrary independent covariant-curvature derivatives. Distinct tuples may
be related by a Lorentz frame change; we do not quotient away that frame action.

An r-th-order diffeomorphism-natural local response, on a domain admitting these
germs, therefore restricts directly to F_N(H)=E(g_H)(0). Finite-order dependence
makes it independent of the chosen representative above the r-jet. This is a
representation on realizable jets, requiring NO response values on GL1's
unrealizable formal curvature tuples. The full-neighborhood response domain is
an additional hypothesis: geometric realizability of all H does not prove the
physical response is defined on all H, especially if supplied only on solutions.

## Weighted curvature bounds can transfer without an ambient response

The necessary geometric estimate can be proved by radial Jacobi fields, without
assuming a general smooth extension or the full abstract curvature-jet theorem.
Fix v and the radial geodesic t -> exp(tv). In a parallel frame let A(t) be the
Jacobi matrix with A(0)=0 and A'(0)=I. With a consistently fixed curvature sign,

    A''(t)+C_v(t) A(t)=0,
    C_v^(j)(0)w=(nabla_v^j R)(w,v)v,
    g_ij(tv)=eta(A(t)e_i,A(t)e_j)/t^2.

The actual-metric existence of these objects automatically respects differential
Bianchi and nonlinear commutator constraints. Set C_v(t)=sum c_j(v)t^j,
A(t)=sum a_l(v)t^l. The finite Taylor recursion is

    (n+2)(n+1)a_(n+2)=-sum_{j+l=n} c_j a_l,
    a_0=0, a_1=I, c_j=(nabla_v^j R)(.,v)v/j!.

Assign weight j+2 to nabla^j R. Induction gives weight l-1 to a_l and weight k
to H_k obtained from the product above. Only derivatives through k-2 enter H_k.
All expressions are finite universal polynomials with fixed contractions and
factorials. Polarization converts diagonal v evaluations into coefficient
tensors with finite constants in the chosen finite-dimensional norms. Therefore

    ||nabla^j R|| <= M_j epsilon^(j+2), 0<=j<=r-2
      implies ||H_k|| <= B_k(M_0,...,M_(k-2)) epsilon^k.

This is a bound on actual compatible curvature jets, not a claim that the
curvature tuple space is a linear product. Products such as R*R appear already
at degree four and have precisely the required weight. Dropping these products
would invalidate the transfer. Lorentz signature does not obstruct the ODE,
parallel transport or polarization argument; positive reporting norms are
mathematical controls, not positive Lorentz-invariant norms.

The order-two coefficient is linearly equivalent to algebraic curvature. One
can verify directly the inverse H_2,ij(x)=-(1/3)R_iajb x^a x^b for the usual
all-lowered convention R_1212=positive sectional curvature. Its radial property
follows from curvature antisymmetry; the normal-coordinate second-derivative
formula and Bianchi give back R. A proof must state conventions consistently.
