# OEV1 step4B narrowed published-data diagnostic — frozen rules

This is an exploratory, source-conditional observable constraint table, not
held-out confirmation or a test of a selected native UDT metric. The observation
researcher has already read Pesce et al. MCP XIII (arXiv2001.09213v2) Table1;
the parent has seen source-meaning findings but not the six numerical rows.
Published outcomes and discovery exposure are explicit. Freeze this procedure
before parent extraction/computation, not a fictitious unexposed universe.

Use ALL six galaxy rows in that table, with no outcome-dependent exclusions.
Transcribe name, angular-size distance median and reported asymmetric errors,
CMB-frame optical-convention recession velocity and reported uncertainty.
Preserve table/page/edition and source bytes. Check transcription independently.
The velocity is a processed optical redshift convention, not local proper
recession speed or an actual detector's unreduced received ticking.

With observed clock/ruler calibration c_E=299792.458 km/s, calculate only:

    z_CMB,opt = v_opt,CMB / c_E,
    Z_proxy = 1 + z_CMB,opt,
    ell_proxy = log(1 + z_CMB,opt).

Transform the published marginal velocity endpoints monotonically into z and
ell endpoints. Carry the published distance marginal endpoints separately.
Do not infer a joint confidence rectangle, likelihood, independent Gaussian
errors, covariance zero, a symmetric posterior, or new nuisance constraints.
The table is a collection of source-reported marginal constraints plus exact
coordinate/readout conversions, not a new empirical statistical estimate.

Do not fit H0, subtract a peculiar-flow model, turn D_A into initial proper L,
assign all net redshift to UDT positional slowing, identify ell with an additional
component, or attach a curve to the reciprocal kernel. Disk dynamics, standard
relativistic source corrections and frame reduction are inherited conditional
readout assumptions. D_A and systemic velocity need not be statistically
independent. Model-dependent source reductions cannot be silently promoted to
raw independent clocks.

Acceptance checks: six unique source rows; positive distance endpoints and
Z endpoints; ordered transformed bounds; conversion round-trip; independent
primary-table transcription and numerical recomputation. A broken check rejects
this reduction, not a physical geometry. No redshift-distance shape, global
asymptote, positional scale or native field-law claim is permitted.

Return the reproducible table and the exact additional information needed for
a candidate comparison: a metric with actual source/observer histories and
ray/area readouts, stated source/disk/frame reductions, independent motion
constraints or a justified nuisance model, and the joint source posterior/error
structure. Existing readout identities may constrain a supplied candidate even
though native dynamics is open. If no such candidate is admitted here, retain
these narrowed observable constraints and say the physical inference is open.
