## P02: stratified full-local-jet atlas

P01 sampled a generic full-dimensional family.  Such a sample is excellent for
showing what is generic, but it almost surely misses exact null, repeated-
eigenvalue, rank-deficient, static, and symmetry-enhanced strata.

The next objective map should therefore be stratified, not targeted at a
desired physical answer:

1. replace the current `(t,x)` functional slice with independent complete
   four-coordinate local two-jets for all eight coframe amplitudes;
2. enumerate derivative-rank strata of `dphi`, the angular block, and the shift
   map, including all causal classes and exact rank changes;
3. cross those strata rather than playing one instrument at a time;
4. compute the same invariant local curvature, pair/screen mixing, and tidal
   data without an action or source;
5. record which strata are constructive, incompatible, or still open under
   only the coframe identities; and
6. only afterward build global finite-cell representatives of every surviving
   local stratum.

This is not a hunt for the lightlike branch or Hopf carrier.  It is the missing
measure-zero portion of an honest solution-space map.  It also removes P01's
largest geometric restriction: no `(y,z)` dependence.

The shell-2.50 holonomy problem should remain a separate numerical subtask.  It
may later use extended-precision Cartan/Lorentz logarithms, but it is not a
reason to delay the more informative local-strata map.

No time-live or ODE solve is justified by P01 alone.  Those require a native
background equation or a separately and explicitly conditional equation
family.
