# Lay readout

This produced real forward movement.

We built a mathematically complete finite-cell geometry in which the clock is no longer silent. Its
clock rate varies across the whole cell, and the clock is twisted into the angular geometry. The
metric itself then does two useful things:

- the size of its time-symmetry direction gives the observer-to-observer clock depth; and
- the twist of that direction points along the corresponding ruler direction.

We also proved in a separate complete example that a sufficiently distinctive clock pattern can
make the time-symmetry direction unique, rather than something chosen by coordinates.

The one remaining caution is that these two proofs currently live in two different examples. We
have not yet proved that one single explicit metric has the unique clock direction and the
twist-selected ruler direction at the same time. Joining them by hand would repeat the old branch-
splicing mistake.

So the result is: **complete UDT-like geometries can natively generate clock depth, and clock twist
can identify the angular ruler; the first completely intrinsic all-in-one example remains to be
constructed or proved.** None of these configurations has yet been selected by a law of motion.
