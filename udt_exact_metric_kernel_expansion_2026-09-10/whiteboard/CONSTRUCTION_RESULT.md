## Time-live tape obstruction and exact transformation

On one pair surface, a positive density can always be integrated at each fixed t:

    s(t,σ)=s0(t)+∫_(σ0)^σ m(t,u) du.

The actual full differential is ds=a dt+m dσ with a=∂t s. Therefore imposing
ds=m(t,σ)dσ throughout a two-dimensional patch with the original clock differential
requires ∂t m=0 (local exactness), and a compatible choice of tape origin. In general,

    [∂t,m^-1∂σ]=-(∂t log m)m^-1∂σ.

The true coordinate substitution dσ=(ds-a dt)/m gives

    h'00=-T²(1-aB)²+a²/T²,
    h'01=-T²B(1-aB)-a/T²,
    h'11=T^-2-T²B²,
    det h'=-1.

The new fixed-s clock factor is

    T_fixed-s²=T²(1-aB)²-a²/T²,

only where this is positive. The original clock has new components (1,a), retains
norm -T², and is a different vector from the fixed-s coordinate tangent. Hence
determinant -1 does not justify reusing the old T and B for the new coordinate pair.
This is a changed coordinate clock congruence, not new physics or an extra scalar
correction. G180's valid one-dimensional descent is preserved.
