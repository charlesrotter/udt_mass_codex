# G230 ponder — which extension actually approaches regional history?

Date: 2026-08-23

G229 leaves two superficially similar directions: add one Taylor order at one point, or attempt a
finite-neighborhood reconstruction. Jumping directly to a region would mix three questions:

1. compatibility of neighboring local jets;
2. existence of a smooth field realizing compatible data;
3. generation or selection of the field's numerical values.

Only the first is presently ready for an exact bounded test.

The second curvature derivative is the first useful bridge. For a scalar, mixed derivatives
commute. For curvature they do not: the Levi-Civita commutator acts on every curvature index and
produces a term quadratic in curvature. Therefore independently assigning second changes around a
small two-direction tile may fail even when every vertex passes G227 and every first edge passes
G228.

This makes G230 more than another basis census. It tests whether the already derived identities
close the first infinitesimal overlap square. If they do not, it identifies the exact missing
interlock. If the differentiated Bianchi and Ricci commutator conditions are also sufficient for a
metric 4-jet, the remaining regional question becomes sharply higher-order/formal rather than an
untyped search for a new mechanism.

The result must remain descriptive mathematics of the metric. It may say which supplied local
data fit together; it may not say what values Nature chooses.
