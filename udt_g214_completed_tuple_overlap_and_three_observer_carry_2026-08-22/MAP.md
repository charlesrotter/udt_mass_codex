# G214 map — completed-tuple overlap and three-observer carry

Date: 2026-08-22

## Whole question

Does the G213 local information equivalence

\[
h_\sigma\longleftrightarrow (m,h_s)
\]

commute with lawful calibrated pair-chart overlaps, smooth ruler reparameterization, orientation
reversal, and three-observer calibration carry, so the G130 compatible-network reconstruction
transfers without silently losing ruler density?

## Bounded frame

- one supplied regular Lorentzian pair surface at a time;
- supplied time- and ruler-oriented calibrated charts;
- smooth upper-triangular overlap Jacobians
  \(P=\begin{psmallmatrix}a&n\\0&d\end{psmallmatrix}\), with \(a,d>0\);
- G176 completed-pair Dual Reciprocity remains a `WORKING_FOUNDATIONAL_CLARIFICATION`;
- the positive ruler density \(m=\sqrt{-\det h_\sigma}\), the normalized metric \(h_s\), and the
  known calibrated germ/orientation are retained as one typed tuple;
- three independently supplied pair germs remain pair-indexed unless explicit incidence matching
  is proved or supplied.

This is metric-led reconstruction/descent algebra. It is not a history-value law, observer
population rule, path selector, fit, transfer model, or `X_max` construction.

## Candidate exact structure

For \(h_j=P_{ij}^{T}h_iP_{ij}\), define

\[
J_i=\operatorname{diag}(1,m_i),\qquad
C_{ij}=J_iP_{ij}J_j^{-1}.
\]

The candidate theorem is

\[
m_j=(\det P_{ij})m_i,\qquad
\det C_{ij}=1,\qquad
h_{s,j}=C_{ij}^{T}h_{s,i}C_{ij},
\]

with \(C_{ij}C_{jk}=C_{ik}\) on triple overlaps. Pure ruler reparameterization should give
\(C_{ij}=I\). General calibrated recharting should be equivariant rather than invariant.

## Anticipated type boundary

Overlap transitions between charts on one pair surface are not the same as composition of the
distinct pair surfaces `AB`, `BC`, and `AC`. The latter has no automatic full-tuple product.
Matched incidence calibrations are a sufficient descent condition; arbitrary three-pair closure is
not preregistered as an identity.
