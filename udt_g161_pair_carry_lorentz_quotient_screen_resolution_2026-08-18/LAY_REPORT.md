# G161 lay report

We were asking whether watching the same observer comparison over many distances would reveal the
hidden frame that one single snapshot could not.

The answer is: it reveals much more, but not that last frame angle.

Think of every pair metric as a chord with three audible properties: overall size, reciprocal
imbalance, and shear. The underlying two-dimensional frame has four numbers. The fourth is not a
missing instrument coefficient. It is the freedom to boost the entire clock-ruler frame without
changing the chord.

A sweep over distance gives us the whole changing chord—the notes, their changing loudness, and
their rate of change. But a smoothly changing hidden boost can still accompany the entire sweep
without changing anything the pair metric hears.

There is a clean payoff: the upper-triangular form we have often used is not just a convenient
guess. In the regular, time-oriented regime it is the unique standard representative of each
equivalence class, much as choosing north-up is a unique way to print a map once orientation is
fixed. That makes the kernel cleaner and removes scaffolding. It does not prove Nature physically
uses that printed orientation.

The angular screen by itself does not fix the hidden boost. In a flat product example, the screen
can remain completely unchanged while the clock-ruler frame is boosted.

However, if a supplied observer-pair surface bends through the full geometry in a sufficiently
unequal way, that bending can mark a preferred clock direction and ruler direction. Mathematically,
the combined extrinsic curvature has two distinct real axes. Those axes can act like grain in a
piece of wood: once visible, they fix how the frame should line up. If the surface is flat, bends
equally in all directions, or hits a degenerate crossing, the grain disappears and the ambiguity
returns.

So this was real linear progress. We now know exactly what a distance sweep determines, exactly
what it cannot determine, and one metric-native circumstance in which the full geometry can supply
the missing orientation. What remains open is whether the physical UDT observer network naturally
stays in that resolving stratum and how it behaves at the exceptional and global regions.
