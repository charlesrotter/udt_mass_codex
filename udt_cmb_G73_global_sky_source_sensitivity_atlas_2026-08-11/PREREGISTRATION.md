Date: 2026-08-11

## Whole question

Given the G72 metric-owned screen response on a supplied observer-sky query, determine whether the
output of a complete sky comparison is necessarily source-sensitive, can become geometry-dominated,
or can acquire a true kaleidoscopic many-to-one/repeated-image structure. Do not demand a CMB-like
answer and do not assume a smooth or homogeneous source.

This is a metric-led response-classification problem. It is not a source model, fit, spectrum,
bootstrap calculation, or physical last-scattering construction.

## Exact bounded regimes

1. `R1_REGULAR_SINGLE_BRANCH`: an oriented one-to-one sky map with a pointwise invertible G72
   response `M(n)` on a supplied common query.
2. `R2_REGULAR_STRONG_SHEAR`: the same regular class as the singular-value ratio grows, with no
   caustic crossed.
3. `R3_CAUSTIC_OR_FOLD_LIMIT`: rank loss or a noninjective sky map, characterized only as a domain
   boundary/potential mechanism; no continuation or branch-combination law is supplied.
4. `R4_MULTIBRANCH`: branch-labelled paths/images; distinguish the metric-owned set of branch
   responses from any unowned sum, average, phase, or detector rule.
5. `R5_G68_CONTROL_TILE`: all 21 frozen stationary/equatorial G68 endpoint response maps.

This is not a function-space census of complete UDT solutions and not a full physical CMB sky.

## Exact tests

1. Prove that a pointwise `GL+(2)` response over a one-to-one sky map is invertible and therefore
   cannot erase arbitrary source dependence.
2. Derive the canonical strong-shear directional map and the exact fraction of unoriented input
   directions compressed into a declared output cone.
3. Prove that rank loss can collapse source directions but retains source-dependent amplitude.
4. Prove that a branch-labelled family is not a summed observable until weights and a combining
   law are supplied.
5. Replay every G68 `chi` and calculate its singular-value ratio `exp(2 chi)` plus the three
   preregistered orientation-compression diagnostics.
6. Separate scalar pullback, vector/tensor response, branch multiplicity, and physical observable
   ownership.

## Falsification and certification contract

- Reject any claim of source universality on a regular one-to-one invertible response if an exact
  inverse exists.
- Reject any claim that G68 is strongly kaleidoscopic unless its registered singular-value ratios
  demonstrate large directional compression.
- Reject any caustic/fold conclusion derived solely from a regular G68 row.
- Reject any summation, averaging, phase, parity, population, detector, or branch weight not owned
  by the supplied query.
- Reject identification of G68 azimuthal carry `psi` with image polar rotation.
- Reject use of `X_max`, SNe, bootstrap, or CMB observations as a selector.
- Certify a geometry-dominated directional limit only with an exact asymptotic statement and its
  exceptional source set disclosed.
- Preserve scalar-source and polarization/source-state typing.
