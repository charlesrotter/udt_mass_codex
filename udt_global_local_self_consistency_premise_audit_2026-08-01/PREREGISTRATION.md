## Terms that must remain separate

1. A **restriction/readout** maps a supplied complete configuration to local or global data.
2. **Admissibility** says a supplied configuration belongs to an allowed class.
3. **Existence** says at least one configuration satisfies stated conditions.
4. **Uniqueness/selection** distinguishes one configuration, orbit, or branch.
5. A **return operation** maps readout data back into a nonidentity condition on the supplied
   configuration.
6. A **fixed-point equation** uses both directions and must not be inferred from a readout graph.
7. **Variational**, **boundary**, **stability**, and **observational** consistency are separate
   possible realizations and cannot be merged by the word “self-consistent.”

## Passing gate for “derived mutual determination”

An interpretation passes only if current authority supplies all of:

1. one complete domain containing every variable on which the law acts;
2. typed global and local codomains;
3. the metric-native forward readout;
4. a nonidentity return operation or equivalent constraint with no imported action/source/carrier;
5. its comparison/equality or distinguished zero;
6. complete observer/boundary action where needed;
7. a logical implication from the registered premise rather than a restatement of the desired
   fixed point.

Failure of any item prevents `DERIVED`. A verbal bootstrap slogan, existence assumption, or graph
`O=R(X)` cannot pass.
