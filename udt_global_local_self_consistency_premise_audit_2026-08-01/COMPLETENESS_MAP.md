- Exact 1,424-path additions-only source universe.
- Eighteen current premise roles, twelve interpretations, and eight logical return types.
- The distinction among complete specification, readout, admissibility, existence, selection,
  return, fixed point, variation, stability, and observation, plus seven distinct “minimum” levels.

## Not covered

- Unknown future UDT premises or mathematical consequences not present in the frozen source set.
- Construction, ranking, or testing of an actual consistency relation.
- A complete physical readout vector or configuration domain.
- Any action, carrier, source, boundary law, energy/mass functional, density response, dynamics,
  solution branch, stability solve, T4, ODE/PDE, or GPU work.

## Ten-criterion placement

This is one logical/ontology tile, not a solver. Fields, action terms, full equations, boundary,
topology, dynamics, branches, stability, and regime selection remain open or conditional exactly as
in their controlling sources. The only sharpened object is the minimum logical type of an
operational bootstrap premise.

## Quantifier

The result says the exact frozen record contains no derivation of a nontrivial global/local
consistency relation. It does not prove deductive independence from the founding premises, does not
exclude a future theorem from those same premises, and does not judge whether Charles should adopt
the working posit.
