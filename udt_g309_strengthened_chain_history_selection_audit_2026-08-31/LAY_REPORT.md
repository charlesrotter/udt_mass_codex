# G309 lay report

We found the exact place where two ideas had been getting blended together.

The strengthened UDT postulates tell us how one complete geometry must consistently control
clocks, rulers, observer comparisons, and causality. The Hopf work adds a surprisingly coherent
global structure. But those rules still accept more than one evolving geometry.

We proved that directly by taking the attractive round-universe history and making a perfectly
smooth change only in its later evolution. The altered universe keeps the same entire earlier
quiet era, remains causally well behaved, keeps its compact spherical slices, and keeps the same
Hopf structures. Yet its curvature later becomes different. The current postulates do not reject
it.

There is also genuinely good news. The provisional equation found in G301 *does* reject that
change. If we provisionally accept that equation, the positive completed universe is no longer an
arbitrary curve. Its full expansion/contraction history is fixed in shape, leaving only one size
or curvature scale to determine. An observational anchor could then calibrate that one scale.

So the recent work was not merely circling. It has produced a strong, nearly complete conditional
spacetime. The remaining gap is precise: prove or provisionally adopt the G301 equation as a UDT
law. The Hopf agreement is evidence that this candidate is worth taking seriously, but topology
alone cannot prove the equation because it does not hear the changing scale.

An outside adversarial reviewer checked this conclusion and found no scientific error. It did find
that one verification script depended on a math package absent from the sealed review environment.
That replay has now been replaced by a dependency-free version; this repair changes none of the
physics or mathematics. A narrow follow-up review of the repair is still pending.
