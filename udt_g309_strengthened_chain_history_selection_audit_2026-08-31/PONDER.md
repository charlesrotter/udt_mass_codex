# G309 ponder

The recent development may have done two different things that are easy to blend together.

First, the strengthened postulates make the geometry much harder to corrupt: one metric owns local
clocks, rulers, free fall and causal cones; completed pair relations retain their screen data; and
co-presence cannot be turned into instantaneous signalling. These are real constraints.

Second, G301 introduced a deliberately bounded search over possible field-law types. Inside that
search, one exceptional equation admits a positive constant-curvature completion. G304--G308 then
showed that this completion has a regular global `S3` geometry and intrinsic Hopf structure.

The danger is reverse ownership: because the downstream geometry is coherent and unexpectedly
fruitful, one may accidentally treat the upstream G301 candidate equation as though the founding
postulates had derived it. G309 therefore asks a sharper question than another general
"underdetermined" audit: if we retain all of the new global and topological structure, can we still
deform the time-live metric while satisfying the actual founded/working compatibility statements?

If yes, the work is not lost. It means the chain has identified a strong conditional completion but
has not yet proved ownership of its equation. If no, the exact postulate or topological identity
that produces a nonzero residual must be exhibited.
