## Whole question

```text
H_pair = diag(-1,+1),
P(phi) = exp(phi H_pair).
```

Determine which currently available geometric data can solder that abstract
pair into a complete four-dimensional Lorentzian coframe in a way that is
covariant under observer-frame changes. Classify every candidate data type in
`DATA_UNIVERSE.tsv`, including causal and degeneracy strata, rather than
assuming a radial slot, spectator angular sector, carrier, action, or desired
global branch.

The exact local problem is to construct either:

1. an ordered Lorentzian clock/ruler two-frame and a complete endomorphism
   whose restriction is `H_pair`; or
2. a covariant complete endomorphism that realizes the founded pair
   democratically without selecting one ruler direction.

For every construction, quotient local-Lorentz presentation freedom and
record remaining physical moduli. Existence is not selection, and a formula
valid only after an observer, path direction, derivative tensor, or boundary
normal is supplied remains conditional on that input.

## Bounded regime

The primary classification is pointwise and algebraic on an oriented,
time-oriented real four-dimensional Lorentzian vector space. It covers:

- metric and scalar anchors at zero jet;
- one nonzero covector/vector, across timelike, spacelike, and null strata;
- one supplied observer;
- a supplied orthonormal observer/separation pair;
- two independent covectors or a nondegenerate simple bivector;

- scalar dimensional anchors `c_E`, `G_obs`, and provisional `hbar`.

It does not classify arbitrary functions of every metric jet, nonlinear
global bundles, path-dependent transport, or finite-cell solutions. Those
remainders must stay explicit.

## Candidate acceptance rules

A candidate is a covariant lift only if:

1. its formula is tensorial and transforms by conjugation under a frame
   change;
2. every denominator and causal-domain restriction is explicit;
3. it reproduces `H_pair` on an actually identified ordered clock/ruler pair,
   or is clearly labeled a democratic `1+3` realization rather than a
   selected two-plane;
4. local-Lorentz gauge directions are separated from physical metric
   responses;
5. its stabilizer leaves no unrecorded physical modulus; and
6. it uses no data absent from its declared input row.
