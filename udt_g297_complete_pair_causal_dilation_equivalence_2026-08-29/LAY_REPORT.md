# G297 lay report

Charles's clarification was useful, but the audit caught an important overreach.

The useful part is this: the physical observer comparison should come from the metric's complete
two-way causal relation—not from an arbitrary curve someone draws between two clocks. Radar timing
gives us a precise clock-comparison component of that relation. It also preserves causality: it is
an observable probe of co-presence, not instantaneous signalling and not the definition of a
universal “right now.”

We also ruled out a seductive shortcut. One radar clock-rate number is not always equal to the
one-leg mutual scalar from the reciprocal kernel. They agree for ordinary inertial relative motion,
but not for a generic static gravitational clock profile, and transverse screen effects add more
information. The full relation cannot be compressed to one number.

What remains unfinished is the mathematical connector. A two-way causal diamond is a collection of
emission, reflection, return, clock, route, and directional-delay data. The reciprocal kernel
expects a regular two-dimensional pair geometry with two independent directions. We have not yet
proved the unique, gauge-independent construction that turns the first object into the second.

So G297 accomplished three things:

- it confirmed that the clarified causal framing is substantive;
- it derived and checked the clock-timing part;
- it isolated the exact missing construction and prevented us from pretending a flat-space scalar
  coincidence had solved it.

This narrows the next question considerably. We now need to build—or prove impossible—the natural
map from the full bidirectional causal-delay family to the completed two-dimensional observer-pair
geometry. Only after that passes can we say the “germ” gap is closed. The universe-wide metric
history remains a later question.
