# Lay report — we turned the orchestra on

G59 showed us every instrument and the mixing board. This audit finally allowed every instrument
to vibrate through time.

We found a real harmony rule: when reciprocal, angular, shift, scale, and mixing channels also vary
across space, their changes must fit together as parts of one smooth instrument. The mixing channel
cannot vibrate independently of the reciprocal and angular channels.

But this is comparable to saying every musician must remain connected to their instrument while
playing. It does not provide the musical score. Any smooth melody, frequency, rhythm, or phase can
still be played. The metric tells us exactly how to record the resulting sound, but not which song
the universe chooses.

That is productive. We have ruled out “time was accidentally frozen” as the entire missing piece.
Time-live structure exposes a genuine coupled orchestra, while sharpening what remains missing to a
selector of compatible histories. Bootstrap may later tune or select those histories, but it was
not used here.
