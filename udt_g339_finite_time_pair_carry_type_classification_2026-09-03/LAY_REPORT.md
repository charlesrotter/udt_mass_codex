# G339 lay report

The G338 result does not disappear, but we have learned exactly what it describes.

Imagine two nearby observers who remain at fixed labels in the expanding or contracting spacetime.
Their separation changes because the geometry between those fixed observer tracks changes. That is
the carry used in G338, and its finite-time response remains exact.

Now imagine one observer carrying a carefully nonrotating local ruler. Parallel or Fermi transport
is specifically designed to keep that local ruler one unit long. Its own clock-and-ruler matrix
therefore stays quiet. This is not a contradiction: the local ruler and the separation between two
observer tracks are different objects.

We tested a continuous family between those cases. The raw pair readings change continuously with
the carry choice. We also tested rotating local rulers and explicit accelerated observers. Their
own orthonormal clock-and-ruler readings can remain constant even in the nonflat spacetime, because
the missing change is recorded in how the frame is being transported.

Most importantly, the geometry is not lost. If we retain both the pair reading and the declared
carry, we can subtract the frame's contribution and recover the same spacetime deformation. What
remains open is which observer tracks Nature populates; the metric has not selected that population
here.

A fresh external reviewer rebuilt and attacked the result, replayed all checks, and accepted this
bounded distinction without requiring a scientific repair.
