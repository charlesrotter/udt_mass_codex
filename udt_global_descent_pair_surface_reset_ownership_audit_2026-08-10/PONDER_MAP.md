## Lay picture

An observer law must work like a relay race around the whole finite cell. A local running lane is not
enough. The baton must survive each handoff, and an observer rebuilt at the next station must know how
its locally defined baton relates to the one that arrived.

This audit therefore asks three separate questions:

1. **Lane:** does a complete metric branch supply a regular pair surface?
2. **Handoff:** do overlap comparisons compose, including honest path holonomy when present?
3. **Reset:** does the metric own the conversion between carried data and the next observer's
   independently rebuilt data?

Only a branch that owns all three can supply the sought global observer relation. The map is allowed
to end with several partial structures and no winner.
