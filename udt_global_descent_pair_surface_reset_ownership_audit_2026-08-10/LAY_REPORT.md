We found that the bridge is more complete than it looked, but one joint is still missing.

R17 supplies real lanes through the whole finite cell: a family of clock-and-ruler surfaces shaped
like cylinders. It also supplies a lawful way to carry the angular “orchestra” along any chosen
route. The route can return rotated after a loop; that is real geometry, not an error.

The projector-level handoff is now exact: when the baton arriving at observer B is not aligned with
the baton B rebuilds from its own local metric, the geometry supplies the complete circle of valid
alignments and a lawful way to compose that whole circle without arbitrarily choosing one. What it
does not yet supply is the calibration rule that turns that family into one physical clock-and-ruler
reset.

A different branch, R18, has a perfect handoff for clocks alone. But it has no ruler, so it cannot be
quietly combined with R17 to complete the answer.

In short: **the lanes and route transport now exist; the calibration reset and choice of physical
lane/route remain open.** This is a narrower and more useful gap than “the observer law is missing,”
but it is not closure yet.
