## What the proposed FC condition does and does not test

For one fixed experiment or one invertible p(L) curve, writing q=Q(p) can be
only a reparameterization. The meaningful condition is that **the same** Q applies
to all declared events, directions, prepared frames and sufficiently small L.
The proposal states these quantifiers correctly. Its new test does not establish
that current UDT requires them.

Within this product, unboosted aligned experiments range over p=sec(kL)>1 and
fix Q=p/(2-p^2) on a right neighborhood of1. A boosted experiment has p in that
same interval yet yields a different q. Therefore allowing an arbitrary smooth
single-valued Q does not save FC for this product. Merely fitting a different Q
to the boosted curve alone would change the quantifier. Adding L, direction,
transverse curvature or preparation/history as an argument would weaken the
physical trial; those quantities cannot be erased from the complete comparison
and then silently restored only when needed to pass.

This failure does not refute the native scalar kernel: its supplied regular
completed pair and known readout never assert that one first-leg scalar determines
a later future-leg scalar for every experiment. No preferred observer and ULC1's
universal-law meaning similarly require no such compression. FC for an isolated
positional contribution would need its own independently defined attribution;
the present calculation concerns net p,q only.
