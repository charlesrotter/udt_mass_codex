## Independent proof audit of the crossing clock

For the supplied static conformal metric and a future freely falling probe with
zero transverse momentum, conserved `E=Omega^2 dt/dtau` and unit normalization
give

```
(dx/dtau)^2=(E^2-Omega^2)/Omega^4.
```

Taking the positive root and requiring `E>max Omega` yields exactly the proposed
T(E) and tau(E) integrals. Since Omega=1 at launch, `E=gamma(v)` uses a shared
local preparation; any finite positive maximum can be crossed by a sufficiently
large but finite gamma. This is a declared ideal test-clock availability
assumption, not a new matter law or a statement about practical apparatus.

Put q=Omega^2 and D=E^2-1. Direct differentiation at epsilon zero gives

```
d/depsilon [E/sqrt(E^2-q)] = E f/D^(3/2),
d/depsilon [q/sqrt(E^2-q)] = (2E^2-1) f/D^(3/2).
```

The compact interval and a fixed positive no-turn margin justify integration
of these derivatives and a uniform O(epsilon^2) remainder for each fixed bounded
f and E. Both proposed first variations are correct. For a nonnegative nonzero
bump and positive epsilon, there is also an exact distinction: each integrand
is strictly increasing with q wherever its no-turn denominator stays positive.
Thus neither discrimination nor its sign depends solely on the asymptotic
expansion. Negative or sign-changing bumps should keep their declared different
scope; a zero first variation is not exact indistinguishability.

The proposal appropriately repairs the *data set* by allowing an interior
timelike clock, which Trial N excluded. It does not derive a selecting law.
Reporting the probe's accumulated proper time at reception avoids synchronizing
separate exterior clocks. The optional return requires its declared controlled
turnaround; it is not a geodesic through the turnaround and is not an inverse
future comparison. The text already acknowledges this ideal protocol.

Transit-integral blindness to profile order is correct, since both integrands
are functions of q(x) without derivatives and dx integration is unchanged by
`x -> L-x`. To use a reversed profile as a distinct example, choose a bump that
is not symmetric under that reflection. Reflection swaps the labeled endpoint
laboratories, so it is not an allowed endpoint-fixing relabeling. This is a
minor explicit-choice clarification; the distribution-dependence observation
already supplies the claimed limit and no general rigidity claim is made.

The radar formula is also correct in this particular strip. With the exterior
clock at x=0, conformally unchanged rays give `s=t-x`, `r=t+x`, so radar time
and position are t,x. Along the measured probe,
`d tau^2=Omega^2(dT_radar^2-dX_radar^2)`. Dividing gives the proposed identity
for Omega^2. The proper tick labels and their echo pairing are new data compared
