Date: 2026-08-16

## Whole question

For a supplied smooth time-oriented spherically symmetric metric in areal form

\[
g=h_{ab}(x)dx^a dx^b+R(x)^2\gamma_{AB}d\vartheta^A d\vartheta^B,
\]

a regular center event, a central observer, and one normalized radial point-observer exponential
query, does the metric itself force the full angular Jacobi screen map on every finite regular
branch to be

\[
\mathcal D_{\rm sky}=R\,O,\qquad O\in O(2),\qquad
|\det\mathcal D_{\rm sky}|=R^2?
\]

This is an arbitrary finite-radius, time-live theorem test. It is not an SNe fit, a history solve,
or a transfer-law derivation.
