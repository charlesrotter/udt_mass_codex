# G223 map — null-ribbon density overlap and calibration carry

Date: 2026-08-22

## Whole question

On an atlas of supplied regular affine null-incidence ribbons, determine the exact geometric type
and overlap law of the G222 quantity locally written

\[
\vartheta_i=a_i[d\lambda_i],
\qquad a_i=-g(J_i,K_i)>0.
\]

Separate four questions that must not be conflated:

1. what the metric owns without a chart;
2. what a supplied clock calibration and affine parameter trivialize;
3. when a local or global scalar ruler coordinate exists;
4. what additional identification is needed to carry ruler data through an actual three-observer
   composition.

## Exact bounded regime

- one smooth Lorentzian pair surface at a time;
- a regular null vertical line field generated locally by affine future null tangents;
- positive time and ruler orientations;
- smooth foliation-preserving affine overlap maps;
- supplied observer-event incidence maps when composition is tested.

Singular ribbons, caustics that destroy the declared atlas, branch aggregation, physical protocol
selection, history selection, transfer, `X_max`, source, action, matter, mass, bootstrap, and
signalling are omitted.

## Metric-led versus template-led

Metric-led: the load-bearing object must be constructed from the pullback metric and its null
vertical line. Coordinate formulas are representations and controls, not added physics.

## Prior boundaries

- G214: completed pair tensors descend on a supplied calibrated cover; arbitrary distinct pair
  surfaces do not acquire a product merely by sharing observer labels.
- G216: proper-clock event-pairing derivatives compose by the chain rule on an actual composite.
- G222: one supplied affine null family owns a local Lorentz pair plane and the positive scalar
  `a=-g(J,K)` along each ray; `a[d lambda]` is invariant under positive affine rescaling with the
  clock chart held fixed.
