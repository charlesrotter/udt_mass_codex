# G223 ponder

Date: 2026-08-22

The likely simplification is that no new ruler mechanism is missing locally. A regular Lorentzian
two-plane with a null vertical line already pairs the quotient clock line perfectly with the
vertical ruler line. The metric owns that mixed pairing.

What can remain open is global trivialization. Calling the local coefficient `a d lambda` a global
one-form hides two independent choices: a clock coframe and a horizontal extension of the vertical
covector. A scalar can always be integrated locally along an interval fiber. A single scalar over a
whole atlas additionally needs compatible clock and affine trivializations, no forbidden periods,
and a vanishing Cech mismatch after the permitted transition maps.

The three-observer question is similarly typed. G216 composes clock derivatives. It cannot by
itself identify the vertical lines of two distinct pair ribbons. If a compatible vertical gluing is
supplied, the metric pairing should carry functorially. Without it, multiplying ruler densities is
not a geometrically typed operation.

This audit must be willing to correct the provisional startup phrase “closedness permits a scalar
ruler coordinate” if closedness belongs only to one chosen full one-form representative.
