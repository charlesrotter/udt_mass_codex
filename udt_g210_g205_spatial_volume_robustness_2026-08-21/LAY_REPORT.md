# G210 lay report

The new piece is the metric's spatial-volume knob. Earlier work changed overall spacetime size,
changed spatial shape without changing volume, mixed spatial directions, and shifted the causal
cone. This calculation isolates the remaining scalar that makes all three spatial directions
larger or smaller together while leaving the clock coefficient fixed.

It interlocks cleanly:

- spatial shape decides the ellipsoid's shape;
- spatial volume makes the ellipsoid uniformly wider or narrower;
- shift moves its center;
- the completed observer pair reads the combined metric afterward.

Controlled volume histories behave well. But an exact smooth shrinking example keeps causal time
well organized while light reaches the outer end in finite affine time. So spatial volume is a
real global metric degree of freedom, not harmless decoration.

Nothing here chooses Nature's volume profile or `X_max`. It classifies what the supplied metric
does when this instrument is active.
