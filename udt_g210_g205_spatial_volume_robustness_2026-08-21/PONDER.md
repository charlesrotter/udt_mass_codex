# G210 ponder — why this is the next scalar

Date: 2026-08-21

For positive three-dimensional spatial metrics, the determinant supplies one invariant relative
volume scalar. Relative to a supplied positive reference `H`, every positive `K` has the unique
decomposition

```text
K = exp(2 sigma) K_bar
sigma = (1/6) log(det(K)/det(H))
det(K_bar) = det(H).
```

This scalar is not G206's common spacetime conformal mode because the lapse is held fixed. It is
not G207/G208 determinant-one shape and it is not G209 shift. It therefore closes one genuine
missing diagonal slot without inventing a mechanism.

The calculation may show how supplied sectors interlock, but it cannot select `sigma(r,t,angles)`.
Selection, observation, and `X_max` remain outside this tile.
