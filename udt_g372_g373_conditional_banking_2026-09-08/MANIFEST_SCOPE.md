# Evidence manifest scope

EVIDENCE_SHA256SUMS is generated only after banking and final fidelity gates.
It covers every file in this new banking package, including ignored captured
stdout, except exactly itself and the later COMPLETION_RECORD.md publication
receipt. It does not rewrite or subsume the original CD campaign manifest.
No protected payload is enumerated by this manifest.

INTEGRATION_FREEZE_SHA256SUMS pins the12 targets during the full356 audit
and initial fidelity review. FINAL_TARGET_SHA256SUMS pins those12 after the
three completion-only status sentences changed. This preserves the actual
sequence; later status words do not acquire earlier review timestamps.
The bank/proposal/checker, registry and verifier remain identical across the
two freezes. A manifest is correspondence evidence, not scientific proof,
independence, adoption or chronology by itself.

SOURCE_PINS_SHA256SUMS covers the15 declared unchanged core-source/CD-manifest/
completion/helper inputs. All81 original CD campaign files and all354 earlier
registry rows are independently compared with the baseline by the review.
Historical original NOT_BANKED records stay unchanged; current disposition
is supplied by this later owner-authorized banking record and execution gates.

The later COMPLETION_RECORD.md will report actual scoped commit/push and
post-publication checks, not predict success. It is excluded to avoid a
self-referential hash or mutating the frozen evidence to add a Git receipt.
Its own final commit remains visible in repository history.
