## 2. Covariant selection: off-shell identity and variational integrability

    nabla^a E_ab=(a/2+b) nabla_b R.                              (V1)

S=0 gives constant R on connected solutions, where the divergence vanishes for
every b. One cannot use that on-shell fact to recover the off-shell identity.

The same coefficient is necessary for E itself to be an unrestricted metric
Euler-Lagrange response in this class. Use its correctly densitized source

index variations multiply zero, leaving minus the index-raised linear response.
The common minus sign does not affect formal self-adjointness. Its curvature
linearization is

    RicL_ab(h)=1/2(partial_c partial_a h^c_b
                  +partial_c partial_b h^c_a-box h_ab-partial_a partial_b tr h),
    RL(h)=partial_a partial_b h^ab-box tr h.                     (V2)

Helmholtz integrability requires the linearized Euler-Lagrange operator to be

    <k,EL(h)>-<h,EL(k)>=-2(a+2b).                               (V3)

This symbol test is a necessary condition, not a finite-sample proof of general
integrability. Sufficiency is supplied separately: for b=-a/2 the full nonlinear

The a=0 case is retained: b must also be zero under either test, leaving the
