# Lay report

We found a plausible missing **joint**, not the finished bootstrap.

Imagine the universe-cell as a musical instrument whose ends are joined in one of several ways.
The previous audit proved that all eight joins can be built as complete instruments. This audit
asked whether the way the instrument is joined can send information back into how its local pieces
must behave.

The answer is yes, on specific branches.

Whenever the angular screen has to change while going around the cell, it bends in two opposite
directions at once. That bending creates a nonzero geometric response throughout the interior. It
is not added physics; it comes directly from the complete metric.

In three kinds of completed cell, the whole cell also has exactly one global harmonic direction.
Think of it as the one note that remains well defined only after listening to the entire instrument.
Its strength at any one location depends on an integral over the whole cell. The global instrument
therefore returns a locally varying geometric direction.

The local midpoint curvature cannot choose that direction—it is spatially equal in every
direction there. The selection genuinely comes from the topology and metric of the whole completed
cell, which is why this is more interesting than another local curvature marker.

One frozen branch—the hyperbolic join—has both properties unavoidably: its screen must change, and
its global harmonic direction is unique. The parabolic join must also change, but it has two global
harmonic directions, so the metric has not told us which one should play the ruler role.

The flat cases teach the complementary lesson. Three of them select the entire clock-and-ruler
plane but do not select one observer's clock axis. Different observers may split that plane
differently, which is consistent with frame reciprocity.

This could explain why local mass calculations have repeatedly stalled: a local configuration may
need a global completion before its correct geometric response is even defined. But we have not
shown that this response governs matter, stability, density, or mass. We have found wiring; we have
not yet connected it to the engine.

`X_max` also remains open. The present cells contain a freely chosen circumference and constant
depth, not the position-dependent observer-pair dilation needed to derive the asymptotic distance
limit.
