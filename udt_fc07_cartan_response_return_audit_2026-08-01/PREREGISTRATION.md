# FC07 Cartan, intrinsic-response, and return-channel audit — preregistration

Date: 2026-08-01  
Mode: metric-led, CPU exact/symbolic, no physical selection

## Whole question

For the eight complete off-shell FC07 mapping-torus metrics already constructed, determine what
the **complete metric itself**—as opposed to the displayed bundle coordinates—recovers about:

1. a timelike clock line;
2. a spacelike reciprocal/ruler line and rank-one projector in its positive complement;
3. the complementary rank-two screen;
4. their full Levi-Civita/Cartan connection, curvature, transport, and degeneracy strata; and
5. a possible global-completion-to-local-response channel.

The audit will then type-check whether any surviving object could enter the future observer-pair
dilation/`X_max` and global–local bootstrap chain. It will not assume that connection and will not
construct an action, source, density rule, stability condition, or matter interpretation.

## Exact bounded regime

The frozen witness family is

```text
Sigma_M = ([0,1] x T2)/((1,y)~(0,My)),    M in the eight frozen GL(2,Z) controls,
h(s) = (1-chi(s))h0 + chi(s) M^T h0 M,
g = -c_E^2 exp(-2phi0) dt^2 + L^2 exp(2phi0) ds^2 + dy^T h(s)dy.
```

The calculation covers:

- the full symbolic positive-definite `h0=[[a,b],[b,d]]` domain;
- the general local jets of a smooth interpolation, expressed through `h,h',h''` and through
  `chi,chi',chi''`;
- all eight monodromies, all seven frozen endpoint-congruence operator classes, and both
  orientation strata;
- the six invariant constant-screen subfamilies and the varying-screen strata, including the
  forced parabolic and hyperbolic cases; and
- exact generic rational controls at `h0=[[2,1/3],[1/3,5]]`, including the symmetric midpoint of
  the registered flat-step interpolation.

This is not all four-dimensional UDT metrics, non-block extensions, nonconstant `phi`, time-live
geometry, other completions, or an on-shell solution space.

## Required derivations

1. Derive the complete torsion-free Cartan/Levi-Civita connection for arbitrary `h(s)` without
   diagonalizing or suppressing screen shear/rotation.
2. Derive the full Riemann, Ricci, scalar, spatial curvature-operator, and curvature-nullity data
   in basis-covariant matrix form.
3. Distinguish the coordinate/bundle split from metric-intrinsic candidates using uniqueness,
   degeneracy, local continuation, global descent, and lattice-basis covariance gates.
4. When a rank-one ruler projector is genuinely metric-recovered, calculate `DP` and the parent
   relative projector curvature. Where it is not recovered, retain the response only as a
   bundle-relative conditional readout.
5. Carry all objects through the seam, including determinant-minus-one reflection transitions.
6. Record constant-screen, rank-one-variation, full-rank-variation, curvature-degenerate, and
   response-degenerate strata rather than deleting them.
7. Type-check whether any derived object supplies (a) only a forward readout, (b) a parametric
   global-to-local admissibility channel, or (c) the missing nonidentity bootstrap return. The
   third requires an independently defined return relation and cannot be inferred from variation.
8. Test whether `c_E`, `L`, `phi0`, completion, and the derived invariants determine an observer-pair
   dilation endpoint. Merely carrying the freely chosen `L` or constant `phi0` does not derive
   `X_max`.

## Falsification and certification contract

- A universal metric-derived clock/ruler/projector claim fails if any complete witness lacks a
  unique intrinsic continuation or if the object depends on the displayed bundle chart.
- A global object fails if it does not transform correctly under the seam or lattice basis change.
- A response channel fails if its nonzero/zero classification changes under screen-frame gauge or
  if it is only one selected curvature component.
- A bootstrap-return claim fails unless a nonidentity relation maps independently computed global
  data back to local admissibility on the same complete configuration.
- An `X_max` derivation fails if it merely reports the free proper circumference/length or constant
  depth rather than a metric-fixed observer-pair asymptote.
- Constant, zero-curvature, degenerate, nonorientable, and no-projector strata must remain in the
  result.
- All load-bearing algebra must be reconstructed by an implementation that imports no production
  functions.
- Semantic mutations must fail for universal promotion, branch ranking, projector substitution,
  response promotion, bootstrap promotion, `X_max` promotion, or physical interpretation.

## Maximum allowed conclusion

At most the audit may establish a complete, stratified, metric-derived geometric response channel
on specified FC07 strata and show exactly how monodromy/completion data alter it. It may not select
a completion or metric, supply a native field equation, close bootstrap, derive `X_max`, or claim
matter, mass emergence, stability, carrier emergence, or an action.
