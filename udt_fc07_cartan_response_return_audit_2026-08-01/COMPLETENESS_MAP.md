# Completeness map

## Covered completely within the registered family

- all eight frozen monodromy controls and seven endpoint metric-readout classes;
- both orientation strata;
- arbitrary SPD `h0` and general local screen two-jets;
- the complete coordinate and orthonormal Cartan/curvature algebra;
- constant versus nonconstant interpolation strata;
- full rank-one projector response where the projector is supplied or recovered;
- first-cohomology dimensions and the unique harmonic representative when `b1=1`;
- constant-screen discrete-holonomy fixed spaces; and
- explicit bootstrap and `X_max` type gates.

## Exact but conditional

- the complete metrics use the chosen constant-depth block extension;
- the nonzero response is bundle-relative until the line-selection gate passes;
- the harmonic ruler is metric-intrinsic on the registered product only when `b1=1`;
- the curvature clock certificate is established on the six generic varying controls;
- the midpoint isotropy uses the symmetric flat-step interpolation; and
- constant-screen reciprocal planes do not select observer axes.

## Not covered

- nonconstant `phi`, reciprocal-screen cross terms, shift, or time dependence;
- every possible nonlocal invariant that might resolve a `b1>1` line ambiguity;
- other completion families or all `GL(2,Z)` conjugacy/diffeomorphism classes;
- native equations, action, source, boundary dynamics, density, carrier, or stability;
- same-solution global/local feedback; and
- observer-pair dilation or an `X_max` asymptote.
