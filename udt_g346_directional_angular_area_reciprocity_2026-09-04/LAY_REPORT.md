# G346 lay report — what the two observers' sky patches become

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: externally accepted, derived, conditional, and bounded

Take observer A and draw an extremely small patch around one direction in A's sky. The metric tells
us how the neighboring null paths from that patch spread out into a small physical screen patch at
observer B. G346 calculated the ratio:

```text
screen area at B / sky-angle patch at A.
```

It also calculated the reverse ratio, starting with B's sky and ending on A's screen.

The two ratios are generally not equal. That is not a failure of reciprocity. The observers' clocks
measure the null direction at different frequencies, and the forward-to-reverse area ratio is
exactly the square of that already-known metric frequency ratio. Once that directional clock effect
is balanced, the geometric mean of the two area ratios is exactly the inverse of the symmetric G345
scalar. So G345 now has a clear geometric role rather than being an isolated invariant.

The result also joins consistently through an intermediate observer, but not by simply multiplying
the two segment areas. The shared intermediate screen must be eliminated with the stationary join
factor already derived in G344/G345.
