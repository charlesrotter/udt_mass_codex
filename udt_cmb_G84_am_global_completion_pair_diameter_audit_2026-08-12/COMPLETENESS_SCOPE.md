# G84 completeness scope

G84 is one stationary metric tile.

Covered:

- every one of the 197 frozen G75 `AM` profiles;
- the exact radial and full angular spatial geometry;
- the transparent analytic continuation of the registered formulas;
- the minimal doubled, simply connected zero-mixing constant-curvature Lorentzian completion
  candidate;
- the full nonzero-mixing coefficient at the candidate surface;
- central geodesic observer re-centering on the zero-mixing isometry orbit.

Dropped or still open:

- the generic ten-function complete coframe;
- time-live mixing histories and nonstationary observer queries;
- arbitrary lapse functions and non-polynomial mixing;
- accelerated-observer frame sharing;
- a physical pair-separation operator;
- uniqueness of the global topology or identification;
- physical profile, topology, scale `R`, endpoint, and source ownership;
- action, equations of motion, matter, bootstrap feedback, boundary variation, CMB readout, and
  local signalling.

Those omissions can carry structure. In particular, the mixed stationary continuation obstruction
is a reason to examine time-live completion later, not a no-go for the mixed metric or UDT.
