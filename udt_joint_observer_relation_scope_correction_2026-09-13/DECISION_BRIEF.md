No new physical law or geometry promotion.

We had allowed an important distinction to blur in conversation. UDT's primary
metric already makes its clock, ruler and angular behavior part of one geometry.
The broader angular examples do not establish permission to adjust these
independently in physical UDT.

A shared clock reading can still belong to different full comparisons: direction,
ruler information and neighboring geometry are additional parts of the record.
Our mathematical examples did not hold that complete record fixed. That surviving
fact must not be used to claim arbitrary physical angular freedom.

The repair is recorded in the maintained roadmap, research plan and consolidated
account. The immediate priority is to recover and enforce the full joint relation
before declaring broader geometries admitted or choosing a complete response law.
The volume-response proposal stays unadopted; we have not justified its completeness.

This could eventually narrow the geometries available to UDT and require work on
their dependent predictions. Our impact map distinguishes that possible scientific
consequence from undoing sound mathematical results. We have not yet established
an additional native restriction, so we cannot honestly say which further
geometries are excluded or estimate the resulting rework.

The unresolved question is specific: beyond the primary round branch, what exact
UDT relation couples the clock/ruler/angular sectors across complete comparisons?
The existing conditional NAP1 restriction is retained, with its unclosed response
membership premise explicit. We have neither filled the gap with a new law nor
proved that a new postulate is necessary. Return for discussion after verification.
