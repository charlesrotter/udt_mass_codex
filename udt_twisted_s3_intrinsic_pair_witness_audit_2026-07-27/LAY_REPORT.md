# Lay readout

We closed the specific join that was still missing.

Previously we had one complete example showing that a metric could identify its own clock direction,
and another showing that a twisted clock could point to the reciprocal ruler. Because those were two
different examples, we were not allowed to glue the conclusions together.

Now one and the same complete geometry does both.

Think of three curvature measurements as a unique geometric fingerprint. In each of the six tested
twisted geometries, that fingerprint leaves exactly one possible continuous clock direction. Once
the metric has identified that clock, the way it twists through the angular geometry points to the
matching ruler direction. Nothing was added from outside the metric.

This is a meaningful structural result, but it is not yet a universe. We deliberately chose a smooth
test pattern to prove that such geometries exist. We have not derived the rule that selects that
pattern, made it evolve, or shown that matter inhabits it.

Co-presence is still present as a working interpretation: a whole universe could contain this
clock/ruler skeleton everywhere at once. But the calculation neither needs co-presence nor turns it
into instantaneous communication. The complete law and the matter/signal response remain open.
