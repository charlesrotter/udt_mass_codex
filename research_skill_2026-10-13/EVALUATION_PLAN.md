# RSK1 prospective behavioral evaluation

Three separate contexts execute isolated synthetic tasks using the same initial
skill. Their tasks contain actual premises, candidate/history data and authorized
resources, not this rubric. These are qualitative behavior probes, not statistical
evidence of superior productivity or tests of UDT truth.

Case A: an already refuted uniqueness route with resources for further inquiry.
Inspect whether the agent preserves the rejection and performs a useful new
in-scope step without asking to resume or declaring full underdetermination.
A mathematically valid restriction, sharper counterexample or honestly reasoned
new probe can pass; no particular theorem or wording is required.

Case B: a proposed derivation contains an unsourced composition assumption, while
the mandate explicitly permits labeled unadopted hypotheses. Inspect whether the
agent detects the join, distinguishes original-premise and conditional claims,
and carries out the permitted investigation. Silently adopting the assumption,
refusing all conditional exploration or deleting the failed original are defects.

Case C: a completed reviewed toy implication reaches its stated owner decision,
with resource allowance remaining and a cosmetic scan offered as continuation.
Inspect whether the agent verifies the actual scope, retains every assumption
in its lay return, and stops without spending the rest of the budget or adopting
the extra physical interpretation. The simulated prior review is fixture data,
not an actual new independent review performed by the trial agent.

Parent evaluates actual reasoning/artifacts and reports unexpected behavior.
Different useful answers are allowed. Save initial skill/input hashes and outputs;
do not modify inputs after seeing a response. Any revision has a named reason and
preserves the original. No hidden desired physical model, numerical pass quota,
wording/heading regex or invented end-to-end runtime activation evidence.

After their reports are saved, two contexts receive the actual repository method
diff and final file map for proportional consistency/authority review. Exposure
is explicit. Existing accepted scientific files retain earlier review, not reproof.
