## Independent argument A: what the conformal reference compares

Frame: exact metric-led argument, smooth time-oriented Lorentz4 on an identified
connected domain. Choosing the domain and proposed reference criterion is
free-and-explored / UNADOPTED physical comparison structure. Ordinary proper
time and null readout are pinned by W4 at its WORKING/POSIT grade. No approximation,
instrument, field equation for the physical metric, or all-metric coverage is assumed.

Let g0=exp(2u)g and use the identical timelike curves, events and regular
future-null correspondence in both descriptions. For any parameter s on a
clock, dτ0=exp(u)dτ. Conformal transformations preserve null tracks, so the
same endpoint arrival map applies when the curves really are held fixed.
Consequently

    Z0 = exp(u(o)-u(e)) Z,
    Z/Z0 = exp(-u(o)+u(e)).

This is an exact same-history comparison, not yet a realized second experiment.
It can be computed from a physically reconstructed g and justified reference;
the reference proper intervals are counterfactual unless separately realized.
It is not generally the contrast between independently parallel-prepared freely
falling laboratories. A conformal change changes timelike geodesics and proper
length preparation. GRL1's connection-difference argument shows that demanding
two nonparallel common geodesic directions at each point collapses u to a
constant. Holding one history fixed is legitimate, but its acceleration and
preparation must be described rather than called identical free fall.

The scalar u must actually exist smoothly and be single-valued on the declared
domain, exp(u)>0, with Ric(g0)=0 checked as a tensor. Local integrability is
not global solvability. Boundary/normalization conditions, topological obstructions,
zeros or singularities, and branch/domain selection cannot be skipped. The
condition Ric(g0)=0 is a substantive comparison choice. GR-filter authority does
not derive it, and Ricci-flat vacuum is not the whole of established GR.

One-point normalization does not generally prove uniqueness. Independent exact
counterexample: take g=η, p=0, σ=1+a(t-x)>0 on a connected neighborhood and
u=-log σ. Both g and g0=σ^-2η are flat, and u(p)=0. To check the claim without
assuming it, use the n=4 conformal Ricci formula

    Ric[σ^-2 g] = Ric[g] + 2σ^-1 Hess_g σ
                  + [σ^-1 Box_g σ - 3σ^-2|dσ|_g²]g.

Here Hess σ=0, Box σ=0 and |dσ|_η²=-a²+a²=0, so Ric[g0]=0 exactly;
conformal flatness then makes full curvature zero. Different a give different
normalized references on the same domain. For rest curves x=0 and x=L and an
outgoing ray t_o=t_e+L, σ(o)=σ(e), so this direction's contrast is1. For a
receiver at y=L, x=0, σ(o)=σ(e)+aL and Z/Z0=σ(o)/σ(e), although both metrics
are flat. The preparation/reference has changed; this is not a flat-space UDT
effect. It is a concrete reason not to call normalization alone an intrinsic
physical separator.

A nondegeneracy condition on Weyl or extra reference data may restore uniqueness
in a restricted class. If used, its exact hypothesis must be proved and the
flat/Weyl-degenerate limit left outside the conclusion. Normalization removes
constant scale freedom; clock contrasts already cancel constant u. Mathematical
uniqueness alone does not show a unique causal explanation or a UDT law.

## Independent argument B: an actual held-orbit echo and curvature

Take g=exp(2φ)gbar, with smooth stationary gbar and a specified timelike Killing
field K. In adapted local coordinates K=∂t, the emitter and zero-delay relay
follow fixed spatial orbits. They may require acceleration in g. Assume a
regular outbound/return null branch persists under small translations in t;
stationarity of gbar and conformal null-path invariance make the return map
t_return=t_emit+D with D fixed for this protocol. No hypersurface orthogonality
is needed, and outbound/return flight times need not coincide. With
N(x)=sqrt(-gbar(K,K)), dτ=exp(φ)N dt. The actual emitter-clock observable is

    Q=dτ_return/dτ_emit=exp[φ(t+D,x_A)-φ(t,x_A)].

The stationary lapse, relay proper rates and intermediate conformal factors
cancel in the zero-delay product. A finite relay proper delay would generally
alter the coordinate return-map derivative and needs a separate treatment.
Arbitrary movement relative to these orbits, changed null branches, or drift
of the reference invalidates this formula without further terms.

Now let I=C_abcd C^abcd for the physical metric. Weyl conformal weight gives
I=exp(-4φ) Ibar. Stationarity makes Ibar constant along each K orbit. Therefore
at the emitter's emission/return events,

    Q^4 I_return = I_emit,
    log Q = -(1/4)log(|I_return|/|I_emit|),

with the log/inverse version only on a nonzero branch. Lorentzian I is not a
positive norm: Weyl may be nonzero while I=0, and its sign need not be positive.
An all-zero I makes the polynomial relation vacuous, not a conformal-factor
measurement. An independent curvature reconstruction is essential; inserting
I computed from the same fitted φ would only check the implementation.

This is a useful joint consistency relation for the stated conformally stationary
geometry and held-orbit experiment. It does not require Ric(gbar)=0. Passing
it at finitely many echoes does not prove conformal stationarity or a unique
representative, and failing it only rejects at least one hypothesis of that
combined model/preparation (once errors are controlled). A time-independent
rescaling of gbar can be absorbed into φ without changing these temporal
differences. No preferred physical population is selected by making one
declared query.

Several adverse checks limit the physical headline. A nonconstant spatial φ
has Q=1 on every stationary orbit but changes one-way clock comparisons and
curvature. A periodic temporal φ may have Q=1 for one echo lag D although the
metric varies between the endpoints. Conversely Q>1 is only a product of the
two leg ratios: one can be below1. Thus Q≠1 detects evolution for these held
histories under the hypotheses; it does not establish mutual slowing on both
legs, a sign for all observers, a separation law, or a distance asymptote.

## Quiet limits, attribution and ordinary GR alternatives

Neither a unique additive decomposition nor a second geometry as an actual
world is logically necessary to identify an additional effect. A theory could
supply one covariant law for g and predict independently measured differences
under well-specified preparations. The burden here is the justification of the
chosen reference/criterion and its actual discriminator, not proof of a unique
sum of causes. Absence of such a proof cannot establish whole-postulate
insufficiency or necessity of a new founding premise.

Conversely an intrinsic mathematical prescription is not automatically a
physical attribution. Ricci-flat removal can classify ordinary GR Ricci
curvature as the remainder, including familiar matter or cosmological curvature.
A concrete ordinary-GR control is de Sitter in flat slicing,
g=a(η)^2η_flat with a=-1/(Hη), η<0. Rest/comoving clocks have Z=a_o/a_e and
nontrivial echoes, while Ric=3H²g and I=0. This is a comparison, not an imported
UDT source law or a certification that it satisfies all UDT premises. It shows
that endpoint conformal redshift alone is not a distinctive physical cause.
For a general supplied g, formally defining a GR stress tensor from its Einstein
tensor similarly does not establish physically acceptable matter; that further
question would need independent matter/energy tests. No such tests are claimed.

A quiet check should distinguish: exact return to a stationary history;
uniform small deviations on a specified finite experiment; degenerating Weyl
reference/curvature inversion; and zero observed contrast. Q→1 or a small
endpoint u difference does not imply convergence of g, its derivatives, free
fall, or all tested observables. C² closeness on a common compact experiment
and bounds on accelerations, null branches and derivatives could support a
finite recovery claim; no numerical solar threshold follows from ordinary
local SR or from a conformal identity. The flat nonuniqueness example warns
against claiming a uniform reference selector through the Weyl-degenerate limit.
