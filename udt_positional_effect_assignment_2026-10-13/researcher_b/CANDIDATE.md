## 2. Inverse reference criterion and exact scope

Use the conventional curvature indexing R_abcd=g_ae R^e_bcd, with
Ric_bd=R^a_bad, and define

    P_ab = (Ric_ab - R g_ab/6)/2,
    A_abc = nabla_b P_ca - nabla_c P_ba.

Here A is the Cotton tensor, not COC1's four-index curvature tensor. Let W be
the Weyl tensor with this indexing. Suppose the linear map

    beta_d -> beta^d W_dabc

is injective at every event of the patch. This is the precise weak-genericity
condition, not merely W!=0 or a positive curvature-squared invariant.

If g0=e^(2u)g is Ricci-flat, the conformal Schouten identity gives

    Q_ab = P_ab - nabla_a beta_b + beta_a beta_b
           - (beta_c beta^c/2)g_ab = 0,       beta=du.            (1)

The transformed Cotton tensor vanishes, hence

    A_abc + beta^d W_dabc = 0.                                  (2)

Equation(2) has at most one beta by injectivity. If a solution exists, it is a
smooth one-form on a constant-rank patch: any local nonsingular4x4 minor gives
the same unique solution, and overlap uniqueness patches it. No indefinite
normal-equation pseudoinverse is assumed.

An exact inverse test is therefore: solve all of(2), then check the **full**
tensor(1), not only its trace-free part. Its antisymmetric part forces d beta=0.
Contractibility makes beta=du, and the conformal Schouten identity proves
Ric(e^(2u)g)=0. Conversely every such reference passes. One value u(p)=0 fixes
the integration constant. On a noncontractible region, vanishing periods or an
already supplied global u is additionally necessary; no global claim follows.

Thus CVA is locally a well-defined differential restriction/evaluator on this
sector. It does not determine g or its free conformal factor. At a finite noisy
measurement, differentiating curvature and solving(2) needs error control; exact
uniqueness gives no conditioning or instrument guarantee.

## 3. The actual matched residual and the added sign law

Take arbitrary timelike endpoint curves with the same event parameters in both
metrics. Their proper intervals satisfy d tau0=e^u d tau. The conformal metrics
have identical unparameterized null curves, so the selected regular incidence
map between the endpoint parameters is also identical. Therefore the actual
received inter-tick ratios obey exactly

    Z_g / Z_0 = exp(u_emit-u_receive).                          (3)

This derivation uses the arrival map, not merely coordinate lapse rates.
Endpoint velocities cancel in the contrast, but proper accelerations and
freefall preparations need not agree. GRL1 T1 already excludes a nonconstant
factor if null paths and two nonparallel freefall directions at every point
must both match. Same-history matching is the present weaker, explicit protocol.

For all sufficiently short future null segments, (3)>=1 iff beta(k)<=0 for
every future null vector k. Necessity differentiates u along each local null
geodesic; sufficiency integrates along the chosen segment. In an orthonormal
frame beta(k)=beta0+beta_spatial.n for k=(1,n). The condition is precisely
beta0+|beta_spatial|<=0: beta-sharp is future causal or zero. A nonzero future
timelike beta-sharp gives strict inequality for every future null segment.
This is an exact local equivalence under the declared smoothness/time orientation;
the causal covector condition remains an unadopted physical requirement.

For a short same-history flight, log(Z_g/Z_0)=-beta(k)lambda+O(lambda²).
This possible O(L) effect cannot be identified with PSW1's O(L²) coefficients
under its separately parallel-transported/freefall preparation. The protocols
are different. A finite two-leg protocol also cannot infer each individual
sign merely from the product; A's explicit control demonstrates that limit.

## 4. Supplied Weyl-nonzero positive control

Let g_S be Schwarzschild with M>0 on a fixed regular exterior patch r>2M:

    g_S=-F dt²+F^-1 dr²+r²dOmega², F=1-2M/r,
    g=exp(2Ht)g_S, H>0.                                       (4)

This is a known conformal-Schwarzschild/Thakurta-type comparison, not a new
metric construction. Its unique CVA anchor is g_S up to constant calibration,
because Schwarzschild Weyl contraction is injective. One direct proof uses a
static orthonormal frame: the electric Weyl matrix has nonzero eigenvalues
proportional to(-2,1,1)M/r³ and the magnetic Weyl part is zero. In
v^d W_dabc=0, choosing abc=(i,0,j) first
forces v0=0; choosing abc=(0,0,j) then forces each spatial vi=0. Conformal
rescaling and pullback preserve this injectivity.

Here u=-Ht, beta-sharp is future timelike, and every future null segment in
the exterior has t_receive>t_emit. Consequently

    Z_g/Z_0=exp(H Delta t)>1.

Weyl tides survive. On each fixed compact regular exterior region g approaches
g_S in C-infinity as H->0. This witnesses mathematical compatibility of this
optional assignment with a Schwarzschild recovery limit, not empirical recovery
bounds or a native history. It does not prove that the residual is physically
additional to ordinary GR source effects.

CHECK_PLAN.md and CVA_CHECK_RESULT.json preserve the original-metric controls.
The differentiated Ricci has Ric_tt=0, Ric_tr=2HM/[r(r-2M)] and
R=6H² exp(-2Ht)/F. All64 equations(2) and full Q vanish for beta=-Hdt.
At the declared exact rational point, inverse solution of all64 rows returns

The source/normalization controls are author regressions with a reused reviewed

An adverse exact sector is Kottler, F=1-2M/r-kappa r², M*kappa!=0.

on that sector. This is a genuine restriction of CVA, not a reason to reject

## 5. Degenerate Weyl is an assignment issue, not just a missing inverse

In flat coordinates eta, both eta and

    g0=[1+b(t+x)]^-2 eta

are Ricci-flat on a positive local denominator patch. They agree in units at
the origin, but are distinct reference tensors under the fixed event identity.
The null-affine denominator has zero norm gradient and Hessian, so the full
conformal Ricci tensor vanishes. The original-coordinate symbolic check also
verifies this. A denominator1+bt instead gives Ric=3b² g0 and is rejected; this
guards against silently dropping the gradient-norm term in the full equation.

This reproduces a special case of the already credited VS1/VS2 degeneracy.
The proposed universal extension cannot simply select an arbitrary inverse or
pretend one clock calibration resolves it. Section6 gives the stronger new
discriminator: a path-dependent limiting assignment even when every nonflat
member has a unique reference.

## 6. No continuous flat extension across all these admissible approaches

This construction develops the coordinator's proposed test, rather than an
independent researcher-B origin. It is local and exact; no singular center,
boundary identification or physical reference population is involved.

In coordinates X=(t,x,y,z), put rho²=x²+y²+z² and choose a>0. Define

    v=1+2at+a²(t²-rho²),
    F(X)=(t+a(t²-rho²),x,y,z)/v.                              (5)

On a sufficiently small neighborhood D of p=0, v>0,1+at>0,
F(p)=p, DF(p)=Id and

    F*eta=v^-2 eta, det DF=v^-4,
    eta^-1(dv,dv)=-4a²v<0.                                  (6)

The inverse-function theorem and shrinking D give a regular time-oriented local
diffeomorphism; its inverse is the same construction with a replaced by -a.
The exact symbolic Jacobian check is specified separately in
FLAT_LIMIT_CHECK_PLAN.md; it supports these identities, not the family argument.

Choose a spatial point c=(R,0,0) outside the closures of D and F(D), and shrink
D as needed so both remain a positive distance from c. In Cartesian coordinates
let S_epsilon be Schwarzschild centered at c with mass epsilon R>0. Explicitly,
with r_c=|x-c| and n=dr_c,

    S_epsilon=eta+(2epsilon R/r_c)dt²
              +[(1-2epsilon R/r_c)^-1-1]n².

For sufficiently small epsilon>0 both domains lie outside its horizon. The
coefficients and all derivatives converge uniformly on each compact subpatch
to eta as epsilon->0, and its Weyl contraction is injective for every epsilon>0.

Now compare two families of **physical metrics** on the same D:

    g_epsilon=exp(2epsilon t/R) S_epsilon,
    h_epsilon=v² F*S_epsilon.                                (7)

Both converge to eta in C-infinity; at p their physical tangent metrics also
agree exactly. By section2 and weak genericity their unique normalized CVA
anchors are respectively S_epsilon and F*S_epsilon. Their positive factors
Omega=e^-u are exp(epsilon t/R) and v, both normalized to1 at p. Thus their
factors converge to1 and v, different functions on the same flat limit.

This remains a counterexample after imposing **strict** future-null positivity.
For g_epsilon, d log Omega=(epsilon/R)dt is strictly positive on all future null
vectors in the regular exterior. For h_epsilon, dv has past timelike raised
gradient relative to eta throughout a sufficiently small compact closure, by
(6) and the future time orientation. Timelikeness and orientation are open;
smooth h_epsilon->eta gives the same property for h_epsilon for all sufficiently
small epsilon>0. Hence d log v(k)>0 for every future null k. Both paths obey
the added strict sign condition, while their limiting assignment differs.

For an actual finite null-clock contrast, fix the emitter history x=y=z=0 and
receiver history x=L,y=z=0, with L>0 small enough that p and q=(L,L,0,0) lie
inside the common regular tube. Emit at p. For both metric families the receiver
intersection q_epsilon tends q. In a common convex normal tube the squared
geodesic interval is smooth in metric/endpoint data; its reception-time
derivative at the limiting null intersection is -L!=0. The implicit-function
theorem gives a smooth local incidence map, including its emission-time
derivative. Proper-clock rates also converge. Therefore the actual **net**
received ratios in both families tend the same Minkowski static-clock value1.

Their assigned CVA contrasts instead have distinct limits by(3):

    lim Z_g_epsilon/Z_anchor_g = 1,
    lim Z_h_epsilon/Z_anchor_h = v(q)/v(p)=1+2aL > 1.           (8)

Every nonflat member has a unique anchor and both paths satisfy strict CVA+.
Consequently no continuous extension at the flat metric can agree with this
normalized CVA assignment on **all** such smooth approaches under the stated
fixed event/history calibration. This is stronger than saying a particular
Weyl matrix becomes small or a chosen numerical inverse is ill conditioned.
It does not exclude continuity on a further restricted family with extra
reference data, or a different physical positional assignment.
Requiring a physical attribution to extend continuously through this flat limit
would itself be an additional, UNADOPTED regularity criterion; the mathematical
lack of such an extension is the result proved here. No blanket exclusion of
all low-curvature sectors or all positional assignments follows.

## 7. Decision and maximum surviving claim

CVA replaces an arbitrary supplied reference by an intrinsic, unique conditional
one in its exact weak-generic sector and has a finite same-history redshift
meaning. It imposes a real geometric restriction, preserves a nonzero-Weyl
Schwarzschild limit, and admits the separately stated causal-gradient sign law.
It neither selects the free geometry nor derives its physical attribution.

The stronger return is adverse to treating CVA as a universal, stable positional
assignment: the flat limit is path dependent even with calibration and strict
future-null red contrast. Exact pointwise reconstruction cannot cure this.
The metric's total clock observables remain smooth. A further boundary rule or
smaller domain could change that claim, but is additional physical structure,
not supplied by the present criterion or a successful symbolic calculation.

Ordinary GR can also realize conformal-Schwarzschild metrics with source terms;
A's primary-source review gives that comparison its exact scope. Geometry alone
does not label their redshift as beyond GR. A stationary-anchor echo can test a
conditional geometric relation, but its hypothesis/preparation and inability to
infer both leg signs survive. This is a useful restricted evaluator and a
concrete rejected universality/continuity identification, not native selection,
an insufficiency theorem for all UDT commitments, or a ban on other assignments.

Omissions: no empirical precision, finite-noisy curvature reconstruction,
source-free physical-sector test, universal stationary congruence, global domain,
flat-to-Weyl transition classification, nonconformal assignment, or dynamics.
