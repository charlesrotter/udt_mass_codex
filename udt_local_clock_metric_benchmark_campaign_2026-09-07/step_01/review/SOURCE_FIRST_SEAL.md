   G312/G313's provisional vacuum constitution supplies no Earth source

   laboratory worldlines and conventional readout/metrology remain supplied.
2. For timelike Killing K, write g(K,K)=-c_E^2 N^2 and u=K/N. Killing's
   identity gives a_mu=c_E^2 partial_mu ln N. Integrating along an identified
   proper vertical path gives ln(N2/N1)=integral a.e dh/c_E^2. The actual
   clock comparison must implement the proper-rate ratio; a received photon
   frequency ratio is a different endpoint convention. With height increasing
   downward as in the paper's pair ordering, the leading signed gradient is
   r=-|g|/c_E^2, or r=-10^17 |g|/c_E^2 in Table1's units 10^-19/cm.

   standard uncertainty u_y=2.6, in those units. The paper's split .7(stat),
   2.5(sys) and listed rows document construction/rounding; they must not be
   added again to the already total2.6 or substituted as a more precise new
   primary uncertainty. The correction signs satisfy raw+correction=corrected.
4. Independent control channels supply g=-9.803m/s^2 (rounded), DDS/camera
   geometry and tilt, with effective extent .99(.01)cm. These are supplied

   the .01cm geometry uncertainty cannot be reverse engineered into a new
   independent joint covariance. Printed g's rounding precision is not a
   certified gravimeter error bound. A geometry uncertainty already carried

   listed marginal errors are contributions to the same linear output; the

   Such envelopes bound standard deviation/variance conditionally, not the
