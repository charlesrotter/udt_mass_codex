# Lay report

The earlier local-curvature work had already shown that the metric can play every possible local
curvature “note.” This audit sorted those notes by instrument using the corrected UDT metric.

The result matches the orchestra metaphor surprisingly well:

- the reciprocal-depth instrument alone can play 8 of the 20 independent curvature notes;
- the other base instruments together can play 11;
- the angular screen can play 14;
- the mixing instruments can play 18.

No instrument family plays the complete score alone. In the particular local way we divided the
metric into reciprocal, angular, and mixing instruments, reaching all 20 notes needs the angular
screen and mixing families plus some base family. Founded depth can fill that base slot, but it is
not the only option. This is a real local bookkeeping bridge, not yet a unique physical division of
the metric or a law saying which song the universe plays. (`SUPPLIED_SPLIT_LOCAL_ATLAS`.)

There is also one universal harmony rule. One direct reciprocal-to-angular curvature component must
equal a signed combination of two mixed components. This is the Bianchi identity. It ties the
sections together but still leaves 20 freely variable curvature notes, so it is not an equation of
motion.

The causal regimes change the size of the tidal stage:

- timelike or spacelike depth gives six freely variable tidal components;
- lightlike depth gives three components on a natural two-dimensional quotient screen;
- zero depth gives no depth-selected tidal screen.

That is an elegant stratified structure already inside the metric. What is still missing is the
conductor: a native rule saying how one local curvature arrangement follows another and which
global arrangement is self-consistent. This calculation did not invent that rule and is not a
time-live simulation.

One further caution matters. We sorted second-curvature changes in a frame where first derivatives
are set to zero at one point, and separately asked what tidal geometry looks like for a supplied
depth direction. We have not yet proved that those two descriptions are generated together by one
physical UDT solution: `SAME_SOLUTION_JOIN_OPEN_NOT_DERIVED`.
